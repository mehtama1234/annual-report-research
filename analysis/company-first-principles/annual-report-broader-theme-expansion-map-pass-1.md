## The Bigger Picture

There are two different writing modes in the archive.

The first is the hard proof mode:

`who supplied money -> what wrapper carried it -> who received it -> what it funded -> what cash came back -> who had legal claim -> what return was earned`

That is the named-cash capital-flow lane.

The second is the broader annual-report theme mode:

`what scarce function does this company or cluster perform, why is that function becoming more valuable, what operating evidence supports it, and what would disprove it`

Both modes matter.

The capital-flow lane keeps the writing honest. It prevents broad stories from becoming loose claims.

The broader theme lane makes the work useful for understanding the economy. It explains the real operating forces: power scarcity, healthcare burden transfer, digital control, industrial uptime, built-environment upkeep, pressured consumers, and private-market capital routing.

## How To Choose What To Dig Next

Use this order:

1. If the goal is strongest evidence, choose a theme with named documents and cash fields.
2. If the goal is best writing, choose a theme with a simple human mechanism and multiple company examples.
3. If the goal is originality, choose a theme where annual reports show something public narratives miss.
4. If the goal is investment usefulness, choose a theme where operating conversion, margin, cash flow, and disproof tests are visible.
