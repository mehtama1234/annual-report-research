## What Costco Is Doing In Simple Words

Costco is not just selling cheaper goods.

It is selling a relationship where the customer pays first and shops later. That matters. A normal retailer has to win the customer trip by trip. Costco gets the customer to join, then the customer has a reason to keep using the membership. The household thinks: "I already paid to belong, so I should use this store to make the fee worthwhile."

That changes the behavior. The membership creates commitment. Commitment creates more trips. More trips create more volume. More volume helps Costco buy efficiently and run warehouses efficiently. That helps keep the value promise credible. The value promise supports renewal.

This is why Costco is a strong value-retail example. It turns household caution into a system. People are not only trying to pay the lowest price. They are trying to avoid feeling foolish, overcharged, or manipulated. Costco gives them a shopping format where spending feels disciplined.

## Money Path

The safe money path is:

`household budget pressure -> membership decision -> warehouse trips and digital orders -> merchandise sales plus membership fees -> inventory throughput and margin -> operating profit and cash flow`

The consumer psychology path is:

`fear of overpaying -> trusted value format -> paid access -> repeated trips -> habit -> renewal`

Both paths matter. The first is the accounting path. The second is why the accounting path may persist.

## Why This Matters For The Value-Retail Theme

Costco shows that value retail is not only "cheap stuff."

Value retail has several forms:

- Costco sells paid access to trusted value.
- Walmart sells basket consolidation and scale convenience.
- Target shows middle-income discretionary pressure.
- Dollar General shows small-basket necessity shopping.
- Burlington shows off-price bargain discovery.

Costco is the cleanest membership version. It is less about promotional noise and more about customer trust in the format.

## Safe Claim

`Costco is source-visible as a value-retail example: the local evidence shows FY2025 net sales of 269.9B USD, total revenue of 275.235B USD, membership fees of 5.323B USD, net income of 8.099B USD, Q3 2026 net sales of 69.15B USD, adjusted comparable sales of 6.6%, and digitally enabled comparable sales growth of 21.5%.`

## Do Not Overclaim

Do not say Costco proves the entire consumer is strong.

Do not say membership fees prove unlimited pricing power.

Do not say comparable sales prove unit growth without checking traffic, ticket, inflation, fuel, currency, and category mix.

Do not say digital growth is automatically good unless fulfillment cost and warehouse cannibalization are checked.

## Upgrade Test

Costco moves from value-access evidence to stronger cash-quality proof only if the next pass extracts:

2. traffic versus ticket
3. unit volume versus price
4. gross margin and merchandise margin

6. membership fee growth versus member count growth
7. digital margin or fulfillment cost
8. operating cash flow and free cash flow
