## Big Decision

Do not create twenty more top-level themes.

The old HTML trend names are strong, but they should become subthemes inside the newer framework.

The reason is simple:

`A reader needs one map, not two competing maps.`

The newer `15` themes should remain the main table of contents. The older `20` HTML dynamics should supply sharper explanations, examples, and subthemes.

## First-Principles Translation

The old HTML site gives us theme names that sound like the world people live in.

The newer markdown system gives us theme names that can hold evidence.

The writing job is to translate between the two.

For example:

`The Affordability Ceiling` is the human phrase.

`Value retail` is the evidence container.

The human phrase says:

`People are running into a budget wall.`

The evidence container asks:

`Which retailers gain traffic, membership fees, private-label mix, basket consolidation, and operating cash because households are trying to lower the risk of bad spending?`

That is the pattern across the whole project.

The old HTML names should open the reader's eyes. The new theme system should keep the claims honest.
