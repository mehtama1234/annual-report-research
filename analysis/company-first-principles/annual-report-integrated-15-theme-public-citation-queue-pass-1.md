## Citation Attachment Rules

1. A named company plus a metric needs a public source.
2. A named company plus a qualitative operating claim should still cite the annual report or filing section where the operating model is described.
3. A sector-wide pattern can be framed as synthesis if the section gives company examples and a boundary.
4. The `15`-theme map should be cited to the internal synthesis for review, but external publication should cite the underlying public company filings.
5. Do not cite AUM, ARR, backlog, capex, traffic, volume, or revenue as if those metrics prove cash return.
