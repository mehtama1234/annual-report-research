## Source Attachment Standard

For internal reader review:

`The integrated article can cite the local synthesis artifacts and packet anchors.`

For external publication:

`Every named company and metric needs a public source citation.`

The minimum public-source attachment for each theme is:

1. One annual report or 10-K for each major named example.
2. One recent quarterly filing or earnings release when the section uses current-period metrics.
3. A public docket, tariff, statutory schedule, or trustee/collateral document where the claim depends on legal recovery, legal-entity assets, billing, or collateral.
4. A final note saying the theme map is synthesis, not complete proof of all 519 companies.
