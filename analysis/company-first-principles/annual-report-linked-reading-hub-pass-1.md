`This company sits near a bottleneck and gets paid through this kind of route.`

`Can we prove who supplied cash, where it legally went, what it funded, what cash came back, and who had the legal claim on that cash?`

## Why This Was Not Linked Before

The short answer is sequencing.

The project started as research and proof chasing, not as a finished reading product. As each pass added a new layer, the file set grew:

1. first-principles cluster synthesis
2. capital-flow proof pages
3. article draft pages
4. theme pages
5. company extraction packets
6. cash-quality bridges
7. completion audit
8. what-else map
9. big-findings page

Each layer was individually useful, but the project needed a final navigation pass to connect them by reader need.

This page is that navigation pass.

## Safe Use

Use this hub as the main linked entry point for the annual-report work.

Do not treat this hub as new evidence. It is a navigation and organization layer over existing artifacts.
