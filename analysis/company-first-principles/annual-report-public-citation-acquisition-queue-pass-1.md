## Minimum Public Release Standard

Public release needs one of three outcomes for every company-specific claim:

1. A public source citation is attached.
2. The claim is softened into a synthesis statement.
3. The claim is removed.

The source citation should point to the filing, annual report, investor presentation, tariff, docket filing, statutory schedule, debt schedule, cash-flow statement, or workpaper that supports the exact claim.

## Fastest Path To External Release

1. Attach company annual-report citations for all seven theme chapters.
2. Attach docket/statutory citations for the two deeper proof examples: FPL regulated recovery and Apollo/KKR statutory asset income.
3. Run paragraph-level overclaim review against the final publication draft.
4. Keep the final boundary paragraph.
