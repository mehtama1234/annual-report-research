## Public Citation Rules

1. Cite the company filing or source document when naming a company and a metric.
2. Keep synthesis claims labeled as synthesis.
3. Do not cite an internal artifact as if it were the primary source.
4. Use internal artifacts to find the source route and proof boundary.
5. Do not remove the final boundary paragraph.

## Reader Review Checklist

- The article explains the thesis without assuming prior project knowledge.
- Every major theme has a plain-English claim.
- Every major theme has a proof boundary.
- The capital-platform section answers the money-flow question without pretending AUM is proof.
- The proof boxes appear before the final takeaway or source notes.
- The final boundary says the article is not a stock recommendation and not completed named-cash proof.
