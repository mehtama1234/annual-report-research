# Annual Report Publication Readiness Review Pass 1

## Purpose

This pass reviews the annual-report writing package as a publication object. It asks whether the reader-facing essay, exhibits, chapter spines, and proof boxes can be used now, and where the writing must keep explicit boundaries.

The answer is yes, with boundaries. The package is ready as a synthesis essay about annual reports as maps of economic bottlenecks. It is not ready to claim named-cash proof across every company, borrower, asset, project, receipt, debt waterfall, collateral certificate, or return model.

## Publication Verdict

`publication-ready-with-boundaries`

The writing can proceed as a detailed, no-background-required essay. The central claim is strong enough: annual reports show where money is trying to move through bottlenecks in power, capital routing, industrial uptime, healthcare access, built-environment upkeep, digital control, and value retail.

The writing must not imply that every money chain is fully proven. The strongest evidence remains theme-level and company-level in many lanes. Full cash proof still requires receipt, billing, settlement, legal-entity, borrower-use, collateral, debt-waterfall, working-capital, project-margin, and return-model evidence.

## Required Exhibit Placement

- Exhibit 2: place before the capital-platforms chapter or before the proof-lane section.
- Exhibit 3: place after the seven-chapter introduction.
- Exhibit 4: place before the proof-box section.
- Exhibit 5: place before the conclusion or in an appendix.
- Exhibit 6: use as an introductory sidebar.

## Safe External Claims

1. Annual reports can be read as maps of economic bottlenecks.
2. Strong companies often absorb burdens that customers, governments, households, borrowers, builders, operators, and digital users cannot easily manage themselves.
3. The evidence supports theme-level writing, but not full named-cash proof.
4. The seven themes are power scarcity, capital platforms, industrial uptime, healthcare access, built-environment upkeep, digital control, and value retail.
5. Full cash proof needs receipts, billing support, settlement records, borrower-use evidence, legal-entity schedules, collateral certificates, debt waterfalls, working-capital conversion, project margins, and return models.

## Forbidden Claims

1. The package proves all covered companies are investment winners.
2. AUM, inflows, fundraising, backlog, ARR, patient volume, traffic, or capex automatically equal productive capital deployment or investor returns.
3. Revenue, booked demand, regulated recovery authority, or operating scale automatically prove cash conversion.
4. Refinancing always creates value.
5. Broad theme evidence proves a named-cash chain from source of funds to final receipt and return.
