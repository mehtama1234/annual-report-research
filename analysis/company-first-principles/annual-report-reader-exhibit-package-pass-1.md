## Purpose

This pass creates compact exhibits for the reader-facing essay:

`/cluster/annual-report-reader-facing-essay-pass-1.md`

The companion table is:

`analysis/company-first-principles/data/annual-report-reader-exhibit-package-pass-1.csv`

## Exhibit 1: The Whole Map

| Theme | What Is Scarce | Plain-English Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Power scarcity | Reliable delivered electricity | Growth needs power that actually shows up. |
| Capital platforms | Trusted capital-routing channels | Big managers route savings into assets. |
| Industrial uptime | Equipment, parts, support, and execution | Physical work fails when access breaks. |
| Healthcare access | Organized care under cost and labor pressure | Demand is obvious; access is the bottleneck. |
| Built-environment upkeep | Repair, replacement, and civic systems | Expensive shelter keeps creating maintenance burdens. |
| Digital control | Permission, routing, security, and governance | More online activity needs trusted checkpoints. |
| Value retail | Spending formats that reduce regret | Pressured consumers split the wallet by job. |

## Exhibit 3: What Each Chapter Proves

| Chapter | Strong Current Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Power scarcity | Load, contracts, capex plans, regulatory recovery, backlog, contractor execution. | Not full project-level customer receipt or earned return. |
| Capital platforms | AUM, inflows, fundraising, originations, dry powder, statutory and borrower partials. | Not borrower receipt, settlement cash, waterfall, or final return. |
| Industrial uptime | Fleet OEC, rental revenue, vending, branches, project backlog, gross margin, OCF. | Not fleet-class ROIC, borrowing-base certificate, or project collection. |
| Healthcare access | Revenue, patients, admissions, census, pharmacy/services, reimbursement, labor indicators. | Not care quality, denial fairness, receivable collection, or trust. |
| Built environment | Sales, Pro channels, coatings, lumber cycle, contractor distribution, waterworks demand. | Not one housing beta and not project-level receipt or ROIC proof. |
| Digital control | ARR, RPO, deferred revenue, traffic, bookings, AI ARR, security demand, FCF. | Not automatic operating leverage or durable product-level cash conversion. |
| Value retail | Traffic, comps, ticket, membership, e-commerce, ads, margin, inventory signals. | Not durable cohort quality, shrink control, or long-term margin proof. |

## Exhibit 4: The Three Proof Boxes

| Proof Box | Core Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Working capital and inventory | Does growth become cash? | Revenue can rise while cash gets trapped in receivables, inventory, reserves, or supplier finance. |
| Acquisition and backlog conversion | Does acquired scale or backlog convert after close? | Buying companies or announcing backlog is not the same as earning cash and margin. |
| Refinancing and debt reshaping | Did new money improve future cash economics? | Refinancing can reduce pressure or simply push risk forward. |

## Exhibit 5: What Not To Overclaim

| Visible Metric | Tempting Claim | Safer Claim |
|---|---|---|
| AUM and inflows | Productive capital was deployed into named assets. | The platform controls or attracts capital; destination and return need separate proof. |
| Capex plan | Project return is proven. | Capital deployment intent or plan is visible; output and return remain open. |
| Backlog | Cash is secured. | Future work visibility exists; customer funding, margin, and collection remain open. |
| Revenue growth | Cash conversion is proven. | Demand or billing is visible; working-capital and OCF proof remain needed. |
| Patient volume | Care quality is proven. | Access and demand are visible; outcomes, trust, and collection remain separate. |
| ARR/RPO | Durable software economics are proven. | Contracted or recurring demand is visible; margin and cash conversion remain needed. |
| Traffic/comps | Consumer model is durable. | Customer activity is visible; cohort quality, shrink, and margin durability remain open. |
| Refinancing proceeds | Value was created. | Source/use mechanics are visible; cost, settlement, and forward economics remain needed. |

## Exhibit 7: Final One-Page Thesis

`Annual reports show the economy becoming a system of bottlenecks. The best companies are not only selling goods or services. They are trusted operators of routes, permissions, assets, workflows, and financial channels that keep the system moving. The evidence is writing-ready at the theme level, but full cash proof still requires receipts, billing support, settlement records, borrower use evidence, legal-entity schedules, collateral certificates, debt waterfalls, working-capital conversion, project margins, and return models.`
