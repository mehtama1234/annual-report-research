## Reader Instructions

Read the final publication draft once for the argument, not for line editing.

On the first read, answer four questions:

1. Is the main idea clear in the first page?
2. Do the seven themes feel connected rather than forced?
3. Does the capital-platform section answer the money-flow question in plain English?
4. Do the boundaries make the piece more credible rather than slowing it down?

On the second read, mark only three types of issues:

1. Confusing sentence.
2. Unsupported company-specific claim.
3. Overclaim that sounds like a stock recommendation or full named-cash proof.

## What Is Locked

- The title frame: `Annual Reports Are Maps Of Economic Bottlenecks`.
- The seven-theme structure.
- The money-flow ladder.
- The three proof boxes.
- The final boundary that says the article is not a stock recommendation and not completed cash-chain proof.

## What Can Change

- Section ordering if the reader thinks capital platforms should come before power.
- Paragraph length and pacing.
- Company examples if a better public citation path exists.
- Amount of detail in the proof boxes.
- Whether the public version uses all exhibits or only the whole-map and money-flow ladder.
