## Plain-English Rule For Every Extraction

For each company, the work should answer six questions:

1. What real-world need does this company satisfy?
2. Who pays the company?
3. What exactly is the customer paying for?
4. What accounting line shows the payment or obligation?
5. What cash-flow line shows whether revenue becomes cash?
6. What fact would make the claim weaker or false?

If an extraction cannot answer those questions, the company can still be a theme example, but it should not be used as a strong proof case.
