## Use

Use these pages as the detailed theme and subtheme writing base.

Use the linked reading hub when you want every major annual-report artifact connected in one place.

Use the what-else map when the reader needs the big picture first: the bottleneck, the plain-English question, the money path, the example companies, the evidence strength, and the proof gap for every theme.

Use the company-to-theme matrix when the question is not "what is the theme?" but "which companies show the theme, how do they get paid, and what evidence should we check?"

Each page follows the same logic:

1. what the theme really says
2. why the need exists
3. what companies are doing
4. how money moves
5. subthemes
6. what the theme tells us about society, industry, finance, or investment
7. safe claims
8. overclaim boundary
9. proof still needed
