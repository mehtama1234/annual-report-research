## Best Research Choice

The best research choice is:

`Power/grid customer cash`

Why:

1. It is the strongest public-document route.
2. It connects the reader hook to the money question.
3. It uses evidence types we already know how to inspect: tariffs, riders, dockets, rate factors, billing determinants, recovery schedules, and contractor backlog.
4. It can upgrade the first chapter, not just a technical appendix.

The target chain is:

`load request -> customer contract or tariff -> utility capital spend -> approved recovery -> billing determinant -> collected cash -> earned return`

That is a real end-to-end question.

## Best Big-Money Choice

The best big-money-player choice is:

`Insurance/statutory named asset income`

Why:

1. It reconnects the project to Apollo, KKR, Blackstone, BlackRock/HPS, Brookfield, Ares, and Carlyle.
2. It avoids weak AUM claims.
3. It uses legal-entity evidence instead of platform marketing language.
4. It can show named securities, income, proceeds, and liability-cost context.

The target chain is:

`insurance or retirement liability -> legal entity -> named security or loan -> income or proceeds -> liability cost -> spread or return`

This is the right lane if the main article wants the big-money players at the center.

## Best Article Choice

The best article choice is:

`Writing package finalization`

Why:

The seven themes are already coherent enough to write:

1. power scarcity
2. capital platforms
3. industrial uptime
4. healthcare access
5. built-environment upkeep
6. digital control
7. value retail

The remaining article risk is not structure. It is citation and overclaim control.

The target chain is:

`draft section -> internal evidence artifact -> public citation -> softened claim if citation is missing -> final review`

## What Counts As Done

For a deep dig, done means:

1. a named company, legal entity, borrower, project, collateral pool, or customer route is selected
2. the source documents are named
3. the money source is identified
4. the use or destination is identified
5. the cash-back route is visible or explicitly missing
6. the legal claim or waterfall is visible or explicitly missing
7. the return model is built or blocked by missing inputs
8. the final claim is marked pass, hold, or fail

For writing, done means:

1. the final article has no unsupported company-specific claims
2. every company-specific paragraph has public source notes or softened language
3. the proof boundaries remain visible
4. the article does not imply stock recommendations
5. exhibits are placed where they help the reader
