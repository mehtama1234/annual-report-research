## Mechanism

The batch mechanism is:

`519-company radar -> 71 selected lane candidates -> lane-specific proof fields -> case-study bridge pattern -> primary-source extraction -> normalized row -> denominator/disproof test -> proof-level refresh -> claim-control update`

The important rule is that every lane has its own proof standard. A utility row needs dockets and recovery evidence. A contractor row needs backlog quality and cash conversion. A private-credit row needs source/vehicle/borrower/facility evidence. An insurance row needs statutory asset quality.

## Lane Batch Questions

### Private Credit Direct Lending

Question:

`Can platform-scale private-credit capital be tied to vehicles, borrowers, facility size, use of proceeds, and bank-role outcomes?`

First candidates:

`KKR; Apollo; Ares; BlackRock; Blackstone; Carlyle; Brookfield`

Do not promote AUM, fundraising, or deployment as borrower allocation. The row must show vehicle or borrower-level evidence.

### Insurance And Retirement Capital

Question:

`Do insurance and retirement liability pools become durable credit supply with visible asset quality and capital treatment?`

First selected candidates:

`UnitedHealth; Cigna; MetLife; Prudential`

Platform exemplars already bridged:

`Apollo/Athene; KKR/Global Atlantic; Brookfield Wealth Solutions; Blackstone Credit & Insurance`

Do not promote channel scale into a safety or risk claim without statutory schedules, NAIC treatment, capital charges, affiliated exposure, and impairment/rating migration evidence.

### Power, Grid, And Project Finance

Question:

`Which companies are turning load demand into approved, recoverable, in-service infrastructure?`

First candidates:

`ONEOK; MasTec; Plains; Targa; NextEra; Vistra; Dycom; ABM; Granite; CACI; Constellation; Duke; NRG; Primoris`

Use the AEP/Duke bridge as the pattern. Do not treat load forecasts, turbines, or capital plans as recoverable investment until tariffs, dockets, approvals, recovery mechanisms, customer obligations, and status are visible.

### Capital Intensity And Capex

Question:

`Which companies turn capex and assets into output, cash, recovery, or durable return?`

First candidates:

`Jacobs; Steel Dynamics; Eaton; Energy Transfer; Sterling; Teledyne; AECOM; Knife River; Nucor; KBR; Verizon; Reliance; URI; Cheniere; ONE Gas`

Use URI, Sterling, and Cheniere as patterns. Do not treat capex, backlog, or assets as return without output and cash/recovery evidence.

### Debt Refinancing And Facilities

Question:

`Where is the capital stack changing, and does the change fund growth, refinance old debt, reduce risk, or preserve liquidity?`

First candidates:

`PBF; Devon; Matador; Liberty Broadband; Wheaton; Enhabit; Coterra; CNX; Ovintiv; Cenovus`

Do not treat refinancing as growth funding unless use-of-proceeds and before/after capital-stack evidence support it.

### Acquisition Finance

Question:

`Which deals buy assets, customers, capacity, or operating systems that later show integration, margin, cash, or output improvement?`

First candidates:

`Core & Main; Verizon; Sherwin-Williams; Lowe's; Roblox; Expand Energy; Rio Tinto; Packaging Corp.; Silgan; Phillips 66`

Do not treat deal value as productive investment without financing source, acquired asset/use evidence, and post-close operating evidence.

### Asset-Backed And Securitization

Question:

`Where do receivables, leases, inventories, or collateral pools convert operations into financeable assets?`

First candidates:

`Zebra; Target; MSC Industrial; Global Industrial; Pool; Dollar General; Honeywell; Synchrony; Arrow; Henry Schein`

Do not treat receivables or inventory as financeable capital without facility, collateral, advance-rate, lender/noteholder, and credit-performance evidence.

## Proof-Level Ladder

The batch refresh should use this promotion ladder:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `radar` | Company has a plausible role but no current extraction. |
| `source-table-visible` | A primary source has metrics, periods, and caveats extracted. |
| `normalized-bridge-visible` | Source facts are normalized into lane-specific rows. |
| `denominator-tested` | Company facts have outside or same-period denominator checks. |
| `proxy-grade` | Source, use, output, cash/recovery, and risk proxies align. |
| `project-and-cash-grade` | Named project/asset/facility joins source, use, output, cash, and risk controls. |
| `publication-ready-bounded` | External sentence is allowed with proof level and boundary attached. |
| `internal-only` | Useful internally but not safe for external empirical claim. |
