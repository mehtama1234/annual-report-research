## Upgrade Rule

A company can move from batch-ready to source-table-visible only if the next extraction captures:

- named company, project, asset, borrower, facility, vehicle, or category
- source document and local path
- period
- cash, billing, collection, revenue, repayment, investment income, margin, debt-service, or collateral-performance field
- source/use/output context
- disproof signal
- safe claim and do-not-claim boundary

## What This Does Not Prove

This pass does not prove:

- the `35` pilot companies are representative of all `519`
- every pilot company has source-grade cash evidence
- high score equals proof
- aggregate cash equals asset-level cash
- platform AUM equals borrower funding
- backlog equals funded collectable work
- capex equals return
- regulatory recovery equals category customer receipt
