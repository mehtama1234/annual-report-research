# AEP / Duke Large-Load Customer Security and Earnings-Quality Bridge

Research date: `2026-09-16`

This pass advances the power-grid customer-cash lane using current primary
company and SEC sources. It is a control bridge, not a forecast of utility
earnings. The question is whether a large-load headline can be promoted from
expected demand into customer-backed, recoverable, collected utility cash.

The required chain remains:

`customer request -> financial security -> interconnection/ESA status ->
utility investment -> approved recovery -> billed/collected cash -> durable
owner cash`

## QoE and financial-shenanigans control

The financial-shenanigans risk here is not an allegation of accounting fraud. It
is the risk that a headline load or capital-plan number is allowed to behave as
if it were earned, funded, or recoverable cash. The following controls remain
mandatory:

1. Keep signed/secured, LOA, prospective, late-stage pipeline, under-
   construction, and in-service buckets separate.
2. Keep cash collateral, guarantees, and letters of credit separate; none is
   automatically revenue or unrestricted cash.
3. Do not treat a capital plan as rate base, a rate-base addition as cash
   collection, or a customer-offset estimate as realized savings.
4. Test regulatory assets, deferred recovery, construction work in progress,
   and financing costs against period-matched operating cash and customer
   billing rather than adjusted earnings alone.
5. Search for cancellations, attrition, tariff conditions, contribution-in-
   aid, minimum-demand obligations, security-release terms, and cost-allocation
   provisions before promoting a demand row.

## Promotion result

Status: `partial-upgrade; customer-security evidence improved; cash bridge
open`.

The AEP observation is stronger than an expected-load claim because a filing
identifies a financial-security package. It is still not a receipt ledger,
approved cost-recovery order, or owner-cash denominator. The Duke observation
is a comparable control-language observation but lacks a disclosed amount in
the filing passage. Neither company can yet be promoted to project-level
return proof.
