## What Improved

## Why It Still Does Not Pass

The proof standard requires one named row to connect:

1. source instrument
2. recipient
3. legal entity
4. named use
5. receipt or settlement cash
6. payback/legal availability
7. return support

Antamina clears the first layers better than any current case, but it still breaks at the exact places that determine economics.

## Decision

`antamina-proof-package-acquisition-hold-use-settlement-waterfall-return-missing`

Antamina remains the lead named-cash candidate. It is not full named cash proof.
