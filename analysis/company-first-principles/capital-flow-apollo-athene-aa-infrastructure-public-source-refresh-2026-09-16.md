# Apollo–Athene AA Infrastructure Fund 2 public-source refresh

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Question

Can public sources identify the `00024D-AL-7` AA Infrastructure Fund 2
disposal/holding candidate and establish its legal-entity or infrastructure
investment context?

## Result

`evidence-insufficient` for Athene settlement, underlying asset cash, or
realized return.

An Athene Annuity and Life Company supplemental investment schedule identifies
AA Infrastructure Fund 2 LLC as one of its ten largest non-sovereign foreign
issues at `$762.348M` as of December 31, 2024, equal to `0.3%` of admitted
assets. The same schedule also identifies AA Infrastructure Fund 2 Depositor
LLC in the related-entity listing. The Apollo subsidiary exhibit independently
shows Apollo infrastructure-management entities, but it does not establish
that the Athene statutory row is owned by a particular Apollo fund, nor does it
provide asset-level cash flows.

These observations improve the legal-entity and wrapper map behind the
Athene `00024D-AL-7` candidate. They do not prove current Athene ownership,
the approximately `$348.0M` disposal settlement, infrastructure-project
receipts, debt or preferred claims, or cash available to Apollo.

## Decision

The route advances from a named statutory issuer row to a cross-entity
infrastructure-wrapper context. It remains `evidence-insufficient` for the
cash loop and return. The 2024 related-entity observation is historical and
must not be used as a 2025/2026 balance without a current filing.

## Next documents

Request the current Athene statutory investment schedule, AA Infrastructure
Fund offering/subscription documents, underlying project or asset schedule,
fund distribution notices, custodian settlement records, and liability-cost
allocation support.

## Safe claim

`Public statutory evidence links AA Infrastructure Fund 2 LLC and its depositor
to an Athene insurance investment perimeter and identifies an infrastructure-
wrapper context. It does not prove the Athene 00024D-AL-7 disposal settlement,
underlying project cash, or Apollo-owner return.`
