# Apollo/Athene AP Grange call-settlement search boundary pass 3

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Result

`ap-grange-public-call-and-prepayment-anchor-confirmed; settlement-document-search-negative`

The latest official public disclosures confirm the event but do not provide
the settlement ledger:

- Athene's Q2 2026 filing says AP Grange called its outstanding ABS debt in
  Q2 2026 and Athene recognized a `$673M` GAAP gain.
- Apollo's Q2 2026 filing says credit-strategy redemptions included
  approximately `$5.0B` related to the AP Grange prepayment.
- Athene's June 30, 2026 concentration table no longer lists AP Grange as a
  concentration, consistent with the call event, but does not identify the
  called tranche, paying agent, settlement date, or Athene cash account.

Targeted public searches for an AP Grange call notice, trustee or paying-agent
remittance, tranche-level redemption statement, and custody allocation did not
locate a certificate-grade settlement artifact. Public fund holdings continue
to show the instrument family (AP Grange Holdings LLC, 6.50%, due 03/20/2045)
in other portfolios, but those observations are not Athene settlement proof.

## 2026-09-18 targeted recheck

Exact searches for `AP Grange Holdings` with `call notice`, `trustee`,
`paying agent`, `redemption`, `G2964#`, and `ABS` returned the same Athene
issuer-level event and public-holder observations, but no new certificate-grade
call, remittance, custody, or settlement document. The SEC-hosted Athene
concentration exhibit preserves the `$673M` footnote and issuer table; the
Apollo filing preserves the approximately `$5.0B` credit-strategy
redemption-flow classification. Public fund reports that still show the
6.50% / 03/20/2045 instrument are holder-continuity context only and do not
identify Athene's lot or proceeds.

This recheck does not change the proof grade. It strengthens the searched-
negative boundary and leaves the next object as an authorized custody,
trustee/paying-agent, or fund-redemption allocation record.

### Exact-row settlement recheck

After the Q2 AAIA Schedule D Part 4 row was found, a narrower search for the
exact `$4,052,553,175` consideration, `G2964#-AA-7`, and the `04/10/2026`
disposal date was run against public web results and insurer-document routes.
It found no trustee notice, paying-agent statement, counterparty confirmation,
or bank/remittance record. A Brighthouse filing surfaced as an historical
March 31, 2026 holder-side CUSIP observation, not as the AAIA disposition's
settlement record. The new AAIA row therefore remains the strongest public
legal-entity consideration candidate, while the `Various` purchaser field is
still unresolved.

## Evidence boundary

```text
AP Grange issuer call
  -> Athene $673M GAAP gain
  -> Apollo approximately $5.0B credit-strategy redemption flow
  -> unresolved exact tranche/CUSIP, call date, paying agent, settlement cash,
     Athene allocation, liability release, and Apollo common-owner cash
```

The `$5.0B` figure is an Apollo AUM/net-flow classification, not a bank
receipt. The `$673M` figure is an issuer-level investment gain, not a disclosed
cash amount or realized owner return. The absence of a public call notice is a
search result, not proof that no notice exists; the likely next source is an
authorized holder, custodian, trustee, or paying-agent record.

## Next source object
