# Apollo–Athene AP Hansel public-source refresh

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Question

Can public sources connect Athene's `G2963@-AA-0` AP Hansel statutory disposal
candidate to an Apollo-controlled destination, identify the underlying asset
and cash mechanics, and establish a return-relevant transaction bridge?

## Result

`partial-upgrade` for the destination, ownership, asset-rights, dividend, and
transaction-timing layers; `evidence-insufficient` for exact CUSIP settlement,
Athene allocation, liability-adjusted return, or Apollo common-owner cash.

The local Athene Schedule D parser identifies `G2963@-AA-0`, described as
`AP Hansel SPV, LLC A`, as a disposal-only Paydown candidate with approximately
`$239.567M` of consideration and `$7.097M` of interest/dividend fields. The
disposal window runs from January 1 through February 7, 2025.

Aldar Properties' official 2024 annual report provides a direct Apollo and
asset-level destination route. It states that Aldar established Aldar Hansel
SPV Restricted Limited, a restricted-scope Abu Dhabi entity with 51% Class A
and 49% Class B shares, and transferred cash-flow rights over `2.6M` square
meters of land under a 20-year Deferred Land Sale and Purchase Agreement. It
then states that Class B shares were sold to AP Hansel SPV LLC, a wholly owned
company of Apollo Capital Management L.P. Apollo's returns were not
predetermined and depended on land values or gains from land sale.

The same report states that Hansel paid an AED `154.775M` dividend in 2024,
after AED `150.040M` in 2023. It also records a January 30, 2025 Class B share
repurchase deed under which the Class B shares were transferred back to Aldar
for `USD 493.226M`, effective February 5, 2025. The timing overlaps the
Athene statutory AP Hansel disposal window and is a strong transaction-level
lead, but it does not prove that the Athene row was a share of this repurchase,
that the consideration was remitted to Athene, or that the `$7.097M` field is
an attributable realized return.

## Decision

Promote AP Hansel from an unexplained statutory candidate to a named
Apollo-controlled real-estate and land-rights cash-flow route with public
destination, ownership, dividend, and exit-timing evidence. Do not promote
the Athene `$239.6M` row to an exact settled receipt, a proportional share of
the Aldar `$493.226M` repurchase, a realized Athene return, liability-adjusted
spread, or Apollo common-owner cash.

## Next documents

Request the AP Hansel subscription/share-transfer instrument, Aldar settlement
statement and bank confirmation, Athene trade confirmation and custodian
ledger, the Class B share ownership and distribution records, the DLSPA cash-
flow schedule, valuation support, taxes, fees, and any Apollo/Athene allocation
or upstream distribution records. Reconcile the statutory principal and
interest fields to the February 5 repurchase and to dividends received.

## Safe claim

`Aldar's official reports directly identify AP Hansel SPV LLC as wholly owned
by Apollo Capital Management and describe its 2.6M-square-meter, 20-year land-
rights structure, dividends, and February 2025 $493.226M Class B share
repurchase. Athene's $239.6M AP Hansel statutory Paydown row is temporally and
economically relevant, but remains an unallocated cash-like candidate rather
than proven Athene settlement or common-owner return.`
