# Apollo–Athene Apollo Debt Solutions payment-observability boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Question

Does the public record move the Apollo Debt Solutions `03770D-AC-7` route from
an exact instrument identity toward an observed coupon or principal payment?

## Result

`partial-upgrade` for the note's contractual payment mechanics and public
instrument observability; `evidence-insufficient` for Athene ownership,
Athene settlement, trustee remittance, borrower cash, liability-adjusted
return, or Apollo common-owner cash.

The SEC-filed second supplemental indenture identifies CUSIP `03770DAC7`,
Apollo Debt Solutions BDC as issuer, a 6.700% senior-unsecured note due July
29, 2031, an initial principal amount of `$600M`, and U.S. Bank Trust Company
as trustee. It specifies semiannual interest payment dates of January 29 and
July 29, with the first scheduled payment on January 29, 2025, and payment
through the registered-holder/DTC structure.

That document proves the contractual payment route, not an executed payment
into Athene's account. The public search did not locate a trustee remittance
history, Athene custodian settlement ledger, trade confirmation, or a
note-level principal/coupon allocation that joins the local Athene Schedule D
row to cash.

Two third-party statutory holdings documents independently make the CUSIP
publicly observable in the market: a West Virginia health-plan statement
contains a `03770D-AC-7` holding, and a Florida Treasury holdings statement
contains the same Apollo Debt Solutions CUSIP and July 29, 2031 maturity.
These are instrument-observability context only. They are not Athene records,
do not establish a counterparty to Athene's disposal candidate, and are not
used as Athene cash or return evidence.

## Safe claim

`Public sources establish how the 03770DAC7 note is contractually paid and
confirm that the instrument is observable under its exact CUSIP. They do not
establish that Athene received a coupon, sold the note to either observed
third-party holder, received the approximately $215.8M consideration, or
earned a liability-adjusted return.`

## Next upgrade

Obtain the Athene trade confirmation and custodian settlement ledger; request
the U.S. Bank trustee payment history or paying-agent statement; and obtain
Apollo Debt Solutions quarterly debt and portfolio schedules. Reconcile the
Athene lot, settlement date, accrued interest, principal/coupon remittance,
issuer funding source, borrower collections, debt cost, and residual available
to Apollo owners.
