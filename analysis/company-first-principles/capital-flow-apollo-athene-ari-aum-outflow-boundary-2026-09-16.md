# Apollo–Athene ARI AUM outflow boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## New buyer-side observation

Athene's Q2 2026 earnings release identifies `$7.8B` of 2Q'26 and last-twelve-
months total-AUM outflows related to ARI after the sale of ARI's commercial
mortgage loan portfolio to Athene. The same note separately identifies `$5.0B`
of outflows related to the prepayment of Intel's investment.

This is useful transaction-perimeter evidence: Athene's management reporting
recognizes the ARI transfer as an AUM outflow event. It is not a cash receipt,
purchase-price allocation, borrower repayment, or asset-level return. The
release defines AUM outflows as redemptions and other decreases in available
capital, so the `$7.8B` figure cannot be substituted for Athene's approximately
`$8.7B` purchase including accrued interest or ARI's approximately `$8.6B`
seller consideration.

## Proof-grade result

`buyer management reporting confirms an ARI-linked AUM outflow classification;
settlement, receipt, borrower cash, and return remain unproven`

The observation narrows the buyer-side reporting bridge and provides another
cross-check against treating AUM movement as cash. The required upgrade remains
the executed settlement statement, buyer funding/receipt ledger, borrower-level
servicing or repayment records, and liability-cost-adjusted return schedule.
