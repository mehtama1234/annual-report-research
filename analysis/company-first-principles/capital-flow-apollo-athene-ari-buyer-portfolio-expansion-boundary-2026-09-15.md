# Apollo–Athene ARI Buyer-Side Portfolio Expansion Boundary

Research date: `2026-09-15`

## Purpose

Add a buyer-side perimeter check to Q-09 without converting a portfolio-level
balance change into borrower repayment or Athene return evidence.

## Observed buyer-side change

Athene's Q2 2026 filing reports commercial mortgage loans at fair value of
`$48.291B` on June 30, 2026, versus `$39.071B` at December 31, 2025. The
mechanical increase is `$9.220B`:

```text
48.291B - 39.071B = 9.220B
```

The same filing separately states that Athene completed the purchase of a
commercial mortgage loan portfolio, including accrued interest, for
approximately `$8.7B` from Apollo Commercial Real Estate Finance, Inc. on
April 24, 2026.

## What this proves

The buyer-side filing independently shows a large commercial-mortgage asset
base after the ARI closing and a year-end-to-Q2 increase whose scale is
directionally consistent with a material portfolio acquisition. It improves
the transaction-perimeter bridge beyond seller-only evidence.

## What it does not prove

The `$9.220B` change is not an ARI attribution: it may include purchases,
disposals, fair-value changes, accrued interest, FX, consolidation, or other
portfolio movements. The filing does not identify the ARI loans inside the
aggregate balance, borrower collections, principal paydowns, collateral
proceeds, or Athene/Apollo allocation. Athene's separate `$1.027B` of
commercial mortgage loans 90 days past due or in non-accrual status is also a
portfolio-level risk figure, not an ARI-specific loss measure.

## Safe claim

`Athene's buyer-side Q2 filing shows a $48.291B commercial-mortgage portfolio,
up $9.220B from year-end, alongside the separately disclosed $8.7B ARI
purchase; the balance change supports a buyer-perimeter boundary but does not
prove ARI attribution, borrower cash, or return.`

Primary source: Athene Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Notes to financial statements and
MD&A mortgage-loan tables.
