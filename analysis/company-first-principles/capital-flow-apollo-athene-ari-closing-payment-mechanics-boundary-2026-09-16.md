# Apollo–Athene ARI closing-payment mechanics boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Purpose

The definitive ARI–Athene Asset Purchase and Sale Agreement identifies the
documents and payment routes that would close the buyer-side cash leg of the
transaction. It strengthens the transaction perimeter beyond the public
approximately `$8.6B–$8.7B` consideration descriptions, but it is not itself a
bank statement or a borrower-collection record.

## Source-backed mechanics

The agreement names Athene Holding Ltd. as Buyer and Apollo Commercial Real
Estate Finance, Inc. as Seller. At closing, Buyer must pay or cause to be paid
by wire transfer of immediately available funds: (a) amounts directed by
Seller to payoff recipients under the release documentation, and (b) the
remaining Closing Consideration Amount to account(s) designated by Seller.

The agreement requires a Closing Date Calculation Notice at least four business
days before closing. That notice includes the portions paid to payoff
recipients and a purchase-price allocation schedule by asset. The closing
documents include one approved settlement statement covering consideration,
closing expenses, credits, adjustments, and prorations.

The payment object is not necessarily final at closing. Buyer must prepare a
final accounting within 120 days, and any resulting true-up is payable by the
owing party within five business days after final determination. The agreement
also permits Athene to designate affiliates, managed accounts, or portfolio
companies as buyers while retaining joint and several liability for Buyer’s
obligations.

After transfer, notices to each borrower are intended to direct debt-service
and other payments to Buyer or its designee, and the applicable servicer or
registrar is to record Buyer as the registered holder. These clauses identify
the borrower-to-buyer collection route, but the public agreement does not name
the designated buyer, account, wire reference, settlement amount, true-up, or
subsequent borrower receipts.

## Proof-grade result

`closing-payment and post-transfer collection mechanics confirmed;
settlement statement, designated buyer, wire confirmation, true-up, borrower
receipts, and buyer return remain unresolved`

## Required next proof objects

- Closing Date Calculation Notice and purchase-price allocation schedule;
- signed settlement statement and payoff-recipient schedule;
- wire confirmations for Seller and payoff recipients;
- final accounting and any true-up notice/payment;
- designated-buyer and servicer/registrar transfer records;
- borrower-level principal, interest, escrow, collateral, and impairment
  collections; and
- Athene liability-cost and Apollo common-owner allocation.
