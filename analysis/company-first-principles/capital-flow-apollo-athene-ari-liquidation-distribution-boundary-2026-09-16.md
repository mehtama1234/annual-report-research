# ARI liquidation-distribution boundary — 2026-09-17

## What the new public source adds

ARI's definitive proxy statement for the dissolution proposal supplies a
seller/common-owner residual estimate after the April 24, 2026 sale of its
commercial-mortgage portfolio to Athene. If stockholders approve the plan and
the liquidation is completed by the first half of 2028, management estimates
aggregate liquidating distributions of `$7.75–$8.50` per common share on a
fully diluted basis.

The estimate excludes the separately declared July 15, 2026 common dividend of
`$3.75` per share. Including that dividend, the proxy estimates total book
value per share returned to common holders of `$11.50–$12.25`. Using the proxy's
`130,764,290` fully diluted share count, these imply mechanical aggregate
ranges of approximately `$1.013B–$1.111B` for the liquidation distributions
and `$1.504B–$1.602B` including the July dividend. These are calculations from
the proxy's per-share range, not observed cash receipts.

## Burden and timing controls

The proxy says the distribution estimate deducts remaining-asset sale fees and
expenses, operating expenses and capital expenditures, general and
administrative expenses including manager fees, wind-up costs, and company
taxes. It also identifies remaining real-estate-owned assets, interest income,
market conditions, liquidation timing, reserves for contingent liabilities,
and potential litigation as drivers of the final amount.

The proxy's estimate was prepared as of July 9, 2026, is unaudited, assumes
completion by the first half of 2028, and may not be realized. The `$3.75`
dividend was declared as predominately a return of capital; the distribution
range is not recurring operating cash flow.

table](data/capital-flow-apollo-athene-ari-liquidation-distribution-boundary-2026-09-16.csv)
and checked by the combined-pilot verifier.

## September 16 status check

The latest ARI Q2 2026 Form 10-Q was checked for a subsequent vote result,
liquidating-distribution notice, or evidence that liquidation-basis accounting
had begun. The filing still describes the dissolution proposal as subject to
stockholder approval and states that liquidation-basis accounting would apply
if the plan is approved. No approved-plan notice or actual liquidating cash
distribution was found in the checked ARI Q2 filing and definitive proxy
routes. This is a `searched-negative` result for those public routes, not
evidence that no later private or subsequent filing exists.

The definitive proxy schedules the virtual special meeting for September 29,
2026. Therefore, as of the research date of September 16, the absence of a
vote result is consistent with the scheduled process and is not a failed
transaction conclusion. The next promotion-ready source is the meeting-result
8-K or a later liquidation filing showing approval, initial cash distribution,
or a revised estimate.

## Legal-entity and return boundary

This is evidence of ARI's expected common-stockholder residual after the seller
asset sale and planned liquidation. It does not prove:

- Athene's payment wire or borrower-level repayment;
- collateral cash, loan-level collections, or the `$8.7B` buyer portfolio
  return;
- any distribution from Athene to Apollo Global Management;
- Apollo parent unrestricted cash or AGM common-owner residual; or
- the final amount or date actually paid to ARI stockholders.

Result class: `seller-common-residual-estimate-confirmed; buyer-repayment-and-parent-receipt-open`.

Primary sources: [ARI definitive dissolution proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1467760/000119312526363807/d119099ddefm14a.htm) and [ARI Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1467760/000119312526342628/ari-20260630.htm).
