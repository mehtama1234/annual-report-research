# Apollo–Athene ARI transaction-perimeter reconciliation — 2026-09-15

## Result

The public documents describe the same completed related-party portfolio sale
using four different perimeters:

| Source / description | Amount | Safe use |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| ARI proxy: approximately `$9B` portfolio; consideration based on 99.7% of each loan’s total commitment, subject to adjustments | `$9.0B` rounded | Transaction perimeter and purchase formula |
| ARI Q2 2026 10-Q: portfolio sale closed in April | `$8.6B` approximately | Subsequent seller-side accounting amount |
| Athene Q2 2026 10-Q: purchased portfolio including accrued interest | `$8.7B` approximately | Buyer-side acquired-asset perimeter |
| Athene Q2 2026 earnings release: ARI-linked AUM outflow after the sale | `$7.8B` | Buyer management-reporting classification; not purchase consideration or cash |

The `$100M` difference between the `$8.6B` seller-side amount and `$8.7B`
buyer-side amount is approximately `1.15%` of the buyer-side amount. The
`$9.0B` proxy perimeter is approximately `$300M` or `3.45%` above the buyer
amount. These are mechanical comparisons, not evidence that the difference is
profit, a fee, or a cash transfer.

## Interpretation

The transaction is clearly named and completed, but the public record does not
provide a loan-level settlement schedule that reconciles commitment value,
accrued interest, purchase-price adjustments, unfunded advances, reserves,
expenses, and final cash payment. The correct claim remains:

`completed related-party sale with seller-use and buyer-perimeter evidence`

not:

`$8.7B of borrower repayment or Apollo cash received`.

The AUM observation improves the buyer-side cross-check but is intentionally not
combined arithmetically with the consideration figures: Athene defines AUM
outflows as redemptions and other decreases in available capital. This
reconciliation therefore defines the exact accounting perimeter that a future
trustee, loan tape, or settlement statement must reconcile.

## Required next evidence

1. Loan-level purchase-price and accrued-interest schedule.
2. Final settlement statement and cash payment ledger.
3. Borrower repayment, collateral, and reserve/deferred-advance schedule.
4. Athene legal-entity booking and subsequent interest/credit-collection
   records.
