# Apollo–Athene ARI post-close proof search boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Purpose and perimeter

This search tests whether the public post-close record exposes the proof
objects identified in the ARI–Athene purchase agreement. The checked perimeter
is the ARI April 24 completion announcement, ARI Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Apollo Q2
2026 Form 10-Q, Athene Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, the definitive agreement, and the
proxy/transaction materials available through the SEC routes below.

The result is a public-disclosure boundary. It does not search private closing
files, bank records, servicer systems, borrower notices delivered outside an
SEC filing, or non-public accounting workpapers.

## Search result

The agreement requires or contemplates a Closing Date Calculation Notice,
purchase-price allocation schedule, seller-directed payoff wires, a signed
settlement statement, a final accounting and possible true-up, buyer-affiliate
designation, and borrower notices directing post-transfer debt service to Buyer
or its designee. The searched post-close filings confirm completion and
aggregate seller cash uses, but do not disclose those transaction-specific
objects or a loan-level borrower receipt ledger.

The classification is therefore:

`searched-negative for the named public post-close proof objects in the checked
SEC perimeter; not evidence that the objects do not exist`

The seller-side cash-flow reconciliation remains valid as an entity-level
movement. It cannot be promoted to an Athene payment receipt, borrower
collection, liability-cost-adjusted return, or Apollo common-owner cash.

## Upgrade request

The next request should target the executed settlement statement and final
accounting first. If the transaction used designated buyer affiliates or
servicers, the request must also obtain the designation, transfer register,
borrower notices, and post-close collections by loan or asset. The requested
cash ledger must distinguish purchase consideration, payoff-recipient wires,
true-up, borrower principal, borrower interest, escrow, collateral proceeds,
fees, CECL, liability cost, and any transfer to AGM.
