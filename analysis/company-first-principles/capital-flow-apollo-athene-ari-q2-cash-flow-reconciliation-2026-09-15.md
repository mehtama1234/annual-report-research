# Apollo–Athene ARI Q2 cash-flow reconciliation

Research date: `2026-09-15`

## Purpose

The ARI Q2 2026 Form 10-Q permits a complete arithmetic reconciliation of the
seller's reported cash movement for the six months ended June 30, 2026. This
is a seller-entity cash-flow proof object, not a buyer-side borrower-return
proof.

## Reconciliation ($ millions)

```text
beginning cash                                           139.825
+ net cash provided by operating activities               46.483
+ net cash provided by investing activities           8,899.484
+ net cash used in financing activities              (7,848.008)
+ foreign-currency effect                                  1.696
= ending cash                                           1,239.480
```

Arithmetic check:

```text
139.825 + 46.483 + 8,899.484 - 7,848.008 + 1.696
= 1,239.480
```

The investing subtotal includes `$9.497267B` of commercial-mortgage
repayment/sale proceeds, `$67.578M` of subordinate/other lending-asset
repayments, loan funding, fees, derivative collateral, and real-estate capital
expenditure. The financing subtotal includes `$6.929540B` of secured-debt
repayments, `$746.250M` of term-loan principal repayment, `$500M` of senior
notes repayment, real-estate mortgage repayment, equity repurchases, and
common and preferred dividends.

## Perimeter discipline

ARI separately describes the April 24 sale to Athene as approximately `$8.6B`
of cash consideration. The `$9.497267B` cash-flow line is broader: it is
labelled repayment and sale proceeds and can include loans repaid before
closing, the post-close Chicago Hotel Loan, and transaction-perimeter
adjustments. The filing therefore supports a reconciled seller cash movement,
but not a one-to-one mapping from the Athene purchase price to the cash-flow
line.

## What this proves

The seller-side cash movement is internally reconciled, and the principal
debt-use categories, distributions, and ending cash balance are visible in the
same filing.

## What this does not prove

It does not prove Athene's payment account, loan-level borrower collections,
collateral proceeds, buyer-side realized return, an Athene-to-AGM receipt, or
Apollo common-owner cash. It also does not permit the seller's aggregate cash
flow to be substituted for the transferred portfolio's post-close cash.

## Proof-grade result

`seller-entity cash-flow reconciliation confirmed; buyer-side borrower cash
and upstream common-owner cash remain unresolved`
