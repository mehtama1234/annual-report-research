# Apollo–Athene ARI Q2 seller-cash and debt-waterfall upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

## Purpose

The ARI Q2 2026 Form 10-Q supplies a more precise seller-side cash bridge
after the April 24 asset sale. It improves the capital-flow chain from the
portfolio sale to ARI cash and debt repayment, but it does not identify the
Athene payment account, borrower collections, or an Apollo parent receipt.

## Newly explicit primary evidence

ARI's six-month 2026 statement of cash flows reports:

- `$9.497267B` of proceeds received from repayment and sale of commercial
  mortgage loans;
- `$67.578M` of proceeds from repayment of subordinate loans and other lending
  assets;
- `$746.250M` of senior secured term-loan principal repayments;
- `$500.000M` of senior secured-notes repayments;
- `$30.7M` of loss on extinguishment of debt tied to the sale-related debt
  repayment; and
- `$1.239480B` of cash and cash equivalents at June 30, 2026, versus
  `$139.825M` at December 31, 2025.

ARI also states that the commercial real-estate loan portfolio was sold to
Athene for approximately `$8.6B` of cash consideration, while the statement
of cash flows presents the broader repayment-and-sale proceeds line. The two
figures must not be silently treated as the same perimeter: the cash-flow line
can include loan repayments before or after closing, subordinate assets, and
other transaction adjustments.

## What this proves

The seller-side route is now source-backed through a cash-flow and debt-use
boundary: ARI received a disclosed aggregate stream of loan repayment/sale
proceeds, repaid identified senior claims, and ended the period with a visible
cash balance after the transaction.

## What this does not prove

It does not prove the Athene payment ledger, the allocation between portfolio
purchase consideration and borrower-level repayments, the post-close cash
collections of the transferred loans, collateral proceeds, Athene's realized
return, or any Apollo Global Management HoldCo/common-owner residual. The
`$9.497267B` line is a seller-side aggregate and cannot be used as a borrower
cash proxy.

## Proof-grade result

`seller-side cash proceeds and identified debt use confirmed; buyer-side
borrower cash, Athene return, and Apollo common-owner cash remain open`
