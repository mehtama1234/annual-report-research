# Apollo–Athene ATLAS Funding 1 public-source refresh

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Question

Can public sources connect Athene's two ATLAS Funding 1 statutory redemption
candidates to Apollo's ATLAS structured-credit platform and establish the
underlying collateral, settlement, and return path?

## Result

`evidence-insufficient` for exact Athene settlement, collateral cash, liability-
adjusted return, or Apollo common-owner cash, with a meaningful Apollo
platform-perimeter upgrade.

The local Athene Schedule D parser identifies two disposal-only `Redemption
100` candidates:

- `04941*-AB-0`, ATLAS Funding 1 A2A 5.688% due 05/25/63: approximately
  `$243.635M` consideration and `$11.727M` of interest/dividend fields.
- `04941*-AD-6`, ATLAS Funding 1 A2B 5.688% due 05/25/63: approximately
  `$243.635M` consideration and `$5.810M` of interest/dividend fields.

The two rows total approximately `$487.270M` of consideration. They have no
year-end holding match in the current parser. The same issuer family also has
year-end and disposal rows for the A1A and A1B classes, creating a broader
instrument-family route, but not lot-level continuity.

Apollo's public history and investor-relations disclosures state that funds
managed by Apollo acquired the Credit Suisse Securitized Products Group and
launched ATLAS SP Partners, a standalone credit firm focused on asset-backed
financing and capital-markets solutions. Apollo further describes ATLAS as a
warehouse-finance and securitized-products business majority owned by Apollo
funds. This is strong platform evidence. It does not, by itself, prove that
the exact ATLAS Funding 1 statutory issuer was owned by a particular Athene
legal entity, that the redemption consideration was remitted to Athene, or
that the underlying collateral generated the reported interest fields.

## Decision

Promote ATLAS Funding 1 to a named Apollo-backed structured-credit platform
target. Do not promote the approximately `$487.3M` statutory consideration to
settled Athene cash, borrower repayment, collateral proceeds, liability-
adjusted spread, realized IRR/NPV, or Apollo common-owner cash.

## Next documents

Request the ATLAS Funding 1 offering memorandum, collateral and asset schedule,
trustee distribution reports, redemption notice, servicing/warehouse waterfall,
Athene trade confirmations, custodian settlement ledger, and any borrower or
asset-level payment history. Reconcile class-level principal, coupon, fees,
losses, funding cost, insurance-liability cost, taxes, and the common-owner
residual.

## Safe claim

`Apollo public disclosures establish an Apollo-backed ATLAS structured-credit
and securitization platform. Athene's two ATLAS Funding 1 statutory redemption
rows total approximately $487.3M of consideration, but remain unproven as
settled Athene receipts, collateral cash, liability-adjusted returns, or Apollo
common-owner cash.`
