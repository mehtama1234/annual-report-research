# Apollo–Athene Concord 2022-1 public-source refresh

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Question

Can public sources identify the `20633K-AA-6` disposal-only statutory
candidate as a real Concord 2022-1A instrument and establish collateral or
use-of-proceeds context?

## Result

`evidence-insufficient` for Athene settlement, receipt, or realized return.

Concord's official December 8, 2022 announcement identifies the transaction as
an approximately `$1.8B` asset-backed securitization backed by more than one
million copyrights. It says the transaction was intended to finance Concord's
acquisition of music rights. Independent statutory investment statements
identify `20633K-AA-6` as `CONCORD MUSIC ROYALTIES LLC TUNES 2022-1A` and show
the instrument in insurer portfolios. These sources jointly support the
issuer/instrument/collateral/use-of-proceeds map behind the Athene statutory
candidate.

The public sources do not identify Athene as the holder of this exact
disposal-only row, provide the `$624.9M` Athene disposition settlement, show a
custodian receipt, identify a redemption or secondary-market counterparty, or
join royalty collections to Athene liability cost and return.

## Decision

The route advances from an unnamed disposal-only CUSIP to a public issuer and
collateral/use-of-proceeds context. It remains `evidence-insufficient` for
cash-loop and return promotion. The official Concord source is transaction
context; the independent statutory statements are identifier cross-checks,
not Athene receipt evidence.

## Next documents

Request the 2022-1A offering memorandum, trustee/remittance reports, note
redemption or transfer notice, Athene trade/allocation support, and the exact
settlement ledger for `20633K-AA-6`.

## Safe claim

`Public sources identify 20633K-AA-6 as a Concord Music Royalties TUNES
2022-1A instrument backed by a large music-rights securitization whose stated
use included music-rights acquisitions. They do not prove Athene ownership,
settlement cash, royalty remittance, or asset-level return.`
