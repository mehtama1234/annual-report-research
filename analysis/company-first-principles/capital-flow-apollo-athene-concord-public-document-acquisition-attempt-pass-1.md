## September 16, 2026 public recheck

The targeted public recheck independently reconfirmed the boundary for the
Series 2025-3 route. Concord's official closing release describes the `$1.765B`
music-rights ABS, more than `1.3M` copyrights, Apollo Global Securities and
ATLAS SP Securities as joint bookrunners, Redding Ridge as structuring agent,
and Bank of New York Mellon as trustee. The KBRA Series 2025-3 new-issue page
is publicly locatable but remains premium-gated. These observations strengthen
the borrower, collateral, platform, and trustee map; they do not provide the
offering memorandum, trustee remittance, Athene allocation, settlement account,
or liability-cost return evidence.

Sources: [Concord closing release](https://concord.com/news/concord-closes-1-765-billion-abs-to-fuel-continued-growth/)
and [KBRA Series 2025-3 new-issue report page](https://www.kbra.com/publications/ZTyQYFzg/concord-music-royalties-llc-series-2025-3-new-issue-report?format=web).
The KBRA route redirects to this canonical page and displays the premium-
subscription requirement; no report body or downloadable attachment is
available in the public response.

## What The Search Improved

The public search improved three parts of the Concord route:

1. The offering circular / indenture route is not hypothetical. A DealX document index appears to list an offering circular, indenture exhibits, and an indenture supplement for Concord Music Royalties.
2. KBRA public releases and transaction pages identify rating-report and surveillance-report routes, but the exact Series 2025-3 new-issue report and full surveillance reports require premium access or login. The accessible July 2026 page confirms the payment-date scope and timely-interest proxy; the underlying transaction pages do not expose the report tables in the public response.
3. Public Concord, KBRA, Apollo, and Asset Securitization Report sources further support the borrower/wrapper/use/collateral route.

## What Still Blocks Full Proof

The hard documents remain missing:

1. actual offering circular content
2. actual indenture/supplement waterfall terms
3. trustee remittance reports
4. Series 2022-1 payoff ledger
5. collateral catalog or royalty tape
6. Athene trade/allocation support
7. Athene receipt/custodian cash movement
8. liability-cost and spread support

## What This Tells Us In Simple Terms

The Concord case did not collapse. It got sharper.

We found enough to say the important documents likely exist in controlled or premium document channels. But we did not get the documents needed to prove the cash all the way through.

So the right status is:

`document routes located; full cash proof still access-controlled`

## What This Proves

This pass proves:

1. all `8` Concord cash-loop document families were tested through public routes
2. offering/indenture/rating-report routes are at least partly located
3. use-of-proceeds and collateral proxy evidence improved
4. trustee remittance, Athene allocation, and liability-cost support were not found publicly
5. the next step is access/request execution, not more broad searching

## What It Does Not Prove

This pass does not prove:

1. the offering circular was obtained
2. the indenture waterfall was obtained
3. trustee reports were obtained
4. Athene received cash
5. Concord paid off Series 2022-1 through a verified cash ledger
6. royalty cash collections supported note payments
7. Athene earned a liability-adjusted return
8. IRR, NPV, ROIC, cash-on-cash return, or platform profit

## Safe Claim

`The first Concord public document acquisition attempt tested all eight controlled-document families. Public search located access-controlled routes for the offering circular, indenture/supplement materials, and KBRA full reports, and strengthened public use-of-proceeds and collateral proxy evidence. It did not find trustee remittance reports, Athene allocation/trade support, Athene receipt evidence, or liability-cost/spread support. Concord remains the strongest Apollo/Athene public borrower/wrapper-use route, but full named cash proof remains access-controlled.`

## Decision

`concord-public-document-routes-located-full-proof-access-controlled`

The next move is to pursue access-controlled routes first:

1. DealX offering circular and indenture documents
2. KBRA full new-issue and surveillance reports
3. trustee/remittance reports through investor or trustee channels
4. Athene allocation/trade and liability-cost support through insurance statutory/examiner routes
