# Apollo Athene Concord SEC N-PORT Holder Observation

## Purpose

Add an independent public observation to the Concord route without confusing
instrument existence with Athene ownership, receipt, servicing, or return.

## Observation

The SEC-hosted N-PORT filing for a registered investment company identifies
`CONCORD MUSIC ROYALTIES LLC TUNES 2024 1A A 144A` as an issuer/instrument and
reports CUSIP `20633KAE8` (ISIN `US20633KAE82`). The filing reports `200,000`
units, reported value of `$197,037.56`, and a fund-net-asset percentage of
approximately `0.148%` for the position.

Source: [SEC N-PORT filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1478482/000114554925042590/xslFormNPORT-P_X01/primary_doc.xml).

## Latest Public Cross-Check

A current public search also rechecked the transaction-level route. KBRA's
public Concord publication describes the Series 2025-1, 2025-2, and 2025-3
notes as sharing the collateral pool and describes proceeds as redeeming the
Series 2022-1 notes and supporting general corporate purposes. That is useful
deal-level collateral and use-of-proceeds context, but it does not identify an
Athene holding, a settled lot, a trustee remittance, or an Athene cash receipt.

Source: [KBRA Concord publication](https://www.kbra.com/publications/bcGzRKWf).

A second public portfolio observation identifies a different member of the
same transaction family: a John Hancock N-PORT-P filing reports Concord Music
Royalties LLC TUNES 2025-2A A 144A, CUSIP `20633KAL2`, with `250,000` units and
reported value of approximately `$253.6M` as of `2025-10-31`. An independent
SEC-hosted portfolio schedule corroborates the same 2025-2A issue and reports
`$1.293B` principal and approximately `$1.319B` value for that portfolio
position. This expands the publicly observable Concord Series 2025 instrument
set, but it still does not identify Athene as holder and does not establish the
2025-3A CUSIP, settled lot, trustee remittance, or cash receipt in the Athene
route.

Source: [John Hancock N-PORT-P filing mirror](https://www.otcmarkets.com/filing/html?guid=XZ3-knPGGQmXB3h&id=19021686).
Corroborating source: [SEC-hosted portfolio schedule](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/792858/000119312526016274/8de553c803d6589.htm).

A newer official SEC-hosted John Hancock Bond Fund quarterly portfolio report
adds a dated February 28, 2026 observation for the same 2025-2A issue: par
value `$14.561M` and reported value `$14.999229M`, at a `5.785%` rate and July
20, 2075 maturity. This is a current-period instrument cross-check, not an
Athene holding or a payment record.

Source: [John Hancock Bond Fund quarterly portfolio report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/45288/000119312526184473/edgar.htm).

The targeted public search also surfaced SEC-hosted portfolio schedules for
Concord 2025-1A, including a BlackRock schedule that identifies the issue as
Concord Music Royalties LLC, Series 2025-1A, Class A2. Across the checked SEC
and public portfolio routes, the search therefore finds 2024-1A, 2025-1A, and
2025-2A observations, including a current February 2026 2025-2A observation,
but no 2025-3A observation under the checked CUSIP or issue-name variants.
This is a bounded searched-negative result for public portfolio observability,
not evidence that the security does not exist or that Athene did not hold it.

Source: [SEC-hosted portfolio schedule](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/908406/000119312526077888/acmv_q3holdings704.htm).

## What this improves

- The Concord securitization is independently observable in an official SEC
  portfolio filing, rather than only through issuer, Apollo, or rating-agency
  materials.
- The observation supplies a public issuer/CUSIP/quantity/value reference that
  can be used to cross-check future trustee, custodian, rating, or statutory
  documents.
- It strengthens the instrument-observability and wrapper-existence portion of
  the capital-flow map.

## Boundary

This filing is not evidence that Athene held this position. It is not a trade
confirmation, settled-lot record, custodian statement, trustee remittance,
borrower royalty collection, principal repayment, or parent-company receipt.
The instrument is also the 2024-1A security identified by CUSIP
`20633KAE8`, while the current Athene same-CUSIP statutory bridge for Concord
uses CUSIP `20633K-AN-8` for the 2025-3A security. Those are not interchangeable
without a documented instrument relationship.

The KBRA deal-level description and the independent 2025-2A portfolio
observation do not resolve that identifier gap. They support transaction-level
family observability, not a relationship between the observed 2024-1A or
2025-2A positions and Athene's 2025-3A statutory row.

## Safe claim

`An official SEC N-PORT filing independently identifies a Concord Music
Royalties 2024-1A security and reports a 200,000-unit, $197,037.56 position at
approximately 0.148% of fund net assets. This upgrades public instrument
observability and provides a cross-check reference; it does not establish
Athene ownership, cash receipt, servicing remittance, or investment return.`
