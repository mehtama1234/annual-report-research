# Apollo–Athene Eliant public-search boundary

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Result

`searched-negative-for-public-borrower-settlement-remittance; legal-entity-and-instrument-perimeter-confirmed`

The public search was narrowed to the two exact Athene statutory Eliant routes:

| Athene marker | Statutory identity | Cash-like consideration candidate | Public-search result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `28655*-AA-7` | Eliant Invest Holding LP, Class B, 5.683%, 12/15/2041 | `$358,251,536` | No public offering, borrower, settlement, or remittance record located |
| `28655*-AB-5` | Eliant Invest Holding LP, Class C, 6.133%, 12/15/2041 | `$189,464,130` | No public offering, borrower, settlement, or remittance record located |

## What the public record does establish

- Apollo's SEC subsidiary exhibit lists Eliant Invest GP LP, Eliant Invest
  Holding LP, Eliant Invest Management LP, Apollo Eliant GP Advisors LLC, and
  Apollo Eliant Management GP LLC in the Apollo entity perimeter.
- Athene's Q3 2025 Schedule Y organizational chart independently lists Eliant
  Invest GP LP and Eliant Invest Holding LP.
- The active LEI record identifies Eliant Invest Holding LP as a Delaware
  limited partnership, LEI `549300GBHFWXPLBUNT21`, registration identifier
  `5871670`.
- A FINRA BrokerCheck report refers to Eliant Invest Holding LP and Eliant
  Trade Finance LP as Apollo-affiliated portfolio-company/board-role entities.
  This is corroborative relationship context only; it does not identify a
  borrower, collateral pool, cash event, or Athene receipt.

These sources are sufficient to route the statutory rows to a corroborated
Apollo-related legal-entity perimeter. They do not establish that Eliant is
the underlying borrower, that the two rows represent debt repayment rather
than another disposition or restructuring event, or that either consideration
field was collected as cash.

## Public business-model context

Apollo's January 24, 2022 launch announcement supplies a useful but older
platform-level description: Eliant was structured to own inventory; BNP
Paribas would provide debt and receivables financing plus structuring and
referral services; Athene would serve as the primary capital provider; and
Apollo would act as investment manager. The announcement also described
`$1.3B` of signed or awarded inventory programs at launch.

This establishes the intended control-point architecture and explains why an
inventory-finance asset could appear across Apollo, Athene, Eliant, and BNP
records. It does not map either current Athene statutory consideration row to
a named inventory program, borrower, receivable pool, financing draw, payoff,
or cash remittance.

## Targeted searches and result

The checked public routes included:

1. exact and punctuation-variant searches for `28655*-AA-7`, `28655*-AB-5`,
   and the corresponding `28655` CUSIP fragments;
2. searches for `Eliant Invest Holding LP`, `Eliant Trade Finance LP`, the
   Apollo Eliant management entities, and combinations with notes, bonds,
   borrower, collateral, repayment, redemption, settlement, trustee, and
   remittance;
3. SEC filing and exhibit routes, statutory statements, public entity records,
   and public market-document searches; and
4. public searches for an offering memorandum, note-purchase agreement,
   private-placement memorandum, collateral schedule, trustee notice, trade
   confirmation, custodian statement, or paying-agent record.

The searches located entity and organizational corroboration, but no public
document that closes any of the following joins:

- Eliant legal entity → underlying borrower;
- borrower → named collateral or project;
- statutory event → dated settlement or repayment;
- consideration → trustee, paying-agent, custodian, or Athene receipt;
- receipt → liability-cost allocation; or
- asset-level proceeds → Apollo/common-owner return.

This is a bounded `searched-negative` result for the checked public routes,
not a claim that no such document exists in a restricted data room, private
credit file, custodian system, or non-indexed filing.

## Promotion rule and next source

Do not promote `$358.252M` or `$189.464M` to collected cash, borrower
repayment, or Apollo owner cash. The next useful object is an authorized
holder/custody record, trustee or paying-agent remittance, Eliant transaction
document, borrower payoff/use-of-proceeds schedule, or asset-level return
statement that joins the legal entity, event, amount, date, and recipient.
