## Evidence To Claim

The annual report strengthens the safe claim:

`Apollo/Athene is not just a large retirement-services balance sheet. It is a managed credit-and-spread system: Apollo discloses large direct-origination and asset-backed-finance AUM, while Athene-related accounts represent hundreds of billions of Apollo-managed and fee-generating AUM.`

It also narrows the claim:

`The FY2025 10-K still does not prove exactly which Athene liabilities funded which private-credit loans. It proves scale, credit-lane capacity, invested-asset denominators, rate exposure, deployable capital, and liability stickiness.`

## Claim To Evidence

| Stronger Claim | Missing Proof |
|---|---|
| Athene liabilities directly fund private-credit origination. | Same-period bridge from Athene liabilities to specific credit strategies, allocations, or originated assets. |
| Apollo private credit is replacing bank credit. | Borrower-level credit documents showing bank facility repayment or bank syndicate displacement. |
| Insurance-linked credit risk is safe or unsafe. | Statutory schedules, rating-agency capital adequacy, impairments, below-investment-grade exposure, Level 3 exposure, and affiliated-asset detail. |
| Bermuda reinsurance meaningfully changes risk location. | Bermuda statutory financials, capital ratios, reserve/liability details, and related-party investment schedules. |

## Simple Version

Apollo/Athene now has a stronger evidence chain:

`annuity and retirement liabilities -> Athene-related managed accounts -> Apollo credit strategy scale -> invested assets and spread earnings`

But the final borrower-level link is still missing:

`which exact liabilities funded which exact private-credit loans, and which bank loans were displaced`

That is the next proof layer.
