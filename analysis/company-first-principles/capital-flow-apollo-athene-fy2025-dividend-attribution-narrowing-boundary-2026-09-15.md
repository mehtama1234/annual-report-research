# Apollo–Athene FY2025 dividend-attribution narrowing boundary

Research date: `2026-09-15`

## Purpose

The FY2025 record contains a matching amount that narrows, but does not close,
the Apollo/Athene parent-receipt attribution question.

## Evidence

- Apollo's FY2025 Schedule I parent-company disclosure reports `$750M` of cash
  dividends received from subsidiaries during 2025.
- Athene's official corporate-structure materials state an intended regular
  common dividend of `$750M` per year to its parent, subject to capital needs,
  management review, and board discretion.
- Apollo owns Athene within the consolidated group, so the policy is a
  plausible source route for a subsidiary dividend, but the parent-only
  schedule does not identify the payer.

## What the amount match does and does not do

The matching `$750M` amount narrows the candidate attribution set: Athene's
stated annual policy is consistent with the entire Apollo parent-receipt line.
It does not prove that Athene paid the full `$750M`, because the Apollo line is
explicitly from subsidiaries and could include AAM or other entities. It also
does not supply a transfer date, bank account, intercompany elimination, legal
entity, or cash available to common owners.

| Test | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Apollo parent-only cash receipt | `$750M` observed in FY2025 |
| Athene annual dividend policy | `$750M` intended regular amount |
| Amount consistency | Qualified match |
| Athene payer identity | Not proven |
| Transfer date / receiving account | Not proven |
| Intercompany elimination | Not proven |
| Common-owner residual | Not proven |

Proof grade: `FY2025 attribution narrowed by policy-consistent amount match;
parent-receipt proof remains qualified`.
