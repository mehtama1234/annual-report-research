# Apollo–Athene intercompany note longitudinal refresh

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Question

Does the public filing history establish a recurring AHL-to-AGM financing
channel and improve the Q-07 parent-receipt bridge without mistaking note
balances for dividends or unrestricted common-owner cash?

## Result

`partial-upgrade` for direction, continuity, and balance-history evidence;
`evidence-insufficient` for dated draw cash, use of proceeds, repayment,
intercompany elimination, or common-owner cash.

SEC filings identify AHL as the creditor and AGM as the borrower on an
unsecured revolving promissory note. The public balance history is:

| As-of date | AHL note receivable from AGM |
| --- | ---: |
| December 31, 2022 | `$78M` |
| December 31, 2023 | `$109M` |
| December 31, 2024 | `$142M` |
| September 30, 2025 | `$226M` |
| December 31, 2025 | `$227M` |
| March 31, 2026 | `$280M` |
| June 30, 2026 | `$279M` |

The filings also describe a `$500M` borrowing capacity and maturity terms.
The sequence establishes that the route is recurring and that the legal
direction is AHL receivable / AGM payable. It does not establish the timing
or amount of individual draws, accrued-interest components, repayments, bank
accounts, use of proceeds, or whether any draw funded Apollo dividends,
repurchases, fees, or other parent uses. A period-end increase is therefore a
balance movement, not an observed receipt or transfer amount.

## Parent-side cross-entity corroboration

Apollo's FY2025 parent-only Schedule I independently reports that AGM had no
note receivable from AHL at December 31, 2025 and had a `$227M` unsecured
revolving note payable to AHL. This matches Athene's `$227M` AHL note
receivable at the same date and corroborates the legal direction and amount
across both sides of the relationship. It is still a period-end liability and
receivable presentation, not a dated bank transfer, draw notice, use-of-
proceeds record, or proof that any Athene distribution reached AGM common-owner
cash.

## Q2 2026 parent-side search boundary

Apollo's Q2 2026 Form 10-Q was checked for a June 30 parent-only AGM note
payable to AHL. The filing presents Apollo consolidated and consolidated-fund
financial-condition tables with eliminations, but does not provide a parent-
only Schedule I or a separately identified AGM-to-AHL note-payable balance at
June 30, 2026. The Q2 financial supplement likewise does not expose that
entity-level note line. Therefore the June 30 `$279M` amount remains
Athene-side balance evidence only; it is not upgraded to a two-sided Q2
cross-entity control. This is a source-availability boundary, not evidence
that no AGM payable existed.

## Decision

Upgrade Q-07's direct Athene-to-AGM financing route from a two-date balance
observation to a longitudinal public balance-history control. Keep the
Athene-dividend-to-AGM-receipt and common-owner residual gates open.

## Next documents

Request the note agreement and detailed ledger, dated draw/repayment notices,
bank confirmations, accrued-interest schedule, intercompany eliminations,
AGM use-of-proceeds records, and any board or regulatory distribution support.
Reconcile note principal and interest separately from Athene dividends and
Apollo parent cash uses.

## Safe claim

`Public SEC filings show a recurring unsecured AHL receivable from AGM whose
reported balance rose from $78M at December 31, 2022 to $279M at June 30,
2026, within a disclosed $500M facility. This proves a direct financing route
and direction, not dated cash draws, dividend funding, parent receipt,
unrestricted HoldCo cash, or common-owner residual.`
