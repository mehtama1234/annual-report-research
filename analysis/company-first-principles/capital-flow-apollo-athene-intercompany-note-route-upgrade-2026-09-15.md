# Apollo–Athene intercompany note route upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

Athene's Q2 2026 Form 10-Q identifies a direct financing channel between AHL
and AGM:

- AHL holds an unsecured revolving promissory note receivable from AGM with
  `$500M` borrowing capacity.
- The note accrues interest at the U.S. mid-term applicable federal rate and
  matures December 13, 2028, or earlier at AHL's request.
- The note receivable balance was `$279M` at June 30, 2026, versus `$227M` at
  December 31, 2025.
- The reciprocal unsecured revolving promissory note payable from AHL to AGM
  had zero outstanding at both dates.

## Proof-grade consequence

This proves a direct Athene-to-AGM financing route and establishes direction:
AHL is the creditor and AGM is the borrower on the disclosed note. The
`$52M` period-end balance difference is recorded only as a balance change; it
is not promoted to a draw amount because accrued interest, repayments, and
intra-period activity are not separately disclosed.

The note is not treated as the `$110M` H1 Athene distribution or as common-owner
cash. The filing still does not disclose the draw dates, bank receiving account,
use of proceeds, repayment, intercompany elimination, or whether any note
proceeds funded AGM dividends or repurchases.

Status:

`Direct Athene-to-AGM financing route confirmed; dividend receipt and common-owner residual unresolved`
