# Apollo–Athene investment-spread screen upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

## H1 2026 reported spread screen

Athene's H1 2026 management reporting shows `7.8B USD` of net investment
earnings and `5.7B USD` of cost of funds. The simple difference is:

```text
7.8B USD - 5.7B USD = 2.1B USD
```

That is a bounded pre-tax investment-spread contribution screen before other
expenses, hedges, credit losses, capital charges, taxes, noncontrolling
interests, preferred claims, and Apollo parent allocation. The reported net
investment spread was `1.41%`, down from `1.62%` in the prior-year period, a
`21` basis-point decline and approximately `13.0%` relative compression.

## Why the screen is not a full return

The earnings and cost-of-funds figures are management segment measures for H1
2026; the statutory legal-entity bridge uses a different entity and period.
They must not be mechanically combined into one statutory ROIC or asset-level
IRR. The safe conclusion is narrower: Athene's insurer-level spread engine is
visible in dollars and percentage terms, and it is already showing compression
before named-asset allocation and through-cycle testing.
