## Better Claim

Old claim:

`Insurance and retirement liabilities are being converted into private-credit origination power.`

Better current claim:

`Apollo/Athene has a large retirement-liability inflow channel and a large invested-asset base that produces spread earnings and includes growing alternative-investment exposure. The filing supports the funding-channel mechanism, but exact private-credit origination linkage still needs asset-class and borrower-level composition.`

## Simple Version

Apollo/Athene is not just managing money.

It is collecting retirement and insurance-like money, turning it into a large invested-asset pool, and earning spread income on that pool.

That supports the capital-flow idea. The missing piece is tracing exactly how much of that pool becomes private credit, asset-backed finance, investment-grade origination, or other assets.
