# Apollo–Athene PK AirFinance public-source refresh

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Question

Can public sources connect the Athene PK AirFinance statutory disposal
candidates to an Apollo/Athene aviation-lending platform, identifiable
collateral, and a return-relevant operating perimeter?

## Result

`evidence-insufficient` for exact CUSIP settlement, borrower cash, or realized
return, with a meaningful platform/context upgrade.

Apollo's 2019 acquisition announcement states that Apollo acquired the
PK AirFinance platform while Athene acquired PK AirFinance's existing loan
portfolio. PK's current official website describes the platform as a lender
against commercial aircraft and engines, managed by Apollo affiliates, with a
managed portfolio of more than `$5.5B` across `380+` assets and `95+` airlines
in `45+` countries. It also says PK has issued approximately `$1.25B` of
investment-grade ABS through the PK AIR loan program since 2024.

Athene's statutory disposal candidates include `69346Y-AP-8` (approximately
`$497.703M`), `69346Y-AQ-6` (approximately `$434.184M`), and `69346Y-AU-7`
(approximately `$255.960M`), all described as PK AirFinance PAF 2020 notes.
The public sources establish a strong platform/asset-class route, but do not
identify which borrower or aircraft collateral underlies a specific Athene
disposal, whether the row represents a full redemption, secondary sale, or
transfer, or where settlement cash was received.

## Decision

Promote PK AirFinance from a generic named issuer to an Apollo/Athene platform
and aircraft-collateral proof target. Do not promote the statutory amounts to
borrower repayment, Athene receipt, liability-adjusted return, or Apollo
common-owner cash.

## Next documents

Request the applicable PK AIR offering memorandum, loan tape or collateral
schedule, trustee/remittance report, paydown/redemption notice, Athene trade
confirmation, and borrower/aircraft-level payment history for each CUSIP.

## Safe claim

`Public sources directly connect Apollo and Athene to the PK AirFinance
platform and identify aircraft/engine-secured lending and ABS activity. The
Athene statutory PK AirFinance rows remain exact-CUSIP cash candidates, not
proven borrower receipts or realized returns.`
