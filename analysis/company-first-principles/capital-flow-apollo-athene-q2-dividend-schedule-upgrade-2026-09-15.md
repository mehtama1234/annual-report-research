# Apollo–Athene Q2 dividend schedule upgrade — 2026-09-15

Athene's Q2 2026 Form 10-Q states that it intends to pay regular common-stock
dividends to its parent company of `$750M` per year, generally at quarter-end,
but that declaration and payment remain at the board's sole discretion. It
also gives a dated Q2 schedule: a `$187M` common-stock cash dividend was
declared on April 20, 2026, with a June 12 record date and June 15 payment
date. Athene reports `$375M` of common-stock cash dividends paid for the six
months ended June 30, 2026.

The holding-company liquidity discussion preserves the legal boundary:
insurer dividends are constrained by applicable law, statutory ordinary-
dividend calculations, extraordinary-dividend approval requirements, and
solvency/capital-and-surplus tests.

Source: [Athene Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1527469/000152746926000056/ahl-20260630.htm).
