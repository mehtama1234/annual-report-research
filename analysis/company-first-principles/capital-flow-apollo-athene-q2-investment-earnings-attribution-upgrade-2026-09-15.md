# Apollo–Athene Q2 investment-earnings attribution upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

Athene's Q2 2026 financial supplement provides a more useful return bridge
than a single net-investment-earnings number. For the first half of 2026 it
reports fixed-income and other net investment income of `$7.237B`, alternative
net investment income of `$558M`, strategic capital-management fees of `$73M`,
and cost of funds of `$5.749B`. These reconcile to net investment spread of
`$2.119B` before other operating expenses and interest/financing costs.

For Q2 alone, the corresponding amounts are `$3.686B`, `$348M`, `$37M`, and
`$2.942B`, reconciling to `$1.129B` of net investment spread. The reported
retirement-services net spread was `1.06%` for H1 2026 versus `1.24%` in H1
2025, while net investment spread was `1.41%` versus `1.62%`.

## Alternative-return sensitivity boundary

Athene reports alternative net investment income of `$558M` for H1 2026 and a
`$264M` delta to its long-term expected alternative return. The supplement
states that the alternative portfolio earned a `7.49%` H1 return versus an
`11%` long-term expected average return, and that the difference reduced net
spread by `0.17%`. This is a management sensitivity, not a guaranteed
normalization or an asset-level realized-return adjustment.

## What this adds

- The spread numerator is decomposed into fixed-income/other, alternatives,
  and strategic-capital-management components.
- The liability/funding burden is separately visible as cost of funds rather
  than being hidden inside a net spread.
- The bridge identifies both dollar and rate compression and separates the
  alternative-return sensitivity from the reported spread.

## What it does not add

These figures remain Retirement Services segment or management-reporting
aggregates. They do not allocate income, funding cost, credit losses, capital
charges, or cash to AMAPS, Concord, ARI, or any other named asset. They also do
not prove Athene-to-AGM receipt or Apollo common-owner cash.

## Proof-grade result

`segment-investment-earnings-attribution-confirmed`: Athene's current filing
provides a decomposed spread bridge and an explicitly labeled alternative-return
sensitivity. Asset-level liability-cost allocation, realized borrower cash,
and parent/common-owner attribution remain open.
