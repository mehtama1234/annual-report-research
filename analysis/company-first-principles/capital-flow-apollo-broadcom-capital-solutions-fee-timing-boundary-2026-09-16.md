# Apollo–Broadcom capital-solutions fee-timing boundary — 2026-09-16

Research date: `2026-09-16`

Apollo's Q2 2026 corrected earnings transcript adds timing detail to the
Broadcom capital-solutions route:

| Layer | Transcript observation | Safe use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Origination size | Management described Broadcom as a `$35B` financing supporting its AI XPV platform | Names the transaction scale and origination event | The headline amount is not current Apollo cash, AUM, or fee revenue |
| Draw timing | Apollo expects the `$35B` to be drawn over a multi-quarter timeframe | Establishes a staged funding and fee-recognition model | Actual draw dates, lender cash, and undrawn commitments are not disclosed here |
| Fee recognition | Associated capital-solutions fee revenue is recognized as the `$35B` is drawn, with weighting toward Q4 2026 and the first three quarters of 2027 | Connects origination to future fee timing rather than treating the transaction as one-period revenue | Management expectation is not realized fee collection or parent cash |
| Platform mechanism | Management describes the route as origination, structuring, principal investment, and syndication | Supports the control-point and intermediation model | The transcript does not allocate fees among Apollo entities, funds, Athene, third-party syndicates, or common owners |

The correct model is therefore:

```text
Broadcom financing commitment
  -> staged drawdown
  -> Apollo / fund structuring and syndication
  -> fee recognition as drawn
  -> fee collection and entity-level cash
  -> HoldCo claims and common-owner residual
```

This improves the timing and denominator discipline around Apollo's capital-
solutions earnings. It does not prove the draw schedule, fee percentage,
capital provider cash, WHCO/Athene allocation, borrower use, credit outcome,
or unrestricted Apollo common-owner cash.

Status: `management fee-timing mechanism confirmed; realized fee cash and return unresolved`
