# Apollo HoldCo unrestricted-cash boundary upgrade — 2026-09-15

Apollo's Q2 2026 Form 10-Q states that AGM is a holding company whose primary
cash-flow source is distributions and other intercompany transfers from AAM
and AHL. It reports `$25.4B` of unrestricted cash and cash equivalents at the
Company level as of June 30, 2026, plus `$5.6B` of available funds from the AGM,
Athene credit, and Athene liquidity facilities.

The same liquidity discussion says AGM dividends depend on subsidiary
distributions/intercompany transfers and on applicable surplus, minimum
solvency, preferred-distribution, and subsidiary-level legal constraints. It
also states that AGM's dividend declaration and payment are discretionary.

Source: [Apollo Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1858681/000185868126000040/apo-20260630.htm).

## Interpretation boundary

The `$25.4B` figure is an important consolidated unrestricted-cash disclosure,
but it is not a dated Athene-to-AGM receipt and is not a parent-only bank
balance. It cannot be allocated among AGM, AAM, AHL, regulated entities, VIEs,
NCI, preferred claims, debt, taxes, or common owners without a cash-location
and intercompany schedule.
