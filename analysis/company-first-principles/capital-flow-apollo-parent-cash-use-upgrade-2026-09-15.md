# Apollo parent cash-use upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

The Apollo Q2 2026 filing's SEC XBRL facts expose cash uses at the parent
consolidated level. They improve the common-owner residual bridge, but they do
not prove that Athene fees or regulated cash funded those uses.

## H1 2026 cash outflows

| Cash-use line | Amount | Safe interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Payments of common-stock dividends | `$654M` | Cash paid to Apollo common shareholders during H1; not a cash-generation measure |
| Payments for repurchase of common stock | `$729M` | Cash used for common-stock repurchases during H1; do not confuse with the Q2-only `$102M` disclosed capital-return screen |
| Payments of preferred-stock dividends | `$49M` | Senior/preferred claim paid by the parent consolidated group before common-owner residual |

The cash-use lines are consolidated parent claims. The filing does not join
them to fee-entity collections, Athene upstream dividends, regulated-capital
capacity, or unrestricted HoldCo cash. They therefore belong below the
cash-generation bridge as claims against common-owner cash, not above it as
proof of cash availability.
