# Apollo Q2 fund distributions to Company boundary

Research date: `2026-09-15`

## Question

Does Apollo's Q2 2026 Form 10-Q provide a company-level distribution
observation that can be separated from the unresolved Athene-to-AGM receipt
question?

## Evidence found

Apollo's performance-allocation rollforward reports the following H1 2026
movement (in millions):

| Rollforward item | Amount | Source location | Safe interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Total performance allocations, January 1, 2026 | `$3,554M` | Q2 2026 10-Q, Note 5, p. 26 | Opening performance-allocation balance |
| Change in fair value of funds | `$475M` | Q2 2026 10-Q, Note 5, p. 26 | Non-cash/fair-value movement as presented in the rollforward |
| Fund distributions to the Company | `($799M)` | Q2 2026 10-Q, Note 5, p. 26 | A named company-level distribution observation |
| Total performance allocations, June 30, 2026 | `$3,230M` | Q2 2026 10-Q, Note 5, p. 26 | Closing balance; arithmetic reconciles: 3,554 + 475 - 799 = 3,230 |

The same consolidated cash-flow statement reports `$2,040M` of sales,
maturities, and repayments of investment funds and distributions from equity
method investments during H1 2026. That broader line cannot be substituted for
the `$799M` performance-allocation rollforward item or treated as an AGM-only
receipt.

## Boundary

The `$799M` line is stronger than a generic AUM or earnings observation because
it names distributions to the Company in a balance rollforward. It still does
not prove:

- that the distribution was cash received in an AGM unrestricted account;
- the payment date, payer legal entity, or receiving legal entity;
- that any portion came from Athene rather than Apollo-managed funds or other
  investment vehicles;
- the relationship between the `$799M` line and the `$2,040M` consolidated
  investing cash-flow line;
- intercompany elimination, tax, debt, preferred, NCI, or regulatory-capital
  treatment; or
- cash available to Apollo common owners.

This is therefore a qualified company-level distribution route and a new input
to Q-07. It narrows the search for a parent receipt without replacing the
Athene-specific `$110M` distribution frontier.

## Proof-grade result

`Apollo Q2 reports $799M of fund distributions to the Company in its
performance-allocation rollforward, with a separate $2,040M consolidated
investment-fund/equity-method cash-flow line. The company-level distribution
observation is proven; payer, receipt account, Athene attribution, and
common-owner availability remain unresolved.`
