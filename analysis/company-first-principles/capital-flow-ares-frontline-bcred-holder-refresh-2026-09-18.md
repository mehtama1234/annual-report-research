# Capital Flow Ares Frontline BCRED Holder Refresh

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This is a targeted holder-refresh pass for the Ares/Frontline route. It adds
the June 30, 2026 Blackstone Private Credit Fund filing to the visible lender
map while preserving the existing controlled denominator and its overlap
controls.

## New primary-source observation

Blackstone Private Credit Fund's Q2 2026 Schedule of Investments reports five
`Frontline Road Safety, LLC` first-lien rows with a combined:

- principal of `$29.112M`;
- amortized cost of `$28.983M`; and
- fair value of `$28.524M`.

The rows show `SOFR + 4.75%`, an effective yield of `8.39%` including `2.00%`
PIK, acquisition dates from March through December 2025, and a maturity date
of March 4, 2032.

## Evidence boundary

This strengthens same-period public syndication visibility. It does not prove
that BCRED participated in the Ares-arranged facility, that these rows are
economically distinct from the already captured BXSL/FSK/K-FITS/GSBD rows, or
that BCRED's fair value equals funded cash received by Frontline.

The Ares Strategic Income Fund Q2 2026 supplement separately reports
`$94.924M` fair value and `$96.7893M` principal for a current Frontline
first-lien position; the related revolver is undrawn. This is direct
Ares-managed exposure, but not proof of allocation to the Ares-arranged Bain
facility.

Accordingly, the existing controlled Q2 denominator remains:

`FSK $132.8M + K-FITS $47.936M + GSBD $17.939M = $198.675M fair value`

ARCC's `$23.9M`, ASIF's `$94.924M`, BXSL's `$32.292M`, OHA's `$3.750M` unfunded commitment,
BCRED's `$28.524M`, and Goldman Sachs Private Credit Corp.'s `$38.594M` are
additional breadth observations held outside that denominator until
borrower-name, tranche, facility, and overlap controls are reconciled.

The earlier mechanical snapshot in this pass was `$416.909M` before the later
KKR Enhanced, KKR FS Income Trust, Cliffwater, Sixth Street, West Bay, and OHA
observations were added. The current generated same-period breadth view is
`$622.8244M` of reported fair value plus `$130.796M` of reported commitments.
That current figure remains a visibility measure, not a facility total, because
the tranche and legal-entity overlap map is unresolved.
