## Why This Matters

Ares/Frontline is the right private-credit test case because it gets past abstract AUM and industry allocation. It names the borrower, the sponsor, the financing role, the instrument type, and visible holder dollars. That is much better than platform-level private-credit evidence.

It still does not answer the named cash question end to end. To answer that question, the file needs the legal and cash trail: total facility, lender schedule, funded allocations, use-of-proceeds ledger, borrower receipt, collateral package, debt-service waterfall, cash-generation support, and repayment or payoff evidence.

## Upgrade Test

Upgrade only if a source package proves all or most of the following:

1. Frontline borrower legal names and facility borrower/guarantor structure.
2. Total facility size, tranche type, maturity, pricing, amortization, covenant, and collateral package.
3. Lender allocation schedule showing Ares-managed vehicles and other lenders.
4. Use-of-proceeds schedule tying facility proceeds to acquisition, refinancing, add-on M&A, capex, working capital, dividend, fees, or debt repayment.
5. Borrower financials or rating report showing revenue, EBITDA, free cash flow, leverage, and debt-service coverage.
6. Interest and principal cash receipts, repayment schedule, payoff letter, or refinancing waterfall.
7. Prior bank facility termination or residual bank role if the claim is bank replacement.
