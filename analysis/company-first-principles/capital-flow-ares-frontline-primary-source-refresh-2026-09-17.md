## Route upgrade

The route is now:

`Ares primary origination disclosure -> Bain acquisition or growth purpose -> Frontline operating borrower -> SEC-filed debt instrument marker -> public holder exposure proxy`

That is stronger than an undated private-credit marker because the acquisition
and later growth roles are independently dated. It still stops before:

`full facility size -> lender allocation -> closing funds flow -> prior debt payoff -> borrower free cash flow/debt service -> Ares vehicle receipt -> repayment/return`

The two Ares releases describe selected transactions and do not disclose the
facility amount, lender schedule, funding date, proceeds recipients, collateral
package, or debt-service history. The SEC schedules give instrument-level terms
and broader holder visibility, but not the full financing or Ares-specific
ownership allocation.

## QoE and financial-integrity boundary

- Arranger/bookrunner status is a role claim, not proof that Ares funded the
  entire facility.
- Fair value is not funded principal, and an unfunded commitment is not a cash
  outflow.
- A 2032 maturity and stated spread are not realized yield or liability-
  adjusted return.
- Acquisition or growth purpose is not a closing settlement or borrower-use
  ledger.

These are source-integrity controls, not manipulation findings.

## Next decisive documents

1. Frontline credit agreement and amendments.
2. Administrative-agent and lender allocation schedule.
3. Bain acquisition debt commitment and closing funds-flow memorandum.
4. Prior debt payoff or refinancing settlement record.
5. Collateral/security package and covenant schedule.
6. Borrower EBITDA, free-cash-flow, debt-service, and repayment records.
7. Ares vehicle-level trade, principal, interest, and recovery records.
