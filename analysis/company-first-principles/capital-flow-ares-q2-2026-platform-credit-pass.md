# Capital Flow Ares Q2 2026 Platform Credit Pass

## Purpose

This pass adds Ares as a direct private-credit routing case.

Ares is different from Apollo/Athene, KKR/Global Atlantic, and Brookfield Wealth Solutions. The key evidence here is not an owned insurance balance sheet. The evidence is that Ares has a very large credit platform, large direct-lending AUM, large current commitments, and a borrower-facing direct-lending release that names where capital was deployed.

## Evidence To Claim

| Evidence | Claim It Supports |

| Q2 credit new commitments were `23.686B USD`, or `65.03%` of total new commitments. | Current capital formation is also credit-heavy. |
| Q2 U.S. direct lending closed `8.2B USD` across `69` transactions, and LTM direct lending closed `52.3B USD` across `347` transactions. | Ares connects platform capital to recurring borrower-facing origination. |

## Claim Boundary

This pass does not prove that Ares is funded by owned insurance liabilities.

It proves something else:

`Ares is a credit-heavy asset-management platform with large direct-lending AUM, large new credit commitments, and recurring borrower-facing origination activity.`

It also does not prove broad bank displacement. To say that, we still need borrower documents showing prior bank facilities, use of proceeds, new lender groups, and post-close bank roles.

## Simple Version

Ares is the clearest pure private-credit router in the current set.

The numbers say:

`Ares had 671.319B USD of total AUM, 440.544B USD of Credit Group AUM, and 285.725B USD of U.S. plus European direct lending AUM.`

That means:

`credit was 65.62% of Ares AUM, and direct lending was 64.86% of Ares Credit Group AUM.`

Then the borrower-facing release says:

`Ares closed 8.2B USD of U.S. direct lending commitments across 69 transactions in Q2 2026, and 52.3B USD across 347 transactions over the last twelve months.`

So the safe claim is:

`Ares is routing very large pools of private credit into real borrowers, across multiple operating industries.`

The careful caveat is:

`This is borrower-facing origination evidence, not yet borrower-level proof that banks were displaced in each transaction.`
