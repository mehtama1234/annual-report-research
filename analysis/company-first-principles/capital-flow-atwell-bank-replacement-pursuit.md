## Purpose

This pass resolves queue item `CFARQ-001` as far as current public/local evidence allows.

`Did the 2026 Ares/KKR private-credit package repay, amend, replace, or coexist with Atwell's prior 200M USD Bank of America-led facility?`

## Resolution Outcome

`hold - replacement not proven`

The current evidence proves a clean before/after financing setup, but it does not prove the bank-replacement mechanism.

## Current Safe Answer

`Atwell is a high-quality bank-replacement test case, but not a confirmed bank-replacement case.`

The defensible claim is:

`Atwell had a disclosed 200M USD Bank of America-led senior credit facility in 2024 and later had a disclosed 2026 sponsor acquisition financing trail involving Ares and Antares, with KKR-related SEC-filed holders reporting Atwell first-lien debt as of Q2 2026. Current public/local evidence does not show whether the 2024 bank facility was repaid, terminated, amended, or left in place.`

## Why This Matters

This is exactly the distinction the broader thesis needs.

If the missing document shows repayment or termination, Atwell becomes another borrower-level bank-replacement case.

If the missing document shows banks remained in a revolver, agency, treasury, hedging, or collateral role, Atwell becomes a coexistence case.

If the missing document cannot be found, Atwell remains a bounded private-credit acquisition-finance and holder-exposure case.
