# Capital Flow Atwell Bank-Replacement Test

## Purpose

This is the first borrower-level bank-replacement test from the Ares selected direct-lending examples.

The question is:

`Did private credit replace a prior bank facility, or did it finance a sponsor transaction alongside or above still-existing bank credit?`

## What Is Proven

Atwell had a prior named bank-led facility:

`200M USD senior credit facility -> Bank of America led -> U.S. Bank, TD Bank, Old National Bank also included`

Atwell later appears in a sponsor/private-credit financing chain:

`Advent investment -> Ares senior secured facility role -> Antares undisclosed first-lien joint lead arranger role`

The latest search adds one financing-structure clue:

`senior secured term loan and revolving facility`

That clue comes from a web-rendered Weil representative-transaction page. The local file is a Cloudflare block page, so this is a clue to pursue rather than a full local extraction.

The latest SEC portfolio schedule adds real holding numbers:

`K-FITS Atwell first-lien funded fair value 3.986M USD + unfunded commitment fair value 0.486M USD`

The cross-holder SEC search adds a second reported holder:

`FSK Atwell first-lien funded fair value 3.2M USD + unfunded commitment 0.4M USD`

Atwell is a real operating destination:

`critical infrastructure services -> power and energy -> commercial and digital infrastructure -> engineering, consulting, construction services`

## What Is Not Proven

We cannot yet say:

- Ares replaced Bank of America.
- Antares replaced U.S. Bank, TD Bank, or Old National Bank.
- The 2024 facility was repaid.
- The 2024 facility was terminated.
- Banks had no post-close role.

The safer claim is:

`Atwell is a live bank-replacement candidate because it has a disclosed 2024 bank-led credit facility and a disclosed 2026 Ares/Antares private-credit acquisition-financing trail. The currently visible sources do not prove displacement.`

## Simple Version

The simple answer is:

`Atwell is the first clean test case, not the final proof.`

We can show that Atwell had a `200M USD` bank-led facility in 2024. We can also show that Ares and Antares appear in the 2026 Advent acquisition financing. That is enough to ask the right question:

`Did private credit replace the bank facility, or did it fund the sponsor deal while banks remained somewhere else in the capital structure?`

Right now the honest answer is:

`We do not know yet. The next real-number work is to find the debt document or rating report that says whether the 2024 bank debt was repaid, amended, terminated, or left in place.`
