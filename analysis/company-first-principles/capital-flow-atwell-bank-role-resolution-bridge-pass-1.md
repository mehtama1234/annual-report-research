# Capital Flow Atwell Bank-Role Resolution Bridge Pass 1

## Purpose

This page answers the next bank-role operating-return question:

`Did the 2026 Ares/Antares private-credit package repay, amend, replace, or coexist with Atwell's prior 200M USD Bank of America-led facility?`

The structured companion table is:

`analysis/company-first-principles/data/capital-flow-atwell-bank-role-resolution-bridge-pass-1.csv`

## Short Answer

Atwell remains `replacement not proven`.

It is still one of the best bank-role test cases because the before/after evidence is real:

`2024 Bank of America-led facility -> 2026 Advent sponsor transaction -> Ares acquisition-financing role -> Antares first-lien role -> KKR-related SEC holder rows`

But the decisive document is still missing. The current evidence does not show whether the 2024 bank facility was repaid, terminated, amended, replaced, or left in place beside the new private-credit financing.

## Mechanism

The bank-role test is:

`prior bank facility -> sponsor transaction -> new senior secured debt -> holder schedule evidence -> payoff/termination/amendment/coexistence document -> bank-role classification`

Atwell passes the first four steps. It fails the fifth step.

## Source Notes

The public web refresh on August 25, 2026 found confirmatory evidence, not decisive replacement evidence:

- Advent and Atwell both announced the March 27, 2026 sponsor investment and described Atwell as an infrastructure-services operating platform.
- Antares' public transaction list shows Advent/Atwell as an April 2026 undisclosed first-lien credit facility where Antares was joint lead arranger.
- Weil's public attorney profile identifies Atwell LLC in a senior secured term loan and revolving facility.

Those sources strengthen the financing trail, but they do not answer the bank-exit question.

## Decision Tree

Atwell has four possible final classifications:

| Classification | Evidence Needed | Claim Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed replacement | Payoff, termination, or sources-and-uses showing the Bank of America-led facility was repaid or commitments ended. | Atwell becomes another confirmed bank-facility takeout case. |
| Amendment/restatement | Amendment or restated agreement showing bank facility continuation or modification. | Atwell becomes a refinancing/restructuring case, not clean replacement. |
| Coexistence | New lender list, revolver schedule, UCC continuation, treasury/hedging role, or ancillary-bank evidence. | Atwell becomes a private-credit-plus-bank coexistence case. |
| Unresolved | No decisive document found. | Atwell stays a live candidate with strong private-credit destination and holder proof. |

Current classification:

`unresolved - replacement not proven`

## What This Upgrades

This bridge upgrades Atwell from:

`open bank-replacement test`

to:

`resolved as unresolved after current evidence`

That sounds narrow, but it matters. It prevents the broader private-credit thesis from overstating bank displacement.

## Safe Claim

`Atwell had a disclosed 200M USD Bank of America-led senior credit facility in 2024 and later had a disclosed 2026 sponsor acquisition-financing trail involving Ares and Antares, with KKR-related SEC-filed holders reporting Atwell first-lien exposure as of Q2 2026. Current public/local evidence does not show whether the 2024 bank facility was repaid, terminated, amended, or left in place. Atwell is therefore a high-quality bank-role test case, not a confirmed bank-replacement case.`

## Claims Not To Make Yet

- private credit replaced Bank of America at Atwell
- Ares or Antares took out the 2024 bank facility
