# Capital Flow Bank Role After Private Credit Pass 1

## Purpose

This pass answers the highest-priority open meta question:

`Is private credit replacing banks in named borrower cases, or is it sitting beside banks elsewhere in the capital stack?`

The operating table is:

`analysis/company-first-principles/data/capital-flow-bank-role-after-private-credit-pass-1.csv`

## Current Answer

The current answer is mixed:

`Private credit can replace bank facilities in specific borrower cases, but the broader borrower set mostly proves private-credit destination and holder visibility rather than bank displacement. Banks may still retain revolver, agent, treasury, hedging, collateral-facility, or other ancillary roles unless a document proves otherwise.`

Medallia now has an additional Q2 2026 control: BCRED's SEC filing reports two
same-instrument first-lien rows totaling `$1.118387B` par and `$553.601M` fair
value, while an Antares SEC filing reports another `$105M` par / `$60M` fair
value row with the same pricing and maturity signature. These are holder-side
stress and lender-breadth observations around the lender-led recapitalization,
not proof of borrower cash, exact allocation, or the `$150M` new-money
waterfall.
Medallia's August 3, 2026 completion release now confirms the recapitalization
closed, debt was significantly reduced, `$150M` of new capital was secured, and
ownership transferred to the Blackstone/Apollo/FSK-led group. The event is
therefore completed rather than merely announced, but the dollar-level
debt-reduction and lender-settlement waterfall remain open.

Precinmac now has a current Q2 2026 holder refresh: Blue Owl reports four
Paris US Holdco / Precinmac first-lien rows with a common December 2031
maturity, totaling `$324.270M` reported par and `$223.821M` fair value. The
rows improve current lender-side visibility, but their par-to-cost differences,
facility overlap, Ares allocation, borrower receipt, and bank role remain
unresolved. This is a holder-breadth upgrade, not bank-replacement proof.

## What This Says

The research can now separate three different claims:

1. Private credit can replace named bank facilities in specific cases.
2. Private credit can fund named borrowers and sponsor acquisitions even when bank replacement is not proven.
3. Banks may remain in adjacent roles unless the evidence directly proves repayment, termination, amendment, or full lender replacement.

## What It Does Not Say

Do not say:

- private credit is broadly replacing banks
- banks disappeared from the borrower relationship
- visible BDC fair value equals total facility size
- transaction value equals debt size
- a holder row proves use of proceeds
- a financing-role press release proves bank displacement

## Safe Claim

`The bank-role evidence now supports a narrow but useful conclusion: private credit can take out named bank facilities in specific cases, and it clearly reaches named borrowers through filed holder rows and transaction-role evidence. The broader claim that private credit is replacing banks across borrower lanes remains unproven until repeated cases show repayment, termination, amendment, or full lender replacement and until lane-matched bank denominators are tested.`
