## What The Lane View Adds

The platform view tells us who routes the money.

The lane view tells us what the economy is absorbing.

This matters because the same platform can appear in several lanes:

1. Blackstone appears in private credit, insurance-client capital, data centers, power, real estate, wealth alternatives, and infrastructure.
2. Apollo/Athene appears most strongly in insurance/retirement yield, private credit, ABF, and investment-grade origination.
3. KKR/Global Atlantic appears in insurance assets, private credit, infrastructure, private equity, ABF, and wealth products.
4. Brookfield appears in real assets, power, renewables, infrastructure, insurance-linked capital, and Oaktree credit.
5. Ares appears in private credit, borrower finance, infrastructure credit, secondaries, and wealth channels.
6. Carlyle appears in private equity, global credit, secondaries, co-investment, and wealth.
7. BlackRock appears in public-market implementation plus expanding private credit/private-market workflow.

The destination lane is the better unit for finding operating beneficiaries. Power/grid, fleet, LNG, midstream, industrial distribution, contractors, and healthcare/service rollups are where capital needs to become physical capacity, service capacity, or cash flow.
