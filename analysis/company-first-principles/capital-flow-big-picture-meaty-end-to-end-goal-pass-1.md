## The Meaty Goal

`Build a document-backed map of how giant pools of money move from capital sources through major platforms into named real-economy borrowers, projects, assets, collateral pools, and operating companies, then prove selected cases all the way through cash coming back.`

In simple words:

`Who has the money, who routes it, where does it go, what does it fund, what cash comes back, and what document proves each link?`

The project is not finished when we know that BlackRock, Blackstone, Apollo/Athene, KKR/Global Atlantic, Brookfield, Ares, or Carlyle are large.

The project is finished only when the system can separate:

1. `map evidence`: the platform, wrapper, lane, or destination is visible
2. `bridge evidence`: a named borrower, issuer, asset, project, cash proxy, or legal schedule is visible
3. `named-cash hold`: most of the chain is visible, but one decisive receipt/waterfall/schedule/model is missing
4. `full named-cash proof`: the source, wrapper, destination, use, cash-back evidence, legal route, and return are joined without guessing

## The Chain We Are Trying To Prove

Every serious row should try to walk this chain:

`capital source -> big-money platform -> legal wrapper -> instrument -> named destination -> use of proceeds -> operating output or asset ownership -> receipt/settlement/income/billing/repayment -> waterfall/legal availability -> return`

That means a complete answer needs more than AUM, fundraising, capex, backlog, EBITDA, or investment-income totals.

It needs the hard proof families:

| Proof Need | What It Answers |
|---|---|
| receipts and billing | Did a customer, counterparty, borrower, shipper, utility customer, or offtaker actually pay? |
| settlement ledger | Did a transaction, stream, sale, maturity, paydown, or redemption actually settle in cash? |
| debt waterfall | Who got paid first, what debt was serviced, and what cash was legally available afterward? |
| legal-entity schedule | Which legal entity owned the asset, carried the liability, earned the income, or realized the proceeds? |
| collateral certificate | Which assets supported borrowing, at what advance rate, after what reserves and eligibility haircuts? |
| return model | Was the cash return attractive after cost, duration, risk, leverage, losses, and timing? |

## What We Have Found So Far

The simple answer is:

`We found the pipes. We found named destinations in several pipes. We have not yet proven a full cash loop from original source to final return.`

The current work already shows that capital moves through recognizable wrappers into real-economy lanes:

1. insurance balance sheets and retirement liabilities
2. private-credit funds and BDC schedules
3. bank facilities, ABLs, revolvers, and collateral routes
4. public debt and refinancing channels
5. utility recovery mechanisms and tariff routes
6. infrastructure and project-finance lanes
7. fleet, backlog, acquisition, working-capital, and capex engines

The strongest proof lanes so far are not the same thing as full proof:

| Case | What It Tells Us | Why It Still Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Apollo / Athene | Statutory filings expose legal-entity assets, liabilities, Schedule D holdings, investment income, sales, maturities, repayments, and proceeds fields. | Need CUSIP-level income/proceeds matching, liability-cost spread, borrower receipt/use, and final return model. |
| KKR / Global Atlantic | Global Atlantic statutory filings are now locally acquired, located, compact-extracted, and partially parsed into an Accordia Schedule D named-security universe. | Column reconciliation is still on hold, and named rows are not yet tied to final income/proceeds/return. |
| Ares / Frontline | Private credit can be tied to a named borrower, holder/arranger evidence, and partial facility/use/cash route. | Need credit agreement, lender allocation, funds flow, borrower cash, repayment, and residual bank role. |
| FPL Distribution Inspection | Regulated recovery can tie costs, recovery authority, component evidence, and aggregate clause revenue. | Need actual customer billed/collected cash and category allocation to the named program. |
| URI fleet | Fleet investment, collateral route, rental revenue, productivity, and operating-cash proxies are visible. | Need borrowing-base certificate, collateral availability, fleet-class cash return, and lender waterfall. |
| Wheaton / Antamina | A named upfront cash transfer and stream mechanics are visible. | Need PMPA-only settlement ledger, mine-level use allocation, lender waterfall, and return model. |
| Cheniere / Energy Transfer | Project/output/capex/debt and cargo/tariff-style evidence are visible. | Need named project cash contribution, customer receipts, debt waterfall, and return model. |

## What We Need To Do Next

The next meaty end-to-end goal is:

`Turn one insurance-capital platform case and one private-credit borrower case from map/bridge evidence into named-cash hold or named-cash proof.`

The first insurance-capital case is:

`KKR / Global Atlantic / Accordia -> statutory legal entity -> Schedule D named securities -> column-reconciled book/fair/cost fields -> investment income/proceeds -> liability context -> cash-return verdict`

The first private-credit borrower case is:

`Ares / Frontline -> holder or vehicle -> facility -> use of proceeds -> borrower cash -> repayment or waterfall -> return/bank-role verdict`

These two cases matter because they test the two biggest routes in the project:

1. retirement and insurance money becoming credit assets
2. private-credit money reaching named operating borrowers

## Success Criteria For The Next Pass

The next pass succeeds only if it produces:

1. a joined table for the selected case, not just a memo
2. named legal entity or vehicle
3. named borrower, issuer, project, or asset
4. amount, period, instrument, and source document
5. use-of-proceeds evidence or an explicit use hold

7. legal/waterfall evidence or an explicit legal-route hold
8. return evidence or an explicit return-model hold

10. the exact next document that would upgrade or disprove the row
