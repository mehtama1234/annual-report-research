## Better Claim

Old claim:

`Insurance and retirement liabilities are being converted into private-credit origination power.`

Better current claim:

`Apollo/Athene, KKR/Global Atlantic, Brookfield Wealth Solutions, and Blackstone Credit & Insurance now show a four-manager pattern. The mechanism has two forms: owned or affiliated insurance/retirement liability engines at Apollo, KKR, and Brookfield, and third-party insurance-client capital routed through Blackstone SMAs and credit strategies.`

## Simple Version

Blackstone changes the shape of the answer.

It is not the same as Apollo/Athene or Brookfield. The evidence here is not "Blackstone owns the insurance liabilities." The evidence is that insurers are clients, and their money is flowing into Blackstone-managed credit strategies.

So the broader point is stronger: insurance money is becoming credit fuel through more than one structure.
