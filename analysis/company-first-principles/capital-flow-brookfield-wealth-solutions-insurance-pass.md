## Better Claim

Old claim:

`Insurance and retirement liabilities are being converted into private-credit origination power.`

Better current claim:

`Apollo/Athene, KKR/Global Atlantic, and Brookfield Wealth Solutions now show a three-manager pattern: insurance or retirement-linked liabilities support large invested-asset pools, visible spread or insurance earnings, and meaningful credit/private-asset exposure. Brookfield gives the clearest allocation row so far with a 33% private-credit portfolio allocation.`

## Simple Version

Brookfield is the bridge we needed.

Apollo showed retirement inflows and spread earnings. KKR showed insurance AUM with a big credit component. Brookfield shows the portfolio mix directly: its Wealth Solutions investment portfolio is `33%` private credit and `49%` public credit.

So the simple answer is stronger now: insurance and retirement money is becoming a funding source for credit and private assets across more than one platform. The next question is quality: what exactly is in those credit portfolios, how risky is it, and who ultimately borrowed the money?
