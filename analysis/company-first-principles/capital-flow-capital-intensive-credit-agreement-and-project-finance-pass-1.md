## Theme And Subtheme Interpretation

This page deepens the same broad theme:

`capital-intensive real-economy buildout`

The new subtheme is:

`credit stack and project-finance proof`

That subtheme sits between company funding capacity and true project-level source-of-funds proof.

The evidence ladder is now:

`cash generation -> liquidity/facility capacity -> actual draws/refinancing -> entity debt -> collateral/covenants -> project source-of-funds -> physical output and return`

This pass moves the lane one step rightward on that ladder. It does not finish the ladder.

| Subtheme | What It Tracks | Companies In This Pass | What The Evidence Currently Says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revolver and commercial-paper bridge | Whether a company has a committed facility, active borrowings, and short-term funding channels around its buildout. | ET | ET has a large unsecured Five-Year Credit Facility, active borrowings, and commercial paper included in the facility balance. |
| Refinancing and use-of-proceeds | Whether debt issuance proceeds are tied to repayment or refinancing language. | ET | ET's January 2026 notes are explicitly linked to refinancing existing indebtedness, including commercial paper and Five-Year Credit Facility borrowings. |
| Entity-level project debt | Whether debt can be separated by legal issuer or project-like entity. | Cheniere | Cheniere's Q2 2026 debt table separates SPL and CQP debt, which is stronger than one company-level debt number. |
| Asset-backed fleet funding | Whether operating assets or receivables provide a funding base. | URI | URI has AR securitization and ABL balances alongside term debt and senior notes. |
| Platform credit reset | Whether a company changes its credit architecture after scaling. | Sterling | Sterling moved from a term-loan and small revolver structure toward a much larger July 2026 revolving-credit platform. |
| Governance boundary | Whether credit evidence proves actual project source-of-funds. | All four | The answer is still no. This is credit-stack evidence, not full project-finance proof. |

## Simple Bottom Line

In simple words:

`We can now see more of the pipes that fund the buildout. ET uses a big revolver, commercial paper, and note refinancing. Cheniere has debt separated by LNG entities and tells us part of growth capital is equity-funded. URI funds the rental-fleet model with receivables securitization, ABL, term debt, notes, and cash flow. Sterling enlarged its company credit platform dramatically.`

The important boundary:

`Credit-stack evidence is not the same as project source-of-funds proof.`
