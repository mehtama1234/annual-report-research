## Four Separate Mechanisms

### 1. Energy Transfer: Midstream And NGL Logistics

Current source-backed evidence:

- FY `2025` growth capex of `5.250B USD`
- FY `2025` maintenance capex of `1.150B USD`
- FY `2026` growth-capex guide raised to `5.6B-5.9B USD`
- Nederland ethane export capacity addition of `240000 bpd`
- Nederland LPG capacity addition of `55000 bpd`
- Lone Star Express incremental Permian NGL takeaway capacity above `90000 bpd`
- long-term y-grade transportation/fractionation agreements of about `300000 bpd`
- Lake Charles LNG suspension and `256M USD` impairment
- Q2 `2026` revolver availability of `3.76B USD`
- Q2 `2026` long-term debt less current maturities of `68.393B USD`

Outside tests:

- EIA petroleum/NGL/LPG/export denominators
- EIA product/basin/PADD flow data where available
- SEC project and debt tables
- credit agreements, maturity tables, and rating notes

Simple claim:

`ET is a midstream bottleneck-relief case: capital is being pushed toward NGL export, Permian takeaway, fractionation, and natural-gas pipeline projects.`

Boundary:

`The project return is not proven until capacity, contract term, project cost, in-service timing, utilization, and funding source are tied together.`

### 2. Cheniere: LNG Liquefaction And Contracted Production

Current source-backed evidence:

- FY `2025` revenue of `19.98B USD`
- FY `2025` DCF of `5.29B USD`
- FY `2025` `670` cargoes and `2424 TBtu`
- Q2 `2026` `184` cargoes
- about `90%` of anticipated SPL/CCL production contracted
- about `15` years weighted-average remaining contract life
- expected capacity above `60 mtpa`
- more than `9 mtpa` under construction at year-end `2025`
- CCL Stage 3 `94.1%` complete at year-end `2025`
- CCL Trains 8/9 `31.8%` complete at year-end `2025`
- CPC Taiwan SPA for up to `1.2 mtpa` through `2050`
- FY `2025` growth capital of `2.6B USD`
- FY `2025` total debt of `23.0B USD`

Outside tests:

- DOE LNG export reports
- EIA LNG export data
- FERC eLibrary LNG project dockets
- SEC contract summaries
- project finance and entity-level debt tables

Simple claim:

`Cheniere is a contracted capacity-conversion case: long-duration contracts and project finance are being turned into LNG train completions, cargoes, and cash generation.`

Boundary:

`Company DCF and cargo count do not prove train-level returns without train cost, SPA mix, debt service, and capacity economics.`

- fleet OEC of `22.48B USD`
- `1.095M` equipment units
- fleet age of `49.5` months
- FY `2025` fleet productivity up `2.2%`
- Q2 `2026` OEC of `23.8B USD`
- H1 `2026` gross rental capex of `2.931B USD`

Outside tests:

- U.S. equipment rental market size
- industry fleet investment and utilization benchmarks

Simple claim:

Boundary:

`Fleet capex is not automatically high-return growth. It needs utilization, rate, mix, fleet age, recovery, and debt/facility proof.`
