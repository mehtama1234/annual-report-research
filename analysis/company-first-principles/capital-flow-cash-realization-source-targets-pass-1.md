## What Counts As An Upgrade

A source target upgrades only when it contains:

- a named asset, category, borrower, facility, project, or vehicle
- same-period linkage
- cash, billing, collection, repayment, revenue, investment income, or debt-service evidence
- source, use, output, or entitlement context
- a boundary that prevents aggregate evidence from being treated as asset-level proof

## What Does Not Count

The following can support lower proof levels but cannot by themselves prove cash realization:

- AUM
- BDC fair value
- aggregate operating cash flow
- aggregate utility clause revenue
- authorized revenue requirement
- rate factor approval
- backlog/RPO
- cargo count
- capex plan
- company-level debt capacity
- broad management commentary
