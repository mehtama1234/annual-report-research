## What The Queue Proved

The queue proved the shape of the answer, not the final investment conclusion:

- Customer cash remains the biggest missing layer.
- Project or asset contribution remains mostly unproven.
- Debt-service and source/use routing improved in Matador, PBF, Liberty, and Devon.
- Statutory, borrower-facility, and collateral availability evidence remains the legal proof bottleneck for insurance/private-credit rows.
- Return evidence is strongest for Wheaton, FPL, and URI, but still partial.
