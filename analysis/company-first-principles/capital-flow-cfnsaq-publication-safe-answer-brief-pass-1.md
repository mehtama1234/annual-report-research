## Do Not Say

The current evidence does not support these sentences:

- `The capital-flow thesis is fully proven.`
- `Any case has full asset-level cash-return proof.`
- `AUM or platform inflows prove borrower-level deployment.`
- `Backlog proves customer cash collection.`
- `Tariff rate proves route revenue.`
- `Company EBITDA or DCF proves named project return.`
- `Pledged collateral value proves legal availability.`
- `Partial yield or component math proves IRR, NPV, or ROIC.`
