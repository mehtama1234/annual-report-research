## Question

`Can we tie Cheniere LNG output and customer contracts to a legal entity waterfall for debt service and distributions?`

## Short Answer

`Partly. Cheniere can be upgraded from a pure contracted-output/company-cash proxy to entity-waterfall-mechanics visible. Public filings show that SPL and CCH are restricted from distributions until debt-service reserves are established with cash or letters of credit and historical/projected debt service coverage ratios of at least 1.25:1.00 are satisfied. Prior annual-report language also shows that SPL and CCH cash received is deposited into reserve accounts controlled by collateral trustees and used for project liabilities and restricted payments. Current evidence still does not show train-level customer receipts, cargo-to-contract revenue, debt-service reserve account balances, actual DSCR calculations, project EBITDA, distribution amounts by entity, or project IRR/NPV.`

## What This Upgrades

Before this pass, Cheniere was:

`contracted LNG output bridge visible`

After this pass, Cheniere is:

`entity-waterfall-mechanics visible`

That upgrade matters because the evidence now includes the legal movement architecture:

`customer cash receipts -> SPL/CCH collateral-trustee reserve accounts -> project liabilities and debt-service reserves -> DSCR test -> restricted distributions`

The current evidence still lacks the numbers needed to prove the path in a specific period.

## Upgrade Test

Cheniere reaches train/entity cash-waterfall proof only if a source joins:

1. customer or SPA invoice/receipt support
2. cargo-to-contract and cargo-to-terminal/train mapping
3. train or terminal revenue and operating cost
4. SPL/CCH cash account or reserve account balances
5. debt-service reserve deposits and letters of credit
6. actual historical and projected DSCR calculations
7. restricted-payment or distribution schedule
8. debt principal and interest paid by entity
9. train/entity EBITDA, DCF, or cash contribution
10. project IRR, NPV, DSCR model, or lender payback schedule

## Decision

`cheniere-entity-dscr-waterfall-bridge-hold-with-waterfall-mechanics-visible`

Cheniere improves from output/demand/company-cash proxy to legal entity waterfall mechanics. It remains below named train/entity cash proof because the public record still does not provide the period cash amounts inside the waterfall.
