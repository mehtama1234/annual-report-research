# Capital Flow Cheniere Train Source-Use-Return Bridge Pass 1

## Purpose

This page answers the next energy operating-return question:

`Can Cheniere cargoes be tied to train-level debt, equity contributions, SPA economics, and debt service?`

The structured companion table is:

`analysis/company-first-principles/data/capital-flow-cheniere-train-source-use-return-bridge-pass-1.csv`

## Short Answer

Cheniere can be upgraded from `entity/output/funding evidence with partial project bridge` to `contracted LNG output bridge visible`.

It should not yet be upgraded to full `train source-use-return grade`.

The evidence now lines up across company cash generation, DOE cargo reconciliation, contracted production, long-duration SPA/IPM coverage, construction milestones, growth-capex funding mix, and entity debt buckets. The missing step is still train-level matching: exact project cost, financing source, construction draw, SPA economics, cargo attribution, cash return, and debt-service/waterfall proof by train or terminal.

## Mechanism

The working mechanism is:

`long-duration LNG customer demand -> SPA/IPM contract coverage -> entity debt/equity funding -> train and terminal construction -> substantial completion -> DOE-visible cargoes and volumes -> company EBITDA/DCF -> debt service and distributable cash`

This is the cleanest energy buildout bridge in the current evidence set because physical output is externally checkable through DOE cargo rows.

## Quality Read

The positive read is:

- FY 2025 revenue and DCF show a large cash-generating LNG platform.
- FY 2025 and Q2 2026 cargo counts reconcile closely to DOE vessel rows.
- Q2 2026 is especially strong: `184` company-reported cargoes and `184` DOE vessel rows.
- Contract coverage is visible: about `90%` of anticipated SPL/CCL production under SPAs/IPM agreements.
- Duration is visible: about `15` years weighted-average remaining SPA/IPM life at year-end 2025.
- Named customer demand is visible through the CPC Taiwan SPA for up to `1.2 mtpa` from `2026` through `2050`.
- Construction progress is visible through CCL Stage 3, Midscale Trains 8 and 9, Train 6, and Train 7 milestone evidence.
- Funding stack evidence is visible through growth capital, equity-funded growth capital, long-term debt, SPL debt, and CQP debt.

The caution read is:

- company cargoes and DOE cargoes do not assign output to individual trains
- contracted production does not disclose full SPA pricing or credit support
- growth capital is not allocated by train in the current bridge
- entity debt buckets do not prove construction draw or debt-service coverage by train
- DCF is company/platform cash evidence, not project-return proof
- debt waterfalls, restricted accounts, EPC cost-to-complete, and coverage ratios remain missing

## What This Upgrades

This bridge upgrades Cheniere from:

`entity funding and output evidence`

to:

`contracted LNG output bridge visible`

The reason is that the evidence now spans the operating chain:

- demand: SPA/IPM coverage and CPC Taiwan SPA
- use: liquefaction capacity construction and train milestones
- output: company cargoes and DOE vessel rows
- cash: revenue and DCF
- funding: growth capital, equity-funded growth capital, SPL debt, CQP debt, and total debt
- disproof control: train-level economics and waterfall gaps are explicit

## Decision

`upgrade-with-boundary - Cheniere is contracted LNG output bridge visible, not train source-use-return grade.`

## Safe Claim

`Cheniere has the strongest current energy buildout bridge because long-duration contracted production, growth-capex funding evidence, entity debt buckets, train milestones, company cargoes, and DOE vessel rows all point to real LNG output. The claim must stay bounded because train-level cost, funding source, SPA economics, cargo attribution, cash return, and debt-service waterfalls remain unproven.`

## Claims Not To Make Yet

Do not say:

- Cheniere has train-level source-of-funds proof
- DOE cargo rows prove train-level returns
- contracted production proves SPA price or margin
- equity-funded growth capital can be assigned to a specific train without a source/use schedule
- SPL or CQP debt balances prove debt service for a named train
- cargo output proves cost of capital coverage
- company DCF proves project-level return
