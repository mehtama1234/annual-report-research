# Capital Flow Cross-BDC Industry Lane Comparison

## What This Adds

The ARCC and OBDC passes now give reconciled official industry totals.

BXSL now gives filed 10-Q industry percentages across its full portfolio.

This page puts them on one normalized lane map so we can ask:

`Across major public BDC loan books, where is private-credit capital visibly landing?`

## What The Pattern Says

The strongest repeated lanes are:

- software and IT systems
- commercial and professional services
- healthcare services and technology
- insurance and financial services
- consumer services
- industrial and capital-goods support

That is a more concrete statement than "private credit is growing."

It says private credit is visibly concentrated in cash-flowing, service-heavy, sponsor-financeable businesses where lenders can underwrite recurring revenue, enterprise value, contracts, and collateral packages.

## What This Does Not Say

This is not yet a market-share table.

Limitations:

- ARCC and OBDC are exact by official industry subtotal, while BXSL dollars are calculated from official filed percentages and filed total investment fair value.
- BXSL percentage conversion is strong for lane ranking, but not a substitute for borrower-by-borrower schedule parsing.
- Industry overlap does not prove the same borrower, sponsor, loan purpose, or displaced bank lender.

## Better Claim

Old claim:

`Private credit is going into the real economy.`

Better claim:

`Across three public BDC lane disclosures, private-credit exposure is visibly concentrated in software/services, healthcare, commercial/professional services, financial services, insurance, consumer services, capital goods, consumer/retail, materials, and media/entertainment. This supports a targeted borrower-lane thesis, not a generic all-economy replacement-of-banks thesis.`

## Simple Version

The private-credit money is showing up most clearly in software, healthcare, services, financial services, insurance, consumer businesses, industrial suppliers, materials, and media/entertainment.

That means the better thesis is not "private credit replaces banks everywhere."

The better thesis is:

`Private credit is becoming a major financing rail for specific private-company lanes.`
