## Strongest Claim

The strongest broad claim is:

`This is a capital-routing cycle: money is flowing through different wrappers because the underlying economic claims are different. Some capital funds corporate platforms, some funds legally separable assets, some funds collateral pools, some funds regulated rate-base assets, some funds customer projects, and some funds sponsor-backed acquisitions or refinancings.`

## Most Important Caveat

The most important caveat is:

`The evidence does not yet prove a complete source-to-use-to-output chain across all companies, and it does not support broad bank-replacement language.`

## Highest-Value Partial Claim

The highest-value partial claim is the bank-role claim.

Current safe sentence:

`Specific bank-facility takeout can be proven in selected cases, while broad bank replacement remains unproven.`

What would upgrade it:

- new private-credit facility identified
- repayment, termination, amendment, or replacement documented
- post-close bank role checked
