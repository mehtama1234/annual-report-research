## Current Answer

`No, not globally. The five-company debt/refinancing lane now has real Q2 2026 cash, debt, operating-cash-flow, proceeds, repayment, and stream-acquisition funding denominators. ## Best Next Target

The best next target is `Wheaton`, and the first follow-through bridge is now complete at company-level source-use-output-cash grade.

Reason:

Wheaton is the only one of the five where the current evidence already names a large use of cash: `4.5B USD` of upfront PMPA/royalty payments, including `4.3B USD` for BHP Antamina. The debt/cash side is also visible: `1.972B USD` gross bank debt, `1.500B USD` term-loan debt, `472.000M USD` revolver debt, `2.700B USD` of Q2 bank debt drawn, and `728.000M USD` repaid.

That makes the next test concrete:

`Can the Antamina PMPA documents and subsequent delivery/cash tables connect upfront funding to expected metal deliveries, stream economics, operating cash receipts, and debt service?`

The follow-through bridge now moves Wheaton from `denominator-visible-stream-return-open` toward `source-use-output-cash-bridge-visible-company-level`.

If no, Wheaton stays as a financing-and-contract-funding case, not a return case.

## Disproof Tests

| Company | What Would Weaken The Claim |
|---|---|
| PBF | Refinancing costs offset debt benefits, ABL exit is routine, or refinery cash return cannot be tied to the debt event. |
| Devon | Debt changes are routine maturity management, and cash returns are funded by operating cash flow rather than incremental financing. |
| Matador | Debt proceeds refinance old obligations or provide general liquidity without measurable production/cash uplift. |
| Liberty Broadband | Debt proceeds and repayments are transaction mechanics with no durable operating cash output or shareholder realization. |
| Wheaton | Deliveries or stream economics do not cover the cost of funding, or financing cannot be tied to cash receipts and debt service. |

## Answer Mechanism

For each debt/refinancing row, the next pass should extract:

1. The named debt instrument or facility.
2. The amount drawn, issued, repaid, refinanced, or terminated.
3. The stated use of proceeds.
4. The operating or transaction asset tied to the use.
5. Same-period cash or output evidence.
6. Covenant, maturity, pricing, and collateral terms.
7. Whether the change improves resilience, funds output, funds shareholder return, or only reshuffles liabilities.
8. A safe claim and a disproof test.

## Decision

`debt-refinancing-use-return-frontier-defined`

The debt/refinancing lane now has enough denominator evidence to ask the harder question, but not enough to answer it globally. Wheaton's first follow-through bridge is now visible at company-level source-use-output-cash grade, while stream-level return remains open. Matador's first follow-through bridge is now visible at borrowing-base-level source-use-output-cash grade, while asset-level return remains open. Liberty Broadband's first follow-through bridge is now visible as holding-company collateral/liquidity restructuring, not operating-return evidence. The next extraction should continue with Wheaton delivery/cash-receipt evidence, Matador source-to-specific-use allocation, and Liberty final transaction funds-flow.

## Safe Claim

`The debt/refinancing pilot now has Q2 2026 source-visible cash/debt/cash-flow denominators across all five rows. That supports a use/return frontier, not a completed return claim. Wheaton now has a company-level Antamina source-use-output-cash bridge, Matador has a borrowing-base-level source-use-output-cash bridge, Liberty Broadband has a holdco collateral/liquidity restructuring bridge, PBF has a company-level refining liquidity/cash bridge, and Devon has a company-level merger-period allocation bridge. None of the five should be promoted to asset-level return without dollar-level source/use/cash allocation evidence.`
