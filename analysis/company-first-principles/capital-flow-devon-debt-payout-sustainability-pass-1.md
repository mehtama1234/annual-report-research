## Current Answer

`debt-payout-sustainability-proxy-visible-full-cycle-not-proven`

Devon's Q2 `2026` filing supports a proxy-grade debt/payout sustainability answer. First-half operating cash flow was `5.329B USD`, capex was `2.157B USD`, dividends were `521M USD`, buybacks were `266M USD`, long-term debt repayments were `500M USD`, and interest based on debt outstanding was `262M USD`. A simple post-capex cash-flow proxy was `3.172B USD`, about `4.03x` first-half dividends plus buybacks and about `12.11x` interest based on debt outstanding.

That is strong same-period coverage evidence. It is not full-cycle sustainability proof because Devon says net earnings and OCF are highly dependent on volatile oil, gas, and NGL prices, and the filing does not provide a full commodity-stress free-cash-flow bridge.

## Debt Economics

The filing gives useful debt-quality facts:

- Total debt was `11.388B USD`.
- `10.7B USD` of debentures and notes had fixed rates averaging `5.49%`.
- The `750M USD` Term Loan had a `4.96%` variable rate at June `30`, `2026`.
- Devon repaid `250M USD` of Term Loan principal in June and the remaining `750M USD` in July.
- Devon early redeemed `250M USD` of `3.77%` senior notes due September `2026` under make-whole provisions.
- There were no quarter-end borrowings under the Senior Credit Facility or commercial-paper program.

This is enough for a debt-service quality proxy. It is not enough to claim value creation from repayment or refinancing because make-whole cost, tax effects, exact cash source, and pro forma interest after the July repayment are not fully extracted.

## Payout Sustainability

The simple first-half proxy is:

`OCF - capex = 5.329B USD - 2.157B USD = 3.172B USD`

First-half shareholder returns were:

`dividends + buybacks = 521M USD + 266M USD = 787M USD`

So:

`3.172B USD / 787M USD = 4.03x`

That supports a same-period payout-quality proxy. It does not prove that the payout is durable through a weaker commodity-price environment, a different capex program, or a full post-merger run rate.

## Safe Claim

`Devon's Q2 2026 filing supports proxy-grade debt/payout sustainability evidence. First-half OCF was 5.329B USD, capex was 2.157B USD, dividends were 521M USD, buybacks were 266M USD, debt repayments were 500M USD, interest based on debt outstanding was 262M USD, and the simple post-capex cash proxy was 3.172B USD. That proxy covered dividends plus buybacks about 4.03x and interest based on debt outstanding about 12.11x. Devon also had 4.0B USD of liquidity, no quarter-end revolver or commercial-paper borrowings, and an 18.1% funded-debt-to-capitalization ratio versus a 65% covenant limit. This supports same-period debt/payout resilience language, not full-cycle payout sustainability, commodity-stress resilience, exact debt-economics value creation, asset-level return, or realized Coterra synergy.`
