## Current Answer

`Devon now has a company-level merger-period allocation bridge. The filing shows Coterra merger debt assumption and exchange offers, a Senior Credit Facility maturity extension, covenant headroom, no quarter-end revolver or commercial-paper borrowings, term-loan and senior-note repayments, operating cash flow funding capital expenditures, dividends, repurchases, and debt retirements, a cash-funded Permian federal lease acquisition, Q2 production, and Coterra post-close revenue and earnings contribution. ## Why This Matters

Devon completes the five-company debt/refinancing lane with a fifth distinct mechanism:

Wheaton is named stream-acquisition funding. Matador is reserve-backed borrowing-base source/use/output. Liberty Broadband is holding-company collateral and transaction-liquidity restructuring. PBF is refining liquidity and balance-sheet management. Devon is merger-period allocation: operating cash flow, cash balances, assumed debt, exchanged notes, capex, land acquisition, dividends, buybacks, and debt retirement all sit in the same filing period.

That is useful because it prevents the debt/refinancing lane from overclaiming. Devon has strong cash generation and visible capital allocation, but the safe statement is not that debt financed output. The safe statement is that Devon had a source-visible allocation bridge and still needs dollar-level tracing.
