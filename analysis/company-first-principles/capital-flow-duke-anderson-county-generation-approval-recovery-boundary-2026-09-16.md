# Duke Anderson County Generation: Approval-to-Cash Boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

This pass narrows the Duke power-grid lane from portfolio-level load growth to
one named project. The South Carolina Public Service Commission's March 26,
2026 directive records Commission action approving the joint application for
the approximately `1,365 MW` Anderson County combined-cycle gas plant. The
directive requires compliance with applicable Commission, state, and local
requirements and quarterly fuel-supply project updates. The full Commission
Order `2026-244`, dated April 24, 2026, is now located: it grants the
certificate subject to required federal, state, and local permits,
consultations, and certifications. A later Office of Regulatory Staff filing
confirms that continuous/monthly construction monitoring began. Duke's official announcement gives the
anticipated summer 2027 construction start and early 2031 customer-service
target. Central Electric Power Cooperative and North Carolina Electric
Membership Corporation are described as owning `95 MW` and `100 MW`,
respectively.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

The approval is not earnings. A project can be approved yet remain subject to
construction spending, financing, fuel, taxes, shared ownership, rate-case
conditions, timing slippage, and customer affordability constraints. The
following fields must remain separate in the next pass:

- nominal MW versus net Duke-owned MW;
- approved project versus construction work in progress versus in-service
  plant;
- rate-base eligibility versus billed revenue and cash collection;
- customer benefit or projected economic impact versus utility return;
- turbine/equipment procurement versus completed plant;
- corporate capital-plan dollars versus project-specific spending and funding.

## Result

Status: `partial-upgrade; full certificate order located; approval conditioned and monitored; recovery and cash open`.

This is stronger than Duke's aggregate pipeline or secured-ESA headline because
it identifies Commission approval action, a capacity figure, an ownership
allocation, conditions, a monitored execution path, and a current budget
denominator. The certificate record also separates proposed accounting
attribution from final co-op ownership agreements. It is still not project
return proof. The required next documents
are the project cost and financing schedule,
ownership/Power Purchase or
participation agreement, CWIP/rate-base treatment, customer contribution or
large-load allocation, and post-construction billing/collection evidence.
