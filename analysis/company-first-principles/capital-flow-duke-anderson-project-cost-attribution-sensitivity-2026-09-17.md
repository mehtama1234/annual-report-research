# Duke Anderson Project Cost Attribution Sensitivity

Research date: `2026-09-17`

This is a mechanical sensitivity built from the South Carolina certificate
record's approximately `$3.218B` proposed total project cost and the stated
nominal capacity allocation: DEC `1,170 MW`, NCEMC `100 MW`, and Central `95
MW`, out of `1,365 MW`.

It is not a statement of final funding, cash paid, rate-base treatment, or
economic ownership. The certificate record says proposed costs were represented
as `100% DEC costs` while co-op negotiations were not finalized. The table
therefore shows the proportional ownership case beside the interim accounting
perimeter.

## Perimeter controls

1. The proportional values are not invoices, paid capex, construction-work-in-
   progress, or cash contributions.
2. The `100% DEC costs` representation is an interim proposed-cost perimeter,
   not proof that DEC ultimately bears 100% of economic cost.
3. DEC control and construction/operation responsibility do not eliminate the
   co-ops' ownership or participation claims.
4. Rate-base eligibility, allowed return, fuel cost recovery, customer billing,
   and collected cash remain separate joins.
5. The sensitivity must not be used as owner cash, project IRR, ROIC, or a Duke
   valuation input until final participation agreements and funding/cost
   schedules are available.

## Result

Status: `mechanical-allocation-sensitivity-only`.

This improves the denominator map by making the ownership/cost attribution
frontier explicit. It does not close the owner-cash gap.
