## Execution Rule

Do not upgrade a graph row because the story is plausible.

Upgrade only when the next artifact can add at least one missing link:

- capital source allocation
- vehicle or instrument detail
- use-of-proceeds evidence
- asset/project/borrower output
- cash receipt, repayment, margin, spread, or debt-service evidence
- durability or disproof evidence
