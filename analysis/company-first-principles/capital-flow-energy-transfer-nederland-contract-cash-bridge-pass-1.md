## Upgrade Test

ET reaches named-project cash proof only if a source joins:

1. named project capital cost and in-service schedule
2. source-to-project funding allocation
3. customer or shipper identity
4. contract term, volume, rate, fee, and take-or-pay or MVC economics
5. billing volume and utilization
6. terminal, pipeline, or fractionation revenue by project
7. project EBITDA, DCF contribution, and maintenance capital
8. debt-service allocation or project-finance support
9. DSCR, IRR, NPV, or return model
10. actual cash receipts or settlement/remittance evidence

## Decision

`energy-transfer-nederland-contract-cash-bridge-hold-with-committed-capacity-visible`
