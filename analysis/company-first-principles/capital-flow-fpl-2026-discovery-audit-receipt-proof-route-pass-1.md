## What This Means

The proof path is now narrower:

`docket -> discovery request -> FPL response/POD production -> non-confidential attachment or confidentiality index -> billing determinant / receipt / category allocation / source-of-funds schedule`

The public docket has not produced the decisive middle documents. It has produced the fact that those routes were used.

## Full Proof Still Requires

1. actual FPL discovery answers or POD attachments
2. non-confidential billing determinant schedules
3. confidential-document indexes naming billing, receipt, or workpaper attachments
4. actual billed and collected SPPCRC revenue by rate class/month
5. category allocation from aggregate SPPCRC revenue to Distribution Inspection
6. Distribution Inspection subaccount or regulatory ledger
7. source-of-funds allocation and legal-entity waterfall
8. earned ROE, IRR, NPV, payback, or return model

## Decision

`fpl-2026-discovery-audit-receipt-proof-route-hold-with-discovery-route-visible`

The discovery/audit route is visible and worth pursuing, but the public PDFs sampled here do not tie cash all the way through.
