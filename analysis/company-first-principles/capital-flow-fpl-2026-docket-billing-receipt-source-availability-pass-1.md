2. DN `02559-2026` for program/project testimony and AP exhibits.

5. Discovery and POD routes that could lead to non-confidential billing, receipt, and workpaper support.

## What Still Blocks Full Proof

Full named cash proof still requires the same exact documents:

1. actual billing determinants by rate class and month
2. actual SPPCRC billed and collected cash by period
3. Distribution Inspection category allocation from aggregate SPPCRC receipts
4. Distribution Inspection subaccount or regulatory ledger
5. source-of-funds allocation for Distribution Inspection spending
6. debt/equity/capital-provider waterfall or legal-entity funding schedule
7. earned ROE, IRR, NPV, payback, or return model tied to the category
