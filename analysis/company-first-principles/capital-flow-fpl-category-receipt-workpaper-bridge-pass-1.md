## Question

`Can FPL Distribution Inspection move from aggregate SPPCRC recovery proxy to category customer receipt proof?`

## Short Answer

`Not yet. The public/current source route confirms that the SPPCRC is the right legal and regulatory file family: the rule requires program/project cost recovery filings, true-ups, proposed cost recovery factors, and subaccount separation from base rates. The current FPL packet already shows Distribution Inspection output, category cost, final recovery, recovery components, aggregate SPPCRC clause revenue, true-up collection/refund mechanics, and approved factor authority. But neither the local packet nor the public route found here exposes billing determinants, rate-class usage, a Distribution Inspection category receipt ledger, source-of-funds allocation, actual earned ROE, or project-level return.`

## What Improved

The source route is narrower. The missing FPL document is not another high-level SPPCRC order. It is a workpaper or schedule that connects:

`rate class factor -> billing determinants / usage -> billed or collected SPPCRC revenue -> category allocation -> Distribution Inspection`

The rule confirms that program/project detail, factor calculations, true-ups, and subaccounts are part of the correct regulatory framework. The found materials still stop before category-level cash receipt proof.

## What Still Blocks Full Proof

Full FPL category customer-cash proof requires:

1. billing determinants by rate class and month
2. rate-class usage or customer-count billing base
3. applied SPPCRC factor by rate class
4. billed or collected SPPCRC revenue by period
5. allocation of aggregate SPPCRC revenue to Distribution Inspection
6. Distribution Inspection subaccount or regulatory ledger
7. source-of-funds allocation for Distribution Inspection capital spending
8. actual earned ROE or project-level return workpaper

## Decision

`fpl-category-receipt-workpaper-bridge-hold-with-legal-route-visible`

FPL remains the strongest regulated recovery proxy, but it is not category customer receipt proof. The next source target is a billing determinant/category allocation workpaper, not another aggregate revenue or factor order.

## Safe Claim

`FPL Distribution Inspection has strong regulated recovery evidence and a clear legal source route. The SPPCRC rule and FPL filings support program/project recovery, true-up, factor, and subaccount mechanics; the local packet shows 193199 projects, 61.500000M USD actual cost, 15.942971M USD final recoverable amount, 804.620369M USD aggregate SPPCRC clause revenue, true-up mechanics, and factor authority. Current evidence still does not prove Distribution Inspection-specific billing determinants, billed customer revenue, collected cash, source-of-funds allocation, actual earned ROE, or project-level return.`
