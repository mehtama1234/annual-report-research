## What Would Upgrade The Claim

The claim can move up only if a source provides one of these:

| Gate | Required Source | Upgrade Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Category receipt | Distribution Inspection receipt, billing, or collection schedule | Same-period billed or collected customer cash is tied to the category. |
| Billing determinant allocation | Rate-class determinant or category allocation workpaper | Rate-class factors can be reconciled to category cash rather than only aggregate SPPCRC recovery. |
| Later true-up | `2027` SPPCRC actual/estimated true-up filing | The `2026` factor-year collection/refund trail shows Distribution Inspection allocation. |
| Financing allocation | Treasury, construction funding, debt use-of-proceeds, or testimony | Debt, equity, operating cash, or general treasury funding is allocated to the category. |
| Audit/workpaper | PSC audit, discovery, or workpaper package | Category costs and recovery are traced to account-level or invoice-level support. |

## Key Source Clue

The May `2025` SPPCRC testimony states that actual data is taken from FPL accounting books and records, that SPP costs are booked to and tracked through the SPPCRC, and that the impact of approved rate-case changes will be reflected in FPL's `2026` SPPCRC actual/estimated true-up filing to be submitted in `2027`.

That means the next search should not be a broad FPL search. It should target:

- `2027` actual/estimated SPPCRC filing
- Form 2A / 5P / 6P / 7A / 8A workpapers
- billing determinant exhibits
- customer-class allocation support
- category-level recovery allocation schedules
- non-confidential audit or discovery support
- FPL/NextEra debt use-of-proceeds and construction funding language

## Disproof Tests

The stronger FPL hypothesis should weaken if a source shows:

- Distribution Inspection was materially excluded from actual recovery
- recovery was reallocated away from the category
- a refund or disallowance reversed category economics
- the category was recovered through a different mechanism than assumed
- Form 7A recovery components were not billed or collected
- source-of-funds language is only general corporate purposes or refinancing
- category cash cannot be separated from aggregate SPPCRC revenue

## Decision

`fpl-customer-receipt-source-pursuit-ready-hold`

The source routes and pass/fail gates are now explicit, but the claim does not upgrade. The next evidence must be an acquisition/extraction pass against the `2027` SPPCRC true-up route, billing determinant/workpaper route, audit/discovery route, and debt use-of-proceeds route.

## Safe Claim

`FPL Distribution Inspection is the strongest current regulated recovery bridge, but it remains below cash-realization proof. The current evidence proves output, cost, plant addition, final recovery, component attribution, aggregate SPPCRC revenue, factor authority, and capital-structure support; it does not prove category customer receipts or source-of-funds allocation.`
