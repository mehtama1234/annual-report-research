## What We Can Say

FPL is the clearest public regulated-cash route in the current system. The PSC packet shows how costs move into a regulated recovery mechanism and how aggregate clause revenue/factors are authorized or supported.

## What We Cannot Say

We cannot say FPL proves named cash all the way through for Distribution Inspection. The missing layer is the actual source package that joins:

1. rate-class/month billing determinants
2. billed SPPCRC customer revenue
3. collected cash
4. Distribution Inspection category allocation from aggregate receipts
5. subaccount or regulatory ledger
6. source-of-funds/legal-entity funding waterfall
7. earned return, IRR, NPV, or payback

## Decision

`fpl-proof-package-acquisition-hold-public-route-visible-served-proof-missing`

The public PSC route is acquisition-grade for source mapping and recovery mechanics. It is not full named-cash proof.

## Safe Claim

`FPL's public SPPCRC route shows a strong regulated recovery bridge: public docket filings, petition and testimony routes, factor/billing-base support, aggregate SPPCRC revenue evidence, Distribution Inspection category cost/recovery proxies, discovery routes, response notices, and audit routing. The public packet still does not expose Distribution Inspection-specific billed or collected customer cash, category receipt allocation, source-of-funds allocation, legal-entity funding waterfall, collateral or funding certificate, earned return, IRR, NPV, payback, or full named cash return.`
