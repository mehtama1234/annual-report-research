## Purpose

This pass executes the next step from:

The structured companion table is:

`analysis/company-first-principles/data/capital-flow-fpl-sppcrc-2027-factor-to-category-reconciliation-pass-1.csv`

The question is:

`Can the corrected 2027 Form 5P customer-factor pool be reconciled to projected recovery and Distribution Inspection category recovery?`

## Short Answer

Yes for total recovery.

No for Distribution Inspection customer receipts.

The current evidence shows:

`Form 1P projected revenue requirement -> true-up offsets -> jurisdictional amount to recover/refund -> corrected Form 5P rate-class factor pool`

The same filing family also shows:

`Distribution Inspection O&M recovery + Distribution Inspection capital recovery -> combined Distribution Inspection projected recovery`

But no source joins:

`corrected rate-class factor pool -> billed customer dollars -> collected customer cash -> Distribution Inspection category allocation`

So FPL improves again, but it remains below named customer-cash proof.

## Total Recovery Reconciliation

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Form 1P total projected period revenue requirement | `1.138572247B USD` |
| Less current-period actual/estimated over-recovery | `17.107391M USD` |
| Less prior-period final over-recovery | `16.579976M USD` |
| Form 1P jurisdictional amount to recover/refund | `1.104884879B USD` |
| Corrected Form 5P total SPPCRC cost pool | `1.104884879B USD` |

The total pool reconciles.

That matters because the corrected Form `5P` rate-class factors are not floating alone. They tie back to the total projected recovery amount after true-up offsets.

## Corrected Factor Pool

Corrected Form `5P` splits the total projected recovery pool into:

| Pool | Amount |
|---|---:|

| GNCP demand/customer and energy-related cost pool | `1.028550306B USD` |
| Total corrected SPPCRC cost pool | `1.104884879B USD` |

This is still projected factor math. It is not actual billed or collected cash.

Diagnostic scale:

|---|---:|

These are diagnostic ratios only. They are not official customer-cash allocation methods.

## What This Proves

This pass proves:

1. The corrected `2027` Form `5P` total equals the Form `1P` jurisdictional amount to recover/refund.
2. The corrected factor pool is the current rate-class factor basis after DN `03227-2026`.
3. Distribution Inspection has a projected `2027` category recovery amount.
4. The category recovery amount can be quantified at `32.239986M USD`.
5. The proof gap is now narrower: we need a category allocation or receipt workpaper, not another broad recovery schedule.

## What This Does Not Prove

This pass does not prove:

1. actual customer billing
2. actual customer cash collection
3. Distribution Inspection receipts by rate class
4. allocation of the corrected Form `5P` factor pool to Distribution Inspection
5. source-of-funds allocation
6. legal-entity cash movement
7. earned ROE or project return

## Practical Meaning

In simple words:

`FPL shows the total amount it wants to recover from customers through the 2027 SPPCRC factors. It also shows the Distribution Inspection category amount. But the public table we have does not say which customer classes paid which part of Distribution Inspection, or whether the cash was actually collected.`

That is a strong public recovery bridge.

It is still not receipt proof.
