# Frontline Facility-Size and Bank-Replacement Search Boundary

## Purpose

This pass closes the current public-search route for the Frontline Road Safety
facility-size and bank-replacement questions. It is a searched-negative
boundary, not evidence that no facility document exists.

## 2026-09-18 targeted official-source recheck

The targeted Ares search returned the same two dated primary disclosures
already used in the route: the May 5, 2025 release identifies Ares as lead
arranger and bookrunner for the Bain acquisition facility, and the July 31,
2026 release identifies Ares as lead arranger and bookrunner for a senior
secured facility supporting Bain's continued growth plans. The recheck did
not add a facility amount, lender allocation, borrower draw, or bank-payoff
record. It therefore strengthens the dated arranger-role chronology but does
not change the promotion boundary below.

## Search result

The checked public material does not provide a credit agreement, amendment,
agent allocation schedule, populated draw/settlement record, total facility
commitment, bank payoff/termination, or borrower-level use-of-proceeds ledger.
The Ares release is transaction-role evidence; the SEC and N-PORT schedules
are holder-level evidence. Neither category is facility-size or
bank-replacement proof.

## Safe claim after this pass

Frontline has a primary-source financing trail and broad public holder
visibility across related legal borrower names and 2031/2032 first-lien
instruments. The total facility, non-overlapping lender allocation, borrower
cash receipt/use, repayment, and replacement of any prior bank facility remain
unproven.

## Next source gate

Stop repeating broad web and SEC holder searches. Advance only on a newly
located credit agreement or amendment, agent/lender allocation, closing
funds-flow, draw/settlement record, borrower financial statement, UCC or bank
termination record, or equivalent primary document that changes one of the
open proof fields.
