# Capital Flow Guidehouse Bank Competition Proof Pass 1

## Purpose

This pass resolves queue item `CFARQ-004` as far as current local and public evidence allows.

The question is:

`Can Guidehouse be upgraded from a secondary-source bank-competition lead to a proven bank-versus-private-credit financing case?`

## Resolution Outcome

`hold-after-pass-1 - high-quality market bank-competition evidence, primary credit document still missing`

Guidehouse remains the best current bank-competition lead, but it does not yet pass the primary-document proof gate.

The evidence supports a bounded statement that private lenders competed with banks for the Bain / Guidehouse buyout financing. It does not yet prove final facility terms, complete lender allocation, full bank exclusion, or post-close bank roles.

## What Did Not Pass

| Missing Link | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Primary credit agreement | Needed to prove final borrower, borrower group, facility size, tranches, maturity, pricing, collateral, agent, and lender group. |
| Debt commitment letter | Needed to prove whether banks or private-credit funds committed final acquisition financing. |
| Rating report | Needed to independently verify debt size, issuer, instrument, sponsor, leverage, and lender role. |
| Lender allocation schedule | Needed to identify the full lender group and avoid over-attributing the package to one platform. |
| Post-close bank-role map | Needed to check whether banks retained revolver, treasury, hedging, agency, or collateral-facility roles. |
| Payoff/refinancing document | Needed to prove whether existing debt was amended, refinanced, repriced, or left in place. |

## Current Safe Answer

`Guidehouse is a strong bank-competition lead, but not yet a primary-source-proven bank-replacement case.`

Safe sentence:

`Guidehouse's 5.3B USD Bain acquisition is supported by company transaction evidence, and Bloomberg Law reports that Blackstone-led private lenders held off banks competing to finance the buyout. That supports Guidehouse as a high-value bank-competition lead, but the final credit agreement, debt commitment letter, rating report, lender allocation schedule, and post-close bank-role map are still needed before calling it proven bank replacement.`

## Why This Matters

Guidehouse is different from Auctane and Medallia.

Auctane proves a narrow bank-facility termination.

Medallia proves acquisition debt, bank-credit repayment, and later lender-control evidence, but with lender allocation still split across sources.

Guidehouse is the cleanest current market example of private credit competing directly with banks for a sponsor buyout financing. The problem is proof quality: the decisive bank-competition facts currently come from market reporting, not from a final borrower credit document.
