## What Changed

The claim started as a broad intuition:

`retirement and insurance money is helping fund private credit`

The evidence now supports a narrower, stronger claim:

`Large asset managers are building insurance-linked credit funding channels, but the channel structure differs by platform. Apollo/Athene, KKR/Global Atlantic, and Brookfield show owned or affiliated liability platforms. Blackstone shows third-party insurance-client capital flowing into credit strategies through SMAs and managed credit products.`

That is materially better than saying "private credit is replacing banks." The numbers show funding channels and asset-routing capacity. They do not yet prove broad bank displacement.

## Claim To Evidence

| Claim We Might Want To Make | Evidence Still Needed |
|---|---|
| Insurance liabilities are replacing bank credit. | Borrower-level credit agreements showing prior bank facility, new lender group, use of proceeds, repayment or termination language, and post-close bank role. |
| Insurance money is going into private credit rather than public bonds. | Portfolio allocation tables by asset class, statutory schedules, rating-agency asset-quality exhibits, and manager supplement tables that separate private credit, public credit, structured credit, real estate credit, and alternatives. |
| The capital is funding real economy investment rather than financial engineering. | Borrower use-of-proceeds tags: acquisition finance, refinancing, dividend recap, capex, rescue financing, working capital, infrastructure buildout, or asset-backed origination. |
| The channel is growing faster than banks in the same lane. | Time series that pairs private-credit inflows/originations with bank commercial-and-industrial, leveraged lending, syndicated loan, and industry-specific lending denominators. |
| The channel is creating new credit risk outside banks. | Credit-quality migration, non-accruals, ratings, loan-to-value, attachment point, spread, covenant, payment-in-kind, and maturity-wall data. |

## Simple Version

The simple answer is:

`Insurance and retirement money is becoming one of the raw materials for private credit.`

But the real claim has to be more careful:

`Some managers own or control insurance liability engines. Some manage insurer money as third-party client capital. Both can feed credit strategies, but they are not the same proof. To say banks are being displaced, we need borrower-level documents showing an old bank loan got repaid or lost to a private-credit lender.`

## Next Best Dig

The next best research pass is a two-sided test:

| Direction | Work |
|---|---|
| Evidence to claim | Pull statutory or rating-agency asset schedules for Athene, Global Atlantic, Brookfield/AEL, and large insurer SMA channels. Extract asset allocations, credit quality, and private-credit exposure. |
| Claim to evidence | Start with the strongest borrower-bank-displacement claim, then collect final credit agreements, 8-K exhibits, debt commitment letters, refinancing language, and lender-agent roles. |

This keeps the research honest. The evidence can make the claim stronger, weaker, or more specific.
