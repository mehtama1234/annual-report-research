## Short Answer

The next non-FPL dig should be:

`insurance statutory named-asset income proof`

In simple words:

`Find the insurance legal entity, list the named assets it owns, identify the income or proceeds those assets produced, compare that cash with the liability cost of the insurance money, and stop if the evidence cannot reach receipts, waterfalls, collateral, or return.`

This is the best route back to the big money players because it gets below broad AUM.

It asks:

`Which legal entity held the asset? Which CUSIP or issuer was it? How much was invested? What income or proceeds came back? What liability funded it? What spread or return was actually visible?`

## Decisive Documents

The decisive source package for this lane is:

| Document | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Statutory annual statement | Identifies legal entity, assets, liabilities, investment income, capital, and cash-flow context. |
| Schedule D detail | Names bonds and issuer/CUSIP-level holdings. |
| Schedule BA detail | Names alternatives, private assets, funds, and structured/non-bond investments. |
| Investment income exhibit | Shows income earned and collected by asset class, sometimes close to cash. |
| Disposal/proceeds schedules | Shows sale, maturity, repayment, consideration, and gain/loss. |
| NAIC designation / credit-quality support | Shows credit risk, migration, impairment, and RBC treatment. |
| Liability-cost or credited-rate support | Lets us compare asset income with the cost of insurance money. |
| Reinsurance / funds-held / FHLB support | Shows legal routing, pledged assets, funding-agreement economics, and possible collateral paths. |
| Trustee/remittance/collateral documents | Needed for full named-cash proof on ABS, private-credit, or structured-credit rows. |
| Return model | Needed before claiming IRR, NPV, spread, ROIC, or final investment success. |

## Pass / Hold Standard

Promote a case only if it can show:

`legal entity -> named asset or CUSIP -> investment amount -> income/proceeds -> liability cost or funding source -> cash route or waterfall -> return evidence`

Hold the case if it only shows:

1. platform AUM
2. insurance AUM
3. spread-related earnings
4. gross deployment
5. Schedule D holdings without income/proceeds
6. income without named holding join
7. proceeds without lot continuity
8. borrower names without receipts
9. collateral without remittance
10. returns without source/use/cash proof
