# Capital Flow Jiffy Transaction Gap Pass

## Purpose

This pass tests the Jiffy Lube selected borrower from Ares' Q2 2026 direct-lending release.

The operating data file is:

`analysis/company-first-principles/data/capital-flow-jiffy-transaction-gap-pass.csv`

## Question

`Can we turn the Ares selected-borrower Jiffy Lube item into actual holder-level debt numbers?`

Not yet.

Jiffy is strong as transaction-value and financing-role evidence, but it is not yet a holder-dollar case.

## 2026-09-18 completion refresh

Shell's official July 1, 2026 release confirms that the `$1.3B` sale of Jiffy
Lube International and Premium Velocity Auto to a Monomoy affiliate closed.
The completion evidence upgrades the transaction from announced to completed
and preserves the perimeter distinction: Shell retained its lubricant brands
and entered a long-term lubricant supply agreement with Monomoy. The release
still does not disclose the senior-facility amount, lender allocation,
borrower cash receipt, or bank payoff.

The current safe status is therefore:

`transaction-completed; Ares-financing-role-confirmed; holder-and-bank-role-unproven`

## What We Can Prove

| Evidence | Source | Number / Fact | Claim Effect |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Transaction value | Shell / PRNewswire and Jiffy/Shell web page | `1.3B USD` | Jiffy gives a hard acquisition denominator. |
| Buyer | Shell / Jiffy transaction pages | Monomoy Capital Partners affiliate | Confirms sponsor-backed acquisition context. |
| Ares role | Ares Q2 2026 direct-lending release | Joint lead arranger for a senior secured credit facility | Confirms private-credit financing-role evidence. |
| Operating footprint | Ares release | Approximately `19M` customers annually and more than `2,000` service centers | Shows this is a large consumer-service network. |
| PVA footprint | Shell / Jiffy transaction pages | PVA had over `360` locations across `20` states | Shows owned/franchisee operating-company scale inside the transaction. |

## What We Cannot Prove Yet

| Missing Item | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Funded debt amount | The `1.3B USD` transaction value is not the debt facility size. |
| Ares allocation | Ares says joint lead arranger, but not how much Ares funds held. |
| SEC holder fair value | No reliable Jiffy/PVA/Monomoy holder row has been captured yet. |
| Full lender group | Joint lead arranger language implies other lenders may exist. |
| Bank repayment | No captured source says bank debt was repaid, terminated, amended, or retained. |
| Use of proceeds beyond acquisition financing | The financing supported the acquisition, but debt allocation among purchase price, fees, liquidity, and working capital is undisclosed. |

## Holder-Search Result

Searches for these terms did not produce a clean current SEC holder row in the captured corpus:

- `Jiffy Lube`
- `Jiffy Lube International`
- `Premium Velocity Auto`
- `PVA`
- `Monomoy`

That does not prove no holder row exists. It means the current evidence set is not sufficient to extract a funded fair-value number.

## Claim Update

Jiffy strengthens this claim:

`Private credit is financing sponsor-backed acquisitions of large consumer-service networks.`

It does not yet support this stronger claim:

`We can observe the funded Jiffy debt in SEC-filed holder schedules.`

Completion of the sale does not change that holder-dollar boundary.

## Simple Version

Jiffy is a good transaction case, not yet a holder-dollar case.

We can show a `1.3B USD` sale to Monomoy and Ares' joint-lead-arranger role for acquisition financing.

We still cannot show the funded debt amount, Ares' allocation, or bank replacement.
