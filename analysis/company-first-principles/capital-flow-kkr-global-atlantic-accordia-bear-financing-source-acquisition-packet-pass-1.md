## Promotion controls

The `$17.419245M` interest-received field remains a statutory observation. It
cannot be promoted to borrower cash or platform profit until a settlement or
custodian record distinguishes cash received from accrual and identifies the
legal entity receiving it.

The `8.500%` stated rate and `6.999%` effective rate are instrument-row
observations. They are not a realized yield, spread, or return after liability
cost.

The issuer text `2023 Bear Financing L.P.` is source-visible identity evidence.
It is not yet a complete legal-instrument, guarantor, collateral, borrower-use,
or control-rights proof packet.

No amount from this row may be assigned to KKR parent cash, Global Atlantic
common-owner cash, or an owner-level residual.
