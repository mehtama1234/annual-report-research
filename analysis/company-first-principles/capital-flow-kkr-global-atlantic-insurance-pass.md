# Capital Flow KKR/Global Atlantic Insurance Pass

## Purpose

This pass extends RNQ-007 beyond Apollo/Athene:

`How broad is the insurance-private-assets mechanism across managers?`

The answer after this first KKR/Global Atlantic pass is:

`KKR gives a second manager-specific example of the mechanism: an insurance platform with large AUM, a large credit AUM component, reinsurance vehicles, and insurance operating earnings. This broadens the evidence beyond Apollo, but still does not prove the whole industry mechanism until Brookfield, Blackstone, and statutory/rating-agency rows are added.`

## Evidence To Claim

| Evidence | Claim It Can Carry |
|---|---|
| Global Atlantic had `220B USD` of AUM, including `164B USD` of credit AUM. | KKR has an insurance-linked capital channel with a large credit allocation. |
| Ivy and sponsored reinsurance vehicles had `62B USD` of AUM. | KKR is not only using owned insurance liabilities; it also has reinsurance-side vehicles around the insurance channel. |
| Insurance operating earnings were `288.220M USD` in Q2 2026. | The insurance channel is economically visible inside KKR's segment results. |
| KKR raised `34B USD` of new capital in the quarter and `133B USD` over the last twelve months. | KKR is a current capital-routing platform, not only a legacy asset owner. |
| KKR invested `24B USD` in the quarter and `104B USD` over the last twelve months. | Capital is being deployed from the platform into assets, but this row alone does not identify borrower destinations. |

## Better Claim

Old claim:

`Insurance and retirement liabilities are being converted into private-credit origination power.`

Better current claim:

`Apollo/Athene and KKR/Global Atlantic both show large insurance or retirement-linked capital channels with credit or alternative-asset exposure and visible earnings. The mechanism is now supported by more than one manager, but exact private-credit origination and borrower-destination linkage still needs deeper source work.`

## Simple Version

Apollo showed us the retirement-liability engine.

KKR/Global Atlantic shows the same kind of engine exists at another major platform: big insurance AUM, lots of credit AUM, reinsurance vehicles, and real earnings.

So the thesis is stronger than before, but still not finished. The next proof is not another headline AUM number; it is the bridge from insurance liabilities to exact credit assets and borrowers.
