## What This Adds

The earlier KKR pass showed `220B USD` of Global Atlantic AUM and `164B USD` of Global Atlantic credit AUM from the Q2 2026 earnings release.

The 10-Q adds a more balance-sheet-specific layer:

## Evidence To Claim

The 10-Q strengthens this claim:

`Global Atlantic is not only an AUM label inside KKR. It has a large insurance investment balance sheet with fixed maturity securities, mortgage and other loans, real assets, reinsurance-linked balances, investment income, credit allowances, and loan-performance disclosures.`

It also narrows the claim:

`Global Atlantic credit AUM is not automatically private credit. The 10-Q shows credit-heavy insurance assets, but still requires asset-class, statutory, and rating-agency detail to separate public credit, private credit, structured credit, real estate credit, and related-party exposure.`

## Claim To Evidence

| Stronger Claim | Missing Proof |
|---|---|
| Global Atlantic liabilities fund private-credit origination. | Same-period liability-to-asset bridge by asset class and origination source. |
| Global Atlantic is mainly private credit. | Public/private credit split, statutory schedules, and asset manager allocation tables. |
| KKR's insurance channel is bank-displacing. | Borrower-level credit agreements and bank denominator comparisons. |
| The insurance credit risk is contained. | Rating-agency reports, statutory capital ratios, ratings mix, below-investment-grade exposure, and impairments by asset class. |

## Simple Version

KKR/Global Atlantic now has the next proof layer:

`insurance AUM -> insurance investments -> investment categories -> income -> credit-quality indicators`

That is much stronger than only quoting AUM.

But it still does not prove:

`which exact liabilities funded which exact private-credit loans`

or:

`which bank loans were displaced`

Those still require statutory schedules, rating-agency reports, and borrower documents.
