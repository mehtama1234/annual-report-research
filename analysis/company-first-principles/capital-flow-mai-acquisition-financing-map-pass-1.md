# Capital Flow MAI Acquisition Financing Map Pass 1

## Purpose

This pass resolves `CFARQ-008`:

`Can MAI Capital's holder rows be tied to a specific acquisition timeline, continued M&A strategy, named facility purpose, full lender group, facility size, or bank-role change?`

## Resolution Outcome

`hold-after-pass-1 - wealth-management consolidation and holder/commitment evidence visible, acquisition-to-facility map incomplete`

MAI can be used as a bounded wealth-management consolidation borrower case. Ares identifies MAI Capital Management as a selected Q2 2026 direct-lending borrower tied to a continued M&A strategy, and SEC-filed schedules show MAI Capital Management Intermediate LLC exposure across ARCC, ASIF, New Mountain Private Credit Fund, and New Mountain Guardian IV.

Current evidence does not prove which acquisition or operating need was funded, total facility size, full lender group, Ares allocation, New Mountain allocation, prior bank repayment, bank termination, or post-close bank role.
