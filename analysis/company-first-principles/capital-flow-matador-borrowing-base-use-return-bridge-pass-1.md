## Current Answer

`Partly. Matador now has a source-use-output-cash bridge at company and borrowing-base level. The evidence ties a reserve-based revolving credit facility, a June 2026 borrowing-base reaffirmation, a 500M USD commitment increase, large first-half 2026 facility draws/repayments, unsecured note issuance and retirement, development capex, acquisition cash use, operating cash flow, and production/revenue output. ## Decision

`source-use-output-cash-bridge-visible-borrowing-base-level`

This is an upgrade from:

`denominator-visible-project-open`

The upgrade is narrow. Matador now has agreement-grade borrowing-base evidence and same-period use/output/cash evidence. The missing step is attribution: which draws or notes funded which acquisitions, wells, midstream assets, or debt retirements, and what those assets earned.

## Why This Matters

Matador is now the cleanest debt/refinancing case for reserve-backed bank credit. Wheaton is the cleanest named-use streaming case. Together they show that the debt/refinancing lane is not one thing:

- Wheaton tests contract-funded stream economics.
- Matador tests reserve-backed bank financing and acquisition/development use.
- PBF, Devon, and Liberty Broadband still need more allocation work before source/use/output can be separated.

## Hypothesis

`Matador is likely a bank-led reserve-backed acquisition/development financing bridge, not a private-credit displacement case. The current evidence supports a borrowing-base-level source/use/output/cash bridge, but the operating-return claim depends on reserve quality, asset-level production, acquisition contribution, and cash margin.`

## Safe Claim

`Matador's Q2 2026 evidence ties reserve-based bank credit, a 3.25B USD borrowing base, a 2.75B USD elected commitment, 2.154B USD of first-half Credit Agreement draws, 1.613B USD of Credit Agreement repayments, 750M USD of senior note proceeds, 1.407674B USD of operating cash flow, 745.343M USD of drilling/completion/equipping capex, 1.228834B USD of oil-and-gas property acquisitions, and Q2 production/revenue output. This supports a borrowing-base-level source/use/output/cash bridge, not asset-level return proof.`
