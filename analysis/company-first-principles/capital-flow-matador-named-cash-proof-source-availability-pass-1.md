## What We Can Say

Matador has public-route evidence for:

- official BLM sale-results source family
- named Federal lease acquisition inventory
- reserve-based credit facility capacity
- lender-evaluated reserve-report mechanics
- collateral/title/proceeds mechanics
- aggregate source/use reconciliation
- lease-sale borrowing repayment clue

That is enough to tell a source/use story with boundaries.

It is not enough to prove named cash.

## What We Still Need

Matador reaches named cash proof only if the next source package joins:

1. BLM parcel-level award and payment records for the Matador leases
2. BLM or Matador closing funds-flow and settlement statement
3. Credit Agreement borrowing and repayment notices around the lease sale
4. lender schedule and elected-commitment allocation
5. populated borrowing-base certificate and reserve-redetermination package
6. reserve report or engineering deck values for the acquired acreage
7. asset-area drilling, completion, and production records
8. LOE, realized pricing, cash margin, EBITDA, OCF, debt service, IRR, NPV, and payback
