The core question is:

`When large pools of money move through private credit, insurance, infrastructure, public debt, asset-backed finance, and bank facilities, where do they actually land in the real economy, what do they fund, and what can we prove versus merely infer?`

## Answer Standard

Each answer has to identify:

- the current answer
- the confidence level
- the open question
- the working hypothesis
- the evidence needed
- the next artifact
- the overclaim boundary

This keeps the research useful without pretending the full mechanism is already proven.

## Overclaim Boundaries

Do not say:

- every funded exposure is new productive capacity
- a holder vehicle proves the ultimate source of funds
- vehicle exposure proves the full source-of-capital chain
- borrower financing necessarily funds organic growth
- capex, backlog, or AUM is operating output without conversion metrics
- partial borrower evidence proves broad bank displacement
- AUM or deployment proves borrower allocation
- mixed-period rows can be summed as current exposure
- hypotheses are conclusions before the evidence level changes
- the case-study set represents the full `519` company universe

## Current Answer

`The research now supports a serious intermediate capital-flow thesis: capital is moving through distinct financial wrappers into identifiable borrowers, assets, collateral pools, projects, and operating systems. The full source-to-use-to-output-to-bank-displacement chain remains incomplete and needs facility-size, use-of-proceeds, source-of-capital, bank-role, denominator, and durability evidence.`
