## What We Can Say Now

The useful answer is no longer vague.

`Capital is reaching identifiable real-economy borrowers, assets, and operating systems through distinct financial wrappers. The visible uses are mixed: acquisition finance, consolidation support, continued-growth facilities, incremental commitments, selected bank-credit repayment or termination, fleet/collateral funding, energy infrastructure, grid/power assets, and backlog execution.`

That is meaningful, but it is still an intermediate answer.

## What We Cannot Say Yet

Do not say:

- the full source-to-use-to-output chain is proven for every case
- private credit is broadly replacing banks
- holder fair value equals total borrower facility size
- transaction value equals debt size
- ultimate investor source is known just because the vehicle is known
- capex, backlog, or funding capacity automatically equals operating output
- the case-study pattern is source-proven or representative across all `519` companies

## Highest-Value Partial Answer

The bank-role question is the most valuable partial answer.

Current status:

`Specific bank-facility takeout can be proven in selected cases, but broad bank replacement remains unproven.`

The next evidence has to show whether banks disappeared, stayed as revolver lenders, stayed as administrative agents, supported collateral facilities, or retained treasury/hedging roles.
