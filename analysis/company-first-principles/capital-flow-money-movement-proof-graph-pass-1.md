## What This Adds

The earlier synthesis answered where the money appears to move. This proof graph adds the missing beneficiary question:

`who gets paid back if the cash channel is real?`

That beneficiary is still bounded. In most cases, the current record supports company-level or category-level payback logic rather than asset-level return proof.

## Decision

`six-case-money-movement-proof-graph-ready`

The first six executed source tests are now represented as explicit proof chains. This is the clearest current answer to the user's operating question, but it remains a proof graph with holds, not a final investment-return thesis.

## Safe Claim

`The six-case money-movement proof graph can explain who controls capital, where it moves, what it funds, what output or cash proxy appears, who would benefit from that cash channel, and which source would be needed next. It does not prove full asset/project cash return for Wheaton, FPL, URI, Cheniere, Sterling, or Energy Transfer.`
