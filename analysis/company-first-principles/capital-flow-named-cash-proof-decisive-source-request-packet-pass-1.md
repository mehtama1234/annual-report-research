## Acquisition Rule

Do not promote a case because it has:

1. aggregate revenue
2. company EBITDA
3. backlog
4. capex
5. production or throughput
6. borrowing capacity
7. management commentary
8. a legal route without populated schedules

Those are route and proxy evidence only.

## Promotion Rule

A case can move to named-cash proof only when one named row joins:

1. money source
2. legal instrument or channel
3. named destination
4. cash use
5. receipt, billing, settlement, borrower cash, or project cash
6. payback, debt waterfall, collateral availability, or legal-entity schedule
7. return model, earned return, IRR, NPV, ROIC, or payback
