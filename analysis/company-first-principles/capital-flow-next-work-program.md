## The Main Research Question

The big question is:

`Is private credit taking share from banks in specific borrower lanes, or is it mostly adding a parallel credit channel beside still-growing bank credit?`

The answer has to be built from borrower documents, holder schedules, capital-source documents, and bank denominators.

## Priority Lanes

| Priority | Lane | Next Output | Acceptance Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bank replacement proof | Bank role after private credit pass | Promote displacement language only when a named document shows repayment, termination, amendment, or full lender replacement. |
| 2 | SEC holder expansion | Borrower SEC holder crosswalks | Promote holder-breadth claims only when multiple filing vehicles disclose matching borrower rows or matching pricing/maturity terms. |
| 3 | Facility-size reconstruction | Atwell facility-size reconstruction table | Do not infer total facility size from partial holder rows. Use explicit total facility disclosure or clearly label coverage as partial. |
| 4 | Capital source to vehicle | Capital source to vehicle bridge pass | Tie funding source and borrower holding in the same period before claiming source-to-destination flow. |

| 8 | Claim strength governance | Capital-flow claim strength scorecard | Every claim gets a score, evidence type, boundary, and next disproof test. |

## Claim Strength Rubric

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | unsupported or contradicted | The broad claim is not supported, or available evidence points the other way. |
| 2 | secondary-source clue | Useful direction, but not enough for a primary-source-backed claim. |
| 3 | primary-source trail | Named documents support the transaction path, but the decisive proof is missing. |
| 4 | primary-source amount or holder evidence | SEC filings or company documents give actual dollars, holdings, or repayment language. |
| 5 | confirmed mechanism | A primary document proves the mechanism, such as bank-facility termination or repayment. |
