# Capital Flow Operating Conversion Scorecard Pass 1

## Purpose

This pass resolves `CFARQ-011`:

`Does United Rentals prove that capital absorbed into fleet and collateral channels converts into durable operating output, rather than only balance-sheet capacity or capex?`

The operating table is:

`analysis/company-first-principles/data/capital-flow-operating-conversion-scorecard-pass-1.csv`

## Resolution Outcome

`upgrade-with-boundary - repeated fleet revenue, productivity, cash conversion, and collateral proxies visible; true fleet ROIC and legal availability still unproven`

URI passes the operating-conversion threshold at proxy level. The existing URI work shows a repeated pattern from Q1 2024 through Q2 2026: rental revenue growth, positive fleet productivity, positive average OEC growth, gross and net rental-equipment investment, operating cash flow coverage, owned-rental revenue mix, specialty segment trend, segment asset-return proxy, public collateral support, and instrument-level Yak acquisition funding.

The upgrade is bounded. URI still does not prove asset-level fleet ROIC, true utilization by asset class, rate/time/mix decomposition, growth-versus-replacement capex, legal ABL availability, or exact source-of-funds allocation to specific fleet purchases.

## What This Answers

The URI evidence now answers the operating-conversion question better than most borrower cases:

`Capital is not merely sitting in financial wrappers. In URI's case, capital is absorbed into reusable rental fleet, receivables, collateralized funding channels, and acquisition funding, while the public record shows repeated revenue, productivity, cash conversion, equipment recycling, and segment economics.`

This is still a proxy answer, not a full asset-level return model.

## Why URI Matters To The Meta Question

URI is useful because it shows a different mechanism than the private-credit borrower cases:

`capital markets debt + ABL + AR securitization + fleet recycling -> reusable rental equipment and matting capacity -> rental revenue, specialty mix, fleet productivity, operating cash flow, and collateral support`

That mechanism is closer to operating-output proof than a BDC holder row. It shows how funding wrappers can connect to physical capacity and cash generation.
