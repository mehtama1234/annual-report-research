# PBF Q2 post-redemption liquidity boundary

Research date: `2026-09-17`

The PBF Q2 2026 Form 10-Q materially improves the refinancing evidence chain.
It confirms that all `$801.6M` of 2028 6.00% notes were redeemed on June 25,
2026, funded with net proceeds from the `$500.0M` 2034 7.25% note issuance and
available cash. As of June 30, PBF reported operational liquidity of more than
`$3.5B`, consisting of more than `$800M` cash and approximately `$2.7B` of
borrowing availability under the revolving credit facility.

## What is now observed

| Object | Observation | Proof grade | Remaining boundary |
|---|---:|---|---|
| New notes | `$500.0M` principal; `$492.1M` net proceeds in the Q2 10-Q | Source/use visible | Detailed fee and purchaser allocation remain unavailable |
| Old notes | `$801.6M` aggregate principal redeemed June 25, 2026 | Completed redemption visible | Trustee payment and holder distribution are not disclosed |
| Funding direction | New-note proceeds plus available cash funded redemption | Issuer source/use visible | Exact cash-on-hand amount and source priority remain open |
| Post-redemption liquidity | More than `$3.5B` operational liquidity: more than `$800M` cash and approximately `$2.7B` revolver availability | Period-matched liquidity visible | Borrowing-base composition, restricted cash, and refinery-level availability remain separate questions |
| Revolver activity | H1 statement shows `$1.100B` borrowings and `$1.200B` repayments | Financing movement visible | No account-level draw/paydown ledger or source-priority waterfall |
| Accrued interest | Redemption price was principal plus accrued interest through the redemption date | Contractual obligation visible | Accrued-interest dollar amount and payment record remain missing |

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

- Do not treat the `$3.5B+` operational-liquidity figure as refinancing NPV,
  refinery owner cash, or unrestricted cash without the borrowing-base,
  restricted-cash, and subsidiary waterfall joins.
- Do not treat the `$11.846M` simple annual coupon difference from the earlier
  bridge as value creation before fees, accrued interest, taxes, cash
  opportunity cost, revolver movement, and post-redemption liquidity are
  reconciled.
- Do not treat the completed redemption as proof that the financing was
  economically accretive; it proves settlement at the issuer/source-use level,
  not refinery-level return.

## Decision

`pbf-completed-redemption-and-period-liquidity-visible; trustee-accrued-interest-and-refinery-return-open`

The next decisive objects remain the trustee redemption statement, accrued-
interest calculation, detailed fee/tax treatment, borrowing-base availability,
and refinery-level cash contribution. The Q2 filing is a new primary source
that changes the liquidity boundary, so the route should not be classified as
an unresolved announcement; it also should not be promoted to refinancing NPV
or common-owner cash.
