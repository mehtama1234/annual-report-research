## What Moved

The FPL evidence moved from `regulated-return-proxy` to a stronger recovery bridge:

- `PSC-2026-0022-S-EI` provides the commission-order anchor.
- The settlement term runs from January 1, 2026 through at least December 31, 2029.
- Rates and cost-recovery clauses use a `10.95%` ROE, within a `9.95%` to `11.95%` authorized range.
- The regulatory capital structure includes a `59.6%` equity ratio.
- Settlement base-revenue steps are `$945M` for 2026 and `$705M` for 2027, compared with the as-filed request baseline of about `$1.55B` and `$927M`.
- Solar and battery base-rate adjustment mechanics are visible for solar projects entering service in 2027-2029 and battery projects entering service in 2028-2029.
- The SPPCRC filing adds a `859.244393M USD` 2026 total jurisdictional revenue-requirement request and `873.977263M USD` projected program revenue-requirement line before true-up.

NextEra Energy Resources backlog quality also improved:

- Q1 2026 SEC exhibit shows `4 GW` of new renewables/storage backlog additions, including `1.3 GW` of battery storage origination.
- FPL placed about `600 MW` of new solar into service and had more than `8.5 GW` of owned and operated solar.
- The Meta release gives named customer evidence: `2.5 GW` of clean-energy contracts, `11` PPAs, `2` ESAs, `2.1 GW` through `9` solar projects across ERCOT/SPP/MISO, plus `190 MW` solar and `168 MW` battery storage through PNM Rate 36B.
- The Meta package covers `13` projects scheduled online between 2026 and 2028.
