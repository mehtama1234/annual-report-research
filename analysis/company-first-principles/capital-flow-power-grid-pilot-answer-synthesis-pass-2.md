- PSC order anchor: `PSC-2026-0022-S-EI`
- settlement term from January 1, 2026 through at least December 31, 2029
- `10.95%` ROE used for rates
- authorized ROE range of `9.95%` to `11.95%`
- `59.6%` equity ratio
- settlement base-revenue steps of `$945M` in 2026 and `$705M` in 2027
- SoBRA mechanics for solar and battery projects
- SPPCRC 2026 jurisdictional revenue requirement of `$859.244393M`
- SPPCRC category rows including Distribution Feeder Hardening actual/estimated amount of `$334.058644M`
- Distribution Inspection 2025 expenditures of `$38.500000M`, additions to plant of `$58.131189M`, and recoverable expenses of `$16.835584M`
- Transmission Inspection 2025 expenditures of `$48.373799M`, additions to plant of `$54.672380M`, and recoverable expenses of `$22.954303M`
- 2025 actual/estimated SPPCRC under-recovery of `$7.172014M` including interest
- Form 5P 2026 projected RS1/RTR1 SPP factor of `0.00869 USD/kWh`
- 2026 projection WACC total weighted cost of `7.0891%` and pre-tax total weighted cost of `8.9464%`
- 2025 final net true-up over-recovery of `$16.579976M` including interest
- final 2025 clause revenues of `$804.620369M` and total jurisdictional revenue requirements of `$729.233535M`

## Mechanism Hypothesis

The pilot now suggests a sharper mechanism:

`Power/grid capital becomes evidence-grade when it moves into one of four channels: a regulatory recovery channel, a named customer contract channel, a tariffed movement channel, or a physical-output channel with cash conversion.`

The open question is whether any channel can be completed end to end.

## Three-Target Workbench

The next pass should attempt one complete chain in each of the strongest channels:

| Target | Chain To Test | Pass Standard |
|---|---|---|
| FPL recovery category | capital category -> PSC/MFR/tariff schedule -> approved revenue requirement -> allowed return -> timing | recovery-grade if project/category dollars and recovery terms are explicit |
| NextEra/Meta projects | customer -> PPA/ESA -> project/MW -> online date -> financing/capex -> economics | contract-grade if price/term/customer/project economics are visible |
| Plains Cactus III route | tariff route -> committed/uncommitted rate -> shipper/term -> volume -> revenue -> acquisition/capex base | commercial-grade if committed volume or realized tariff revenue is visible |
