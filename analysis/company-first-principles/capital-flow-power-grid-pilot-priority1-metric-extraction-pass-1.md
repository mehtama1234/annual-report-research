## What Passed

The strongest promotions are narrow:

- MasTec now has segment backlog rows for Clean Energy and Infrastructure and Power Delivery.
- Plains now has a named Cactus III capacity expansion tied to the growth-capital program.

## What Failed Promotion

The project-return standard still fails on the most important fields:

- ONEOK: aggregate capex and percentage volume growth do not identify project economics.
- MasTec: aggregate/segment backlog does not prove funded status, margin, or cash recovery.
- Plains: Cactus III capacity and synergy targets do not prove shipper/tariff durability or return.
- Targa: record volumes and segment margins do not allocate contribution to Train 11, Delaware Express, East Driver, or other named assets.
- NextEra: FPL capital employed and Energy Resources backlog do not substitute for docket/order/rider and project contract economics.
