## What This Lets Us Answer

The answer is now sharper:

But the documents mostly stop one step before project-level return: they show capex, backlog, capacity, volume, segment margin, and regulated/development backlog, not complete project economics.`

## What Failed To Upgrade

The common blockers are:

- project cost is not tied to named asset contribution
- backlog is not tied to funded status, margin, or cash conversion by segment
- capacity additions are not tied to shipper, tariff, customer, or PPA economics
- FPL capital employed is not tied to docket/order/rider recovery at project level
- Energy Resources backlog is not tied to customer, contract price, financing, and in-service economics
