## Short Answer

The first five-company pilot is not blocked by lack of interesting evidence. It is blocked by four repeatable missing joins:

1. project capex to named asset
2. named asset to output or backlog conversion
3. output/backlog to cash, margin, tariff, contract, or recovery mechanism
4. cash/recovery to durability and downside controls

This queue adds `20` proof tasks, `4` per company, with pass tests, fail tests, source targets, and promotion outcomes.

## Working Hypotheses

1. ONEOK will likely clear output-denominator evidence before project-return evidence because integrated optimization and acquisitions blur asset-level contribution.
2. MasTec may become the best scalable backlog-quality template if segment backlog, margins, and working-capital conversion can be extracted cleanly.
3. Plains may be easier to upgrade through capital-stack durability than project return because the Canadian NGL sale and debt reduction are clearer than Cactus III economics.
4. Targa should have strong project-output evidence, but return proof will depend on whether supplements disclose utilization or contribution by project rather than only record aggregate volumes.
5. NextEra must be handled as two related but separate mechanisms: FPL regulated recovery and Energy Resources contracted development.
