### Plains Cactus III

Plains is the best tariffed movement candidate because the record includes:

- Texas RRC tariff `TX 3.6.0`, effective July 1, 2026
- Karnes County to Cactus III Crude Pipeline Hobson Terminal route
- `178.98` cents-per-barrel base rate
- negotiated-rate mechanic for term-agreement shippers
- FERC-index escalation mechanic
- over `600,000` bpd Cactus III capacity
- acquisition consideration, debt assumed, expansion earnouts, property/equipment acquired, and operator-of-record evidence

The failure point is shipper/cash proof: no Cactus III-specific committed shipper, committed volume, realized average tariff, EBITDA contribution, or debt-service row is extracted.
