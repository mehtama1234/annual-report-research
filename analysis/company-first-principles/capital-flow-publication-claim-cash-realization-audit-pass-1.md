`Yes, with boundaries. All 14 publication-ready claims remain usable if their existing proof limits are carried forward. ## Key Finding

The publication layer is mostly sound because it already uses bounded language. The risk is not that the existing approved sentences are false. The risk is that readers or downstream drafts could over-upgrade them.

The main forbidden upgrade is:

`aggregate cash, authorized recovery, holder fair value, backlog, cargo count, OCF, or AUM -> asset-level cash return`

That jump is not allowed unless a same-period source ties cash to the named asset, category, borrower, project, or facility.

## Required Writing Rule

Any external paragraph using these claims must carry:

- proof level
- evidence route
- period and amount when numerical
- whether cash is direct, aggregate, proxy, or not claimed
- what is still not proven

## Decision

`publication-claims-cash-realization-audited`

The publication register remains usable, but the cash-realization audit becomes the control layer for external writing. It prevents publication language from turning recovery mechanisms, output metrics, holder rows, backlog, or aggregate cash into asset-level cash-return claims.

## Safe Claim

`The capital-flow publication layer is usable with cash-realization boundaries. The strongest claims are mechanism and proxy claims, not universal source-to-use-to-cash-return claims.`
