`Use the strongest sentence that the evidence actually supports, and carry the boundary in the same paragraph.`

## Forbidden Shortcuts

Do not say:

- the full capital-flow chain is proven across all sectors
- private credit is broadly replacing banks
- visible BDC fair value equals total borrower debt
- all insurance assets are private credit
- AI demand has already created fully funded in-service power assets everywhere
- every utility capex dollar is recoverable at attractive returns
- ET and Cheniere are project-and-cash-grade for every named asset
- all URI fleet capex is high-return growth capex
- Sterling's backlog is fully customer-funded and risk-free
- funding type is proven for every company
- capex, backlog, AUM, revolver size, L/C capacity, or cargo counts equal productive investment by themselves
- a few company examples prove a market-wide capital shift
- one quarter proves durability
- the `519` company universe is source-proven or representative of the final thesis

## Required Use Conditions

Every external paragraph should carry at least three controls:

1. Evidence route: name the artifact or source family.
2. Proof level: say whether it is platform-scale, holder-visible, agreement-term-visible, proxy-grade, denominator-tested, or project-and-cash-grade.
3. Boundary: state what is not proven.

For numerical claims, also carry:

- amount
- period
- row type
- source or vehicle
- whether the number is same-period, mixed-period, derived, or partial
