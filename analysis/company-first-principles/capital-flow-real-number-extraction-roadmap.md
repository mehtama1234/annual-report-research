| Private credit fully excluded banks from Auctane or Medallia. | Final credit agreements are needed to see revolvers, administrative roles, hedging, treasury services, and arranger/agent positions. | The whole private-credit market is software. | Software is a top visible lane, but healthcare, services, financials, insurance, consumer, capital goods, and materials are also large. |
| All capital-flow beneficiaries are asset managers. | The operating-company side still needs current 10-Q/10-K evidence for utilities, suppliers, internet infrastructure, and healthcare operators. |

## Annual And Quarterly Report Fields

For every company report, extract the same minimum fields:

| Field | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Period and filing date | Prevents mixing stale annual facts with current quarterly facts. |
| Segment or business line | Keeps private credit, insurance, infrastructure, real estate, wealth, and operating revenue separate. |
| Metric name and value | Makes the claim numerically auditable. |
| Units | Avoids mixing USD millions, USD billions, percentages, MW, GWh, patient count, and backlog. |
| Source location | Page, table, exhibit, worksheet, or text marker. |
| Claim effect | Says whether the number proves, narrows, weakens, or contradicts the claim. |
| Denominator needed | Forces broad market claims to use Fed, FDIC, EIA, FERC, CMS, ISO, or similar outside data. |

## Next Deep Question

The next meaty question should be:

`Are private-credit platforms taking share from banks in specific borrower lanes, or are they mainly adding leverage beside still-growing bank credit?`

The answer requires three linked tables:

| Table | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| BDC/manager destination table | Which industries and borrowers receive private-credit dollars. |
| Borrower transaction table | Whether the use of proceeds was acquisition financing, refinancing, bank-credit repayment, dividend recap, rescue capital, or restructuring. |
| Bank denominator table | Whether commercial-bank credit is shrinking, growing, or merely losing share in those exact lanes. |

## Simple Version

We should not just collect reports.

We should collect numbers that can change a claim.

The useful loop is:

`read filing -> extract number -> attach it to claim -> ask what would disprove it -> revise the thesis`

The companion derived loop is:

`extracted number -> formula -> implied ratio or dollars -> safer claim -> next disproof test`
