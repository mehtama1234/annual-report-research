# Capital Flow Relation Holder To Facility Bridge Pass 1

## Purpose

This pass resolves `CFARQ-006`:

`Can the Relation Insurance holder and commitment rows be tied to a specific acquisition-finance facility, use of proceeds, facility size, Ares allocation, or bank-replacement claim?`

## Resolution Outcome

`hold-after-pass-1 - acquisition-finance and holder/commitment evidence visible, row-to-facility bridge incomplete`

Relation can be used as evidence that private credit reaches an insurance-brokerage consolidation platform. Ares identified a senior secured credit facility supporting BayPine's acquisition of Relation Insurance, Latham identified a financing source representation for the same acquisition, BayPine confirmed the acquisition context, and SEC-filed Goldman Sachs vehicles reported AQ Sunshine / Relation debt or commitments.

Current evidence does not prove that the Goldman Sachs holder rows are the same BayPine/Ares facility, nor does it prove total facility size, Ares allocation, use-of-proceeds split, bank repayment, bank termination, or full lender group.

## 2026-09-18 targeted official-source recheck

The targeted recheck found no promotion-grade document beyond the existing
route. Ares's official Q2 2026 origination release again identifies itself as
joint lead arranger and joint bookrunner for a senior secured facility
supporting BayPine's acquisition. BayPine's acquisition announcement confirms
the sponsor transaction but says financial terms were not disclosed. The
public SEC search also surfaces older AQ Sunshine / Relation holder rows and a
current 2033 first-lien marker, but those holder observations do not identify
the acquisition facility, lender allocation, borrower receipt, or bank payoff.

This recheck strengthens the dated transaction chronology only; it does not
change the `hold-after-pass-1` resolution.

## Safe Answer

`Relation Insurance is a bounded acquisition-finance borrower case. It shows private credit transaction-role evidence plus SEC-filed holder and commitment evidence for an insurance-brokerage consolidation platform. The current same-period holder-visible number is 4.5480M USD of Q2 2026 GSBD fair value plus 1.1960M USD of Q2 2026 GSBD unfunded commitment. The larger 77.5410M USD visible commitment trail is mixed-quarter and should not be treated as drawn debt.`

## Do Not Claim

- Do not claim the Goldman Sachs rows are definitively the BayPine/Ares facility.
- Do not claim the full Relation facility size.
- Do not claim Ares' exact funded amount.
- Do not claim broad bank replacement.
- Do not combine mixed-quarter commitments and same-period funded fair value as a single current exposure number.
