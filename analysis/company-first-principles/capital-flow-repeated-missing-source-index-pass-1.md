## Short Answer

`The largest proof blockers are not company names. They are repeated source families: customer receipt/billing support, source-to-use allocation, project or asset cash contribution, debt-service waterfalls, borrower facility documents, statutory investment schedules, contract pricing, physical-output attribution, collateral certificates, working-capital collection, acquisition funds-flow, and return models. These are the source packages needed to turn money-route evidence into cash-return proof.`
