## What Actually Moved

The strongest upgrades are not final proof. They are proof-boundary improvements:

- PBF and Liberty now have stronger transaction and debt/payback mechanics.
- Devon has statement-level treasury source/use reconciliation.
- Matador has borrowing-base and aggregate source/use context, but not reserve-backed asset return.
- Cheniere has contract/output evidence, but not train-level cash waterfall.
- URI has the cleanest fleet operating proxy, but not fleet-class ROIC or legal availability.
- FPL has the best regulated recovery bridge, but not category customer receipts or earned return.
- Sterling and MasTec remain backlog/conversion proxy cases, not project cash-collection proof.
- KKR/Global Atlantic and Apollo/Athene remain below statutory legal-entity investment proof.
- Plains remains tariff/acquisition context, not route-revenue or asset-return proof.
