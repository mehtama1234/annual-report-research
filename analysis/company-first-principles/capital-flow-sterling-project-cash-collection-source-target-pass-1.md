## What This Proves

Sterling is a strong project-execution cash proxy:

`customer/project demand -> backlog/RPO -> contract assets and liabilities -> revenue -> operating cash after capex -> revolver/L/C/surety support`

That is not the same as proving:

`named owner funding -> signed project contract -> Sterling guarantee or credit usage -> project margin -> cash collection -> project return`

## Decision

`sterling-project-cash-collection-source-target-executed-local-boundary`

The Sterling graph row can stay at company-level project-execution cash-proxy grade. It cannot be promoted to project cash collection proof.

## Safe Claim

`Sterling shows a strong company-level backlog-to-cash conversion mechanism: RPO more than doubled across the observed period, Q2 2026 net contract-liability timing was 646.306M USD, quarterly and H1 operating cash flow after capex were positive, debt was low relative to RPO, and the July 2026 credit wrapper added 1.500B USD of revolver capacity with a 600M USD L/C sublimit. The evidence supports project-execution cash-proxy language, not named project-owner funding, project margin, actual L/C usage, bonded backlog, cash collection, or project-return proof.`
