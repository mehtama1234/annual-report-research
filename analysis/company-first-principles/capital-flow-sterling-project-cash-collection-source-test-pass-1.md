## Short Answer

`No full upgrade yet. Sterling has a strong backlog-to-company-cash and credit-wrapper bridge, but current local evidence still does not prove named project-owner funding, project margin, retainage collection, actual L/C usage, bonded backlog, or project return.`

The local evidence does answer the money-movement question at project-execution proxy level. Customer/project demand enters Sterling through backlog and RPO. Contract assets and liabilities show billing/performance timing. Revenue and operating cash flow after capex show company-level conversion. A larger July `2026` credit wrapper adds liquidity and guarantee capacity. Bonding and surety mechanics explain why a contractor with large backlog needs contingent support. Acquired-versus-organic backlog evidence keeps the backlog expansion from being overstated as purely organic.

The strongest visible data points are RPO growth from `2055.081M USD` in Q3 `2024` to `4233.618M USD` in Q2 `2026`, Q2 `2026` contract liabilities of `802.601M USD`, Q2 `2026` contract assets of `156.295M USD`, Q2 `2026` net contract-liability timing of `646.306M USD`, Q2 `2026` OCF less capex of `112.436M USD`, H1 `2026` OCF less capex of `258.375M USD`, Q2 `2026` debt/RPO of `6.7%`, a July `2026` `1.500B USD` revolver, `600M USD` L/C sublimit, `50M USD` swing-line, `8.0x` L/C sublimit increase, L/C capacity equal to `40.0%` of the revolver, bid-bond range of `5%` to `10%`, performance/payment bond potential up to `100%` of construction cost, `74.0%` of RPO expected within twelve months, `22.9%` unsigned-award share of combined backlog, `2.56B USD` acquired signed-plus-unsigned contribution, `45.6%` acquired share of combined backlog, `250.8M USD` Q2 acquisition revenue contribution, Q2 total debt of `285.305M USD`, Q2 cash of `464.451M USD`, and total debt about `0.32x` the FY `2026` adjusted EBITDA guide midpoint.

That is still not project cash-collection proof. The current local source set lacks named owner funding, signed project contracts tied to owner budgets, project margin, retainage aging and collection, receivable collection by project, bonded backlog, actual post-reset L/C outstanding, surety exposure by project, source-to-project allocation, and project return.
