# Capital Flow Theme Evidence Program

## Purpose

This page turns the theme/subtheme map into an execution program for gathering real numbers.

The operating data file is:

`analysis/company-first-principles/data/capital-flow-theme-evidence-program.csv`

This program sits between the broad scan and the deep proof passes:

`519-company radar -> 190 high-signal rows -> 71 lane candidates -> theme evidence program -> primary-source extraction -> claim-grade thesis`

## What This Adds

The previous work answered three important questions:

| Question | Current Answer |
|---|---|
| What is the broad universe? | `519` company packets. |
| Where are the high-signal capital-flow candidates? | `190` high-signal rows. |
| Which companies should become primary-source queues? | `71` lane candidates across seven themes. |

This page answers the next question:

`For each theme and subtheme, what exact reports, numbers, outside datasets, and disproof tests should we pull next?`

## Core Research Loop

The work must keep moving in both directions.

Claim to evidence:

`theme claim -> subtheme -> company set -> document target -> number -> acceptance rule -> claim decision`

Evidence to claim:

`filing number -> ratio or comparison -> bounded claim -> missing proof -> next source`

The reason to do both is simple: the filings should not merely decorate a thesis we already like. They should also generate new claims and weaken bad ones.

## Theme-Level Detail

### TEP-001: Private-Credit Platforms, Direct Lending At Scale

Companies:

`Ares; KKR; Apollo; Blackstone; BlackRock; Carlyle; Brookfield`

Documents:

- annual reports
- quarterly reports
- quarterly supplements
- BDC schedules
- direct-lending releases
- lender transaction notes
- rating reports where accessible

Numbers to extract:

- total AUM
- fee-paying AUM
- credit AUM
- direct-lending AUM
- gross deployment
- originations
- available capital
- number of transactions
- borrower count
- portfolio fair value

What the numbers can prove:

If platform-scale metrics and borrower/fund schedule evidence appear in the same period, we can say these platforms are routing committed capital into real borrower loans.

What they cannot prove alone:

They do not prove bank displacement. AUM and deployment prove scale, not replacement. Replacement needs borrower documents showing repayment, termination, amendment, or lender change.

What the numbers can prove:

### TEP-008: Debt Refinancing And Facility Change

Companies:

`PBF; Liberty Broadband; Matador; Devon; Coterra; CNX; Ovintiv; Cenovus; Enhabit; Wheaton`

Documents:

- 10-K
- 10-Q
- 8-K
- indenture
- credit agreement
- borrowing-base redetermination
- bond prospectus
- rating report

Numbers to extract:

- old principal
- new principal
- coupon or spread
- maturity
- revolver capacity
- drawn amount
- collateral
- repayment use
- liquidity before and after

Claim test:

This lane can show whether capital is moving risk through time, changing lenders, changing collateral, or relieving balance-sheet pressure.

Disproof:

If the refinancing is routine and does not change risk, lender mix, maturity pressure, or liquidity, it should not carry a broad capital-flow claim.

### TEP-009: Acquisition Finance And Consolidation

Companies:

`Core & Main; Verizon; Sherwin-Williams; Lowe's; Rio Tinto; Expand Energy; Packaging Corp.; Silgan; Phillips 66; Roblox`

Numbers to extract:

- transaction value
- cash paid
- debt issued
- equity issued
- bridge loan
- target debt assumed
- target debt repaid
- acquired revenue
- acquired sites/assets/reserves

Claim test:

M&A becomes a capital-flow story when documents show what money bought, what funding instrument paid for it, and how control or capacity changed.

Disproof:

The claim weakens if the deal is immaterial, all-cash without broader funding signal, or unrelated to control, capacity, customers, routes, reserves, or consolidation.
