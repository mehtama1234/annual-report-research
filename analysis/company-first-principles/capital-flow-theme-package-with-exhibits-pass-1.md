## Executive Summary

The research shows that large asset managers and financial platforms are acting as capital-routing systems.

The central idea is simple:

`large pools of money -> platforms -> legal wrappers -> credit/assets/projects/borrowers -> cash-back evidence`

The important point is not only that BlackRock, Blackstone, Apollo/Athene, KKR/Global Atlantic, Brookfield, Ares, and Carlyle are large. The important point is that these platforms organize money from pensions, insurance liabilities, retirement savings, institutions, wealth clients, lenders, and public markets, then route it into credit assets, infrastructure, utilities, real assets, private borrowers, asset-backed pools, project vehicles, and operating companies.

The strongest current evidence is not a broad AUM statistic. It is the KKR/Global Atlantic / Accordia statutory proof stack:

| Evidence | Amount / Result |
|---|---:|
| Coordinate-owned Schedule D bond book | `7.318321094B USD` |
| Variance versus statutory bond base | `-1,069 USD` |
| Statutory interest received on owned bond rows | `229.507957M USD` |
| Interest received / legal-entity gross investment income | `37.930604%` |
| Interest received / owned-bond book | `3.136074%` |
| Top interest rows tested for same-CUSIP events | `40` rows |
| Same-CUSIP acquisition/disposal event rows | `10` rows |

| Coordinate-extracted disposal consideration for selected rows | `1.552579M USD` |

What this means:

`We can document a real legal entity, named bond holdings, tight book-value reconciliation, statutory interest received, and selected disposal/proceeds columns. That is strong capital-routing and cash-back proxy evidence. It is not yet full source-to-return proof.`

### What This Exhibit Proves

1. Accordia is a real legal entity in the Global Atlantic proof route.
2. Accordia held named Schedule D bond assets.
3. The owned-bond book is near-reconciled to the statutory filing.
4. The owned-bond rows include statutory interest received.
5. Selected same-CUSIP disposal rows include coordinate-extracted consideration and gain/loss fields.

### What This Exhibit Does Not Prove

1. It does not prove original policyholder-dollar allocation to each asset.
2. It does not prove borrower use of proceeds.
3. It does not prove bank or custodian settlement.
4. It does not prove trustee remittance.
5. It does not prove funds-held or reinsurance waterfall economics.
6. It does not prove liability-cost spread.
7. It does not prove IRR, NPV, ROIC, or KKR platform profit.

## Do-Not-Overclaim Box

Use this box in the final writeup.

`The evidence supports capital-routing and cash-back proxy claims. It does not yet prove full source-to-use-to-receipt-to-waterfall-to-return cash loops across the major platforms.`

Avoid these overclaims:

| Overclaim | Safer Version |
|---|---|
| KKR has proved cash returns on these assets. | Accordia reports statutory interest received and selected disposal consideration columns. |
| Disposal consideration proves cash settlement. | Disposal consideration is statutory proceeds-column evidence; settlement needs custodian or bank support. |
| Insurance liabilities were directly used to fund each named CUSIP. | Accordia has insurance liabilities and named assets in the same legal entity; asset-level source allocation is not proved. |
| Private credit money clearly reached the borrower. | Borrower exposure is visible; cash receipt and use need facility and funds-flow documents. |
| Infrastructure projects have proved returns. | Capex, output, tariff, revenue, or EBITDA proxies are visible; project return needs customer cash and waterfall evidence. |
| Public filings prove the whole loop. | Public filings often get to bridge evidence; controlled documents are usually required for full proof. |

## Final Writing Spine

Use this order for the final narrative:

1. **Thesis:** the economy is increasingly financed through capital-routing systems.
2. **Mechanism:** money moves from source pools through platforms, legal wrappers, and instruments into named assets and borrowers.
3. **Lead proof case:** KKR/Global Atlantic / Accordia shows the strongest legal-entity cash-back proxy.
4. **Broader pattern:** Apollo/Athene confirms the statutory insurance route; Ares/Frontline shows the private-credit borrower route; power/infrastructure cases show physical capital sinks.
5. **Evidence discipline:** distinguish map evidence, bridge evidence, named-cash holds, and full named-cash proof.
6. **Remaining frontier:** receipts, settlement ledgers, debt waterfalls, legal-entity allocation, collateral certificates, liability-cost schedules, and return models.

## One-Paragraph Final Draft

Large asset managers are becoming capital-routing systems. They gather money from retirement savers, insurance liabilities, pensions, institutions, wealth clients, lenders, and public markets, then route it through legal wrappers into credit, infrastructure, utilities, real assets, operating borrowers, and asset-backed pools. The strongest current evidence is KKR/Global Atlantic's Accordia statutory stack: a `7.318321094B USD` owned-bond book, `229.507957M USD` of statutory interest received, and selected same-CUSIP disposal consideration columns. That proves a strong legal-entity cash-back proxy, not the full cash loop. The final proof frontier is receipts, settlement ledgers, debt waterfalls, collateral certificates, liability-cost schedules, and return models.
