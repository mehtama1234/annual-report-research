## Best Current Upgrade Candidate

PBF is the cleanest source/use transaction case because it has the most direct refinancing chain:

1. new note issuance
2. stated use of proceeds plus available cash
3. target redemption instrument
4. redemption amount
5. redemption date context

It still does not have the final settlement schedule. Without that settlement, the row cannot prove exact accrued interest, fees, cash-on-hand contribution, tax treatment, or final liquidity impact.

## What Would Count As Full Funds-Flow Proof

Full transaction funds-flow proof requires:

1. source instrument and proceeds amount
2. settlement date and cash receipt
3. cash on hand or facility draw contribution
4. payee or destination
5. accrued interest, fees, taxes, purchase-price adjustments, and make-whole amounts
6. debt closeout or new debt rollforward
7. post-transaction liquidity and covenant effect
8. operating or asset-level cash outcome
