## The Clean Separation

There are two different mix concepts:

| Concept | Where It Lives | What It Means | Current Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fleet-productivity mix | Owned equipment rental revenue bridge | customer mix, fleet mix, geographic mix, and business mix including Specialty | Deck-defined; Q4 `2025` transcript gives qualitative Matting impact |
| Ancillary/re-rent revenue mix | Total rental revenue and segment gross margin | lower-margin ancillary/re-rent revenue growing faster than owned rentals | Filed MD&A, deck bridge, transcript commentary |

The same business reality can affect both, but we should not treat them as the same metric.

## What We Can Connect

| Evidence | What It Supports | Boundary |

## What We Are Saying

The better interpretation is:

`URI's access model is generating more revenue through both owned fleet productivity and broader customer services, but the revenue mix is getting more complex. Ancillary/re-rent helps growth and customer solution depth, yet it can dilute specialty rental gross margin. Matting can move the fleet-productivity mix bucket quarter to quarter because project timing affects how specific assets contribute to owned-equipment rental revenue.`

That is different from saying:

`Specialty is deteriorating.`

The evidence does not support that. Specialty is gaining revenue and gross-profit share, but with compressed margin.

## Safe Claim

`URI now has a bridge between the fleet-productivity evidence and the specialty-margin evidence. The deck/transcript trail shows Matting as a qualitative Q4 2025 fleet-productivity mix driver, while the filed Q2 2026 release and 10-Q identify lower-margin ancillary and re-rent revenue mix as a specialty-margin pressure point. These are related access-model complexities, but they are not the same metric. Specialty is still gaining revenue and gross-profit share, while its gross margin has compressed from 48.9% in FY2023 to 43.6% in FY2025 and to 43.1% in H1 2026.`

## Claims Not To Make Yet

Do not say:

- Matting explains the whole specialty margin decline
- ancillary/re-rent explains all specialty margin compression
- Matting mix and ancillary/re-rent mix are the same metric
- specialty is structurally deteriorating
- higher ancillary revenue is bad
- Q2 `2026` has a quantified product-category margin bridge
- fleet productivity proves ROIC
