## What Would Upgrade URI

URI upgrades from proxy-grade to named cash proof only if a source joins:

1. period-tested borrowing-base certificate
2. eligible collateral by category
3. eligible rental-equipment NBV and NOLV
4. reserves, ineligible assets, outstandings, and letters of credit
5. Combined Borrowing Base and Combined Availability
6. specific debt draw or cash source
7. source-to-purchase allocation for fleet or acquisition use
8. fleet-class OEC, utilization, rental rate, time, and mix
9. disposal recovery and maintenance/growth capex split
10. lifecycle ROIC, IRR, NPV, or cash-yield model

## Decision

`uri-collateral-fleet-return-bridge-hold-with-strongest-fleet-collateral-proxy`

URI is not the place where we currently have named cash proof. It is the best place to show what the proof system is doing: visible facility/collateral/fleet/cash metrics are strong, but the private lender certificate and asset-return model still block a full source-to-return claim.

## Safe Claim

`United Rentals has the strongest public collateral/fleet cash proxy in the current named-cash proof system. Public evidence shows July 2025 ABL availability of about 2.428B USD, a May 31 2025 borrowing-base certificate delivery condition, a 1.000B USD minimum closing Combined Availability condition, Q2 2026 implied ABL plus AR availability of about 2.887B USD, 37.0% ABL draw intensity, 125.8% AR collateral-pool coverage, 17.350B USD of rental equipment NBV, 23.8B USD of OEC, 3.849B USD of Q2 rental revenue, H1 OCF coverage of 112.8% of gross rental capex and 162.0% of net fleet cash investment, 11.8% company ROIC, and a 26.4% Specialty gross-profit/assets proxy. Current evidence still does not prove populated borrowing-base certificate values, eligible collateral, NOLV, reserves, L/C schedules, source-to-purchase allocation, fleet-class utilization, lifecycle ROIC, IRR, NPV, or named cash return.`
