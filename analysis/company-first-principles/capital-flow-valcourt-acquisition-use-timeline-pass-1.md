# Capital Flow Valcourt Acquisition Use Timeline Pass 1

## Purpose

This pass resolves `CFARQ-007`:

`Can Valcourt's large holder-visible exposure be tied to a specific acquisition timeline, continued-growth program, refinancing event, full facility size, or bank-role change?`

## Resolution Outcome

`hold-after-pass-1 - large holder-visible roll-up exposure and acquisition-platform context visible, specific use and refinancing bridge incomplete`

Valcourt can be used as a strong borrower-destination case. It shows private-credit exposure to a building-maintenance and facility-services roll-up through SEC-filed holder schedules, and separate operating-context evidence says the platform completed `10` acquisitions in `15` months.

Current evidence does not prove which acquisition, add-on program, refinancing, amendment, or bank repayment was funded by the visible debt rows. The Q2 2026 ARCC row also has a different maturity from the older SDLP and Cliffwater rows, but current evidence does not prove whether it reflects a refinancing, extension, replacement tranche, separate debt package, or lender reallocation.

## Safe Answer

SEC-filed holder schedules show large first-lien exposure to Valcourt-related borrowers, including a conservative same-period Q2 2026 ARCC fair-value number of 117.6000M USD and a latest-visible mixed-period lower-bound holder exposure of 459.6000M USD across SDLP and ARCC.
