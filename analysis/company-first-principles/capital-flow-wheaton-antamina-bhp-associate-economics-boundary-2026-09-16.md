# BHP–Antamina associate economics boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

BHP's FY2026 Annual Report gives an entity-level operating and balance-sheet
view of Compañía Minera Antamina S.A. It identifies Antamina as a Peru-based
associate, reports a `33.75%` BHP ownership interest, and states that the
investment is accounted for using the equity method. The report is therefore
useful for the operator/equity-owner burden map, but it is not a silver-stream
settlement statement.

| FY2026 Antamina measure | Reported basis | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Revenue | Antamina 100% | `$7.473B` |
| Profit | Antamina 100% | `$3.029B` |
| BHP share of equity-accounted profit | BHP group share | `$1.022B` |
| Net assets | Antamina 100% | `$5.531B` |
| BHP group share of net assets | BHP group share | `$1.867B` |
| Current liabilities | Antamina 100% | `$1.561B` |
| Non-current liabilities | Antamina 100% | `$2.532B` |

The same note reports `$895M` of dividends received from equity-accounted
investments in FY2026, but the table does not identify that amount as an
Antamina-only dividend. It must not be used as an Antamina cash receipt or as
Wheaton stream cash.

This source improves the Antamina operating and balance-sheet denominator. It
does not provide BHP-only silver ounces, payable-ounce conversion, Wheaton
metal credits, invoice or settlement date, stream-specific tax, stream-related
interest, lender waterfall, or PMPA return.

Source: [BHP FY2026 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/811809/000119312526354647/bhp-20260630.htm), Note 29, pages 177–179.
