# Wheaton–Antamina BHP Contract Denominator Upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

The April 1, 2026 closing disclosure gives a BHP-only contractual denominator
that is more precise than the combined Antamina production row. Under the BHP
Antamina PMPA, Wheaton purchases the equivalent of BHP's `33.75%` of payable
silver from the effective date until `100 million ounces` have been delivered;
after that threshold, the BHP stream falls to `22.5%` of payable silver for the
life of mine. The payable factor is fixed at `90.0%`, and ongoing payments are
`20%` of spot silver price.

This improves the valuation and delivery model because the BHP tranche can be
modeled separately from the legacy Glencore stream. It does not prove how many
BHP-only ounces were delivered in Q2, what metal-credit quantity was invoiced,
or when cash was settled. The contractual `100 million ounce` threshold is a
term, not a forecast or a realized delivery schedule.

Primary source: [Wheaton announcement of closing the BHP Antamina PMPA](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1323404/000127956926000263/ex991.htm).
