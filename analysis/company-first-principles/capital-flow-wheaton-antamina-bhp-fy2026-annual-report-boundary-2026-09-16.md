# Wheaton–Antamina BHP FY2026 public-filing boundary — 2026-09-16

Research date: `2026-09-16`

BHP's FY2026 public SEC filing set adds a current primary-source boundary to
the Wheaton–Antamina chain. Both documents are now preserved separately: the
canonical Form 20-F and a Form 6-K containing FY2026 results material. They
remain separate filing records and are not merged into one source.

| Evidence item | FY2026 report observation | Use in the pilot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal and operating perimeter | BHP owns 33.75% of Antamina; Compañía Minera Antamina S.A. operates it independently; Antamina is not a party to the streaming agreement | Confirms the BHP-subsidiary obligation remains distinct from the Antamina JV |
| Upfront consideration | BHP received `$4.3B`; Note 24.3 specifies the payment was received on April 2, 2026 | Strengthens the dated source/use leg of Q-01 |
| Settlement mechanism | Silver is delivered by reference to BHP's Antamina share, settled through metal credits, with no physical silver delivery | Confirms the accounting object required for Q-01 |
| Contract floor | No minimum or fixed delivery requirement | Prevents treating the $100M threshold as a guaranteed schedule |
| FY2026 BHP-interest silver production | BHP reports `5.588M` ounces of payable silver in concentrate for its `33.75%` Antamina interest | Adds a full-year counterparty production denominator for the delivery screen |
| Forward operating boundary | FY2027 Antamina copper guidance is `120–140 kt`; zinc guidance is `35–55 kt`, due to planned lower feed grades | Current mine-operating context only; not a silver or BHP-PMPA delivery forecast |
| Liability estimation | Future production assumptions use existing development plans plus risked reserves/resources that do not currently meet proved criteria | Keeps the reserve-backed delivery curve below proved status |

The filing set therefore upgrades the dated settlement/source boundary, the
full-year BHP-interest production denominator, and the forward operating
context. The `5.588M` ounces are BHP's mine production table, not Wheaton's
metal-credit or receipt ledger. It does not disclose BHP-PMPA silver ounces,
metal-credit invoices, settlement assays, quotation-period pricing, payment
receipt, or an annual silver delivery curve. No silver quantity is inferred
from the copper or zinc guidance.

Status: `FY2026 BHP settlement and forward-operating boundary confirmed; silver delivery and receipt remain unresolved`
