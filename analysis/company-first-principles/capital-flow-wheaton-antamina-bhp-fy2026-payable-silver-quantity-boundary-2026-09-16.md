# Wheaton–Antamina BHP FY2026 payable-silver quantity boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Purpose

This memo adds a BHP-only annual quantity calibration to the Antamina
delivery chain. It does not treat BHP's operating disclosure as a Wheaton
settlement ledger.

## Source-backed observation

BHP's FY2026 annual report identifies Antamina as a 33.75%-owned BHP asset and
reports `5.588M` troy ounces of payable silver in concentrate for BHP's
interest. The same report describes the Wheaton stream as delivering the
equivalent of BHP's `33.75%` of Antamina silver production, subject to a fixed
`90%` payable rate.

The structured companion table records the reported BHP-interest payable
quantity, a forward application of the PMPA factor, and a reverse-factor gross
equivalent screen:

`5.588M × 90% = 5.029M ounces`

and, under the alternative assumption that BHP's reported payable number is
already post-factor:

`5.588M / 90% = 6.209M ounces`

Both numbers are only calibrations. Neither is a claim about ounces produced,
delivered, invoiced, or paid to Wheaton. BHP's “payable metal in concentrate”
convention may not be identical to the PMPA's settlement convention without a
smelter, assay, and contract reconciliation.

## What this improves

The Antamina case now has a BHP-only annual operating denominator that can be
used to calibrate the initial `100M`-ounce threshold and to test whether a
future BHP-PMPA settlement quantity is directionally consistent with public
production data. It prevents the combined Wheaton Antamina stream rows from
being used as if they were BHP-only.

## What remains unproven

This observation does not prove:

- BHP-PMPA metal-credit ounces transferred to Wheaton;
- the PMPA application of the 90% payable factor to the reported quantity;
- an invoice, assay, quotation-period price, or settlement date;
- a bank receipt or Wheaton cash collection;
- BHP's allocation of the reported `$41M` streaming-liability settlement; or
- Antamina-specific tax, financing, debt-waterfall, IRR, or NPV economics.
