# Wheaton–Antamina BHP FY2026 upfront receipt and use boundary

Research date: `2026-09-17`

## Why this update matters

The BHP FY2026 Form 20-F closes an important recipient-side part of the Q-03
source/use chain. BHP reports that it completed the Antamina silver stream with
Wheaton, received `$4.3B` of upfront consideration, and included the proceeds
in net financing cash-flow analysis. The filing also states that settlement is
through metal credits with no physical silver delivery and that Antamina is not
a party to the BHP/Wheaton agreement.

This is stronger than an announced-term proxy: it is a post-close annual
issuer record of the upfront receipt and its financing-cash-flow classification.
It still does not provide the recurring BHP-PMPA credit, sale, receivable, bank
collection, tax, or facility-allocation ledger required for full return proof.

## Evidence boundary

| Evidence | Observation | What it proves | What remains open |
|---|---|---|---|
| BHP FY2026 Form 20-F | BHP completed the long-term silver stream and received `$4.3B` upfront consideration | Completed BHP-side upfront receipt, legal counterparty, and transaction status | Receipt date at bank level, fees, tax, and internal cash allocation |
| BHP FY2026 Form 20-F | Stream references BHP's Antamina share; settlement is via metal credits with no physical delivery | Correct legal and settlement object; Antamina is not a contracting party | BHP-only recurring credit issuance, sale/receivable, and collection |
| BHP FY2026 Form 20-F | Net financing cash outflows include the `$4.3B` stream proceeds, alongside debt and dividends | Period-level classification of proceeds in BHP's financing cash-flow narrative | Whether proceeds funded debt repayment, growth, dividends, or other uses at transaction level |
| Wheaton April 2026 closing exhibit | Wheaton paid BHP `$4.3B` on April 1, 2026 | Counterparty-side closing payment and effective date | BHP bank receipt confirmation and recurring metal-credit cash trail |
| Wheaton Q2 2026 report | Term-loan proceeds, revolver draw, and cash on hand partially funded the PMPA | Buyer-side closing-funds-flow direction | Asset-level interest, tax, repayment, and owner-return allocation |

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

The `$4.3B` is a financing/source-use item and must not be treated as recurring
Antamina operating revenue, silver-sale cash, or normalized common-owner cash.
The BHP filing's financing-cash-flow classification also must not be converted
into a claim that the proceeds were used for a specific project, debt repayment,
or shareholder distribution without a matched cash-use schedule.

The model keeps separate:

1. BHP's completed upfront receipt;
2. Wheaton's `$4.3B` closing payment and funding mix;
3. recurring BHP-PMPA metal credits and any sale/receivable;
4. legacy Glencore stream credits;
5. ongoing 20% production payments;
6. tax, interest, debt service, and facility allocation; and
7. the residual available to either company's common owners.

## Promotion decision

`bhp-upfront-receipt-and-period-classification-proven; recurring-bhp-cash-return-open`

The Q-03 gate is upgraded for the completed upfront receipt and issuer-level
period classification. It is not upgraded to full named cash or return proof:

- **Proven:** BHP completed the transaction and received `$4.3B` upfront;
  Wheaton's closing payment and financing direction are independently reported.
- **Still open:** BHP-only credit units, credit date, invoice or receivable,
  realized price, bank collection, ongoing production-payment reconciliation,
  tax, debt-service, and facility allocation.
- **Not permitted:** assigning combined Antamina production, sales, cash flow,
  or the `$4.3B` proceeds to a BHP-only recurring owner-cash return.
