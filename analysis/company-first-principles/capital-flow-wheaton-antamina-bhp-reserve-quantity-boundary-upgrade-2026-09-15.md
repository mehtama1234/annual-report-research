# Wheaton–Antamina BHP reserve-quantity boundary upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

Wheaton's transaction materials include a technical reserve and resource table
for BHP's `33.75%` share of Antamina silver. The table reports `65.7M`
contained silver ounces of total Proven and Probable Mineral Reserves,
comprising `36.4M` ounces in copper zones and `29.3M` ounces in copper-zinc
zones. It also reports `38.4M` ounces of Measured and Indicated Resources and
`109.9M` ounces of Inferred Resources.

The September 16 Investor Day presentation provides a post-event denominator
cross-check: its Antamina reserve table reports `72.8M` P&P ounces in copper
zones and `58.8M` in copper-zinc zones, or `131.6M` ounces for the combined
`67.5%` Wheaton stream. The BHP `33.75%` tranche is approximately one half of
that combined stream, consistent with the separately reported `65.7M`
contained-ounce BHP-interest figure. This corroborates the tranche
denominator; it does not provide BHP-only delivered credits, settlement, or an
annual mine-plan curve.

The same presentation states that mineral resources that are not mineral
reserves do not have demonstrated economic viability, and that the reserve and
resource tables are generally reported as of December 31, 2025. The `65.7M`
figure is therefore a dated P&P reserve input with a defined classification,
not a claim that all later resources are economic or that the reserve table is
the current delivery schedule.

## Mechanical threshold comparison

Applying the PMPA's fixed `90%` payable factor mechanically to the P&P reserve
quantity gives approximately `59.13M` ounces. That is below the PMPA's
`100M`-ounce initial delivery threshold. This is a boundary screen—not a
forecast—because contained reserve ounces are not the same as payable,
delivered, credited, or settled ounces, and the threshold could be affected by
future reserve conversion, mine-plan changes, recoveries, and adjustments.

| Quantity | Amount | Safe interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| BHP-interest P&P contained silver reserves | `65.7M oz` | Source-backed reserve quantity proxy |
| Mechanical 90% equivalent | `59.13M oz` | Simple payable-factor screen, not a delivery forecast |
| Initial PMPA step-down threshold | `100M oz` | Contract threshold requiring actual delivered ounces |
| Gap to threshold under mechanical screen | `40.87M oz` | Additional resources, reserve conversion, or higher payable deliveries would be needed; not a shortfall conclusion |

## Valuation consequence

The Antamina workbench should not silently assume that the initial BHP stream
share reaches the `100M`-ounce step-down threshold within the currently
disclosed Proven and Probable reserve quantity. The base and bull scenarios can
show threshold and reserve assumptions separately, while the final financed
return remains open until a production schedule, payable recovery, metal-credit
ledger, tax allocation, and debt waterfall are available.
