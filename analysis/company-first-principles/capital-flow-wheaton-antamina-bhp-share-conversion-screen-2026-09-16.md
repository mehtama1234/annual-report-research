# Antamina BHP-share silver conversion screen

Research date: `2026-09-16`

This screen converts the operator's reported metallic-silver tonnes into a
mechanical BHP `33.75%` share using `32,150.7466` troy ounces per metric tonne.
It then applies the contractual `90%` payable factor as a sensitivity. The
2025 row is kept as the directly reported BHP-share observation from BHP's
February 2026 Form 6-K and is not reverse-engineered from an operator total.

| Period | Source basis | Operator silver | Mechanical BHP share | 90% payable sensitivity |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2023 | Antamina operator actual | `360 t` / `11.574 Moz` | `3.906 Moz` | `3.515 Moz` |
| 2024 | Antamina operator actual | `0.35 Kt` / `11.253 Moz` | `3.798 Moz` | `3.418 Moz` |
| 2025 | BHP direct BHP-share observation | — | `5.400 Moz` | `4.860 Moz` sensitivity only |
| 2026–2030 | Wheaton purchaser estimate for acquired stream | — | `6.000 Moz` average/year | Management profile; not settled ounces |
| 2026–2035 | Wheaton purchaser estimate for acquired stream | — | `5.400 Moz` average/year | Management profile; not settled ounces |

The 2023–2024 rows are calculated screens, not reported BHP metal-credit
quantities. The 2025 BHP figure is reported on a BHP-share basis, but the
announcement does not identify payable ounces, delivered ounces, or the
Wheaton settlement quantity. The two Wheaton profile rows are forward
management estimates for the acquired stream and are not annual guarantees.
The 90% column is therefore a contractual
conversion sensitivity, not a receipt forecast.

The screen improves the tranche denominator and makes the unit conversion
visible. It does not establish annual production through the 100 Moz threshold,
smelter weights/assays, invoice, settlement date, metal-credit quantity, or
cash receipt.

Sources: [Antamina Sustainability Report 2023](https://www.antamina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/sustainability-report-antamina-2023.pdf), [Antamina Sustainability Report 2024](https://www.antamina.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/sustainability-report-antamina-2024.pdf), and [BHP SEC Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/811809/000119312526052837/d29257d6k.htm).
