# Wheaton–Antamina BHP 100M-ounce threshold timing screen

Research date: `2026-09-15`

This screen translates the newly available BHP-interest production/sales
series and the transaction's public production-profile proxy into a bounded
timing sensitivity for the PMPA's `100M`-ounce initial-tranche threshold. It
is not a reserve-backed mine plan, a settlement ledger, or a forecast of the
actual drop-down date.

## Mechanical screens

| Denominator | Annual ounces | Mechanical years to 100M ounces | Use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| BHP FY2026 payable silver produced | `5.588M` | `17.90` | Reported BHP-interest operating screen |
| BHP FY2026 payable silver sold | `5.406M` | `18.50` | Reported BHP-interest sales/timing screen |
| Transaction profile, BHP incremental first five years | `6.0M` | `16.67` | Public profile proxy |
| Transaction profile, BHP incremental first ten years | `5.4M` | `18.52` | Public profile proxy |

The calculations are `100 / annual ounces`. They assume a constant annual
series and ignore ramp-up, grade, recoveries, mine sequencing, interruptions,
inventory timing, contract conventions, and any difference between BHP's
operational reporting unit and the PMPA metal-credit unit.

## Interpretation

The screens cluster around roughly `16.7–18.5` years under the stated
mechanical assumptions. That is useful for valuation sensitivity: the
initial `33.75%` tranche should not be modeled as an immediate drop to `22.5%`
without a long-duration threshold assumption. But the screen cannot establish
that the mine has enough reserve-backed payable silver to reach the threshold.
The existing BHP-interest P&P quantity screen is `65.7M` contained ounces,
with a mechanical `90%` equivalent of `59.13M`, below the `100M` threshold;
contained reserves, payable recovery, future resources, and contract settlement
are not interchangeable.

## Proof-grade result

`threshold-timing-sensitivity-confirmed`: public operating/profile inputs now
bound the mechanical timing range for the initial-tranche threshold. A
reserve-backed annual delivery curve, exact PMPA metal-credit quantity, and
actual threshold date remain open.
