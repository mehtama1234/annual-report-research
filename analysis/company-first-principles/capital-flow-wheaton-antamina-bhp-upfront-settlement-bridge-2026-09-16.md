# Wheaton–Antamina BHP upfront-settlement bridge — 2026-09-16

## Purpose

This bridge isolates the one part of Q-01 that is now source-backed at the
upfront-transaction level: Wheaton's `$4.300B` Antamina PMPA payment and BHP's
reported receipt of the same consideration on April 2, 2026. It is a
source/use and timing bridge, not proof of a bank-wire match or of subsequent
metal-credit operating receipts.

## Evidence chain

| Leg | Evidence | Safe interpretation | Not proven |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Named use | Wheaton Q2 2026 materials report a `$4.300B` Antamina PMPA payment | The named acquisition use is quantified | Seller-account wire confirmation and exact funds-flow allocation |
| Source context | Wheaton reports Q2 bank-debt draw and repayment activity and a new `$1.500B` term loan | Financing was active around the payment | Exact draw dollars assigned to the PMPA or lender-level waterfall |
| Seller receipt | BHP FY2026 Note 24.3 reports receipt of `$4.3B` on April 2, 2026 | BHP's reported consideration receipt is dated and amount-consistent with the named Wheaton payment | Independent bank confirmation, settlement statement, taxes, fees, or net proceeds |
| Contract object | BHP reports silver delivered by reference to its Antamina share through metal credits, with no physical silver delivery | The later operating settlement object is identified | BHP-PMPA payable ounces, invoice, assays, quotation price, settlement date, and Wheaton cash collection |

## Classification

`promotion-ready` for the narrow claim that a `$4.300B` named Antamina
consideration payment and a BHP-reported `$4.3B` receipt on April 2, 2026 are
visible in contemporaneous primary filing materials, subject to the boundary
that amount-and-date consistency is not a bank-wire reconciliation.

`evidence-insufficient` for the full Q-01 claim. The bridge does not show the
first BHP-PMPA metal-credit quantity, invoice, realized quotation-period price,
cash collection, or a separation from Wheaton's legacy Glencore Antamina
stream.

## Required next proof

Obtain the executed PMPA funds-flow or settlement statement, BHP/Wheaton bank
or custodian confirmation, and a post-close BHP-PMPA metal-credit ledger. Then
join delivered ounces, the `90%` payable factor, the `20%` cash-payment formula,
invoice price, settlement date, and cash or receivable entry.
