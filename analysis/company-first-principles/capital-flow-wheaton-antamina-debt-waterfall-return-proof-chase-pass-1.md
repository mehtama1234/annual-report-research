## Why This Matters

This is the best current test of the whole capital-flow system because it has all the early layers:

`bank/cash funding -> named PMPA payment -> named Antamina stream -> contract entitlement -> stream-level revenue/cost/profit -> asset denominator`

The missing layers are also clear:

`delivered-ounce receipts -> tax/interest allocation -> debt-service waterfall -> reserve-backed delivery curve -> IRR/NPV`

That means the case is close enough for a strong named cash-return proxy, but still below full named cash proof.

## Upgrade Test

Wheaton Antamina can move to full asset-level cash-return proof only if a source joins:

1. exact funding source amounts by cash, term loan, and revolver
2. lender commitments and debt-service terms
3. PMPA closing funds-flow
4. delivered ounces and realized price by period
5. cash receipts and per-ounce delivery payments
6. Antamina-specific tax and interest allocation
7. reserve-life and delivery curve
8. IRR, NPV, or full payback model after tax and financing

The current evidence does not satisfy that test.

## Decision

`wheaton-antamina-debt-waterfall-return-proof-chase-hold-with-strongest-named-asset-proxy`

The Wheaton Antamina proof chase is executed against local 6-K evidence and targeted public sources. It improves the named asset story with financing-channel, delivery-threshold, payable-factor, and reserve-life context, but it remains below full PMPA return, debt-waterfall, lender-payback, and IRR/NPV proof.
