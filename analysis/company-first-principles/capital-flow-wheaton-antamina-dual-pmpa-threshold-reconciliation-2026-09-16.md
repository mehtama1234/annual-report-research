# Wheaton–Antamina dual-PMPA threshold reconciliation — 2026-09-16

Research date: `2026-09-16`

The September 2026 Wheaton presentation makes an important denominator
distinction visible inside its own Antamina materials:

| Presentation location | Counterparty / PMPA | Threshold and post-threshold right | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Antamina asset page, p. 37 | BHP | `33.75%` of payable silver until `100 Moz`, then `22.5%` for life of mine | New BHP PMPA denominator |
| Company acquisition history, p. 41 and note 10 on p. 43 | Glencore | Historical entry dated `3-Nov-2015`; after `140 Moz` received under the Antamina agreement, attributable silver purchased reduces to `22.5%` | Legacy Glencore PMPA denominator |
| Company acquisition history, p. 42 and note 31 on p. 44 | BHP | Entry dated `16-Feb-2026`; `33.75%` until `100 Moz`, then `22.5%` life of mine | Cross-check of the new BHP PMPA |

The apparent `100 Moz` versus `140 Moz` difference is not treated as a source
error or averaged into one stream. The page references identify two separate
agreements with the same Antamina asset and the same headline percentage, but
different threshold balances. This is precisely why the combined Antamina
received/sold and revenue rows cannot be used as BHP-only settlement evidence.

The reconciliation upgrades the contract-denominator layer: BHP's initial
tranche threshold is now separately corroborated in the asset page and the
acquisition-history appendix, while the legacy Glencore threshold is preserved
as a distinct obligation. It does not disclose how the two agreements' actual
deliveries, payability, metal credits, invoices, or receipts are allocated in
the post-close combined operating rows.

Status: `dual-PMPA threshold distinction confirmed; delivery and cash allocation remain unresolved`
