# Wheaton–Antamina Investor Day extraction checklist

Research date: `2026-09-16`

This is the controlled refresh checklist for the Wheaton Investor Day held on
September 16, 2026. It converts the current Q-01 and Q-02 gaps into
fields that can be promoted only when the presentation, webcast, transcript,
or subsequent filing supplies primary evidence.

## Pre-event source boundary (historical snapshot)

Before the event, Wheaton's public Q2 materials confirm that the BHP Antamina
PMPA became effective April 1, 2026, that first BHP-stream deliveries occurred
during Q2, and that Wheaton reported `2.319M` combined attributable Antamina
silver ounces produced and `2.063M` sold. The Q2 release also confirms the `$4.3B` BHP Antamina upfront
payment and the `$1.5B` term-loan draw used with other funding sources. None of
those disclosures allocates the combined ounces to BHP, identifies a
metal-credit invoice, or proves a settlement receipt. This section preserves
the pre-event information boundary; it is not the current event status.

The event notice says that technical presentations from partner
representatives may be included and that a PDF slideshow and recording will be
available. The local source packet and the official presentations page were
checked before the event; no post-event deck or transcript was available at
that time.

The official September Corporate Presentation is now archived locally. It
confirms the BHP PMPA's `33.75%` payable-silver right through `100 Moz`, then
`22.5%` for life of mine, and repeats the approximately `6.0 Moz` / `5.4 Moz`
first-five/first-ten-year production-profile inputs. Its appendix separately
shows the legacy Glencore Antamina PMPA at a `140 Moz` threshold. This is a
meaningful denominator upgrade, but the deck supplies no BHP-only delivered
ounces, metal-credit invoice, settlement price/date, receipt account, or

## Extraction fields

| Field | Required observation | Promotion consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BHP entitlement | BHP's mine-interest percentage, payable-silver basis, and stream percentage for the current tranche | Confirms the denominator for a BHP-only quantity test; contract terms alone remain insufficient |
| Actual BHP quantity | Ounces delivered or credited to Wheaton for the BHP PMPA, separated from the legacy Glencore stream | Can split the `2.319M` produced / `2.063M` sold combined Q2 quantities only if the source is explicit |
| Payability and assay | Payable ounces, recovery or smelter/assay adjustment, and any PBND treatment | Prevents gross mine production from being substituted for delivered metal credits |
| Pricing and settlement | Quotation period, realized price, production payment, invoice/credit-note amount, settlement date, and currency | Required for the BHP-only settlement bridge |
| Receipt location | Wheaton legal entity, receivable or cash account, and settlement status | Required to move from delivery evidence to named-cash evidence |
| Reserve and plan | Updated BHP-interest reserves, mine plan, production curve, and expected timing of the 100M-ounce threshold | Can replace the mechanical threshold screen with a reserve-backed delivery curve |
| Liability and recourse | Any updated parent/holding-company guarantee, recourse reduction, or counterparty obligation | Updates the legal-risk and debt/claim map without treating a guarantee as cash |
| Financing allocation | Any Antamina-specific debt, interest, tax, or repayment allocation | Required for an Antamina after-tax financed return rather than a company-level sensitivity |

## Promotion rules

1. A production forecast or combined Antamina number cannot be labeled a
   BHP-only receipt.
2. A metal-credit mechanism cannot be labeled settled cash without a quantity,
   price or settlement amount, date, and receiving-entity route.
3. A reserve total cannot be labeled a delivery curve without the relevant
   BHP-interest denominator and timing assumptions.
4. A company-level financing sensitivity remains separate from an
   Antamina-specific after-tax IRR or NPV.
5. The event's presentation supplies the entitlement and management-profile
   fields but not the named-cash or reserve-backed fields. Q-01 therefore
   remains open, while Q-02 receives only a forward-profile/denominator
   upgrade and remains below a reserve-backed delivery curve.

## Further refresh procedure

Wheaton pilot verifier, and update the first-delivery boundary and next-
