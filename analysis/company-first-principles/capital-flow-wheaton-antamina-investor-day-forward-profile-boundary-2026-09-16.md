# Wheaton–Antamina Investor Day forward-profile boundary — 2026-09-16

Research date: `2026-09-16`

Wheaton's September 2026 Corporate Presentation provides a new, official
forward-looking profile for the BHP Antamina PMPA. It is useful for the
expectation and concentration layers, but it does not convert the public
production profile into a BHP-only settlement ledger.

| Evidence item | Presentation observation | Safe use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contract terms | `33.75%` of payable silver until `100 Moz` is delivered, then `22.5%` for life of mine; ongoing payment is `20%` of spot silver | Reconfirms the BHP-specific contractual denominator | Does not report actual BHP-only credits, invoices, or cash receipts |
| Forward production profile | BHP Antamina attributable production is forecast at approximately `6.0 Moz` of silver per year for the first five years and `5.4 Moz` for the first ten years | Labeled scenario input for threshold and valuation sensitivity | Management forecast; not a reserve-backed annual mine plan or realized delivery series |
| Portfolio concentration | Antamina is shown as `18%` of 2025 actual production mix and `12%` of 2030 expected production mix on the presentation's GEO basis | Tests concentration and diversification expectations | GEO mix is not an Antamina silver-revenue or cash-flow allocation |
| Growth narrative | Wheaton describes Antamina as an immediate-production, operating-cash-flow asset and includes it among key assets supporting approximately `50%` estimated organic growth by 2030 | Identifies the management expectation that the valuation must underwrite | Does not prove incremental cash flow, mine economics, or return on the `$4.3B` upfront payment |

The new presentation therefore improves the forward-expectation map in two
ways: it restates a usable BHP-specific production-profile sensitivity and
shows how Antamina is expected to affect portfolio concentration over time.
The existing threshold screens may use the `6.0 Moz` and `5.4 Moz` figures as
explicit management-scenario inputs, but they must remain labeled as
mechanical sensitivities. The presentation does not close the missing
BHP-PMPA metal-credit quantity, assay, quotation-period price, settlement date,
receipt account, reserve-backed delivery curve, or Antamina-specific financed
after-tax return.

Status: `forward profile and concentration expectation confirmed; named cash
and reserve-backed delivery remain unresolved`
