# Wheaton–Antamina local packet settlement search boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

The preserved Wheaton Q2 2026 6-K exhibits and BHP FY2026 Antamina materials
were searched for invoice, settlement, metal-credit, receipt, delivered,
delivery, Antamina, and PMPA terms. The packet contains the metal-credit
settlement mechanism, BHP's aggregate `$41M` streaming-liability settlements,
Wheaton's combined Antamina received/sold and cash-flow rows, and BHP-interest
production/sales proxies. It does not contain a BHP-PMPA-only invoice,
metal-credit quantity, settlement statement, realized-price schedule, payment
date, or receipt account.

This is a local-source availability boundary, not evidence that no settlement
occurred. It prevents the combined Antamina rows or the aggregate `$41M` from
being promoted to BHP-only owner cash. The next decisive source is a BHP-PMPA
settlement statement, delivery invoice, custodian/receipt record, or company
note that separates the BHP tranche from the legacy Glencore stream.

## Public-source cross-check

The local packet boundary was cross-checked against the latest public filings
and Investor Day materials available on September 16, 2026:

- [BHP FY2026 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/811809/000119312526354647/bhp-20260630.htm)
  confirms that the stream is with a wholly owned BHP subsidiary, that
  Antamina is not a party, and that settlement is through metal credits with
  no physical delivery. A targeted scan did not surface a BHP-PMPA invoice,
  metal-credit quantity, payment date, or receipt-account schedule.
- [Wheaton Q2 2026 MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1323404/000119312526338641/d15525dex992.htm)
  reports the BHP-driven increase in attributable Antamina production and
  describes the risk of estimating produced-but-not-yet-delivered ounces, but
  does not disclose a BHP-only invoice or settlement receipt.
- [BHP’s April 2, 2026 closing release](https://www.bhp.com/news/media-centre/releases/2026/04/bhp-completes-silver-streaming-agreement-with-wheaton-precious-metals)
  confirms the `$4.3B` upfront consideration and metal-credit mechanism, but
  likewise does not provide a quantity-level settlement schedule.
- [Wheaton’s current Antamina portfolio page](https://www.wheatonpm.com/portfolio/operating-mines/antamina/default.aspx)
  separately lists the BHP stream terms and `$4.3B` upfront consideration,
  but provides no BHP-only delivery quantity, invoice, settlement, or receipt
  schedule. It therefore corroborates the contract boundary without closing
  the named-cash gate.

This cross-check strengthens the source-availability boundary; it does not
upgrade the BHP-only settlement gate. The required upgrade remains a
BHP-PMPA settlement statement, delivery invoice, custodian/receipt record, or
future filing that separates the BHP tranche from the legacy Glencore stream.

### Expanded official-source search — 2026-09-16

An additional targeted search of the official BHP and Wheaton routes returned
the same boundary. BHP's February streaming announcement and FY2026 Form 20-F
repeat the contract terms and metal-credit mechanism; Wheaton's closing
exhibit, Q1 report, and Q2 MD&A repeat the `$4.3B` upfront payment, first-
delivery timing, and combined operating disclosures. None adds a BHP-PMPA-only
metal-credit quantity, invoice, quotation-period settlement price, payment
date, receipt account, or settlement ledger. The result is recorded as
`searched-negative` for this public-source perimeter: the documents were
available and searched, but the decisive field was not disclosed. This is not a
claim that no settlement occurred.

The search also included BHP's April 2, 2026 Form 6-K, which independently
states that the stream is settled through metal credits with no physical
delivery. It corroborates the settlement-object boundary already found in the
annual report and BHP closing release, but it does not disclose the credited
quantity, invoice, quotation-period price, or receipt account. It therefore
strengthens the accounting-mechanism evidence without changing the
`searched-negative` result for BHP-only cash proof.
