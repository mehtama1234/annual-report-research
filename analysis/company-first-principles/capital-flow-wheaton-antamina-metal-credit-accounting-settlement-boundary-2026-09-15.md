# Wheaton–Antamina metal-credit accounting and settlement boundary

Research date: `2026-09-15`

The latest public Wheaton reporting makes the accounting object behind the
BHP–Antamina settlement more precise, while still not exposing the BHP-only
receipt ledger.

## Source-backed mechanism

Wheaton describes its stream contracts as an initial upfront payment followed
by an additional cash payment for each ounce delivered. Its 2025 annual
information form states that revenue from precious-metal credit sales is
recognized when control of the precious metal is transferred to the customer.
It also states that final settlement is based on the applicable quotation
period and the actual recovered ounces calculated from confirmed smelter
weights and settlement assays.

For the BHP Antamina PMPA, the public terms separately say that settlement is
through metal credits with no physical delivery, and that Wheaton pays 20% of
the spot silver price for ounces delivered. This means the decisive proof
object is not a trucked shipment receipt. It is the BHP-PMPA-specific metal-
credit quantity, payability calculation, quotation/realized price, settlement
statement, and receipt or receivable entry joined to the relevant period.

## What this adds

| Question | Public evidence | Safe conclusion | Still missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What is transferred? | Metal credits; no physical silver delivery | The settlement must be traced through credit/accounting records | BHP-PMPA credit ledger |
| What determines quantity? | Actual recovered ounces, confirmed smelter weights, settlement assays, and the contract payability factor | Quantity cannot be inferred from the combined Antamina production row alone | BHP-only recovered ounces, payability, and assay/settlement support |
| When is revenue recognized? | When control of the precious-metal credit transfers to the customer | A dated transfer/settlement event is required for a cash-quality bridge | BHP-PMPA transfer date and invoice/receipt |
| What is the ongoing price? | 20% of spot silver for delivered BHP-PMPA ounces | A price formula is visible | Period-specific realized price and settlement amount |

This is a mechanism and proof-object upgrade, not BHP-only settlement proof.
It does not change the pilot's `qualified-not-full-proof` grade.
