# Antamina mine-plan burden schedule

Research date: `2026-09-16`

The February 2025 Antamina NI 43-101 provides annual mine-plan burden data for
the remaining operating period. The plan runs from 2025 through 2036, with
2036 a partial year. The capital-cost table is stated in nominal terms; the
operating-cost table is stated in real terms. Those bases are preserved rather
than blended into a single cash-flow number.

| Period | Operating cost, real `$M` | Capital cost, nominal `$M` |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2025 | 1,387 | 825 |
| 2026 | 1,409 | 850 |
| 2027 | 1,351 | 667 |
| 2028 | 1,367 | 464 |
| 2029 | 1,492 | 333 |
| 2030 | 1,435 | 221 |
| 2031 | 1,318 | 155 |
| 2032 | 1,252 | 190 |
| 2033 | 1,066 | 70 |
| 2034 | 1,053 | 108 |
| 2035 | 953 | 109 |
| 2036, partial | 597 | 31 |
| **Total** | **14,681** | **4,022** |

The report also describes approximately `546 Mt` of ore and `1,535 Mt` of
waste in the remaining open-pit plan. The schedule is a mine-operator burden
and reserve-support input. It does not disclose annual silver contained,
payable silver, BHP-only production, Wheaton metal credits, stream-specific
tax, or a cash waterfall. It therefore cannot be subtracted directly from
Wheaton stream revenue to produce owner cash without a legal-entity and
economic-allocation bridge.

Source: [Antamina NI 43-101 technical report](https://minedocs.com/28/Antamina-TR-12312024.pdf), Tables 1-4 and 1-5, pages 1-20 through 1-22.
