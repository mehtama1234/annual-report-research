# Wheaton–Antamina post-Q2 public settlement search refresh

Research date: `2026-09-17`

## Purpose

Refresh the public-source perimeter after Wheaton's Q2 2026 filing to test
whether a newer BHP-only metal-credit quantity, invoice, settlement date, or
receipt ledger has become available.

## Search result

The checked official routes continue to disclose the legal mechanism, effective
date, first-delivery confirmation, and operating production/sales proxies. No
post-Q2 public document located in this refresh discloses a BHP-only credited
ounce quantity, invoice, realized settlement price, settlement date, payment
account, or receivable entry.

This is a `searched-negative` result for the checked public perimeter. It is not
evidence that the records do not exist privately, and it does not negate
Wheaton's statement that first BHP-PMPA deliveries occurred during Q2 2026.

The latest official Wheaton Q2 results page was also rechecked as an additional
public route. It confirms aggregate Antamina production, company-level PBND,
and the funding/cash balance around closing, but still exposes no BHP-PMPA-only
metal-credit quantity, invoice, quotation-period settlement, receivable, or
cash-receipt line. The Q-01 classification therefore remains
`searched-negative` for the checked public source perimeter.

The live [Wheaton Antamina portfolio page](https://www.wheatonpm.com/portfolio/operating-mines/antamina/default.aspx)
was also rechecked on September 16, 2026. It separately identifies the BHP
stream as 33.75% of payable silver until 100 million ounces have been
delivered, then 22.5% of payable silver for life of mine. It lists the `$4.3B`
upfront consideration and ongoing delivery payments equal to 20% of spot.
This strengthens the contract-route and BHP-versus-Glencore boundary, but the
page is not a settlement ledger: no BHP-only delivered quantity, invoice,
quotation-period price, settlement date, payment account, or receivable entry
is disclosed. Q-01 therefore remains `searched-negative`.

The same release gives a useful current operating observation without changing
that classification: Antamina produced approximately `2.3M` attributable
silver ounces in Q2 2026, while the detailed table reports `2.319M` produced
and `2.063M` sold. Management attributes the increase primarily to the BHP
PMPA's effective-April-1 expansion of Wheaton's combined Antamina share from
`33.75%` to `67.5%`, while also noting lower grades and the timing of planned
maintenance. These figures are combined Wheaton attributable production and
sales, not BHP-only metal credits or a settlement statement.

The Q2 financial-statements contract table independently labels Antamina at
`67.5%` attributable payable production to be purchased and its footnote
separately describes the BHP `100M`-ounce threshold and the Glencore `140M`
threshold. This strengthens the two-contract boundary, but the table remains
a contractual combined entitlement: it does not disclose how much of the
Q2/H1 production or sales belongs to the BHP PMPA versus the legacy Glencore
PMPA.

The same table reports a combined Antamina `$1.171862B` of cash flow generated
to date, `56.718M` units received and sold to date, and `1.412M` of Q2 payable
metal produced but not delivered. These are useful aggregate cash-realization
and timing anchors, but they predate and span both Antamina PMPAs; they cannot
be assigned to BHP or used as a BHP-only receipt ledger.

A separate BHP operational review dated April 22, 2026 was also checked. It
updates Antamina copper guidance to `150–160 kt` for FY26, but does not provide
an annual silver forecast, BHP-PMPA delivery curve, or metal-credit settlement
ledger. The copper guidance is therefore retained as mine-operating context
only and is not converted into a silver-volume or Wheaton-cash assumption.

### FY2026 BHP filing precision refresh — 2026-09-17

The latest BHP FY2026 annual-report extraction was rechecked against the
Wheaton packet. It adds no quantity-level BHP settlement record, but it makes
the recipient-side control more exact: BHP reports receipt of `$4.300B` on
April 2, 2026, `$4.300B` of streaming proceeds, and `$41M` of streaming-
liability settlements. It also states the `33.75%` / `90%` payable tranche,
`100M`-ounce threshold, post-threshold `22.5%` tranche, metal-credit
settlement, and no minimum or fixed delivery requirement. These are legal,
date, and cash-flow classification controls only; the BHP-only credit
quantity, invoice, settlement-price/date allocation, and Wheaton receipt
remain undisclosed in the checked public perimeter.

## Status change

The result updates source freshness and perimeter coverage only:

`Q-01: searched-negative public perimeter refreshed; BHP-only settlement proof held`

The next promotion test remains the same: join BHP entitlement, payability,
recovered ounces, metal credits, invoice price, settlement date, and Wheaton
cash or receivable entry for the same period and legal counterparty.

### Live official-source recheck — 2026-09-17

A fresh recheck of the live Wheaton Antamina portfolio page, Wheaton's filed
Q2 2026 MD&A/results exhibits, and BHP's FY2026 Form 20-F returned the same
boundary. The sources repeat the BHP contract terms, the effective April 1
date, first-delivery/combined-production observations, and BHP's `$4.300B`
recipient-side receipt, but no BHP-only credited ounces, invoice, settlement
price/date, payment account, or receivable entry. The searched-negative result
is therefore current as of this pass and remains limited to the checked public
source perimeter.
