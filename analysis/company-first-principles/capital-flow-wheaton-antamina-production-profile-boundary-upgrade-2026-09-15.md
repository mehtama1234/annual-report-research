# Wheaton–Antamina Production-Profile Boundary Upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

Wheaton's official transaction materials provide a source-backed production
profile that can inform, but not replace, a reserve-backed delivery curve. The
BHP incremental stream is expected to contribute approximately `6.0M` silver
ounces per year over the first five years and `5.4M` over the first ten years.
When combined with the existing Glencore stream, total Antamina attributable
production is expected to average approximately `12.0M` ounces per year over
the first five years and `10.8M` over the first ten years. The materials also
state that current declared reserves support mining through approximately
`2036`, with expansion options under evaluation.

These are transaction and management production expectations, not realized
BHP-only metal credits, invoices, cash receipts, or an independent reserve
report. The scenario workbench can use them as a labeled profile proxy while
keeping the delivery, tax, interest, and financing waterfall open.

Primary source: [Wheaton transaction announcement and production profile](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1323404/000127956926000133/ex991.htm).
