# Wheaton–Antamina Public Primary-Source Upgrade

Date: 2026-09-15

This memo records a primary-source upgrade discovered while deepening
[Integrated Pilot 01](combined-investment-research-pilot-01-wheaton-antamina.md).

## What is now proven

- Wheaton's April 1, 2026 closing release says the BHP Antamina PMPA closed,
  that Wheaton paid BHP `4.3B USD`, and that ongoing payments are `20%` of the
  spot silver price for ounces delivered.
- Wheaton's Q2 2026 filing repeats the April 1, 2026 `4.3B USD` closing
  payment and says first deliveries under the BHP PMPA were expected at the
  end of May 2026.
- BHP's FY2026 operational review reports approximately `4.3B USD` of
  proceeds received from the Antamina silver-streaming transaction in its
  financing cash-flow presentation.
- BHP's FY2026 annual-report filing presents `4.300B USD` of proceeds received
  from the streaming arrangement and separately identifies the transaction in
  the financing cash-flow table.

These sources upgrade the upfront transaction from a one-sided “Wheaton says
it paid” observation to a two-sided public cash-receipt confirmation:

```text
Wheaton payment disclosed
  -> BHP proceeds received disclosed
  -> named Antamina streaming agreement
```

## Public evidence boundary that remains

The public filings still do not provide:

1. BHP-PMPA-only delivered ounces separated from the legacy Glencore stream;
2. delivery-level invoices, settlement statements, or receipt dates;
3. Antamina-specific tax and interest allocation;
4. lender-by-lender debt-service or restricted-account waterfall; or
5. a reserve-backed delivery curve sufficient for a source-backed IRR/NPV.

The correct status is therefore `upfront cash loop publicly confirmed; delivery
and financed-return loop still incomplete`.
