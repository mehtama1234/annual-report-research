# Wheaton–Antamina Q2 2026 delivery-boundary upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

Wheaton's Q2 2026 report adds the first post-close delivery evidence for the
BHP Antamina PMPA, but it does not yet provide a BHP-only settlement ledger.

## Newly explicit primary evidence

- Wheaton states that it received its first deliveries during Q2 2026 under
  the recently acquired BHP Antamina PMPA.
- Wheaton's Q2 operating table reports `2.319M` attributable Antamina silver
  ounces produced and `2.063M` sold. Its GEO bridge separately identifies
  `837,000` silver-ounce-equivalent GEO production; the increase was primarily
  driven by the BHP PMPA,
  which increased Wheaton's Antamina share from `33.75%` to `67.5%` effective
  April 1, 2026.
- The reported production and sales are combined Antamina attributions. The
  report does not split either measure between the legacy Glencore stream and
  the BHP PMPA, and it does not disclose BHP-only invoices, metal-credit
  quantities, realized settlement prices, or cash receipts.

## Consequence for the proof grade

The delivery gate improves from “no post-close delivery evidence” to
`first delivery confirmed; quantity-level BHP-only cash remains unproven`.
The overall case remains:

`upfront cash loop confirmed; settlement mechanism and first delivery confirmed; BHP-only quantity, cash allocation, and financed return incomplete`
