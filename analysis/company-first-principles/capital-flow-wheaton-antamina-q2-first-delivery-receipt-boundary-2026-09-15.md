# Wheaton–Antamina Q2 first-delivery receipt boundary — 2026-09-15

Wheaton's Q2 2026 MD&A provides the first direct post-close delivery
confirmation for the BHP Antamina PMPA: Wheaton states that it received its
first deliveries under the newly acquired BHP stream during Q2 2026.

That is stronger than a production or contract-timing proxy, but it is not a
settlement receipt ledger. The same report's Q2 operating table presents
`2.319M` attributable Antamina silver ounces produced and `2.063M` sold; its
GEO-bridge narrative separately expresses Antamina as `837,000` silver-ounce
equivalents and says the increase was primarily driven by
the BHP PMPA, which raised Wheaton's combined Antamina share from `33.75%` to
`67.5%`. It does not allocate the reported ounces between the legacy Glencore
stream and the BHP stream.
