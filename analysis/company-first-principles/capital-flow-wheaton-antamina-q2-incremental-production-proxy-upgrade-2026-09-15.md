# Wheaton–Antamina Q2 incremental-production proxy upgrade

Date: `2026-09-15`

This upgrade extracts the more precise post-close production signal from
Wheaton's Q2 2026 report. The report's GEO bridge says Antamina attributable
silver-ounce-equivalent production increased by `837,000` GEOs in Q2 and by
`1.341M` GEOs for the
first half, with the increase primarily driven by the BHP Antamina PMPA. The
PMPA became effective April 1, 2026, and increased Wheaton's combined
Antamina share from `33.75%` to `67.5%`.

## What the evidence supports

| Period | Reported signal | Safe interpretation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q2 2026 | `837,000` silver-ounce-equivalent GEO increase from Antamina; Q2 operating table reports `2.319M` attributable silver ounces produced and `2.063M` sold | Post-close incremental production and sales scale, primarily associated with the BHP PMPA | Not BHP-only delivered ounces, metal credits, invoices, realized price, or cash receipts |
| H1 2026 | `1.341M` silver-ounce increase from Antamina | H1 incremental production signal after the April 1 effective date | H1 production includes the legacy Glencore stream and is not a settlement ledger |
| Contract | Combined share increased from `33.75%` to `67.5%` effective April 1, 2026 | The timing and scale of the post-close production change are consistent with the BHP tranche becoming active | Contract share does not identify the ounces credited or cash settled to the BHP stream |

The correct use is to calibrate a production-profile or delivery-timing screen.
It should not be used to promote the BHP-only receipt question to proven. The
remaining decisive evidence is still the BHP metal-credit quantity, invoice or
settlement statement, realized price, and cash receipt.
