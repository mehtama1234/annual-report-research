# Wheaton–Antamina reserve-life denominator boundary — 2026-09-15

This boundary prevents a portfolio-level mine-life statistic from being used as
an Antamina-specific delivery forecast.

## Two different denominators

| Source | Scope | Published observation | Safe use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wheaton investor overview | Entire Wheaton portfolio | Approximately `23 years` of mine life based on Proven and Probable reserves | Portfolio context only; not an Antamina or BHP-PMPA delivery curve |
| Wheaton Antamina transaction materials | Antamina mine / BHP-interest tranche | Current declared reserves sufficient to support mining activities through approximately `2036`; BHP-interest P&P contained silver reserves of `65.7M oz` | Antamina-specific reserve boundary and payable-ounce screen |

The company-wide figure aggregates multiple operating and development assets and
does not identify the BHP Antamina tranche, its payable recovery, its delivery
sequence, or the timing of the 100M-ounce contractual threshold. The Antamina
transaction materials are therefore the controlling source for the Q-02 reserve
screen.

## Modeling rule

The valuation workbench must not use the company-wide `23-year` statistic as an
Antamina tail assumption. It may show the figure as portfolio context, but the
BHP tranche must remain bounded by the `65.7M` contained-ounce input, `90%`
payability, the disclosed `2036` reserve-life context, and any separately
source-backed mine-plan extension.

This is a source-denominator upgrade, not a reserve-backed annual delivery
curve. It leaves the 100M-ounce threshold, metal-credit settlement, tax,
financing, and mine-life extension questions open.

## Proof-grade result

`Portfolio mine-life statistic separated from Antamina-specific reserve
denominator; annual BHP-PMPA delivery curve remains unproven.`
