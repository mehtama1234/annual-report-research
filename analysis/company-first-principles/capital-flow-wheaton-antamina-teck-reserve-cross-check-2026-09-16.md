# Wheaton–Antamina Teck reserve cross-check

Research date: `2026-09-16`

Teck's 2025 Annual Information Form supplies an independent Antamina
joint-venture reserve reference. It reports total Proven and Probable
Antamina reserves of `528.4 Mt` and `31.430 Moz` of recoverable silver
attributable to Teck's `22.5%` interest. The same filing states that the mine
life is expected to extend through `2036`, that its estimate is consistent
with an approved 2026 life-of-mine plan, and discloses the long-term
silver-price assumption used for reserves.

The underlying February 2025 NI 43-101 technical report is also publicly
available as a copy of the filed report. Its life-of-mine section states that
the remaining mine life is 12 years from 2025 through 2036, with 2036 a partial
year, and that approximately 546 Mt of ore and 1,535 Mt of waste are planned
for the remaining open-pit schedule. The report does not expose an annual
silver-production table that can be mapped directly to Wheaton's BHP tranche.
It does provide an independent stream-contract cross-check: Teck's
Franco-Nevada stream equals 22.5% of payable silver sold by Minera Antamina,
with a 5% spot-price payment, an 86 Moz step-down threshold, and 29.1 Moz
delivered from 2015 through December 31, 2024.

## Evidence boundary

This is useful independent reserve context, but it cannot be substituted
directly for Wheaton's BHP-tranche table. The Teck figure is a `22.5%`
attributable recoverable-metal figure, while the Wheaton/BHP transaction
materials present a BHP `33.75%` tranche reserve table with a different
ownership, contract, reporting, and potentially reserve/production
denominator. The two rows therefore require a controlled reconciliation before
any quantity is added or annualized.

What the Teck source adds:

- an independent JV-owner reserve reference;
- an explicit 2026 life-of-mine-plan context; and
- a public mine-life endpoint through 2036; and
- an independent, named stream precedent with an observed delivered-ounces
  history; and
- an owner-share operating-burden reference that distinguishes sustaining,
  growth, and capitalized stripping; and
- a project-debt and non-recourse boundary; and
- a cross-check against the proposition that Antamina has a reserve and mine-
  plan base beyond the current quarterly production proxy.

What it does not add:

- a BHP-PMPA-specific payable or metal-credit quantity;
- a BHP settlement, invoice, quotation-period price, or receipt;
- a Wheaton-specific 90% payability reconciliation; or
- an annual delivery curve through the BHP `100M`-ounce threshold; or
- a BHP/Wheaton allocation of Teck's mine-level capital or operating-cost
  estimates; or
- a stream-level allocation of Antamina project debt or debt service.

The technical report's `2025–2036` mine plan is mine-level schedule context,
not a BHP-attributable payable-silver schedule. The Teck/Franco-Nevada stream
is a useful contract precedent and delivery-history anchor, but it cannot be
used as Wheaton/BHP settlement evidence because it has a different owner,
stream contract, upfront consideration, threshold, and payment formula.

The same Teck AIF adds a useful operating-burden boundary for the mine-level
case. Teck's 22.5% share of Antamina's 2026 projected capital costs is
`$215M–$270M`, split into `$110M–$135M` sustaining, `$15M–$20M` growth, and
`$90M–$115M` capitalized stripping. Its projected cash operating cost is
`$255M–$310M`, with labor, supplies, energy, and other costs shown separately;
the filing says transportation and royalties are excluded. These are Teck
owner-share burden estimates, not BHP/Wheaton costs, taxes, debt service, or
silver-delivery deductions. They narrow the mine-level burden map while
remaining outside the BHP delivery curve.

Teck also reports that its proportionate share of Antamina borrowings was
`$225M` at December 31, 2025 and that the Antamina facilities are non-recourse
to Teck and the other project sponsors. This is a mine-level financing and
recourse boundary: it makes project debt visible without treating Teck's
borrowings as BHP/Wheaton debt or proving any allocation to stream receipts.

For denominator control, a non-promotable scale sensitivity is also recorded:
`31.430 Moz × (33.75% ÷ 22.5%) = 47.145 Moz`, or `42.4305 Moz` after a
mechanical 90% payability factor. This is not a reserve reconciliation. Teck's
recoverable-silver denominator and BHP's contained P&P denominator have
different source and classification boundaries; the calculation is retained
only to show the size of the unjoined bridge and is excluded from the delivery
curve and valuation workbench.

The BHP FY2026 Form 20-F was also checked as the counterparty's current
forward-operating source. It supplies the historical `5.588M` ounces of
BHP-interest payable silver used by the existing proxy, but its Antamina
forward guidance is expressed in copper and zinc rather than an annual silver
production schedule. That source boundary prevents converting copper/zinc
guidance into a silver delivery curve by inference.

## Q-02 consequence

Q-02 advances from a single-company reserve proxy to an independently sourced
reserve cross-check, but remains `evidence-insufficient` for a reserve-backed
BHP delivery curve. The next upgrade requires the Teck reserve/mine-plan
denominator to be reconciled with the BHP tranche, payable factor, recoveries,
production schedule, and contract threshold without double counting.
