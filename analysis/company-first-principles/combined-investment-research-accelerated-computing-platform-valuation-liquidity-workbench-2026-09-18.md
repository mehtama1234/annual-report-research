# Accelerated-computing platform valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves NVIDIA from strong growth evidence into a company-specific
valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps architecture, CUDA/software,
networking, systems, outsourced manufacturing, customer financing, export
controls, AI-cloud commitments, and diluted common-owner cash separate from
memory, foundry, lithography, and process-control economics.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NVIDIA accelerated-computing platform | Platform cash after architecture and software R&D, systems/networking integration, supply commitments, inventory and receivable financing, export exposure, AI-cloud guarantees, SBC, debt, and dilution | Wafer/packaging and memory commitments, inventory, customer terms, engineering compute, R&D, data-center/partner commitments, acquisitions, SBC replacement, and repurchases | AI-capex reversal, product-transition obsolescence, export restrictions, customer concentration, extended collection, partner failure, guarantee draw, margin compression, or debt/refinancing pressure | Data-center revenue and margins grow while sell-through, receivable collection, inventory reserves, partner utilization, software durability, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Fiscal 2026 revenue was `$215.9B`, Data Center revenue `$193.7B`, operating
  cash flow `$102.7B`, PP&E and intangible investment `$6.0B`, acquisitions
  `$1.5B`, and SBC `$6.4B`.
- Fiscal 2026 included a `$4.5B` H20 excess-inventory and purchase-obligation
  charge after export requirements changed; this is a direct product-transition
  and supply-commitment control.
- Q2 fiscal 2027 revenue was `$96.2B`, Data Center revenue `$89.0B`, and GAAP
  gross margin `75.0%`; the quarter still recorded `$985M` of inventory and
  excess-purchase-obligation provisions.
- At Q2 fiscal 2027, receivables were `$63.059B`, inventory `$31.575B`, and
  six-year cloud-service commitments totaled `$36B`; land, power, and shell
  guarantees had maximum gross exposure of `$3.5B` for select AI-cloud
  partners.
- Five direct customers represented `22%`, `14%`, `13%`, `11%`, and `10%` of
  accounts receivable at quarter end, while NVIDIA issued `$25B` of senior
  notes in June 2026. Aggregate liquidity therefore does not equal unencumbered
  common-owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Track architecture-specific inventory, reserves, supplier commitments,
   cancellation rights, customer acceptance, and sell-through. Do not let
   “one-time” export or launch provisions disappear from the recurring risk
   perimeter.
2. Reconcile direct and indirect customer revenue with receivable aging,
   payment terms, deposits, acceptance conditions, and cash collected. A highly
   rated customer can still shift financing and bargaining power to NVIDIA.
3. Separate chips, complete systems, networking, and software/service economics
   by product generation. Launch gross margin is not a durable platform margin
   without yield, memory, packaging, installation, and warranty evidence.
4. Trace AI-cloud commitments, partner investments, guarantees, service
   purchases, utilization, pricing, and counterparty liquidity. These objects
   can coordinate demand while moving infrastructure financing risk onto NVIDIA.
5. Remove realized and unrealized investment gains from operating valuation;
   value strategic equity stakes and operating cash separately.
6. Treat `$6.4B` of SBC, tax-withholding cash, repurchases, and diluted shares
   as one per-share residual test. Repurchases do not automatically replace
   equity compensation or prove surplus cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what future data-center growth,
platform share, gross margin, software attachment, reinvestment rate, customer
returns, and cost of capital the valuation requires after normalizing launch
charges, investment gains, SBC, and working capital. It must value the core
operating platform separately from equity investments and financing-like
commitments.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether AI infrastructure customers can
fund deployment through higher rates, power constraints, export controls,
geopolitical disruption, weaker utilization, tighter credit, or a product
transition. The balance sheet passes only when inventory, receivables,
guarantees, supplier claims, debt, and diluted common residual remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`accelerated-computing-qualified; platform-return-and-liquidity-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from architecture/system
shipments and software/service attachment to customer collection, inventory
and supplier settlement, AI-cloud utilization, required R&D and infrastructure
reinvestment, debt, SBC replacement, and diluted common residual. Data Center
revenue, gross margin, OCF, backlog, customer demand, and repurchases remain
diagnostic inputs.
