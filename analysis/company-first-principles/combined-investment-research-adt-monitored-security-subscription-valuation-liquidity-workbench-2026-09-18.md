# Monitored security subscription and smart-home valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves ADT from current-period security-services evidence into a company-specific valuation, subscriber, installation, attrition, platform, debt, and owner-cash test. It treats ADT as a monitored physical-security relationship business, not as a hardware seller or institutional communications company.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ADT | Collected recurring monitoring cash after customer acquisition, installation, equipment subsidy, service labor, attrition, payback, smart-home platform, claims, debt, swaps, and dilution | Installation equipment, field service, monitoring infrastructure, ADT+, Google Nest/Yale integration, ambient sensing, customer acquisition, self-install channels, and working capital | Gross attrition, acquisition cost/payback, housing and small-business activity, device replacement, emergency/service obligations, platform competition, debt cost, refinancing, and dilution | RMR and cash flow rise while attrition, payback, installation cost, customer acquisition, ADT+ adoption, service claims, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue increased `5%` to about `$5.1B`; end-of-period recurring monthly revenue was about `$359M`; gross revenue attrition `13.1%`; revenue payback `2.3 years`; operating cash flow about `$1.9B`; and adjusted free cash flow including swaps about `$863M`.
- FY2025 capital returned through repurchases and dividends was about `$791M`; average debt cost was about `4.3%`, with weighted-average maturity extended to about `5 years`.
- Q1 2026 revenue increased `1%` to about `$1.3B`; RMR remained about `$359M`; attrition `13.1%`; payback `2.3 years`; and adjusted free cash flow including swaps was about `$414M`.
- Q2 2026 revenue increased `2%` to about `$1.3B`; RMR rose to about `$360M`; attrition remained `13.1%`; payback `2.3 years`; and adjusted free cash flow including swaps was about `$406M`.
- ADT+ represented about `30%` of new customer additions in Q1 2026; Google Nest/Yale integration, Trusted Neighbor, Origin AI, and ambient sensing are intended to deepen the monitored relationship.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile RMR to customer count, price, attrition, cancellations, installation completion, monitoring delivery, collection, and equipment subsidy; recurring revenue is not automatically owner cash.
2. Test revenue payback through customer-acquisition cost, installation labor/equipment, channel mix, self-install, service calls, churn, and cohort lifetime value.
3. Separate ADT+, Google Nest/Yale, ambient sensing, and Origin AI adoption from hardware revenue, support cost, data/privacy, platform investment, and incremental retention.
4. Keep adjusted free cash flow including swaps separate from working-capital timing, equipment funding, customer advances, debt service, installation investment, and cash returns.
5. Reconcile debt cost, maturity extension, securitization or financing arrangements, service/claims obligations, and common-share dilution before promoting capital returns.
6. Treat repurchases, dividends, adjusted income, RMR, attrition, and payback as residual diagnostics requiring same-period customer and cash joins.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what subscriber growth, RMR, attrition, acquisition payback, installation cost, service margin, smart-home attachment, platform retention, debt cost, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether households and small businesses keep funding monitored protection through housing, employment, insurance, rates, device, privacy, and security shocks while field service and monitoring obligations remain.

## Promotion boundary

`adt-monitored-security-qualified; rmr-attrition-payback-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from customers and RMR to collection, installation, acquisition payback, attrition, service delivery, ADT+ adoption, equipment replacement, debt/swaps, claims, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, RMR, attrition, payback, adjusted free cash flow, capital returns, and platform adoption remain diagnostic inputs.
