# Agribusiness crop-flow valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Bunge Global into a company-specific agribusiness
origination, processing, merchandising, and commodity-finance object. It keeps
crop-flow infrastructure separate from agricultural inputs, foodservice
distribution, materials producers, and direct commodity producers. The object
tests realized processing/merchandising spreads after inventory, receivables,
payables, hedging, freight, productive capex, Viterra integration, debt,
collateral, dividends, and diluted common residual.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment/denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bunge Global | Through-cycle crop-processing and merchandising cash after realized spreads, inventory/receivable funding, freight, productive capex, Viterra integration, debt, collateral, taxes, and diluted common residual | Crush/refining assets, storage/ports/logistics, inventory, receivables, seasonal financing, traceability/biofuel investment, Viterra acquisition cost, goodwill, debt, and working capital | Commodity-price and spread shock, inventory/receivable absorption, collateral calls, crop/weather/trade disruption, policy change, refinancing, and integration failure | Revenue, adjusted EBIT/EPS, mark-to-market gains, synergies, or reported OCF rise while realized spreads, working capital, capex returns, Viterra cash return, leverage, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$16.944B`, OCF `$844M`, PP&E purchases `$1.723B`, and
  business acquisitions `$4.201B`, producing a post-growth cash screen of
  negative `$5.080B` before dividends. The acquisition figure primarily reflects
  the Viterra combination and is not a normal operating year, but it is a real
  owner-capital burden.
- FY2025 inventory was `$13.198B`, receivables `$3.870B`, debt `$14.051B`, cash
  `$1.135B`, and goodwill `$3.141B`. Inventory and debt must be separated into
  seasonal operating funding, commodity collateral, and durable leverage.
- Bunge reported more than `$70M` of Viterra cost synergies by year-end 2025.
  Synergy claims require integration spending, working-capital, capex, and
  realized-cash evidence rather than adjusted EBIT alone.
- Q1 2026 diluted EPS was `$0.35` versus adjusted EPS `$1.83`; derivative marks,
  inventory valuation, freight, and foreign-exchange timing are business
  exposures that need realized-cash reconciliation, not automatic exclusion.
- Q2 2026 adjusted total EBIT improved to about `$665M`, but one strong quarter
  does not establish through-cycle crush spreads, origination margins, or
  post-Viterra return on capital.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate realized crush spreads, origination, storage, freight, specialty
   ingredients, biofuel exposure, and mark-to-market movements.
2. Reconcile adjusted earnings to derivative settlements, inventory revaluation,
   FX, integration, restructuring, asset sales, and acquisition accounting.
3. Track Viterra revenue, EBIT, cash flow, synergies, integration cost,
   working capital, capex, debt, goodwill, and return on invested capital.
4. Test inventory days, receivable aging, supplier payables, collateral,
   seasonal borrowing, and whether cash release reflects temporary destocking.
5. Keep dividends, buybacks, policy-funded demand, traceability spending, and
   debt-funded acquisition cash separate from normalized owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what mid-cycle crush spread,
origination margin, specialty return, inventory funding, capex, Viterra synergy,
debt cost, and cost of capital the equity value requires. The Lyn Alden-style
stress test asks whether weather, energy, crop flows, trade policy, biofuel
mandates, rates, collateral requirements, and customer liquidity preserve cash
through a commodity and working-capital cycle.

## Promotion boundary

`agribusiness-crop-flow-qualified; spread-working-capital-and-viterra-return-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from physical crop flows to
realized spread, inventory/receivable settlement, collateral, productive
capex, Viterra cohort return, debt, tax, dividends, diluted shares, and
common-owner residual. Revenue, volume, adjusted EBIT/EPS, mark-to-market
changes, synergies, OCF, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
