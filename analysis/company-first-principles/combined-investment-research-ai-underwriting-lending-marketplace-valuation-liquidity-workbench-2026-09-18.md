# AI underwriting and lending-marketplace valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Upstart from AI-underwriting and origination evidence into
a company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps underwriting
model performance, borrower conversion, partner funding, loan-sale economics,
repurchase and indemnification exposure, fair-lending compliance, product
cohorts, technology investment, and diluted common-owner cash separate from
Affirm/Synchrony's balance-sheet lending and from payment-network economics.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upstart AI underwriting marketplace | Risk-adjusted fee and workflow cash after borrower acquisition, model monitoring, compliance, servicing, partner support, retained credit exposure, technology, working capital, and dilution | Underwriting/model development, data and monitoring, product integration, partner onboarding, servicing, compliance, capitalized software, repurchase reserves, and common capital | Partner funding withdrawal, adverse credit vintages, model drift, fair-lending or regulatory action, loan-sale/repurchase claims, product concentration, or rates | Originations, conversion, automation, revenue, or adjusted EBITDA rise while funded-loan quality, partner returns, fee rate, retained exposure, cash conversion, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 originations were `$11.0B` across `1,497,149` loans, revenue was
  approximately `$1.0B`, and originations grew `86%` while revenue grew `64%`.
- Q2 2026 originations reached approximately `$4.2B` across `558,014` loans;
  fee revenue was approximately `$348M` and GAAP net income approximately
  `$16.5M`. Unsecured fee rate fell to `35%` from `176%` a year earlier.
- More than `90%` of loans were described as fully automated. Automation is a
  cost and conversion claim until model monitoring, exceptions, compliance,
  loss performance, and partner economics are joined.
- Upstart carries less direct loan-balance risk than Synchrony, but partner
  funding is a dependency. Measure capital by partner, product, risk tier,
  committed versus discretionary capacity, pricing, sale terms, and funding
  timing.
- Current packet evidence remains open on complete owner-cash conversion,
  retained loans, guarantees, indemnities, repurchase reserves, and through-
  cycle partner funding resilience.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile inquiries, approvals, offers, funded loans, fee rates, borrower
   acquisition cost, partner economics, and lifetime credit outcomes. Loan
   originations are not the correct valuation unit.
2. Test whether model-driven conversion improves risk-adjusted returns or merely
   accepts more risk. Track vintages, credit bands, delinquency, charge-offs,
   partner losses, and changes in funding terms.
3. Treat partner funding as a conditional supply of capital, not a permanent
   moat. Concentration, discretionary capacity, pricing, whole-loan sale terms,
   and withdrawal behavior belong in liquidity analysis.
4. Reconcile loan sales with representations, warranties, indemnities, servicing
   obligations, retained exposure, fair-value changes, and repurchase reserves.
5. Show GAAP earnings, adjusted EBITDA, technology and compliance cost, SBC,
   diluted shares, working capital, guarantees, and cash together. Automation
   does not prove owner cash without this bridge.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what funded volume, fee rate,
conversion, partner retention, risk-adjusted credit performance, product mix,
technology cost, compliance burden, terminal growth, and cost of capital the
valuation requires. It values normalized fee cash, not originations.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether banks and institutional buyers keep
funding loans through unemployment, credit losses, spread widening, higher rates,
capital pressure, and regulatory scrutiny. Liquidity passes only when external
funding, retained exposure, indemnities, cash, and common residual remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`ai-underwriting-qualified; partner-funding-and-credit-cycle-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from product originations and
conversion to funded-loan cohorts, partner capital, fee collection, credit
performance, loan-sale terms, retained exposure, repurchase/indemnity claims,
model and compliance cost, SBC replacement, and diluted common residual.
Originations, automation, revenue, adjusted EBITDA, OCF, and repurchases remain
diagnostic inputs.
