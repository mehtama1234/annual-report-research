# Alumina, aluminum, and energy conversion valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Alcoa from current-period commodity evidence into a company-specific valuation, restart, energy, carbon, portfolio, and owner-cash test. It treats Alcoa as a vertically integrated alumina/aluminum conversion system rather than as a generic mining or metal-price proxy.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alcoa | Through-cycle alumina, aluminum, bauxite, and energy cash after realized price/premium, production/shipment, power, refinery/smelter renewal, restarts, remediation, JV obligations, debt, and dilution | Mines, refineries, smelters, power, maintenance/growth capex, restart capital, environmental remediation, inventory, labor, and strategic-material projects | Aluminum/alumina prices, power and gas, weather, geopolitical curtailment, tariffs, restart execution, carbon policy, labor, debt, and FX | EBITDA and free cash flow rise while realized price, production/shipment, restart cost, power burden, remediation, JV funding, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$12.8B`, net income attributable to Alcoa `$1.2B`, adjusted EBITDA excluding special items `$2.0B`, cash from operations `$1.2B`, total debt `$2.4B`, and adjusted net debt `$1.5B`.
- Alcoa has approximately `25` operating locations across `8` countries and two main businesses, Alumina and Aluminum; five aluminum smelters and one alumina refinery reached annual production records in 2025.
- Q2 2026 revenue reached `$4.0B`, net income attributable to Alcoa `$407M`, adjusted net income `$562M`, adjusted EBITDA `$901M`, and free cash flow `$422M`.
- Q2 alumina production fell `6%` sequentially to `2.2M` metric tons because of Pinjarra instability and gas disruption, while aluminum production rose `5%` to `636,000` metric tons after the San Ciprian restart; aluminum shipments rose `18%` sequentially.
- Portfolio and capital actions include the Ma'aden stake sale, permanent Kwinana closure, San Ciprian restart through a joint venture, AliGroup transaction, gallium-plant approval, and a `$65M` Mosjoen smelter investment.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate Alumina, Aluminum, Bauxite, Energy, third-party shipments, internal transfers, realized prices, premiums, and production; reported EBITDA is not through-cycle owner cash.
2. Reconcile free cash flow for sustaining smelter/refinery/mining capital, restart expenditure, inventory, power, environmental remediation, pensions, and JV obligations.
3. Test San Ciprian, Pinjarra, Kwinana, and other restart/closure effects for recurring capacity, ramp cost, reliability, labor, and cash requirements.
4. Keep aluminum price, alumina price, Midwest premium, tariffs, FX, power/gas, and weather/geopolitical disruption separate from operating improvements.
5. Reconcile Ma'aden proceeds, AliGroup, strategic-material projects, joint ventures, debt reduction, and any associated claims before promoting cash or per-share value.
6. Treat adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income, dividends, and buybacks as residual claims requiring remediation, debt, closure, restart, and diluted-share validation.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what mid-cycle aluminum/alumina prices, premiums, production, power costs, restart probability, sustaining capital, remediation, JV funding, taxes, debt, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether electrification, transport, packaging, strategic-material demand, energy security, geopolitical curtailment, and carbon policy can support returns through commodity and power shocks while fixed smelters, refineries, labor, and environmental obligations remain.

## Promotion boundary

`alcoa-alumina-aluminum-qualified; price-production-power-restart-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from realized price and production/shipment to collection, power, sustaining and restart capital, remediation, JV/portfolio cash, debt, labor, environmental obligations, and diluted common residual. EBITDA, free cash flow, production records, prices, premiums, restarts, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
