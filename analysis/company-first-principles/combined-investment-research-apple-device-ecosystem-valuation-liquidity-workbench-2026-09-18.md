# Apple device ecosystem valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Apple from broad consumer-technology evidence into a
company-specific valuation object. It separates iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables,
Home and Accessories, and Services, then tests installed-base retention,
replacement cycles, supply-chain concentration, services collection, R&D,
working capital, tariffs, regulatory claims, capital returns, and diluted common
residual. It does not treat device units, Services revenue, gross margin,
operating cash flow, free cash flow, or repurchases as normalized owner cash.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apple | Ecosystem cash after device sell-through, channel settlement, Services billing and collection, component and manufacturing commitments, R&D, silicon and software investment, warranty, regulation, debt, and dilution | Product and silicon R&D, tooling and supplier commitments, inventory, retail and support infrastructure, data and platform costs, acquisitions, warranty, working capital, and SBC replacement | Premium-device replacement slowdown, China or geographic weakness, tariff and supply-chain shock, App Store or payment regulation, services take-rate pressure, product execution failure, debt/refinancing, or dilution | Installed base and Services grow while device sell-through, channel collections, Services regulation, R&D intensity, supply commitments, gross-margin quality, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 net sales were `$416.2B`; total gross margin was `$195.2B`, with
  Services gross margin at `75.4%`; R&D was `$34.6B`.
- The active installed base exceeded `2.5B` devices. FY2026 Q3 revenue was
  `$109.4B` and net income was `$29.8B`; Q2 revenue was `$111.2B` and net
  income was `$29.6B`; Q1 revenue was `$143.8B` and net income was `$42.1B`.
- FY2025 capital return included `$89.3B` of repurchases and `$15.4B` of
  dividends. The Q2 materials added a `$100B` repurchase authorization and a
  dividend increase, but authorization is not cash spent and buybacks are not
  proof of surplus owner cash.
- Tariffs and trade measures can affect component availability, pricing, gross
  margin, and geographic supply routes. DMA and DOJ antitrust pressure remain
  live claims against Services economics.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile device demand from units and channel sell-in to end-user sell-
   through, returns, inventory, receivables, supplier commitments, and cash
   settlement. Revenue growth is not the same as ecosystem cash.
2. Split Services billing into App Store, subscriptions, licensing, payments,
   and other streams; test take-rate, developer settlement, deferred revenue,
   regulation, and the costs needed to preserve the platform.
3. Test the installed base for active use, replacement timing, geography,
   product mix, and attachment rather than treating the device count as a
   perpetual annuity.
4. Keep tariffs, temporary refunds, currency, mix, and channel timing separate
   from durable gross-margin improvement; connect R&D to product, silicon, AI,
   and software delivery rather than expensing it away as ordinary overhead.
5. Treat repurchases and dividend growth as residual claims only after supply
   commitments, inventory, warranty, litigation, regulatory remedies, R&D, and
   debt obligations are funded.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what active-device retention,
replacement rate, Services attachment, take-rate, geographic mix, R&D burden,
reinvestment rate, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn
Alden-style stress test asks whether premium consumption, China exposure,
tariffs, supplier concentration, regulation, platform dependency, and rates can
remain liquid through a hardware downcycle without confusing ecosystem scale
with durable common-owner cash.

## Promotion boundary

`apple-qualified; device-ecosystem-and-services-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from device sell-through and Services
billing to collection, supplier and manufacturing commitments, R&D and platform
reinvestment, warranty and regulatory claims, debt, capital returns, and diluted
common residual. Revenue, installed-base size, Services margin, OCF, FCF, and
buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
