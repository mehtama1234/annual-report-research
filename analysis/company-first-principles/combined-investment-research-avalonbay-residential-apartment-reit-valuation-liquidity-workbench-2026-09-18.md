# Residential apartment REIT and merger valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves AvalonBay from current-period residential real-estate evidence into a company-specific valuation, occupancy, rent, same-store, development, maintenance, capital-structure, merger, and owner-cash test. It treats AvalonBay as a multifamily housing platform, not as a logistics, data-center, or generic property REIT.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AvalonBay | Collected apartment rent and property cash after vacancy, concessions, operating expenses, maintenance, redevelopment, development, property taxes, interest, dividends, and dilution | Apartment maintenance and renovation, development pipeline, redevelopment, land, property operations, leasing, technology, environmental work, and merger integration | Rent growth and affordability, occupancy, turnover, concessions, development yields, supply, rates, debt refinancing, merger execution, property taxes, and dilution | FFO, Core FFO, same-store NOI, occupancy, rent growth, development pipeline, dividends, or share issuance rise while rent collection, concessions, maintenance burden, development yield, debt, merger economics, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Q4 `2025` FFO per share was `$2.80` and Core FFO per share `$2.85`; full-year 2025 same-store residential revenue increased about `2.5%`.
- Q2 `2026` FFO per share was `$2.73` and Core FFO per share `$2.86`; same-store residential revenue increased about `1.6%` year over year and same-store NOI also rose.
- Q2 `2026` materials described a broad apartment development pipeline with weighted-average projected NOI on cost in the low-`6%` range.
- AvalonBay proposed an all-stock merger of equals with Equity Residential, creating a major strategic and capital-allocation overlay for the 2026 interpretation.
- The residential cash object remains property-level rent collected after occupancy, concessions, operating expenses, maintenance, development, interest, taxes, dividends, and common dilution; FFO and Core FFO are diagnostic measures, not automatically owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile leased occupancy, rent collection, effective rent, concessions, turnover, same-store revenue, property expenses, and NOI; headline rent growth is not collected cash.
2. Separate same-store, new development, redevelopment, acquisition, disposition, and merger cohorts; yields and maintenance burdens differ.
3. Test the Equity Residential merger through exchange ratio, integration, portfolio overlap, debt, dilution, property economics, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep FFO, Core FFO, AFFO, development yield, dividends, and share issuance separate from recurring common-owner cash after maintenance and capital burdens.
5. Reconcile property taxes, insurance, repairs, capitalized costs, debt, interest, liquidity, preferred or noncontrolling claims, taxes, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what rent growth, occupancy, same-store NOI, development yield, supply, maintenance capex, leverage, and cost of equity the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether affordability, new apartment supply, recession, rates, property taxes, insurance, refinancing, and merger execution preserve residential cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`avalonbay-residential-reit-qualified; rent-occupancy-development-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from property cohorts to rent collection, occupancy, concessions, property expenses, maintenance and development cash, merger funding, debt service, taxes, dividends, and diluted common residual. FFO, Core FFO, same-store NOI, occupancy, rent growth, development yield, dividends, and share issuance remain diagnostic inputs.
