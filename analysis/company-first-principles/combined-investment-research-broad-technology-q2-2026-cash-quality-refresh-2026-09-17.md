# Broad technology Q2 2026 cash-quality refresh

Research date: 2026-09-17

## Finding

The latest official interim filings sharpen the contrast between two broad-
technology control points. Fortinet monetizes a hardware-attached security
and support position with substantial billings and deferred revenue. Cloudflare
monetizes an edge network and software platform whose growth is accompanied by
meaningful infrastructure, contract-acquisition, and stock-compensation costs.
Neither reported FCF nor contracted revenue is treated as normalized common-
owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

Fortinet's deferred revenue is primarily unrecognized service and support
revenue. Its H1 deferred-revenue increase is included in OCF, and its billings
measure adds the change in deferred revenue to recognized revenue. This is a
legitimate contract-timing mechanism, not a fraud finding, but it means the
cash conversion screen must test renewal, service cost, term length, and
future delivery rather than treating the entire increase as recurring excess
cash. Fortinet also reports $154.0M of H1 SBC and $972.8M of repurchases; the
repurchase decision therefore requires a diluted-share and replacement-cost
bridge.

Cloudflare's deferred revenue increased by $127.1M, while remaining
performance obligations were $2.732B at June 30, 2026, with 64% expected to be
recognized over the following twelve months. These are useful visibility
objects, not current-period cash or guaranteed margin. Revenue grew 35% H1
over H1, but gross margin declined to 72% from 75%; management attributed the
cost increase in part to third-party technology services, colocations, network
and bandwidth, and server deployment. H1 OCF included $274.1M of SBC and $63.5M
of deferred contract-acquisition-cost amortization, while the cash flow also
included $84.5M of new contract-acquisition-cost capitalization. The adjusted
FCF surface therefore requires a retention, cost-to-serve, infrastructure-
replacement, and dilution bridge.

## Cloudflare Q2 standalone cash-quality refresh — 2026-09-18

Cloudflare's Q2 release makes the current-period separation more explicit:
revenue was `$696.1M`, GAAP operating loss was `$205.7M`, operating cash flow
was `$117.6M`, and reported free cash flow was `$56.4M`. Q2 also included
`$150.7M` of restructuring and other charges, while PP&E and capitalized
internal-use software consumed `$49.96M` and `$11.22M`, respectively. Cash,
cash equivalents, and available-for-sale securities were `$4.163B` at June 30.

The release also reports current RPO growth of `35%`, but RPO remains a future
performance obligation rather than collected owner cash. The Q2 standalone
bridge therefore reinforces the required test: separate restructuring,
network/compute cost, capitalized software, SBC, contract-acquisition spend,
and GAAP losses before assigning value to AI or edge growth.

## Owner-cash and valuation implication

- Fortinet's reported FCF is a liquidity diagnostic after PP&E, not a finished
  owner-cash measure. The next promotion requires service-renewal durability,
  hardware-refresh burden, deferred-revenue conversion, SBC dilution, taxes,
  acquisitions, and debt-service treatment.
- Cloudflare's reported FCF is more visibly constrained by network and software
  investment, but the H1 result still does not capture the full economic cost
  of R&D, service labor, contract acquisition, SBC, leases, and future
  capacity. RPO enters valuation only through retention, conversion, and
  cost-to-serve assumptions.

Damodaran-style valuation should state the growth, margin, reinvestment, and
terminal-duration assumptions implied by price. Lyn Alden-style stress should
test enterprise budgets, semiconductor and infrastructure cycles, rates,
cyber incidents, compute/network costs, export or supply constraints, and
long-duration-growth repricing. A billings, RPO, or FCF multiple cannot replace
those tests.

## Promotion consequence

This is a material current-period upgrade to the broad-technology lane. The
control-point thesis is stronger because service commitments, performance
obligations, infrastructure investment, gross-margin pressure, SBC, and
financing actions are now period-controlled for Fortinet and Cloudflare. The
lane remains `cash-quality-qualified; owner-cash-and-cycle-open; no-ranking`.
The decisive next evidence is multi-period service renewal and cost-to-serve
for Fortinet, and Cloudflare's retention-to-margin-to-infrastructure-capacity
bridge with diluted-share treatment.
