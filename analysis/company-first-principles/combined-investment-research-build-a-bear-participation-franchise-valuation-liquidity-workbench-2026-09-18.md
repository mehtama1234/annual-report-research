# Branded participation, ceremony, and franchise valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Build-A-Bear from current-period toy and retail evidence into a company-specific valuation, participation, channel, inventory, tariff, and owner-cash test. It treats the company as a retailtainment and memory-making platform, not as a conventional toy manufacturer.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Build-A-Bear | Collected ceremony, product, gifting, licensing, partner, and franchise cash after store labor/occupancy, plush and apparel inventory, tariffs, royalties, marketing, commercial terms, and dilution | Owned and partner stores, stuffing/experience equipment, inventory, licensed product, e-commerce, entertainment, franchise support, marketing, and working capital | Direct-store traffic, e-commerce, mall occupancy, tariff/sourcing, seasonal inventory, licensing, partner/franchise execution, and dilution | Revenue and pre-tax income rise while ceremony/store traffic, inventory turns, tariff-adjusted margin, partner/franchise contribution, owned-store occupancy, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 total revenue was `$529.8M`, pre-tax income `$67.2M`, diluted EPS `$3.99`, and cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash about `$26.8M`; the company had no outstanding borrowings on its credit facility.
- At FY2025 year-end, Build-A-Bear had `662` global locations: `375` corporately managed, `178` partner-operated, and `109` franchised; fiscal 2025 net new experience locations were `64`.
- Q4 2025 revenue was `$154.5M`, up `2.7%`; quarterly pre-tax income was `$21.5M` versus `$27.5M`; full-year capital returned to shareholders was `$39.0M`.
- Q1 2026 revenue was `$125.3M`, down `2.4%`; commercial and international franchise revenue increased `34.1%` to `$11.8M`; cash was `$26.2M`; inventory was `$77.8M`, up `7.7%`; and capital returned was `$14.2M`.
- Q1 included approximately `$10.4M` of recorded tariff-related benefit, including a `$7M` IEEPA refund tied to prior-year costs; production was primarily in Vietnam after prior China exposure, leaving tariff and sourcing risk open.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile ceremony/store revenue to foot traffic, conversion, units, product attach, average transaction, e-commerce, returns, and occupancy; a plush sale is not the whole experience economics.
2. Separate direct-to-consumer, commercial/wholesale, partner-operated, and international franchise revenue; each has different margin, working capital, royalty, and capital requirements.
3. Keep tariff refunds and inventory-value benefits separate from recurring pre-tax income; Q1 guidance improvement partly reflected non-recurring tariff accounting.
4. Test licensed characters, fandom, gifting, and collectibles through launch productivity, royalties, inventory turns, markdowns, and repeat/occasion behavior.
5. Reconcile store expansion, leases, occupancy, labor, stuffing/experience equipment, franchise support, and e-commerce investment before promoting capital returns.
6. Treat capital returned, adjusted pre-tax income, and franchise growth as residual claims requiring tariff-normalized cash, inventory, lease, and diluted-share validation.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what visit conversion, product attach, repeat gifting, licensed-product margin, owned-store productivity, franchise royalty, commercial sell-through, inventory turns, tariff-normalized cost, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether discretionary gifting, fandom, mall traffic, household budgets, tariffs, freight, and seasonal shocks can weaken the direct relationship while stores, inventory, leases, and experience labor remain.

## Promotion boundary

`build-a-bear-participation-qualified; ceremony-store-franchise-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from visits and product/experience sales to collection, inventory, tariff-normalized margin, store occupancy/labor, partner/franchise contribution, licensing, working capital, leases, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, pre-tax income, EPS, location counts, franchise growth, tariff refunds, and capital returns remain diagnostic inputs.
