# CA-06 quantified allocation surface

Research date: `2026-09-16`

This artifact consolidates existing source-backed sensitivities that quantify
the remaining cash-denominator uncertainty. It is an allocation surface, not a
new owner-cash estimate and not a cross-sector ranking.

| Pilot / case | Lower screened cash ($M) | Upper screened cash ($M) | What the range varies | What remains unobserved |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Wheaton–Antamina annualized stream | -42.310 | 444.446 | 0%/0% to 100%/100% mechanical allocation of company finance cost and tax expense; separate 0%–100% maturity-bullet screen | BHP-only settlement, Antamina tax/interest, principal allocation/repayment source, waterfall, and corporate residual |
| TJX H1 FY2027 | 1,631 | 2,186 | Conservative support-removal screen versus reported cash-after-property | Maintenance mix, inventory, leases, tax, services, dilution, and seasonality |
| Target H1 2026 | 597 | 2,115 | Conservative support-removal screen versus the derived `$4.519B - $2.404B` cash-after-property figure; the 10-Q separately reports `$2.397B` total investing cash use after `$7M` of other investing activity | Maintenance mix, supplier finance, inventory, leases, tax, services, dilution, and seasonality |
| Walmart H1 FY2027 | 981 | 5,529 | Known-growth/support screen versus reported cash-after-capex | Maintenance mix, supplier finance, leases, tax, services, SBC, and seasonality |
| Apollo–Athene parent flow | 0 | 110 | 0% to 100% attribution of Athene H1 distributions to AGM | Dated AGM receipt, unrestricted cash, eliminations, senior claims, and common residual |

The ranges are not additive across rows. The Wheaton range is a paired
company-level tax/finance allocation sensitivity; retail lower screens remove
identified support candidates but do not solve every burden; and Apollo's range
is an attribution frontier whose unrestricted-parent and common-owner fields
remain unknown. The Wheaton maturity-bullet screen is a separate
terminal-liability sensitivity and is not added to the annualized range. The
surface therefore makes uncertainty quantitative while
preserving legal-entity, period, and denominator boundaries.

Target's refreshed FY2025 annual filing supplies a separate reported
cash-after-property screen of `$2.835B` (`$6.562B` operating cash flow less
`$3.727B` property spending), and confirms that ordinary repairs and
maintenance are expensed as incurred. That annual observation is retained in
the retail owner-cash bridge but is not inserted into the H1 Target range
above: the periods, support effects, working-capital timing, and capex mix are
not interchangeable. The annual filing still does not provide a capitalized
maintenance-versus-growth allocation.

## Promotion rule

No row may enter a promoted normalized-owner-cash conclusion unless the missing
allocation is replaced by a dated source-backed settlement, allocation, or
reconciliation. Until then, use the ranges only to test valuation sensitivity
and thesis-breaker conditions.
