# Defense mission systems and federal technology valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves CACI from current-period defense-technology evidence into a company-specific valuation, contract, backlog, acquisition, working-capital, and owner-cash test. It treats CACI as a technology-first national-security mission-systems company, not as generic IT outsourcing or broad defense hardware.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CACI | Collected federal mission-system cash after contract performance, cleared labor, software and sensing delivery, customer acceptance, ARKA and other acquisition integration, working capital, debt, interest, and dilution | Technical labor and clearances, software and mission R&D, electronic warfare and sensing platforms, bid/proposal costs, acquisitions, contract mobilization, working capital, and integration | Appropriations and procurement timing, contract protests, fixed-price execution, customer concentration, cleared-talent scarcity, acquisition integration, leverage, reimbursement, and dilution | Revenue, backlog, funded backlog, awards, EBITDA, adjusted EPS, or FCF rise while funded work converts poorly, program margin, customer acceptance, ARKA integration, collections, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$8.63B`, net income `$499.8M`, diluted EPS `$22.32`, adjusted diluted EPS `$26.48`, EBITDA `$966.8M`, and free cash flow about `$442.5M`; contract awards were `$10B`.
- FY2026 Q4 revenue was `$2.71B`, up `17.6%` with `11.6%` organic growth; annual revenue reached `$9.6B`, annual EBITDA margin `12.3%`, and annual awards `$10.2B`.
- FY2026 Q4 total backlog was `$32.0B` and funded backlog `$5.4B`; these are visibility measures, not receipts or owner cash.
- FY2026 Q3 revenue was `$2.4B`, EBITDA `$289.7M`, EBITDA margin `12.3%`, and ARKA-related transaction expense `$17.4M`; backlog was `$33.4B` and funded backlog `$5.0B`.
- CACI's technical surface includes electronic warfare, counter-unmanned systems, secure networks, optical communications, space sensing, geospatial intelligence, software-defined modernization, and AI-enabled mission tools.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile awards, backlog, funded backlog, revenue, billings, contract assets, receivables, customer acceptance, and cash collection; backlog is not a bank receipt.
2. Separate cost-plus, fixed-price, time-and-materials, classified, and program cohorts; margin and cash risk differ by contract structure.
3. Test ARKA and other acquisitions through purchase price, integration expense, technical talent retention, cross-sell, program acceptance, working capital, leverage, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EPS, FCF, transaction expenses, contract modifications, and organic growth separate from recurring collected common-owner cash.
5. Reconcile cleared-labor costs, bid/proposal spending, software and sensing R&D, contract mobilization, debt, interest, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what funded backlog conversion, award renewal, program margin, technical differentiation, acquisition return, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether federal budgets, geopolitical urgency, procurement delays, protests, fixed-price overruns, talent scarcity, rates, and leverage preserve CACI's cash conversion while mission complexity rises.

## Promotion boundary

`caci-defense-mission-systems-qualified; contract-conversion-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from named programs and contract types to customer acceptance, invoice and collection, cost-to-complete, acquisition integration, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, awards, backlog, funded backlog, EBITDA, adjusted EPS, organic growth, and FCF remain diagnostic inputs.
