# Caesars Rewards and digital gaming valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Caesars Entertainment from current-period evidence into a company-specific valuation object. It tests regional casinos, Las Vegas, Caesars Rewards, universal wallet, sportsbook, iGaming, Racebook, customer frequency, gaming hold, capex, leases, debt, cash interest, and diluted common residual. It does not pool Caesars with DraftKings’ digital-only model, Las Vegas Sands’ Asia resorts, or hotel franchisors.

## Decision matrix

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 net revenues were `$11.486B`, total adjusted EBITDA `$3.624B`, Caesars Digital revenue `$1.408B`, and Caesars Digital adjusted EBITDA `$236M`.
- Q2 2026 revenue was about `$3.0B`, consolidated adjusted EBITDA `$920M`, Caesars Digital revenue `$351M`, Caesars Digital adjusted EBITDA `$68M`, and Regional revenue `$1.570B`.
- Caesars Rewards spans resorts and digital wagering; the universal digital wallet links sportsbook, Racebook, iGaming, rewards, and wagering credits.
- The business remains asset- and debt-heavy: property capex, leases, interest, regional gaming, and digital promotions all compete with loyalty investment and capital returns.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate Las Vegas, regional, digital, sportsbook, iGaming, Racebook, hotel, food, entertainment, and rewards economics.
2. Reconcile gaming hold, promotions, wallet balances, loyalty credits, state taxes, payment settlement, and customer acquisition.
3. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow to property capex, leases, cash interest, digital investment, debt repayment, and dilution.
4. Test whether Rewards raises frequency and spend or simply subsidizes gaming and digital promotions.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The expectation test asks what gaming volume, hold, Rewards frequency, digital margin, regional recovery, capex, leverage, and cost of capital are embedded in the valuation. The stress test asks whether consumer entertainment budgets, regulation, rates, gaming volatility, digital competition, leases, and debt service support durable collected cash.

## Promotion boundary

`caesars-qualified; rewards-digital-and-debt-service-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from physical and digital gaming to collection, hold, promotions, wallet settlement, loyalty retention, property capex, leases, debt, claims, funding, and diluted common residual. Revenue, adjusted EBITDA, digital growth, Rewards members, guidance, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
