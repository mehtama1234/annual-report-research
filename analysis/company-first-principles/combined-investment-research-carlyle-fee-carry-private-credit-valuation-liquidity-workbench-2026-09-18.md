# Private-markets fee, carry, and credit-platform valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves The Carlyle Group from annual-report evidence into a
company-specific private-equity, private-credit, AlpInvest, fee-related,
performance-income, and common-owner residual test. It keeps fee-bearing AUM,
realized carry, accrued marks, perpetual capital, client-owned fund assets,
partner allocations, and available-but-undrawn capital separate from ordinary
public-market asset managers and operating conglomerates.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carlyle | After-tax fee-related earnings plus normalized realized performance income after compensation, fundraising, partner allocations, clawbacks, balance-sheet investments, debt, and dilution | Investment professionals, fundraising/distribution, technology, compliance, valuation, co-investment, GP commitments, credit platforms, and strategic product buildout | Fundraising slowdown, fee-rate compression, redemptions, weak exits, clawbacks, credit losses, marks, partner claims, debt, or distribution shortfall | AUM, available capital, distributable earnings, or accrued carry rise while fee-paying capital, realizations, net returns, common cash, or diluted value deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Q2 2026 total AUM was approximately `$485B`, fee-earning AUM `$334B`, and perpetual-capital fee-earning AUM `$113B`. Total AUM and fee-earning AUM are different valuation denominators.
- Q2 2026 fee-related earnings were approximately `$358M`, distributable earnings `$472M`, and realized net performance revenue `$115M`; Q1 realized performance revenue was only about `$21M` while FRE remained about `$300M`.
- FY2025 ended with approximately `$1.2B` FRE, `$1.7B` distributable earnings, `$357M` realized net performance revenue, `$88B` available capital, `$53.7B` inflows, and `$54.5B` deployment.
- Approximately `$2.9B` of accrued net performance revenues at year-end 2025 were not equivalent to cash received. Carry requires exit, distribution, hurdle, tax, clawback, escrow, and partner-allocation tests.
- Available capital is not invested capital, and client-owned fund assets are not parent liquidity. Carlyle's platform must be valued after personnel, fundraising, compliance, technology, GP commitments, debt, and common claims.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile total AUM to fee-earning AUM, fee rates, retention, fundraising,
   deployment, redemptions, market appreciation, and product mix.
2. Separate FRE, distributable earnings, realized carry, accrued performance
   revenue, GAAP income, and actual parent cash after taxes and partner claims.
3. Test private-credit performance through borrower losses, nonaccruals,
   restructuring, valuation marks, leverage, and fund-level versus parent-level
   exposure.
4. Roll carry through realizations, distributions, hurdles, clawbacks, escrow,
   holding periods, taxes, and net-of-fee/net-of-carry investor outcomes.
5. Treat available capital, dividends, buybacks, and balance-sheet investments
   as diagnostic until common cash and diluted-share effects are evidenced.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what fee-paying AUM, fee rate, FRE
margin, realized carry, realization cadence, reinvestment, taxes, partner share,
and cost of capital the equity price requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test
asks whether rates, refinancing, private-credit losses, exit markets, liquidity
premiums, institutional allocations, and capital calls preserve the platform's
cash and credibility.

## Promotion boundary

`carlyle-private-markets-qualified; fee-carry-realization-and-common-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from fee-paying capital to fee
collection, operating cost, compensation, realized performance cash, carry
claims, taxes, GP commitments, debt, and diluted common residual. AUM, available
capital, distributable earnings, accrued carry, marks, and buybacks remain
diagnostic inputs.
