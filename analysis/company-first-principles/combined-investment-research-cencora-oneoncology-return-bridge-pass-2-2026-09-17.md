# Cencora OneOncology acquisition-return bridge — pass 2 — 2026-09-17

## Decision

Cencora's OneOncology purchase is now bounded as a named acquisition cohort,
but no post-close cohort return can yet be calculated. The `$1.087B`
remeasurement gain and adjusted FCF must remain outside operating performance;
the return test must carry cash consideration, contingent consideration,
retained equity, physician economics, debt, working capital, amortization, and
incremental cash.

Status: `OneOncology-cohort-bounded; acquisition-return-open; no-ranking`.

## Financing and operating perimeter

Cencora raised `$3.0B` of senior notes and `$1.5B` of variable-rate term loans
to fund part of OneOncology, while repurchasing `$1.008B` of shares and paying
`$360.6M` of dividends. The acquisition return must therefore be tested after
interest, refinancing, share count, and contingent consideration—not merely
against adjusted EPS.

Working capital used approximately `$1.1B` of operating cash over nine months:
receivable growth was approximately `$0.8B`, inventory growth approximately
`$1.0B`, and accounts-payable growth supplied approximately `$0.6B`. At June
30, cash was `$2.815B`, receivables `$25.448B`, and inventory `$21.030B`.
The cohort bridge must distinguish organic distribution funding from
OneOncology/VIE/Profarma and held-for-sale balances.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

1. Do not call `$1.142B` adjusted FCF acquisition-adjusted owner cash while
   `$4.590B` net OneOncology cash and `$752M` contingent consideration remain
   outside the metric.
2. Keep the `$1.087B` remeasurement gain and `$381.9M` LIFO credit out of
   terminal operating margins unless their economic recurrence is demonstrated.
3. Treat the `$102M` opioid-liability reduction and `$86.8M` derivative-lawsuit
   credit as non-recurring/claim-related until cash-paid schedules prove otherwise.
4. Join acquired revenue, gross profit, amortization, physician economics,
   integration cost, inventory, receivables, and collections to the cohort.
5. Carry senior notes, variable-rate interest, repurchases, dividends,
   retained equity, noncontrolling interests, and dilution into common-owner
   residual cash.

## Promotion gate and thesis breaker

Promotion requires a same-entity OneOncology cohort schedule for the first
post-close periods: revenue, gross profit, operating cost, management-service
agreement economics, amortization, physician/retained equity, integration
cash, working-capital settlement, debt interest, contingent consideration,
taxes, and diluted common-owner cash.

The thesis breaker is activated if specialty-acquisition revenue and adjusted
EPS rise while incremental cash fails to cover working-capital funding,
integration and physician claims, debt/contingent consideration, and dilution.
This is a filing-based falsifier, not an allegation of misconduct.
