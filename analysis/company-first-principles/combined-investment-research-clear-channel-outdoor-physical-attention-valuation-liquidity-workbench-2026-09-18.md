# Clear Channel Outdoor physical attention valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Clear Channel Outdoor from current-period evidence into a company-specific valuation object. It tests billboard, transit, airport, street-furniture, and digital-display inventory; advertiser collection; municipal and airport contracts; capex; portfolio sales; merger consideration; very high interest burden; debt; and diluted common residual. It does not pool the company with agencies, publishers, broadcasters, or ad-tech platforms.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clear Channel Outdoor | Place-based advertising cash after inventory utilization, contract renewals, digital-display conversion, maintenance, permits, lease commitments, advertiser collection, debt service, merger costs, and dilution | Display capex, maintenance, municipal and airport contracts, portfolio repositioning, data/programmatic tools, working capital, debt, and shares | Advertiser slowdown, contract loss, digital conversion failure, lease or permit pressure, merger failure, asset-sale dependence, or interest burden | Revenue and adjusted EBITDA rise while inventory utilization, advertiser collection, contract renewal, AFFO after capex and interest, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- 2025 revenue was about `$1.604B`, up `6.6%`; Q2 2026 revenue was about `$438.0M`, adjusted EBITDA about `$143.4M`, and AFFO about `$44.9M`.
- The company announced a pending take-private merger at `$2.43` per share and expected 2026 cash interest of about `$401M` and 2027 cash interest of about `$390M`.
- The Spain sale closed for approximately `$132.3M`, with proceeds intended for debt reduction subject to the merger outcome.
- Digital displays, data analytics, and programmatic buying are modernization claims layered on a physical inventory base exposed to municipal, airport, transit, maintenance, and traffic economics.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate reported revenue from digital conversion, portfolio sales, foreign exchange, advertiser mix, and contract timing.
2. Reconcile AFFO to maintenance capex, display investment, leases, permits, interest, merger costs, working capital, and debt repayment.
3. Test whether programmatic and data tools improve inventory utilization and collection or merely repackage fixed physical assets.
4. Keep Spain proceeds, merger consideration, and asset sales outside recurring owner cash until legal-entity funding and common-residual joins are clear.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The expectation test asks what occupancy, pricing, digital-display mix, contract renewal, capex, interest burden, leverage, and cost of capital are embedded in the valuation. The stress test asks whether advertiser demand and public-space visibility survive rates, traffic changes, municipal and airport negotiations, digital competition, maintenance needs, and refinancing pressure.

## Promotion boundary

`clear-channel-outdoor-qualified; inventory-utilization-and-debt-service-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from physical inventory and advertiser spend to collection, utilization, contract renewal, capex, permits, leases, merger funding, debt service, claims, and diluted common residual. Revenue, adjusted EBITDA, AFFO, guidance, asset-sale proceeds, and merger value remain diagnostic inputs.
