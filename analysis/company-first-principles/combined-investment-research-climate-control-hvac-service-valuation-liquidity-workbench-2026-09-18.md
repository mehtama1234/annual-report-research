# Climate-control HVAC and service valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Trane Technologies from cooling-demand and backlog
evidence into a company-specific valuation and liquidity object. It separates
HVAC/transport refrigeration equipment, controls, service, retrofit, heat
pumps, and data-center cooling from HVAC distribution, electrical interfaces,
and industrial automation.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trane Technologies | Qualified climate-control and installed-base cash after equipment, service/controls attachment, backlog delivery, technician labor, warranty, inventory, capex, R&D, acquisitions, debt, and dilution | HVAC and transport systems, controls/software, service technicians, inventory, warranty, capacity, refrigerant transition, R&D, acquisitions, and common capital | Commercial/data-center project delay, service non-attachment, technician shortage, refrigerant transition, customer concentration, working-capital use, acquisition under-return, or refinancing | Bookings, backlog, adjusted EPS, and FCF conversion rise while service attachment, project margin, collection, technician economics, acquisition return, or diluted common cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was about `$21.3B`, organic growth `6%`, adjusted continuing EPS `$13.06`, and adjusted EBITDA margin `20.1%`.
- FY2025 continuing OCF was `$3.220B`, capex `$383M`, cash acquisitions `$276M`, and OCF less capex/acquisitions about `$2.561B`.
- FY2025 bookings were `$22.6B`, book-to-bill `106%`, and backlog about `$7.8B`; Q2 2026 backlog reached `$12.1B` with organic bookings up `37%`.
- Q2 2026 Americas Commercial HVAC bookings rose `50%`; demand is strong but must be separated from customer concentration, project timing, and margin.
- Management-defined FCF was `$2.887B` and included restructuring, legal, M&A, asset-sale, and incentive-program adjustments that require cash-paid persistence testing.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate equipment, project, service, controls/software, retrofit, transport, and data-center revenue; backlog is not recurring service cash.
2. Reconcile backlog to customer funding, permits, capacity, technicians, acceptance, billing, collection, cancellation, deferral, and gross margin.
3. Measure installed-base service attachment, renewal, technician utilization, labor cost, warranty, parts inventory, and customer retention.
4. Test data-center orders separately from broader commercial HVAC; concentration and hyperscaler negotiation can alter returns.
5. Reconcile management FCF to GAAP OCF, working capital, capex, acquisitions, restructuring, legal payments, asset-sale proceeds, and incentive cash payments.
6. Test acquisition cohorts after purchase price, integration, service attachment, working capital, capex, and cash return.
7. Treat bookings, backlog, adjusted earnings, FCF conversion, and buybacks as diagnostic until recurring service and diluted cash joins are evidenced.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what equipment growth, service/controls
attachment, project margin, backlog conversion, technician productivity,
refrigerant transition, capex, acquisition return, and cost of capital the
valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether commercial
construction, data-center finance, energy costs, rates, regulation, cold-chain
demand, and labor supply can support cooling investment through a cycle.

## Promotion boundary

`climate-control-hvac-service-qualified; backlog-attachment-and-common-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from equipment and backlog to
service/controls renewal, project acceptance, collection, technician capacity,
warranty, inventory, capex/R&D, acquisition return, debt, incentive payments,
and diluted common residual. Bookings, backlog, adjusted EPS, OCF, and FCF
conversion remain diagnostic inputs.
