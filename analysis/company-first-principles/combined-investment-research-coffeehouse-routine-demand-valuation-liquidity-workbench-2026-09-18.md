# Coffeehouse routine-demand valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Starbucks from routine-demand and turnaround evidence into
a company-specific valuation and liquidity object. It separates company-
operated coffeehouse economics, licensed stores, comparable transactions,
Rewards/stored value, labor/service investment, and store capex from restaurant
franchise systems and branded beverage manufacturers.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starbucks | Mature coffeehouse and loyalty cash after transactions, ticket, labor/service, product costs, occupancy, Rewards, stores, technology, closures, remodels, debt, and dilution | Company-operated labor, store maintenance/remodels, new units, mobile ordering, loyalty/payment, coffee/food/packaging, licensed-store support, restructuring, and common capital | Transaction decline, labor/input/tariff inflation, service failure, store closures, stored-value claims, licensee weakness, China transaction, debt, or dilution | Comparable sales or ticket rises while transactions, store contribution, service, maintenance cash, stored-value economics, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$37.2B`, operating income `$2.9B` versus `$5.4B` prior year, operating margin `7.9%` versus `15.0%`, and diluted EPS `$1.63` versus `$3.31`.
- Q2 FY2026 global comparable sales rose `6.2%`, transactions `3.8%`, and average ticket `2.3%`; revenue rose `9%` to `$9.5B` while operating margin remained under repair.
- Q3 FY2026 global comparable sales rose `7.9%` led by transactions, but China transaction effects distorted consolidated revenue.
- FY2025 OCF was `$4.748B`, PP&E purchases `$2.306B`, SBC about `$318M`; the residual after PP&E and SBC was about `$2.124B` before repair-program and other claims.
- FY2025 dividends were about `$2.771B` and net acquisition cash `$177M`; dividends exceeded the SBC-adjusted residual, so payout was not proof of fully funded owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Split comparable sales into transactions, ticket, price, mix, promotions, delivery, and product category; ticket growth alone is weak demand evidence.
2. Reconcile labor/service investment to throughput, wait time, retention, transactions, store contribution, and customer experience rather than treating labor as a simple margin lever.
3. Separate company-operated and licensed economics, including licensee health, standards, product supply, support, and brand-control claims.
4. Reconcile stored-value/card loads and Rewards liabilities to redemption, breakage, deferred revenue, refunds, and future service obligations.
5. Split PP&E into maintenance, remodels, new stores, technology, closures, and repair-program spending; do not use lower capex from underinvestment as durable cash.
6. Isolate China transaction proceeds/exposure, restructuring, closures, and repeated turnaround costs from continuing store economics.
7. Treat comparable sales, adjusted earnings, OCF, dividends, and buybacks as diagnostic until mature-store and diluted cash per share recover.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what transaction recovery, ticket,
store contribution, labor productivity, product margin, licensed mix, Rewards
economics, maintenance capex, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The
Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether routine demand survives household
budget pressure, coffee/food costs, wages, rent, tariffs, rates, and a shift to
home preparation or lower-priced competitors.

## Promotion boundary

`coffeehouse-routine-demand-qualified; service-and-common-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from transactions and ticket
to store margin, labor/service, product cost, Rewards/stored-value settlement,
licensed-store health, maintenance/growth capital, debt, and diluted common
residual. Comparable sales, traffic, ticket, adjusted earnings, OCF, and
dividends remain diagnostic inputs.
