# Commercial landscape services valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench adds BrightView as a distinct commercial landscaping and
outdoor-site-services lane. It separates recurring maintenance, development
projects, seasonal labor, snow/weather, fleet renewal, and leveraged common
claims from ABM facility services, pest-control routes, environmental services,
and specialty construction.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BrightView | Maintenance-contract cash after seasonal staffing, wage recovery, route density, fleet/equipment renewal, fuel, insurance, working capital, development projects, debt, preferred claims, and diluted common residual | Crews, H-2B labor, recruiting/training, vehicles, mowers, irrigation, branch density, equipment, maintenance/growth capex, project materials, receivables financing, and common capital | H-2B availability, wage inflation, weather/snow, commercial-property budgets, rebids, fleet replacement, receivables timing, leverage, preferred dividends, or refinancing | Revenue declines while adjusted margin rises, or adjusted FCF and buybacks rise while staffing, fleet renewal, collections, debt, preferred claims, or common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 operating cash flow was `$291.8M`; capex was `$254.2M`, including `$205.0M` in Maintenance Services; adjusted FCF was `$65.2M` after equipment-sale proceeds.
- FY2025 revenue was `$2.673B`; Maintenance revenue `$1.891B`; Development revenue `$789.1M`; GAAP net income `$56.0M`; adjusted EBITDA `$352.3M`.
- BrightView used approximately `1,500` H-2B seasonal workers in FY2025 versus `2,000` in FY2024, making immigration capacity a direct operating variable.
- At September 30, 2025, total debt was `$790.2M`, cash `$74.5M`, preferred dividends `$35.8M`, finance-lease repayments `$48.4M`, receivables-financing repayments `$29.1M`, and common repurchases/distributions `$24.2M`.
- Maintenance adjusted EBITDA rose to `$245.5M` and margin to `13.0%` while revenue declined `3.7%`; margin improvement must be tested against service capacity and fleet investment.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate maintenance renewal, price, volume, route density, weather, and
   Development project timing and margin.
2. Track H-2B workers, vacancies, wages, overtime, turnover, recruiting,
   productivity, safety, workers' compensation, and pricing recovery.
3. Treat `$254.2M` capex and equipment-sale proceeds as a fleet-renewal test,
   not simply growth or maintenance labels.
4. Normalize receivables, unbilled/deferred revenue, accounts payable,
   receivables financing, seasonal working capital, and billing timing.
5. Charge interest, finance-lease principal, preferred dividends, debt
   repayment, and preferred conversion/dilution before common-owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what maintenance renewal, route
density, labor productivity, H-2B availability, development margin, fleet
replacement, leverage, preferred claims, and cost of capital the equity value
requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether commercial property
budgets, construction, wages, fuel, weather, immigration policy, rates, and
receivables financing can preserve liquidity through a seasonal downturn.

## Promotion boundary

`commercial-landscape-qualified; labor-fleet-and-leverage-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from maintenance contracts
and development projects to renewal, staffing, collection, fleet/equipment
replacement, claims, debt/lease/preferred funding, and diluted common residual.
Revenue, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted FCF, OCF, equipment-sale proceeds, and
buybacks remain diagnostic inputs rather than normalized owner cash.
