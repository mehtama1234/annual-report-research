# Comscore audience measurement valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Comscore from current-period evidence into a company-specific
valuation, recurring-contract, data-asset, product-transition, recapitalization,
and common-owner cash test. It does not pool cross-platform measurement, local-TV
currency, syndicated panels, Proximic, and research services into a generic ad-tech
multiple.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Comscore | Collected measurement and data-licensing cash after panels, platform integration, data rights, privacy/compliance, sales, customer support, product transition, debt, preferred claims, and dilution | Cross-platform measurement, local-TV currency, Proximic, identity/data infrastructure, research, technology, sales, and contract implementation | Legacy product attrition, advertiser data-strategy changes, customer concentration, contract renewal, recapitalization, term-loan cash, preferred claims, or dilution | Cross-platform and local-TV growth continue while recurring collections, legacy runoff, Proximic demand, customer retention, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Full-year 2025 revenue was `$357.5M` versus `$356.0M`; cross-platform solutions grew `24%` and local TV grew double digits.
- Q1 2026 revenue was `$85.3M`, cross-platform solutions grew `30%`, net loss was `$6.2M`, and adjusted EBITDA was `$5.0M`.
- At Q1 2026, cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash totaled `$25.1M`; term-loan principal was `$39.0M`; Comscore made a `$5.0M` voluntary prepayment.
- The 2025 recapitalization removed an `$18M` annual preferred-dividend burden and a special-dividend overhang of at least `$47M`, but the capital structure remains a central valuation object.
- National TV and syndicated digital products were under pressure while cross-platform, local-TV currency, and Proximic represented the transition case.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile contracted measurement revenue to renewal, usage, implementation,
   data rights, customer concentration, collections, and deferred revenue.
2. Separate cross-platform, local TV, Proximic, syndicated audience, national TV,
   digital, and research products by retention, margin, and required data cost.
3. Keep panel/data acquisition, platform integration, privacy compliance, product
   development, sales, customer support, and restructuring in the reinvestment base.
4. Test whether recapitalization changed cash economics or merely moved preferred,
   debt, and dilution claims around the capital structure.
5. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA, restructuring, contract costs, term-loan payments,
   preferred dividends, SBC, receivables, and diluted common shares before calling
   measurement relevance owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what cross-platform renewal, local-TV
currency, Proximic growth, legacy runoff, margin, data cost, reinvestment rate,
leverage, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress
test asks whether advertising budgets, platform power, privacy rules, data strategy,
customer concentration, and refinancing can preserve liquidity through a media cycle.

## Promotion boundary

`comscore-audience-measurement-qualified; product-transition-recap-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from measurement contracts and
data products to renewal, collection, data cost, required product investment,
preferred/debt claims, recapitalization terms, and diluted common residual. Revenue,
cross-platform growth, local-TV growth, adjusted EBITDA, guidance, and buybacks
remain diagnostic inputs.
