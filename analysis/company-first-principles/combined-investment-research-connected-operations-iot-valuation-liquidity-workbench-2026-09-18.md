# Connected-operations IoT valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Samsara into a company-specific connected-operations IoT
and physical-workflow valuation/liquidity object. It keeps sensor, telematics,
fleet, site, and frontline-worker economics separate from enterprise workflow
software, satellite connectivity, industrial uptime, and equipment rental. The
object tests ARR and renewal after device inventory, cellular/cloud cost,
implementation, support, commissions, AI infrastructure, SBC, capex, and
diluted common claims.

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2026 revenue was `$1.619B`, ending ARR `$1.890B`, OCF `$236.2M`, and FCF
  `$207.4M`; property/equipment purchases were `$28.8M`. FCF is not complete
  owner cash until device costs, commissions, SBC, and replacement economics are
  joined.
- Q1 FY2027 revenue was `$478.8M`, ARR `$1.991B`, OCF `$81.4M`, and FCF
  `$73.2M`; net new ARR was `$100.7M`. ARR is forward contracted value, not
  collected cash or proof of renewal.
- FY2026 GAAP gross margin was `77%` and non-GAAP operating margin `17%`;
  FY2026 non-GAAP operating income was `$282.4M` against OCF `$236.2M`. Q4 GAAP
  operating margin was `2%` versus non-GAAP `21%`, showing a material adjustment
  gap.
- The platform funds connected devices, inventory, cellular/cloud costs,
  implementation, support, and commissions before all subscription economics
  are realized. Device activation, returns, replacement, warranty, and supplier
  terms are therefore central owner-cash controls.
- Operational AI and AI Agents require proof of customer ROI and incremental
  contribution after data, compute, support, model, and liability costs.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Bridge ARR and net new ARR to recognized revenue, renewal, expansion,
   collection, gross profit, and cash per diluted share.
2. Reconcile connected-device inventory, supplier commitments, activation,
   returns, replacement, warranty, and hardware cash timing.
3. Test cellular, cloud, data, support, implementation, and AI cost per
   customer or data point rather than treating the model as pure software.
4. Compare deferred commissions, additions, amortization, churn, payback, and
   expansion; include SBC and employer tax in the owner-cash bridge.
5. Require customer ROI, renewal, and expansion evidence before crediting AI
   adoption or data scale as durable value.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what ARR growth, gross retention,
application expansion, device attachment, AI pricing, customer ROI, hardware/
cloud cost, sales efficiency, SBC, and cost of capital the valuation requires.
The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether transportation, construction,
logistics, utilities, manufacturing, labor, safety, insurance, fuel, and
regulatory budgets preserve connected-workflow spending through a downturn.

## Promotion boundary

`connected-operations-iot-qualified; hardware-renewal-and-gaap-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from ARR and customer cohorts
to renewal, expansion, collection, device activation/replacement, inventory,
cellular/cloud/AI cost, implementation, commissions, SBC, diluted shares, and
common-owner residual. ARR, net new ARR, data volume, non-GAAP margin, OCF,
FCF, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
