# Beauty, fragrance, and portfolio-renovation valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Coty from current-period personal-products evidence into a company-specific valuation, brand-renovation, channel, inventory, portfolio, and owner-cash test. It treats Coty as a prestige/fragrance and consumer-beauty portfolio with trend and execution exposure, not as a defensive staple.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coty | Collected beauty cash after prestige/fragrance and consumer-beauty sell-through, licensing, retailer/channel inventory, innovation, marketing, portfolio review, restructuring, debt, FX, and dilution | Fragrance/cosmetics innovation, brand marketing, e-commerce/social commerce, retailer support, inventory, licensing, R&D, restructuring, and international distribution | Fragrance versus mass-beauty mix, trend failure, promotion, retailer inventory, China/FX, geopolitical travel/retail shocks, consumer softness, strategic review, refinancing, and dilution | Prestige/fragrance growth and EBITDA rise while Consumer Beauty declines, sell-through, retailer inventory, licensing economics, brand investment, cash generation, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 official materials reported revenue about `$7.0B`, gross margin about `75%`, operating margin about `20%`, EPS `$5.10`, and free cash flow about `$1.3B`.
- FY2026 Q3 net revenues were `$1,281.6M`, down `1%` reported and `7%` LFL; gross margin was `61.8%`; adjusted EBITDA `$127.0M`; and adjusted loss per share `$0.03`.
- FY2026 Q2 net revenues were `$1,678.6M`, up `1%` reported but down `3%` LFL; Prestige revenue was `$1,133.6M`; Consumer Beauty `$545.0M`; adjusted EBITDA `$330.2M`; and adjusted EPS `$0.14`.
- Management withdrew prior FY2026 EBITDA and free-cash-flow guidance in Q2 and shifted to quarter-specific guidance, while framing the business around `Coty. Curated.`, balance-sheet improvement, and a strategic review of Consumer Beauty.
- FY2026 Q3 showed the split clearly: fragrance and cosmetics remained more resilient, while Consumer Beauty weakness and Middle East conflict weighed on results; no retailer exceeded `10%` of FY2025 global revenue.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile reported sales to full-price sell-through, units, average selling price, retailer inventory, returns, promotions, travel retail, and channel loading; gross margin is not brand quality.
2. Separate Prestige, fragrance, cosmetics, skincare, Consumer Beauty, licensing, and discontinued/reviewed assets; prestige growth cannot automatically support the full portfolio.
3. Test innovation and brand marketing through repeat purchase, launch productivity, social/e-commerce conversion, retailer placement, inventory aging, and contribution after promotion.
4. Keep strategic review, restructuring, leadership transition, licensing, FX, China, geopolitical effects, and portfolio transactions separate from recurring brand economics.
5. Reconcile debt, refinancing, working capital, inventory, retailer exposure, contingent claims, restructuring cash, and free cash flow before promoting adjusted EBITDA or EPS.
6. Treat buybacks, dividends, adjusted EPS, and brand valuation as residual claims requiring ongoing brand investment, debt, tax, and diluted-share validation.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what fragrance/cosmetics sell-through, brand heat, repeat rate, channel mix, licensing margin, innovation return, inventory turns, marketing, restructuring, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether discretionary beauty, travel retail, China, FX, geopolitical, and household-income shocks can weaken demand while innovation, marketing, retailer support, and debt obligations remain.

## Promotion boundary

`coty-beauty-qualified; fragrance-consumer-beauty-sellthrough-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from brand demand to full-price sell-through, collection, retailer inventory, promotion, licensing, innovation, marketing, portfolio/restructuring cash, debt, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, gross margin, Prestige growth, adjusted EBITDA, EPS, guidance, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
