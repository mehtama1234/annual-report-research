# Commerce media and AI shopping-interface valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Criteo from current-period commerce-media evidence into a company-specific valuation, retailer, brand, contribution-margin, concentration, AI-interface, and owner-cash test. It treats Criteo as a transaction-adjacent commerce-demand platform, not as a generic ad network or publisher inventory intermediary.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Criteo | Collected retailer and brand media cash after retailer revenue share, traffic acquisition, measurement, platform delivery, client onboarding, AI decisioning, working capital, debt, and dilution | Commerce-data and AI decisioning, retailer integrations, measurement, sales and implementation, product discovery, OpenAI/agentic-commerce delivery, traffic and media acquisition, and working capital | Retailer concentration and scope changes, brand budgets, retailer bargaining power, privacy and attribution, AI-interface economics, media cost, client churn, debt, and dilution | Revenue, media spend, contribution ex-TAC, retail-media growth, brand count, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, or AI partnerships rise while client scope, retailer settlement, conversion, measurement delivery, collections, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$1.945B`, gross profit `$1.049B`, contribution ex-TAC `$1.175B`, adjusted EBITDA `$407M`, media spend `$4.3B`, and FCF `$211M`.
- Q4 `2025` revenue was `$541M`, contribution ex-TAC `$330M`, adjusted EBITDA `$120M`, media spend `$1.4B`, and net income `$46M`.
- Q1 `2026` revenue was `$425M`, contribution ex-TAC `$250M`, adjusted EBITDA `$65M`, media spend above `$1.0B`, and FCF `$16M`; two client scope changes created an estimated `$27M` headwind.
- Q2 `2026` revenue was `$428M`, contribution ex-TAC `$255M`, adjusted EBITDA `$73M`, media spend `$1.1B`, net income `$12M`, and FCF `$(38M)`.
- Criteo became OpenAI's first advertising-technology partner, with more than `2,000` brands advertising on ChatGPT across seven countries; this is strategic evidence, not yet proof of durable collected common-owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile media spend, retailer and brand billing, retailer share, contribution ex-TAC, conversion, measurement, collection, and refunds; media spend is not Criteo cash.
2. Separate retail media, performance media, retailer, brand, AI-interface, and client cohorts; two large scope changes can materially distort reported trends.
3. Test retailer integrations and AI partnerships through contract scope, implementation, data rights, customer retention, incremental margin, traffic cost, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep contribution ex-TAC, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, media spend, underlying growth adjustments, and partnership announcements separate from recurring collected owner cash.
5. Reconcile retailer revenue share, traffic and cloud cost, sales, measurement, working capital, debt, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what retailer and brand retention, conversion, contribution ex-TAC, AI monetization, media spend, integration, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether retailer bargaining power, privacy and attribution changes, AI disintermediation, advertising cycles, media costs, rates, and customer concentration preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`criteo-commerce-media-qualified; retailer-settlement-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from retailer and brand campaigns to billing, collection, retailer share, conversion, measurement and delivery cost, AI-interface economics, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, media spend, contribution ex-TAC, retailer growth, brand count, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, and AI partnerships remain diagnostic inputs.
