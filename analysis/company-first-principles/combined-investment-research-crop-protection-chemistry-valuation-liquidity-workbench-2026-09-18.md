# Crop-protection chemistry valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves FMC into a company-specific crop-protection chemistry,
registration, patent-transition, and leveraged portfolio-repair object. It
keeps pesticide and active-ingredient economics separate from fertilizer,
agribusiness processing, materials, and industrial chemicals. The object tests
product replacement and realized cash after formulation, registration,
manufacturing reset, working capital, receivable factoring, restructuring,
asset sales, debt, taxes, and diluted common claims.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment/denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FMC | Product-life-cycle cash from mature and new crop-protection chemistry after price/volume, formulation, registration, manufacturing, channel credit, inventory, factoring, restructuring, India/portfolio changes, debt, and diluted common residual | Active ingredients, patents/registrations, launch/R&D, manufacturing footprint, inventory, receivables, non-recourse factoring, Project Foundation, environmental obligations, asset sales, debt, and SBC | Patent erosion, new-product failure, grower/channel credit, inventory discounting, factoring dependence, site-exit cash, India sale/liabilities, refinancing, and regulatory cost | Adjusted EBITDA, new-product sales, asset-sale proceeds, factoring, or reported margin rise while continuing OCF, product replacement, working capital, debt, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$3.467B`; adjusted EBITDA `$843M`; continuing OCF
  approximately negative `$6M`; PP&E spending `$96M`; and the post-PP&E /
  acquisition cash screen negative `$105M`.
- Cash was `$585M`, total debt `$4.075B`, inventory `$1.220B`, and non-recourse
  receivables factoring about `$300M`. Cash availability was concentrated in
  foreign subsidiaries, so parent liquidity and debt service remain separate.
- FY2025 included a `$1.356B` goodwill write-off and approximately `$522M` of
  India-held-for-sale adjustments. These are not all current cash uses, but they
  are evidence that prior acquisition and portfolio assumptions require repair.
- Fluindapyr, Isoflex, and Dodhylex generated about `$200M` of 2025 sales and
  grew `54%`; that proves product replacement activity, not yet replacement of
  legacy margin and cash.
- Rynaxypyr and other legacy products face patent and generic pressure. Product
  price/volume, branded/basic mix, registrations, manufacturing cost, and launch
  cash must be joined before the recovery case can be promoted.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Bridge adjusted EBITDA to continuing OCF through receivables, factoring,
   inventory, rebates, supplier terms, taxes, restructuring, environmental, and
   pension cash.
2. Track product age, patent/registration life, branded/basic formulation,
   price, volume, margin, launch cost, and cash by active ingredient.
3. Separate non-recourse factoring from ordinary customer collection and test
   whether factoring expands as operating cash weakens.
4. Maintain a cash schedule for Project Foundation, site exits, India, severance,
   supply-chain changes, environmental obligations, and realized savings.
5. Keep goodwill impairment, asset-sale proceeds, adjusted EPS, dividends,
   buybacks, debt, and dilution separate from normalized common-owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what product replacement, registration
probability, realized pricing, manufacturing margin, working capital, asset-sale
cash, debt paydown, and cost of capital the equity value requires. The Lyn
Alden-style stress test asks whether food-production demand, grower liquidity,
commodity prices, regulation, patent cycles, foreign cash, rates, and
refinancing preserve liquidity while the portfolio is repaired.

## Promotion boundary

`crop-protection-chemistry-qualified; product-replacement-and-leverage-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from active ingredients and
registrations to price/volume, formulation, collection, inventory, factoring,
manufacturing and restructuring cash, asset-sale proceeds, debt, taxes,
diluted shares, and common-owner residual. Revenue, adjusted EBITDA, new-
product sales, factoring, OCF, FCF, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic
inputs.
