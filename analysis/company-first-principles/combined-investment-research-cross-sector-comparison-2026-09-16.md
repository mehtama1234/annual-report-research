## What the comparison reveals

The three cases are not interchangeable “growth” stories. Retail is a
through-cycle demand-to-margin-to-owner-cash problem; Wheaton is a
contractual-entitlement-to-delivery-and-return problem; Apollo–Athene is a
legal-entity-and-liability-allocation problem. The shared discipline is to
identify the control point, name the burden carrier, choose the denominator,
and demand a falsifiable receipt or cash bridge.

The comparison also explains why a single valuation multiple is inadequate:

- retail requires a normalized recurring cash denominator;
- Wheaton requires delivery timing, threshold mechanics, tax, and financing;
- Apollo requires segment economics plus regulated-capital, liability, and
  parent-attribution constraints.

## Public-evidence decision surface: what we can use now

The missing private or delivery-level records do not make the work unusable;
they define the correct use of each pilot. This is a public-evidence-only
handoff, not a recommendation or a normalized ranking.

| Pilot | Public evidence is strong enough to use for | Public evidence is not strong enough to claim | Next move | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wheaton–Antamina | A named-asset contract, upfront amount-and-date source/use observation, entitlement mechanics, threshold sensitivity, and a focused delivery-return diligence plan | A BHP-only ounce/invoice/receipt ledger, reserve-backed delivery curve, after-tax financed IRR, or investment return | Carry forward as the deepest named-asset case study and test the next company filing for delivery and settlement evidence | Do not convert the $4.300B payment into realized stream return or assume the contract is economically self-funding |
| TJX / Target / Walmart | A comparative affordability/substitution cohort, operating-quality screen, cash-after-property observation, and explicit forensic follow-up flags | A maintenance-capital-adjusted, working-capital-normalized, service-level owner-cash ranking or causal affordability-to-cash conclusion | Carry forward as a cohort screen; promote only when the missing normalization gates are source-backed | Do not rank the three companies on reported cash-after-property or treat the pooled lagged panel as causal evidence |
| Apollo–Athene–ARI | A capital-flow, legal-entity, fee/spread, statutory-asset, and related-party research map with visible attribution questions | Common-owner residual cash, Athene-to-AGM receipt attribution, borrower-level return, or liability-cost-adjusted asset return | Carry forward as a platform-structure map; test entity-level filings and controlled schedules if they become available | Do not treat consolidated cash, segment cash, fees, or statutory assets as common-owner cash |

### Move-on rule

For the next research cycle, use the pilots as differentiated instruments:

1. Use Wheaton to develop the named-asset contract-and-delivery method.
2. Use retail to develop the through-cycle operating-quality and earnings-to-
   cash screen.
3. Use Apollo to develop the legal-entity, liability, and parent-attribution
   method.

The unresolved gates remain live thesis breakers, but they no longer block
broader candidate selection or theme expansion. Any later candidate should
enter through the same controls: control point, payer, burden carrier,
owner-cash denominator, valuation object, quality-of-earnings flags, and
falsifier.
