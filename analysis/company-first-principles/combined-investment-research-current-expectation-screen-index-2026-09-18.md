# Combined investment research current expectation-screen index

Observation date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This index consolidates the dated market-expectation screens added to the
company workbenches. It is a navigation and burden-control surface, not a
ranking, target-price table, or normalized owner-cash portfolio.

The screens answer a narrow question:

> What reported or mechanical cash surface would the current equity value
> require if that surface were sustained?

They do not answer whether the cash is durable, distributable, asset-level,
after-tax, financed, or attributable to common owners.

## Comparability rules

These rows must not be sorted into a single “cheapest” list. The denominators
mix EV with equity value, TTM with H1 and nine-month annualizations, reported
cash with management-defined FCF, and USD with CAD. The physical and legal
objects also differ: a fleet, a data-center project, a semiconductor tool, a
licensed product, a distributor, and a steel mill do not share one maintenance
definition.

The index is useful for expectation burden only. A screen can become a
promotion candidate only after the workbench joins:

```text
reported activity
  -> collection / acceptance / settlement
  -> working capital and required replacement or growth capital
  -> acquisition, debt, tax, lease, JV, preferred, SBC, and dilution claims
  -> legal-entity common-owner residual
```

## Current interpretation

The screen surface shows where the project has enough market-implied burden to
justify deeper source acquisition. It does not establish an investment rank.
The recurring open pattern is not “find a lower multiple”; it is prove whether
the denominator survives the physical, financial, and legal-entity bridge.
