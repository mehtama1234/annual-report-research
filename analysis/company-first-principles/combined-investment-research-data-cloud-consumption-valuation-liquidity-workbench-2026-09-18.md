# Data-cloud consumption valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Snowflake from data-platform and AI-demand evidence into a
company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps consumption,
customer expansion, RPO, prepaid capacity, third-party cloud and GPU cost,
capitalized software, deferred commissions, SBC, strategic investments, and
diluted common-owner cash separate from Oracle's owned-capacity cloud build and
from broader advertising or security platforms.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Snowflake data cloud | Durable product-consumption cash after cloud, GPU, inference, support, security, capitalized-software, deferred-commission, SBC, and diluted-share burdens | Public-cloud capacity, GPU/inference, capitalized internal-use software, engineering, sales commissions, security/availability, working capital, and equity-compensation replacement | Consumption optimization, cloud-provider pricing, AI gross-margin pressure, prepaid-capacity delivery, enterprise budget delay, customer concentration, or dilution | Product revenue, NRR, RPO, and adjusted FCF rise while usage durability, product margin, prepaid-capacity conversion, cash after SBC, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2026 revenue was `$4.684B`, product revenue `$4.472B`, operating cash flow
  `$1.222B`, and reported free cash flow `$1.120B`.
- FY2026 net revenue retention was `125%`, with `733` customers above `$1M` of
  trailing twelve-month product revenue. These are expansion indicators, not
  proof that future consumption will remain durable.
- FY2026 RPO was `$9.772B`; deferred revenue increased `$755M` from prepaid
  capacity agreements. RPO and prepayment create visibility and liquidity but
  also future delivery and margin obligations.
- FY2026 stock-based compensation was `$1.600B`, approximately `34%` of
  revenue. Cash after dilution therefore remains materially different from
  reported OCF or company-defined FCF.
- Product cost includes third-party cloud infrastructure, GPUs, AI inference,
  support, availability, and security. Capitalized internal-use software and
  deferred commissions are additional reinvestment objects.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile consumption cohorts, NRR, large-customer counts, RPO conversion,
   and product revenue. Customers can expand while optimizing workloads or
   negotiating price.
2. Treat prepaid capacity as customer-funded liquidity with a future service
   obligation. Compare cash collection with delivery cost, gross margin, and
   usage realization.
3. Track third-party cloud, GPU, inference, support, and security cost per
   workload. Asset-light infrastructure still carries a variable replacement
   and availability burden.
4. Include capitalized software, deferred commissions, engineering, and sales
   efficiency in the reinvestment denominator; do not equate physical capex
   with total platform investment.
5. Show GAAP loss, SBC, diluted shares, repurchases, strategic investment
   gains, and cash together. Adjusted FCF is a diagnostic input until common
   residual after recurring equity compensation is evidenced.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what product growth, consumption
expansion, retention, RPO conversion, cloud-cost efficiency, AI margin,
reinvestment rate, terminal growth, and cost of capital the valuation requires.
The narrative must be translated into cash per diluted share, not only revenue
or NRR.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether customers keep funding workloads
through enterprise-budget pressure, optimization, cloud-provider bargaining,
AI inference cost, higher rates, and a shift toward open formats or competing
data layers. Liquidity passes only when prepaid capacity, cloud obligations,
capitalized software, deferred commissions, SBC, and common residual remain
visible.

## Promotion boundary

`data-cloud-qualified; consumption-margin-and-dilution-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from consumption cohorts,
NRR, RPO, and prepaid capacity to recognized product revenue, cloud/GPU cost,
capitalized software, deferred commissions, cash collection, SBC replacement,
and diluted common residual. Product revenue, RPO, OCF, company-defined FCF,
adjusted metrics, and repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
