# Digital advertising and measurement valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves The Trade Desk and DoubleVerify from advertising-platform
evidence into separate valuation objects. It keeps open-internet budget
decisioning distinct from independent media verification and from content,
commerce, and participation platforms.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment/denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Trade Desk | Open-internet advertising decisioning cash after advertiser/agency collection, publisher/supply settlement, identity/data, hosting, R&D, SBC, and dilution | Data/identity, bidding infrastructure, software, sales, customer support, privacy, working capital, SBC, and common capital | Advertiser budget slowdown, closed-platform competition, identity/privacy change, agency concentration, supply cost, receivable timing, or growth deceleration | Gross spend and revenue rise while take rate, customer retention, contract/receivable quality, platform differentiation, SBC, or diluted cash weaken |
| DoubleVerify | Independent measurement/verification cash after transaction proof, data/cloud/product cost, platform access, customer collection, merger terms, SBC, and dilution | Measurement data, classification/fraud models, activation/supply integrations, privacy, product development, customer support, and deal costs | Advertising slowdown, platform access restriction, fee-per-transaction decline, customer concentration, merger failure or loss of independence | Measured transactions and adjusted EBITDA rise while fee rate, retention, activation, data cost, platform dependence, or standalone common value deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- The Trade Desk FY2025 revenue was `$2.896B`, operating cash flow `$992.7M`,
  PP&E and capitalized-software spending about `$209.8M`, SBC `$490.6M`, and
  repurchases `$1.380B`; gross platform spend was `$13.4B`, not company revenue.
- Q2 2026 Trade Desk revenue was `$715.1M`, up `3%`, after Q1 growth of `12%`.
- DoubleVerify FY2025 revenue was approximately `$748.3M`, operating cash flow
  about `$211M`, adjusted EBITDA about `$245.6M`, cash about `$260M`, and no debt.
  Q2 2026 revenue was approximately `$193.8M`, up `3%`; activation declined `1%`
  while measurement rose `6%` and supply-side rose `13%`.
- DoubleVerify entered a Nielsen-parent merger agreement in August 2026; the
  standalone business and transaction process must remain separate until close,
  consideration, financing, and independence effects are evidenced.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Keep advertiser gross spend, measured transactions, billings, revenue, take
   rate/fee per transaction, supply cost, and collected cash separate.
2. Reconcile receivable days, agency/customer concentration, publisher or
   partner settlement, data/cloud cost, privacy, product investment, and SBC.
3. For DoubleVerify, track measurement, activation, and supply-side economics
   separately and preserve the pre-merger standalone perimeter.
4. Test whether identity, independent verification, platform access, and
   customer retention survive closed-platform competition and privacy changes.
5. Treat adjusted EBITDA, reported OCF, measured volume, gross spend, buybacks,
   and merger consideration as diagnostic or residual claims, not normalized
   owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what advertiser budget share, take
rate, fee per transaction, retention, data cost, growth, SBC, and cost of
capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether
ad budgets, identity rules, platform access, privacy regulation, agency power,
and data/cloud costs can interrupt cash before measurement or decisioning
workflow proves durable.

## Promotion boundary

`digital-advertising-qualified; take-rate-platform-and-transaction-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from advertiser spend or media
transaction through decisioning/verification, customer and partner settlement,
data/platform costs, privacy/legal claims, SBC, debt, merger terms where
relevant, and diluted common residual. Gross spend, measured transactions,
revenue, adjusted EBITDA, reported OCF, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
