# Digital real-estate Q2 2026 cash-quality refresh

Research date: `2026-09-17`

## First-principles question

Are powered sites, interconnection density, and data-center leases producing
common-owner cash after development capital, acquisitions, power, debt,
partners, and dilution—or only visibility metrics and REIT-defined cash?

## Current-period comparison

| Company | H1 2026 operating/cash observation | Growth-capital and claim surface | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equinix | OCF `$1.784B`; PP&E capex `$2.834B`; real-estate acquisitions `$224M`; dividends `$1.029B`; AFFO attributable to common stockholders `$2.233B` | 2026 total-capex guide `$5.0–6.0B`; unaccrued capex commitments about `$6.1B`; xScale equity-method investments `$735M` | AFFO and bookings do not prove project return or common-owner cash |
| Digital Realty | OCF `$1.595B`; cash-basis capex `$1.627B`, including `$1.477B` development and `$136M` recurring; acquisitions/asset acquisitions about `$2.170B` | `$2.5B` net ATM equity proceeds year-to-date; `$18.768B` consolidated debt; 4.7x net debt/adjusted EBITDA; backlog about `$1.4B` | FFO/AFFO, backlog, equity proceeds, and partner capital do not prove per-share value creation |

## Interpretation

Both companies occupy a physical control point: powered, connected space that
customers cannot create instantly. But the current-period cash surface is
capital intensive. Equinix's OCF was below PP&E capex before acquisitions and
dividends. Digital Realty's OCF was approximately `$32M` below cash-basis capex
before acquisitions, unconsolidated investments, financing, and distributions.

This does not imply that growth destroys value. It means the valuation must
join project commencement, power availability, customer acceptance, lease-up,
stabilized NOI, partner funding, debt, and dilution to the capital spent.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

- Reconcile AFFO/FFO add-backs, recurring capex, straight-line rent, leasing
  costs, SBC, capitalized interest, and transaction costs.
- Separate development, recurring, acquisition, JV/fund, and power commitments.
- Track signed rent and bookings through commencement, incentives, collection,
  operating cost, and stabilized yield.
- Reconcile ATM equity, operating-partnership units, preferred claims, and SBC
  against diluted per-share cash.
- Do not treat backlog, annualized signed rent, AFFO, FFO, or partner proceeds
  as current owner cash.

These are forensic controls, not allegations of misconduct.

## Decision

`digital-real-estate-q2-capital-burden-visible; project-return-and-common-owner-cash-open`

The lane supports a real scarcity/control-point thesis, but Equinix and Digital
Realty remain incomparable for ranking until project-level return, financing,
partner, and per-share cash joins are complete. No owner-cash promotion is made.
