# Diversified clinical-products valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Abbott Laboratories from diversified healthcare evidence
into a company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps Medical
Devices, Diagnostics, Nutrition, and Established Pharmaceuticals on separate
reinvestment and reimbursement denominators, while testing Exact Sciences
acquisition return, instrument placement, quality, working capital, debt, and
diluted common-owner cash.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abbott diversified clinical products | Portfolio cash after device development, diagnostic instruments and tests, nutrition manufacturing, pharmaceutical access, reimbursement, quality, acquisition integration, debt, and dilution | R&D, clinical evidence, manufacturing, lab instruments, consumables, nutrition plants, working capital, Exact Sciences integration, amortization, debt, SBC, and capital returns | Hospital/payer pressure, diagnostic reimbursement, test utilization, product recall, nutrition input/safety shock, generic competition, acquisition underperformance, rates, or debt burden | Sales and adjusted EPS grow while acquired-cohort return, instrument utilization, reimbursement, quality cash, debt, or diluted per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Q2 2026 sales were `$12.593B`; Medical Devices sales `$5.853B` and
  Diagnostics `$3.092B`. GAAP diluted EPS was `$0.53` versus adjusted EPS
  `$1.31`, making the acquisition and special-item bridge central.
- First-half OCF was `$3.803B`, PP&E purchases `$896M`, dividends `$2.200B`,
  and repurchases `$1.225B`; Abbott spent `$19.962B` on acquisitions and
  technologies and issued `$19.827B` of long-term debt.
- Exact Sciences contributed `$1.0B` of first-half sales, while its earnings
  contribution was not material to consolidated net earnings. Goodwill rose
  `$11.4B` and gross amortizable intangible assets `$12.3B`; estimated 2026
  amortization was `$2.6B`.
- FY2025 segment sales were Medical Devices `$19.290B`, Diagnostics `$9.888B`,
  Nutrition `$8.820B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals `$6.330B`; these are
  different lifecycles and should not be valued on one adjusted-EPS multiple.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile GAAP EPS and adjusted EPS to acquisition amortization,
   restructuring, litigation, taxes, integration, SBC, diluted shares, and
   cash after acquisitions. Recurring portfolio costs remain economic costs.
2. Separate legacy Diagnostics from Exact Sciences revenue, test volume,
   reimbursement, operating income, integration cash, and return on purchase
   price.
3. Track laboratory instrument placement, utilization, consumable pull-through,
   service, replacement, customer concentration, and cash return; installed
   equipment is not automatically a high-return asset.
4. Keep Medical Devices, Nutrition, and Established Pharmaceuticals separate by
   clinical evidence, manufacturing, input, reimbursement, and competition.
5. Test debt issuance, amortization, acquisition funding, dividends, buybacks,
   SBC, and diluted shares against post-acquisition common cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what procedure/device growth,
diagnostic utilization, reimbursement, nutrition margin, pharmaceutical
durability, acquisition return, R&D rate, and cost of capital the valuation
requires. Portfolio breadth is valuable only when each segment earns its capital
and acquisition burden.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether hospitals, laboratories, payers,
patients, and institutions can fund products through reimbursement pressure,
rates, quality events, input inflation, generic competition, and weaker health
utilization. Liquidity passes only when debt, claims, integration, and product
quality obligations remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`diversified-clinical-products-qualified; acquired-cohort-and-reimbursement-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from segment sales and
utilization to reimbursement, instrument/test collection, manufacturing cost,
acquired-cohort return, integration cash, debt, SBC, and diluted common
residual. Sales, adjusted EPS, OCF, reported FCF, dividends, and repurchases
remain diagnostic inputs.
