# Diversified gold producer and portfolio-renewal valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Newmont from gold-price and portfolio-scale evidence into
a mine-by-mine, project, divestiture, closure, and common-cash test. It keeps
Newmont distinct from Lundin Gold's single-asset operation, Freeport's focused
copper/smelter model, BHP's broad commodity portfolio, and royalty companies.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Newmont | Producing gold portfolio plus silver/copper byproducts, projects, and operating rights after realized prices, grades, recovery, sustaining capital, closure, divestitures, debt, and dilution | Mine replacement, sustaining and growth capital, Ahafo North, Lihir, Cadia, Tanami Expansion 2, Red Chris, reclamation, and environmental work | Gold-price normalization, mine/project underperformance, host-country restrictions, closure burden, acquisition/divestiture error, or payout pressure | Ounces and cash rise while sustaining capital, reserve replacement, closure, project returns, asset quality, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$22.669B`, operating cash flow `$10.334B`, productive-
  asset spending `$3.035B`, and post-spending cash screen `$7.299B`.
- Shareholder returns were about `$3.400B`; cash was `$7.647B`, long-term debt
  `$5.115B`, attributable gold production `5.889M oz`, and asset-retirement
  obligations `$6.8B`.
- Reported reserves were `118.2M oz`; reserves are future production
  assumptions, not current cash. Silver and copper byproducts add revenue and
  cost credits but introduce additional commodity-price exposure.
- Ahafo North, Lihir, Cadia, Tanami Expansion 2, and Red Chris require permits,
  construction, capital, ramp-up, and operating execution. Divestitures have
  generated about `$4.5B` of after-tax proceeds, which are not recurring cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Recalculate cash at mid-cycle gold, silver, and copper prices, separating
   volume, grade, recovery, unit cost, byproduct credit, and realized price.
2. Reconcile sustaining capital to mine renewal, deferred stripping,
   exploration, tailings, closure, and environmental spending.
3. Track each mine's production, grade, recovery, availability, reserves,
   project budget, schedule, ramp-up, and cash return.
4. Test acquisitions and divestitures by cash paid/received, lost or added
   production, reserves, debt, reclamation, and future capital.
5. Include the `$6.8B` asset-retirement obligation, host-country terms,
   indigenous/community obligations, and debt before payout analysis.
6. Treat ounces, reserves, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, divestiture proceeds,
   dividends, and repurchases as diagnostic until full-cycle cash is joined.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test values producing mines, byproducts,
reserves, development projects, and portfolio options separately using
mid-cycle prices, sustaining capital, mine life, closure, taxes, and
probability-weighted completion. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether
gold's monetary and fiscal-uncertainty demand offsets energy, labor, equipment,
political, environmental, and financing inflation. Scale diversifies mine risk
but expands the closure and project-renewal perimeter.

## Promotion boundary

`diversified-gold-qualified; mine-portfolio-renewal-and-closure-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from mine production,
grade/recovery, realized metals, sustaining and growth capital, reserves,
project return, closure, host-country obligations, divestitures, debt,
dividends, and diluted common residual. Ounces, reserves, FCF, adjusted EBITDA,
divestiture proceeds, and repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
