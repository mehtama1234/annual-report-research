# Diversified mining and metals valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Rio Tinto from commodity and critical-minerals evidence
into a company-specific valuation and liquidity object. It separates Pilbara
iron ore, aluminium, copper, lithium, logistics, project development, partner
funding, rehabilitation, and dividends from uranium mining, precious-metals
streaming, chemicals, and equipment suppliers.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rio Tinto | Through-cycle mine-and-logistics cash after realized price, grade/recovery, sustaining and replacement capital, project spend, partner claims, rehabilitation, debt, taxes, dividends, and dilution | Pilbara mines/rail/ports, aluminium, copper, lithium, Oyu Tolgoi, Simandou, exploration, project ramp, closure, water/tailings, partner funding, debt, and common capital | Iron-ore/metal-price fall, grade decline, weather/logistics, project overrun, permitting/host-country risk, rehabilitation, partner delay, debt, or dividend funding | Production, EBITDA, FCF, first ore, critical-minerals growth, or dividends rise while mid-cycle return, reserve replacement, project cash, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 sales were `$57.638B`, attributable profit `$9.966B`, OCF `$16.800B`, free cash flow `$4.025B`, and capital expenditure before partner funding `$12.285B`.
- Net debt was about `$14.4B` after the `$7.6B` Arcadium acquisition and `$6.1B` of dividends; the ordinary dividend was about `$6.5B`, above FY2025 FCF.
- Pilbara iron ore remained the cash anchor while copper, aluminium, and lithium growth projects increased capital and execution burden.
- Oyu Tolgoi, Simandou, Western Range, and lithium projects require ramp, cost, partner, schedule, and final-capital evidence; first production is not a completed return.
- Mine cash must include reserve depletion, grade/recovery, rail/port, rehabilitation, water/tailings, closure, host-government, and partner claims.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Rebuild cash by commodity through price, volume, grade, recovery, freight, currency, royalties, taxes, and logistics.
2. Separate sustaining/replacement capital from growth and project capital; include exploration, closure, rehabilitation, water, and tailings obligations.
3. Test Arcadium and each copper/lithium/aluminium project by approved budget, partner share, ramp, unit cost, realized price, cash return, and revised capital.
4. Distinguish consolidated earnings, equity-accounted dividends, partner funding, non-controlling interests, and cash legally available to Rio owners.
5. Test reserves and mine life against depletion, grade decline, permitting, community obligations, and replacement investment.
6. Compare dividends and buybacks with mid-cycle FCF after capital, debt, taxes, environmental claims, and project funding; payout policy is not coverage.
7. Treat production, underlying EBITDA, FCF, first ore, and critical-minerals narrative as diagnostic inputs.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what mid-cycle iron ore, aluminium,
copper, and lithium prices, production decline, reserve replacement, sustaining
capital, project probabilities, partner ownership, taxes, debt, and cost of
capital the equity value requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether
industrial demand, energy-transition investment, trade, geopolitics, weather,
ports, rates, and host-country policy can support large projects through a
commodity downturn.

## Promotion boundary

`diversified-mining-metals-qualified; replacement-capital-and-common-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from commodity production and
realized price to grade/recovery, logistics, sustaining capital, project ramp,
partner distributions/funding, reserves, rehabilitation, debt, dividends, and
diluted common residual. Production, EBITDA, FCF, project milestones, and
dividends remain diagnostic inputs.
