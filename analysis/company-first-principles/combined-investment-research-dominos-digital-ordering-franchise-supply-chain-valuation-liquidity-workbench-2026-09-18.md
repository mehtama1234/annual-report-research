# Digital ordering, franchise, and supply-chain valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Domino's from current-period restaurant evidence into a company-specific valuation, digital-ordering, franchise, royalty, supply-chain, store-growth, and owner-cash test. It treats Domino's as an asset-light delivery-and-carryout network with a technology-enabled ordering flow, not as a simple company-operated restaurant chain.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domino's | Collected royalty, supply-chain, technology-fee, and company-store cash after franchisee health, food and distribution costs, digital delivery, store support, taxes, debt, and dilution | Franchise support and field operations, supply-chain centers and logistics, digital ordering and technology, store growth, advertising, food inventory, acquisitions, and working capital | Same-store sales, value and affordability, franchisee economics, food and labor cost, delivery and aggregator competition, store openings, DPC Dash remeasurement, debt, and dilution | Retail sales, digital penetration, store growth, royalties, adjusted EPS, or buybacks rise while franchisee health, order frequency, supply-chain margin, store economics, technology cost, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenues were `$4.94B`, income from operations `$954.0M`, net income `$601.7M`, diluted EPS `$17.57`, and global net store growth `776`, ending with `22,142` stores.
- More than `85%` of U.S. retail sales in 2025 came from digital channels; the company disclosed a `$0.375` per-digital-transaction technology fee.
- Q2 `2026` global retail sales growth excluding FX was `3.0%`, U.S. same-store sales growth `0.1%`, international same-store sales `-0.1%`, and global net store growth `209`; diluted EPS rose `6.8%` to `$4.07`.
- Q1 `2026` global retail sales growth excluding FX was `3.4%`, U.S. same-store sales growth `0.9%`, international same-store sales `-0.4%`, and global net store growth `180`; EPS was affected by DPC Dash remeasurement.
- Q4 `2025` global retail sales growth excluding FX was `4.9%`, U.S. same-store sales growth `3.7%`, international same-store sales `0.7%`, and global net store growth `392`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile orders, same-store sales, digital transactions, royalty billings, franchisee sales, supply-chain shipments, food cost, inventory, and collection; retail sales are not Domino's cash.
2. Separate U.S., international, franchise, company stores, digital, aggregator, supply-chain, and DPC Dash cohorts; economics and accounting differ.
3. Test store growth and franchisee expansion through franchisee returns, closures, labor, food, occupancy, supply-chain service, market cannibalization, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep technology fees, adjusted EPS, royalties, supply-chain revenue, buybacks, and DPC Dash remeasurement separate from recurring collected owner cash.
5. Reconcile advertising, delivery and technology cost, food and distribution, debt, interest, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what order frequency, same-store sales, digital conversion, royalty rate, supply-chain margin, store growth, franchisee return, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether affordability, wage and food inflation, aggregator dependence, consumer trade-down, franchisee leverage, rates, and debt preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`dominos-digital-franchise-qualified; order-flow-franchisee-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from order and franchise cohorts to royalty collection, franchisee health, supply-chain shipment and margin, digital delivery cost, store capex, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Retail sales, digital penetration, store count, royalties, supply-chain revenue, EPS, DPC Dash remeasurement, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
