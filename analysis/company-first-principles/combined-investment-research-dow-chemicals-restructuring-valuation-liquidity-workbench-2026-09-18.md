# Dow chemicals restructuring valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench tests Dow as a diversified chemicals and materials-science platform in a cyclical reset, not as a generic basic-materials producer. It separates polyethylene and feedstock exposure, downstream applications, plant closures, cost programs, working capital, dividends, litigation, debt, and diluted common residual.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dow | Through-cycle chemicals cash after feedstock, plant, environmental, restructuring, working-capital, dividend, debt, and diluted common burdens | Maintenance and turnaround capex, plant closures/restarts, environmental remediation, cost transformation, R&D, inventory, receivables, feedstock commitments, debt, and claims | Polyethylene and industrial-margin downturn, feedstock disruption, tariffs, plant outage, litigation, dividend pressure, restructuring execution, or refinancing | Self-help EBITDA rises while volumes, spreads, cash conversion, asset utilization, dividend coverage, or diluted per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 sales were `$40.0B`, GAAP net loss was about `$2.4B`, operating EBIT about `$0.4B`, and operating cash flow from continuing operations about `$1.1B`.
- Q2 2026 sales were `$12.1B`, GAAP net income `$802M`, operating EBIT `$1.648B`, operating EBITDA `$2.312B`, and operating cash flow `$1.324B`.
- Packaging & Specialty Plastics generated Q2 operating EBIT of `$1.278B`; Industrial Intermediates & Infrastructure `$246M`; Performance Materials & Coatings `$133M`.
- Transform to Outperform targets at least `$2B` of near-term operating EBITDA improvement on top of a previously announced `$1B` structural cost program, but the cash timing and persistence remain open.
- Dow’s global footprint spans `91` manufacturing sites in `29` countries, making feedstock, energy, plant, environmental, trade, and geopolitical exposure central to valuation.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile operating EBITDA and self-help savings to cash after working capital, turnaround spending, closures, environmental obligations, and restructuring payments.
2. Separate cyclical price/spread recovery from durable cost reduction and downstream mix improvement.
3. Test dividend coverage against through-cycle cash, debt maturities, pension/litigation claims, and required maintenance capital.
4. Keep plant utilization, feedstock advantage, inventory, receivables, tariffs, and segment transfers in the cash-quality bridge.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what through-cycle spreads, volumes, utilization, cost savings, reinvestment rate, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether energy, feedstock, trade, environmental, geopolitical, and refinancing shocks can absorb the apparent recovery before it reaches common owners.

## Promotion boundary

`dow-qualified; chemicals-cycle-and-restructuring-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from segment spreads and self-help actions to collected cash, maintenance and transformation capital, environmental and litigation claims, debt service, dividends, and diluted common residual. Revenue, operating EBITDA, guidance, cost savings, and dividends remain diagnostic inputs.
