# DraftKings real-money participation valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves DraftKings from current-period evidence into a company-specific valuation object. It tests sportsbook handle, hold, promotions, iGaming, fantasy, lottery, prediction markets, user retention, state launches, regulatory permissions, marketing, payment settlement, cash, debt, and diluted common residual. It does not pool DraftKings with casinos, media companies, or generic consumer-internet platforms.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DraftKings | Regulated real-money participation cash after handle, hold, promotions, state taxes, payment settlement, product development, marketing, licensing, debt, and dilution | Customer acquisition, promotions, platform and compliance technology, state launches, payment and fraud systems, content, debt, and shares | Hold volatility, promotional intensity, regulatory or tax changes, state licensing, payment/fraud losses, prediction-market uncertainty, or leverage | Users and handle rise while hold, ARPMUP, promotional efficiency, cash collection, regulatory economics, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was about `$6.055B`, adjusted EBITDA `$620M`, and GAAP net income attributable to common stockholders was about `$3.7M` versus a large prior-year loss.
- Q1 2026 revenue was about `$1.646B`; Q2 2026 revenue was about `$1.443B`, illustrating hold and promotional volatility despite continued engagement.
- FY2025 average MUPs were about `4.0M`, ARPMUP `$125`, Sportsbook handle `$53.6B`, and Sportsbook net-revenue margin `7.1%`.
- DraftKings Predictions launched on `2025-12-19` under CFTC oversight, adding a separate regulated participation and legal-entity perimeter.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile handle, gross gaming revenue, hold, promotions, state taxes, payments, chargebacks, and customer acquisition cost.
2. Separate sportsbook, iGaming, fantasy, lottery, media, and prediction-market economics by jurisdiction and product.
3. Test whether user growth and ARPMUP reflect durable retention or expensive promotional reinvestment and favorable short-term outcomes.
4. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA and cash to licensing, compliance, fraud, payment, marketing, product, debt, and diluted-share requirements.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The expectation test asks what MUPs, ARPMUP, handle, hold, promotional efficiency, state expansion, prediction-market adoption, margin, leverage, and cost of capital are embedded in the valuation. The stress test asks whether regulation, tax, responsible-gaming limits, hold volatility, competition, rates, payment losses, and household discretionary pressure leave durable collected cash.

## Promotion boundary

`draftkings-qualified; hold-promotion-regulatory-and-collection-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from users and handle to collection, hold, promotion, state tax, payment settlement, compliance, product return, debt, claims, funding, and diluted common residual. Revenue, MUPs, ARPMUP, handle, adjusted EBITDA, guidance, and capital returns remain diagnostic inputs.
