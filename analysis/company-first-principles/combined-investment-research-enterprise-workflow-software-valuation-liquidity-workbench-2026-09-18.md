# Enterprise workflow software valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Adobe and ServiceNow from recurring-software evidence into
separate embedded-workflow valuation objects. It tests renewal and expansion,
AI monetization, deferred revenue and commissions, cloud delivery, R&D, SBC,
acquisitions, debt, and diluted common cash.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment/denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adobe | Creative/document/experience workflow cash after renewal, seat/usage pricing, AI compute and support, R&D, customer incentives, SBC, buybacks, acquisitions, and dilution | Product/R&D, cloud and storage, sales/marketing, customer support, AI infrastructure, acquisitions, SBC, and common capital | Enterprise/creative budget pressure, seat reduction, AI substitution, compute cost, privacy/security, pricing, or buyback funding | ARR and AI-first ARR rise while renewal, price realization, AI contribution margin, SBC/dilution, R&D, or per-share cash weaken |
| ServiceNow | Enterprise workflow-control cash after subscription renewal/expansion, cloud delivery, deferred commissions, AI contract value, R&D, acquisitions, debt, SBC, and dilution | Workflow platform, cloud/data-center capacity, security, sales/implementation, deferred commissions, acquisitions, R&D, SBC, and common capital | Enterprise budget pause, renewal/module rationalization, AI cannibalization, cloud cost, implementation failure, acquisition integration, or debt | Subscription/RPO/AI ACV rise while renewal, deferred commissions, cloud/support cost, SBC, acquired return, debt, or diluted residual weaken |

## Current evidence anchors

- Adobe FY2025 revenue was `$23.77B`, operating cash flow `$10.03B`, capex
  approximately `$179M`, ARR `$25.20B`, SBC approximately `$1.94B`, and
  buybacks roughly `$11.76B`.
- Adobe Q2 FY2026 ARR was `$27.10B`, AI-first ARR exceeded `$500M`, and RPO
  remained around `$22B`; usage, compute, support, and renewal economics remain
  open.
- ServiceNow FY2025 revenue was `$13.278B`, operating cash flow `$5.444B`,
  property purchases `$868M`, SBC `$1.955B`, and deferred commissions used
  `$758M` of cash. It spent `$1.084B` on business combinations and `$1.056B` on
  strategic investments.
- ServiceNow Q2 FY2026 subscription revenue was `$3.877B`, current RPO `$13.20B`,
  total RPO `$29.0B`, and AI ACV exceeded `$1B`; it raised `$4B` of debt in May
  2026.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile ARR, RPO, ACV, billings, deferred revenue, renewal, seats/modules,
   usage, price realization, and recognized revenue.
2. Charge AI inference, storage, cloud, support, security, and customer-success
   cost before accepting AI ARR or ACV as incremental owner cash.
3. Keep SBC, repurchases, deferred commissions, acquisitions, strategic
   investments, and debt visible when measuring cash per diluted share.
4. Separate Adobe Creative, Document, and Experience economics; separate
   ServiceNow subscription, professional services, and acquired workflows.
5. Treat ARR, RPO, ACV, adjusted EPS, reported OCF, FCF, dividends, and buybacks
   as diagnostic or residual measures until renewal and delivery-cost joins are
   evidenced.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what renewal, expansion, AI price and
usage, gross margin after compute, R&D, acquisition return, SBC, debt, and cost
of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether
enterprise budgets, cloud costs, privacy/security, customer adoption, and AI
substitution can interrupt cash before embedded workflow proves durable.

## Promotion boundary

`enterprise-workflow-qualified; renewal-ai-cost-and-dilution-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from subscription/workflow
usage through renewal, collection, deferred commissions, cloud/support cost,
AI delivery, required R&D, acquisitions, debt, and diluted common residual. ARR,
RPO, ACV, reported OCF, FCF, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
