# Etsy two-sided marketplace and trust infrastructure valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Etsy from a retail-catalog packet into a company-specific valuation object. It separates buyer and seller liquidity, GMS, take rate, payments, advertising, seller services, discovery, trust and safety, cross-border shipping, tariffs, AI-mediated commerce, working capital, debt, and diluted common residual. It does not treat GMS, active buyers, active sellers, take rate, revenue, adjusted EBITDA, or buybacks as normalized owner cash.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Etsy | Two-sided marketplace cash after buyer and seller matching, transaction take rate, payments, advertising, seller services, trust/safety, fraud, discovery, cross-border logistics, tariffs, platform investment, debt, and dilution | Search and recommendation, AI and agentic commerce, payments, trust and safety, customer acquisition, seller onboarding, moderation, data, working capital, and repurchases | Buyer demand or frequency decline, seller churn, fraud, trust loss, tariff/de minimis change, cross-border shipping failure, AI disintermediation, take-rate pressure, payment/regulatory issue, debt, or dilution | GMS and active users rise while buyer frequency, seller retention, take rate, fraud loss, payment collections, platform cost, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 year-end Etsy marketplace active buyers were about `86.5M` and active sellers about `5.6M`; approximately `74%` of GMS came from U.S. buyers and `26%` internationally.
- Q4 2025 consolidated GMS was about `$3.59B`, consolidated revenue `$881.6M`, take rate `24.5%`, adjusted EBITDA `$222.5M`, and diluted EPS `$0.92`.
- Q4 Etsy marketplace GMS was about `$3.29B`; app GMS grew `6.6%`; habitual buyers were about `5.9M`; and Home and Living grew year over year.
- Q3 2025 consolidated GMS was about `$2.72B`, revenue `$678.0M`, take rate `24.9%`, net income `$75.5M`, and adjusted EBITDA `$171.9M`.
- The 2026 shareholder-letter chain emphasizes loyalty, AI-powered discovery, agentic commerce, trust, and strategic investment; the current evidence is therefore partly forward-looking and should not be promoted beyond the directly quantified Q4/Q3 baseline.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile GMS to buyer payment, seller settlement, refunds, chargebacks, payments revenue, take rate, and cash collection; marketplace volume is not platform cash.
2. Separate Etsy marketplace from other brands and services, then test buyer frequency, habitual buyers, seller retention, category mix, advertising, and subscription/service attachment.
3. Keep trust and safety, fraud, counterfeit, moderation, customer support, shipping, tariffs, de minimis, and cross-border costs visible as required marketplace investment.
4. Test AI discovery and agentic checkout claims against adoption, conversion, incremental cost, control of the customer relationship, and platform disintermediation risk.
5. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA, operating cash flow, working capital, payment obligations, debt, SBC, repurchases, and diluted shares before accepting GMS growth or buybacks as owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what buyer frequency, seller retention, GMS growth, take rate, payments and advertising attachment, trust cost, reinvestment rate, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether meaning-rich discretionary purchases survive household pressure, whether cross-border rules and shipping consume the marketplace spread, and whether AI interfaces strengthen Etsy’s network or disintermediate it.

## Promotion boundary

`etsy-two-sided-marketplace-qualified; buyer-seller-trust-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from buyer and seller activity to payment collection, seller settlement, take rate, retention, fraud and trust cost, cross-border logistics, AI/discovery investment, debt, claims, funding, and diluted common residual. GMS, active buyers, active sellers, take rate, revenue, adjusted EBITDA, guidance, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
