# Evidence-chain handoff matrix

Research date: `2026-09-17` (refresh)

## Purpose

This matrix makes the central end-to-end chain auditable for all three active
pilots:

`force -> industry control point -> company selection -> filing -> operating model -> QoE -> owner cash -> valuation -> macro/liquidity -> capital flow -> thesis breaker`

The structured [handoff matrix CSV](data/combined-investment-research-evidence-chain-handoff-matrix-2026-09-16.csv)
is an index of current proof grades, not a replacement for the underlying
ledgers. `diagnostic-panel` means the quality-of-earnings tests exist but do
not establish misstatement; `partial` means an owner-cash or capital-flow
perimeter is visible but not fully allocated; `qualified` means the chain
supports a bounded conclusion with material assumptions.

## Reading the three rows

- **Retail** reaches a filing-backed operating model, historical and current
  QoE diagnostics, valuation expectations, and a macro falsifier. Its bottleneck
  is matched maintenance, lease, tax, service, dilution, and repeated causal
  evidence before common-owner cash can be promoted. The [annual cohort
  denominator control](combined-investment-research-pilot-02-retail-annual-cohort-denominator-control-2026-09-17.md)
  now gives TJX, Target, and Walmart a fiscal-year-aligned reported
  cash-after-property-per-diluted-share screen while preserving those missing
  allocations. The annual reported-cash expectation screen then exposes the
  market-cap burden on that reported denominator without promoting it to
  normalized owner cash.
- **Wheaton–Antamina** reaches a named contract, two-sided upfront cash loop,
  stream economics, financing boundary, QoE settlement controls, valuation
  burden screen, and thesis breaker. Its bottleneck is BHP-only delivery,
  receipt, reserve curve, tax, and debt-waterfall proof.
- **Apollo–Athene–ARI** reaches institutional funding, insurance/legal-entity
  operating economics, statutory QoE ratios, valuation expectations, and a
  transaction perimeter. Its bottleneck is the Athene-to-AGM receipt, borrower
  cash, liability-adjusted return, and common-owner waterfall.

## Promotion rule

The matrix is complete as a handoff control only when every row names its next
bottleneck and safe claim. It is not permission to average the statuses into a
single investment ranking. A downstream stage cannot upgrade an upstream gap:
a valuation screen cannot create owner cash, a statutory ratio cannot create an
Apollo receipt, and a contract cannot create a delivered-ounce ledger.
