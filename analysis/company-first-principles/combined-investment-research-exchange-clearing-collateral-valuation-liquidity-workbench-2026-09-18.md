# Exchange, clearing, and collateral valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench adds CME Group as a distinct derivatives-exchange and central-
counterparty clearing lane. It separates transaction/clearing fees, market
data, client collateral, repo/collateral investment income, technology,
regulatory capacity, and corporate liquidity from information vendors, banks,
brokers, and ordinary financial companies.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CME Group | Exchange and clearing fee cash plus market-data cash after technology, cybersecurity, market supervision, regulatory capacity, client-service, collateral-interest economics, SBC, dividends, and diluted common residual | Matching/clearing systems, resilience, surveillance, cyber, connectivity, market-data platforms, regulatory capital/capacity, private investments, and common capital | Volume migration, fee pressure, rate normalization, collateral/spread income reversal, clearing stress, counterparty/margin event, outage, regulation, or dilution | Revenue, volume, market-data growth, OCF, dividends, and buybacks rise while core fee volume, collateral-adjusted earnings, technology resilience, clearing obligations, or per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$6.521B`; operating income `$4.230B`; OCF `$4.277B`; property purchases `$83.5M`; market-data revenue `$803.1M`; clearing/transaction fees `$5.281B`.
- FY2025 contract volume was `7.060B` round turns, average daily volume `28.129M` contracts, and average rate per contract `$0.696`.
- FY2025 included approximately `$159.656B` of performance bonds and guaranty-fund contributions matched by clearing-system liabilities; this is client collateral, not ordinary corporate cash.
- FY2025 investment income was `$5.737B` and other non-operating expense `$4.834B`; the collateral-interest and distribution bridge must be separated from pure fee economics.
- Corporate cash and cash equivalents were `$4.417B`; long-term debt `$3.422B`; SBC `$94.8M`; dividends `$3.933B`; buybacks `$266.1M`.
- Q1 2026 showed the key normalization issue: OCF was `$2.207B`, while first-half investment income was `$2.819B` and other non-operating expense `$2.510B`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Keep performance bonds, guaranty funds, and restricted collateral out of
   unrestricted corporate liquidity and common-owner cash.
2. Bridge contract volume and rate per contract to clearing/transaction fees,
   incentives, product mix, market share, and customer participation.
3. Separate collateral interest, distributions, funding cost, and rate-cycle
   effects from recurring exchange and market-data fee economics.
4. Charge technology resilience, cybersecurity, surveillance, regulatory
   capacity, outage risk, SBC, and market-data platform maintenance.
5. Test dividends and buybacks against core fee cash after collateral-related
   earnings normalize and clearing obligations remain fully funded.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what volume, rate per contract,
market-data retention, collateral spread, technology investment, regulatory
cost, and cost of capital the equity value requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress
test asks whether volatility, rates, collateral balances, repo/liquidity,
counterparty confidence, market structure, and regulation preserve the clearing
franchise through a market shock.

## Promotion boundary

`exchange-clearing-qualified; collateral-rate-and-volume-normalization-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from contract volume and
market-data users to transaction/clearing fees, collateral balances and
distribution economics, technology/regulatory cost, clearing obligations,
dividends, SBC replacement, and diluted common residual. Client collateral,
volume, OCF, investment income, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic
inputs rather than normalized owner cash.
