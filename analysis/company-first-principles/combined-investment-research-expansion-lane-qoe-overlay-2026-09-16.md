# Expansion-Lane Quality-of-Earnings and Financial-Shenanigans Overlay

Research date: `2026-09-16`

This overlay applies the existing diagnostic QoE and financial-shenanigans
method to the three next-cycle lanes. It is not a fraud score. The purpose is
to prevent a regulatory recovery proxy, statutory income row, facility
availability figure, or fleet cash metric from being promoted into collected
owner cash without the required period, entity, denominator, and receipt
join.

The field-level [structured expansion-lane QoE register](data/combined-investment-research-expansion-lane-qoe-overlay-2026-09-16.csv)
is authoritative. `observed`, `partial`, `searched-negative`, and
`not-assembled` describe evidence state, not wrongdoing.

## Lane-specific diagnostics

### Power-grid customer cash

The central risk is recovery-to-cash timing and capitalization, not an
accusation that regulated revenue is improper. Test whether category recovery,
true-ups, regulatory assets, billing determinants, collections, debt/equity
funding, and operating cash are period-matched. A factor or approved revenue
requirement is not a receipt, and aggregate FPL clause revenue is not
Distribution Inspection cash.

The official PSC refresh now feeds the overlay's cost-timing diagnostics: Form
6P reports `180,000` poles and `$92.1M` of 2026 actual/estimated cost, `$94.1M`
of 2027 projected cost, and `$917.1M` of estimated 2026–2035 program cost.
These observations sharpen the capitalized-cost and true-up denominator, but
they remain program cost and projection fields; they do not establish billed
or collected category cash, source-of-funds allocation, or shareholder return.

The AEP/Duke comparison adds two related controls: customer security is not
automatically unrestricted cash, and a regulator-approved named project is not
yet rate-base cash or owner return. AEP's LOA/prospective-load/security buckets
and Duke's Anderson County project must remain separate from in-service load,
net utility ownership, project spending, billing, and collection. The South
Carolina PSC directive adds conditions and quarterly fuel-supply reporting, but
the full Order `2026-244` is now located and grants the certificate subject to
permit and certification conditions; project cost recovery, billing, and the
cash denominator remain open.

### Insurance statutory named-asset income

The central risks are statutory income versus collected cash, disposal
consideration versus settled proceeds, same-CUSIP appearance versus lot
continuity, related-party/entity perimeter, and asset income versus liability
cost. Interest received in an Accordia row or cash-like consideration in an
Athene row is a diagnostic proxy until remittance, collateral, liability-cost,
and return joins are present.

### Asset-backed collateral and borrowing base

The central risks are stated liquidity versus legal availability, fleet book
value/OEC versus eligible collateral/NOLV, facility capacity versus populated
borrowing-base components, rental revenue/OCF versus maintenance and growth
capex, and used-equipment proceeds versus lifecycle return. A certificate
delivery condition is not the certificate itself.

## Promotion rule

Clear a diagnostic only when the relevant source joins the same legal entity,
period, accounting treatment, denominator, and cash or claim perimeter. Keep a
warning as a thesis breaker when repeated filings change the preferred cash
denominator, reverse cash conversion, or invalidate the valuation assumption.
An unusual ratio alone is never a manipulation conclusion.

The overlay therefore extends the system's chain:

```text
reported recovery, income, liquidity, or fleet output
  -> accrual/timing test
  -> capitalization and recurring-support test
  -> legal-entity and collateral/perimeter test
  -> collected cash and owner denominator
  -> valuation expectation and filing-based breaker
```
