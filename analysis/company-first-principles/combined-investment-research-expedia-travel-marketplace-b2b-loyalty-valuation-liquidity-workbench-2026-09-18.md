# Travel marketplace, B2B distribution, and loyalty valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Expedia from current-period travel-marketplace evidence into a company-specific valuation, agency/merchant, B2B, advertising, loyalty, partner, payment, and owner-cash test. It treats Expedia as a multi-sided travel workflow and distribution platform, not as an airline, hotel owner, or simple referral website.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expedia | Collected travel-marketplace cash after lodging and supplier settlement, agency/merchant payment timing, refunds, B2B partner economics, advertising, One Key loyalty, technology, marketing, debt, and dilution | Marketplace technology, partner integrations, customer acquisition, loyalty rewards, payment and fraud systems, brand and B2B sales, advertising, working capital, and acquisitions | Travel demand and cancellation, supplier concentration, hotel/alternative-accommodation quality, merchant credit, refunds, B2B partner dependence, ad-cycle, competition, debt, and dilution | Gross bookings, room nights, revenue, B2B growth, advertising, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, loyalty adoption, or buybacks rise while supplier settlement, cancellation/refund, customer acquisition, partner economics, collection, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was about `$14.733B`, gross bookings `$119.590B`, operating income `$1.871B`, net income attributable to Expedia Group `$1.294B`, and adjusted EBITDA `$3.501B`.
- FY2025 B2C gross bookings were about `$83.867B`, B2B gross bookings `$35.723B`, B2C revenue `$9.474B`, B2B revenue `$4.842B`, EG advertising `$758M`, and trivago third-party revenue `$417M`.
- Q2 `2026` revenue was `$4.315B`, gross bookings `$33.928B`, operating income `$800M`, net income `$878M`, adjusted EBITDA `$1.119B`, and booked room nights `111.5M`.
- Q1 `2026` revenue was `$3.426B`, gross bookings `$35.530B`, adjusted EBITDA `$542M`, B2B gross bookings grew `22%`, and B2B revenue grew `25%`; Expedia repurchased about `$700M` of shares.
- One Key links Expedia, Hotels.com, and Vrbo across lodging, flights, packages, cars, cruises, and activities; it is a relationship asset, not automatically common-owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile room nights, gross bookings, agency/merchant revenue, supplier settlement, customer payment, refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, and working capital; gross bookings are not cash.
2. Separate B2C, B2B, agency, merchant, Vrbo, hotel, alternative accommodation, advertising, trivago, and One Key cohorts; contract and margin economics differ.
3. Test loyalty and B2B growth through incremental booking, customer acquisition cost, rewards and payment burden, partner retention, fraud, supplier quality, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep adjusted EBITDA, FCF, advertising revenue, gross bookings, room nights, loyalty adoption, dividends, and buybacks separate from recurring collected owner cash.
5. Reconcile supplier payables, deferred merchant cash, refunds, technology, marketing, credit risk, debt, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what room-night growth, take rate, B2B mix, advertising monetization, loyalty retention, supplier economics, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether travel cycles, inflation, geopolitical shocks, cancellations, supplier bargaining power, payment losses, competition, rates, and leverage preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`expedia-travel-marketplace-qualified; b2b-loyalty-supplier-settlement-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from travel and partner cohorts to supplier settlement, customer collection, refunds and cancellations, B2B economics, loyalty cost, advertising delivery, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Gross bookings, room nights, revenue, B2B growth, advertising, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, loyalty adoption, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
