# Combined investment research force-to-company atlas

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Purpose

This atlas makes the front of the research chain explicit. It joins social
signals, Inc. 5000 discovery, IBIS industry framing, macro/liquidity tests, and
primary company filings into a bounded research question. The rows are
hypothesis routes, not claims that an upstream signal proves company value.

The source families retain separate jobs:

```text
social observation
  -> private-company formation and pain-point discovery
  -> industry structure and bottleneck
  -> macro transmission mechanism
  -> public-company filing test
  -> owner-cash / valuation / falsifier gate
```

## Source boundaries

- The [social evidence matrix](/home/mehtama1/git-repo/social-research/analysis/social/cross-repository-theme-evidence-matrix.md) reports signals and mapped business context. It does not prove prevalence, causality, market size, or retailer economics.
- The [IBIS crosswalk](../../notes/ibis-industries-crosswalk.md) supplies industry and force framing. It does not prove a company-period fact.
- The [Inc. 5000 research layer](/home/mehtama1/git-repo/inc5000-analysis/README.md) identifies growth-company patterns and discovery lenses. It does not prove profitability or owner cash.
- The [current synthesis](combined-investment-research-current-synthesis.md) and three pilot ledgers carry the primary filing and cash conclusions.
- The [BHP FY2026 boundary](capital-flow-wheaton-antamina-bhp-fy2026-annual-report-boundary-2026-09-16.md), [Wheaton forward-profile boundary](capital-flow-wheaton-antamina-investor-day-forward-profile-boundary-2026-09-16.md), [WHCO guarantee boundary](capital-flow-apollo-athene-broadcom-anthropic-whco-guarantee-boundary-2026-09-16.md), and [Broadcom fee-timing boundary](capital-flow-apollo-broadcom-capital-solutions-fee-timing-boundary-2026-09-16.md) update the selected routes without changing their qualified proof grades.
- The post-pilot [FPL PSC refresh](capital-flow-fpl-distribution-inspection-official-psc-source-refresh-2026-09-16.md), [MF1 SEC refresh](capital-flow-apollo-athene-mf1-public-sec-document-refresh-2026-09-16.md), and [URI collateral refresh](capital-flow-uri-borrowing-base-collateral-availability-proof-chase-pass-1.md) extend the atlas to the next-cycle public-source routes. These rows explicitly do not claim upstream prevalence, Inc. 5000 discovery, owner cash, or final return.

## Reading rule

An atlas row is complete only when the filing test is visible and its missing
document is named. A strong social signal or large private-company revenue sum
can prioritize a lane; it cannot promote the lane's company conclusion.
