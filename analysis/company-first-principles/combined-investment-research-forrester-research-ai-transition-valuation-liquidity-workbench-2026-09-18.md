# Forrester research and AI-transition valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench extends the institutional-research/advisory lane with Forrester
as a negative-transition case beside Gartner. It tests whether research,
consulting, events, and AI-enabled delivery can stabilize contract value and
cash after client-budget pressure, goodwill impairment, restructuring, office
investment, SBC, debt, and dilution.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forrester | Research-membership cash plus separately modeled consulting and event cash after renewal, contract value, analyst/consultant labor, AI delivery, restructuring, office capex, debt, SBC, and dilution | Research workflow, AI product, analyst labor, consulting staff, events, client acquisition, office renovation, technology, and restructuring | Contract-value decline, AI substitution, client-budget cuts, event weakness, recurring restructuring, office spending, debt covenants, or liquidity draw | AI usage and adjusted margin rise while contract value, billings, deferred revenue, GAAP cash, goodwill, or diluted residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$396.9M`, contract value about `$292.4M` down `6%`,
  operating cash `$21.1M`, property spending `$3.0M`, and GAAP net loss
  `$119.4M`.
- FY2025 included `$110.7M` of goodwill impairment: `$83.9M` in Q1 and
  `$26.8M` in Q4. The non-cash charge is still evidence that prior research-
  unit expectations declined.
- Deferred revenue fell to `$141.8M` from `$145.4M`; 2024 operating cash was
  negative `$3.9M` while the company repurchased `$15.9M` of stock.
- Forrester had `$63.3M` cash, `$64.3M` marketable investments, `$35.0M` debt,
  and expected roughly `$28M` of 2026 office capital spending, partly offset by
  a `$17.2M` landlord allowance.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Bridge contract value to renewal, billings, deferred revenue, collection,
   wallet expansion, and cash—not merely AI usage or client activity.
2. Separate research membership, consulting utilization, event attendance and
   sponsorship, and AI delivery cost.
3. Keep goodwill impairment, restructuring, SBC, acquisition amortization,
   office renovation, and product migration visible if they recur.
4. Test whether AI improves retention, pricing, delivery cost, and cash rather
   than only increasing usage or reducing reported labor.
5. Reconcile cash, investments, borrowing capacity, debt covenants, deferred
   revenue, accrued liabilities, repurchases, and diluted shares.
6. Treat contract value, adjusted earnings, client retention, AI adoption, OCF,
   and buybacks as diagnostic until common residual stabilizes.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test converts retained research clients,
contract value, renewal, consulting utilization, event contribution, AI
pricing, analyst labor, restructuring, office capex, debt, and dilution into
owner cash. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether corporate and
government budgets, AI substitution, rates, travel, and client liquidity keep
decision-support demand intact. A low-capital business can still have weak
liquidity when contracts and cash conversion decline.

## Promotion boundary

`forrester-qualified; contract-stabilization-and-ai-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from contract value,
retention, billings, deferred revenue, AI usage and pricing, consulting and
event contribution, labor, restructuring, office capital, goodwill, debt, SBC,
and diluted common residual. Adjusted earnings, client activity, AI adoption,
OCF, and repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
