# Live sports, news, affiliate fees, and AVOD valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Fox from current-period media evidence into a company-specific valuation, live-rights, affiliate, advertising, Tubi, direct-to-consumer, acquisition, and owner-cash test. It treats Fox as a national appointment-content and hybrid distribution platform, not as generic linear television.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fox | Collected affiliate, advertising, sports/news, Tubi AVOD, and direct-subscription cash after content and sports rights, distribution, production, platform delivery, Roku integration, debt, taxes, and dilution | Sports and news rights, production, local and network operations, Tubi content and technology, FOX One, advertising sales, distribution systems, Roku integration, and working capital | Rights inflation, sports calendar, political cycle, affiliate churn, ratings, Tubi monetization, ad budgets, acquisition integration, debt, and dilution | Revenue, affiliate fees, advertising, adjusted EBITDA, Tubi growth, audience, sports rights, or buybacks rise while rights cost, affiliate renewal, ad collection, Tubi delivery, content obligations, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was about `$16.30B` and adjusted EBITDA about `$3.62B`; Q4 FY2025 revenue was about `$3.29B` and adjusted EBITDA about `$939M`.
- FY2026 Q4 revenue was about `$4.21B`, net income `$696M`, and adjusted EBITDA `$1.20B`; full-year FY2026 revenue reached `$17.13B` and adjusted EBITDA `$3.91B`.
- Q4 FY2026 distribution revenue grew `5%` and advertising grew `78%`, driven substantially by FIFA Men's World Cup inventory and continued Tubi growth.
- FY2026 Q3 revenue was about `$3.99B`, adjusted EBITDA `$954M`; advertising was about `$1.56B` versus `$2.04B` prior year because the prior year included the Super Bowl.
- FY2026 Q2 revenue was about `$5.18B`, adjusted EBITDA `$692M`; distribution revenue grew `4%`, advertising `1%`, and Tubi, sports, news, political comparisons, FOX One, and the announced Roku acquisition shaped the platform.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile affiliate subscribers, distribution fees, advertising, sports and news inventory, ratings, Tubi impressions, subscription collection, content delivery, and cash; audience is not cash.
2. Separate cable networks, broadcast, sports, news, Tubi, FOX One, local, political, and sublicensing cohorts; calendar and rights economics differ.
3. Test sports rights, Tubi investment, FOX One, Roku acquisition, and programming through rights cost, audience retention, ad yield, platform fees, debt, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep adjusted EBITDA, event-driven ad spikes, audience growth, Tubi growth, affiliate fees, buybacks, and acquisition claims separate from recurring collected common-owner cash.
5. Reconcile content commitments, rights payments, production, affiliate receivables, platform cost, debt, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what sports and news rights, affiliate retention, advertising yield, Tubi monetization, FOX One conversion, content investment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether rights inflation, political and sports calendar swings, cord-cutting, streaming substitution, rates, advertising recession, and acquisition leverage preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`fox-live-attention-qualified; rights-affiliate-avod-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from rights, affiliate, advertising, Tubi, and subscription cohorts to collection, content and rights cash, platform delivery, Roku integration, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, affiliate fees, advertising, adjusted EBITDA, audience, Tubi growth, sports rights, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
