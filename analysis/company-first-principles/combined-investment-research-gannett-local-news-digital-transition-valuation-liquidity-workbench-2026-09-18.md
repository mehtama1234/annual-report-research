# Gannett local-news and digital transition valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Gannett/USA TODAY Co. from current-period evidence into a
company-specific valuation, subscription, advertising, LocaliQ, print-transition,
AI-licensing, restructuring, debt, and common-owner cash test. It does not pool
local newspapers, premium national subscriptions, digital advertising, and agency
services into a generic media multiple.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gannett | Collected local-news and digital cash after print production, circulation, subscriptions, advertising, LocaliQ delivery, content, traffic acquisition, restructuring, pension/debt, and dilution | Newsrooms, print plants, digital products, subscriptions, LocaliQ platform, audience data, AI licensing, content rights, and working capital | Print decline, ad recession, traffic-platform dependence, subscriber churn, LocaliQ customer loss, AI discovery disruption, debt, pension/claims, or dilution | Digital mix and subscribers rise while print runoff, collection, LocaliQ margin, traffic cost, debt service, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$2.302B`, digital revenue `46%` of total, adjusted EBITDA `$263.0M`, operating cash flow `$114.4M`, and digital-only subscriptions about `1.5M`.
- Q2 2026 revenue was `$536.3M`, down `8.3%`; digital revenue was `$254.3M` or `47.4%` of revenue; adjusted EBITDA `$56.9M`; operating cash flow `$35.4M`; and FCF `$19.6M`.
- Q2 average monthly unique visitors were `158M`; digital-only subscription revenue was `$45.6M`; LocaliQ core-platform revenue was `$106.3M`.
- Q1 2026 cash was `$85.2M`, total debt principal `$988.3M`, and first-lien net leverage `2.3x`.
- Management reported AI licensing deals and about `$136M` of debt repayment during 2025, but digital growth still had to outrun ongoing print erosion.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile print circulation, digital subscriptions, advertising, LocaliQ,
   licensing, commerce, and audience traffic to renewals, churn, collections, and
   platform costs.
2. Separate USA TODAY Media, local news, Newsquest, digital subscriptions, digital
   ads, LocaliQ, and AI licensing by recurring revenue, margin, and customer control.
3. Keep newsrooms, print plants, digital product, traffic acquisition, platform
   fees, restructuring, pensions, debt, and working capital in the denominator.
4. Test whether digital revenue growth represents durable direct relationships or
   traffic arbitrage dependent on search/social platforms and temporary ad demand.
5. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA, restructuring, impairment, pension/claims, debt,
   interest, asset sales, SBC, and diluted shares before treating FCF as owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what digital subscription growth, churn,
ad yield, LocaliQ retention, print runoff, AI licensing, margin, reinvestment rate,
leverage, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress
test asks whether local advertising, household subscription budgets, platform power,
AI discovery, printing costs, and refinancing can preserve liquidity through a media
transition.

## Promotion boundary

`gannett-local-news-qualified; print-digital-transition-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from circulation, subscriptions,
digital products, LocaliQ, and licensing to collections, churn, traffic cost,
required newsroom/product spend, restructuring, debt, claims, and diluted common
residual. Revenue, digital mix, visitors, subscriptions, adjusted EBITDA, guidance,
and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
