# Global technology-services valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Accenture from AI-services and consulting evidence into a
company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps advisory,
implementation, managed services, and advanced-AI bookings separate from
Jacobs engineering, Huron institutional consulting, Korn Ferry talent advisory,
and enterprise software, while testing utilization, labor, acquisitions,
deferred revenue, SBC, and diluted common-owner cash.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accenture global technology services | Collected advisory, implementation, and managed-service cash after professional labor, utilization, training, attrition, subcontractors, acquisitions, optimization costs, deferred revenue, SBC, and dilution | Workforce hiring/training, delivery centers, R&D, AI capability, acquisitions, client-transition costs, working capital, SBC replacement, and repurchases | Enterprise budget pause, AI pricing or labor substitution, utilization decline, wage inflation, client concentration, delayed collection, acquisition underperformance, or restructuring recurrence | Bookings and adjusted margin rise while conversion, revenue per professional, utilization, renewal, labor cost, SBC, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$69.670B`, OCF `$11.474B`, capex `$600M`, and business
  purchases/investments `$1.471B`; the broader residual after those uses was
  approximately `$9.403B` before labor, SBC, and shareholder claims.
- FY2025 bookings were `$80.6B`, generative-AI bookings `$5.9B`, GAAP operating
  margin `14.7%`, adjusted operating margin `15.6%`, and FCF about `$10.9B`.
- FY2025 SBC was approximately `$2.094B`; business-optimization costs were
  `$615M`, and acquisitions, research, and learning/development consumed about
  `$3.3B` in the packet's broader capability-investment view.
- Receivables and contract assets used `$1.021B` of FY2025 cash, while deferred
  revenue supplied `$707M`; bookings are not revenue and revenue is not
  collected common cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile bookings into revenue, acceptance, collection, margin, renewal,
   and managed-service attachment; track cancellation, duration, and client
   concentration.
2. Separate advisory, implementation, managed services, and advanced-AI work by
   revenue per professional, utilization, labor cost, pricing, and delivery
   risk.
3. Keep SBC, shares issued, repurchases, retention, and diluted shares together;
   FCF is not fully unrestricted while the workforce requires equity.
4. Treat acquisitions as recurring capability and labor investment; measure
   acquired employees, attrition, utilization, cross-sell, integration cost,
   goodwill, and post-close cash return.
5. Retain recurring retraining, severance, bench, recruiting, and optimization
   costs in the owner-cash denominator.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what bookings conversion, utilization,
revenue per professional, managed-service renewal, AI pricing, wage burden,
acquisition return, reinvestment, and cost of capital are required. AI bookings
must become delivered and collected work at durable margins.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether enterprise customers can fund
transformations through higher rates, recession, wage inflation, currency,
cloud/vendor changes, and AI-driven labor substitution. Liquidity passes only
when receivables, deferred revenue obligations, workforce costs, debt, SBC, and
common residual remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`global-technology-services-qualified; bookings-conversion-and-labor-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from bookings to delivered
revenue, acceptance, utilization, labor/subcontractor cost, renewal,
collection, acquisition return, deferred revenue, SBC replacement, and diluted
common residual. Bookings, book-to-bill, adjusted margin, OCF, FCF, AI revenue,
and repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
