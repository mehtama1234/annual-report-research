# GoDaddy small-business digital-presence valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench tests GoDaddy as customer-edge internet infrastructure for small businesses and creators. It keeps domains, hosting, websites, commerce, payments, subscriptions, customer care, AI tools, bookings, gross payments volume, working capital, debt, and diluted common residual separate from reported revenue, ARR, and free cash flow.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GoDaddy | SMB digital-presence and commerce cash after domain identity, hosting, website/services delivery, payments, customer support, AI product investment, acquisitions, debt, and diluted common residual | Platform infrastructure, domain renewals, hosting capacity, customer care, commerce/payments, AI development, sales, acquisitions, working capital, debt, and shares | SMB formation/spending slowdown, domain renewal weakness, platform competition, payment/commerce losses, AI displacement, support-cost inflation, or refinancing | ARR and ARPU rise while domain retention, customer count, commerce GPV, support cost, payment margins, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$4.9511B`, operating cash flow `$1.5994B`, approximate free cash flow `$1.5755B`, ARR `$4.3362B`, and ARPU `$242`.
- Q2 2026 revenue was `$1.2980B`, A&C revenue `$514.8M`, Core revenue `$783.2M`, customers `20.5M`, ARPU `$250`, ARR `$4.4214B`, and free cash flow `$443.5M`.
- GoDaddy served more than `20M` entrepreneurs and more than `80M` domain names; Q4 commerce gross payments volume was `$3.4B`, up `31%` year over year.
- Airo AI Builder, Agent Name Service, Agentic Resource Discovery, and the GoDaddy Developer Platform are growth and reinvestment claims requiring retention and cash-cost testing.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile ARR and ARPU to customer count, domain renewals, churn, bookings, collections, refunds, and support cost.
2. Separate domain/hosting persistence from commerce, payments, AI, acquisitions, and pricing/mix effects.
3. Test GPV growth against payment processing, fraud, customer-support, infrastructure, and merchant-credit exposure.
4. Keep stock compensation, debt, buybacks, acquisitions, working capital, and diluted shares in the common-owner residual schedule.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what customer growth, renewal, ARPU, commerce adoption, AI conversion, reinvestment rate, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether small businesses and creators continue funding digital identity, hosting, and payments through recession, fraud, platform, privacy, and AI-interface shocks.

## Promotion boundary

`godaddy-qualified; smb-identity-and-commerce-collection-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from domains and subscriptions to renewals, customer collection, commerce/payment settlement, support and infrastructure costs, AI reinvestment, debt, claims, and diluted common residual. Revenue, ARR, ARPU, GPV, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, guidance, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
