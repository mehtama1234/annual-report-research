# Partner enrollment and workforce-education services valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Grand Canyon Education from current-period education evidence into a company-specific valuation, contract, enrollment-quality, reinvestment, and owner-cash test. It treats GCE as an outsourced university-partner and workforce-education platform, not as a conventional tuition-funded campus operator.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grand Canyon Education | Collected partner-service cash after enrollment acquisition, online delivery, off-campus classroom/lab sites, student support, compliance, contract modifications, litigation, taxes, capital spending, and dilution | Enrollment technology, online course/content, support labor, off-campus sites, clinical/lab capacity, partner implementation, compliance, and working capital | Partner bargaining, revenue-per-student compression, enrollment mix, regulatory/Title IV changes, teach-outs, healthcare program demand, site utilization, litigation, and dilution | Enrollment and service revenue rise while revenue per student, partner retention, collection, site economics, compliance, litigation, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 service revenue was `$1.106B`; reported operating income was `$265.9M`; net income was `$216.2M`; and adjusted EBITDA was `$368.6M`.
- Q2 2026 service revenue was `$264.0M`, operating income `$58.2M`, net income `$45.9M`, diluted EPS `$1.75`, adjusted diluted EPS `$1.81`, and adjusted EBITDA `$73.4M`.
- Q2 university-partner enrollments rose `7.6%` to `126,231`; GCU enrollments rose `7.5%` to `121,921`; off-campus site enrollments rose `16.8%` to `5,829`; and GCU online enrollments rose `7.8%` to `113,011`.
- Management said revenue per student was pressured by partner contract modifications and mix shift, while ABSN students at off-campus sites generated higher revenue per student than the core GCU agreement.
- The packet records `47` off-campus classroom/laboratory sites at year-end 2025, one to two additional sites planned for the second half of 2026, and teach-outs at three partner sites where recruiting had stopped.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile enrollment growth to student starts, persistence, completions, partner collections, refunds, contract terms, revenue per student, and actual service cash; enrollment is not equivalent to durable revenue.
2. Separate GCU, university-partner, online, off-campus site, ABSN, and other program cohorts; mix can grow while price and contribution deteriorate.
3. Test partner contract modifications, revenue-share terms, recruiting restrictions, teach-outs, and bargaining power as recurring economics rather than isolated adjustments.
4. Keep off-campus classroom and laboratory site expansion separate from site utilization, clinical/lab capacity, staffing, compliance, maintenance, and future capital needs.
5. Reconcile litigation settlements, regulatory/Title IV exposure, student-support cost, bad debt, capitalized technology, and working capital before promoting adjusted EBITDA or EPS.
6. Treat buybacks, dividends, adjusted EPS, and partner enrollment growth as residual claims requiring compliance, contract, reinvestment, and diluted-share validation.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what partner enrollments, persistence, revenue per student, program mix, site utilization, online delivery cost, compliance, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether labor shortages and credential demand survive household affordability, partner budget, regulatory, demographic, and employment shocks while clinical capacity, support, technology, and compliance obligations remain.

## Promotion boundary

`grand-canyon-education-qualified; partner-contract-enrollment-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from enrollment and partner contracts to collection, persistence, revenue per student, site utilization, compliance, litigation, technology/site reinvestment, taxes, and diluted common residual. Service revenue, adjusted EBITDA, EPS, enrollment, online growth, and site counts remain diagnostic inputs.
