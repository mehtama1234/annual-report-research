# Cencora FY2026 Q3 acquisition, working-capital, and cash-quality refresh

Research date: `2026-09-17`

This refresh advances the [healthcare-distribution first pass](combined-investment-research-healthcare-distribution-first-pass-2026-09-17.md) with Cencora's nine-month FY2026 filing. It tests the earlier acquisition-heavy cash screen against current OneOncology accounting, working-capital funding, debt, share repurchases, litigation credits, and the adjusted-free-cash-flow definition.

## Reported cash versus acquisition cash

The nine-month cash bridge is:

```text
operating cash flow                         $1,687.530M
  - capital expenditures                       $511.033M
  - acquired companies, net of cash          $4,973.696M
  = residual after visible acquisition     $(3,797.199M)
```

The residual is a burden screen, not normalized owner cash. It excludes dividends, interest, debt repayment, taxes, legal claims, and dilution. It does, however, establish that the current acquisition program is not funded by contemporaneous operating cash after capex. The financing statement shows `$3.0B` of senior notes and `$1.5B` of variable-rate term loans raised to fund part of OneOncology, alongside `$1.0B` of share purchases and `$360.6M` of dividends. This is the capital-allocation boundary that adjusted EPS alone hides.

Management's adjusted free-cash-flow measure is `$1,142.3M` for the nine months after excluding a `$34.2M` antitrust-settlement gain from OCF less capex. That is a useful supplemental operating measure, but it is not acquisition-adjusted owner cash: the `$4.974B` acquisition outflow sits outside the metric, and the definition excludes legal-settlement items according to management's classification.

## OneOncology: economic consideration is larger than net cash paid

The preliminary purchase-price allocation reports `$4,648.720M` cash consideration, `$58.891M` cash acquired, and `$4,589.829M` net cash paid. It also records `$752.141M` estimated contingent consideration, `$1,934.224M` fair value for Cencora's previously held equity-method investment, and a `$51.990M` settlement of a pre-existing receivable. Estimated total consideration is `$7,387.075M`.

The acquisition generated a `$1,086.6M` remeasurement gain from the previously held investment and extinguishment of a put-option liability. That gain is economically distinct from operating cash and is excluded from adjusted EPS. The purchase allocation remains preliminary; acquired intangible assets total `$3.062B`, including a `$2.330B` management service agreement. The return hurdle must therefore include cash paid, retained-equity/contingent consideration, amortization, integration spending, debt interest, physician economics, and the previously held stake—not just the headline net cash outflow.

## Working-capital funding is the central operating risk

Management says the nine-month increase in accounts payable, receivables, and inventories used approximately `$1.1B` of operating cash. The filing specifically attributes approximately `$0.8B` of receivable growth to sales and payment timing and approximately `$1.0B` of inventory growth to higher business volume, partly offset by approximately `$0.6B` of accounts-payable growth.

At June 30, 2026, reported consolidated cash was `$2.815B`, accounts receivable `$25.448B`, and inventories `$21.030B`. The company also has a receivables securitization facility, and the filing distinguishes consolidated balances from held-for-sale MWI Animal Health and consolidated VIE/Profarma balances. Those perimeter distinctions matter: a distributor can show scale and accounting profit while common-owner cash depends on supplier terms, receivable monetization, inventory turns, and subsidiary/legal-entity liquidity.

## Quality-of-earnings and financial-shenanigans controls

1. **Acquisition exclusion:** do not call adjusted free cash flow “owner cash” while OneOncology's `$4.6B` net cash acquisition and `$752M` contingent consideration remain outside it.
2. **Working-capital timing:** test whether the `$1.1B` operating cash use is temporary volume build, a recurring funding requirement, or a deterioration in collection and inventory economics.
3. **LIFO:** the Q3 reconciliation excludes a `$381.9M` LIFO credit. Because the issuer says LIFO depends on inflation, product mix, and manufacturer pricing, it should be treated as a volatile margin and inventory-quality item, not a permanent earnings add-back.
4. **Litigation:** Q3 adjusted operating results include a `$102M` opioid-liability reduction and the nine-month period includes an `$86.8M` derivative-lawsuit credit. Credits are not recurring operating cash and should not support terminal margins.
5. **Remeasurement:** the `$1.087B` OneOncology gain is a purchase-accounting event, not operating performance or cash received from customers.
6. **Debt and dilution:** the acquisition used senior notes and variable-rate term loans while the company repurchased `$1.008B` of shares. The per-share thesis must carry interest, refinancing, contingent consideration, and share-count effects together.
7. **Perimeter:** held-for-sale MWI, VIE/Profarma, noncontrolling interests, and specialty physician retained equity must be separated before assigning cash to Cencora common owners.

## Decision

Cencora's Q3 filing strengthens the specialty-acquisition growth story but also makes the financing and QoE burden more explicit. Revenue and adjusted EPS growth coexist with a `$3.797B` residual after visible acquisition cash and capex, `$11.723B` total debt, a `$1.0B` share repurchase, volatile LIFO and litigation adjustments, and more than `$1B` of working-capital cash absorption.

Status: `q3-acquisition-cash-updated; working-capital-and-perimeter-open; no-ranking`.

Promotion requires a post-close OneOncology cohort schedule showing incremental gross profit, integration cash, physician/retained-equity economics, working-capital settlement, debt interest, contingent consideration, and diluted common-owner cash. Until then, Cencora remains a high-value healthcare-distribution diagnostic, not a promoted owner-cash candidate.
