## QoE interpretation

- **Cash conversion:** compare gross profit and collected cash, not revenue,
  because much of the product value passes through the distributor.
- **Working capital:** inventory, receivables, rebates, returns, chargebacks,
  and payables can move OCF independently of current-period gross profit.
- **Acquisitions:** acquisition cash may be growth capital, maintenance of a
  specialty platform, or a recurring use; it cannot be classified without the
  acquired cohort's post-close cash return.
- **Adjusted earnings:** a larger adjusted-to-GAAP ratio increases the required
  reconciliation; it does not establish aggressive accounting.
