# Hospital consumables and infusion valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Baxter from hospital-demand evidence into a
company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps IV solutions,
infusion systems, injectable pharmaceuticals, connected care, patient support,
surgery, and compounding separate from Abbott's diversified clinical products
and procedure-platform medtech, while testing Kidney Care separation effects,
quality holds, inventory, debt, and diluted common-owner cash.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baxter hospital consumables and infusion | Remaining-portfolio cash after hospital consumables, infusion reliability, manufacturing, quality/remediation, product holds, working capital, Kidney Care separation, debt, leases, and dilution | Production capacity, quality systems, IV/infusion R&D, inventory, receivables, product remediation, technology, stranded separation costs, debt reduction, SBC, and replacement capital | Product hold or recall, hospital procurement pressure, inventory reserve, reimbursement/quality event, stranded costs, leverage/refinancing, divestiture indemnity, or margin recovery failure | Sales and adjusted EPS recover while gross margin, quality cash, inventory, debt reduction, or diluted per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 continuing-operations OCF was `$951M`, PP&E capex `$513M`, developed
  technology/investments `$9M`, and the mechanical residual about `$429M` before
  financing, dividends, and other claims.
- FY2025 net loss attributable to shareholders was `$957M`; special items
  adversely affected results by approximately `$2.09B`. Repeated categories must
  remain in the economic denominator.
- Kidney Care was sold for `$3.8B` in cash in January 2025; Baxter repaid about
  `$3.81B` of debt during the year and still ended with `$9.48B` of debt and
  finance-lease obligations.
- Q2 2026 sales were approximately `$2.96B`, first-half FCF approximately
  `$257M`, Medical Products & Therapies sales about `$2.1B`, and inventory
  `$2.316B` versus `$2.232B` at year-end 2025.
- The Novum IQ shipment/installation hold and higher-cost inventory make product
  quality and remediation cash central to the recovery case.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile GAAP earnings and cash to special items: divestiture/separation,
   quality remediation, product holds, impairment, restructuring, legal,
   environmental, stranded costs, and recurring manufacturing investment.
2. Keep Kidney Care sale proceeds and discontinued-operation cash separate from
   continuing-portfolio operating cash; the sale changed the business denominator.
3. Track product holds, returns, reserves, remediation, customer concessions,
   inventory aging, and quality-system spending by product generation.
4. Reverse temporary VAT-receivable collections and working-capital releases;
   test whether inventory, payables, and receivables normalize with sales.
5. Split sustaining quality/compliance capex from growth capex and test debt,
   leases, interest, dividends, SBC, and diluted shares against residual cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what recurring hospital volume,
infusion utilization, pricing, manufacturing margin, quality cost, capital
intensity, post-separation cost, debt burden, and cost of capital are required.
The remaining portfolio must earn its own capital after the divestiture.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether hospitals, pharmacies, and home
care users can maintain procurement through reimbursement pressure, product
shortages, quality events, rates, and tighter credit. Liquidity passes only
when remediation, debt, inventory, and service obligations remain fundable.

## Promotion boundary

`hospital-consumables-qualified; quality-reset-and-debt-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from product volume and
installed workflow to quality, inventory, receivable collection, remediation,
maintenance/growth capex, separation cost, debt service, SBC, and diluted common
residual. Sales, adjusted EPS, FCF, divestiture proceeds, dividends, and
repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
