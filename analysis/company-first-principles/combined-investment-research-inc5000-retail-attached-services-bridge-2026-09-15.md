# Inc. 5000 to retail filing bridge: Grocery TV and attached services

Research date: `2026-09-15`

## Purpose

This bridge turns the Inc. 5000 layer from a general discovery reference into
one concrete company-to-control-point hypothesis. It does not treat private-
company growth as proof of public-company economics. The candidate identifies
a business model and routes that model into primary filing tests.

## Hypothesis and public-company route

```text
in-store media / retailer monetization
  -> retailers and brands pay for attention and measurement
  -> retailer owns the physical and digital control point
  -> Target Roundel / card / other services and Walmart advertising /
     membership / fulfillment become filing tests
  -> test payer, burden carrier, accounting classification, collection,
     capex, tax, and common-owner cash
```

| Discovery hypothesis | Public filing test | Current evidence | Falsifier or missing proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retail media monetizes an existing store/customer workflow | Target Roundel revenue and Walmart advertising/ecosystem disclosures | Target reports `$525M` H1 advertising and `$1.141B` attached-revenue pool; Walmart reports advertising growth and segment-level income | Advertising growth without durable gross profit, or service burden consumes the benefit |
| The retailer, not necessarily the media vendor, controls the scarce interface | Identify payer, owner, and risk carrier | Target Roundel classification is arrangement-dependent; TD owns/funds/underwrites card receivables; Walmart combines membership and other income | Costs, data rights, or collections sit outside the retailer in a way that defeats the control-point thesis |
| Attached services can improve retail economics | Join service revenue to direct cost, working capital, capex, tax, and cash | Walmart says advertising benefits gross-profit rate; Target discloses the aggregate service pool and consolidated cash | Service-level cash conversion is negative, temporary, or not allocable to common owners |

## Proof-grade result

`Inc. 5000 discovery-to-public-filing route confirmed; private-company growth
is not public-company cash proof; attached-service cash conversion remains
unresolved.`

This bridge strengthens the front of the chain and identifies a falsifiable
control-point test. It does not add Grocery TV revenue to Target or Walmart,
and it does not promote advertising, membership, card profit sharing, or
fulfillment revenue into normalized owner cash.
