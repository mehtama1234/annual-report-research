# Industrial contractors H1 2026 cash-quality refresh

## Purpose and period control

This refresh updates the contractor lane with the six-month period ended June
30, 2026 for Comfort Systems USA, EMCOR Group, and Quanta Services. It does not
pool these H1 figures with FY2025 or compare them as a ranking. The objective is
to test whether current cash, capital spending, acquisitions, and receivable
pressure change the project-to-owner-cash boundary.

| EMCOR Group | $289.9M | $59.9M | $95.0M | $230.1M |

The arithmetic is a screening surface only. It excludes working-capital
normalization, debt and lease service, taxes, claims, integration costs,
maintenance-versus-growth classification, and dilution.

## What changed in the cash question

Comfort Systems' H1 operating cash and acquisitions remain large relative to
the asset-spend line, but the cash number cannot be assigned to a named
data-center, mechanical, or electrical project without billing and collection
evidence. EMCOR's H1 OCF is much lower than FY2025's annual OCF; that is a
period observation, not proof of deterioration, because seasonality, timing,
acquisition integration, and working capital must be reconciled. Quanta's H1
acquisition spending is material relative to H1 OCF, which makes acquired versus
organic cash and purchase-price return the decisive current-period test.

The filing packets also expose a recurring risk configuration: receivables and
contract-related balances can grow while reported work and backlog remain
strong. That does not establish a quality problem, but it raises the required
tests around contract assets, retainage, customer advances, claims, cost to
complete, and cash collection.

## QoE / financial-shenanigans refresh

The current period must be tested for:

- revenue recognized from progress estimates versus amounts billed and
  collected;
- contract assets, billed receivables, retainage, allowances, and aging;
- favorable or adverse cost-to-complete revisions, change orders, claims, and
  loss contracts;
- acquisition accounting, acquired backlog, integration cash, and incremental
  margin;
- fleet and equipment additions split between replacement and expansion;
- customer advances, supplier terms, surety capacity, revolver use, SBC, and
  diluted shares.

No fraud or manipulation finding is made. These are period-matched control
tests for whether reported earnings and adjusted EBITDA represent repeatable
cash economics.

## Promotion status

The refresh strengthens the current-period evidence surface but does not
promote owner cash. A promotion requires a named project or customer cohort
with funded scope, billed amount, collection, cost-to-complete, paid capital,
claims, acquisition/debt allocation, and diluted common-owner residual.
