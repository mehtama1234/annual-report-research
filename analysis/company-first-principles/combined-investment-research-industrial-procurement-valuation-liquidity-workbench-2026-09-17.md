# Industrial procurement valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-17`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Applied Industrial Technologies, MSC Industrial Direct,
and W.W. Grainger from company analyses into distinct valuation, reinvestment,
liquidity, and thesis-breaker objects. It separates technical engineered
solutions, embedded metalworking procurement, and high-touch/digital MRO from
the electrical-distribution and fleet models already routed.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Applied Industrial Technologies | Technical distribution and engineered-solutions cash after MRO, automation, talent, projects, inventory, acquisitions, and debt | Engineered labor, technical specialists, inventory, branches, automation projects, acquisitions, SBC, and diluted shares | Manufacturing cycle, technical talent, project delays, customer capex, supplier terms, and acquisition leverage | Engineered Solutions grows while project collection, service cost, inventory, or diluted per-share cash deteriorates |

| W.W. Grainger | High-touch enterprise plus digital endless-assortment cash after inventory, fulfillment, product data, service, and platform investment | `4.6M` customers, inventory, distribution centers, digital catalog, Zoro/MonotaRO, High-Touch service, acquisitions, SBC, and debt | Tariffs, freight, digital competition, enterprise share, inventory, customer service, and rates | Sales and customer growth continue while fulfillment cost, digital margin, high-touch share, or diluted residual deteriorates |

## Current evidence anchors

- Applied FY2025 sales were a record `$4.6B`; Engineered Solutions was about
  `40%` of sales. Fiscal Q3 2026 organic sales grew `6.0%` and Engineered
  Solutions `9.3%`.
- MSC FY2025 net sales were `$3.7695B`, gross margin `40.8%`, operating margin
  `8.0%`, with `30,790` vending machines and `426` In-Plant locations.
- Grainger served more than `4.6M` customers in 2025; FY2025 sales were
  `$17.942B`, High-Touch Solutions North America `$13.993B`, and Endless
  Assortment `$3.625B`. Q2 2026 sales were `$5.021B`, up `10.3%`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate volume, price, mix, acquisitions, engineered service, vending,
   high-touch, digital assortment, and emergency demand before accepting
   distribution margin as durable.
2. Reconcile inventory turns, supplier terms, rebates, freight, customer
   concentration, and service labor to operating cash.
3. Treat technical labor, project execution, fulfillment, catalog/data,
   vending, In-Plant, and branch density as reinvestment burdens.
4. Test acquisition cohorts through purchase price, integration, working
   capital, margin realization, debt, SBC, and dilution.
5. Keep customer count, vending footprint, branches, and sales separate from
   collected common-owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what engineered mix, customer share,
inventory turns, service margin, digital growth, reinvestment, and cost of
capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether
manufacturing, facility, and contractor liquidity, rates, tariffs, freight,
and supplier credit preserve cash through an industrial slowdown.

## Promotion boundary

`industrial-procurement-qualified; embedded-service-and-working-capital-normalization-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-period collection, inventory and supplier settlement,
service/project cost, maintenance/growth investment, acquisition return, debt,
SBC replacement, and diluted common-owner residual. Sales, customer counts,
vending, branches, engineered mix, and reported OCF remain diagnostic inputs.
