# Industrial uptime longitudinal cash bridge — WESCO and Fastenal — 2026-09-17

## Purpose

This bridge compares FY2025 with H1 2026 using each company's own filing
taxonomy. It tests whether the H1 cash screen looks like a recurring operating
feature or a period-specific working-capital/timing result.

Amounts are USD millions.

## WESCO

| Period | Revenue | Operating income | OCF | Productive-asset spend | OCF less spend | OCF / revenue | OCF less spend / revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FY2025 | 23,510.9 | 1,233.0 | 125.0 | 99.8 | 25.2 | 0.53% | 0.11% |
| H1 2026 | 12,745.2 | 675.7 | 275.1 | 51.6 | 223.5 | 2.16% | 1.75% |

WESCO's H1 2026 cash conversion is materially stronger than FY2025. That is
not automatically a durable improvement. The H1 filing also shows a `$314.8M`
operating working-capital use in the simple balance bridge, while the cash-flow
discussion identifies large changes in other current/noncurrent assets and
trade receivables. The longitudinal interpretation is therefore:

`H1 cash improvement -> investigate timing, receivables, other assets, project mix, and acquisition effects`

The H1 result should not be annualized into normalized owner cash until the
next comparable quarter shows whether the cash conversion persists.

## Fastenal

| Period | Revenue | Operating income | OCF | Property/equipment spend | OCF less spend | OCF / revenue | OCF less spend / revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FY2025 | 8,200.5 | 1,655.7 | 1,295.9 | 245.3 | 1,050.6 | 15.80% | 12.81% |
| H1 2026 | 4,588.6 | 949.4 | 644.1 | 123.0 | 521.1 | 14.04% | 11.36% |

Fastenal's H1 2026 cash conversion is directionally closer to its FY2025
baseline than WESCO's. That supports a stronger recurring-cash hypothesis, but
does not close the owner-cash gate: H1 receivables increased `$312.1M`, the
operating working-capital bridge used approximately `$216.3M`, and service,
lease, tax, maintenance, dilution, and customer-mix burdens remain unallocated.

## QoE interpretation

The contrast is useful because it prevents a pooled industrial conclusion:

- WESCO's H1 improvement is a **conversion-change diagnostic**. The key test
  is persistence after working-capital and project timing normalize.
- Fastenal's H1 result is a **continuity diagnostic**. The key test is whether
  embedded-service economics and inventory availability sustain cash conversion
  after reinvestment and owner claims.

Neither result is a fraud finding. The relevant financial-shenanigans prompts
are timing, receivable quality, current-asset movements, capitalization,
service burden, supplier terms, and dilution.

## Promotion decision

WESCO and Fastenal remain `reported-cash-conversion-screen` rather than
`normalized-owner-cash`. A promotion requires at least one more comparable
period plus a matched lease/tax/service/maintenance and diluted-owner claim
bundle.

The screen is now strong enough to inform the Damodaran low/base/high cases,
but not strong enough to establish a cross-company ranking.
