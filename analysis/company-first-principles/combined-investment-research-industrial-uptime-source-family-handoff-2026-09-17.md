## The resulting chain

`social force -> industry adjacency -> private-company discovery -> public
control point -> reported operating denominator -> QoE/cash reconciliation ->
Damodaran price expectation -> Lyn Alden durability stress -> thesis breaker`

The industrial cohort currently reaches the valuation and macro layers through
qualified expectation screens. It does not yet reach normalized owner cash,
certificate-grade collateral, project-level collections, or a cross-company
ranking.

## Boundary rules

- Social and Inc. 5000 inputs are directional discovery evidence.
- IBIS organizes the industry but does not verify company economics.
- Management backlog, OEC, facility capacity, and adjusted EBITDA are not
  owner cash.
- Sloan-/Schilit-/Beneish-style diagnostics are reconciliation prompts, not
  manipulation findings; no pooled score is assembled without comparable
  entity, period, taxonomy, accrual, and cash/claim inputs.
- Damodaran and Lyn Alden layers describe assumptions and stress paths; they do
  not upgrade a missing receipt, collection, or legal-availability document.
