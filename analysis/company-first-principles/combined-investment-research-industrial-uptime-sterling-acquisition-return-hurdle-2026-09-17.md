# Sterling acquisition return hurdle — 2026-09-17

## Purpose

Sterling's mission-critical backlog cannot be evaluated separately from the
capital used to acquire CEC and other capacity. This memo creates a cash,
stock, earn-out, contribution, and reinvestment boundary before any acquisition
return is called attractive.

## Filed acquisition perimeter

| Item | Amount | Proof meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| CEC purchase price | `$562M` | Total announced purchase-price denominator |
| Cash consideration | approximately `$443M` | Immediate cash capital at close |
| Stock consideration | approximately `$79.5M` | Dilution and non-cash purchase claim |
| Maximum operating-income earn-out | up to `$80M` | Conditional future claim; not current cash paid |
| Identifiable intangible assets | approximately `$227.8M` | Future amortization and return hurdle |
| Goodwill recorded in purchase allocation | approximately `$308.9M` | Residual acquisition claim; impairment is lagging evidence |
| 2025 E-Infrastructure contribution from CEC, net of `$3.0M` intangible amortization | approximately `$19.4M` | Partial post-close operating contribution, not standalone free cash flow |

The cash, stock, and earn-out amounts are not interchangeable. The earn-out
must not be added to current cash consideration unless paid, and stock
consideration remains an owner claim through dilution even though it is not a
cash outflow at close.

## Cash-burden context

Sterling's FY2025 cash-flow bridge records `$440.0M` of operating cash flow,
`$77.3M` of company capex, `$482.3M` of cash acquisitions net of cash acquired,
and `$2.0M` of disposal proceeds, leaving a residual of approximately
`($117.6M)` after those listed uses. This is not proof that CEC was a poor
acquisition. It proves that the growth program was not self-funding from
current-period OCF after capex and acquisitions.

The bridge also records `$74.2M` of repurchases, `$40.1M` paid to
noncontrolling-interest owners, `$21.0M` of withholding taxes on equity awards,
`$24.9M` of debt repayment, and `$79.5M` of stock consideration. The valuation
must therefore carry both cash claims and dilution claims.

## Damodaran-style annual cash hurdle

The following are analytical hurdle cases, not observed returns:

| Consideration base | 10% annual cash hurdle | 15% annual cash hurdle | 20% annual cash hurdle |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cash consideration `$443M` | `$44.3M` | `$66.5M` | `$88.6M` |
| Cash plus stock `$522.5M` | `$52.3M` | `$78.4M` | `$104.5M` |
| Cash plus stock plus maximum earn-out `$602.5M`* | `$60.3M` | `$90.4M` | `$120.5M` |

`* The `$602.5M` analytical base is `$443M` cash + `$79.5M` stock + `$80M`
maximum earn-out. It is not a statement that all earn-out consideration was
paid or that the earn-out is currently owed.`

The approximately `$19.4M` disclosed 2025 contribution, net of intangible
amortization, is below even the 10% hurdle on the cash consideration alone. That
comparison is intentionally incomplete: contribution is not standalone cash,
2025 was a partial post-close period, synergies may arrive later, and the
acquired work carries working capital, labor, equipment, taxes, integration,
and customer-collection burdens.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

The acquisition-return test must check:

- acquired revenue versus same-store or organic revenue;
- acquired gross margin versus group margin and original purchase estimates;
- intangible amortization and adjusted-EBITDA add-backs;
- earn-out accrual and payment timing;
- stock consideration, SBC, repurchases, and diluted shares;
- acquired receivables, contract assets, retainage, and cash collections;
- project-estimate revisions, claims, rework, and warranty costs; and
- goodwill impairment as a lagging, not leading, return signal.

These are QoE and financial-shenanigans diagnostics, not allegations. A
promotion requires a post-close cohort that joins revenue, margin, cash,
working capital, integration costs, earn-outs, and dilution.

## Decision

Sterling remains:

`acquisition-enabled growth; return hurdle quantified; post-close owner-cash return unproven`

The backlog and acquisition cannot receive full Damodaran terminal-growth
credit until incremental after-tax cash exceeds the relevant hurdle after
working capital, capex, claims, earn-outs, dilution, and funding costs.

## Next evidence object

The highest-value document is a post-close CEC cohort roll-forward showing
revenue, gross profit, cash collection, contract assets/liabilities, capex,
integration cost, earn-out status, and diluted-share impact by quarter.
