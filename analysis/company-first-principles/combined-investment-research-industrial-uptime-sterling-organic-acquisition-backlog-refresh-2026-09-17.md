# Sterling organic/acquisition backlog refresh — 2026-09-17

## Public-source upgrade

Sterling's official Q2 2026 earnings presentation and filed earnings release
provide a useful control against treating all backlog growth as organic. At
June 30, 2026:

| Observation | Amount / result | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Contractual signed backlog | `$4.33B` | Executed contracts, RPOs, and MSAs |
| Combined backlog | `$5.6B` | Adds unsigned awards, including CEC LOI awards |
| Future-phase opportunities | `$1.4B+` | High-probability active-site opportunities outside backlog |
| Backlog growth year over year | `+116%` | Reported continuing-operations comparison |
| Backlog growth excluding CEC and Stone Ridge | `+50%` | Stronger organic/acquired control, still not cash conversion |
| CEC and Stone Ridge contribution to signed backlog | `$1.32B` | Acquired signed backlog |
| CEC and Stone Ridge contribution to unsigned awards | `$1.24B` | Acquired unsigned backlog |

The acquisition contribution therefore accounts for `$2.56B` of the combined
signed-plus-unsigned visibility stack, consistent with the existing separation
memo. The new presentation also warns that RHB deconsolidation affects
historical backlog comparability.

## What this changes

This is a real improvement to the operating model: the public record now
supports a cleaner organic-versus-acquired demand split. It strengthens the
contracted/base valuation case by allowing unsigned awards and acquired work
to be carried separately.

It does not prove project-level billings or customer collections, margin or
cost-to-complete by backlog cohort, CEC or Stone Ridge post-close cash return,
the timing or collectability of retainage and contract assets, or normalized
owner cash.

The appropriate interpretation is `organic-demand-control-upgraded`, not
`project-cash-conversion-proven`.

## QoE / thesis-breaker implication

The next filing should test whether the roughly 50% ex-acquisition backlog
growth is accompanied by comparable organic revenue, margin, working-capital,
and collection performance. If backlog growth remains acquisition-led while
contract assets, retainage, or receivables rise without cash, TB-IUP-STRL-01 and
TB-IUP-STRL-03 remain active.
