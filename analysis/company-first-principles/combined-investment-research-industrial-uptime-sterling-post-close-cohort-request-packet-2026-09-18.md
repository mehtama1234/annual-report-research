## Q2 filing evidence now available

Sterling's June 30, 2026 Form 10-Q identifies Stone Ridge Contracting as
acquired on June 1, 2026 and CEC Facilities Group as acquired on September 1,
2025. The purchase-consideration table reports:

| Cohort | Cash | Equity | Earn-out consideration | Total fair value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Stone Ridge | `$139.985M` | `$27.406M` | `$9.700M` | `$178.007M` |
| CEC | `$442.937M` | `$79.458M` | `$39.194M` | `$561.589M` |

The filing also identifies acquired receivables, contract assets, contract
liabilities, lease obligations, customer-relationship intangibles, trade
names, and goodwill. Stone Ridge's revenue and net income from June 1 through
June 30, 2026 had no material impact on reported results. These are stronger
legal-entity and acquisition-perimeter controls, not cohort cash proof: the
filing still does not provide post-close collections, integration cost, or
cohort-level return.

## Promotion test

Promotion requires the same entity and period to reconcile backlog or acquired
revenue to billings, contract assets/retainage, collected cash, integration and
earn-out costs, capex, taxes, funding cost, and diluted common-owner claims.
Unsigned awards, future phases, percentage-of-completion revenue, and adjusted
EBITDA remain expectation inputs until that join exists.

## Current boundary

Sterling's Q2 filing supports `$328.021M` H1 operating cash flow, `$69.646M`
capital expenditures, `$139.985M` acquisition cash, `$7.767M` earn-out payments,
and a `$110.623M` residual after those visible uses. It does not provide a
complete post-close CEC/Stone Ridge collection ledger or cohort-level return.
