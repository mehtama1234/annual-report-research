# Sterling project-to-cash bridge — pass 1 — 2026-09-17

## What the Q2 filing adds

Sterling's June 30, 2026 Form 10-Q provides a stronger project-accounting
bridge than the headline backlog disclosures, but not a project-level
collection ledger. Amounts below are USD millions.

| Field | June 30, 2026 | December 31, 2025 | Movement / observation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Accounts receivable | 770.671 | 501.163 | +269.508; collection timing remains unresolved |
| Contract assets | 156.295 | 101.154 | +55.141; higher unbilled revenue and retainage |
| Contract liabilities | 802.601 | 652.357 | +150.244; billing/work-progression timing |
| Contract-asset retainage | 72.8 | 40.7 | +32.1; approximately 60% expected within twelve months |
| Contract-liability retainage | 197.1 | 147.6 | +49.5; remains tied to contract-by-contract completion |
| Accounts payable | 316.019 | 226.810 | +89.209; supplier funding effect not separately allocated |

The filing states that revenue recognized from opening contract liabilities was
$488.4M in H1 2026, versus $281.0M in H1 2025. Contract liabilities therefore
provide evidence of customer billing and work progression, but they are not
equivalent to collected cash. Contract assets and retainage remain claims on
future billing or collection rather than owner cash.

## Cash and acquisition perimeter

H1 2026 operating cash flow was $328.021M and capital expenditures were
$69.646M, producing a mechanical cash-after-company-capex screen of $258.375M.
The same period also reports $139.985M of acquisitions net of cash acquired,
$7.767M of earn-out payments, and $27.406M of non-cash share consideration.
The mechanical screen therefore cannot be called acquisition-adjusted owner
cash without deciding which acquisition and earn-out burdens are recurring.

The Q2 purchase-accounting table identifies:

- Stone Ridge total consideration of $178.007M: $139.985M cash, $27.406M
  equity, $9.700M earn-out, and $0.916M estimated working-capital adjustment;
- CEC total consideration of $561.589M: $442.937M cash, $79.458M equity, and
  $39.194M earn-out; and
- acquired CEC working-capital items of $73.549M accounts receivable, $40.632M
  contract assets, $53.555M contract liabilities, and $45.460M accounts payable.

These are purchase-accounting perimeter observations, not proof of post-close
CEC cash return. The filing says both purchase-price allocations remain
preliminary, and CEC/Stone Ridge results are included in E-Infrastructure.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans interpretation

The filing reports $2.9M of unapproved change orders and claims included in
project price at both June 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025. It also reports net
revenue increases from changes in prior-period contract estimates of $138.9M
for H1 2026, versus $72.5M for H1 2025. These are not manipulation findings,
but they are high-value estimate-quality prompts:

- reconcile estimate changes to project margin, contract assets, retainage,
  collections, and final settlement;
- distinguish approved change orders from unapproved claims;
- test whether receivables and contract assets convert to cash on the stated
  timetable; and
- separate acquired working capital and revenue from organic project cohorts.

## Promotion decision

This pass upgrades Sterling from `backlog-quality-separated` to
`project-accounting-cash-bridge-pass-1`, not to normalized owner cash. The
remaining decisive object is a project or acquisition-cohort roll-forward
joining signed backlog, recognized revenue, gross margin, billings, contract
assets/liabilities, retainage, receivables, collections, and post-close CEC
cash. Without that join, backlog and contract liabilities remain leading or
accounting indicators rather than realized owner cash.
