Derived surfaces:

- OCF less gross rental-equipment purchases: `$585M`;
- OCF less rental-equipment purchases plus rental-equipment sale proceeds:
  `$1.265B`;
- OCF less rental and non-rental equipment/intangible purchases: `$420M`; and
- rental-equipment net book value of `$17.350B` versus OEC of `$23.8B`, or
  approximately `72.9%` net-book-value/OEC.

These are diagnostic bridges, not free cash flow or owner cash. Resale
proceeds depend on fleet age, utilization, used-equipment prices, and the
company's replacement/growth decisions. Gross capex cannot be treated as
entirely growth or entirely maintenance.

## Operating conversion and QoE

The operating cash bridge includes a `$272M` receivables use, `$54M` inventory
use, `$623M` accounts-payable source, and `$114M` accrued-liability source.
That pattern supports cash generation in the period but makes liquidity and
supplier-term persistence part of the test. OCF also includes `$1.615B` of
depreciation and amortization, a `$314M` gain on rental-equipment sales, and
`$79M` of stock compensation.

The Q2 operating release reports fleet productivity up `3.4%`. Productivity
is a useful operating control point, but it does not by itself establish
utilization, rental rate, maintenance burden, replacement capex, or lifecycle
return by asset class.

## Financing and availability perimeter

Public Q2 disclosures report `$2.802B` of ABL borrowing capacity net of
letters of credit and `$85M` of AR-securitization capacity, totaling `$2.887B`.
That reconciles to `$2.999B` of total liquidity less `$112M` of cash. The
facility bridge remains a public availability proxy. It is not a populated
certificate and does not disclose eligible collateral, NOLV, reserves, or
live Combined Availability components.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans interpretation

The relevant diagnostic prompts are:

- capitalization: replacement versus growth fleet purchases;
- disposal gains: resale proceeds and gains versus recurring rental economics;
- working capital: receivables and supplier terms;
- maintenance: repairs, delivery, labor, and fleet-service burden;
- financing: debt, interest, liquidity, covenant headroom, and lease claims;
- owner claims: repurchases, tax withholdings, dividends, and contingent
  consideration; and
- collateral: OEC, NBV, eligible equipment, NOLV, reserves, and legal
  availability.

None of these prompts is a manipulation finding. A pooled forensic score is not
assembled because the required comparable accounting and cash perimeter is not
available.

## Promotion decision

This pass upgrades URI to
`fleet-lifecycle-proxy-pass-1; certificate-hold`. It does not promote the
`$2.887B` facility proxy to legal availability, the `$585M` screen to owner
cash, or the resale loop to a lifecycle return.

The decisive next object remains a populated borrowing-base certificate or
equivalent lender collateral report joined to utilization, replacement-capex,
fleet funding, and used-equipment recovery.
