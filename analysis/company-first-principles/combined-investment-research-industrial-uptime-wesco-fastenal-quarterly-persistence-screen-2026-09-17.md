# WESCO/Fastenal quarterly persistence screen — 2026-09-17

This is a new-period persistence test for the industrial uptime cohort. It
updates the FY2025-to-H1 2026 bridge; it does not replace the owner-cash
burden bundle or promote either company to an investment conclusion.

## Control conclusion

The quarter adds a live persistence test to the WESCO/Fastenal burden bundle:
WESCO's strong demand signal is accompanied by a quarter-level cash-conversion
drop, while Fastenal's growth and capital returns are accompanied by lower OCF
conversion and working-capital use. These are Sloan-/Schilit-style diagnostic
prompts involving accruals, timing, capitalization, and owner claims. They are
not evidence of fraud or manipulation. No pooled Beneish score is appropriate
because the legal-entity, period, denominator, and cash-burden inputs are not
yet assembled as a comparable panel.
