# Institutional consulting and managed-services valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Huron Consulting Group from institutional-adaptation
evidence into a company-specific valuation and liquidity object. It separates
professional labor, reimbursable revenue, managed-services execution, digital
investment, client collection, and acquisition economics from enterprise
software, talent advisory, and public-safety workflow platforms.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Huron Consulting Group | Institutional workflow and managed-services cash after professional labor, utilization, client collection, capitalized software, acquisitions, SBC, repurchases, debt, and dilution | Skilled professionals, compensation, contractors, delivery/support, software and data hosting, client implementation, working capital, acquisitions, and common capital | Hospital/education budget stress, unbilled-service aging, payroll timing, wage inflation, utilization decline, acquisition integration, debt, or client concentration | RBR and adjusted EBITDA grow while utilization, collections, renewal, operating cash, acquisition return, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 RBR was about `$1.66B` and adjusted EBITDA about `$237.5M`; Q2 2026 RBR was `$465.6M`, up `15.7%`, with `10.8%` organic growth.
- Q2 2026 Healthcare RBR was `$232.3M`, Education `$139.4M`, and Commercial `$94.0M`; consulting and managed services grew faster than Digital.
- First-half 2026 adjusted EBITDA was `$123.2M`, but operating cash flow was negative `$41.7M`; receivables, unbilled services, tax balances, and an `$88.7M` reduction in accrued payroll absorbed cash.
- First-half 2026 SBC was `$28.7M`, capitalized software `$10.1M`, acquisitions `$27.8M` cash plus `$8.7M` deferred consideration, and repurchases `$206.7M` plus employee tax-withholding shares.
- FY2025 OCF was `$193.4M`, PP&E purchases `$10.4M`, net acquisition cash `$111.6M`, and SBC `$46.5M`; the initial residual after PP&E, acquisitions, and SBC was about `$24.8M`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Use RBR as the services denominator, not as profit or cash; keep reimbursable expenses separate from economic value.
2. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA to GAAP profit and OCF, including fair-value changes, transaction costs, restructuring, amortization, SBC, and capitalized software.
3. Join revenue to professionals, utilization, billing rate, compensation, contractors, service-level obligations, client outcomes, and retention.
4. Explain unbilled services, receivable aging, tax balances, and payroll timing before treating margin expansion as cash conversion.
5. Separate organic RBR from RelateCare, Eclipse Insights, AXIOM, Treliant, WP&C, and other acquisition contributions; test acquired cash return after integration.
6. Treat repurchases as a residual claim, especially when operating cash is negative and borrowings fund capital allocation.
7. Value recurring managed services, digital/implementation work, and project consulting separately; a common multiple can hide different renewal and labor economics.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what organic RBR growth, utilization,
revenue per professional, renewal, segment margin, software reinvestment,
acquisition return, working-capital intensity, and cost of capital the valuation
requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether hospitals, universities,
and commercial clients can fund transformation while wage, collection, and
interest pressure reduce Huron's liquidity.

## Promotion boundary

`institutional-consulting-managed-services-qualified; collection-and-labor-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from RBR to professional
capacity, utilization, client billing and collection, managed-services renewal,
software and acquisition reinvestment, debt, SBC, repurchases, and diluted
common residual. RBR, adjusted EBITDA, GAAP earnings, OCF, and buybacks remain
diagnostic inputs.
