# Institutional research and advisory valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Gartner into a company-specific institutional research,
advisory, conference, and consulting valuation/liquidity object. It keeps
decision-support subscriptions separate from enterprise workflow software,
talent advisory, insurance brokerage, and advertising measurement. The object
tests Insights renewal and wallet retention, conference and consulting mix,
deferred revenue/commissions, analyst and consultant labor, collection, debt,
SBC, repurchases, and diluted common residual.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment/denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gartner | Insights contract and institutional decision-support cash plus separately modeled conference and consulting cash after renewal, wallet retention, event delivery, consultant utilization, research/analyst labor, deferred commissions, collection, debt, SBC, and diluted common residual | Insights contract value, retention, wallet share, conferences/attendance/exhibitors, consulting utilization, analyst and consultant labor, deferred revenue/commissions, technology, debt, SBC, and buybacks | Enterprise/government budget cuts, AI substitution, conference/travel weakness, consulting utilization, deferred-revenue timing, receivable/commission funding, rates, and debt | Contract value, retention, adjusted EBITDA, conferences, consulting, OCF, FCF, or repurchases rise while Insights renewal, wallet, cash normalization, labor economics, SBC, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was approximately `$6.5B`, OCF `$1.290B`, property/equipment/
  leasehold additions `$115.1M`, and recurring-capital residual `$1.175B`.
- FY2025 contract value was `$5.2B`, up only `1%` FX-neutral; Insights client
  retention was `85%`. Contract value is forward visibility, not recognized
  revenue or collected cash.
- FY2025 product mix was approximately Insights `$5.1B`, Conferences `$644.7M`,
  and Consulting `$552.5M`. These products carry different renewal, event,
  labor, utilization, and margin burdens.
- FY2025 OCF benefited from working-capital movements; FY2024 also included a
  `$300M` insurance settlement. Fees receivable supplied `$38.0M` in 2025,
  deferred commissions `$25.3M`, while deferred revenue used `$41.9M`.
- FY2025 SBC was `$155.9M`; Gartner repurchased 7.0M shares for about `$2.0B`.
  Cash per diluted share must include equity awards, exercises, repurchases,
  and debt.
- Gartner sold Digital Markets for approximately `$110M` in February 2026;
  continuing-operation comparisons must not confuse portfolio cleanup with
  organic growth.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Bridge contract value to Insights revenue, billings, deferred revenue,
   retention, wallet expansion, collection, and cash.
2. Separate Insights, Conferences, and Consulting revenue, margin, labor,
   utilization, event execution, and renewal.
3. Normalize insurance proceeds, receivable and commission timing, deferred
   revenue, divestiture effects, SBC, and adjusted metrics.
4. Test whether AI tools change research pricing, renewal, client wallet, or
   analyst productivity rather than assuming either benefit or disruption.
5. Keep repurchases, debt, dividends, conferences, consulting labor, and SBC
   separate from recurring Insights owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what Insights retention, contract
value, wallet share, price, conference attendance, consulting utilization,
labor cost, cash conversion, debt cost, and cost of capital the valuation
requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether corporate and government
budgets, AI substitution, rates, travel, analyst labor, and client liquidity
preserve decision-support demand through a downturn.

## Promotion boundary

`institutional-research-advisory-qualified; renewal-mix-and-cash-normalization-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from contract value and
client cohorts to renewal, wallet retention, billings, collection, deferred
revenue/commissions, conference delivery, consulting utilization, labor,
divestiture perimeter, debt, SBC, diluted shares, and common-owner residual.
Contract value, retention, adjusted EBITDA, OCF, FCF, dividends, and buybacks
remain diagnostic inputs.
