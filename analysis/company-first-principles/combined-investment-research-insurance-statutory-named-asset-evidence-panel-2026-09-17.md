## Apollo/Athene

The preserved Athene route is the strongest because it crosses more than one
accounting surface. It includes approximately `$158.619B` of parsed Schedule D
bond base, `$12.733B` statutory net investment income, `$12.282B` cash-flow net
investment income, `$13.601B` collected gross investment income, `$54.035B`
bond sale/maturity/repayment proceeds, and `$12.676B` mortgage-loan
sale/maturity/repayment proceeds.

That proves a legal-entity asset-income/proceeds bridge. It does not prove that
a named borrower paid Athene, what liability cost funded the asset, what cash
went through a trustee or remittance waterfall, or what residual reached Apollo
common owners. Concord, AMAPS, AP Aristotle, Eliant, AA Infrastructure, and
MF1 remain controlled-document targets, not completed returns.

## Blackstone and Brookfield

Blackstone's insurance SMA route proves that insurer-client capital enters a
credit channel, but it does not establish that Blackstone owns the liabilities
or that any named statutory asset is held by a legal entity in the current
packet. Brookfield's insurance/wealth route is strategically relevant, but a
private-credit allocation or invested-asset figure is not a Schedule D/BA
holding schedule and does not prove liability-adjusted return.

The method therefore keeps both platforms as candidate extensions rather than
forcing them into the Apollo/Accordia proof grade.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans overlay

The lane's diagnostic tests are:

1. statutory income versus cash-flow and collected income;
2. named holding book value versus fair value, proceeds, gain/loss, and
   impairment;
3. liability cost, credited rates, surrender behavior, reinsurance, and
   funds-held balances versus asset yield;
4. NAIC designation, rating migration, non-accrual, allowance, and capital
   treatment; and
5. affiliated/related-party asset flows and the actual remittance path.

A high income number or low loss allowance is not enough to claim a durable
spread. These are prompts for reconciliation, not findings of misconduct.
