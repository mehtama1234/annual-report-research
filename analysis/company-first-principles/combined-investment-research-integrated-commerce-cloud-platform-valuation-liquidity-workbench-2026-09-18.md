# Integrated commerce and cloud platform valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Amazon from broad growth evidence into a company-specific
valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps online stores, third-party seller
services, advertising, subscriptions, fulfillment, and AWS connected but
separately measured, while testing shared infrastructure allocation, capex,
leases, inventory, payables, labor, legal claims, debt, SBC, and diluted
common-owner cash.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon integrated commerce and cloud platform | Consolidated retained cash after commerce gross-margin/fulfillment, seller and advertising contribution, AWS utilization, technology and data-center capex, leases, inventory/payables, labor, legal claims, debt, SBC, and dilution | AWS servers/power/networking, fulfillment capacity/automation, lease-funded assets, inventory, seller/customer receivables, technology/R&D, maintenance, acquisitions, and SBC replacement | AWS capex return failure, consumer slowdown, seller attrition, payables reversal, labor/fuel inflation, lease burden, regulatory/legal cost, cloud price pressure, or debt/refinancing stress | Revenue, AWS operating income, OCF, FCF, GMV, advertising, or backlog grow while full-capital return, utilization, working capital, lease burden, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 net sales were `$716.924B`, operating income `$79.975B`, OCF
  `$139.514B`, net cash property/equipment spending `$128.320B`, and
  acquisitions/other investments `$3.841B`; the broader listed-use residual
  was only about `$7.353B` before debt, leases, and other claims.
- FY2025 AWS revenue was `$128.725B` and AWS operating income `$45.606B`, while
  North America operating income was `$29.619B`. Shared infrastructure costs
  prevent treating segment profit as fully separable owner cash.
- FY2025 cash property/equipment purchases were `$131.819B` before `$3.499B` of
  proceeds/incentives; property and equipment rose to `$357.025B`.
- Operating-lease cash paid was `$15.038B`; present-value lease liabilities were
  `$101.538B`; finance-lease principal repayments were `$1.557B`.
- Accounts payable supplied `$11.231B` of FY2025 OCF, while inventory used
  `$3.002B` and receivables/other used `$7.333B`. Holiday timing makes the
  payable contribution a required reversal test.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate online stores, seller services, advertising, subscriptions,
   fulfillment, and AWS by retained economics; gross merchandise or traffic is
   activity, not owner cash.
2. Reconcile OCF and company-defined FCF to full property spending,
   acquisitions, finance-lease principal, operating leases, assets acquired but
   not paid, debt, and maintenance/capacity capital.
3. Test AWS utilization, server/power/network renewal, depreciation, pricing,
   customer concentration, and shared-cost allocation; do not assume AWS owns
   every dollar of consolidated infrastructure return.
4. Reverse seasonal accounts-payable support and reconcile inventory,
   receivables, seller settlements, labor, freight, and fulfillment lease cash.
5. Keep SBC, repurchases, regulatory/legal claims, debt, leases, and diluted
   shares in the common-owner denominator.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test separates retail contribution, seller
services, advertising take rate, subscription retention, AWS utilization and
margin, fulfillment reinvestment, shared infrastructure, and cost of capital.
The valuation must cover the full system capital bill, not just AWS or segment
operating income.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether consumers, sellers, advertisers,
and enterprises continue funding the system through higher rates, labor/fuel
inflation, cloud price competition, payables reversal, power constraints, lease
obligations, regulation, and weaker capital markets. Liquidity passes only when
legal-entity cash and senior claims remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`integrated-commerce-cloud-qualified; full-capital-return-and-allocation-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from segment revenue and
contribution to customer/seller collection, AWS utilization, fulfillment
renewal, inventory/payables settlement, lease cash, maintenance/growth capex,
legal claims, debt, SBC, and diluted common residual. Revenue, AWS profit, OCF,
FCF, GMV, advertising revenue, subscriptions, and repurchases remain diagnostic
inputs.
