# Integrated device ecosystem valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench adds Apple as a distinct integrated-device, software, and
Services ecosystem lane. It separates premium hardware replacement, platform
attachment, R&D, supply-chain commitments, regulatory exposure, and capital
returns from enterprise workflow software, merchant commerce, transaction
marketplaces, and electronic interconnect content.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apple | Integrated device-and-Services cash after product replacement, Services take rate, platform/payment/content costs, R&D, silicon, supply chain, warranty, regulation, tariffs, debt, SBC, and diluted common residual | Hardware design and manufacturing, silicon, software, AI/cloud, R&D, support/security, inventory and purchase commitments, capex, legal/regulatory remediation, and common capital | Consumer affordability/replacement, China, tariffs, component supply, Services regulation, platform trust, AI investment, channel inventory, or buyback/underinvestment tension | Revenue, Services growth/margin, installed base, adjusted earnings, OCF, and buybacks rise while replacement, take rate after platform costs, R&D productivity, regulatory cost, or per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 net sales were about `$416.2B`; total gross margin was approximately `$195.2B` or `46.9%`; Services gross margin was `75.4%`; and R&D was `$34.6B`.
- FY2025 repurchases were approximately `$89.3B` and dividends `$15.4B`; Q2 FY2026 included a new `$100B` repurchase authorization and a `4%` dividend increase.
- The active installed base exceeded `2.5B` devices. FY2026 Q3 revenue was about `$109.4B` and net income `$29.8B`, with double-digit growth described across iPhone, Mac, Services, and geographies.
- Greater China declined `4%` in FY2025 while other geographies had different growth and margin profiles; regional economics cannot be pooled.
- Q3 tariff refunds supported margin, so that policy benefit must be removed before treating the quarter's gross margin as a normalized baseline.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Split hardware revenue and margin from Services subscriptions, payments,
   advertising, licensing, cloud, and App Store distribution economics.
2. Reconcile Services growth to paid accounts, usage, take rate, third-party
   shares, processing, content, cloud, fraud, refunds, support, and regulation.
3. Treat R&D, silicon, AI, security, supply-chain capacity, and support as
   ecosystem-defense reinvestment rather than discretionary add-backs.
4. Normalize tariff refunds, product mix, FX, channel inventory, supplier terms,
   deferred revenue, and bundled-device allocation before extrapolating margin.
5. Test `$89.3B` of repurchases against diluted shares, SBC, debt, product/legal
   investment, overseas cash, and conservative per-share owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what device retention and replacement,
Services attachment, take rate after platform costs, R&D productivity, China
economics, tariff normalization, regulatory cost, and cost of capital the
equity value requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether household
affordability, rates, currencies, supply chains, geopolitical restrictions,
component shortages, and platform regulation can weaken hardware and Services
cash together.

## Promotion boundary

`integrated-device-ecosystem-qualified; services-regulation-and-reinvestment-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from device shipments and
installed-base cohorts to replacement, Services collection and take rate,
platform costs, R&D/supply-chain reinvestment, regulatory cash, SBC, and
diluted common residual. Installed base, Services growth/margin, revenue,
adjusted earnings, OCF, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs rather than
normalized owner cash.
