# Integrated midstream hydrocarbon-logistics valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves ONEOK from integrated-network and throughput evidence into
a company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps gathering,
processing, NGL fractionation, storage, transport, refined products, crude,
affiliate economics, acquisition cohorts, maintenance/growth capex, debt,
commodity exposure, and diluted common-owner cash separate from upstream
production, merchant power, and industrial infrastructure models.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ONEOK integrated midstream logistics | Collected segment and affiliate cash after maintenance capex, working capital, commodity/optimization exposure, acquisition integration, interest, debt, dividends, and diluted common claims | Gathering and processing plants, NGL fractionation/storage/export, pipelines, refined-product/crude assets, integrity and maintenance, growth projects, working capital, acquisitions, and environmental obligations | Producer-volume decline, spread compression, export disruption, project delay/underutilization, affiliate distribution mismatch, high rates, refinancing, or leverage | Adjusted EBITDA, throughput, and dividends rise while organic cash, maintenance-capex coverage, affiliate distributions, acquired-asset returns, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 operating cash flow was `$5.599B` against `$3.152B` of capex and `$25M`
  of net acquisition payments. The pre-acquisition cash-after-capex screen was
  `$2.447B`, or about `$2.422B` after acquisition payments.
- FY2025 adjusted EBITDA was `$8.020B`, including `$516M` from unconsolidated
  affiliates and a `$386M` equity-earnings deduction. EBITDA is therefore not a
  substitute for cash controlled by ONEOK.
- Year-end total debt was `$33.965B` and cash was only `$78M`; after dividends
  of `$2.583B` and repurchases of `$75M`, the residual was approximately negative
  `$236M` before debt and other financing effects.
- The EnLink and Medallion acquisitions materially changed the comparison base;
  2025 EBITDA included about `$295M` from those acquired assets while the
  original acquisition price and financing burden remain part of the return test.
- 2026 capex guidance was approximately `$2.7B` to `$3.2B`; Q2 2026 adjusted
  EBITDA was `$2.121B` and guidance was raised to `$8.2B`–`$8.5B`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate organic volume, rate, spread, optimization, and acquired EBITDA.
   Record throughput is not proof of durable, high-return owner cash.
2. Split maintenance from growth capex by segment and project. Test utilization,
   contracted volume, completion timing, cost overruns, and return above the
   incremental financing hurdle.
3. Reconcile affiliate equity earnings to actual distributions and classify the
   cash correctly. Reported earnings and controlled cash can diverge in timing.
4. Keep gathering/processing percent-of-proceeds exposure, NGL spreads,
   refined-product and crude marketing, basis differentials, hedges, inventory,
   receivables, and working capital visible rather than calling all revenue tolls.
5. Treat debt, interest, dividends, acquisition consideration, environmental
   obligations, and diluted shares as common-owner claims. Adjusted EBITDA and
   distributable-cash labels remain diagnostic until the full bridge is closed.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what organic volume, utilization,
segment margin, maintenance-capex burden, growth-project return, acquisition
synergy, leverage path, affiliate cash conversion, terminal growth, and cost of
capital the valuation requires. The model should value a normalized midstream
cash stream, not capitalize one cycle's adjusted EBITDA.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether producer supply, industrial demand,
NGL and refined-product flows, export routes, rates, and debt markets support
utilization through a downturn. Liquidity passes only when project commitments,
integrity spending, affiliate funding, debt maturities, working capital, and
common residual remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`integrated-midstream-qualified; organic-return-and-leverage-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from organic and acquired
volume to collected segment cash, maintenance/growth capex, working capital,
affiliate distributions, project completion and utilization, interest, debt,
environmental claims, and diluted common residual. Throughput, adjusted EBITDA,
OCF, FCF, dividends, and repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
