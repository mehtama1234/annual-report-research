# Ionis antisense commercial platform valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Ionis from current-period biotechnology evidence into a
company-specific valuation, franchise, partner-economics, clinical-risk, launch,
and common-owner cash test. It does not pool collaborative milestones, royalties,
owned-product sales, and pipeline optionality into one generic biotech multiple.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ionis | Collected commercial, royalty, and collaboration cash after launch costs, patient finding, medical affairs, clinical trials, regulatory work, manufacturing, partner economics, taxes, debt, and dilution | Owned launches, antisense platform research, trials, label expansion, manufacturing, commercialization, patient support, and partner-funded versus self-funded programs | Clinical setback, launch uptake, reimbursement, milestone timing, partner termination, trial cost, manufacturing, cash burn, or dilution | Revenue grows through milestones and early launches while recurring product economics, trial returns, royalty durability, cash burn, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Q2 2026 total revenue was about `$268M`; commercial revenue about `$119M`; collaborative agreement revenue about `$133M`; TRYNGOLZA net sales about `$5M`.
- Q1 2026 total revenue was about `$246M`; commercial revenue about `$108M`; collaborative agreement revenue about `$120M`; TRYNGOLZA net sales about `$27M`.
- Q4 2025 total revenue was about `$203M`; full-year revenue about `$944M`; full-year commercial revenue about `$436M`; TRYNGOLZA generated about `$108M` in first-year net product sales.
- Management said Ionis remained on track for 2026 guidance after the negative CARDIO-TTRansform readout for eplontersen in ATTR-CM, illustrating the difference between a platform and any one asset.
- Ionis is targeting cash-flow breakeven around `2028`, so partner-funded revenue, owned-product launches, pipeline spending, and dilution must be joined rather than treated as interchangeable cash sources.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile commercial revenue, product sales, royalties, milestones, upfronts,
   collaboration revenue, patient support, rebates, returns, and cash collections.
2. Separate TRYNGOLZA and other wholly owned launches from partner-funded programs,
   royalty streams, and pipeline assets by recurring economics and control.
3. Keep clinical trials, patient finding, medical affairs, regulatory work,
   manufacturing, launch costs, and required platform research in the reinvestment
   denominator.
4. Test whether milestone or upfront revenue is repeatable, whether partner terms
   transfer economics away from Ionis, and whether a failed indication changes the
   value of the remaining franchise.
5. Reconcile adjusted operating loss, R&D capitalization or classification,
   collaboration receivables, inventory, debt, SBC, buybacks, and diluted shares
   before treating revenue or a breakeven target as owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what launch uptake, price, patient finding,
royalty rate, milestone cadence, trial success, reinvestment rate, cash burn, and
cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether
specialty reimbursement, diagnosis, adherence, regulation, partner incentives,
manufacturing, and capital markets can support liquidity across clinical and launch
cycles.

## Promotion boundary

`ionis-antisense-commercial-platform-qualified; franchise-partner-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from owned products, royalties,
and collaborations to cash collections, trial and launch reinvestment, regulatory
claims, debt, partner obligations, and diluted common residual. Revenue, milestones,
royalties, guidance, pipeline catalysts, adjusted EPS, and buybacks remain
diagnostic inputs.
