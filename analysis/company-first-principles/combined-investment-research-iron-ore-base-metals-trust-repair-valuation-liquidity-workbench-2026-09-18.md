# Iron ore, base metals, and trust-repair valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Vale from annual-report evidence into a company-specific valuation, corridor economics, base-metals growth, legacy-claims, liquidity, and common-owner residual test. It keeps Vale's Brazilian iron-ore mine/rail/port system, copper and nickel growth, Samarco/Brumadinho obligations, and expanded net debt separate from gold producers and physical commodity marketers.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vale | Mid-cycle iron-ore corridor and Base Metals cash after all-in sustaining capital, product/freight/currency, partner and streaming claims, remediation, Samarco/Brumadinho, debt, dividends, and dilution | Mine/processing/rail/port renewal, pellets, copper/nickel projects, tailings and dam work, water/community obligations, JV funding, and working capital | Iron-ore/China shock, freight/currency, base-metals impairment, dam/remediation escalation, Samarco cash, partner claims, Brazil policy, debt, or payout ahead of renewal capital | Production and recurring FCF rise while all-in cost, legacy cash burden, base-metals return, expanded net debt, or diluted common value deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$38.403B`, OCF `$8.801B`, PP&E/intangible investment `$6.006B`, management recurring FCF `$4.762B`, dividends/interest on capital paid `$3.561B`, and expanded net debt `$15.579B`.
- Iron-ore production reached `336M` tonnes; Vale Base Metals adjusted EBITDA was `$3.355B` versus `$1.453B` in 2024. Volume and segment EBITDA require price, grade, cost, capital, partner, and claims normalization.
- Sustaining investment was approximately `$4.371B`; iron-ore all-in cost including sustaining investment was about `$63.8` per tonne versus C1 cash cost of `$21.3`.
- Vale paid `$874M` for Brumadinho and `$2.298B` related to Samarco in 2025, while carrying year-end provisions of `$1.911B` and `$2.613B` respectively. These are long-lived economic claims even when excluded from a recurring FCF label.
- FY2025 included a `$4.599B` impairment/disposal charge and `$579M` of net derivative-settlement cash. Neither supports a simple adjusted-EBITDA owner-cash conclusion.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Rebuild recurring FCF after working capital, derivatives, taxes, interest, sustaining/growth capex, associates/JVs, streaming, Brumadinho, Samarco, and dam cash.
2. Separate iron-ore price, volume, grade, product mix, premiums, freight, currency, C1 cost, and all-in sustaining cost.
3. Test copper/nickel projects through production, unit cost, ownership, streaming, partner claims, capital, schedule, and impairment.
4. Roll provisions, payments, dam-decharacterization, remediation, tailings, water, and community commitments; “exceptional” labels do not eliminate recurring trust-repair burden.
5. Reconcile controlled subsidiaries, associates/JVs, NCI, streaming, dividends received, debt, expanded net debt, dividends, and diluted shares to common cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style test values the iron-ore corridor, pellets, copper, nickel, and project options separately using mid-cycle prices, all-in cost, reserve renewal, project capital, taxes, ownership, legacy claims, and debt. The Lyn Alden-style stress test focuses on China/industrial demand, freight, energy, Brazilian currency, resource nationalism, trade, and institutional trust.

## Promotion boundary

`iron-ore-base-metals-trust-repair-qualified; all-in-cost-legacy-claims-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from product price/volume to all-in cost, sustaining capital, project return, partner/streaming claims, Brumadinho/Samarco/dam cash, expanded net debt, dividends, and diluted common residual. Production, recurring FCF, adjusted EBITDA, derivative settlements, impairment, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
