# Kohl’s department-store turnaround valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Kohl’s from current-period evidence into a company-specific valuation object. It tests department-store traffic, comparable sales, inventory and markdowns, Sephora, loyalty and credit, omnichannel fulfillment, leases, working capital, debt, and diluted common residual. It does not pool Kohl’s with warehouse clubs, grocery, specialty retail, or stronger omnichannel ecosystems.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kohl’s | Stabilizing department-store cash after merchandise margin, inventory cleanup, Sephora, loyalty, credit, stores, omnichannel fulfillment, leases, taxes, debt, and dilution | Store maintenance, inventory, markdowns, technology, Sephora buildout, fulfillment, leases, working capital, debt, and shares | Traffic and comparable-sales decline, promotional intensity, inventory aging, credit losses, lease burden, vendor terms, or refinancing | EPS and operating cash flow improve while sales, traffic, inventory quality, Sephora productivity, liquidity, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Fiscal 2025 net sales declined `4.0%` to `$14.8B`, comparable sales declined `3.1%`, diluted EPS was `$2.38`, and adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.62`.
- Q1 2026 net sales declined `1.7%` to `$3.0B`, comparable sales declined `1.1%`, inventory declined `8%`, and revolver borrowings were `$0`.
- Q4 2025 inventory declined `7%`, operating income reached `$212M`, and full-year operating cash flow increased to about `$1.4B`.
- The model is a pressured omnichannel department-store base with `1,153` stores, value positioning, loyalty, credit, omnichannel tools, and Sephora as the principal repair levers.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate traffic, transactions, average ticket, price, promotion, comparable sales, Sephora, credit, and online fulfillment.
2. Reconcile inventory reduction to sell-through, markdowns, vendor terms, margin, shrink, and future assortment quality.
3. Test whether operating-cash improvement reflects durable merchandise economics or temporary working-capital release and expense cuts.
4. Reconcile adjusted EPS and cash to leases, store reinvestment, Sephora, credit losses, debt, vendor financing, dividends, and dilution.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The expectation test asks what traffic, comparable sales, merchandise margin, Sephora productivity, inventory turns, store reinvestment, leverage, and cost of capital are embedded in the valuation. The stress test asks whether value-oriented consumers, private-label and brand mix, leases, rates, credit, promotions, and omnichannel competition allow stabilization to become recurring owner cash.

## Promotion boundary

`kohls-qualified; department-store-stabilization-and-inventory-quality-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from customer demand and merchandise to collection, inventory and markdowns, Sephora return, store maintenance, leases, credit, debt, claims, and diluted common residual. Sales, comparable sales, EPS, OCF, FCF, guidance, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
