# Las Vegas Sands integrated-resort valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Las Vegas Sands from current-period evidence into a company-specific valuation object. It tests Marina Bay Sands, Macao gaming, lodging, conventions, luxury retail, hold, tourism, concessions, expansion capex, debt, cash, buybacks, and diluted common residual. It does not pool Sands with digital wagering, U.S. regional casinos, hotel franchisors, or hotel REITs.

## Current evidence anchors

- 2025 total net revenue was `$13.017B`, operating income `$2.818B`, and net income attributable to Las Vegas Sands about `$1.63B`.
- Q2 2026 revenue was `$3.15B`, operating income `$618M`, net income `$373M`, and adjusted property EBITDA `$1.12B`; unusually low rolling-play hold reduced reported results.
- Q2 unrestricted cash was `$3.38B`, total debt `$15.11B`, revolving availability `$4.26B`, and delayed-draw availability `$4.68B` for Marina Bay Sands expansion.
- Q2 buybacks were `$787M`; Marina Bay Sands and Macao are the core engines, with gaming, lodging, conventions, retail, and entertainment reinforcing one another.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate gaming volume, hold, rolling and mass-market mix, hotel, convention, retail, food, and entertainment economics.
2. Reconcile property EBITDA and free cash flow to expansion capex, maintenance, concessions, debt, taxes, and buybacks.
3. Test Macao and Singapore license, concession, tourism, and regulatory risk rather than treating property scarcity as permanent.
4. Keep delayed-draw expansion funding and capital returns separate from recurring owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The expectation test asks what gaming volume, hold, tourism, hotel and convention demand, retail contribution, expansion return, leverage, and cost of capital are embedded in the valuation. The stress test asks whether Asia travel, regulation, concession stability, rates, geopolitical risk, and construction costs preserve cash after the capital burden of iconic resorts.

## Promotion boundary

`las-vegas-sands-qualified; macao-singapore-hold-and-capex-return-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from property activity to collection, hold, concession rights, hotel and convention demand, capex return, debt service, claims, funding, and diluted common residual. Revenue, property EBITDA, gaming volume, guidance, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
