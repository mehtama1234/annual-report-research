# Live events, ticketing, and sponsorship valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Live Nation from current-period entertainment evidence into a company-specific valuation, event-settlement, deferred-revenue, venue, legal, and owner-cash test. It treats Live Nation as a concerts-led live-experiences platform with ticketing and sponsorship layers, not as a generic media or advertising company.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Live Nation | Collected live-event cash after artist and promoter settlements, venue operations, ticketing delivery, sponsorship obligations, event-related deferred revenue, legal costs, venue investment, debt, and dilution | Concert production and artist guarantees, venue acquisitions and improvements, Ticketmaster technology, event working capital, sponsorship delivery, legal/compliance costs, and international expansion | Attendance and event cancellation, artist economics, ticketing regulation or antitrust, venue capacity, legal accruals, deferred-revenue conversion, promoter settlement, debt, and dilution | Revenue, attendance, ticket volume, deferred revenue, AOI, or sponsorship growth rises while event settlement, collection, venue economics, legal cash, delivery cost, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$25.2B`, operating income was `$1.3B`, AOI was `$2.4B`, and event-related deferred revenue ended at `$4.0B`.
- FY2025 mix was Concerts `$20.9B` or `83%` of revenue, Ticketing `$3.1B` or `12%`, and Sponsorship & Advertising `$1.3B` or `5%`.
- Q2 `2026` revenue was `$7.7B`, operating income `$522M`, and AOI `$817M`; nearly `49 million` fans attended shows and Ticketmaster sold about `90 million` fee-bearing tickets.
- Q1 `2026` included a `$450M` legal accrual; event-related deferred revenue reached `$6.6B`, ticketing deferred revenue `$368M`, and more than `85%` of large-venue shows for the year were already booked.
- FY2025 fan attendance was `159 million`; Ticketmaster concert fee-bearing GTV was `$26B`, and Sponsorship AOI was `$845M`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile ticket volume, GTV, attendance, sponsorship, and deferred revenue to actual event settlement, promoter economics, artist guarantees, venue costs, and cash collection.
2. Keep Concerts, Ticketing, and Sponsorship & Advertising separate; platform throughput is not automatically Live Nation common-owner cash.
3. Test deferred revenue through event completion, cancellation/refund exposure, ticketing delivery, venue obligations, and working-capital timing.
4. Keep the `$450M` legal accrual, antitrust exposure, artist economics, regulatory remedies, and recurring compliance burden in the cash-paid persistence test.
5. Reconcile venue investment, acquisitions, technology spending, debt, taxes, stock compensation, and diluted shares before promoting AOI or buybacks.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what attendance, ticket yield, fee-bearing GTV, sponsorship conversion, venue capacity, deferred-revenue turnover, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether consumers continue prioritizing scarce live experiences while regulation, artist bargaining power, venue bottlenecks, legal costs, recession, event cancellations, and funding needs compress collected cash.

## Promotion boundary

`live-nation-live-events-qualified; settlement-deferred-revenue-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from events and ticketing to fan payment, artist/promoter settlement, venue and delivery costs, cancellation/refund obligations, deferred-revenue release, legal cash, debt, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, attendance, tickets, GTV, AOI, deferred revenue, sponsorship growth, and legal accruals remain diagnostic inputs.
