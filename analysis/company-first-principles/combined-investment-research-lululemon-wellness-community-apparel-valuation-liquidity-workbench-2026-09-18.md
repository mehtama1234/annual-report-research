# Wellness-community performance apparel valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves lululemon from current-period apparel evidence into a company-specific valuation, brand-community, product, inventory, geographic, and owner-cash test. It treats lululemon as a premium performance-and-wellness identity system, not as a generic apparel retailer.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lululemon | Collected performance-apparel cash after full-price sell-through, product innovation, community/marketing, store and digital productivity, inventory, international expansion, supply chain, tariffs, returns, leases, debt, and dilution | Fabric/product design, stores, digital, community events, marketing, inventory, distribution, international infrastructure, technology, and working capital | Americas demand, product relevance, markdowns, international execution, FX, tariff/sourcing, inventory, store productivity, community engagement, and dilution | International growth and EPS rise while Americas decline, full-price sell-through, inventory/markdowns, product innovation, store/digital productivity, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue increased `5%` to `$11.1B`, diluted EPS was `$13.26`, Americas net revenue decreased `1%`, and international net revenue increased `22%`.
- Q1 2026 revenue increased `4%` to `$2.5B`; comparable sales increased `1%`; Americas net revenue decreased `3%` and comparable sales decreased `5%`; international net revenue increased `22%` and comparable sales increased `13%`.
- Q1 gross margin decreased `410` basis points to `54.2%` and operating margin decreased `730` basis points to `11.2%`.
- Q4 2025 revenue increased `1%` to `$3.6B`; full-year revenue increased `5%`; Americas revenue decreased `4%` while international revenue increased `17%`; full-year gross margin decreased `260` basis points to `56.6%`.
- Q3 2025 showed the same split: revenue up `7%`, Americas comparable sales down `5%`, international comparable sales up `18%`, and gross margin down `290` basis points.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile revenue to units, full-price sell-through, average selling price, store/digital channel, returns, markdowns, inventory aging, and customer repeat behavior; brand identity is not cash conversion.
2. Separate Americas repair work from international growth, new-store productivity, local marketing, FX, and differing product/category mixes.
3. Test community, wellness, and product innovation through repeat purchase, customer acquisition cost, retention, product returns, launch productivity, and contribution after marketing.
4. Keep inventory, markdowns, sourcing, freight, tariff, foreign-exchange, store opening, and distribution investment separate from normalized gross margin.
5. Reconcile digital, store, wholesale/partner, membership/community, technology, leases, and working capital before promoting EPS or free cash flow.
6. Treat buybacks, adjusted EPS, gross margin, and international growth as residual claims requiring product reinvestment, leases, debt, and diluted-share validation.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what full-price sell-through, repeat rate, product innovation, store productivity, digital conversion, international growth, gross margin, inventory turns, brand/community investment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether wellness and performance apparel spending survives household, employment, currency, tariff, and trend shocks while stores, inventory, product development, and community/marketing commitments remain.

## Promotion boundary

`lululemon-wellness-apparel-qualified; americas-international-sellthrough-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from product/community demand to full-price sell-through, collection, repeat behavior, inventory/markdowns, store/digital productivity, sourcing/tariffs/FX, leases, debt, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, comps, EPS, gross margin, international growth, community claims, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
