# Luxury home and destination retail valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves RH from annual-report evidence into a company-specific valuation, reinvestment, liquidity, and diluted common-owner residual test. It keeps luxury home identity, galleries, design services, and hospitality separate from household-value retail, branded staples, and digital bulky-goods marketplaces.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RH | Luxury home product and design-relationship cash after inventory, freight/tariffs, galleries, delivery, labor, hospitality, leases, debt, stock compensation, and diluted common claims | Product depth, galleries, design technology, delivery/service, hospitality and destination projects, maintenance capital, and working capital | Housing and mortgage cycle, affluent-consumer liquidity, inventory aging/markdowns, gallery utilization, hospitality losses, supplier commitments, debt, leases, or dilution | Brand and order growth continue while order-to-revenue conversion, mature-gallery return, inventory quality, hospitality cash, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 operating cash flow was `$452.2M` against `$199.8M` of PP&E purchases; after `$43.9M` of stock compensation, the preliminary residual was about `$208.5M` before inventory, lease, hospitality, and maintenance normalization.
- The comparable OCF-less-PP&E residuals were approximately negative `$67.1M` in FY2023, negative `$213.7M` in FY2024, and positive `$252.4M` in FY2025. One improved year is not steady-state owner cash.
- Q1 FY2026 revenue was `$800.3M`, gross profit `$331.3M`, operating income `$34.2M`, and net loss about `$13.7M`, showing current demand and fixed-cost sensitivity despite the brand narrative.
- RH's model includes products, galleries, design services, restaurants, wine bars, hospitality, and destination properties. These surfaces have different margins, capital needs, labor, occupancy, licensing, and collection mechanics.
- Demand or orders placed precede recognized revenue and can be cancelled, delayed, returned, discounted, or fulfilled at lower margin. Inventory turns, aging, markdowns, freight, tariffs, and delivery cost must be joined to cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile orders placed to recognized revenue, cancellations, delivery, returns, gross margin, and cash collection; demand is a leading indicator, not cash.
2. Separate mature-gallery productivity from new-gallery, hospitality, and destination-project investment; charge maintenance capital and recurring pre-opening costs.
3. Split core product retail, design services, restaurants, and hospitality by revenue, margin, labor, occupancy, capital, and cash.
4. Test gross-margin changes for freight, tariffs, markdowns, damage, returns, product mix, and large-ticket project timing.
5. Reconcile adjusted earnings to GAAP and owner cash, including restructuring, pre-opening, amortization, impairment, SBC, leases, debt, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what qualified customers, project frequency, average order value, gross margin, gallery productivity, service attachment, reinvestment return, and cost of capital the price requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether affluent-household liquidity, housing turnover, mortgage rates, employment, and wealth effects can support the fixed gallery and hospitality system through a normal cycle.

## Promotion boundary

`luxury-home-destination-retail-qualified; inventory-gallery-hospitality-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from orders and product sales to collection, inventory aging, mature-gallery return, design/hospitality contribution, maintenance versus growth capital, lease/debt claims, and diluted common residual. Demand, revenue, adjusted earnings, OCF, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
