# Combined investment research current-regime macro validation

Research date: `2026-09-17`

The macro layer now has both an official external anchor and company-period
observations. This memo tests whether the proposed transmission mechanisms are
visible in the current reporting period. It is a validation screen, not a
through-cycle causal conclusion.

The historical context is now separated in the [historical macro-regime
validation](combined-investment-research-macro-historical-regime-validation-2026-09-15.md),
which spans official inflation, real-wage, and policy-rate observations from
2022 through the current 2026 regime. That panel improves regime coverage but
does not promote the company tests to causal proof.

## Reading rule

An observed response upgrades a mechanism from purely hypothetical to
current-regime-visible. It does not establish causality when several company,
commodity, accounting, and policy variables moved together. The system should
only promote a mechanism to through-cycle validation after later filings show
the same condition changing demand, margin, funding, liquidity, or valuation
with a reproducible denominator.

## Current status

`current-regime-validation-partial`: all three pilots have an official macro
anchor and at least one company-period observation aligned with the proposed
transmission route. Through-cycle validation and causal attribution remain
open.

## Live macro-source refresh — 2026-09-17

The current official macro packet now includes the September 16 FOMC decision:
the federal-funds target range was raised by 25 basis points to `3.75%–4.00%`,
with the Federal Reserve describing economic activity as expanding at a solid
pace while inflation remains elevated. The August 2026 CPI release reports
`3.4%` year-over-year headline inflation, `2.4%` core inflation, and `16.3%`
energy inflation. BEA's July 2026 personal-income release reports disposable
personal income up `0.5%` month over month, PCE up `0.2%`, and a `3.0%` saving
rate.

These observations sharpen the live macro stress inputs: real affordability,
energy/logistics cost, short-rate financing burden, and household saving
capacity. They do not repair the company-level confounds or create causal
through-cycle evidence. The current status remains
`current-regime-validation-partial`.
