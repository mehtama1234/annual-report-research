# Combined investment research macro/liquidity current anchor

Research date: `2026-09-15`

This memo adds a dated external anchor to the Lyn Alden-style transmission
layer. It is a current-regime observation, not a through-cycle forecast.

## Transmission to the three pilots

### Retail cohort

Energy inflation and food-away-from-home inflation can reduce discretionary
room even while nominal sales grow. Retail evidence should therefore separate
ticket, volume, mix, markdowns, wage/freight cost, vendor terms, and inventory
cash conversion. The current labor reading supports a base case of continued
employment-supported demand, not a permanent affordability floor.

### Wheaton–Antamina

The policy rate raises the discount-rate and refinancing sensitivity of a
long-duration PMPA, while energy and input inflation can affect the mine
operator's production incentives and the stream's delivery timing. The current
macro anchor does not determine silver price, BHP-only deliveries, taxes, or
Wheaton's Antamina debt allocation; those remain separate source gates.

### Apollo–Athene

The policy-rate and liquidity regime transmits through asset marks, cost of
funds, policyholder flows, private-credit redemptions, collateral values, and
the ability to upstream cash. The current snapshot supports a stress mechanism
but does not provide the legal-entity duration, capital, restricted-cash, or
parent-receipt denominators needed for a common-owner cash calculation.

## Boundary

This anchor upgrades the macro layer from a company-only mechanism map to a
dated external-condition map. It does not prove causality, forecast the next
cycle, or replace company-period cash and liability evidence. The through-cycle
test remains open until subsequent releases and filings show how the mapped
conditions changed demand, margins, funding, liquidity, and valuation.
