# Combined Investment Research Macro and Liquidity Transmission Matrix

Research date: `2026-09-16`

This matrix makes the Lyn Alden-style macro layer company-specific. It maps a
macro or liquidity condition to the operating line it can affect, the current
evidence route, and the filing test that could confirm or weaken the mechanism.
It is a transmission map, not a macro forecast.

| Pilot | Macro/liquidity condition | Transmission mechanism | Current evidence route | Next measurable test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retail cohort | Inflation, wage pressure, consumer credit, and household affordability | Changes traffic, trade-down, ticket, mix, private-label demand, vendor terms, fulfillment cost, and bad-debt or card economics | Retail cohort operating matrix, attached-service boundary, and common-period cash matrix | Next filings: traffic/transactions, real wage and price effects, inventory/payables reversal, service contribution, and cash per diluted share |
| Wheaton–Antamina | Silver price, inflation in mine inputs, rates, and refinancing liquidity | Changes stream revenue, operator production incentives, Wheaton debt service, discount rate, and the value of a long-duration delivery contract | Antamina bear/base/bull workbench, BHP contract denominator, delivery and debt-boundary ledgers | Next filings: BHP-only metal credits, realized price, delivery curve, interest/tax allocation, and debt waterfall |
| Apollo–Athene | Rates, credit spreads, policyholder flows, surrender/liquidity pressure, regulatory capital, and compute-financing draw timing | Changes Athene spread, cost of funds, asset marks, withdrawals, capital availability, fee earnings, upstream distributability, and the timing of Broadcom capital-solutions fee recognition | Apollo/Athene common-owner bridge, credit-quality boundary, related-party bridge, upstream-receipt boundary, and Broadcom fee-timing boundary | Next filings/statutory schedules: spread and loss roll-forward, withdrawals, capital/dividend capacity, fee collections, Broadcom draw/fee realization, and parent receipt |

## Interpretation boundary

The matrix connects macro conditions to specific business mechanisms, satisfying
the durability test without promoting macro narratives into company proof. A
future filing must still show the operating, cash, legal-entity, or valuation
effect before the mechanism can be upgraded from qualified to proven.
