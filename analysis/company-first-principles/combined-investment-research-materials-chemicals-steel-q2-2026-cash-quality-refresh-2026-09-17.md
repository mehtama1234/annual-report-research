# Materials, specialty chemicals, and steel Q2 2026 cash-quality refresh

Research date: `2026-09-17`

## First-principles question

Which physical inputs become valuable because they are scarce, embedded in a
workflow, or difficult to replace—and how much cash must be reinvested to keep
that control point operating?

## Current-period observations

| Company / model | H1 2026 observation | What it tests | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ecolab, specialty chemicals/service | Operating cash `$1.1754B`; capex `$588.6M`; receivables used `$197.0M`; inventory used `$131.4M`; SBC `$75.1M`; `$60M` one-time equity-incentive payments tied to the Ovivo acquisition | Embedded service conversion, working-capital funding, acquisition integration, replacement capital | Durable organic owner cash or pricing power independent of service labor and equipment |
| Nucor, steel/low-cost capacity | Operating cash `$2.286B`; capex `$1.232B`; receivables used `$952M`; inventory used `$560M`; payables supplied `$454M`; 2026 capex estimate `$2.50B`; West Virginia sheet mill and NTS expansion are major projects | Steel-cycle cash, working-capital burden, project capacity, raw-material exposure, and capital return | Through-cycle return, maintenance/growth split, or durable margin at current steel prices |

## Interpretation

Ecolab sells a combination of chemistry, compliance, monitoring, equipment, and
specialist service. The customer may be paying to keep a plant, hotel,
restaurant, hospital, or industrial process safe and operating. That embedded
workflow can create retention, but it also means service labor, dispensing
equipment, acquisitions, receivables, and inventory are part of the cash model.

Nucor converts scrap, energy, labor, furnaces, mills, and distribution into
steel capacity. Its H1 cash result improved sharply, but `$1.512B` of working
capital was invested through receivables and inventory before the current cash
result can be called through-cycle. The West Virginia sheet mill and NTS
expansion make project capital and future utilization central to the return
question.

## Nucor Q2 filing-detail refresh — 2026-09-18

Nucor's Q2 results release adds two separate quality controls to the steel cash
bridge. Steel-mills earnings included a `$130M` reduction to cost of products
sold from cash refunds associated with prior-period raw-material procurement
costs. That is a named cash benefit in the current period, not evidence of a
recurring steel spread. Separately, marketing, administrative and other
expenses included a `$61M` non-cash pre-tax benefit from the increased value of
Nucor's Helion investment. The two items must not be combined or treated as the
same kind of support.

At quarter-end Nucor reported `$2.69B` of cash and short-term investments and
an undrawn `$2.25B` revolving credit facility. These are liquidity and funding
capacity observations, not proof that the West Virginia sheet mill or NTS
expansion has earned an acceptable return. The project test still requires
paid capital, start-up/ramp costs, utilization, realized spread, working
capital, environmental burden, and diluted common-owner residual.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

- Do not treat Ecolab adjusted operating income as recurring without separating
  special charges, acquisition effects, service/lease cost, and SBC.
- Reconcile Ecolab receivable and inventory investment to customer growth,
  pricing, collection, and equipment/service commitments.
- Do not treat Nucor's H1 operating cash increase as normalized steel cash while
  steel prices, scrap costs, receivables, inventories, payables, and project
  utilization are changing.
- Separate Nucor's West Virginia and NTS expansion capital from maintenance
  spending and test the return after ramp-up, labor, energy, and environmental
  costs.
- Keep buybacks, debt maturities, affiliates, and investment-sale proceeds
  separate from operating cash.

These are reconciliation controls, not allegations of misconduct.

## Decision

`materials-chemicals-steel-control-points-visible; working-capital-cycle-and-project-return-open`

The filings broaden the materials thesis from raw scarcity to two distinct
control points: embedded operational chemistry/service and flexible steel
capacity. Their cycles and denominators remain incomparable; no normalized
owner-cash ranking is promoted.
