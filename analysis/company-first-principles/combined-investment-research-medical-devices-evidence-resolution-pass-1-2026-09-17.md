# Medical devices and clinical workflow evidence resolution — pass 1

Research date: `2026-09-17`

The [structured resolution register](data/combined-investment-research-medical-devices-evidence-resolution-pass-1-2026-09-17.csv)
populates six of the highest-value rows from the filing-evidence queue. It
records filed observations without implying that a named transaction, revenue
line, or adjusted metric is already owner cash.

## Stryker: acquisition cash is now tied to a named cohort

The FY2025 10-K identifies the Inari Medical acquisition and reports total
consideration of approximately `$4.810B`, net of cash acquired. The broader
FY2025 net acquisition cash line was approximately `$4.960B`. This materially
improves the control-point map: the acquisition burden is not an abstract
adjusted-EPS adjustment; it is concentrated in a named clinical-platform
transaction.

The same filing identifies Guard Medical as a negative-pressure wound-therapy
adjacency to Orthopaedic Instruments. That establishes strategic adjacency,
not return. The required next join is acquired revenue, margin, integration
cash, amortization, debt, impairment, and subsequent-period cash generation.

## Intuitive Surgical: usage-linked revenue is real, but cash collection remains a gate

The FY2025 10-K reports operating-lease revenue of `$874M`, including `$531M`
of variable revenue tied to usage-based arrangements. This is an important
control-point confirmation: part of the installed-system model is linked to
actual use rather than only upfront system placement.

It does not close the owner-cash question. The next bridge must join usage and
procedure metrics to lease receivables, deferred revenue, allowances, credit
losses, and cash collections. Variable recognition can be economically sound
while still creating timing or collection risk.

The filing also creates a deliberate SBC reconciliation gate. The note reports
`$802.8M` of pre-tax share-based compensation expense, the cash-flow bridge
uses `$788.2M`, and `$126.8M` of stock-based compensation expense was
capitalized to the balance sheet. The `$14.6M` difference between the first two
figures is not averaged away, and the capitalized amount is not silently added
or subtracted. The income-statement, cash-flow, capitalized, segment, and
diluted-share scopes must be reconciled before using SBC as a final owner
claim. This is a denominator boundary, not evidence of misconduct.

## Henry Schein: restructuring is explicitly multi-year

The FY2025 10-K states that the 2024 Plan produced `$105M` of restructuring and
related charges in FY2025 and is expected to continue through the end of 2027.
That makes “adjusted EBITDA less restructuring” an unsafe durable-cash proxy
without a remaining-cost schedule and cash-paid bridge.

The same filing reports approximately `$850M` of FY2025 repurchases against
year-end cash of approximately `$156M` and reported debt of approximately
`$3.1B`. Repurchase activity therefore must be placed in the financing and
liquidity waterfall; it cannot be treated as evidence of surplus owner cash.
