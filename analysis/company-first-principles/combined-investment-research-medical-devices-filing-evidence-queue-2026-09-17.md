# Medical devices and clinical workflow filing-evidence queue — 2026-09-17

The [structured evidence queue](data/combined-investment-research-medical-devices-filing-evidence-queue-2026-09-17.csv)
converts the first-pass owner-cash bridge into primary-filing work orders.
Every row names the artifact, period, observable, unresolved question,
promotion rule, and owner-cash consequence. The queue is intentionally open:
an identified filing route is not the same as a completed proof.

## Stryker

The FY2025 10-K is the controlling artifact for the acquisition bridge. The
filing's acquisition-axis disclosures, acquisition cash, inventory step-up,
amortization, and impairment signals must be joined to acquired revenue,
margin, integration cash, debt, and subsequent cash return. The Q1 and Q2 2026
10-Qs then test whether the cyber disruption and recovery altered procedure
volume, remediation cost, inventory, customer retention, or margin.

The promotion decision should not be based on the existence of a named
acquisition or on adjusted EPS. It requires a cohort return table. The
recurring strategy makes recurring integration, quality, and acquisition costs
economically relevant even when management excludes some from adjusted
earnings.

## Intuitive Surgical

The FY2025 10-K provides the installed-base control point: systems, procedures,
instruments/accessories, services, and usage-based lease arrangements. The
next proof is utilization and pull-through per installed system, not merely
system placements. The lease work must reconcile recognized variable revenue
to receivables, deferred revenue, allowances, and collected cash.

Inventory is an operating cash-flow movement already embedded in the reported
OCF bridge. The queue therefore asks for normalization and turns, not a second
deduction. Stock compensation and dilution remain separate owner claims, while
PP&E and manufacturing expansion must be split between maintenance and growth.

## Henry Schein

The FY2025 10-K must connect acquisition-axis and restructuring-plan
disclosures to incremental sales, margin, cash cost, and post-close return.
The working-capital queue is especially important because the residual is thin
relative to receivables and inventory. The debt/repurchase row tests whether
per-share improvement was funded after operating needs or by consuming
liquidity and increasing senior claims.

## Promotion rule

Status: `filing-evidence-queue-open; primary-artifacts-present; no-ranking`.

A company can move beyond this queue only when the relevant rows are populated
with same-entity, same-period evidence and the resulting cash denominator is
carried into the valuation expectation register. Until then, the medical-
devices cohort remains a qualified research lane rather than an investment
ranking.
