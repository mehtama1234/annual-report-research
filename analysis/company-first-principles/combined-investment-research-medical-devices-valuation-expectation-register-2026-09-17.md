## Interpretation

Stryker's snapshot sits below the dossier's `$4.0B` base owner-cash scenario
when compared with the selected multiple, but that does not make it cheap:
the base case requires acquisition cohorts to earn through integration,
amortization, debt, stock compensation, and dilution. The valuation question
is incremental acquisition return, not continued adjusted-EPS growth.

Intuitive is the most expectation-sensitive surface in this cohort. Its
installed base and recurring instruments/services can justify substantial
duration, but the recorded snapshot requires a cash outcome materially above
the conservative screen or a very durable high multiple. Procedure growth,
instrument consumption per system, service economics, new-platform adoption,
inventory normalization, SBC, and manufacturing investment must all cooperate.

Henry Schein's market value is approximately `31.391x` the first-pass residual.
The dossier's simple 25x reference on the same residual would imply only about
`$8.1B`; therefore the current snapshot requires either higher normalized cash
or a long period of mix-led improvement. The burden is especially important
because repurchases and debt can make per-share optics appear stronger while
cash remains tied up in inventory, receivables, software, restructuring, and
acquisitions.
