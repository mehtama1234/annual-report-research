# MGM destination gaming and digital valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves MGM Resorts from current-period evidence into a company-
specific valuation, destination, gaming, digital, Macau, capital-allocation, and
common-owner cash test. It does not pool Las Vegas, regional gaming, Macau, MGM
Digital, BetMGM affiliates, and resort development into a generic casino multiple.

## Current evidence anchors

- Full-year 2025 consolidated net revenue was `$17.5B`, consolidated adjusted EBITDA `$2.4B`, and adjusted EPS `$3.31`.
- FY2025 Las Vegas Strip revenue was `$8.4B`, Regional Operations `$3.8B`, MGM China `$4.5B`, and MGM Digital `$654M`.
- Q2 2026 consolidated revenue was `$4.5B`, net income attributable to MGM `$292M`, adjusted EPS `$0.59`, and adjusted EBITDA `$610M`.
- Q2 Las Vegas Strip revenue was `$2.2B` and up `3%`; Regional Operations same-store revenue was `$904M` and up `3%`; MGM Digital revenue was `$196M` and up `20%`.
- MGM Northfield Park operations were sold for `$546M` in April 2026; MGM Osaka remains targeted for a `2030` opening, creating a distinct development and capital-commitment test.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile rooms, occupancy, ADR, casino hold, gaming volume, food/beverage,
   conventions, entertainment, and customer cash by Las Vegas, regional, Macau,
   and digital perimeter.
2. Keep MGM Digital and unconsolidated BetMGM affiliates separate; accounting
   presentation can obscure who funds, controls, and collects digital economics.
3. Keep resort maintenance, luxury investment, Osaka development, technology,
   loyalty, regulatory compliance, debt, and working capital in the owner-cash base.
4. Test whether property sales and buybacks improve common value or merely move
   assets and claims around a leveraged destination portfolio.
5. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA, casino hold, promotional credits, affiliate income,
   leases, debt, minority claims, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares before treating
   adjusted EPS or capital returns as surplus cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what occupancy, room yield, casino hold,
regional and Macau growth, digital margin, development return, leverage, and cost
of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether
household travel budgets, gaming regulation, China/Macau policy, labor, geopolitics,
property capex, and refinancing can preserve liquidity across a destination cycle.

## Promotion boundary

`mgm-destination-gaming-qualified; segment-digital-development-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from property, gaming, digital,
and affiliate activity to cash collections, customer incentives, required capex,
development spend, debt, claims, regulatory obligations, and diluted common residual.
Revenue, bookings, occupancy, adjusted EBITDA, guidance, property sales, and buybacks
remain diagnostic inputs.
