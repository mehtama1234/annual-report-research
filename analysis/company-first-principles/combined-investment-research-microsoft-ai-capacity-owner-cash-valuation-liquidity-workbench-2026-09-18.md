# Enterprise software, AI capacity, and owner-cash valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Microsoft from annual-report evidence into a company-specific
software-renewal, Azure-utilization, AI-capacity, physical-infrastructure,
investment-gain, and diluted common-owner residual test. It keeps Microsoft's
identity/productivity control point, cloud infrastructure, Copilot adoption, and
OpenAI-related investment exposure separate from lower-capital software and from
other data-center hardware platforms.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft | Software and cloud cash after renewal/seat retention, Azure usage and serving cost, data-center and equipment replacement, power, leases, R&D, SBC, legal claims, investment gains, debt, and diluted shares | Data centers, servers/networking, power and cooling, software/R&D, model-serving capacity, leases, security, support, acquisitions, and working capital | AI-capacity overspend, Azure margin compression, power/chip constraints, enterprise budget cuts, model obsolescence, customer concentration, regulatory cost, investment volatility, debt, or dilution | Revenue, RPO, Azure growth, Copilot seats, OCF, or investment gains rise while serving cost, capacity return, SBC-adjusted cash, RPO conversion, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2026 revenue was `$331.839B`, operating cash flow `$182.935B`, and property additions `$115.948B`, leaving a simple OCF-less-property screen of `$66.987B` before acquisitions, leases, debt, dividends, repurchases, and SBC.
- Microsoft Cloud revenue reached `$214.4B`, Azure and other cloud services grew `41%`, and commercial remaining performance obligation reached `$678B`. These are demand and commitment evidence, not proof of incremental return after capacity and serving costs.
- FY2026 stock compensation was `$12.405B`; repurchases and dividends totaled `$48.716B`. Reconcile diluted shares and repurchase offset rather than treating the buyback as proof of surplus cash.
- More than `30M` paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats and strong Azure demand require retention, usage, inference-cost, support, and incremental-budget tests. Paid seats and RPO do not establish durable owner cash.
- FY2026 included approximately `$4.963B` of reported net-income benefit from investment gains, including OpenAI-related investments. Separate investment value and realization from software and cloud operating cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile renewal, seat expansion, usage, RPO invoicing, deferred revenue,
   receivables, cloud consumption, and actual collection by business model.
2. Separate Azure and AI serving economics from the broader Intelligent Cloud
   segment; track utilization, pricing, power, chips, depreciation, leases,
   support, and model-serving cost.
3. Classify property additions as replacement, expansion, or capacity built ahead
   of demand; test whether depreciation and maintenance cash keep pace with the
   physical asset base.
4. Treat SBC, investment gains, customer prepayments, acquisitions, tax timing,
   and legal/regulatory costs as separate bridges to common-owner cash.
5. Test whether Copilot adoption is incremental, retained, and economically
   productive after inference, support, sales, and customer implementation cost.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what renewal, Azure growth, cloud margin,
Copilot monetization, utilization, capacity-reinvestment rate, SBC replacement,
and cost of capital the equity price requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test
asks whether enterprise budgets, power and chip supply, rates, model competition,
regulation, and a data-center buildout preserve liquidity and return on capital.

## Promotion boundary

`enterprise-software-ai-capacity-qualified; capacity-return-and-diluted-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from contracts and usage to
collection, serving cost, capacity utilization, replacement capital, leases,
SBC, investment gains, debt, and diluted common residual. Revenue, RPO, Azure
growth, Copilot seats, OCF, FCF, and investment gains remain diagnostic inputs.
