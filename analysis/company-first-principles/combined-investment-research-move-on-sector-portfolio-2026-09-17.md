# Move-on sector portfolio — 2026-09-17

## Decision

The blocked receipt and owner-cash gates in the original three pilots should
remain open, but they should not stop the broader research system. The next
sector cycle began with three distinct, filing-rich lanes and now includes a
fourth restaurant-franchise lane:

1. **Healthcare distribution:** McKesson, Cencora, and Cardinal Health.
2. **Semiconductor process control:** KLA as the flagship.
3. **Digital infrastructure real estate:** Equinix, followed by Digital Realty.
4. **Restaurant franchising:** McDonald's, Chipotle, CAVA, Restaurant Brands
   International, Wingstop, and Yum.
5. **Insurance brokers versus carriers:** Marsh McLennan, Arthur J. Gallagher,
   and Chubb.
6. **Energy midstream:** Cheniere Energy and Energy Transfer.
7. **Exchange and information infrastructure:** CME Group and S&P Global.
8. **Materials, specialty chemicals, and steel:** Ecolab, Sherwin-Williams,
   and Nucor.
9. **Integrated oil and gas:** ConocoPhillips and Exxon Mobil.

These sectors are different from the affordability, precious-metals streaming,
institutional-capital, power-grid, and industrial-uptime lanes already in the
system. They preserve the same end-to-end method:

```text
force -> control point -> company selection -> primary filings
      -> operating burden -> QoE / financial-shenanigans tests
      -> owner-cash denominator -> Damodaran valuation
      -> Lyn Alden liquidity stress -> thesis breaker
```

This is a research admission decision, not a ranking or investment
recommendation.

## Common admission protocol

Each lane must produce:

1. A dated force and alternative explanations.
2. An industry map showing customer, supplier, intermediary, asset owner,
   regulator, lender, and end payer.
3. A selection reason and comparable boundary for each company.
4. Annual, quarterly, results-release, and source-ledger routes with period
   and legal entity identified.
5. An operating model separating revenue, gross profit, operating income,
   operating cash, and cash available to the relevant owner.
6. QoE and financial-shenanigans diagnostics covering receivables, inventory,
   payables, supplier finance, capitalization, acquisitions, recurring
   adjustments, SBC/dilution, related parties, and claims.
7. An owner-cash denominator separating maintenance/growth capital, tax,
   leases, financing, acquisitions, dilution, and legal-entity restrictions.
8. A Damodaran-style expectation screen and reinvestment logic.
9. A Lyn Alden-style stress map for rates, inflation, credit, liquidity,
   customer funding, and nominal claims.
10. One filing-based thesis breaker and one exact source object that could
    upgrade the weakest denominator.

Revenue scale, adjusted EBITDA, backlog, AFFO, free cash flow, and a favorable
market multiple cannot substitute for the owner-cash bridge.

## Lane 1 — healthcare distribution

The hypothesis is that drug distribution is a scale-and-workflow control point
with resilient volume but structurally thin economics. The investable question
is whether purchasing scale, specialty services, and working-capital discipline
produce durable cash after litigation, acquisitions, inventory funding, taxes,
capital spending, and dilution.

- **McKesson:** scale and specialty-distribution flagship; test revenue quality,
  working-capital turns, acquisition contribution, and capital return.
- **Cencora:** international and specialty-distribution comparison; test gross-
  profit conversion, inventory/receivable funding, and acquisition burden.
- **Cardinal Health:** diversified distribution and services comparison; test
  segment mix, medical-products working capital, legal claims, and cash access.

Primary routes: [McKesson dossier](../deep-company-pages/mckesson-corporation.md),
[Cencora dossier](../deep-company-pages/cencora-inc.md), and [Cardinal Health dossier](../deep-company-pages/cardinal-health-inc.md).

Promotion test:

`volume / gross profit -> inventory and receivables -> supplier and payer terms -> litigation and acquisition claims -> operating cash -> diluted owner cash`

The first [healthcare-distribution cash and QoE comparison](combined-investment-research-healthcare-distribution-first-pass-2026-09-17.md)
now records the FY2025 mechanical residual screen, company-specific
financial-shenanigans prompts, and the next source object for each company.

## Lane 2 — semiconductor process control

As process complexity and compute investment rise, inspection and metrology may
retain a control point even when wafer-fab equipment spending cycles. KLA is the
flagship because the repository contains its FY2025 annual report, FY2026
quarterly chain, and official IR/SEC routes.

Promotion test:

`wafer-fab and node complexity -> inspection / metrology control point -> equipment and service revenue -> backlog and installed-base conversion -> owner cash after cycle and claims`

The existing [KLA process-control dossier](../deep-company-pages/kla-corporation.md)
already supplies the first filing-backed bridge: FY2026 revenue of `$13.579B`,
service revenue of `$3.126B`, operating cash flow of `$4.143B`, PP&E spending of
approximately `$376M`, `$310M` of stock compensation, and `$2.290B` of
repurchases. It remains a qualified control-point case because cycle,
customer-concentration, R&D, SBC, and peak-price risks are not closed.

The [KLA FY2026 QoE forensic refresh](combined-investment-research-kla-fy2026-qoe-forensic-refresh-2026-09-17.md)
now adds the filing-level cash-quality surface: 19% single-customer revenue,
87% international revenue, 28% receivables growth, 14% inventory growth,
`$515M` of receivables factoring, `$5.97B` of purchase commitments, and the
SBC/buyback relationship. The lane remains qualified and owner-cash-open.

The first QoE tests are backlog-to-revenue conversion, customer concentration,
receivable and inventory timing, capitalization, service-margin persistence,
SBC/dilution, and whether buybacks obscure cyclical cash generation. Do not
expand the comparison cohort until KLA's denominator is assembled.

## Lane 3 — digital infrastructure real estate

AI, cloud, and network demand may increase the value of powered capacity and
interconnection density, but the burden may sit in power procurement,
construction, tenant concentration, financing, and recurring capital.

- **Equinix:** interconnection and network-density flagship; the repository
  contains FY2025 and H1 FY2026 filings and GAAP-to-AFFO inputs.
- **Digital Realty:** scale and powered-capacity comparison, added only after
  the same period and claim perimeter is assembled.

Promotion test:

`cloud / AI / connectivity demand -> powered capacity and interconnection -> lease and service revenue -> occupancy, pricing, capex, power, debt, and dilution -> common-owner cash`

GAAP net income, operating cash, property capex, real-estate acquisitions,
business acquisitions, recurring capex, AFFO, dividends, SBC, and debt
refinancing must remain separate. Company-defined AFFO is an input to reconcile,
not the final owner-cash denominator.

The existing [Equinix dossier](../deep-company-pages/equinix-inc.md) supplies the
first filing-backed bridge: FY2025 operating cash flow of `$3.911B`, recurring
capex of `$284M`, total property/equipment capex of `$4.311B`, real-estate
acquisitions of `$994M`, and AFFO of `$3.761B`. The next test is whether the
network's growth capital, power, debt, joint ventures, and dilution leave
durable common-owner cash.

The [KLA-versus-Equinix comparison](combined-investment-research-semiconductor-digital-infrastructure-comparison-2026-09-17.md)
puts the two flagship lanes on one denominator-controlled review page while
preserving their different capital burdens.

The [Digital Realty Core FFO-to-owner-cash boundary](combined-investment-research-digital-realty-ffo-owner-cash-boundary-2026-09-17.md)
completes the first diagnostic pass for the hyperscale comparison: `$2.412B`
FY2025 OCF sits beside roughly `$4.977B` of development-in-process assets and
the `$3.25B–$3.75B` 2026 development-capex guide. Backlog and Core FFO remain
visibility inputs until commencement, stabilized yield, partner capital, debt,
and per-share cash are joined.

The [Equinix AFFO-to-owner-cash boundary](combined-investment-research-equinix-affo-owner-cash-boundary-2026-09-17.md)
now adds a structured REIT cash bridge and separates the `$3.627B` recurring-
capital screen from the approximately `$(1.645)B` full-investment bridge after
property capex and acquisitions. It preserves the project-level, JV/VIE,
power, lease-up, debt, and dilution gates.

## Lane 4 — restaurant franchising

The hypothesis is that franchisors control a trusted brand and repeat customer
occasion while franchisees fund much of the local real estate, labor,
inventory, and execution burden. The investable question is whether royalties,
fees, property/advertising economics, and company-store cash remain durable
after support, fund restrictions, capex, acquisitions, debt, and dilution.

The [restaurant-franchise first-principles synthesis](annual-report-restaurant-franchise-first-principles-synthesis-pass-1-2026-09-17.md),
[filing evidence panel](combined-investment-research-restaurant-franchise-filing-evidence-panel-2026-09-17.md),
and [owner-cash promotion workbench](combined-investment-research-restaurant-franchise-owner-cash-promotion-workbench-2026-09-17.md)
cover CAVA, Restaurant Brands International, Wingstop, and Yum. The
[current-period refresh](combined-investment-research-restaurant-current-period-refresh-2026-09-17.md)
adds McDonald's and Chipotle H1 2026 with an explicit H1-versus-quarter
verifier and ownership controls.

Promotion test:

`consumer occasion -> brand/franchise control -> franchisee or company-store sales -> royalty/fee/property/advertising flows -> support and fund settlement -> corporate cash after capex, claims, debt, and dilution -> common-owner residual`

Systemwide sales, restaurant count, advertising expense, AUV, and mechanical
OCF-less-PP&E are visibility inputs. They are not normalized owner cash and do
not support a restaurant ranking while royalty collection, advertising-fund,
franchisee-health, and burden allocations remain open.

## Execution order — updated after sector-panel completion

The three sector panels are now built and routed through the reader, evidence
register, QoE controls, valuation handoff, liquidity map, and thesis-breaker
boundary. The next work is therefore source-object-driven:

1. Pursue a joinable CA-06/Q-04–Q-06 denominator or cash object: matched
   maintenance capital, lease/tax payment, recurring working capital, or
   attached-service collection and cost allocation.
2. Pursue KLA's numeric installed-base/service schedule or a comparable
   service-per-system history; otherwise preserve the searched-negative filing
   boundary and move to the next source object.
3. Pursue named digital-infrastructure project capital, commencement,
   stabilized yield, partner funding, and per-share cash evidence.
4. Pursue healthcare acquisition-cohort return, legal/remittance, and
   preferred/NCI allocation evidence for McKesson, Cencora, and Cardinal.
5. Pursue restaurant royalty/advertising-fund collections, franchisee-health,
   and maintenance-versus-growth schedules; keep McDonald's and Chipotle's
   H1 2026 surface separate from the FY2025 cohort.
6. Promote none of these lanes to a ranking until the denominator and
   thesis-breaker tests are complete.
