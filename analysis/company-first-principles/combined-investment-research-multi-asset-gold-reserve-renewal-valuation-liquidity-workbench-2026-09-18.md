# Multi-asset gold and reserve-renewal valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Kinross Gold from annual-report evidence into a company-specific valuation, mine-renewal, jurisdiction, liquidity, and common-owner residual test. It keeps Kinross's multi-asset gold portfolio and development queue separate from mine-only, diversified-major, and physical-marketing models.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kinross Gold | Mid-cycle attributable mine cash after grade/recovery, sustaining capital, reserve replacement, development projects, taxes, reclamation, repatriation, debt, dividends, buybacks, and dilution | Mine replacement, sustaining/development capital, exploration, equipment, reclamation, Great Bear/Curlew/Phase X/Redbird/Lobo-Marte, and working capital | Gold-price normalization, cost inflation, grade/recovery miss, project overrun, host-country/tax/royalty change, repatriation friction, debt, or payout ahead of renewal capital | Production, AISC, FCF, and buybacks rise while reserve renewal, project return, cash location, or diluted common value deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 attributable production was `2.012M` gold-equivalent ounces, OCF `$3.761B`, productive-asset spending `$1.194B`, OCF less productive-asset spending `$2.566B`, attributable FCF `$2.474B`, and capital returned `$752M`.
- The OCF-less-productive-asset screen was approximately `$507M` in 2023, `$1.389B` in 2024, and `$2.566B` in 2025; gold price and cycle conditions must be normalized.
- Proven and probable reserves were `20.9M` ounces across Tasiast, Paracatu, La Coipa, Fort Knox, Manh Choh, Round Mountain, Bald Mountain, and development projects.
- Great Bear, Curlew, Round Mountain Phase X, Bald Mountain Redbird, and Lobo-Marte create a visible renewal queue; each requires separate capital, permits, schedule, ramp, cost, and cash-return evidence.
- Q1 2026 included approximately `$91M` of withholding tax connected to planned repatriation, mainly from Mauritania. Consolidated profit is not automatically parent cash.
- FY2025 included a `$116.1M` Lobo-Marte impairment reversal. The reversal changes accounting profit but does not prove new mine cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Rebuild revenue and cash at mid-cycle gold prices, separating ounces, grade, recovery, fuel, labor, currency, royalties, taxes, and working capital.
2. Reconcile AISC to the filed cash flow and include sustaining capital, non-sustaining capital, exploration, reclamation, closure, and corporate overhead.
3. Compare each mine and project by capital invested, production, cost, reserve life, jurisdiction, taxes, reclamation, and cash return; diversification is not automatically value creation.
4. Track reserves after depletion, price assumptions, grade, recovery, acquisitions, and divestitures; impairment reversals remain accounting evidence until cash confirms them.
5. Test dividends and buybacks after mid-cycle cash, sustaining capital, reclamation, taxes, debt, project funding, cash location, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style test values producing mines, reserves, development projects, and capital returns separately using mid-cycle prices, explicit sustaining capital, probability-weighted project outcomes, and required returns. The Lyn Alden-style stress test focuses on gold monetary demand against energy, labor, equipment, currency, inflation, tax, royalty, permitting, and host-country risk.

## Promotion boundary

`multi-asset-gold-qualified; reserve-renewal-project-return-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from mine production and realized prices to cost, sustaining capital, reserve renewal, project return, reclamation, taxes/repatriation, debt, dividends, buybacks, and diluted common residual. Production, AISC, reserves, FCF, impairment reversals, and capital returns remain diagnostic inputs.
