# National CineMedia physical-attention and cinema advertising valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves National CineMedia from a marketing-services packet into a company-specific valuation object. It separates cinema-screen inventory, exhibitor relationships, attendance, advertiser demand, revenue per attendee, measurement, movie-slate timing, transformation spending, leases, debt, and diluted common residual. It does not treat attendance, advertising revenue, adjusted OIBDA, cost savings, or audience reach as normalized owner cash.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National CineMedia | Collected cinema-attention cash after exhibitor revenue share, screen inventory, advertiser collections, measurement and sales infrastructure, movie-slate cadence, transformation, leases, debt, and dilution | Sales and measurement technology, advertiser service, exhibitor contracts, data, operational transformation, content and campaign execution, working capital, lease obligations, and debt service | Weak movie slate, attendance decline, Olympic or competing-event displacement, advertiser budget cuts, exhibitor concentration, fixed-cost burden, transformation underdelivery, refinancing, or dilution | Revenue per attendee and adjusted OIBDA improve while attendance, advertiser renewal, exhibitor settlement, collection, fixed costs, debt, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was about `$243.2M`, operating loss about `$13.9M`, net loss about `$10.6M`, and adjusted OIBDA about `$39.1M`.
- Q4 2025 revenue was about `$93.2M`, operating income about `$23.8M`, net income about `$29.3M`, and adjusted OIBDA about `$37.2M`; revenue growth of `8%` outpaced attendance.
- Q1 2026 revenue was about `$34.0M`, operating loss about `$26.9M`, net loss about `$28.6M`, and adjusted OIBDA negative `$10.5M`, showing seasonality and slate sensitivity.
- Management launched an operational transformation targeting about `$11.0M` in annualized savings, with approximately `$3.0M` realized and up to `$6.0M` expected during 2026; savings require cash-paid and service-quality validation.
- The company’s monetization thesis depends on premium synchronized theater attention, advertiser demand, exhibitor access, measurement, and release calendars rather than on a recurring household subscription.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile screen inventory, attendance, impressions, revenue per attendee, campaign delivery, advertiser billings, exhibitor revenue share, and collections.
2. Separate movie-slate cadence from durable pricing and measurement improvement; a strong quarter can reflect release timing rather than a stronger franchise.
3. Test exhibitor contracts, concentration, minimum guarantees, makegoods, campaign cancellations, and renewal behavior before accepting audience scale as monetizable cash.
4. Keep transformation spending, AI-enabled efficiency claims, sales restructuring, leases, debt service, and fixed operating costs in the cash bridge.
5. Reconcile adjusted OIBDA, operating cash flow, working capital, debt, preferred or other claims, share count, and any capital returns before accepting per-share value.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what attendance, revenue per attendee, advertiser renewal, exhibitor share, slate quality, reinvestment rate, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether synchronized physical attention remains scarce enough to fund the fixed network through weak release calendars, macro advertising cuts, exhibitor pressure, and refinancing risk.

## Promotion boundary

`national-cinemedia-physical-attention-qualified; slate-exhibitor-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from screen inventory and audience delivery to advertiser collection, exhibitor settlement, renewal, measurement, required transformation and technology spend, leases, debt, claims, and diluted common residual. Attendance, revenue, adjusted OIBDA, audience reach, cost savings, and guidance remain diagnostic inputs.
