# New-sector macro and liquidity transmission register — 2026-09-17

The structured [macro/liquidity CSV](data/combined-investment-research-new-sector-macro-liquidity-register-2026-09-17.csv)
connects a Lyn Alden-style regime question to a company-specific cash or claim
mechanism. It does not claim through-cycle causality; each row requires a
repeated filing observation before promotion.

## Transmission rule

`macro force -> customer or funding behavior -> operating metric -> cash/claim burden -> filing-based breaker`

The register keeps macro context separate from Damodaran valuation and from
company-period proof. A rate or inflation observation is a stress input, not a
company conclusion.

## Current coverage

| Lane | Primary transmission | Main liquidity question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Healthcare distribution | reimbursement, drug-price/mix, customer credit, acquisition funding, and legal claims | Does thin-spread volume become cash after receivables, inventory, claims, debt, and acquisitions? |

| Digital infrastructure real estate | rates, power, construction, lease-up, hyperscaler deployment, and capital-market access | Does powered capacity earn a return after development, debt, JV, leasing, and dilution claims? |

## Boundary

The six rows are `qualified-transmission; repeated-causality-open`. They may
shape scenario ranges and source requests, but they do not activate a thesis
breaker or create a ranking without same-entity, same-period operating and
cash/claim evidence.
