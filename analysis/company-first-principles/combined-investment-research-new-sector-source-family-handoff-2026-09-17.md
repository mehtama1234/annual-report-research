# New-sector source-family handoff — 2026-09-17

## Purpose

This matrix connects the upstream discovery sources to the six new company
lanes without allowing one source family to prove another family's job.

## Source-family roles

| Source family | Contribution to the new lanes | Boundary | Handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Social research | Affordability, aging/health burden, compute complexity, physical infrastructure, power scarcity, and reliability signals | Directional behavior or institutional salience; not company demand, margins, or causality | Name the force, date it, and define the economic question |
| Inc. 5000 discovery | Private-company signals around healthcare workflow, industrial technology, AI infrastructure, specialty services, and distribution | Discovery and customer-pain context; not durable profit or owner cash | Select a public control point with a stated reason |
| IBIS / industry maps | Distribution structure, specialty pharmacy, semiconductor equipment, data-center real estate, power, and adjacency context | Taxonomy and industry mechanism; not exact company-period facts | Identify the payer, control point, burden carrier, and substitutes |
| Annual reports / SEC / official IR | Revenue, segments, gross profit, cash flow, capex, claims, debt, dilution, backlog, lease-up, and legal entities | Reported facts require denominator and cash/claim reconciliation | Build the operating model and primary source ledger |
| QoE / financial shenanigans | Receivables, inventory, payables, adjustments, capitalization, acquisitions, revenue timing, SBC, JV, and claim diagnostics | Warning prompts; not fraud findings or pooled Beneish scores | Identify the exact same-entity reconciliation and breaker |
| Damodaran valuation | Cash, reinvestment, margin, growth, duration, and price-implied expectation scenarios | Assumptions do not prove future performance or owner cash | State what the market price requires |
| Lyn Alden macro/liquidity | Rates, inflation, credit, power, customer funding, refinancing, and nominal-claim stress | Regime consistency is not through-cycle causal proof | Map macro stress to an operating metric and liquidity claim |
| Legal/entity/asset evidence | Acquisition consideration, debt, leases, JV claims, legal settlements, collateral, and owner access | A contract, backlog, AFFO, or facility proxy is not collected owner cash | Close the legal-entity, cash, claim, and return bridge |

## Status and promotion rule

The handoff is `connected; qualified; no-ranking`. Each lane has a source
route, operating model, QoE register, valuation expectation surface,
macro/liquidity mechanism, and thesis-breaker register. Promotion requires the
next proof object in the lane map and cannot be achieved by adding another
theme, market-size statistic, backlog number, or adjusted metric.
