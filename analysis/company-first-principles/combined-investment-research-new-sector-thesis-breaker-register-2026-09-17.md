## Rule

A breaker is an observable filing, legal, or company-period reconciliation
test. It is not a forecast, a ratio-only alarm, or a manipulation finding. A
breaker activates only when the same entity and period provide the relevant
operating, cash, claim, lease, financing, or dilution evidence.

## Interpretation

The healthcare rows focus on pass-through revenue, adjusted earnings, claims,
acquisition returns, and working-capital settlement. KLA's rows focus on
service conversion, customer capex, deferred revenue, R&D, SBC, and buybacks.
The REIT rows focus on AFFO quality, recurring versus growth capital,
lease-up, power, JV/fund obligations, debt, and dilution.

The register deliberately does not use a pooled Beneish score. The companies
have different accounting taxonomies, fiscal calendars, capital structures,
and owner-claim perimeters.
