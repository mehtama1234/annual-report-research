# Local broadcasting, retransmission, and political-cycle valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Nexstar from current-period media evidence into a company-specific valuation, station, retransmission, political-advertising, network, acquisition, and owner-cash test. It treats Nexstar as a hybrid local-broadcast and distribution platform, not as generic national television or pure streaming.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nexstar | Collected retransmission, advertising, local-news, network, and digital media cash after station operations, programming, sports/content rights, TEGNA integration, distribution payments, debt, taxes, and dilution | Station maintenance, local newsrooms, programming and sports rights, NewsNation and CW investment, multicast/digital distribution, TEGNA integration, retransmission negotiations, and working capital | MVPD subscriber attrition, carriage disputes, political-cycle volatility, local advertising, audience decline, network programming, acquisition financing, debt, and dilution | Revenue, distribution revenue, advertising, ratings, audience growth, adjusted EBITDA, political advertising, or buybacks rise while subscriber base, carriage renewal, station cash, programming cost, TEGNA integration, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 net revenue was `$4.949B`, distribution revenue `$2.924B`, advertising revenue `$1.959B`, net income `$83M`, and adjusted EBITDA `$1.561B`.
- Q4 `2025` net revenue was `$1.289B`, distribution `$720M`, advertising `$549M`, net loss `$(170M)`, and adjusted EBITDA `$433M`; political advertising fell to `$21M` from `$233M` prior year.
- Q1 `2026` net revenue was `$1.396B`, distribution `$837M`, advertising `$548M`, net income `$160M`, and adjusted EBITDA `$470M`.
- Q2 `2026` net revenue was `$1.993B`, distribution `$1.116B`, advertising `$862M`, net income `$113M`, and adjusted EBITDA `$633M`, including a larger TEGNA contribution.
- More than `60%` of the subscriber base had distribution agreements renewed around Q4 2025; NewsNation remained a fast-growing ad-supported cable network and The CW audience grew `19%` in Q4.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile subscribers, carriage contracts, retransmission collections, political and non-political advertising, audience, ratings, programming delivery, and cash; distribution revenue is not free cash.
2. Separate local stations, retransmission, political ads, non-political ads, NewsNation, The CW, multicast, digital, and TEGNA cohorts; seasonality and economics differ.
3. Test TEGNA and network investments through purchase price, integration, station overlap, carriage renewal, programming, audience retention, debt, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep adjusted EBITDA, political advertising, audience growth, distribution revenue, one-time costs, and buybacks separate from recurring collected common-owner cash.
5. Reconcile sports and programming rights, local-news labor, station capex, affiliate payments, advertising receivables, debt, taxes, interest, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what retransmission renewal, subscriber retention, local ad yield, political-cycle normalization, network audience, TEGNA synergies, programming investment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether cord-cutting, carriage disputes, political volatility, streaming substitution, rates, acquisition leverage, and local-ad recession preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`nexstar-local-broadcast-qualified; retransmission-political-cycle-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from station, subscriber, advertiser, and network cohorts to carriage collection, advertising collection, programming cost, TEGNA integration, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, distribution, advertising, ratings, audience, adjusted EBITDA, political advertising, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
