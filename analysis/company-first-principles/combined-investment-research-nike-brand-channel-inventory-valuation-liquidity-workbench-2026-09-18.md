# Athletic brand, channel balance, and inventory-repair valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves NIKE from the annual-report archive into a company-specific
brand, wholesale, direct, digital, inventory, marketplace, tariff, and common-
owner residual test. It keeps Nike separate from household staples, off-price
retail, home furnishings, and general store models because the key denominator is
brand-controlled demand versus channel and product-cycle execution.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NIKE | Through-cycle brand cash after wholesale and NIKE Direct sell-through, traffic/conversion, product mix, markdowns, inventory, marketing, athlete/innovation spend, stores/digital, tariffs, working capital, debt, and dilution | Product design and innovation, athlete/brand marketing, digital/member systems, stores, distribution, inventory, marketplace support, logistics, and working capital | Direct-channel contraction, wholesale concentration, inventory aging/markdowns, tariff reversal, China/regional demand, brand heat, retailer credit, or debt/capital-return pressure | Reported margin, direct revenue, digital engagement, or tariff recovery rises while units/sell-through, inventory turns, channel contribution, brand reinvestment, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2026 revenue was approximately `$46.4B`, NIKE Brand revenue `$45.2B`, wholesale revenue `$27.5B`, NIKE Direct revenue `$17.7B`, and gross margin `42.9%`.
- Q4 FY2026 revenue was `$11.0B`, down `1%` reported and `4%` currency-neutral; wholesale grew `4%` reported while NIKE Direct declined `7%` reported and `9%` currency-neutral.
- Q4 gross margin was `49.2%`, including an approximately `900` basis-point tariff-recovery benefit; net income was `$1.1B` and diluted EPS `$0.72`, including approximately `$0.52` of tariff-recovery benefit.
- Q3 and Q2 FY2026 showed wholesale holding up better than NIKE Direct while gross margin was `40.2%` and `40.6%`, respectively. The channel-mix shift is a demand and economics question, not proof of durable direct ownership.
- Nike's customer relationship is strategically valuable but still requires product flow, traffic, conversion, repeat purchase, retailer balance, inventory discipline, and continuous brand investment.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile revenue through units, price/mix, wholesale sell-in, consumer
   sell-through, direct traffic/conversion, returns, promotions, and markdowns.
2. Separate tariff recovery and favorable trade timing from recurring gross
   margin, product cost, freight, channel contribution, and consumer demand.
3. Track finished-goods inventory, purchase commitments, aging, reserves,
   obsolescence, regional mix, China demand, and retailer/customer credit.
4. Test NIKE Direct economics after digital/member systems, stores, fulfillment,
   customer acquisition, service, returns, and brand/athlete investment.
5. Treat direct revenue, digital engagement, adjusted EPS, dividends, and
   repurchases as diagnostic until per-share cash survives inventory and brand
   reinvestment.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what through-cycle volume, price/mix,
channel contribution, brand investment, inventory turns, regional growth, tariff
burden, capital spending, debt, and cost of capital the equity value requires.
The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether household affordability, China and
global demand, trade policy, retailer liquidity, currency, freight, and inventory
cycles preserve cash through a discretionary-consumption downturn.

## Promotion boundary

`nike-brand-channel-qualified; sell-through-inventory-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from consumer demand to
wholesale sell-through, Direct traffic/conversion, product margin, inventory
settlement, markdowns, tariff cash, marketing/innovation capital, debt, taxes,
and diluted common residual. Revenue, NIKE Direct growth, digital activity,
gross margin, tariff recovery, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
