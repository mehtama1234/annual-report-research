# Omnicom integrated advertising and attention valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Omnicom from current-period evidence into a company-specific valuation object. It tests client-budget collection, media and creative workflow, data and identity, AI-enabled measurement, IPG integration, people costs, working capital, debt, restructuring, and diluted common residual. It does not pool Omnicom with publishers, ad-tech platforms, or direct audience owners.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Omnicom | Integrated client-budget and attention-workflow cash after media buying, creative labor, data/identity, technology, IPG integration, client retention, taxes, debt, and dilution | Talent, technology, data and identity platforms, integration, new-business cost, working capital, restructuring, debt, and shares | Client-budget slowdown, agency churn, AI disintermediation, media pass-through timing, IPG integration, restructuring cash, or leverage | Organic growth and adjusted EBITA rise while client retention, fee collection, pass-through economics, integration savings, cash conversion, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- 2025 revenue was about `$17.3B`; full-year adjusted EBITA was about `$2.7B`, while GAAP results absorbed merger, repositioning, disposition, and acquisition-related costs.
- Q2 2026 reported revenue was about `$6.6B`; core-operations revenue was about `$6.0B` with `6.1%` organic growth, adjusted EBITA about `$1.1B`, and core margin `17.8%`.
- The completed IPG merger on `2025-11-26` makes integration, scale, client overlap, talent retention, and cost savings central valuation variables.
- AI, identity, data, connected commerce, social and creator channels, sports, entertainment, and agentic marketing are workflow and measurement claims—not direct owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate core organic growth from IPG consolidation, pass-through media spend, foreign exchange, acquisitions, and dispositions.
2. Reconcile client billings and collection to media pass-through, compensation, talent retention, technology, data, restructuring, and integration cash.
3. Test whether AI improves delivery economics and client retention or merely shifts labor, pricing, and revenue recognition.
4. Reconcile adjusted EBITA and free cash flow to merger costs, working capital, debt, capital returns, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The expectation test asks what client-budget growth, retention, organic margin, IPG synergies, data and AI monetization, reinvestment, and cost of capital are embedded in the valuation. The stress test asks whether fragmented media, weak client budgets, AI disintermediation, talent costs, rates, integration, and pass-through timing leave durable collected cash.

## Promotion boundary

`omnicom-qualified; client-retention-and-ipg-integration-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from client budgets and services to collection, retention, pass-through settlement, talent and technology cost, integration savings, debt, claims, and diluted common residual. Revenue, organic growth, adjusted EBITA, EPS, OCF, FCF, guidance, and merger synergies remain diagnostic inputs.
