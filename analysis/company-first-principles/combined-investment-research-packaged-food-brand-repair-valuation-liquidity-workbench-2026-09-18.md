# Packaged food, brand repair, and household value valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves General Mills from annual-report evidence into a
company-specific packaged-food, pet-food, foodservice, retailer, brand-repair,
and common-owner residual test. It keeps General Mills separate from beverage,
premium-spirits, personal-care, and retailer models even where they share
household staples exposure.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General Mills | Through-cycle branded-food cash after organic volume, price/mix, trade spending, manufacturing, freight, advertising, commodity inputs, working capital, pet/foodservice mix, debt, and dilution | Brand innovation and advertising, plants, product quality, distribution, inventory, retailer support, pet-food capacity, foodservice systems, and restructuring | Household value pressure, retailer destocking, commodity/freight inflation, pet slowdown, brand/share loss, food-safety event, debt/refinancing, or capex/brand underinvestment | Organic sales or adjusted EPS stabilize while volume, household penetration, trade-adjusted margin, inventory, brand investment, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Organic net sales declined `2%` in both FY2025 and FY2026; FY2026 adjusted operating profit and adjusted diluted EPS each declined `16%` in constant currency. Management's FY2027 organic-sales outlook remains `-1.5%` to `+0.5%`.
- North America Retail household penetration stabilized after several years of decline, but stabilization is not the same as renewed pricing power or durable share recovery.
- FY2027 free-cash-flow conversion is targeted at about `95%`; this is a management objective requiring proof after inventory, restructuring, brand support, capital spending, leases, debt, and diluted shares.
- The portfolio spans North America Retail, North America Pet, North America Foodservice, and international businesses. Price/mix, volume, trade spending, commodity inputs, and working capital should not be collapsed into one margin assumption.
- Favorable trade phasing, shipment timing, cost savings, and a 53rd week can lift a reported quarter without proving that household demand or brand economics have fully repaired.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Bridge organic sales through units, price/mix, trade spending, retailer
   inventory, household penetration, market share, and shipment timing.
2. Separate gross margin improvement from commodity, freight, productivity,
   mix, promotions, and brand-support reductions; test whether savings weaken
   future demand or product quality.
3. Track finished goods and raw-material inventory, retailer receivables,
   payables, restructuring, pension, leases, and cash conversion.
4. Value pet food, foodservice, and North America Retail separately by volume,
   margin, customer concentration, capacity, and reinvestment need.
5. Treat dividends and buybacks as residual claims only after brand repair,
   maintenance capital, debt, restructuring, and diluted-share effects.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what normalized volume, price/mix,
trade-adjusted margin, brand investment, working capital, maintenance capital,
debt cost, and cost of capital the equity price requires. The Lyn Alden-style
stress test asks whether household affordability, retailer bargaining power,
commodity and freight inflation, rates, and food-input volatility preserve
liquidity and brand reinvestment.

## Promotion boundary

`packaged-food-brand-repair-qualified; volume-margin-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from consumer demand and
retailer shipment to volume, trade spending, collection, inventory, normalized
margin, brand/plant capital, restructuring, debt, taxes, and diluted common
residual. Organic sales, adjusted EPS, FCF-conversion targets, cost savings,
dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
