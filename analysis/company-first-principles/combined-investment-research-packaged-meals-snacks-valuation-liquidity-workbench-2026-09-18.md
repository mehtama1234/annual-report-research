# Packaged meals and snacks valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves The Campbell's Company from pantry and snack evidence into
a company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps Meals &
Beverages, Snacks, retailer shelf access, price/volume/mix, trade promotion,
ingredients, packaging, freight, inventory, brand investment, Rao's growth,
Sovos integration, debt, dividends, and diluted common-owner cash separate from
beverage, spirits, oral-care, and broad retail models.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Campbell packaged meals and snacks | Collected branded-food cash after consumer takeaway, retailer trade, ingredients, packaging, freight, plants, advertising, innovation, working capital, Sovos integration, debt, and dilution | Manufacturing and maintenance, brand/innovation, advertising, inventory, retailer programs, capacity, acquisitions, integration, and common capital | Household trade-down, private label, snack weakness, retailer pressure, input/tariff inflation, storm disruption, inventory build, integration, debt, or recurring restructuring | Sales, adjusted EBIT/EPS, OCF, dividends, or repurchases rise while in-market volume, share, gross margin, brand return, inventory, Sovos cash, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 net sales were `$10.253B`, adjusted EBIT `$1.487B`, adjusted EPS
  `$2.97`, and operating cash flow `$1.131B`; organic sales declined `1%`.
- Q3 FY2026 sales were `$2.366B`, down `4%`; adjusted EBIT fell `24%` to
  `$274M`, adjusted EPS was `$0.50`, and year-to-date operating cash flow was
  `$839M`.
- Management guided FY2026 organic sales down `2%` to `1%`, adjusted EBIT down
  `20%` to `17%`, and adjusted EPS to `$2.15`–`$2.25`. This is a portfolio
  repair case, not a completed earnings recovery.
- Meals & Beverages showed stronger in-market consumption and Rao's exceeded
  `$1B` of trailing-twelve-month sales, while Snacks remained weaker. The
  consolidated result must not pool those different demand and margin paths.
- Retailer orders, shipment timing, price, trade promotion, tariffs, storms,
  ingredients, packaging, freight, inventory, and brand investment all remain
  part of the cash-quality denominator.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate in-market consumption, household penetration, market share, and
   volume from retailer shipments. A retailer order is not proof of takeaway.
2. Reconcile price, volume, mix, trade spending, gross margin, ingredients,
   packaging, freight, labor, tariffs, and productivity. Price-led sales with
   lost volume can destroy long-term cash quality.
3. Keep Meals & Beverages and Snacks separate; test Rao's and other growth
   brands for contribution after advertising, capacity, distribution, and
   integration cost.
4. Treat repeated storms, tariffs, restructuring, integration, and productivity
   costs as owner burdens when the operating system keeps producing them.
5. Show operating cash, capital spending, inventory, receivables, debt,
   dividends, repurchases, SBC, and diluted shares together. Familiar brands
   and adjusted EBIT are diagnostic, not normalized owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what category volume, household
penetration, price/mix, trade investment, gross margin, brand return, snack
repair, Sovos contribution, reinvestment, terminal growth, and cost of capital
the valuation requires. A mature-staples multiple must survive private-label
and retailer bargaining pressure.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether households keep buying branded meals
and snacks through affordability pressure, input inflation, tariffs, freight,
and unemployment. Liquidity passes only when inventory, retailer incentives,
brand investment, debt, restructuring, and common residual remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`packaged-meals-snacks-qualified; volume-margin-and-portfolio-repair-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from in-market category and
brand volume to collected sales, retailer settlement, trade spending, gross
margin, inventory, advertising/innovation, Sovos integration, restructuring,
debt, SBC replacement, and diluted common residual. Sales, adjusted EBIT/EPS,
OCF, FCF, dividends, and repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
