# Silver-weighted mine portfolio and acquisition valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Pan American Silver from current-period mining evidence into a company-specific valuation, mine, grade, reserve, acquisition, project, jurisdiction, capital-return, and owner-cash test. It treats Pan American as a silver-weighted multi-metal mine portfolio, not as generic precious-metals exposure.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pan American Silver | Collected silver, gold, zinc, lead, and copper mine cash after sustaining capital, development, processing, royalties, taxes, country claims, MAG/Juanicipio economics, debt, dividends, and dilution | Mine development and replacement, sustaining capex, exploration, processing and underground access, La Colorada Skarn, Jacobina optimization, Juanicipio participation, environmental and community work, and working capital | Silver and gold prices, grade and recovery, mine sequencing, operating costs, permitting, jurisdiction, community, project delays, JV allocation, debt, and dilution | Production, attributable revenue, adjusted earnings, AISC, FCF, dividends, or buybacks rise while reserve replacement, grade, recovery, sustaining/development capex, Juanicipio allocation, jurisdictional cash, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$3.6B`, attributable revenue `$3.8B`, net earnings `$980M`, adjusted earnings `$959M`, and operating cash flow `$1.3B`.
- At December 31, 2025, cash and short-term investments were `$1.3B` and total available liquidity `$2.1B`.
- Q1 `2026` revenue was `$1.154B`, operating cash flow `$505M`, attributable operating cash flow `$582M`, attributable FCF `$488M`, and cash and short-term investments `$1.8B` including `$199M` attributable to Juanicipio.
- Q1 `2026` attributable production was `6.44M` ounces of silver and `169.2K` ounces of gold; 2026 guidance was `25–27M` ounces of attributable silver.
- The company holds a `44%` interest in Juanicipio after the MAG Silver acquisition; FY2025 attributable silver production was `22.8M` ounces and attributable gold production `742.2K` ounces.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile payable metal, concentrate shipments, realized prices, grades, recoveries, royalties, treatment charges, inventory, receivables, and cash collection; ounces are not cash.
2. Separate silver, gold, zinc, lead, copper, Juanicipio, La Colorada, Jacobina, and other mine cohorts; cost, ownership, jurisdiction, and sustaining burdens differ.
3. Test MAG/Juanicipio, La Colorada Skarn, Jacobina optimization, and mine-life work through acquisition price, attributable allocation, capex, reserve conversion, integration, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep adjusted earnings, AISC, attributable FCF, dividends, buybacks, and commodity-price leverage separate from recurring collected common-owner cash.
5. Reconcile sustaining and growth capex, exploration, closure and reclamation, taxes, royalties, country claims, debt, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what metal prices, grades, recoveries, mine life, production, cost curve, reserve replacement, project returns, capex, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether commodity cycles, inflation, permitting, jurisdiction, community claims, project delays, rates, and acquisition leverage preserve Pan American's cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`pan-american-silver-qualified; mine-cohort-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from named mines and ownership interests to payable metal, shipment, realized price, collection, sustaining and development capex, reserve conversion, jurisdictional claims, debt service, taxes, dividends, and diluted common residual. Production, attributable revenue, adjusted earnings, AISC, FCF, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
