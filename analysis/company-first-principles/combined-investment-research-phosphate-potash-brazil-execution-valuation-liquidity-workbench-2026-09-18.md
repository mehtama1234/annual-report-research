# Phosphate, potash, and Brazil execution valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves The Mosaic Company from annual-report evidence into a
company-specific fertilizer conversion, potash mine, Brazilian distribution,
inventory, input-cost, and common-owner residual test. It keeps Mosaic separate
from Nutrien's larger farmer-retail and integrated crop-input model, Bunge's
crop-flow processing, and diversified miners.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mosaic | Mid-cycle phosphate conversion and potash cash after sulfur/ammonia/energy, product price and volume, utilization, Brazil working capital, sustaining capital, environmental obligations, debt, and dilution | Phosphate rock and chemical plants, potash mines, turnarounds, reliability, logistics, Brazil distribution, inventory, environmental work, and reserve/asset renewal | Fertilizer-price or sulfur shock, plant curtailment, inventory build, Brazil currency/credit, environmental claim, debt/refinancing, or maintenance capital deferral | Adjusted EBITDA or fertilizer demand rises while input spread, inventory cash, plant utilization, OCF-less-capex, leverage, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$12.052B`, net operating cash flow `$825M`, PP&E spending `$1.359B`, and OCF-less-PP&E approximately negative `$535M` before dividends, debt actions, and other investments.
- FY2025 inventory was `$3.363B` and cash `$277M`; inventory increased approximately `$428M` and Q4 operating cash flow was negative `$56M`. Inventory quality and reversal timing are central liquidity tests.
- FY2025 external sales were approximately `$3.88B` Phosphate, `$2.66B` Potash, and `$4.85B` Mosaic Fertilizantes. These businesses require separate price, volume, cost, utilization, and capital assumptions.
- Phosphate conversion depends on sulfur and ammonia as well as rock. A high fertilizer price does not prove a high conversion margin when raw-material prices rise or plants are idled.
- 2026 capital guidance was reduced to about `$1.2B`; this may preserve near-term liquidity while deferring reliability, maintenance, or environmental cash into later periods.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Rebuild segment cash from tonnes, realized price, sulfur, ammonia, rock,
   energy, freight, utilization, inventory, receivables, payables, and taxes.
2. Separate finished product, raw material, and work-in-process inventory; test
   subsequent sales, carrying value, markdowns, and the cash cost of curtailment.
3. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA to GAAP operating income, OCF, PP&E, turnarounds,
   environmental work, impairments, mark-to-market items, and FX.
4. Test Brazilian revenue, margin, currency, receivables, inventory, local
   financing, distribution assets, and impairment separately from North American
   production.
5. Treat reduced capital guidance as a timing claim until reliability, mine,
   plant, environmental, and replacement spending are evidenced across periods.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what mid-cycle fertilizer prices,
conversion spreads, input costs, utilization, mine/plant capital, Brazil return,
tax, debt, and cost of capital the equity value requires. The Lyn Alden-style
stress test asks whether food-input demand survives energy and sulfur scarcity,
farmer affordability, currency, rates, trade policy, and commodity-inventory
funding pressure.

## Promotion boundary

`phosphate-potash-brazil-qualified; spread-inventory-and-renewal-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from product tonnes and prices
to input costs, inventory settlement, plant utilization, sustaining capital,
environmental claims, Brazil cash, debt, taxes, and diluted common residual.
Revenue, adjusted EBITDA, production, OCF, price recovery, curtailment savings,
and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
