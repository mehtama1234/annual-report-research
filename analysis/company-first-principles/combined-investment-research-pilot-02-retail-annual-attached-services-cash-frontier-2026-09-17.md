# Pilot 02 annual attached-services cash frontier

Research date: `2026-09-17`

This frontier aligns the latest annual attached-service revenue and income
surfaces with the annual retail denominator control. It is arithmetic
sensitivity only. It does not assert that attached-service revenue is cash or
that any conversion rate is observed.

## Annual reported pools

| Company | Fiscal period | Reported attached-service pool | Components or perimeter |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Target | FY2025 | `$2.063B` | `$915M` advertising, `$522M` credit-card profit sharing, `$626M` other revenue |
| Walmart | FY2026 | `$6.750B` | Walmart U.S. `$2.624B`, International `$1.565B`, Sam's Club U.S. `$2.525B`, Corporate/support `$36M`; membership fees alone `$4.4B` |

## Mechanical conversion frontier

| Company | 0% | 25% | 50% | 75% | 100% |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Target FY2025 | `$0M` | `$515.75M` | `$1.031B` | `$1.54725B` | `$2.063B` |
| Walmart FY2026 | `$0M` | `$1.6875B` | `$3.375B` | `$5.0625B` | `$6.750B` |

The columns are not owner-cash estimates. They exclude service labor,
fulfillment, marketing, technology, overhead, working capital, capex, taxes,
leases, debt, stock compensation, dilution, and legal-entity transfer effects.
Target's TD-owned card receivables and Walmart's mixed membership-and-other
category also prevent a one-for-one revenue-to-cash interpretation.

## Promotion boundary

The frontier improves annual sensitivity alignment with the reported
cash-after-property screen, but no value may be added to owner cash or a
valuation workbench until the same period supplies service-level collection,
allocated costs, capex, taxes, working capital, and claim treatment. The
current result remains `evidence-insufficient` for attached-service cash
conversion.
