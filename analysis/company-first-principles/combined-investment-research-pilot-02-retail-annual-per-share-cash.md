# Pilot 02 annual retail cash per diluted share

Research date: `2026-09-15`

This table puts the annual cohort on one reported denominator: operating cash
flow less property spending, divided by weighted-average diluted shares. It is
not normalized owner cash and does not support a target price by itself.

| Company | Annual period | Cash after property | Diluted shares | Reported cash after property per diluted share | Operating lease liabilities |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| TJX | FY2026 | `$4.917B` | `1,128M` | `$4.359` | `$10.620B` |
| Target | FY2025 | `$2.835B` | `455.6M` | `$6.223` | `$3.834B` |
| Walmart | FY2026 | `$14.923B` | `8,022M` | `$1.860` | `$15.572B` |

The arithmetic is intentionally transparent. Target’s higher reported cash per
share is not evidence of a superior retail system: the companies have different
capital intensity, lease structures, fiscal periods, attached businesses,
working-capital cycles, and accounting boundaries. Walmart’s per-share figure
also sits after a very large omnichannel and platform investment program, while
TJX’s figure still requires a more complete inventory-turn and maintenance-capex
test.

## What this establishes

1. The retail cohort can now be compared on annual reported cash after property
   spending per diluted share.
2. Dilution is visible rather than hidden inside total cash.
3. Lease liabilities remain a separate capital burden; subtracting both lease
   cash already inside operating cash flow and lease liability again would
   double count.
4. The denominator is still not normalized owner cash because vendor terms,
   taxes, stock compensation, maintenance capital, inventory turns, and
   attached-service costs remain incompletely separated.

Sources: [TJX FY2026 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109198/000010919926000008/tjx-20260131.htm), [Target FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27419/000002741926000016/tgt-20260131.htm), and [Walmart FY2026 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/104169/000010416926000055/wmt-20260131.htm).
