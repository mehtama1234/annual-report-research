# Retail Attached-Services and Ancillary-Revenue Boundary

Research date: `2026-09-15`

This memo isolates revenue streams attached to the retail customer relationship
but not yet separable as standalone common-owner cash. The filing evidence is
useful for identifying control points and mix, but revenue must not be added to
normalized cash without its costs, working capital, capital intensity, taxes,
and legal-entity allocation.

| Company | Period | Filing evidence | Safe interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target | H1 2026 | `$525M` advertising revenue; `$269M` credit-card profit sharing; `$347M` other revenue; `$50.841B` merchandise sales | Attached services are visible alongside the merchandise engine; contribution margins, allocated costs, and owner-cash conversion are not separately proven. |
| Walmart U.S. | H1 FY2027 | `$1.676B` membership and other income; `$242.358B` net sales; `$244.034B` total revenues; e-commerce is an ecosystem that includes selected advertising, fulfillment, and data-insight offerings | Membership and ecosystem monetization are visible, but the filing does not provide a complete standalone profit or cash bridge. |
| Walmart International | H1 FY2027 | `$851M` membership and other income; segment total revenues are separately disclosed | International ancillary economics are visible, but country-level cost and cash allocation remains open. |
| Sam's Club U.S. | H1 FY2027 | `$1.328B` membership and other income; `$49.118B` net sales; `$50.446B` total revenues; about `$8.8B` e-commerce net sales | Membership is a material attached-service layer, but its incremental margin and reinvestment burden are not separately disclosed. |

Walmart's consolidated H1 FY2027 statement separately reports `$3.904B` of
membership and other income. The three segment amounts above sum to `$3.855B`,
leaving a `$49M` presentation/perimeter difference. The difference is tracked
in the [Walmart attached-income denominator reconciliation](combined-investment-research-pilot-02-walmart-attached-income-denominator-reconciliation-2026-09-16.md)
and is not allocated to a segment, service, cash, or owner return.

## Boundary

The evidence proves that advertising, memberships, card economics, other
income, and ecosystem services are part of the retail control point. It does
not prove that these amounts are incremental to merchandise economics, that
they carry merchandise-like margins, or that their revenue can be converted
one-for-one into owner cash. The normalized cash screen therefore leaves them
out as standalone additions and keeps the next upgrade focused on allocated
cost, capex, working capital, tax, and cash collection.
