# Pilot 02 retail attached-services mix screen

Research date: `2026-09-15`

The filings disclose several revenue streams attached to the retail
relationship. This screen makes their reported scale comparable without
pretending that revenue equals incremental margin or common-owner cash.

## Interpretation rule

The mix screen shows that attached services are visible but not necessarily
large relative to merchandise sales. It must not be used to add service
revenue to an owner-cash bridge. The missing bridge is:

```text
reported attached-service revenue
  -> direct service cost and seller/vendor support
  -> fulfillment, labor, technology, and allocated overhead
  -> working capital, capex, taxes, and dilution
  -> cash collected and legally available to common owners
```

`attached-service-mix-screen`: reported amounts and arithmetic ratios are
source-backed; standalone contribution margin and owner-cash conversion are
unresolved.
