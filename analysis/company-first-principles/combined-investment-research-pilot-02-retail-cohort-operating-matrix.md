and Walmart in one place. It is a comparison aid, not a claim that the periods
or definitions are identical.

## Comparison boundary

The matrix supports a more precise operating question: whether value-seeking
demand is showing up in traffic, transactions, comparable sales, digital use,
or membership—not merely reported revenue. It still does not produce a common
owner-cash ranking. The companies report different periods, segment scopes,
and definitions, and the remaining cash bridge must normalize inventory,
payables, leases, capital spending, ancillary-service costs, taxes, and
dilution.
