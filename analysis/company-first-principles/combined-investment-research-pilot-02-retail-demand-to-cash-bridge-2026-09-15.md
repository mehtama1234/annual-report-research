# Retail affordability demand-to-cash bridge — 2026-09-15

This bridge connects the affordability/substitution hypothesis to reported
company-period operating signals and then to cash-quality tests. It prevents a
social observation or comparable-sales number from being treated as proof of
owner cash.

## Evidence chain

```text
household price pressure / substitution
  -> value-seeking retail control point
  -> traffic, transactions, ticket, mix, digital, and attached-service signals
  -> merchandise margin, inventory, payables, support, and capex
  -> operating cash after property spending
  -> normalized common-owner cash test
```

## Falsifiable tests

The affordability hypothesis strengthens only if future filings show that
value-seeking demand survives after separating:

- traffic or transactions from ticket and price;
- merchandise volume from mix and promotional markdowns;
- attached-service growth from allocated fulfillment, labor, technology, and
  collection costs;
- gross-margin change from tariff refunds, vendor income, freight, and
  payables;
- operating cash from inventory, supplier-finance, and seasonality timing; and
- cash after property spending from maintenance capital, leases, taxes,
  dilution, debt, and common-owner claims.

The thesis weakens if traffic/transactions deteriorate, margin gains reverse
after support removal, inventory or markdown pressure rises, or the demand
signal fails to survive the denominator and cash tests.
