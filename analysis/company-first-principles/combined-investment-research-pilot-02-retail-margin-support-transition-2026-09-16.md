# Retail margin-support transition screen — 2026-09-16

This screen advances Q-04 by separating reported year-over-year gross-margin
movement from identifiable tariff-refund support. It is a gross-profit
sensitivity, not a normalized margin, earnings, or owner-cash conclusion.

## H1 comparison

| Company | Current net sales | Current gross profit | Reported GM | Prior-period GM | Reported change | Identifiable support | Support-removal GM | Support-removal change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| TJX / H1 FY2027 | `$29.503B` | `$9.552B` | `32.4%` | `30.1%` | `+224 bp` | `$331M` tariff-refund candidate | `31.3%` | `+112 bp` |
| Target / H1 2026 | `$51.982B` | `$16.318B` | `31.4%` | `28.6%` | `+280 bp` | `$994M` tariff refund | `29.5%` | `+88 bp` |
| Walmart / H1 FY2027 | `$361.784B` | `$89.922B` | `24.9%` | `24.3%` | `+53 bp` | approximately `$2.9B` tariff refunds | `24.1%` | `-25 bp` |

The support-removal calculation is:

```text
(reported gross profit − identifiable support) / current net sales
```

TJX's filing reports `$331M` of IEEPA-related refunds recognized in cost of
sales, but also reports `$112M` of incremental compensation accruals related to
the refunds; the allocation of that accrual between cost of sales and SG&A is
not fully usable as a gross-margin adjustment. Target's filing states that the
`$994M` refund benefited the H1 gross-margin rate by `1.9` percentage points.
Walmart states that approximately `$2.9B` of refunds were recorded as a
reduction of cost of sales and that the H1 gross-profit rate increased `53`
basis points year over year. Removing the disclosed gross-profit support
candidates produces the bounded screens above.

## Interpretation boundary

The screen shows that reported margin movement can have a materially different
reading after a disclosed support item is removed. It does not claim that the
support would disappear from pricing, customer investment, vendor economics,
or future cash flow. It also excludes mix, markdowns, freight, shrink,
fulfillment, inventory timing, supplier finance, labor, and capital allocation.
TJX is included with the weaker `candidate` label because the related
compensation accrual is not allocated cleanly between cost of sales and SG&A.

Status: `cohort-support-removal-screen-with-TJX-allocation-boundary`; recurring
gross margin and owner cash remain unresolved.
