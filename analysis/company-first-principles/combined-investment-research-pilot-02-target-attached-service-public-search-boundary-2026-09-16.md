# Target attached-service public-search boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Purpose

This control rechecks Target's latest Q2 2026 Form 10-Q for a service-level
profit and cash bridge. The search covers revenue classification, payer and
risk ownership, aggregate operating burdens, cash timing, and any disclosure
that allocates costs to Roundel, Target Circle card profit sharing, Target Plus,
Target Circle 360, or Shipt.

360, Shipt, or card profit sharing and therefore is not treated as attached-
service cash conversion.

## Allocation boundary

The filing states that Roundel services may be reported as Net Sales or as a
reduction of Cost of Sales or SG&A depending on the advertising arrangement.
That prevents advertising revenue from being treated as a clean incremental
gross-profit line.

The filing states that TD Bank Group underwrites, funds, and owns the Target
Circle receivables and controls risk management and regulatory compliance.
Target performs account servicing and primary marketing in exchange for a
percentage of profits, but the filing does not disclose Target's allocated
servicing, marketing, technology, working-capital, tax, or cash-collection
costs.

The filing identifies Target Plus commissions, Circle 360 membership, and
Shipt membership/service revenue inside `Other`, but does not provide a
standalone income statement, cash-flow statement, capex schedule, tax burden,
or legal-entity collection bridge for those activities.

The Q2 and H1 aggregate cost tables also do not allocate merchandising,
fulfillment, SG&A, or D&A across merchandise and attached services. The
`$994M` IEEPA tariff refund recognized as a reduction of Cost of Sales is a
temporary support item and cannot be used as recurring service margin or owner
cash.

The gift-card roll-forward likewise does not provide an attached-service
collection or cost bridge. It is retained as a perimeter and timing control,
not added to the service-revenue sensitivity frontier.

Target's June 2026 Roundel fact sheet adds operating and durability context:
Roundel is described as an 800-plus-person team integrated within Target,
working with more than 2,000 vendors, and generating more than `$2B` of value
for Target. The fact sheet does not define that value as GAAP revenue, profit,
or cash; the 10-K also says advertisers do not have long-term commitments.
These observations identify Target's capability burden and recurrence risk, but
are excluded from the owner-cash denominator.

## Result class

`searched-negative-for-service-level-allocation; evidence-insufficient-for-owner-cash`

The filing improves the payer, revenue, and aggregate-burden map. It does not
close Q-06. Promotion still requires a period-matched allocation of service
costs, capital, working capital, taxes, collections, and legal-entity cash.
