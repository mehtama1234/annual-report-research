# Target gift-card liability and cash-timing boundary

Research date: `2026-09-16`

Target's Q2 2026 Form 10-Q reports a gift-card liability of `$1.197B` at
January 31, 2026, `$376M` of gift cards issued during the first six months,
`$614M` of revenue recognized from the beginning liability, and a `$959M`
ending liability at August 1, 2026.

The roll-forward reconciles mechanically:

`$1.197B + $376M - $614M = $959M`

This is useful denominator evidence because gift-card issuance, redemption,
breakage, and revenue recognition occur at different times. The table is not a
claim that the `$376M` issuance figure equals incremental unrestricted cash,
nor that the `$238M` liability reduction is current-period owner cash. Payment
method, breakage, redemption timing, refunds, and working-capital effects
remain outside this boundary.
