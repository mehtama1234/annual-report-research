# Target H1 2026 owner-cash denominator control — 2026-09-15

This memo puts Target's H1 cash-after-property screen beside debt principal,
dividends, repurchases, share-based compensation, and supplier-finance
exposure. It makes the denominator more complete without claiming that Target
has disclosed a maintenance-capital split or normalized owner cash.

## Filing-backed bridge

Target reported H1 operating cash flow of `$4.519B` and property spending of
`$2.404B`, leaving `$2.115B` of cash after property spending. The financing
statement reports `$1.070B` of debt reduction, `$1.034B` of dividends paid, and
`$3M` of stock repurchases. The cash-flow statement also reports `$154M` of
share-based compensation expense and `$92M` of shares withheld for related
taxes. Diluted weighted-average shares were `456.2M`, compared with `456.1M` in
the prior-year H1 period.

Target's supplier-finance disclosure reports `$3.2B` of vendor obligations
eligible for early payment at August 1, 2026. That balance is not itself an
H1 cash use or proof of manipulation; it is a financing and working-capital
dependency that must remain visible in the owner-cash test.

The targeted H1 filing recheck also sharpens two previously unresolved burden
boundaries. Target reports `$3.733B` of total operating-lease liabilities at
August 1, 2026 and uses `$172M` of trailing-twelve-month operating-lease
interest in its ROIC presentation, but the H1 cash-flow statement does not
disclose a separate operating-lease cash-payment line. Target also reports an
H1 income-tax provision of `$835M` and a `23.9%` effective rate, but does not
provide H1 cash taxes paid in the checked 10-Q. These are burden observations,
not deductions to be layered onto operating cash without a cash-payment join.

## Controlled denominator views

```text
reported cash after property                         $2.115B
less disclosed H1 debt reduction                      $1.070B
                                                   -------------
post-financing residual                               $1.045B

post-financing residual                               $1.045B
less H1 dividends                                      $1.034B
less H1 repurchases                                    $0.003B
                                                   -------------
cash after debt, dividends, and repurchases           $0.008B
```

The final view is a capital-allocation stress display, not reported negative
owner cash. It shows how little room remains if the H1 cash-after-property
screen is treated as the source for debt repayment, dividends, and buybacks
before maintenance capital, taxes, leases, supplier finance, and seasonality.

## Evidence boundary

| Layer | Status | What remains open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 reported cash after property | `filed-and-reconciled` | Tariff/refund normalization and payable/inventory reversal |
| Debt, dividends, and repurchases | `filed-and-reconciled-separate-uses` | Future maturity, dividend coverage, and capital-return sustainability |
| Share-based compensation | `filed-and-reconciled` | Future awards and per-share owner-cash effect |
| Supplier finance | `dependency-visible` | Actual early-payment utilization, settlement timing, and recurring trade-term economics |
| Operating leases | `balance-and-roic-burden-visible` | H1 operating-lease cash payments and any service/lease allocation to owner cash |
| Income taxes | `provision-visible-cash-paid-open` | H1 cash taxes paid, tax timing, and allocation to normalized owner cash |
| Maintenance versus growth capital | `held` | Current-period allocation within stores, remodels, supply chain, technology, and other capital |
| Normalized common-owner cash | `held` | Annual seasonality, attached-service cash, taxes, leases, vendor income, and maintenance capital |
