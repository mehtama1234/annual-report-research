# TJX H1 FY2027 cash-quality screen upgrade

Research date: `2026-09-15`

## Purpose

Extend the retail normalized-cash screen to the same H1 FY2027 period already
used for the TJX/Target/Walmart common-period comparison. This is a cash-
quality boundary, not final owner cash.

## H1 screen

TJX reports H1 FY2027 operating cash flow of `$3.345B` and property additions
of `$1.159B`, producing `$2.186B` of cash after property spending. The same
filing identifies `$470M` of accounts-payable support and `$85M` of share-based
compensation.

| Case | Calculation | Screen cash |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Low | `$2.186B - $470M - $85M` | `$1.631B` |
| Base | `$2.186B - $85M` | `$2.101B` |
| High | Reported cash after property | `$2.186B` |

The filing also identifies tariff refunds and a credit-card interchange-fee
settlement as contributors to operating cash flow, but the current local
evidence does not cleanly isolate their H1 amounts or overlap with the cash-
flow timing items above. They therefore remain outside the arithmetic rather
than being deducted as if independently measured.

## Boundary

The screen does not normalize inventory turns, markdowns, lease structure,
maintenance versus growth capital, taxes, seasonality, or diluted per-share
cash. Lease cash is not subtracted again because it is already embedded in
operating cash flow. The result is therefore a source-bounded cash-quality
range, not a valuation denominator.

## Safe claim

`TJX H1 FY2027 cash after property is $2.186B; subtracting the disclosed $470M
accounts-payable support and $85M SBC produces a conservative $1.631B screen,
while temporary tariff/interchange benefits and maintenance capital remain
unresolved.`
