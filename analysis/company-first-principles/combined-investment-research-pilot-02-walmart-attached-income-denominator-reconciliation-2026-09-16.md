# Walmart attached-income denominator reconciliation

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Finding

Walmart's latest Q2 FY2027 Form 10-Q reports consolidated “membership and
other income” of `$3.904B` for the six months ended July 31, 2026. The segment
rows used in the attached-services frontier sum to `$3.855B`:

`$1.676B` Walmart U.S. + `$0.851B` Walmart International + `$1.328B` Sam's
Club U.S. = `$3.855B`.

The resulting `$49M` difference is a presentation/perimeter reconciliation
item. It is not assigned to membership, advertising, cash, or any segment
without an explicit filing bridge.

| Denominator | Amount | Safe use |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Consolidated membership and other income | `$3.904B` | Consolidated reported-income reference |
| Sum of disclosed segment membership and other income | `$3.855B` | Segment attached-income sensitivity used by the frontier |
| Unreconciled difference | `$49M` | Perimeter check only; not allocated |

The existing 0–100% attached-services frontier intentionally remains based on
the `$3.855B` segment subtotal because its components are separately visible.
This reconciliation prevents that subtotal from being described as the
consolidated global pool. Neither denominator is observed attached-service
cash: Walmart also states that advertising may be recorded in net sales or as
a reduction of cost of sales depending on the arrangement, and “other” is not
fully decomposed.

## Proof-grade result

`denominator-reconciled; attached-service-cash-unproven`

The correction improves denominator discipline but does not close service-level
cost, working-capital, tax, capex, collection, or common-owner cash allocation.
