# Mobile gaming, live-ops, and direct-billing valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Playtika from current-period gaming evidence into a company-specific valuation, title, payer, direct-billing, acquisition, earnout, marketing, debt, and owner-cash test. It treats Playtika as a portfolio of live-ops participation economies, not as a generic software or media company.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Playtika | Collected player spend after app-store and direct-billing economics, live-ops content, user acquisition, title support, SuperPlay integration and earnout, platform costs, debt, and dilution | Game development and live operations, media buys, licensed IP, direct-to-consumer payment tooling, title refresh, acquisitions, earnout cash, customer support, and working capital | Title concentration, payer conversion, consumer spending, platform policy, app-store fees, media-buy efficiency, SuperPlay performance and earnout, debt/refinancing, and dilution | Revenue, DAU/ payer metrics, DTC mix, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EPS, or buybacks rise while title cohorts, payer retention, acquisition return, earnout cash, marketing efficiency, debt service, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$2.755B` and adjusted EBITDA `$753.2M`; direct-to-consumer revenue was `$814.5M`, up from `$694.2M`.
- Q1 `2026` revenue was `$744.7M`, DTC revenue `$291.8M`, adjusted EBITDA `$125.2M`, and cash `$779.2M`.
- Q2 `2026` revenue was `$731.1M`, DTC revenue `$286.9M`, adjusted EBITDA `$206.1M`, net income `$48.0M`, and cash `$438.5M`.
- Six-month 2026 operating cash flow was only `$51.5M` versus `$164.9M` in the prior-year period; the period included `$111.0M` of cash outflow for SuperPlay earnout payment above acquisition-date fair value.
- The 2025 10-K said the top ten games represented `90.2%` of revenue; Playtika carried a `$1.9B` senior secured first-lien term loan and `$600M` of senior notes due `2029`.
- Q4 `2025` contingent-consideration remeasurement was `$394.1M`; the full-year amount was `$398.8M`, reflecting Level 3 SuperPlay earnout valuation.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile bookings and player spend to platform remittance, DTC collections, payer conversion, retention, refunds, virtual goods, and live-ops delivery; revenue is not durable player cash.
2. Separate casual, social-casino, licensed-IP, SuperPlay, and legacy title cohorts; the top-ten concentration means portfolio averages can conceal decay.
3. Test SuperPlay through purchase price, marketing, title contribution, earnout cash, contingent-consideration remeasurement, integration, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income, DTC mix, payer metrics, earnout adjustments, and buybacks separate from recurring collected owner cash.
5. Reconcile media buys, app-store fees, content and support costs, debt, interest, covenants, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what payer retention, title longevity, DTC mix, user-acquisition return, SuperPlay contribution, live-ops reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether consumer spending, platform fees, privacy rules, media-buy inflation, title fatigue, earnout cash, rates, and leverage preserve Playtika's cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`playtika-mobile-gaming-qualified; title-payer-earnout-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from title and payer cohorts to platform or DTC collection, live-ops cost, media-buy return, SuperPlay integration and earnout cash, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, DTC mix, payer metrics, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EPS, earnout remeasurement, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
