# Power-management and aerospace-systems valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Eaton from electrification-demand evidence into a
company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps Electrical,
Aerospace, and Mobility separation economics separate from generation/grid OEMs,
qualified grid components, and thermal/electrical protection, while testing
backlog conversion, unbilled receivables, acquisitions, PP&E, debt, separation
costs, and diluted common-owner cash.

## Decision matrix

| Eaton power management and aerospace systems | Electrical and aerospace cash after capacity, qualification, customer acceptance, unbilled receivables, inventory, acquisition integration, Mobility separation, debt, taxes, and dilution | Electrical/aerospace manufacturing and tooling, capacity, inventory, receivables, R&D, acquisitions, separation systems/costs, debt, SBC, and replacement capital | Data-center/grid order pause, backlog cancellation, unbilled-receivable buildup, acquisition impairment, separation leakage, rates, debt, or capital-return overreach | Orders and adjusted EPS rise while backlog conversion, billed cash, acquisition return, separation-adjusted residual, or diluted per-share cash weaken |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$27.448B`, OCF `$4.472B`, PP&E capex `$919M`, and
  acquisitions `$1.490B`; the mechanical residual after those uses was about
  `$2.063B`.
- FY2025 dividends and repurchases were approximately `$1.628B` and `$1.862B`,
  exceeding that mechanical residual before debt, separation, and normalization.
- FY2025 unbilled receivables rose to `$759M` from `$330M`; receivables used
  `$420M` and inventory `$256M` of cash. FY2026 Q2 backlog was about `$24.1B`.
- Q2 2026 sales reached `$8.531B`, organic growth `14%`, Electrical Americas
  rolling orders rose `41%`, Electrical Global `33%`, and Aerospace orders `17%`.
- Mobility separation is expected through a Reverse Morris Trust transaction;
  separation cash costs, tax effects, stranded expense, debt allocation, and
  continuing-company capital-return coverage remain open.
- FY2025 debt including current maturities was `$9.894B`, cash and restricted
  cash `$622M`, and goodwill `$15.769B`; acquisition return and liquidity are
  central to valuation.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile orders and backlog to production, billing, unbilled receivables,
   contract liabilities, acceptance, segment margin, and cash collection.
2. Track Fibrebond, Resilient, and other acquisition cohorts through revenue,
   margin, integration, goodwill, working capital, and incremental cash return.
3. Keep adjusted EPS separate from acquisition amortization, separation costs,
   taxes, stranded overhead, and dilution.
4. Test whether dividends and repurchases are covered by normalized residual cash
   after PP&E, acquisitions, working capital, debt, and separation spending.
5. Separate Electrical, Aerospace, and Mobility economics by cycle, qualification,
   customer concentration, maintenance, and capital intensity.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what electrical/aerospace volume,
backlog conversion, price/mix, capacity return, acquisition return, separation
benefit, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. A premium
multiple requires cash conversion, not only order strength.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether data centers, utilities, industry,
and aerospace customers can fund projects through higher rates, order deferral,
capacity overbuild, inventory pressure, and tighter credit. Liquidity passes
only when unbilled receivables, debt, separation claims, and common residual
remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`power-management-aerospace-qualified; backlog-and-separation-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from orders/backlog to
shipment, acceptance, billed collection, unbilled-receivable settlement,
inventory, acquisition return, separation cash, debt, SBC, and diluted common
residual. Orders, backlog, adjusted EPS, OCF, FCF, dividends, and repurchases
remain diagnostic inputs.
