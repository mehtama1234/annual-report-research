# Logistics real estate, embedded rent, and strategic-capital valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Prologis from the annual-report archive into a
company-specific industrial/logistics REIT, lease-roll, occupancy, development,
strategic-capital, data-center-power, and common-owner residual test. It keeps
Prologis separate from senior-housing, hotel, office, and agency-mortgage REITs.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prologis | Property-level cash NOI and common residual after occupancy, rent growth, lease rollover, tenant credit, recurring building capital, development, acquisitions, strategic-capital fees, debt, cap rates, and dilution | Logistics buildings, land, development, redevelopment, maintenance, energy/data-center infrastructure, technology, joint ventures, and working capital | Occupancy/rent slowdown, tenant bankruptcy, development yield compression, rates/cap rates, construction cost, power/interconnection delay, JV/strategic-capital funding, or debt refinancing | Core FFO, occupancy, leasing volume, rent growth, or data-center pipeline rise while tenant collection, recurring capital, development return, leverage, cap rates, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Q2 2026 period-end occupancy was `95.5%`; Q4 2025 was `95.8%` and Q1 2026 `95.3%`. Occupancy must be joined to rent, tenant credit, lease rollover, and concessions.
- Core FFO per diluted share was `$1.63` in Q2 2026, `$1.50` in Q1, and `$1.44` in Q4 2025. FFO is a property-operating measure, not automatically common cash after recurring capital and growth funding.
- Q2 2026 net effective same-store NOI growth was `6.4%`, while Q1 cash same-store NOI growth was `8.8%`. Embedded rent growth requires lease expiry, retention, concessions, and collection tests.
- Lease commencements remained unusually strong, with more than `64M` to `67M` square feet highlighted in Q1/Q2 2026 and `43.8M` square feet commenced in Q4 2025. Leasing volume is not the same as profitable, funded occupancy.
- The data-center power pipeline expanded to approximately `5.8 GW` in Q2 2026. Pipeline power is not energized capacity, collected rent, or owner cash until interconnection, construction, customer credit, and return are evidenced.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile occupancy and leasing volume to executed leases, commencements,
   rent, concessions, tenant improvements, commissions, collection, and rollover.
2. Separate same-store NOI from development, redevelopment, acquisitions,
   dispositions, strategic-capital fees, and joint-venture cash.
3. Test recurring property capital, land, construction, power infrastructure,
   development yields, lease-up, and cost-to-complete before valuing growth.
4. Roll tenant concentration, bankruptcies, receivables, security deposits,
   guarantees, cap rates, debt maturities, interest cost, and JV obligations.
5. Treat Core FFO, occupancy, leasing volume, pipeline MW, asset sales,
   distributions, and buybacks as diagnostic until common per-share cash closes.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what occupancy, rent growth, retention,
development yield, recurring capital, land value, cap rate, debt cost, strategic-
capital contribution, and cost of equity the valuation requires. The Lyn
Alden-style stress test asks whether rates, construction costs, trade demand,
tenant credit, energy availability, refinancing, and property-market liquidity
preserve cash through a real-estate cycle.

## Promotion boundary

`prologis-logistics-property-qualified; lease-collection-development-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from executed lease and tenant
demand to collection, same-store NOI, recurring capital, development return,
power/interconnection, JV/strategic-capital funding, debt, taxes, and diluted
common residual. Core FFO, occupancy, leasing volume, rent-growth metrics,
pipeline MW, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
