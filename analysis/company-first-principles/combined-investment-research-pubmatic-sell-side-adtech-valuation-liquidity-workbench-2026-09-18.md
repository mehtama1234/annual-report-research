# PubMatic sell-side ad-tech valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench tests PubMatic as independent sell-side and supply-path infrastructure for fragmented digital advertising. It keeps publisher settlement, demand quality, CTV/mobile/app mix, impressions, AI-powered deals, traffic and platform concentration, working capital, repurchases, and diluted common residual separate from reported revenue and adjusted EBITDA.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PubMatic | Sell-side ad-tech cash after publisher and supply settlement, platform infrastructure, CTV/mobile/app inventory, AI execution, demand diversification, working capital, repurchases, debt, and diluted common residual | Auction and platform engineering, publisher integrations, AI/AgenticOS, data, supply-path optimization, sales, working capital, debt, and shares | Ad-budget or CTV/mobile pullback, publisher concentration, demand-path disintermediation, privacy/regulatory change, legacy-DSP runoff, or refinancing | Revenue and impression volume rise while take-rate quality, publisher settlement, demand diversification, AI cost, or diluted per-share cash weaken |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$282.9M`, gross profit `$179.8M`, adjusted/non-GAAP net income `$16.7M`, operating cash flow `$81.1M`, and free cash flow `$46.2M`.
- Q2 2026 revenue was `$78.6M`, adjusted EBITDA `$19.6M`, operating cash flow `$20.2M`, and free cash flow `$13.7M`; CTV, mobile app, and emerging revenue were about `60%` of total revenue.
- Supply Path Optimization exceeded `55%` of platform activity; nearly `92T` impressions were processed in Q2 2026.
- PubMatic had delivered more than `80` autonomous end-to-end campaigns and more than `4,000` AI-powered deals since launch in January 2026.
- The company repurchased `15.5M` shares for `$211.4M` cumulatively through June 2026, requiring a strict residual and funding test.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile impressions, gross spend, publisher settlement, platform take rate, demand quality, and collected cash.
2. Separate CTV/mobile/emerging growth from political revenue, legacy DSP runoff, partner concentration, seasonality, and mix.
3. Test AI-powered deals and AgenticOS against incremental advertiser outcomes, publisher retention, engineering cost, and data investment.
4. Keep repurchases, stock compensation, working capital, debt, partner claims, and diluted shares in the common-owner residual schedule.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what supply-path activity, CTV/mobile mix, take-rate quality, publisher retention, AI productivity, reinvestment rate, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether fragmented ad supply remains financeable through ad-budget, privacy, platform, macro, and demand-concentration shocks.

## Promotion boundary

`pubmatic-qualified; sell-side-supply-and-publisher-collection-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from impressions and auction activity to publisher settlement, advertiser/demand quality, retention, AI reinvestment, working capital, claims, repurchases, and diluted common residual. Revenue, impressions, adjusted EBITDA, free cash flow, guidance, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
