# Enterprise data cloud and subscription storage valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Pure/Everpure from current-period storage-platform evidence into a company-specific valuation, flash, subscription, ARR, RPO, hybrid-cloud, data-management, acquisition, and owner-cash test. It treats Pure as a governed enterprise data platform layered above raw storage media, not as a classic one-time hardware vendor.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pure/Everpure | Collected subscription, storage, platform, and service cash after flash and component cost, hardware delivery, support, Evergreen//One obligations, cloud and data-management delivery, 1touch integration, working capital, debt, and dilution | Flash and hardware supply, software and control-plane development, cloud and hybrid infrastructure, customer implementation, support and service levels, acquisitions, R&D, and working capital | Flash supply and pricing, hyperscale concentration, subscription renewal, RPO conversion, cloud delivery cost, customer migration, AI demand, acquisition integration, debt, and dilution | Revenue, subscription ARR, RPO, gross margin, adjusted FCF, AI demand, or buybacks rise while renewal, hardware delivery, cloud cost, service obligation, implementation, acquisition return, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2026 revenue was about `$3.663B`, gross profit `$2.578B`, operating income `$114.8M`, net income `$188.2M`, and operating cash flow `$880.1M`; cash and equivalents ended at about `$854.9M` and total cash plus marketable securities around `$1.5B`.
- Q1 FY2027 revenue was `$1.053B`, up `35%`; product revenue `$577M`, subscription services `$476M`, subscription ARR `$2.0B`, RPO `$3.8B`, GAAP gross margin `68.7%`, operating cash flow `$180.2M`, and FCF `$111.8M`.
- Q4 FY2026 subscription revenue was `$440M`, subscription ARR `$1.9B`, RPO `$3.7B`, gross margin `69.9%`, operating cash flow `$268M`, and FCF `$201.4M`.
- Q3 FY2026 subscription revenue was `$429.7M`, subscription ARR `$1.8B`, RPO `$2.9B`, gross margin `72.3%`, operating cash flow `$116M`, and FCF `$52.6M`.
- The platform combines all-flash infrastructure, Evergreen//One, Enterprise Data Cloud, hybrid-cloud control, cyber resilience, AI data access, and the 1touch acquisition; ARR and RPO are visibility measures, not automatically collected owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile product and subscription billing, ARR, RPO, renewals, hardware delivery, support, cloud/data-management cost, implementation, and cash collection; ARR is not cash.
2. Separate product, subscription, hyperscale, enterprise, hybrid cloud, Evergreen//One, Enterprise Data Cloud, and 1touch cohorts; economics and obligations differ.
3. Test platform and acquisition investment through renewal, migration, service levels, cloud cost, customer retention, RPO conversion, integration, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep gross margin, ARR, RPO, adjusted FCF, AI demand, buybacks, and rebrand or synergy claims separate from recurring collected common-owner cash.
5. Reconcile flash and component cost, deferred revenue, support obligations, cloud spend, R&D, capitalized costs, debt, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what subscription renewal, ARR growth, RPO conversion, flash economics, platform adoption, service margin, AI demand, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether cloud costs, flash supply, hyperscale budgets, migration friction, cyber obligations, rates, acquisition leverage, and subscription churn preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`pure-everpure-enterprise-data-qualified; arr-rpo-renewal-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from product, subscription, and platform cohorts to billing, renewal, RPO conversion, hardware delivery, cloud and service cost, implementation, acquisition integration, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, subscription ARR, RPO, gross margin, FCF, AI demand, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
