# Q-10 current six-lane macro overlay

Research date: `2026-09-17`  
Status: `macro mechanism mapped; causal promotion open; no ranking`

## Purpose

The current Q2 refreshes provide company-period observations, but one quarter
cannot establish that a macro force caused an operating or valuation outcome.
This overlay extends the through-cycle protocol to six current lanes. It
defines the proposed transmission mechanism, the observable company response,
the principal confound, and the evidence required before promoting a causal
conclusion.

## Causal testing rules

1. Align the macro observation, company reporting period, and operating measure.
   Do not match a calendar-month macro series to a fiscal quarter without
   recording the mapping.
2. State the predicted direction before reviewing the company result. A
   post-hoc explanation is a hypothesis, not a test.
3. Separate common macro exposure from company action. Pricing, acquisitions,
   store openings, production changes, reserve releases, and buybacks can
   create the same direction as the macro variable.
4. Use both positive and negative cases. A mechanism that only appears in a
   favorable period is not through-cycle evidence.
5. Keep the accounting bridge intact. Revenue, adjusted earnings, OCF, FCF,
   legal-entity cash, and common-owner residual are distinct dependent
   variables.
6. Require a thesis breaker stated in advance. If the predicted demand,
   margin, funding, or liquidity response does not appear after controls, the
   macro thesis loses confidence even if the company remains profitable.

## Current result

The six lanes are `macro-route-visible; company-response-observed;
causal-promotion-open`. The current filings support observations and
mechanism hypotheses, not causal attribution. The next Q-10 upgrade is a
repeated period panel for each lane, with explicit macro series, company
actions, operating denominators, and owner-cash consequences.

Damodaran-style valuation uses the macro overlay to stress growth, margins,
reinvestment, cost of capital, and terminal assumptions. Lyn Alden-style
analysis uses it to stress rates, liquidity, inflation, credit, nominal claims,
and funding access. Neither framework converts a plausible macro path into
company-specific proof.
