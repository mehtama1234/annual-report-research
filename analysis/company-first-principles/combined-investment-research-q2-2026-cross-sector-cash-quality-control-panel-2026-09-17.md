# Q2 2026 cross-sector cash-quality control panel

Research date: `2026-09-17`  
Status: `qualified diagnostic; no pooled ranking`

## Purpose

This panel connects the latest Q2 and current-period refreshes without pretending that their
denominators are interchangeable. Each lane is on a current H1 or Q2 2026
filing surface, but the economic object differs: deferred service revenue,
exceptional proceeds, inventory-funded store growth, credit-loss reserves,
physical energy conversion, and reserve replacement. The panel is therefore a
control map for the larger end-to-end goal, not a valuation table.

## What the panel reveals

### 1. “Cash conversion” has six different meanings

Fortinet's cash is partly a timing consequence of service contracts; CF's cash
contains incident and tax-related items; retail cash moves with inventory and
store growth; franchise cash moves through franchisees; healthcare cash moves
through working capital and acquisitions; financial-company cash requires loss
and capital normalization; insurance cash requires reserves and liability cost;
exchange cash requires collateral/legal-availability separation; digital
infrastructure cash requires project attribution; midstream cash requires
route, cargo, and project attribution; and upstream cash requires reserve
replacement. A single OCF margin or OCF-less-capex
multiple would therefore mix claims with unlike economic objects.

### 2. Exceptional items must be traced, not blindly removed

The appropriate QoE treatment is to identify whether an item is cash-paid,
recurring, operationally embedded, legally restricted, or merely a timing
transfer. Removing CF's litigation proceeds, Exxon’s reserve/disruption items,
or JPMorgan’s Visa gain changes the numerator, but it does not by itself prove
the replacement denominator. Likewise, adding back SBC while ignoring
repurchases can understate the cost of maintaining the diluted share base.

### 3. Growth can consume the cash it appears to validate

Cloudflare's infrastructure and contract-acquisition spending, Burlington's
store investment, Ollie's inventory build, Devon's acreage purchase, and
ConocoPhillips' development program all convert growth claims into funding
questions. The next test is not whether growth occurred; it is whether the
incremental cash return exceeds the capital, claims, and financing required to
produce it.

### 4. The payer and burden carrier can be different entities

Franchisees, suppliers, customers, policyholders, partners, lenders, and
noncontrolling interests may fund or absorb costs that are not visible in the
headline company metric. The system must preserve the legal entity, period,
claim seniority, and cash destination before turning activity into common
owner cash.

## Valuation and macro overlay

Damodaran-style valuation should use lane-specific reinvestment rates and
returns on capital: service renewals and cost-to-serve for technology,
maintenance and cycle margins for materials, store productivity for retail,
loss-adjusted spread and capital for finance, project cash and throughput for
energy, and reserve life plus replacement capital for integrated oil and gas.
The panel provides observed inputs; it does not validate the forecast.

Lyn Alden-style stress should test liquidity and nominal-claim durability under
higher rates, tighter credit, inflation in labor/power/materials, weaker
consumer demand, lower commodity prices, reserve or geopolitical shocks, and
the inability to refinance or continue buybacks. A company passes the stress
screen only when its legal-entity cash and senior claims remain visible; a
large adjusted metric is not a substitute.

## Promotion rule

`same period + same legal entity + named cash/claim object + reconciliation path`

No lane is promoted or ranked from this panel. The next promotion must close a
company-specific denominator: service collection and cost, maintenance
capital, inventory/payables reversal, loss normalization, project cash, or
reserve replacement. If the source is not available, retain the lane as
qualified and state the exact missing document rather than filling the gap
with a proxy.

The current retail denominator lane shows why this rule matters. TJX supplies
a period-matched lease-cash amount but not a maintenance split; Target
separates eligible supplier-finance obligations from actual early payments and
records a large tariff refund; Walmart provides detailed capex categories and
defines FCF without debt service or acquisitions. These are useful control
points, not a comparable owner-cash ranking.
