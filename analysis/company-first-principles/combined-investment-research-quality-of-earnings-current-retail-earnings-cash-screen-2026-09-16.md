# Current retail earnings-to-cash quality screen

Research date: 2026-09-16

## Scope

This screen adds a matched current-period earnings comparison to the broader
[QoE overlay](combined-investment-research-quality-of-earnings-financial-shenanigans-overlay-2026-09-16.md).
It uses six-month reported net income, operating cash flow, and property
spending for TJX, Target, and Walmart. The Walmart numerator uses net income
attributable to Walmart rather than consolidated net income so the owner
denominator is explicit.

## Result

| Company | Period | Net income | Operating cash flow | Property spending | OCF / net income | Cash after property | Cash after property / net income |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| TJX | H1 FY2027 | $2.852B | $3.345B | $1.159B | 1.173x | $2.186B | 0.766x |
| Target | H1 2026 | $2.658B | $4.519B | $2.404B | 1.700x | $2.115B | 0.796x |
| Walmart | H1 FY2027 | $11.696B attributable | $19.710B | $14.181B | 1.685x | $5.529B | 0.473x |

These are reported-period conversion screens, not normalized owner cash. The
ratios reflect working-capital timing, taxes, leases, supplier finance,
temporary support, maintenance/growth capex, services, acquisitions, and
seasonality. They are therefore useful for directing the next QoE tests, not
for ranking the companies or alleging earnings management.

The Walmart result also preserves the noncontrolling-interest boundary:
consolidated net income is $12.019B, while $11.696B is attributable to Walmart.
Using the attributable numerator is more consistent with a common-owner screen,
but it does not solve Walmart's remaining owner-cash claims.
