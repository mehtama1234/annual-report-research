# Retail quality-of-earnings trend diagnostics

Research date: `2026-09-16`

## Purpose

This register turns the three controlled annual QoE vectors into a
falsifier-oriented follow-up queue. It compares each company's first and last
available annual observation for cash conversion, post-property cash,
contractual claims, and stock compensation. It is not a financial-shenanigans score,
fraud finding, or normalized owner-cash ranking; it is a diagnostic screen.

The structured [trend-diagnostics CSV](data/combined-investment-research-quality-of-earnings-retail-trend-diagnostics-2026-09-16.csv)
is the source of truth.

## Read-through

- **TJX — medium follow-up priority.** OCF/earnings and post-property cash
  conversion softened, but claims/OCF improved and SBC/earnings rose only
  slightly. The first follow-up is working-capital timing and temporary
  support, not an accusation of accrual manipulation.
- **Target — high follow-up priority.** OCF/earnings and post-property cash
  conversion weakened, claims/OCF rose from `2.24x` to `3.04x`, and SBC/NI
  increased. The next tests are the incomplete lease perimeter, supplier and
  inventory timing, support removal, and maintenance-versus-growth capital.
- **Walmart — high follow-up priority.** Post-property conversion weakened
  from `0.97x` to `0.68x` of attributable earnings, while SBC/NI rose from
  `13.5%` to `16.5%`. The next tests are supplier finance, capex intensity,
  consolidated-versus-attributable earnings, and OSG&A taxonomy.

## Promotion boundary

These flags prioritize document review. This diagnostic does not establish that earnings are misstated:
operating cash can move because of inventory cycles, payables,
capex timing, acquisitions, support items, tax, leases, or fiscal-calendar
effects. A company can only move from diagnostic follow-up to a thesis impact
after the relevant period-aligned source, denominator, accounting policy, and
cash perimeter are joined.
