## Interpretation boundary

Restaurant count is a scale/control-point denominator, not a valuation
denominator. Advertising expense and cooperative advertising expense are not
automatically corporate owner costs or owner cash: the analyst must establish
the fund's collection, restricted use, spending, and legal-entity perimeter.
The presence of a franchise-revenue tag likewise does not disclose royalty
margin, franchisee health, or collected cash.

The panel therefore advances the next evidence request to a matched schedule:
franchise royalties and fees; advertising-fund inflows/outflows; franchisee
receivables and support; company-store versus franchised unit economics;
maintenance/remodel capex; and cash remitted to the common parent after debt,
leases, acquisitions, taxes, SBC, and other claims.

Wingstop supplies a useful burden-shift diagnostic in its FY2025 filing: domestic
average unit volume was approximately `$2.0M`, while average initial investment
was approximately `$580,000`, excluding real-estate purchase or lease costs and
pre-opening expenses. This is not a franchisee IRR, payback, or franchisor
royalty-margin proof. The excluded real estate and pre-opening items are exactly
why the unit economics must remain a burden-carrier test until a matched
franchisee cash schedule is available.
