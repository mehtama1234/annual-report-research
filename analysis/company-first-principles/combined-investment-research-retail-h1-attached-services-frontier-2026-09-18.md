# Retail H1 attached-services frontier

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This pass aligns the latest H1 attached-service revenue and income surfaces
with the current retail cash denominator. It is a collection and burden
sensitivity, not an owner-cash estimate. The service pools are already inside
consolidated revenue or operating cash and must not be added to OCF again.

## Current cash denominator boundary

Target's H1 reported OCF-less-property screen is `$2.115B`; Walmart's is
`$5.529B`. The attached-service pools are consolidated operating inputs, not
additional amounts. A valid standalone service residual would require:

```text
service collection
  - service-specific labor / fulfillment / technology / overhead
  - working capital, tax, lease, and capital burden
  - external owner or funder claims
  - debt, SBC, dilution, and legal-entity remittance
```

No current public source joins those objects for either company. The safe use
of this frontier is to test how much reported valuation expectation would need
to be supported by service economics, not to increase owner cash.

## Promotion result

`Q-06-evidence-insufficient; CA-06-partial; no-ranking`

The next promotion object remains a same-period legal-entity schedule that
joins collection, allocated cost, capital, tax, and remittance. Target's TD
card route and Walmart's mixed membership-and-other perimeter must remain
separate.
