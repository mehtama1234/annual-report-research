# Retail Q2 2026 supplier-finance obligation refresh

## Finding

The latest official interim filings add current supplier-finance obligation
balances for Target and Walmart. These are important working-capital and
quality-of-earnings controls, but they are not cash-paid settlement amounts.

| Company | Current eligible/outstanding obligation | Opening comparable balance | Mechanical change | What the filing says |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Target, August 1, 2026 | $3.2B | $3.0B at January 31, 2026 | +$0.2B | Vendor obligations are included in accounts payable; early payment is at vendor discretion and does not change Target's remittance amount or payment date; actual early payments have historically been lower than the eligible balance. |
| Walmart, July 31, 2026 | $6.4B | $6.0B at January 31, 2026 | +$0.4B | Supplier financing is optional for suppliers; Walmart pays financial institutions the confirmed invoice amount on the due date; the balance is an outstanding payment obligation, not a settlement schedule. |

## Denominator and QoE implication

The balances demonstrate that accounts payable contains a meaningful supplier-
finance component. They do not prove that the entire balance supplied cash in
the H1 period, that the balance is permanent, or that it should be removed from
OCF. A payable increase and an eligible supplier-finance balance must not be
subtracted again from reported operating cash flow.

The correct next reconciliation is a same-period roll-forward containing
opening obligation, new confirmed invoices, invoices paid by the financial
institution, cash remitted by the retailer, early-payment elections, closing
obligation, payment-date terms, and any supplier-finance reclassification. The
current filings do not provide that complete settlement waterfall.

## Promotion consequence

This is a material Q-04 upgrade: supplier-finance perimeter and opening/closing
balances are now current-period visible for Target and Walmart. It remains
`settlement-unproven`, so CA-06 remains partial. TJX's current filing does not
provide a comparable supplier-finance note in the reviewed interim packet.
