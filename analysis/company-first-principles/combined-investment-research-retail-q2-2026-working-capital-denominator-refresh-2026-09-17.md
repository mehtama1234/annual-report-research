# Retail Q2 2026 working-capital denominator refresh

## Period and scope

This refresh uses the latest official interim filings: TJX and Target for the
26 weeks/six months ended August 1, 2026, and Walmart for the six months ended
July 31, 2026. These are company-specific current-period diagnostics and are
not pooled into a retailer ranking or substituted into older H1 periods.

## Same-period operating surface

| Company | OCF | Property spend | OCF less property | Inventory / cash-flow movement | Payables / cash-flow movement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TJX | $3.345B | $1.159B | $2.186B | $7.862B balance; $(603)M cash use | $5.024B balance; +$470M cash source |
| Target | $4.519B | $2.404B | $2.115B | $13.249B balance; $(945)M cash use | $13.306B balance; +$612M cash source |
| Walmart | $19.710B | $14.181B | $5.529B | $61.600B balance; $(2.660)B cash use | $64.318B balance; +$1.648B cash source |

The balances and cash-flow changes are different objects. A cash-flow inventory
use is not the same as the ending inventory balance, and a payable cash source
does not prove permanent supplier financing. The OCF-less-property figures are
reported screens, not normalized owner cash.

## What the refresh changes

The current period makes the working-capital burden visible. TJX and Target
both used cash for inventory while payables provided cash; Walmart's much larger
inventory use was partly offset by payables and was described as timing of
inventory receipts. These observations support a timing and QoE test, not a
manipulation conclusion. The correct next step is to follow the reversal across
subsequent periods and reconcile supplier-finance obligations and settlement.

Walmart explicitly defines its $5.529B figure as OCF less property and states
that the measure does not deduct debt service, contractual obligations, or
business acquisitions. That disclosure reinforces the archive boundary: even a
company-defined free-cash-flow measure is not automatically residual cash for
common owners.

## Lease, tax, and dilution controls

The same filings show lease liabilities and tax provisions or accrual
movements. TJX additionally reports `$1.147B` of H1 operating-lease cash paid,
while Target and Walmart provide no comparable interim cash-paid lease/tax
schedule and TJX cash taxes remain open. Target reports $154M of H1 share-based
compensation and $92M of shares withheld for taxes; TJX reports $85M of H1
share-based compensation; Walmart's diluted claims and financing flows remain
company-specific. These fields must not be deducted twice or treated as a
complete dilution bridge.

## CA-06 status

This refresh improves the period-matched denominator surface and strengthens
the QoE/financial-shenanigans controls around inventory, payables, temporary
timing, leases, taxes, and dilution. It does not close CA-06. Promotion still
requires maintenance-versus-growth capital, service-cost and collection
allocation, supplier-finance settlement, cash-paid burden where available, and
the diluted common-owner residual.
