# Combined Investment Research: Reviewer's Guide

Research date: `2026-09-17`

This guide is the shortest route through the current end-to-end research
system. It is intended for a skeptical reader who wants to inspect the actual
writeups, understand the recurring themes, and see where the evidence stops.

## Read the argument before the artifacts

The system asks one repeated question:

> What changed, who controls the bottleneck, who funds the burden, how does
> activity become owner cash, what does the price assume, and what document
> could prove the thesis wrong?

The current chain is:

```text
social behavior / macro force
  -> industry control point
  -> company or cohort
  -> operating model
  -> owner-cash denominator
  -> valuation expectation
  -> liquidity and legal-entity test
  -> named capital-flow proof
  -> falsifier and next document
```

The chain is connected, but not every link has the same proof grade. The
system deliberately preserves `proven`, `qualified`, `partial`, and
`unresolved` rather than turning a persuasive narrative into a complete
investment conclusion.

## Source-recovery and move-on rule

The system does not depend on recovering every earlier note, private workbook,
or inaccessible report. When a source cannot be recovered, the claim is kept
open and classified as `source-not-found` or `searched-negative` according to
the checked perimeter; it is never reconstructed from memory or promoted from
a nearby proxy. The next cycle should move to the highest-value joinable
object—a dated receipt, legal-entity cash schedule, facility ledger, settlement
record, valuation input, or filing-based falsifier. This keeps an unavailable
historical artifact from becoming either an unsupported negative conclusion or
a reason to repeat the same dead-end search.

## Five theme review cards

These cards are a fast way to review the argument before opening the detailed
writeups. They summarize the mechanism and the proof boundary; the linked
pilot, ledger, and next-evidence queue remain authoritative.

### Affordability and substitution

**Mechanism:** household price pressure changes product choice, channel mix,
traffic, and willingness to pay; value retailers may control the assortment
and price architecture that captures that shift.

**Supported now:** affordability signals are corroborated, the TJX/Target/
Walmart cohort shows relevant operating responses, and reported cash-after-
The attached-service review also separates Walmart's `$3.855B` segment
subtotal from its `$3.904B` consolidated membership-and-other-income amount;
the `$49M` difference remains a perimeter boundary in the [denominator
reconciliation](combined-investment-research-pilot-02-walmart-attached-income-denominator-reconciliation-2026-09-16.md).

**Promotion gate:** prove durable common-owner cash after inventory and
supplier-finance normalization, maintenance capital, leases, taxes, attached-
service costs, support reversal, and dilution.

### Scarce physical assets and contractual control

**Mechanism:** a streaming contract can secure exposure to scarce, long-lived
production while the operator bears mine labor, sustaining capital, permitting,
and operating risk.

**Supported now:** Wheaton’s Antamina transaction has a two-sided upfront cash
confirmation, a named BHP-PMPA counterparty route, contractual entitlement,
production and reserve proxies, and financing/threshold sensitivities.

The latest official Q2 refresh adds a combined Antamina observation of `2.319M`
attributable silver ounces produced and `2.063M` sold, alongside BHP's
recipient-side `$4.300B` receipt and metal-credit confirmation. Those are
period and counterparty controls, not BHP-only delivery, invoice, settlement,
or Wheaton receipt proof; see the [post-Q2 settlement search refresh](capital-flow-wheaton-antamina-post-q2-public-search-refresh-2026-09-16.md).

**Promotion gate:** join BHP-only payable ounces to a dated metal-credit
settlement, receipt, reserve-backed delivery curve, taxes, debt allocation,
and a source-backed IRR or NPV.

### Institutional capital and financial intermediation

**Mechanism:** capital moves through origination platforms, insurance
liabilities, private-credit wrappers, borrowers, and related legal entities;
reported platform earnings are not automatically parent cash.

**Supported now:** Apollo/Athene/ARI has visible fee, spread, statutory,
related-party, seller-side cash, debt, buyer-side acquisition, and parent-use
boundaries.

The Q2 AMAPS 1 wrapper exposure also supports an approximately `$92.1M`
mechanical liability-cost sensitivity when allocated against the reconciled
bond base and H1 cost-of-funds pool. It is a denominator diagnostic, not
settlement, custodian remittance, asset-level return, or Apollo cash.

**Promotion gate:** identify borrower collections and collateral proceeds,
allocate liability/funding cost and regulated capital, prove the parent
receiving account and intercompany elimination, then calculate the common-owner
residual.

### Valuation and reinvestment

**Mechanism:** value depends on the cash denominator, growth investment,
duration, risk, and terminal economics—not on a headline multiple alone.

**Supported now:** the three workbenches expose price-implied cash, earnings,
reinvestment, and duration burdens using Damodaran-style methods.

**Promotion gate:** replace illustrative denominators with normalized,
business-specific owner cash and rerun valuation ranges after the relevant
liability, tax, capital, and dilution claims are joined.

### Macro and liquidity transmission

**Mechanism:** inflation, wages, rates, credit availability, policy, funding
cost, collateral, and policyholder behavior transmit into demand, margins,
capital access, solvency, and valuation through company-specific channels.

**Supported now:** current-regime and 2020–2026 historical panels connect
official macro observations to pilot-specific tests and falsifiers. The live
marker set now includes the `3.75%–4.00%` federal-funds target, `3.4%` headline
CPI, `2.4%` core CPI, `16.3%` energy inflation, and a `3.0%` saving rate.

**Promotion gate:** demonstrate repeated, period-aligned responses while
separating fiscal support, company actions, seasonality, and other confounds;
the current panels remain descriptive rather than causal.
