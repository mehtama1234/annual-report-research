# Search, advertising, and cloud platform valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Alphabet from search, advertising, and AI-demand evidence
into a company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps Google
Services, YouTube, advertising traffic acquisition, Google Cloud, shared AI
research, Other Bets, data-center capex, regulation, SBC, and diluted common
cash separate from Amazon commerce, Oracle database cloud, and independent ad
measurement.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alphabet search/advertising/cloud platform | Services attention and advertising cash plus Cloud cash after traffic acquisition, AI research, servers, networking, data centers, power, depreciation, regulation, Other Bets, SBC, and dilution | Technical infrastructure, AI/Cloud servers and power, R&D/talent, traffic acquisition, data centers, support, acquisitions, Other Bets, SBC replacement, and repurchases | Search monetization or user behavior shift, AI query economics, advertiser slowdown, data-center overbuild, power/chip constraints, antitrust remedy, Cloud price pressure, or dilution | Revenue and operating income grow while incremental AI/Cloud return, traffic cost, capex utilization, regulatory cash, or diluted per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$402.836B`, operating income `$129.039B`, OCF about
  `$164.7B`, and capex `$91.4B`; the cash-after-capex screen was about
  `$73.3B` before acquisitions, debt, SBC, and other claims.
- Google Services revenue was `$342.721B` and operating income `$139.404B`;
  Google Cloud revenue `$58.705B` and operating income `$13.910B`; Other Bets
  operating loss was approximately `$7.515B` and Alphabet-level activities
  loss `$16.760B`.
- FY2025 advertising revenue was `$294.691B`, including Search and other
  `$224.532B` and YouTube ads `$40.367B`; paid clicks rose `6%` and cost per
  click `7%`.
- Advertising traffic-acquisition costs were `20.3%` of the relevant advertising
  base; distribution payments and mobile defaults remain part of Search economics.
- Q2 2026 revenue was about `$119.8B`; management raised 2026 capex guidance to
  approximately `$195B–$205B` and indicated 2027 spending would increase again.
- FY2025 SBC was `$24.953B` and repurchases `$45.4B`; AI infrastructure return
  must be measured after equity compensation and per-share claims.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate Search, YouTube, Network, subscriptions, devices, and Cloud by
   revenue, margin, traffic cost, infrastructure, and shared-AI expense.
2. Track paid clicks, cost per click, user/query behavior, ad formats, external
   links, traffic acquisition, and cost to serve under AI search.
3. Reconcile Cloud revenue and margin to servers, power, networking,
   depreciation, utilization, customer contracts, and incremental capex return.
4. Allocate shared AI research and Alphabet-level costs transparently; do not
   assign costs away from the business that funds them.
5. Keep Other Bets, antitrust remedies, legal cash, SBC, repurchases, debt,
   capex, and diluted shares in the common-owner denominator.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what Search/YouTube monetization,
traffic cost, Cloud utilization and margin, AI capex return, R&D, Other Bets,
reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. Legacy advertising
cash cannot be capitalized unchanged if the new infrastructure has lower
returns.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether users, advertisers, and cloud
customers continue funding the platform through higher rates, AI interface
changes, power/chip scarcity, regulation, antitrust remedies, and data-center
overbuild. Liquidity passes only when capex, legal claims, SBC, and common
residual remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`search-advertising-cloud-qualified; ai-capex-and-monetization-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from users, clicks, ad revenue,
Cloud usage, and contracts to traffic acquisition, cost to serve, utilization,
capex, depreciation, shared-AI R&D, regulation, Other Bets, SBC, and diluted
common residual. Revenue, operating income, clicks, CPC, Cloud growth, OCF,
FCF, and repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
