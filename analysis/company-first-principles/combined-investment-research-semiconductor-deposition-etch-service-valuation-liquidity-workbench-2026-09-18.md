# Semiconductor deposition, etch, and installed-service valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Lam Research from semiconductor-equipment and process-
complexity evidence into a company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter.
It keeps deposition, etch, clean, systems, customer support, acceptance,
deferred revenue, Japan-held inventory, customer concentration, China/export
exposure, R&D, labs, supplier commitments, and diluted common-owner cash
separate from ASML lithography, KLA process control, and electronic-test models.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lam Research deposition/etch and installed service | Collected systems and support cash after tool qualification, acceptance, R&D, labs, inventory, supplier commitments, capacity, export compliance, SBC, and dilution | Process R&D, field service, labs, manufacturing capacity, inventory, Japan-held tools, customer deposits, supplier commitments, capex, and share replacement | Wafer-fab capex pause, memory/logic cycle, China restrictions, customer concentration, delayed acceptance, inventory build, deferred-profit reversal, or export-control cost | Systems and support revenue rise while customer acceptance, deferred revenue, inventory, China exposure, service attachment, reinvestment, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Fiscal 2026 revenue was `$23.233B`, operating cash flow `$5.858B`, and
  capital/investing cash use approximately `$922M`, leaving a simple screen of
  about `$4.935B` before a fuller lifecycle normalization.
- Customer-support revenue was `$8.347B`, or `35.9%` of sales; systems revenue
  was `$14.885B`. The two streams have different cycle and reinvestment profiles.
- China represented `34%` of fiscal 2026 revenue, and four customers represented
  `16%`, `15%`, `12%`, and `12%`. Policy and customer concentration are linked
  rather than independent risks.
- Fiscal 2026 R&D was `$2.376B`; capital and investing use rose to `$922.2M`.
  These are necessary process and capacity investments, not automatically
  discretionary uses that can be added back to owner cash.
- The company recognized `$2.09B` of opening deferred revenue during fiscal
  2026; Japanese shipments remain in inventory until customer acceptance. Cash,
  revenue, acceptance, and inventory therefore require a joint bridge.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate systems from customer support, and foundry, memory, and logic
   exposure. Installed-base service is durable only if customers keep tools in
   production and continue buying upgrades and parts.
2. Reconcile shipments, customer acceptance, deferred revenue, Japanese-held
   inventory, receivables, customer deposits, and supplier terms. Do not treat
   down-payments or deferred-profit timing as recurring cash conversion.
3. Track China and export-control effects as recurring structural economics,
   including licensing, substitution, tariffs, compliance, and product mix.
4. Include R&D, field support, labs, capacity, inventory, commitments, and
   capitalized or expensed platform investment in the replacement denominator.
5. Show SBC, repurchases, debt, dividends, and diluted shares together. Buybacks
   do not prove per-share value if they merely offset equity compensation.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what process intensity, systems growth,
service attachment, acceptance timing, gross margin, R&D, capacity investment,
China mix, customer concentration, terminal growth, and cost of capital the
valuation requires. A high multiple on a cycle peak must survive a lower-tool-
spend scenario.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether fab financing, memory pricing,
customer utilization, inventory, export restrictions, rates, and supplier
commitments preserve liquidity through a semiconductor downturn. Liquidity passes
only when acceptance, deferred revenue, inventory, customer deposits, R&D,
capacity, and common residual remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`semiconductor-deposition-etch-qualified; acceptance-cycle-and-China-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from systems and support
revenue to customer acceptance, deferred revenue, Japan-held inventory,
receivables, customer deposits, R&D, capacity, supplier commitments, China and
export costs, SBC replacement, and diluted common residual. Revenue, support
growth, OCF, FCF, dividends, and repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
