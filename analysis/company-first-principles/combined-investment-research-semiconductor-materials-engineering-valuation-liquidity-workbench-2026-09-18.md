# Semiconductor materials-engineering valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Applied Materials from semiconductor-cycle evidence into a
qualified process, installed-service, customer-concentration, export-control,
and common-cash test. It keeps Applied distinct from Lam Research's deposition/
etch service model and KLA's process-control lane.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Applied Materials | Qualified deposition, etch, thermal, CMP, inspection, packaging, and AGS service cash after customer acceptance, contract liabilities, inventory, R&D, facility capex, China/export, concentration, SBC, and dilution | Process R&D, field engineering, service/spares, EPIC/facilities, manufacturing capacity, inventory, software, and customer qualification | Semiconductor capex downturn, China licensing restriction, customer concentration, inventory obsolescence, shipment timing, contract-liability reversal, or dilution | Tool revenue, AGS, contract liabilities, or margin rise while customer acceptance, service attachment, inventory, China exposure, capex, or per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$28.368B`, operating cash flow `$7.958B`, capex `$2.260B`,
  and OCF less capex `$5.698B`, down from `$7.594B` in 2023.
- Semiconductor Systems was `$20.798B` and AGS approximately `$6.4B` in
  FY2025. China was `$8.529B`, or `30%` of FY2025 revenue.
- First nine months of FY2026 operating cash flow was `$5.568B`, capex `$1.988B`,
  and contract liabilities increased `$698M` to `$3.271B`; opening-liability
  revenue recognition was approximately `$1.8B`.
- Two customers represented approximately `34%` of first-nine-month FY2026
  revenue. FY2025 SBC was `$660M`; first-nine-month FY2026 SBC was `$545M`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile tool revenue through shipment, customer acceptance, contract
   liabilities, receivables, collections, gross margin, and service obligations.
2. Separate Semiconductor Systems mix by foundry/logic, DRAM, NAND, packaging,
   and customer cycle; keep AGS service/spares distinct.
3. Test contract-liability growth for legitimate advance billings versus future
   delivery and margin obligations.
4. Track inventory by spares, raw materials, WIP, finished goods, write-downs,
   customer commitments, and technology transitions.
5. Normalize China/export restrictions, customer concentration, R&D, facility
   capex, SBC, buybacks, and diluted shares.
6. Treat tool revenue, AGS, contract liabilities, margins, OCF, and repurchases
   as diagnostic until qualified-process and per-share cash are evidenced.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test converts qualified process position, tool
placements, customer utilization, technology transitions, AGS attachment,
R&D, capex, contract conversion, SBC, and diluted shares into owner cash. The
Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether semiconductor capex, memory/foundry
cycles, export rules, China competition, rates, and customer concentration
preserve the installed-process control point. Technical scarcity does not
remove cyclical capital intensity.

## Promotion boundary

`semiconductor-materials-qualified; process-acceptance-and-service-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from process qualification,
tool shipment/acceptance, customer utilization, AGS service, contract
liabilities, inventory, China/export, R&D, capex, customer concentration, SBC,
and diluted common residual. Revenue, AGS, contract liabilities, margins, OCF,
and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.

## September 18, 2026 valuation expectation refresh

The current market snapshot reports Applied Materials equity value of
approximately `$355.656B` at `$444.57` per share. Against the first nine months
of FY2026, operating cash less capex was `$3.580B`; annualizing that nine-month
screen gives approximately `$4.773B` and a mechanical market-cap / screen of
approximately `74.5x`.

This is not normalized owner cash. The nine-month period includes a `$698M`
increase in contract liabilities and approximately `$1.8B` of revenue
recognized from opening contract liabilities; those are delivery and timing
controls, not free cash. China represented about `30%` of FY2025 revenue,
two customers represented approximately `34%` of first-nine-month FY2026
revenue, and first-nine-month FY2026 SBC was `$545M`. R&D, field service,
inventory, qualification, export restrictions, facility investment, SBC, debt,
and dilution remain outside the mechanical screen.

The market-implied burden is therefore a requirement for durable qualified
process and AGS cash after acceptance, delivery, customer concentration, China
policy, and reinvestment—not evidence that reported OCF or contract-liability
growth is distributable owner cash.
