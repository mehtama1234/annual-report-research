# Senior-living operator valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Brookdale Senior Living from aging-demand evidence into a
company-specific valuation and liquidity perimeter. It keeps occupied units,
RevPAR, resident rate and care intensity, staffing, leases, community capital,
quality, affordability, and debt separate from Welltower's property ownership
and HCA's acute-care operating model.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brookdale senior-living operator | Community cash after occupied-unit revenue, resident affordability, care labor, food/supplies, leases, property maintenance, life-safety, quality, debt, taxes, and dilution | Community maintenance/renovation, staffing and training, technology, lease obligations, working capital, acquisitions, insurance, debt, and common capital | Occupancy decline, rate affordability, wage/agency inflation, staffing shortage, quality/regulatory event, lease renewal, capex underfunding, rates, or refinancing | Occupancy, RevPAR, and adjusted EBITDA rise while community contribution, labor coverage, maintenance cash, lease-adjusted debt, or diluted per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$3.18B`, operating income `$131.7M`, adjusted EBITDA
  `$450.3M`, and same-community RevPAR rose `7.6%`; continuing-operations net
  loss was approximately `$(263.5M)`.
- FY2025 OCF was `$218.0M`, capex `$201.5M`, and asset acquisitions `$311.0M`;
  the mechanical residual after those listed uses was approximately
  `$(294.5M)`.
- Brookdale operated `647` communities in `41` states serving about `58,000`
  residents. Q1 2026 weighted average occupancy reached `80.0%` and same-
  community RevPAR rose `5.9%`.
- Occupancy does not prove owner cash: incremental residents require staffing,
  food, supplies, utilities, maintenance, quality, collection, and lease or
  property obligations.
- Care intensity, affordability, agency labor, turnover, resident retention,
  life-safety, and community capital remain open denominators.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Follow each incremental occupied unit through resident rate, care mix,
   staffing, food/supplies, utilities, maintenance, marketing, collection, and
   community cash.
2. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA to GAAP earnings, interest, leases, impairment,
   restructuring, maintenance/development capex, acquisitions, and dilution.
3. Separate rate increases from volume, retention, care mix, move-ins, and
   affordability; higher RevPAR can reduce demand or increase care burden.
4. Track wage rates, agency labor, turnover, open positions, training,
   staffing coverage, quality events, complaints, and regulatory costs.
5. Charge lease payments, life-safety and renovation capital, insurance, taxes,
   debt service, acquisitions, and common-share claims before capital returns.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what normalized occupancy, resident
rate, care intensity, community contribution, labor cost, lease burden,
maintenance capital, acquisition return, and cost of capital are required.
Value the operator on community cash, not occupancy or EBITDA alone.

The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether residents and families can fund
senior living through inflation, labor shortages, rates, housing/wealth shocks,
insurance cost, and weaker affordability. Liquidity passes only when staffing,
leases, maintenance, debt, quality, and common residual remain visible.

## Promotion boundary

`senior-living-operator-qualified; occupancy-labor-and-property-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from occupied units and
RevPAR to community contribution, care labor, collection, resident affordability,
lease cash, maintenance capital, quality, debt, acquisitions, and diluted
common residual. Occupancy, RevPAR, adjusted EBITDA, OCF, FCF, dividends, and
repurchases remain diagnostic inputs.
