# Shentel regional fiber buildout valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench tests Shenandoah Telecommunications as a regional fiber-build and local-connectivity platform. It keeps homes passed, penetration, Glo Fiber additions, incumbent broadband runoff, grants, carrier/backhaul exposure, capex, debt, and diluted common residual separate from revenue and adjusted EBITDA.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shentel | Local fiber-network cash after construction, market penetration, customer acquisition, grants, legacy broadband, carrier contracts, maintenance capex, debt, and diluted common residual | Fiber build, homes passed, network upgrades, customer equipment, grants/milestones, spectrum/backhaul, maintenance, working capital, debt, and shares | Penetration delay, build-cost inflation, grant compliance, legacy video/DSL decline, carrier churn, pricing pressure, liquidity, or refinancing | Fiber revenue and homes passed rise while penetration, ARPU, capex conversion, grant cash, legacy runoff, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$357.9M`, adjusted EBITDA `$119.1M`, cash `$27.3M`, long-term debt `$642.4M`, and capex `$358.9M`.
- Glo Fiber passed about `427,000` homes and businesses; expansion-market revenue grew `42.7%` to `$82.6M` in FY2025.
- The company issued a `$567.4M` secured fiber-network revenue term note intended to fund Glo Fiber completion and reduce annual cash interest by more than `$10M`.
- Q2 2026 Glo Fiber revenue was `$26.3M`, the company added its `100,000th` Glo Fiber customer, fiber businesses represented `51%` of revenue, and grants totaled `$151.2M` for unserved and middle-mile projects.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile homes passed to customer additions, penetration, churn, ARPU, installation cost, and collected cash.
2. Separate Glo Fiber growth from incumbent video/DSL runoff, carrier/backhaul churn, grants, and market cancellations.
3. Test capex, grant milestones, project write-offs, and debt service against the timing of positive free cash flow.
4. Keep term notes, guarantees, stock compensation, buybacks, and diluted shares in the common-owner residual schedule.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what homes passed, penetration, ARPU, network margin, capex intensity, grant support, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether regional households, enterprises, public grants, and carrier contracts can fund a capital-heavy fiber build through competition, rates, construction inflation, and legacy-service decline.

## Promotion boundary

`shentel-qualified; regional-fiber-penetration-and-buildout-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from passings and connections to collections, penetration, grant cash, build capex, legacy runoff, carrier contracts, debt, claims, and diluted common residual. Revenue, homes passed, adjusted EBITDA, guidance, OCF, and fiber growth remain diagnostic inputs.
