# Sonoco packaging platform valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Sonoco from current-period evidence into a company-specific
valuation, packaging-format, portfolio, acquisition, paper, metal, working-capital,
debt, and common-owner cash test. It does not pool Consumer Packaging and Industrial
Paper Packaging into a generic container or paper multiple.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sonoco | Collected multi-format packaging cash after paperboard, metal, recycled fiber, flexible/specialty formats, plant utilization, price-cost, acquisitions, divestitures, capex, debt, and dilution | Consumer packaging formats, industrial paper, mills, converting, protective packaging, acquisition integration, portfolio simplification, and working capital | Volume/mix decline, paper and metal input costs, plant disruption, acquisition integration, customer concentration, restructuring, debt, or dilution | Adjusted EBITDA and operating cash rise while format volume, price-cost, integration, capex, leverage, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Sonoco operated approximately `265` locations in `37` countries; 2025 included Consumer Packaging and Industrial Paper Packaging plus major Eviosys acquisition and ThermoSafe divestiture effects.
- Full-year 2025 GAAP net income attributable to Sonoco was `$1.003B` and GAAP operating profit `$1.018B`, requiring normalization for portfolio actions and acquisition effects.
- Q2 2026 sales were `$1.9B`, GAAP net income `$105M`, adjusted net income `$151M`, adjusted operating profit `$242M`, and adjusted EBITDA `$324M`.
- Q2 North America uncoated recycled paperboard trade volume grew `6%` and EMEA/APAC paper-can volume `9%`; management highlighted record second-quarter operating cash flow.
- FY2026 guidance included adjusted EBITDA of `$1.25B-$1.35B` and operating cash flow of `$700M-$800M`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile Consumer Packaging and Industrial Paper Packaging to format volume,
   paperboard, metal, recycled fiber, price-cost, mix, customer contracts, and
   collections.
2. Separate metal packaging, rigid paper, flexible/specialty, paper cans,
   protective packaging, mills, and industrial paper by margin, utilization, and
   capital burden.
3. Keep Eviosys integration, ThermoSafe divestiture, plant maintenance, mill
   capital, restructuring, inventory, working capital, debt, and taxes in the
   owner-cash denominator.
4. Test whether adjusted measures exclude recurring acquisition, restructuring,
   impairment, environmental, pension, or integration burdens.
5. Reconcile operating cash flow, acquisition and divestiture proceeds, debt,
   interest, dividends, repurchases, SBC, and diluted shares before calling FCF
   surplus common-owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what packaging volume, format mix,
price-cost, plant utilization, acquisition return, portfolio savings, capex,
leverage, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress
test asks whether packaged-goods demand, recycled fiber, paperboard, metal, energy,
freight, customer budgets, and refinancing can preserve liquidity through a
packaging cycle.

## Promotion boundary

`sonoco-packaging-platform-qualified; format-portfolio-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from packaging formats and plants
to customer collections, input costs, utilization, acquisition return, divestiture
effects, required capex, debt, claims, and diluted common residual. Sales, adjusted
EBITDA, guidance, operating cash flow, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
