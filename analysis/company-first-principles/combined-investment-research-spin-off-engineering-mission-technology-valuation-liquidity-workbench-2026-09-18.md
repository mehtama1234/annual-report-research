# Spin-off engineering and mission-technology valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves KBR from backlog and mission-demand evidence into a
company-specific valuation and liquidity object. It keeps Mission Technology
Solutions (MTS), Sustainable Technology Solutions (STS), contract type,
affiliate cash, and planned separation economics separate from defense mission
systems and specialty-construction contractors.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KBR | MTS and STS cash after funded-backlog conversion, contract estimates, labor/subcontractors, working capital, affiliates, pension, debt, separation costs, and dilution | Cleared labor, engineering/process IP, fixed-price project support, contract assets/receivables, capex, pension, leases, guarantees, spin-off systems, and common capital | Federal appropriation/procurement delay, industrial capex deferral, fixed-price overrun, affiliate cash restriction, separation financing, stranded cost, debt, or dilution | Backlog and adjusted EPS rise while funded conversion, contract margin, collected cash, standalone cost, debt service, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$7.786B`, backlog `$16.864B`, backlog plus options `$23.211B`, OCF `$557M`, PP&E purchases `$42M`, and recurring-capital residual about `$515M`.
- FY2025 revenue included about `$3.305B` cost-reimbursable, `$2.311B` time-and-materials, and `$2.170B` fixed-price work; backlog is not a uniform cash asset.
- Q2 2026 revenue was `$1.984B`, backlog plus options `$23.0B`, and book-to-bill `1.1x`; MTS revenue was `$1.308B` and STS `$676M`.
- Spin-off costs were `$31M` in Q2 and `$46M` year to date. Management plans to spin off MTS on January 4, 2027, with financing and a distribution intended to reduce KBR debt.
- Latest packet evidence showed cash about `$312M` and long-term debt above `$2.5B`; Q2 six-month OCF was only about `$2M` after project and working-capital timing.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Separate funded backlog from options and remaining performance obligations; join backlog to revenue, contract assets, receivables, margin, and collections.
2. Reconcile cost-reimbursable, time-and-materials, and fixed-price work; test estimate changes, claims, change orders, loss provisions, and cost-to-complete.
3. Distinguish consolidated earnings and affiliate distributions from cash legally available to KBR common owners; track capital calls, guarantees, and partner claims.
4. Treat spin-off charges as a cash-paid persistence test; identify duplicated systems, facilities, executive costs, stranded corporate expense, and standalone public-company costs.
5. Model MTS and STS separately through the separation financing, debt allocation, pension, leases, guarantees, working capital, and capex.
6. Reconcile adjusted EBITDA/EPS and operating cash conversion to GAAP cash, debt service, pension, separation, SBC, repurchases, and dividends.
7. Treat backlog, options, adjusted EPS, and buybacks as diagnostic inputs, not normalized owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what funded conversion, contract mix,
segment margin, affiliate cash, standalone overhead, separation cost, debt,
reinvestment, and cost of capital the post-spin valuation requires. The Lyn
Alden-style stress test asks whether government budgets, industrial capex,
appropriations, rates, and refinancing can support both entities while cash is
split and financing claims rise.

## Promotion boundary

`spin-off-engineering-mission-technology-qualified; separation-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from funded work to segment
revenue, contract type, margin, billing, collection, affiliate availability,
separation cost, standalone debt and pension, capital requirements, and diluted
common residual. Backlog, options, RPO, adjusted earnings, OCF, and buybacks
remain diagnostic inputs.
