# Online K–12, career learning, and institutional education valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Stride from current-period education evidence into a company-specific valuation, enrollment, public-funding, district-contract, career-learning, curriculum, regulatory, and owner-cash test. It treats Stride as an institutional education-delivery platform, not as a consumer course marketplace or conventional university.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stride | Collected per-pupil, district, career-learning, adult, and employer education cash after instructional labor, curriculum/software delivery, student support, contract compliance, public funding, acquisitions, debt, and dilution | Curriculum and software, teachers and support labor, student services, career pathways, school operations, customer acquisition, regulatory compliance, acquisitions, and working capital | Enrollment, per-pupil funding, district renewal, state rules, audits, reputational risk, Career Learning mix, student persistence, labor, impairment, debt, and dilution | Revenue, enrollment, revenue per enrollment, Career Learning growth, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, or buybacks rise while funding, persistence, contract renewal, instructional cost, regulatory compliance, impairment, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$2.405B`, income from operations `$360.1M`, net income `$287.9M`, and adjusted EBITDA `$571.0M`; the year included `$59.5M` of Galvanize impairment charges.
- Q4 FY2026 revenue was `$636.1M`, operating income `$105.9M`, net income `$81.4M`, diluted EPS `$1.75`, adjusted operating income `$117.8M`, and adjusted EBITDA `$149.8M`.
- Q4 FY2026 average enrollments were `234.2K`, Career Learning enrollments `106.4K`, revenue per enrollment `$2,620`, and cash plus marketable securities `$1.034B`; fiscal 2026 capex was `$78.8M` and repurchases about `$188.7M`.
- Q3 FY2026 revenue was `$629.9M` and adjusted EBITDA `$171.3M`; Q2 revenue was `$631.3M`, adjusted EBITDA `$188.1M`, enrollments `248.5K`, and Career Learning enrollments `111.5K`.
- Q2 FY2026 Career Learning revenue rose `24.5%` while General Education revenue fell `3.6%`; this mix shift is central to the quality-of-growth test.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile enrollment, attendance or persistence, per-pupil funding, district billing, career tuition, employer and government contracts, collection, refunds, and instructional delivery; enrollments are not cash.
2. Separate General Education, Career Learning, adult programs, employer, government, district, and Galvanize cohorts; funding, margins, and regulatory risk differ.
3. Test curriculum/software investment, student support, school operations, and acquisitions through persistence, outcomes, contract renewal, compliance, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep adjusted EBITDA, FCF, revenue per enrollment, Career Learning growth, impairments, and buybacks separate from recurring collected common-owner cash.
5. Reconcile teacher and support labor, curriculum, technology, funding receivables, regulatory reserves, debt, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what enrollment, persistence, per-pupil funding, Career Learning mix, student outcomes, software leverage, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether public budgets, state regulation, district renewals, labor cost, demographic changes, reputational events, rates, and debt preserve Stride's cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`stride-online-education-qualified; enrollment-funding-career-learning-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from student, district, career, and employer cohorts to funding or tuition collection, instructional cost, persistence, contract renewal, compliance, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Enrollment, revenue per enrollment, Career Learning growth, adjusted EBITDA, FCF, impairments, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
