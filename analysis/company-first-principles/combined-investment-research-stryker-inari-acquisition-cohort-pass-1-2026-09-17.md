# Stryker–Inari acquisition cohort — pass 1

Research date: `2026-09-17`

The [structured cohort register](data/combined-investment-research-stryker-inari-acquisition-cohort-pass-1-2026-09-17.csv)
extracts the FY2025 Inari purchase-accounting surface from Stryker's preserved
10-K. This is an acquisition-burden and return-input artifact, not a claim
that Inari was a bad acquisition or that goodwill is an immediate cash cost.

## Purchase allocation

Stryker reports total Inari consideration of approximately `$4.810B`, net of
cash acquired. The purchase allocation includes:

| Component | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Accounts receivable | `$78M` |
| Inventory | `$215M` |
| Deferred tax assets | `$59M` |
| Other assets | `$84M` |
| Deferred tax liabilities | `$(486M)` |
| Other liabilities | `$(191M)` |
| Developed technologies | `$1.458B` |
| Customer relationships | `$330M` |
| Other intangibles | `$72M` |
| Goodwill | `$3.191B` |

Identified intangibles total approximately `$1.860B`, or roughly 38.7% of
reported consideration. Goodwill is approximately `$3.191B`, or roughly
66.3%. Together, goodwill and identified intangibles are approximately
`$5.051B`, more than the net consideration because the purchase allocation
also includes acquired assets, liabilities, and deferred-tax effects. The
important analytical consequence is not that goodwill should be subtracted from
annual cash; it is that future value depends on the acquired clinical platform
earning enough to justify a large intangible and goodwill balance.

The reported weighted-average amortization period for developed technologies
and customer relationships is approximately 13 years. Stryker also recorded a
separate `$139M` charge for Inari employee share-based awards that vested upon
the acquisition. Those awards are an acquisition-related economic burden and
must not disappear from the post-close return analysis merely because they are
presented in selling, general, and administrative expense.

## Return bridge still required

The purchase allocation does not show whether Inari earns its cost. The next
period bridge must identify, where disclosed:

```text
Inari / Vascular revenue
  -> gross and operating margin after amortization
  -> integration, quality, regulatory, and commercial costs
  -> inventory and receivable cash conversion
  -> incremental R&D and sales/service burden
  -> debt, dilution, and acquisition-related claims
  -> post-close cash return and incremental ROIC
```

Stryker's 2025 filing describes Inari's venous-thromboembolism portfolio and
the creation of the Vascular business, but the local evidence does not yet
provide a clean Inari-only revenue, margin, or cash series. Consolidated
Vascular growth therefore cannot be assigned to Inari without a segment or
management disclosure that supports the attribution.

## Quality-of-earnings implications

This cohort creates four falsifiable checks:

1. goodwill and acquired-technology assumptions must be tested against later
   revenue, margin, impairment, and clinical adoption;
2. the 13-year amortization burden must be carried in GAAP economics even if
   an adjusted-EPS presentation excludes it;
3. the `$139M` vested-award charge and subsequent dilution must be joined to
   owner returns; and
4. acquired inventory and receivables must convert into sales and collected
   cash without relying on consolidated Stryker working-capital timing.
