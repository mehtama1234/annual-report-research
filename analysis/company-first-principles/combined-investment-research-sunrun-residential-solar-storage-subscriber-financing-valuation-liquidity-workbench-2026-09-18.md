# Residential solar and storage subscriber-financing valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Sunrun from current-period thematic evidence into a company-specific valuation, reinvestment, financing, and owner-cash test. It treats Sunrun as a residential distributed-energy and subscriber-financing model, not as a utility-scale module manufacturer or regulated utility.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunrun | Contracted household-energy cash after installation, battery deployment, customer payments, service, churn, tax credits, safe-harbor investments, financing, debt, and dilution | Panels, batteries, inverters, installation labor, customer acquisition, service/software, working capital, warranty, tax-equity and project-finance requirements | Rates, household credit/churn, tax-credit policy, net-metering changes, installation cost, battery performance, securitization/project-finance access, and recourse debt | Subscriber value and storage growth rise while installation/collection cash, customer credit, tax-credit support, battery economics, financing access, debt, or diluted per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Q2 2026 total revenue was about `$870.0M`; Aggregate Subscriber Value was about `$1.175B`; Aggregate Contracted Subscriber Value was about `$1.089B`; reported cash generation was `$23M`, or about `$45M` excluding net safe-harbor investments; and Contracted Net Earning Assets were about `$3.677B`.
- Q2 added about `20,979` customers and `19,793` subscribers, reached about `4.647 GWh` of networked storage capacity, reported a `74%` storage attachment rate, and ended with about `1.206M` customers.
- Management reduced 2026 Aggregate Subscriber Value guidance to `$4.6B`–`$4.9B` from `$4.8B`–`$5.2B` and cash-generation guidance to `$200M`–`$375M` from `$250M`–`$450M`, citing affiliate-channel volume, delayed direct-sales ramp, and higher costs.
- Q1 2026 reported negative `$59M` cash generation, or negative `$31M` excluding safe-harbor investments; project-finance timing shifted from Q1 into Q2, and Sunrun paid down `$92M` of recourse debt.
- FY2025 cash generation was `$377M`; Q4 contracted net value creation was `$176M`; year-end Contracted Net Earning Assets were about `$3.571B`, or `$15.28` per share, including about `$1.2B` of total cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile Aggregate Subscriber Value and Contracted Net Earning Assets to installed systems, customer payments, churn, credit losses, service obligations, tax-equity claims, project-finance cash, and the common residual.
2. Keep cash generation separate from safe-harbor investments, tax credits, project-finance timing, and non-recourse or securitized funding; timing support is not recurring owner cash.
3. Test affiliate and direct-sales economics through customer-acquisition cost, installation completion, cancellation, collection, warranty, and battery attachment rather than treating subscriber additions as cash.
4. Separate storage attachment and networked capacity from battery utilization, grid-services revenue, replacement needs, operating cost, and customer consent/compensation.
5. Reconcile recourse debt paydown, tax equity, securitization, restricted cash, and any share issuance before treating contracted value as common-owner value.
6. Treat buybacks or per-share contracted-asset claims as residual claims requiring diluted-share and funding validation, not as proof of surplus cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what subscriber additions, storage attachment, churn, customer payment performance, installation margin, tax-credit realization, financing cost, reinvestment rate, and cost of capital the contracted-asset valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether households can keep funding contracts through rate, affordability, utility-bill, grid-reliability, policy, and employment shocks, and whether Sunrun can fund batteries, service, warranties, and project-finance obligations without recourse-liquidity stress.

## Promotion boundary

`sunrun-residential-solar-qualified; installation-collection-financing-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from customer additions and installed systems to customer collections, churn/credit, storage utilization, tax-credit and financing treatment, required reinvestment, debt, claims, and diluted common residual. Revenue, Subscriber Value, Contracted Net Earning Assets, storage attachment, reported cash generation, and cash-generation guidance remain diagnostic inputs.
