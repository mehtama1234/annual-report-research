# Sunstone hotel redevelopment REIT valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench tests Sunstone Hotel Investors as a concentrated upper-upscale and luxury lodging owner. It keeps property-level RevPAR, occupancy, ADR, renovations, redevelopment ramps, asset sales, hotel capex, debt, dividends, buybacks, and diluted common residual separate from reported revenue, Adjusted EBITDAre, and FFO.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunstone Hotel Investors | Hotel-property owner cash after room and ancillary collections, renovation/redevelopment capex, asset-sale timing, insurance, debt, dividends, and diluted common residual | Property maintenance and redevelopment capex, renovation ramp, hotel working capital, acquisition/disposition costs, debt, and shares | Travel/meeting slowdown, property concentration, renovation disruption, insurance and labor costs, asset-sale dependence, interest rates, or refinancing | RevPAR and Adjusted EBITDAre rise while property-level cash, renovation returns, capex coverage, leverage, or diluted per-share residual weaken |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 owned `14` hotels totaling `6,999` rooms across `7` states; Total Portfolio RevPAR was `$225.12`, occupancy `71.0%`, Adjusted EBITDAre `$236.6M`, and Adjusted FFO per share `$0.86`.
- Q2 2026 RevPAR rose `9.3%` to `$263.61`, ADR was `$339.71`, occupancy `77.6%`, Total RevPAR `$434.00`, and Adjusted EBITDAre `$76.7M`.
- Andaz Miami Beach is a material redevelopment/ramp variable; Hyatt Regency San Francisco was held for sale, making property timing part of the cash bridge.
- Management highlighted more than `$170M` returned through buybacks and dividends in 2025, alongside capital investment and portfolio recycling.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile RevPAR, ADR, occupancy, and Total RevPAR to property-level collections, labor, insurance, renovation disruption, and cash.
2. Separate normalized hotel demand from Andaz ramp, held-for-sale assets, dispositions, and one-time gains or losses.
3. Test Adjusted EBITDAre and FFO against maintenance and redevelopment capex, debt service, preferred claims, and required reserves.
4. Keep dividends, buybacks, asset-sale proceeds, debt, leases, and diluted shares in the common-owner residual schedule.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, property margins, capex, asset values, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether travel, event, resort, and affluent-occasion demand can withstand recession, labor, insurance, rate, and property-specific shocks while the REIT funds its hard assets.

## Promotion boundary

`sunstone-qualified; hotel-property-and-redevelopment-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from rooms and ancillary demand to property collections, renovation return, capex, asset-sale proceeds, debt, dividends, claims, and diluted common residual. RevPAR, ADR, occupancy, Adjusted EBITDAre, FFO, guidance, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
