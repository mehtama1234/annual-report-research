# Taboola open-web performance-advertising valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench tests Taboola as an open-web demand-routing and publisher/OEM distribution platform. It keeps advertiser outcomes, ex-TAC gross profit, publisher and partner settlements, traffic concentration, AI products, data, sales costs, working capital, debt, and diluted common residual separate from reported revenue and adjusted EBITDA.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Taboola | Open-web performance-advertising cash after traffic acquisition, publisher/OEM revenue share, advertiser collection, data and AI investment, sales costs, partner concentration, debt, and diluted common residual | Publisher and OEM distribution, traffic acquisition, recommendation/AI systems, data, sales infrastructure, acquisitions, working capital, debt, and shares | Brand and performance-ad-budget slowdown, traffic or partner loss, platform disintermediation, AI-answer-engine displacement, collection weakness, or refinancing | Ex-TAC gross profit and adjusted EBITDA rise while advertiser retention, publisher settlement, traffic quality, concentration, or diluted per-share cash deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was about `$1.912B`, ex-TAC gross profit `$713.5M`, adjusted EBITDA `$215.5M`, free cash flow `$163.4M`, and net income `$42.3M`.
- Q2 2026 revenue was about `$476.8M`, ex-TAC gross profit `$192.4M`, adjusted EBITDA `$55.5M`, and free cash flow `$17.3M`; ex-TAC gross profit grew `11.8%` while revenue grew `2.4%`.
- The network reaches more than `600M` daily active users across publishers and OEMs, including relationships such as Yahoo, NBC News, Samsung, and Xiaomi.
- Realize, Realize+, conversational AI, answer-engine, and DeeperDive initiatives are strategic growth claims that require separate retention, traffic-quality, and cash-cost testing.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile revenue and ex-TAC gross profit to publisher/OEM settlements, traffic acquisition, advertiser collections, refunds, and partner concentration.
2. Separate Realize-led performance improvement from traffic mix, pricing, seasonality, acquisitions, and one-time cost actions.
3. Test AI and answer-engine investments against measurable advertiser outcomes, retention, incremental traffic, and required data/engineering spend.
4. Keep stock compensation, debt, working capital, buybacks, and diluted shares in the common-owner residual schedule.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what advertiser retention, ex-TAC margin, traffic quality, publisher/OEM scale, reinvestment rate, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether open-web performance demand survives ad-budget, platform, attention, privacy, AI-interface, and partner-bargaining shocks.

## Promotion boundary

`taboola-qualified; open-web-demand-and-partner-collection-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity joins from advertiser spend and traffic to publisher/OEM settlement, retention, collection, data and AI reinvestment, debt, claims, and diluted common residual. Revenue, ex-TAC gross profit, adjusted EBITDA, free cash flow, guidance, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
