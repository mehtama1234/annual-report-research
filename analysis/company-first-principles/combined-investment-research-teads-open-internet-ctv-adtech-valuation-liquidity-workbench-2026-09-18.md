# Open-internet, CTV, and advertising middleware valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Outbrain/Teads from current-period advertising evidence into a company-specific valuation, publisher-yield, CTV, ex-TAC, measurement, acquisition, and owner-cash test. It treats the issuer as an open-internet advertising middleware and workflow platform, not as a content owner, agency, or pure measurement vendor.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outbrain/Teads | Collected advertiser and publisher cash after traffic acquisition, publisher revenue share/TAC, CTV and omnichannel delivery, measurement, sales, Teads integration, debt, and dilution | Publisher and traffic acquisition, CTV partnerships, ad-serving and measurement technology, EngageOS, sales and implementation, acquisition integration, working capital, and restructuring | SME and direct-response demand, advertiser concentration, publisher quality, CTV adoption, privacy/measurement changes, traffic costs, integration, bridge debt, goodwill, refinancing, and dilution | Revenue, ex-TAC gross profit, CTV growth, advertiser or publisher counts, adjusted EBITDA, or adjusted FCF rise while advertiser collection, publisher settlement, traffic cost, measurement delivery, integration cash, debt, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$1.300B`, gross profit `$429.1M`, ex-TAC gross profit `$529.7M`, adjusted EBITDA `$93.4M`, and adjusted FCF `$6.0M`.
- Q2 `2026` revenue was `$284.6M`, down `17%`; gross profit `$95.6M`, ex-TAC gross profit `$123.4M`, adjusted EBITDA `$7.0M`, operating cash flow `$9.2M`, and adjusted FCF `$3.2M`.
- Q2 `2026` CTV revenue grew `67%` year over year and reached `13%` of revenue; branding customers using omnichannel campaigns represented `16%` of CTV spend.
- Q1 `2026` revenue was `$266.0M`, ex-TAC gross profit `$107.9M`, adjusted EBITDA `$0.8M`, operating cash flow `$(34.9M)`, and adjusted FCF `$(41.1M)`; cash and marketable securities were `$98.7M` against total debt obligations of `$623.4M`.
- FY2025 net loss included a `$352.1M` goodwill impairment, `$15.6M` intangible impairment, `$28.9M` acquisition/integration costs, `$12.0M` bridge facility costs, and `$15.3M` restructuring charges.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile advertiser billings, publisher remittance, TAC/traffic acquisition, ex-TAC gross profit, CTV delivery, measurement, and collections; gross profit is not common-owner cash.
2. Separate CTV, branding, performance, SME, enterprise, publisher, and omnichannel cohorts; demand quality and settlement economics differ.
3. Test the Teads combination through purchase price, goodwill and intangible impairments, integration spending, cross-sell, platform migration, publisher retention, debt, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep adjusted EBITDA, adjusted FCF, ex-TAC gross profit, CTV growth, and restructuring adjustments separate from recurring collected cash.
5. Reconcile traffic costs, publisher share, cloud/ad-serving and measurement costs, sales, working capital, bridge debt, refinancing, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what advertiser retention, CTV mix, ex-TAC margin, publisher yield, measurement adoption, integration synergies, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether ad budgets, privacy and attribution changes, walled gardens, AI-mediated discovery, publisher bargaining power, rates, traffic costs, and leverage preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`teads-open-internet-qualified; ex-tac-settlement-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from advertiser and publisher cohorts to billing, collection, TAC/publisher settlement, CTV delivery, measurement cost, acquisition integration, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, ex-TAC gross profit, CTV growth, advertiser or publisher counts, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted FCF, and synergies remain diagnostic inputs.
