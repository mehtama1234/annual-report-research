# Sensing, imaging, and mission-systems valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Teledyne Technologies from the annual-report archive into
a company-specific sensing, imaging, instrumentation, aerospace-defense,
engineered-systems, backlog, program, acquisition, and common-owner residual
test. It keeps Teledyne separate from network-test equipment, electronic
interconnects, generic defense contractors, and semiconductor platforms.

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 net sales were `$6,115.4M`, up from `$5,670.0M` in 2024; diluted EPS was `$18.88` versus `$17.21`.
- Q2 2026 sales were `$1,662.5M`, GAAP diluted EPS `$5.37`, non-GAAP diluted EPS `$6.28`, OCF `$315.2M`, FCF `$284.7M`, funded backlog approximately `$5.0B`, and consolidated leverage `1.1x`.
- Q2 segment sales were approximately `$868.7M` Digital Imaging, `$387.8M` Instrumentation, `$286.4M` Aerospace and Defense Electronics, and `$119.6M` Engineered Systems.
- Q2 growth included infrared imaging, surveillance, industrial/scientific imaging, X-ray, marine, environmental, unmanned, and defense-electronics demand. Segment mix and program timing matter more than consolidated growth.
- Teledyne continued acquisition activity while reducing debt. Acquired cohort return, integration cost, backlog acceptance, skilled-labor capacity, and common dilution require separate tests.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile orders and backlog to funded work, qualification, shipment,
   acceptance, billing, collection, contract margin, and program claims.
2. Separate Digital Imaging, Instrumentation, Aerospace and Defense Electronics,
   and Engineered Systems by cycle, customer, margin, R&D, and capital burden.
3. Test defense/space program timing, government concentration, export controls,
   unmanned demand, marine/energy exposure, and test-and-measurement cyclicality.
4. Roll acquisitions through purchase price, backlog, cross-sell, integration,
   amortization, debt, working capital, and diluted shares.
5. Treat non-GAAP EPS, backlog, FCF, orders, dividends, and buybacks as
   diagnostic until delivered common cash per diluted share is evidenced.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what segment growth, program margin,
backlog conversion, qualification, R&D, acquisition return, working capital,
debt cost, and cost of capital the equity value requires. The Lyn Alden-style
stress test asks whether defense budgets, industrial cycles, energy/marine
demand, export controls, supply chains, rates, and government timing preserve
cash and liquidity.

## Promotion boundary

`teledyne-sensing-imaging-qualified; program-conversion-acquisition-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from orders/backlog to
qualified delivery, acceptance, collection, segment margin, R&D and facility
capital, acquisition return, debt, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue,
orders, backlog, non-GAAP EPS, OCF, FCF, defense demand, and buybacks remain
diagnostic inputs.
