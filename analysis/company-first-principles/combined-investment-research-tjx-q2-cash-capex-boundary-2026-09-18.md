# TJX Q2 cash-quality and capex boundary

Research date: `2026-09-18`

TJX's Q2 FY2027 filing gives a useful same-period control surface without
closing the CA-06 denominator. H1 operating cash flow was approximately
`$3.3B`; capital expenditures were `$1.159B`, split by segment but not by
maintenance versus growth. TJX says the H1 operating-cash increase was driven
primarily by tariff-refund receipt and a decrease in prepaid and other current
assets related to the credit-card interchange-fee settlement. It also plans to
repay `$1B` of notes due September 2026 using operating cash flows.

The implications are deliberately bounded:

- `$3.3B` is reported operating cash, not normalized owner cash.
- `$1.159B` is a real period-matched property denominator, but store
  improvements, new stores, distribution centers, offices, and IT remain
  mixed.
- The identified tariff and interchange effects are cash-quality and
  recurrence controls, not a mechanically deducted owner-cash number.
- The planned `$1B` repayment is a senior forward claim, not an already-paid
  cash use.

Decision: `TJX-Q2-cash-quality-strengthened; maintenance-split-open; CA-06-partial`.
