# United Rentals collateral-to-owner-cash promotion workbench

Research date: `2026-09-17`

## Purpose

This workbench connects URI's operating fleet to the legal collateral and
owner-cash questions:

`fleet purchases -> rental operations -> operating cash -> resale recovery -> collateral eligibility -> borrowing-base availability -> debt and senior claims -> common-owner residual -> lifecycle return`

It keeps three different objects separate:

1. operating cash generated by the rental business;
2. legal borrowing capacity supported by eligible collateral; and
3. residual cash or return available to common owners after reinvestment and
   financing claims.

## Diagnostic cash screens

These are labeled screens, not normalized owner cash:

| Screen | Calculation | Result | Why it is not final owner cash |
|---|---|---:|---|
| OCF less gross rental capex | `$3.305B - $2.720B` | `$585M` | Replacement/growth, acquisitions, taxes, leases, working capital, debt, and common claims remain |
| OCF plus rental resale proceeds less rental capex | `$3.305B + $680M - $2.720B` | `$1.265B` | Resale depends on fleet age, utilization, used-equipment prices, and replacement policy |
| OCF less rental and non-rental reinvestment | `$3.305B - $2.720B - $165M` | `$420M` | Still excludes acquisition claims, lifecycle replacement, debt, leases, dilution, and owner allocation |

The `$2.887B` facility-capacity subtotal (`$2.802B + $85M`) reconciles to
reported total liquidity less `$112M` of cash. It is not additive to the cash
screens and cannot be treated as cash generated by the fleet.

## Promotion gates

| Gate | Current status | Required upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Operating borrower/legal entity | observed | None for current diagnostic |
| Fleet operating cash | observed | Same-period segment/lifecycle allocation |
| Replacement versus growth capex | missing | Fleet purchase schedule by age, class, and replacement need |
| Used-equipment recovery | partial | Asset-class sale proceeds, age/utilization, and realized margin bridge |
| Eligible collateral | partial | Populated borrowing-base certificate and eligibility deductions |
| NOLV/appraisal | missing | Lender appraisal or collateral report |
| Reserves/L/C/outstandings | partial | Live U.S./Canadian certificate components and reserve schedule |
| Combined Availability | facility proxy | Current certificate calculation and covenant reconciliation |
| Source-to-purchase funding | missing | Draw notices joined to fleet purchases and acquisitions |
| Debt service and senior claims | partial | Facility principal/interest schedule, leases, taxes, and contingent claims |
| Common-owner residual | missing | Diluted-share, preferred/NCI, repurchase, dividend, and parent-use bridge |
| Lifecycle return | missing | Utilization, rates, maintenance, replacement, resale, debt, tax, and capital-cost model |

## QoE and financial-shenanigans controls

- Do not use OEC or rental-equipment NBV as eligible borrowing-base collateral.
- Do not use reported ABL capacity as unencumbered cash.
- Do not treat used-equipment proceeds as recurring rental revenue.
- Do not infer maintenance capex from gross purchases or depreciation alone.
- Do not add resale proceeds to operating cash without a fleet-age and
  replacement bridge.
- Do not treat the `$585M` or `$1.265B` screens as common-owner cash while
  debt, leases, taxes, acquisitions, dilution, and lifecycle reinvestment are
  unresolved.
- Do not infer lender availability from an agreement's formula without
  populated collateral, reserves, and agent adjustments.

These controls identify reconciliation requirements; they are not allegations
of manipulation.

## Decision

URI is now `qualified; fleet-lifecycle and facility-availability proxies
visible; certificate-grade availability and owner return open`. The industrial
lane has a complete diagnostic chain from fleet activity to liquidity and
senior claims, but no normalized common-owner ranking should be made until the
borrowing-base certificate, replacement-capital, and lifecycle-return objects
are joined.
