# United Rentals Q2 2026 availability and lifecycle boundary

Research date: `2026-09-17`

## Current-period evidence

United Rentals' June 30, 2026 Form 10-Q gives a stronger current-period
liquidity and lifecycle bridge, but it still does not publish the populated
equipment borrowing-base certificate needed to promote Q-13.

| Object | Q2 2026 disclosure | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Stated available liquidity | `$2.999B` | Cash plus stated ABL and receivables-securitization availability; not equivalent to unencumbered common-owner cash |
| Receivables collateral pool | `$1.779B` net of reserves and other deductions | Supports the receivables facility perimeter; does not establish equipment collateral or NOLV |
| Reported facility components | `$2.802B` ABL borrowing capacity net of letters of credit and `$85M` accounts-receivable securitization capacity | Explains the public `$2.887B` capacity proxy; does not disclose the populated equipment borrowing-base certificate, advance rates, NOLV, or live reserve schedule |

## Legal-entity cash availability boundary

The filing also identifies a parent/subsidiary constraint that matters for
common-owner cash. United Rentals, Inc. is principally a holding company and
conducts operations primarily through United Rentals (North America), Inc. and
its subsidiaries. The filing states that URNA's credit agreements and debt
instruments restrict its ability to transfer funds to its shareholder.

Therefore, URI's `$112M` parent cash, URNA operating cash, facility availability,
and consolidated `$3.305B` operating cash flow cannot be treated as one freely
available common-owner pool. A promotion-grade bridge needs the permitted
distribution capacity, debt-service and covenant constraints, intercompany
transfers, and parent-level cash actually received. This does not change the
equipment borrowing-base gap, but it adds a legal-availability gate before
reported FCF can be considered parent common cash.

## What remains unproven

The filing reports the public facility components—`$2.802B` of ABL borrowing
capacity net of letters of credit and `$85M` of accounts-receivable
securitization capacity—but does not provide, for the equipment-backed route,
the period-end eligible-rental-equipment pool, advance rates, NOLV, reserves,
availability certificate, source-to-purchase draw mapping, or lifecycle return
by fleet cohort. It also does not allocate the `$2.885B` purchase burden between
replacement, growth, and inventory offered for sale. Accordingly, the
liquidity figure cannot be substituted for collateral availability, and the
reported free-cash-flow figure cannot be substituted for common-owner cash.

The targeted review of the June 30, 2026 10-Q also found no populated NOLV or
equipment borrowing-base schedule. A separate June 18, 2026 A/R-facility
amendment confirms the receivables route and identifies collateral-only lender
repayment, eligible-receivable excess requirements, a June 18, 2027 expiration,
and default, delinquency, dilution, and DSO termination controls; it still does
not populate the equipment certificate or prove parent cash availability. The
filing's statement that specified
availability exceeded the covenant threshold is a compliance observation, not
the missing lender certificate or a liquidation-value disclosure. This is a
searched-negative boundary for the checked public filing, not evidence that no
such lender schedule exists privately.

The official [Q2 2026 investor presentation](https://s21.q4cdn.com/336331232/files/doc_earnings/2026/q2/presentation/Second-Quarter-2026-Investor-Presentation.pdf)
was also checked as a public management route. It adds fleet scale, branch,
productivity, maintenance, and capital-allocation context, but it does not
provide eligible-equipment collateral, advance rates, NOLV, reserves, or a
period-tested borrowing-base calculation. It therefore strengthens the
operating control-point map without changing the certificate-grade hold.

## Decision

This is a `Q-13-qualified; availability-partial; lifecycle-return-open`
upgrade. It improves the legal-entity, period, and cash-object joins and
provides a current stress input, but it does not promote URI. The next decisive
source remains the populated borrowing-base certificate or equivalent lender
schedule plus a fleet-cohort replacement and resale bridge.

## Live SEC/public-route recheck — 2026-09-18

The latest checked public route adds no certificate-grade upgrade. URI's
September 8, 2026 Form 8-K concerns management's conference participation; the
latest substantive public liquidity filing remains the June 30 Form 10-Q. That
10-Q still reports facility-level capacity and covenant status without an
eligible-equipment schedule, NOLV, reserves, advance rates, or source-to-
purchase draw ledger. Q-13 therefore remains
`availability-partial; lifecycle-return-open`, and execution moves to the next
routed lane rather than repeating the same public search.
