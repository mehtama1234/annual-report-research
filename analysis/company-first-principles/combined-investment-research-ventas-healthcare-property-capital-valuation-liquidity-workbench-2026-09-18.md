# Healthcare-property operating platform and growth-capital valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Ventas from annual-report evidence into a company-specific
healthcare-property, senior-housing operating, outpatient medical, triple-net,
operator-coverage, and common-owner residual test. It keeps Ventas separate from
Welltower's portfolio, hotel property owners, and healthcare providers because
Ventas's SHOP/OM&R/NNN mix and equity-funded investment pace create a distinct
capital and dilution denominator.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ventas | Property-level cash NOI and common residual after SHOP labor/occupancy, OM&R and NNN lease collection, recurring property capital, development/acquisition return, operator coverage, debt, preferred claims, and equity dilution | Senior-housing communities, outpatient/research properties, recurring building capital, redevelopment, acquisitions, development, technology, insurance, and operator support | Resident affordability/collection, labor and insurance, operator distress, cap-rate/rate shock, acquisition funding, equity-forward dilution, debt/refinancing, or property-value decline | Normalized FFO, same-store NOI, occupancy, or investment growth rise while recurring property capital, operator coverage, equity funding cost, debt, or per-share residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- Q2 2026 normalized FFO per share was about `$0.97`; total NOI grew approximately `17%`, total same-store cash NOI about `10%`, and SHOP same-store cash NOI about `16%`.
- Year-to-date investments reached approximately `$3.4B`, with 2026 investment expectations raised to about `$4.5B`. Investment scale is not owner value until property-level return, funding cost, and dilution are joined.
- Ventas had approximately `$12.7B` of debt, net debt to further adjusted EBITDA around `4.7x`, and liquidity around `$4.9B` at June 30, 2026.
- First-half equity forward sales covered roughly `34.7M` shares and gross proceeds of about `$3.0B`. Equity funding can support growth while transferring part of the economics to new shareholders.
- SHOP exposes Ventas to occupancy, resident rates, labor, food, insurance, operator execution, and affordability; OM&R and NNN reduce some operating burden but retain tenant, lease, and property-renewal risk.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile normalized FFO to property-level cash NOI, recurring property
   capital, redevelopment, leasing costs, interest, preferred claims, and
   diluted common residual.
2. Separate SHOP occupancy/rate/acuity and operator coverage from OM&R rent and
   NNN lease collection; do not pool unlike property cash flows.
3. Test each acquisition and development cohort through purchase price, cap rate,
   stabilization, operator performance, recurring capital, debt, and equity
   issuance rather than headline investment volume.
4. Roll resident and tenant receivables, operator support, insurance, labor,
   property taxes, cap rates, debt maturities, and equity-forward settlement.
5. Treat FFO add-backs, asset-sale proceeds, dividends, and equity-funded growth
   as diagnostic inputs until common per-share cash survives the full burden.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what occupancy, resident pricing,
operator margin, rent growth, same-store NOI, recurring capital, acquisition
yield, cap rates, debt cost, and dilution the equity price requires. The Lyn
Alden-style stress test asks whether rates, financing availability, resident
affordability, labor/insurance inflation, and property-market liquidity preserve
cash through a healthcare-real-estate cycle.

## Promotion boundary

`ventas-healthcare-property-qualified; property-capital-and-diluted-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from property occupancy/rent
and operator/tenant collection to cash NOI, recurring capital, acquisition or
development return, debt, preferred claims, equity-forward settlement, and
diluted common residual. Normalized FFO, same-store NOI, occupancy, investment
volume, asset sales, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
