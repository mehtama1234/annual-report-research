# DNS registry control and internet infrastructure valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves VeriSign from current-period internet-infrastructure evidence into a company-specific valuation, registry-contract, deferred-revenue, uptime, governance, cyber, and owner-cash test. It treats VeriSign as a DNS and registry-control toll franchise, not as generic software, advertising, or raw bandwidth infrastructure.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VeriSign | Collected registry and resolution cash after registrar and registry obligations, DNS operations, carrier and service commitments, cybersecurity, governance, taxes, debt, and diluted common claims | Root-zone and resolution infrastructure, data centers, network and telecom commitments, security and resiliency, registry development, `.web` execution, and governance/compliance | `.com` contract and ICANN continuity, renewal and registration trends, wholesale pricing, cyberattack or outage, substitute naming systems, registrar alignment, carrier dependency, policy, and dilution | Revenue, domain base, renewal rate, deferred revenue, OCF, FCF, or pricing power rise while registry continuity, availability, collection, contract rights, governance, security spending, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$1.6566B`, operating income `$1.121B`, operating cash flow `$1.0911B`, approximate FCF `$1.0683B`, and capex `$22.8M`.
- VeriSign operates `.com` and `.net` registration and authoritative resolution, provides root-zone maintainer services, operates two of the 13 global internet root servers, and has a sole `.com` registry-operator agreement through `2030`.
- Q2 `2026` revenue was `$434.6M`, operating income `$296.3M`, net income `$216.5M`, diluted EPS `$2.38`, and operating cash flow `$232M`.
- The Q2 `2026` domain-name base was `179.1M`, net adds `3.05M`, new registrations `12.7M`, and final Q1 renewal rate `76.3%`.
- Deferred revenue was `$1.3845B` at 2025 year-end, `$1.4294B` at March 31, 2026, and `$1.4489B` at June 30, 2026; the `.com` wholesale fee was scheduled to increase from `$10.26` to `$10.97` on `2026-11-01`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile domain registrations, renewals, registrar remittances, wholesale pricing, deferred-revenue release, collections, refunds, and contract obligations; the domain base is not cash.
2. Separate `.com`, `.net`, `.web`, root-zone, resolution, and other registry cohorts; contract rights and economics differ across them.
3. Test deferred revenue through service delivery, renewal timing, cancellation, registrar settlement, and availability obligations.
4. Keep low capex separate from the continuing need for cybersecurity, DDoS protection, data-center, carrier, redundancy, governance, and resiliency spending.
5. Reconcile OCF, FCF, pricing actions, buybacks, debt, taxes, contract and policy risk, and diluted shares before promoting toll-franchise economics into common-owner cash.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what domain growth, renewal, wholesale pricing, `.com` contract duration, deferred-revenue conversion, uptime, reinvestment, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether cyber incidents, governance changes, substitute naming, AI-mediated discovery, policy, carrier dependence, and digital-behavior shifts impair the collection and continuity of this hidden internet toll bridge.

## Promotion boundary

`verisign-dns-registry-qualified; contract-continuity-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from registry contracts and domain cohorts to registrar remittance, customer collection, deferred-revenue release, uptime and security cost, named commitments, taxes, debt, and diluted common residual. Domain base, registrations, renewal, pricing, deferred revenue, OCF, FCF, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
