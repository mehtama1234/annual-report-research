# Satellite connectivity and orbital infrastructure valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Viasat from current-period satellite and connectivity evidence into a company-specific valuation, fleet, spectrum, service-entry, contract, financing, and owner-cash test. It treats Viasat as capital-intensive orbital and mobility infrastructure, not as generic telecom software or a low-capex recurring-revenue platform.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Viasat | Collected aviation, maritime, government, defense, and fixed-connectivity cash after satellite operations, ground infrastructure, spectrum, launch, service-entry, customer equipment, network delivery, interest, debt, and dilution | Satellite construction and launch, fleet replacement, ground systems, spectrum and regulatory work, terminals, network operations, customer acquisition, service milestones, and working capital | Launch or satellite failure, service-entry delay, spectrum or regulatory restrictions, airline and maritime adoption, government budgets, subscriber churn, leverage/refinancing, backlog conversion, and dilution | Revenue, backlog, RPO, adjusted EBITDA, connected aircraft or vessels, subscribers, or FCF rise while fleet availability, service-entry, collections, capex, customer equipment, debt service, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$4.52B`, net loss attributable to Viasat `$(575.0)M`, and remaining performance obligations about `$3.6B`.
- FY2026 Q4 revenue was `$1.2B`, net income `$59M`, adjusted EBITDA `$370M`, and awards `$1.3B`; FY2026 full-year adjusted EBITDA was `$1.6B` and FCF `$177M`.
- FY2027 Q1 revenue was `$1.2B`, net loss `$(52)M`, adjusted EBITDA `$381M`, and backlog about `$4.2B`.
- The annual filing described `23` in-service or operational satellites across Ka, L, and S bands, about `4,120` commercial aircraft in service, `2,000` connected business jets, about `14,000` vessels served, and roughly `189,000` U.S. fixed-broadband subscribers.
- Viasat's revenue depends on aviation, maritime, government and defense, telecom, and fixed broadband while its physical base depends on satellites, spectrum, ground infrastructure, launch, and financing.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile connected aircraft, vessels, subscribers, government programs, RPO/backlog, service-entry milestones, billing, collection, and network delivery; backlog is not cash.
2. Separate aviation, maritime, government/defense, telecom, and fixed-broadband cohorts; contracts, churn, margins, and equipment burdens differ.
3. Test satellite and network investments through launch, commissioning, service availability, useful life, insurance, replacement, depreciation, and actual incremental cash return.
4. Keep adjusted EBITDA, RPO, backlog, connected endpoints, and FCF separate from recurring collected common-owner cash when capex, launch, customer equipment, or debt service remains heavy.
5. Reconcile leverage, refinancing, interest, spectrum, regulatory obligations, customer concentration, supplier and launch commitments, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what endpoint growth, pricing, utilization, fleet availability, service-entry timing, backlog conversion, satellite replacement, capex, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether launch failures, orbital congestion, spectrum policy, rates, leverage, airline and maritime cycles, government procurement, and remote-connectivity demand preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`viasat-satellite-connectivity-qualified; fleet-service-entry-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from named fleet and customer cohorts to service entry, endpoint billing, collection, network delivery, capex, launch and replacement cash, debt service, taxes, and diluted common residual. Revenue, backlog, RPO, adjusted EBITDA, endpoints, subscribers, and FCF remain diagnostic inputs.
