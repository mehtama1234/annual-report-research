# HDD, persistent storage, and data-retention infrastructure valuation/liquidity workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Western Digital from current-period storage evidence into a company-specific valuation, HDD, capacity, hyperscale, persistent-data, utilization, customer, portfolio-separation, working-capital, and owner-cash test. It treats Western Digital as a post-spin HDD infrastructure platform, not as a generic semiconductor or mixed storage company.

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 revenue was `$9.52B`, gross margin `38.8%`, operating income `$2.33B`, net income from continuing operations `$1.64B`, and operating cash flow about `$1.69B`.
- Western Digital completed the HDD/Flash separation from Sandisk on `2025-02-21`, leaving WD focused on HDD.
- FY2026 Q2 revenue was `$3.017B`, GAAP gross margin `45.7%`, GAAP EPS `$4.73`, operating cash flow `$745M`, and FCF `$653M`.
- FY2026 Q3 revenue was `$3.337B`, GAAP gross margin `50.2%`, GAAP EPS `$8.20`, operating cash flow `$1.12B`, and FCF `$978M`.
- FY2026 Q4 revenue was `$3.747B`, GAAP gross margin `54.1%`, GAAP EPS `$8.21`, operating cash flow `$1.39B`, and FCF `$1.28B`; full-year FY2026 revenue was `$12.919B`, operating cash flow `$3.929B`, and FCF `$3.511B`.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile exabytes, drives, capacity, customer commitments, shipments, qualification, pricing, inventory, warranty, receivables, and cash; AI demand headlines are not WD cash.
2. Separate cloud/data-center, enterprise, edge, surveillance, client, high-capacity, and product-generation cohorts; utilization and pricing differ.
3. Test manufacturing capacity, product transitions, media and component sourcing, and post-spin focus through utilization, yield, reliability, customer retention, capex, and return on invested capital.
4. Keep gross-margin expansion, adjusted EPS, FCF, dividends, buybacks, exabyte growth, and customer commitments separate from recurring collected common-owner cash.
5. Reconcile inventory, fixed-cost absorption, component cost, warranty, capex, debt, interest, taxes, SBC, and diluted shares.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what persistent-data growth, high-capacity mix, price, utilization, hyperscale demand, capacity discipline, capex, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether cloud capex, customer concentration, underutilization, oversupply, price cuts, component shocks, rates, and leverage preserve cash conversion.

## Promotion boundary

`western-digital-hdd-qualified; capacity-utilization-storage-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from drive and customer cohorts to qualification, shipment, collection, inventory, utilization, manufacturing cost, capex, warranty, debt service, taxes, dividends, and diluted common residual. Revenue, capacity, exabytes, gross margin, adjusted EPS, FCF, dividends, and buybacks remain diagnostic inputs.
