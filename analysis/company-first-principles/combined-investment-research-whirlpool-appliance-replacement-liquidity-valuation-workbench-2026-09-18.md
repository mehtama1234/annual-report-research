# Appliance replacement-cycle and liquidity valuation workbench

Research date: `2026-09-18`

## Purpose

This workbench moves Whirlpool from current-period household-durables evidence into a company-specific valuation, replacement-cycle, capital-intensity, and liquidity test. It treats major appliances as essential but deferrable household infrastructure, not as ordinary branded consumer staples.

## Decision matrix

| Company/model | Correct valuation object | Reinvestment denominator | Liquidity stress | Filing-based thesis breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whirlpool | Collected appliance cash after replacement-cycle demand, retailer inventory, promotions, steel and freight, plant utilization, warranty, portfolio restructuring, debt refinancing, pensions, and dilution | Manufacturing plants, tooling, inventory, product launches, engineering, domestic sourcing, retailer support, warranties, restructuring, and maintenance capital | Housing turnover, consumer credit, rates, affordability, channel inventory, promotions, tariffs/raw materials, refinancing, dividend/equity actions, and weak segment mix | Sales or adjusted EPS stabilize while units, sell-through, promotional intensity, free cash flow, plant economics, debt, covenant/liquidity, or diluted common residual deteriorate |

## Current evidence anchors

- FY2025 sales were about `$15.524B`, ongoing EBIT about `$729M`, ongoing EBIT margin `4.7%`, and free cash flow about `$78M`; approximately `90%` of sales were in the Americas.
- Q2 2026 sales were about `$3.517B`, ongoing EBIT about `$62M`, ongoing EBIT margin `1.8%`, GAAP EPS `$1.15`, ongoing EPS negative `$0.21`, and free cash flow about negative `$1.108B`.
- Q1 2026 sales were down `9.6%`, organic sales down `6.1%`, ongoing EBIT margin `1.3%`, ongoing EPS negative `$0.56`, and free cash flow about negative `$896M`; management cited recession-level North American industry demand, weak sentiment, promotions, and inventory reduction.
- Whirlpool announced a more than `10%` price increase, accelerated more than `$150M` of cost takeout, completed a `$2B` ABL facility, and issued `$2B` of secured bonds to address near-term maturities and liquidity.
- Management said approximately `80%` of major appliances sold in the U.S. are produced in the U.S. and about `96%` of U.S. steel used is domestically sourced, which reduces some tariff exposure but does not eliminate cost, demand, or channel pressure.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans prompts

1. Reconcile reported sales to unit volume, price, mix, retailer sell-through, channel inventory, promotions, returns, warranty, and consumer financing; price increases are not demand recovery.
2. Keep MDA North America, MDA Latin America, and SDA Global separate; small-appliance growth cannot automatically repair weak major-appliance cash.
3. Test free cash flow for inventory reduction, payables timing, restructuring, plant underutilization, maintenance capital, and working-capital stress rather than treating a single period as normalized.
4. Reconcile the Beko Europe deconsolidation, Whirlpool India stake changes, equity actions, dividend policy, ABL, secured bonds, maturities, pensions, and refinancing cost before promoting equity value.
5. Test pricing and cost takeout for repeatability against retailer promotions, steel, freight, labor, tariffs, and product-launch spending.
6. Treat ongoing EPS, adjusted EBIT, buybacks, and dividends as residual claims requiring liquidity, debt, warranty, plant, and diluted-share validation.

## Damodaran/Lyn Alden application

The Damodaran-style expectation test asks what replacement rate, housing turnover, unit volume, price/mix, promotional intensity, plant utilization, cost takeout, maintenance capital, debt cost, and cost of capital the valuation requires. The Lyn Alden-style stress test asks whether households can fund essential-but-deferrable durables through rate, employment, affordability, housing, tariff, and credit shocks while factories, retailers, warranty reserves, and refinancing obligations remain fixed.

## Promotion boundary

`whirlpool-appliance-qualified; sellthrough-replacement-liquidity-and-owner-cash-open; no-ranking`

Promotion requires same-entity, same-period joins from units and retailer sell-through to collection, inventory, promotions, plant utilization, warranty, maintenance capital, restructuring, debt/refinancing, pensions, and diluted common residual. Sales, ongoing EBIT, EPS, pricing, cost takeout, free cash flow, and liquidity announcements remain diagnostic inputs.
