# DaVita Inc. Company Analysis

## Core First-Principles Read

DaVita is recurring chronic-treatment infrastructure, not just a dialysis clinic chain.

Start from kidney failure. The patient cannot defer treatment like a discretionary purchase. Dialysis is repeated, time-intensive, clinically necessary, payer-dependent, and operationally demanding. The business exists because chronic disease turns care into a recurring system.

DaVita's first-principles mechanism is treatment density. Centers, clinicians, patient scheduling, reimbursement per treatment, labor productivity, home dialysis, and integrated kidney care all convert non-optional medical need into recurring care economics.

The scarce position is dialysis network scale plus payer and care-coordination capability.

## Customer Insight

The patient is buying survival maintenance, not a healthcare experience.

That makes the economics durable but morally and politically sensitive. The patient needs reliable care; payers and government programs need the cost controlled; the operator needs enough reimbursement and labor productivity to keep the system functioning.

## Industrial / Operating Insight

DaVita shows how chronic care becomes an operating utility.

Treatment frequency, treatments per day, revenue per treatment, patient care cost per treatment, clinic density, home modality, and risk-based arrangements matter more than simple patient demand.

## Societal Insight

DaVita sits inside the social burden of diabetes, cardiovascular disease, aging, and chronic kidney disease.

Healthcare spending is increasingly long-duration maintenance, not episodic rescue.

## Strategic Insight

The strategy is to defend core dialysis economics while expanding integrated kidney care and home dialysis where clinically and economically appropriate.

The risk is reimbursement pressure, labor cost, commercial mix erosion, center utilization weakness, or value-based-care risk that is not priced correctly.

## Theme

Mandatory chronic-treatment infrastructure.

## Subtheme

Dialysis density, payer mix, and integrated kidney care.

## What Would Prove This Wrong

The read weakens if reimbursement falls faster than productivity improves, patient growth slows, labor costs rise materially, home dialysis weakens center economics without offsetting benefits, or risk-based arrangements create losses.

## What This Company Teaches The Archive

DaVita teaches that some healthcare businesses are recurring because illness is recurring. The strategic question is whether the operator can manage the treatment system better than payers and providers could separately.
