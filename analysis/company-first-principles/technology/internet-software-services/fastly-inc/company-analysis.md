# Fastly, Inc. Company Analysis

## Core First-Principles Read

Fastly is a programmable-edge company proving both the value and limits of a narrower traffic-control position.

Start from a developer or digital business that needs an application to be fast, secure, observable, and adjustable close to the user. That customer wants more than static delivery. They want traffic handling, API protection, bot defense, application security, compute, and observability in the same edge environment.

Fastly's first-principles mechanism is programmability at the edge. It tries to make the traffic layer more flexible so developers and enterprises can shape behavior before requests reach centralized infrastructure.

The scarce thing Fastly wants is developer trust in the edge runtime. The problem is that this position is narrower than Cloudflare's broad control plane and less cash-rich than Akamai's legacy base.

## Customer Insight

The customer problem is edge flexibility without rebuilding the whole stack.

Customers want low latency and security, but they also want to customize behavior for commerce, media, APIs, AI traffic, and application logic. Fastly appeals when the edge is not just a pipe but a programmable surface.

The customer risk is concentration. If a small number of large customers account for a large share of revenue, the model is more exposed to usage shifts, pricing pressure, and architecture changes.

## Industrial / Operating Insight

Fastly shows that edge control is valuable but economically contested.

Security and compute are growing faster than network services, but network services remain the dominant revenue line. That means the company is moving toward higher-value control, but still carries delivery-era exposure.

## Societal Insight

Fastly exposes the hidden expectation that digital systems should be responsive and safe everywhere. As commerce, media, APIs, and AI interactions become more immediate, the infrastructure close to the user becomes more important.

## Strategic Insight

Management is trying to move from usage-heavy delivery economics toward a stronger mix of security, compute, and observability.

The strategy works if Fastly becomes a durable programmable edge platform. It fails if it remains a better CDN with some security growth and customer concentration.

## Theme

AI infrastructure toll / programmable edge.

## Subtheme

Focused edge runtime under delivery-era burden.

## Finding-To-Insight Map

| Finding | Company Meaning | Customer / Industrial / Societal Insight |
|---|---|---|
| Fiscal `2025` revenue reached `$624.0M`, up `15%`, with free cash flow of `$45.8M`. | The platform is improving financially. | Edge services can generate cash but are not automatically high-margin software. |
| Security revenue was `$125.1M` in `2025`, up `21%`, while other revenue including Compute and Observability grew `64%`. | Mix is moving toward higher-value control. | Customers increasingly want security and programmable execution at the edge. |
| Q2 `2026` revenue grew `23%` to `$183.3M`, and RPO grew `38%` to `$341M`. | Growth and future commitments improved. | Edge vendors can deepen architectural relevance when execution improves. |
| Q2 `2026` top-ten customers were `37%` of revenue. | Concentration remains material. | The narrower edge model is more exposed to large-customer architecture decisions. |

## What Would Prove This Wrong

The read weakens if security growth slows, net retention falls, customer concentration rises, or free cash flow stays thin despite revenue growth.

The next-filing watch item is whether security, compute, RPO, and net retention improve while top-ten concentration declines.

## What This Company Teaches The Archive

Fastly teaches that the edge is a real control point, but not every edge company owns the same quality of control. It is the narrower programmable-edge case between Cloudflare's broader platform and Akamai's transition model.
