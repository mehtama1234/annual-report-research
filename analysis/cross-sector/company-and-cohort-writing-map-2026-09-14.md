## Writing rule

Every new deep company memo should add one link to the applicable cohort essay.
Every new cohort essay should identify at least two company pages that supply
the facts behind its comparisons. If no suitable essay exists, create the
essay before treating the company conclusion as portable across the sector.

The links are navigation, not evidence. Reported figures remain supported by
the company source ledger, and the scenario register remains the authority for
valuation inputs.
