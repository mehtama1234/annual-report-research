# Metric Glossary And Watchlist

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

Purpose:

This page defines the metrics that matter across the current archive and names what to check in the next filings.

## Consumer And Retail

`Comparable sales`

Sales growth from existing stores or comparable channels. It helps separate real demand from new-store growth.

Watch:

- Costco adjusted comparable sales.
- Ulta comparable sales.
- Department-store comparable sales.

`Membership fees`

Revenue from paid access rather than product sales.

Watch:

- Costco membership-fee growth.
- Renewal behavior if disclosed.
- Whether merchandise comps support the access fee.

`Digitally enabled comparable sales`

Digital ordering, delivery, app, or e-commerce contribution inside an existing retail relationship.

Watch:

- Costco digitally enabled comps.
- Ulta app and e-commerce signals.
- Whether digital growth improves frequency or only shifts channel.

`Inventory growth`

Inventory can signal expansion, new brands, supply-chain pressure, or demand risk.

Watch:

- Ulta inventory versus comparable sales.
- Apparel and footwear inventory against revenue growth.
- Packaging and staples inventory commentary.

## Beauty

`Gross margin`

Shows pricing power, mix, product cost, and channel quality.

Watch:

- e.l.f. gross margin after `83%` in FY2027 Q1.
- Estee Lauder and Coty margin repair.
- Ulta merchandise margin and shrink commentary.

`Brand expansion`

New acquired or developed brands that broaden the platform.

Watch:

- e.l.f. `rhode` and `Naturium`.
- Ulta Space NK and international partnerships.
- Whether growth is broad or tied to one hot brand.

## Media And Participation

`Paid memberships`

Subscriber or member base for recurring access.

Watch:

- Netflix membership quality and engagement after crossing `325M` in 2025.
- Disney+ bundle and membership language.

`Hours watched / hours engaged`

Attention intensity, not just account count.

Watch:

- Netflix hours watched.
- Roblox hours engaged.
- Whether hours convert into revenue and profit.

`Bookings`

Customer spending or committed platform economics before revenue recognition.

Watch:

- Roblox bookings growth.
- Bookings per hour.
- Whether bookings move with DAUs and payer count.

`Developer exchange fees`

Creator payout in participatory platforms.

Watch:

- Roblox developer exchange fees after exceeding `$1.5B` in 2025.
- Whether creator payouts grow in a way that supports platform quality.

`Ad revenue`

Monetization layer on top of attention.

Watch:

- Netflix ad revenue after rising more than `2.5x` to over `$1.5B` in 2025.
- Roku platform revenue.
- Ad-tech verification and routing demand.

## Healthcare

`Treatments per day`

Core volume measure for dialysis and repeated procedure businesses.

Watch:

- DaVita U.S. treatments per day after about `91,650` in Q1 2026.
- Revenue per treatment and reimbursement commentary.

`Risk-based patients / annualized medical spend`

Shows move from service delivery into care-risk management.

Watch:

- DaVita risk-based patient count after about `64,900`.
- Medical spend under management after about `$5.8B`.

`Organic diagnostic growth`

Growth from testing demand excluding acquisitions and one-time effects.

Watch:

- Quest organic growth after `10.0%` in Q2 2026.
- Advanced testing mix.
- Payer reimbursement.

`Equipment deliveries / resupply`

Shows home-care logistics intensity.

Watch:

- AdaptHealth deliveries after about `38,500` per day.
- Resupply frequency.
- Payor documentation and collections.

`Service mix`

The mix of personal care, hospice, home health, infusion, devices, diagnostics, and distribution changes margin and risk.

Watch:

- Addus personal care, hospice, and home health mix.
- Cencora specialty utilization and GLP-1 mix.
- Option Care therapy mix.

## Senior Housing And Healthcare Real Estate

`Occupancy`

Shows whether demand is filling existing capacity.

Watch:

- Brookdale occupancy after Q1 2026 reached `80.0%`.
- Senior-housing occupancy across REITs.

`RevPAR`

Revenue per available room or unit. In senior housing, it captures pricing and occupancy together.

Watch:

- Brookdale same-community RevPAR after 2025 growth of `7.6%`.

`Same-store cash NOI`

Property-level operating growth from comparable assets.

Watch:

- Ventas SHOP same-store cash NOI after more than `15%` growth in 2025 and about `16%` in Q2 2026.

`Investment volume`

Capital being deployed into new assets.

Watch:

- Ventas senior-housing investments after about `$3.4B` year-to-date and `2026` expectations near `$4.5B`.

## Industrial Distribution

`Organic sales`

Underlying sales growth excluding acquisitions, currency, or divestitures.

Watch:

- WESCO organic sales.
- Ferguson organic and non-residential revenue.
- Grainger daily sales.

`Backlog`

Committed demand not yet shipped or recognized.

Watch:

- WESCO backlog after about `60%` growth in Q2 2026.
- Data-center and grid-related backlog conversion.

`Branch count`

Local physical availability.

Watch:

- Ferguson branch count after `1,746` branches as of 2025-07-31.
- Branch productivity and acquisition integration.

`Supplier count`

Shows breadth and procurement complexity.

Watch:

- Ferguson around `37,000` suppliers.
- WESCO more than `35,000` suppliers.

`Working capital`

Cash tied up in inventory and receivables.

Watch:

- WESCO cash conversion during high data-center growth.
- Grainger inventory and tariff pass-through.

`Organic sales growth`

Shows recurring demand strength.

Watch:

- Ecolab organic growth.
- Veralto core sales growth.

Watch:

Watch:

- Ecolab Global Water and Life Sciences.
- Veralto Water Quality.
- Pest elimination and food safety demand.

## AI Physical Infrastructure

`Orders`

Fresh demand for equipment and systems.

Watch:

- Vertiv organic orders.
- Cisco AI infrastructure orders.
- Trane commercial HVAC bookings.

`Backlog`

Capacity demand waiting to be delivered.

Watch:

- Vertiv backlog after `$15.0B`.
- Trane backlog after `$12.1B`.
- Comfort Systems backlog after `$14.06B`.

`Book-to-bill`

Orders divided by revenue shipped. Above 1.0 means demand is building faster than shipments.

Watch:

- Vertiv Q4 2025 book-to-bill after `2.9x`.

`Data-center load agreements`

Utility contracts for power demand.

Watch:

- Duke data-center ESAs after `7.6GW` in Q1 2026.
- Utility capex plans tied to load growth.

`Capital expenditures`

Spending required to build capacity.

Watch:

- Alphabet capex guidance of `$195B-$205B`.
- Duke `$103B` capital plan for 2026-2030.
- Materials and industrial capacity spending.

## Software And Security

`ARR`

Annual recurring revenue. It shows recurring subscription scale.

Watch:

- CrowdStrike ARR after `$5.51B`.
- Security platform consolidation.

`Net new ARR`

Fresh recurring revenue added.

Watch:

- CrowdStrike net new ARR after `$255.8M` in FY2027 Q1.

`Free cash flow`

Cash economics after operating and capital needs.

Watch:

- CrowdStrike free cash flow after `$468.5M` in FY2027 Q1.
- Whether incident-related costs distort non-GAAP quality.

## Finance And Trust

`Underlying revenue growth`

Organic-like growth excluding noise.

Watch:

- Marsh underlying revenue growth after `5%` in Q2 2026.

`Operating margin`

Shows scale and pricing power in data, ratings, and advisory.

Watch:

- S&P Global adjusted operating-profit margin after `+200` bps in Q2 2026.

`AUM and net flows`

For asset managers, this shows whether clients are adding or withdrawing capital.

Watch:

- BlackRock AUM and inflows.
- T. Rowe Price and Franklin net flows and fee rates.

## Commodity Chains

`Realized price`

Price actually received.

Watch:

- Freeport realized copper price after `$6.17` per pound in Q2 2026.

`Unit cash cost`

Cost to produce one unit after byproduct effects.

Watch:

- Freeport unit net cash costs after `$1.97` per pound in Q2 2026.

`Production volume`

Physical output.

Watch:

- Freeport copper pounds.
- CF ammonia tons.
- Nucor steel shipments.

`Utilization`

How much of available capacity is used.

Watch:

- CF running at `98%` of available ammonia capacity year-to-date.
- Steel and packaging plant utilization.

`Capex`

Required investment.

Watch:

- Nucor expected 2026 capex of about `$2.50B`.
- Freeport capex.
- Graphic Packaging capital spending after Waco.
