# Cloudflare, Inc. — edge trust and application control with labor, network, and dilution burdens

Research date: 2026-09-14  
Evidence window: FY2025 and Q2 2026  
Ticker: NET  
Evidence grade: A for reported financials and operating metrics; B for the long-run economics of agentic traffic, platform convergence, and the effect of restructuring on durable margins.

## Bottom line

Cloudflare provides a distributed network and software platform that helps applications handle traffic, security, access, developer execution, and payments. Its control point sits between a customer’s application and the people, devices, bots, and services trying to reach it. The company can sell several functions through one network, which may lower customer complexity and increase platform attachment.

The model has strong demand and recurring characteristics but is not yet a clean GAAP earnings story. FY2025 revenue was $2.168B, operating cash flow was $603M, and free cash flow was $261M after property and equipment plus capitalized internal-use software. GAAP operating loss was $207M, stock-based compensation was $451M, and the company issued $2B of new notes. The cash result is real, but a large portion of operating cash comes from non-cash compensation and deferred revenue while the network requires servers, software, leases, bandwidth, security, and technical labor.

Q2 2026 revenue grew 36% to $696M and current RPO grew 35% year over year. Free cash flow was $56M, but GAAP operating loss widened to $206M and GAAP net loss was $170M. The quarter therefore tests whether Cloudflare’s agentic-AI and machine-to-machine traffic narrative can produce operating leverage after restructuring, infrastructure, security, and equity compensation.

Related first-principles explanation: [Installed network control versus edge trust](../first-principles/installed-network-control-versus-edge-trust.md).

## What Cloudflare controls

The operating chain is:

`application or API -> traffic routing and performance -> identity and security policy -> edge execution -> payment or data transaction -> recurring customer spend`

Cloudflare controls access rules, traffic handling, application protection, developer deployment, and parts of the interaction between a user or machine and an application. The integrated platform can become more valuable as customers face more bots, attacks, APIs, AI agents, and distributed workloads.

Cloudflare does not control customer traffic volume, cloud-provider pricing, application quality, model providers, regulatory rules, attack intensity, or the willingness of customers to consolidate security and networking vendors. It also must keep investing in network capacity and technical reliability even when a customer’s traffic or product mix changes.

## Current operating evidence

Q2 2026 revenue was $696.1M, gross profit was $499.5M, GAAP gross margin was 71.8%, and free cash flow was $56.4M. RPO was approximately $2.732B and current contract liabilities were approximately $812M. The Q2 result had lower gross margin than FY2025, and GAAP operating loss was $205.7M. Non-GAAP operating income was $96.1M, a large difference that requires reconciliation rather than acceptance.

Management raised FY2026 revenue guidance to approximately $2.864B–$2.870B. The company’s narrative emphasizes machine-to-machine traffic, AI agents, Workers, security, payments, and an integrated edge platform. The evidence supports a potentially important control position. The financial test is whether traffic and platform expansion produce improving GAAP operating income and cash per diluted share after the organization is redesigned.

## Earnings quality and cash conversion

### Stock compensation and non-GAAP margins

Cloudflare recorded $451M of stock compensation in FY2025. That was approximately 21% of revenue and 75% of operating cash flow. This does not prove improper reporting. It means that reported free cash flow is not equivalent to cash available to an owner who does not receive new shares.

The company also capitalized $12M of stock compensation for software development and cloud-computing arrangements. Capitalization moves part of the compensation cost from current expense into an asset that is amortized later. The investor should include both expensed and capitalized stock compensation when estimating the recurring labor cost of the platform.

### Free cash flow excludes several claims

Cloudflare defines free cash flow as operating cash flow less purchases of property and equipment and capitalized internal-use software. FY2025 free cash flow was $261M on that basis. The measure does not subtract acquisitions, debt repayment, lease obligations, or dilution. It is a useful liquidity measure, not a final owner-cash measure.

FY2025 financing cash included $2B of gross proceeds from new notes. The balance sheet also includes operating lease obligations and network commitments. A company can have positive free cash flow while increasing debt to fund expansion. The next filings should show whether growth is increasingly self-funded or dependent on external capital.

### Working capital and contract liabilities

Deferred revenue supplied $224M of operating cash in FY2025. This reflects customer billing before Cloudflare has delivered the service. It supports liquidity but creates future obligations. Accounts receivable used $81M and deferred contract acquisition costs used $149M. Sales growth therefore requires both collection and continued spending to acquire and retain contracts.

RPO and contract liabilities provide visibility, but customers can renegotiate, fail to renew, or require support and implementation resources. The investor should compare RPO conversion, gross margin, support labor, network cost, and cash per diluted share.

### Restructuring is a business-model test

Management announced an expected reduction of approximately 1,100 employees in Q1 2026 as part of an agentic-AI-first operating model. Workforce reduction can improve future margins, but it can also create severance, lost technical capacity, customer-support strain, and execution risk. If restructuring charges recur as the company repeatedly redesigns the organization, adding them back as “one-time” costs would overstate sustainable earnings.

The thesis requires evidence that the new model produces more revenue and cash per employee without weakening security, reliability, product development, or customer retention.

## Lyn Alden lens: durability and liquidity

Cloudflare has meaningful liquidity: cash, cash equivalents, and available-for-sale securities were approximately $4.163B at June 30, 2026. That provides resilience against an ordinary slowdown. The macro risk is less about immediate survival and more about the relationship between enterprise budgets, internet traffic, attack intensity, cloud spending, and the cost of maintaining global network capacity.

The company’s durability depends on customers continuing to treat security, traffic control, and application availability as essential. A weaker environment may delay new platform modules while preserving core protection. Higher rates also affect the valuation of a company whose cash flows are expected far in the future. Cloudflare’s debt, lease, network, and workforce commitments matter because the company must continue operating the platform even if growth slows.

## Damodaran lens: narrative to numbers

The narrative is: Cloudflare is becoming an integrated edge, security, developer, and payment layer for an internet increasingly used by machines and AI agents.

That narrative must be translated into:

- large-customer growth, retention, and platform attachment;
- current RPO conversion into revenue and cash;
- traffic and workload growth by product;
- gross margin after bandwidth, colocation, servers, and compute;
- R&D, support, sales efficiency, and restructuring cost;
- stock compensation, diluted shares, and debt-funded investment;
- acquisition returns and the economics of newer products;
- the required network and software reinvestment rate;
- terminal margin after the agentic-traffic investment cycle.

At a market value of approximately $108.614B on September 14, 2026, Cloudflare was valued at about 417 times FY2025 free cash flow and about 50 times FY2025 revenue. A simple cash scenario shows the expectations embedded in that valuation:

| Normalized annual owner-cash screen | Multiple | Implied equity value |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $0.5B bear case | 80x | $40B |
| $1.5B base case | 90x | $135B |
| $3.0B strong case | 100x | $300B |

These are scenario outputs, not price targets. The base case requires major growth in cash after network investment, restructuring, stock compensation, debt, and support cost. The current market value therefore depends on a long period of high growth and significant improvement in owner economics.

## Thesis breakers and next evidence

The thesis weakens if current RPO grows but fails to convert into revenue and cash; if gross margin continues to fall; if AI and machine traffic produces usage without pricing power; if non-GAAP profit grows while GAAP operating loss remains structurally large; if stock compensation remains a large share of operating cash; if restructuring recurs; if large customers demand discounts or consolidate vendors; if debt and lease commitments rise faster than cash generation; or if security incidents damage trust.

The next filings should answer: Did current RPO convert at stable margin? Did the workforce reduction improve GAAP operating leverage? Did free cash flow grow after stock compensation and capitalized software? Did diluted shares decline? Did network infrastructure spending stabilize relative to revenue? Did Workers, security, payments, and agentic products generate incremental customer spend rather than simply bundle existing value?
