# FMC — crop-protection chemistry, patent erosion, and leveraged portfolio repair

As of 2026-09-13. FY2025 figures are filed U.S. GAAP figures in U.S. dollars;
dollar amounts are in millions unless otherwise stated.

## How to read this memo

An active ingredient is the chemical that provides a crop-protection effect.
Formulation combines that ingredient with other materials so it can be applied
to a crop. A patent can protect an ingredient or formulation for a period, after
which generic products can compete. Registration is the regulatory permission
to sell a product in a particular country. Non-recourse factoring is the sale
of receivables where the buyer generally bears the collection risk; it creates
cash today but reduces the future receivable balance and must be tracked as a
financing-quality question.

## Investment question

FMC develops, manufactures, registers, and sells crop-protection chemistry. Its
potential advantage is a portfolio of active ingredients, formulations,
regulatory approvals, technical support, and grower relationships. The central
question is whether new products and lower-cost manufacturing can replace the
cash and pricing power lost as older products face generic competition, while
the company repairs a leveraged balance sheet.

## What the annual and quarterly record reveals

- FMC's economic asset is not simply a chemical plant. It is the combination of
  active ingredients, formulations, registrations, manufacturing know-how,
  channel access, and grower adoption. Each has a different replacement cost
  and duration.
- Rynaxypyr is central to the transition. The company is managing post-patent
  pressure through lower-priced basic formulations, new mixtures, and newer
  active ingredients. Branded sales stability is not enough if price and mix
  deteriorate.
- Fluindapyr, Isoflex, and Dodhylex generated about $200M of 2025 sales and grew
  54%. That is evidence of product replacement, not yet evidence that the new
  portfolio can replace the older portfolio's full margin and cash.
- Project Foundation is a manufacturing-footprint reset. Exiting high-cost
  sites and improving sourcing may raise future margins, but restructuring cash,
  shutdown costs, stranded assets, and supply reliability must be included.
- India is a major strategic and accounting event. The planned commercial sale,
  approximately $522M of 2025 India-held-for-sale adjustments, and continued
  supply agreements change both the reported asset base and the future revenue
  path.
- Brazil direct sales and technical service may improve access to large growers,
  but they also increase local working capital, credit, personnel, regulatory,
  and channel-execution requirements.
- FY2025 continuing OCF was approximately negative $6M even though adjusted
  EBITDA was $843M. That gap is the core earnings-quality issue, not a minor
  reconciliation detail.

## Forensic earnings-quality questions

### 1. Can adjusted EBITDA become cash?

Bridge $843M adjusted EBITDA to continuing operating cash flow. Identify
receivables, factoring, inventory, customer rebates, supplier terms, taxes,
interest, restructuring cash, environmental spending, and pension claims. A
large adjustment schedule is not automatically wrong, but recurring adjustments
reduce the quality of the measure.

### 2. Is the patent transition economically successful?

Track Rynaxypyr price, volume, branded versus basic formulations, geographic
mix, gross margin, and customer retention. Compare new active-ingredient sales
with their manufacturing cost, registration spending, launch spending, and
incremental cash. New-product revenue that requires heavy working capital or
low pricing may not replace lost economics.

### 3. What does factoring conceal?

Track the $300.4M of non-recourse factoring and the receivables that would have
remained without it. Compare factoring cost, customer collection days, revenue,
inventory, and operating cash. Factoring can be commercially normal; it becomes
a warning when it expands as cash generation weakens.

### 4. Are restructuring charges genuinely temporary?

Maintain a year-by-year schedule for Project Foundation, India, site exits,
severance, supply-chain changes, and environmental spending. Compare announced
savings with cash actually saved after replacement capacity, freight, quality,
and customer-service costs.

### 5. Does the India sale repair the company?

Reconcile sale proceeds, taxes, working-capital release, retained liabilities,
supply agreements, lost EBITDA, and debt repayment. The sale may improve risk
without creating value if the company gives up profitable future cash or keeps
the costly obligations.

### 6. Is goodwill write-off a prior capital-allocation signal?

The $1.356B goodwill write-off does not itself use current cash, but it indicates
that earlier purchase-price assumptions no longer held. Review the acquired
businesses' historical margins, cash, impairment tests, and management incentive
metrics before treating future bolt-ons as value creation.

## Lyn Alden / Damodaran / forensic synthesis

- **Lyn Alden lens:** crop protection is tied to food production and is less
  discretionary for growers, but FMC's leverage, foreign cash, regulatory
  exposure, and patent cycle reduce resilience. Strategic need does not remove
  refinancing or cash-conversion risk.
- **Damodaran lens:** value the mature portfolio, post-patent products, new
  active ingredients, manufacturing reset, and India/Brazil changes separately.
  Use explicit product-life assumptions, registration probability, incremental
  margins, working capital, and debt paydown rather than a stable industry
  multiple.
- **Forensic lens:** the largest risks are treating adjusted EBITDA as cash,
  treating factoring as ordinary collection, treating restructuring as one-time
  despite repeated resets, and treating a goodwill write-off as unrelated to
  earlier acquisition returns.

## Valuation framing

The 2026-09-11 market snapshot was approximately $1.43B of equity value at
$11.40 per share. The FY2025 continuing OCF-less-PP&E-and-acquisitions screen
was negative $105M, so a current cash multiple is not meaningful. A recovery
range of $0 / $300M / $600M at 10x / 12x / 15x implies $0 / $3.6B / $9.0B.
The apparent upside is a distressed-repair scenario, not a demonstrated margin
of safety. It requires new-product replacement, manufacturing savings, working-
capital normalization, asset-sale proceeds, and debt reduction to occur
together.

## Thesis breakers and next checks

1. Rynaxypyr and other legacy products lose price faster than new products gain
   volume and margin.
2. New active ingredients require more registration, launch, or manufacturing
   spending than expected.
3. Factoring and inventory release support cash temporarily but do not produce
   repeatable operating cash.
4. Project Foundation savings are offset by shutdown, freight, quality, or
   supply-reliability costs.
5. India sale proceeds, licensing cash, or property proceeds are delayed or
   insufficient to reduce debt materially.
6. Debt remains high while continuing operating cash stays near zero or negative.
