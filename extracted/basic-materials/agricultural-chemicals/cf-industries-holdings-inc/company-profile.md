## What changed

- Business shape: CF is a much purer nitrogen and ammonia producer than Nutrien. That makes it a sharper read on upstream fertilizer pricing, natural-gas input costs, plant utilization, and nitrogen trade disruption.
- 2025 base year: full-year `2025` net earnings were `$1.46 billion`, adjusted EBITDA was `$2.89 billion`, net cash from operations was `$2.75 billion`, and free cash flow was `$1.79 billion`.
- Capital return: CF repurchased `16.6 million` shares for `$1.34 billion` in `2025`, cutting its share count by about `10%` versus year-end `2024`. The company also raised its quarterly dividend to `$0.60` in July `2026`.
- Operations: full-year gross ammonia production in `2025` was about `10.1 million` tons. Through the first half of `2026`, gross ammonia production was about `4.9 million` tons and available ammonia capacity utilization was `98%`.
- Constraint story: the key operating drag is the Yazoo City outage after the November `2025` incident. CF still expects about `9.5 million` tons of gross ammonia production in full-year `2026`, with Yazoo recovery now pointed into the first half of `2027`.
- Demand and pricing: the quarterlies repeatedly point to tight global nitrogen supply-demand conditions, stronger benchmark prices, and geopolitical supply disruption, especially around Iran-related supply tightening in `2026`.
- Clean-energy angle: Blue Point gives the company a second analytical layer beyond commodity fertilizer. CF, JERA, and Mitsui are building a low-carbon ammonia project in which CF owns `40%`, with permits received in July `2026` and construction beginning in August.
- Commercial angle: `Q1 2026` also introduced a low-carbon UAN collaboration with PepsiCo's Frito-Lay potato supply chain, which shows how decarbonization products are beginning to connect with real end-market buyers.

## Open questions

- How durable are current nitrogen margins if geopolitical supply pressure eases?
- When Yazoo fully returns, does CF regain enough volume to lift earnings materially without relying on another pricing spike?
- Will Blue Point become a real earnings-quality improvement or stay mainly a strategic option value story for now?
