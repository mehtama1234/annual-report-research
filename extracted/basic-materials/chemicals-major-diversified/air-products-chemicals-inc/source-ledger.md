## Reconciliation notes

- As of `Monday, August 10, 2026`, the correct quarter set was `FY2026 Q3`, `FY2026 Q2`, and `FY2026 Q1`.
- Air Products has a `September 30` fiscal year-end, so quarter labels should be kept in fiscal-year form rather than converted loosely into calendar-quarter shorthand.
- AnnualReports was useful for taxonomy confirmation and archive status, but the main annual and quarter evidence came from official IR documents and SEC filings.
- The packet should preserve one central distinction:
  - the core industrial-gases franchise shows relatively steady adjusted operating performance
  - the hydrogen, ammonia, and clean-energy megaproject portfolio can create very large GAAP volatility and capital-allocation risk
- Use the explicit total-debt figures from the annual report and `FY2026 Q1` `10-Q`; do not substitute the narrower related-party debt line from later-quarter note detail for total company debt.
