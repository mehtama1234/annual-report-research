## What changed

- Portfolio shape: Dow gives the archive the diversified chemicals and materials-science layer that the current Basic Materials starter set was missing. The `2025` annual filing describes a company operating through `6` global businesses organized into `3` operating segments: Packaging & Specialty Plastics, Industrial Intermediates & Infrastructure, and Performance Materials & Coatings.
- Operating footprint: Dow reported `91` manufacturing sites in `29` countries, approximately `34,600` employees, and sales of approximately `$40 billion` in `2025`. This is a global asset network story, not a narrow commodity producer.
- 2025 base year: the fourth-quarter and full-year materials show a weak `2025` earnings base despite large scale. Full-year `2025` net sales were `$40.0 billion`, GAAP net loss was `$2.4 billion`, operating EBIT was about `$0.4 billion`, and cash provided by operating activities from continuing operations was `$1.1 billion`.
- Transform to Outperform: the annual filing and `2026` quarterlies make clear that management has shifted into a major restructuring and productivity program. Transform to Outperform was approved in late January `2026`, targets at least `$2 billion` of near-term operating EBITDA improvement, and includes a workforce reduction of approximately `4,500` roles.
- Chemicals cycle plus self-help: the current Dow story is not just end-market demand. It is the interaction of polyethylene pricing, European and Middle East supply constraints, downstream mix, asset shutdowns, and management self-help actions.
- Quarter pattern: `Q1 2026` still looked pressured, with net sales of `$9.8 billion` and operating EBITDA of `$873 million`, but `Q2 2026` showed a sharp step-up to `$12.1 billion` of sales and `$2.312 billion` of operating EBITDA as higher polyethylene prices and self-help actions drove margin recovery.
- Segment read: Packaging & Specialty Plastics is the most powerful near-term earnings lever. In `Q2 2026`, the segment delivered `$6.385 billion` of sales and `$1.278 billion` of operating EBIT, far larger than Industrial Intermediates & Infrastructure at `$246 million` of operating EBIT and Performance Materials & Coatings at `$133 million`.
- End-market and cultural signal: Dow sits between upstream commodity markets and downstream industrial and consumer applications. Packaging, infrastructure, mobility, electronics, home care, and data-center-related applications all show up in the quarterlies, making this a good bridge name between raw materials and finished-product demand.
