# LyondellBasell Industries N.V. Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: LyondellBasell Industries N.V.
- Sector: Basic Materials
- Industry: Chemicals - Major Diversified
- Ticker: LYB
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- LyondellBasell adds a large global polyolefins and petrochemical packet that is distinct from `Dow`, `Celanese`, or `Westlake`. This is not mainly a specialty-materials or built-environment read. It is a spread-cycle, feedstock-advantage, utilization, and asset-footprint story with unusually direct exposure to global energy and supply dislocation.
- The annual `2025` read was difficult on a GAAP basis. Sales and other operating revenues were `$30.153 billion`, down from `$33.394 billion` in `2024`. Net loss was `($738 million)`, diluted loss per share was `($2.34)`, and EBITDA was `$1.1 billion`.
- Management's adjusted frame was materially stronger. Excluding identified items, full-year `2025` net income was `$563 million`, diluted EPS was `$1.70`, EBITDA was `$2.5 billion`, and cash from operating activities was `$2.3 billion`.
- The most important annual signal was capital discipline rather than simple volume growth. Management said the Cash Improvement Plan achieved `$800 million` in `2025`, above the original `$600 million` target, and increased the cumulative target to `$1.3 billion` by the end of `2026`.
- Year-end liquidity and cash conversion mattered. Management highlighted `95%` cash conversion in `2025` and said the company ended the year with `$3.4 billion` of cash and cash equivalents.
- The company was also still in reshaping mode. The annual and quarter chain together showed a business exiting weaker footprints, tightening capital allocation, and relying on cost-advantaged North American assets to defend earnings through a volatile market.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: `Q2 2026` sales and other operating revenues were `$9.177 billion`, net income was `$559 million`, diluted EPS was `$1.71`, and EBITDA was `$1.252 billion`.
- Adjusted frame: excluding identified items, net income was `$1.401 billion`, diluted EPS was `$4.30`, and EBITDA was `$2.127 billion`.
- Business read: management said a dynamic macro environment, global supply disruption, and geopolitical instability tightened markets, while commercial actions and an advantaged portfolio helped the company capture stronger polymer margins and co-product pricing.
- Operations read: North American assets operated at about `90%` utilization, and the `O&P Americas and International` business benefited from improved polymer spreads and joint-venture contributions.
- Cash and balance-sheet read: cash from operating activities was `$752 million`, capital expenditures were `$270 million`, dividends paid were `$224 million`, cash and cash equivalents were `$2.6 billion`, and available liquidity was `$7.1 billion`.
- Portfolio read: management said the divestiture of four European assets was completed during the quarter and reiterated the target of another `$500 million` of incremental cash by the end of `2026` under the Cash Improvement Plan.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `Q1 2026` sales and other operating revenues were `$7.197 billion`, net income was `$125 million`, diluted EPS was `$0.38`, and EBITDA was `$568 million`.
- Adjusted frame: excluding identified items, net income was `$163 million`, diluted EPS was `$0.49`, and EBITDA was `$615 million`.
- Business read: management said the Middle East war steepened the global cost curve for the petrochemical industry, but LyondellBasell used cost-advantaged North American production and European raw-material pass-through to improve results sequentially.
- Operating-system read: management linked the quarter to operational discipline, commercial execution, portfolio actions, the Value Enhancement Program, and fixed-cost reductions under the Cash Improvement Plan.
- Interpretation: `Q1 2026` was still a cautious recovery quarter, but it showed why LyondellBasell belongs in this archive. The earnings path depends not just on end demand, but on where the global cost curve moves when energy shocks and regional disruptions reprice plastics and chemicals.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: `Q4 2025` sales and other operating revenues were `$7.091 billion`, net loss was `($140 million)`, diluted loss per share was `($0.45)`, and EBITDA was `$345 million`.
- Adjusted frame: excluding identified items, fourth-quarter net loss was `($79 million)` and EBITDA was `$417 million`.
- Full-year read: for `2025`, net loss was `($738 million)`, diluted loss per share was `($2.34)`, EBITDA was `$1.1 billion`, and cash from operating activities was `$2.3 billion`.
- Capital read: management said the Cash Improvement Plan outperformed the original goal by `$200 million`, reaching `$800 million` in `2025`, and set a new cumulative goal of `$1.3 billion` by the end of `2026`.
- Working interpretation: `Q4 2025` is the cleanest explanation of what LYB is in this archive. It is a commodity-chemicals platform trying to preserve value through cash discipline, footprint rationalization, and advantaged feedstocks during a weak spread environment.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: LyondellBasell expands the branch beyond oil and gas, metals, fertilizers, industrial gases, and specialty materials into the global petrochemical spread and polyolefins lane.
- Consumer: this is still an upstream and intermediate-goods packet, but it ultimately sits behind consumer packaging, auto parts, construction materials, durable goods, and broad plastics demand.
- Cultural and societal: the bigger societal signal is that modern daily-life systems still depend on commodity polymers and chemicals whose economics can change sharply when war, logistics disruption, or regional plant outages tighten supply.
- Industrial: this is one of the cleaner packets for showing how chemicals earnings can hinge on feedstock advantage, utilization, polymer spreads, co-product pricing, and the ability to move production toward the most advantaged regions.
- Energy security and infrastructure dependence: `Q1 2026` and `Q2 2026` both showed that petrochemical margins are inseparable from energy geography. Middle East conflict, global supply disruption, and North American feedstock position all altered the earnings path directly.
- Capital intensity: the packet also shows how a downturn is managed through capex discipline, working-capital focus, fixed-cost cuts, portfolio exits, and liquidity preservation rather than only through volume growth.
- Comparative read: `Dow` is the broader materials-science and spread-cycle packet. `Celanese` is the more leveraged specialty-materials and acetyls repair packet. `Westlake` is the downstream PVC and built-environment packet. LyondellBasell is the clearer global polyolefins, feedstock-advantage, and portfolio-rationalization packet.

## Working interpretation

- LyondellBasell materially improves the heavy-input branch because it shows that some commodity-chemical names are really global cost-curve and capital-allocation systems. Their earnings can swing violently with feedstock position, polymer spreads, regional utilization, and geopolitical shocks.
- The packet works best when the company is read in three layers:
  - a North American feedstock-advantaged chemicals and plastics platform
  - a global spread-cycle system exposed to Europe and broader trade flows
  - a cash-discipline and footprint-rationalization story trying to preserve value through a volatile part of the cycle
- That makes LYB one of the cleaner bridges between energy, chemicals, logistics disruption, and everyday industrial dependence. It is not just a raw commodity bet. It is part of the physical conversion layer behind packaging, mobility, construction, and modern distribution.
