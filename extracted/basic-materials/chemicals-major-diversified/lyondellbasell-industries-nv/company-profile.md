## What changed

- LyondellBasell adds a different chemicals packet from `Dow`, `Celanese`, or `Westlake`. This is a global polyolefins and petrochemical spread-cycle platform whose earnings are heavily shaped by feedstock position, utilization, co-product pricing, geopolitical disruption, and asset-footprint rationalization.
- The `2025` annual read is less about clean growth than about downturn management and balance-sheet preservation. Full-year sales were `$30.153 billion`, net loss was `($738 million)`, diluted loss per share was `($2.34)`, and EBITDA was `$1.1 billion`.
- Management's adjusted frame was much stronger. Excluding identified items, `2025` net income was `$563 million`, diluted EPS was `$1.70`, EBITDA was `$2.5 billion`, and cash from operating activities was `$2.3 billion`.
- The company used `2025` and early `2026` to push a sharper capital-discipline and cash-conversion story. Management said the Cash Improvement Plan delivered `$800 million` in `2025`, above the original `$600 million` target, and increased the cumulative target to `$1.3 billion` by the end of `2026`.
- The operating geography matters. `Q1 2026` and `Q2 2026` both highlighted the value of North American feedstock advantage, while Europe remained more pressured and portfolio rationalization became more explicit through the divestiture of four European assets completed in `Q2 2026`.

## Open questions

- How much of the `Q2 2026` strength was a durable margin reset versus a temporary tightening caused by supply disruption and geopolitical instability?
- Can the Cash Improvement Plan and European footprint actions preserve value if spreads weaken again?
- Does LyondellBasell become a cleaner capital-discipline and portfolio-upgrade story from here, or does it remain primarily a high-beta petrochemical spread vehicle?
