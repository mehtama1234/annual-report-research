# First Quantum Minerals Ltd. Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

- Sector: Basic Materials
- Industry: Copper

- Fiscal year-end: December

- Target reported-period window:
  - latest period: `Q2 2026`
  - period minus 1: `Q1 2026`
  - period minus 2: `Q4 2025`

## Annual report takeaways

- First Quantum belongs in this archive as the concentrated copper-growth and jurisdiction-risk version of the mining story. The diversified giants in this branch spread risk across iron ore, coal, aluminum, trading, fertilizer, or larger geographic portfolios. First Quantum is much more dependent on whether a narrower set of copper and nickel assets execute cleanly and whether political permission arrives in time.
- The saved AnnualReports page confirms taxonomy, but the authoritative source chain had to come from First Quantum's own investor pages, annual report, and quarterly reporting set.
- The annual framing presents First Quantum as one of the world's top `10` copper producers with significant interests in nickel. The `2025` annual materials also confirm `396 thousand tonnes` of copper production, `152 thousand ounces` of gold production, and `23.2 thousand tonnes` of nickel production.
- `2025` shows why asset concentration matters. Kansanshi benefited from the S3 expansion and reached its highest annual copper production since `2021`, while Sentinel was pressured by lower grades and maintenance, and Cobre Panama remained suspended but strategically central to the equity story.
- Development optionality is another core annual signal. First Quantum still carries longer-cycle copper-growth options such as `Taca Taca` and `La Granja`, but those options only matter financially if the existing operating base can fund them and if the balance sheet remains serviceable while Panama is unresolved.

## Reported-period takeaways

### Latest period

- The latest reported period by `Monday, August 10, 2026` is `Q2 2026`, reported on `2026-07-28`.
- Sales revenue was `$1.522 billion`, gross profit was `$297 million`, EBITDA was `$400 million`, and net earnings attributable to shareholders were `$136 million` or `$0.16` per share.
- The quarter still carried important distortions. EBITDA included hedge losses of `$164 million`, and Cobre Panama still contributed negative EBITDA of `$51 million`.
- Total copper production in `Q2 2026` was `100,487` tonnes. Kansanshi produced `43,997` tonnes, Sentinel produced `50,335` tonnes, and Enterprise produced `11,246` tonnes of nickel.
- The strategic headline is that Cobre Panama moved from preservation toward partial operational reality. First Quantum said it produced first concentrate from one milling circuit, processed stockpiled ore, and described the stockpile as about `38 million` tonnes of ore containing around `70,000` tonnes of recoverable copper, enough for roughly `12 months` at current processing rates.
- The latest-period costs also matter. Copper C3 cost was `$4.12` per pound, AISC was `$4.27` per pound, and realized copper price was `$5.34` per pound. Management also noted that sustained higher fuel prices and a stronger Zambian kwacha could add roughly `$0.25` per pound to C1 costs if those conditions persisted.
- Structural theme: `Q2 2026` shows why First Quantum is not just a copper-price packet. It is a leverage, restart, and recommissioning packet where stockpiles, political approvals, FX, and fuel logistics can swing economics almost as much as grade or realized price.

### Period minus 1

- `Q1 2026`, reported on `2026-04-28`, is the clearest bridge quarter between the `2025` year-end reset and the partial Panama restart.
- Gross profit was `$278 million`, EBITDA was `$326 million`, and net loss attributable to shareholders was `$196 million` or `$0.24` per share.
- Management said the weaker quarter relative to `Q4 2025` reflected lower sales volumes, a stronger Zambian kwacha, and hedge losses. EBITDA included realized losses of `$144 million` under the sales hedge program, and management said no derivative contracts were outstanding beyond `June 2026`.
- Operationally, management said Zambia remained on track with stronger performance expected in the second half as higher grades were accessed, while Panama had received formal approval to proceed with removal and processing of stockpiled ore.
- The quarter is also important as an industrial-pressure read. Management tied rising cost risk directly to Middle East disruption, uncertain diesel supply, and tight global sulphur availability, while arguing that First Quantum's own smelter system reduced dependence on external acid supply.
- Structural theme: `Q1 2026` is one of the better packets in the branch for showing how upstream mining economics can be reshaped by fuel sourcing, hedging programs, FX moves, and process-chemicals security before mine output itself has materially changed.

### Period minus 2

- `Q4 2025`, reported on `2026-02-10`, is the right base period for this packet because it fixed the first post-S3 operating picture and framed the Cobre Panama stockpile pathway.
- Gross profit was `$416 million`, EBITDA was `$464 million`, and net earnings attributable to shareholders were `$25 million` or `$0.03` per share.
- Total copper production was `100,374` tonnes, down `4%` from `Q3 2025`, with Kansanshi at `47,655` tonnes and Sentinel at `48,235` tonnes.
- Kansanshi's S3 expansion declared commercial production on `2025-12-01`, which is a major marker because the company has been carrying that expansion as part of its recovery and growth case for several periods.
- Net debt increased by `$441 million` during the quarter to `$5.192 billion`, while management refinanced into a new `$2.2 billion` term loan and revolving credit facility. That refinancing matters because it shows the company was using balance-sheet engineering to buy time and flexibility while Panama remained unresolved.
- The quarter also framed the economics of stockpiled ore at Cobre Panama. Management said processing could yield about `70,000` tonnes of copper and outlined non-recurring cash outflows of roughly `$90` million to `$100` million for commissioning, `$40` million to `$50` million for inventory, and `$100` million to `$130` million in sustaining capital.
- Structural theme: `Q4 2025` makes the company's identity legible. This is a copper-heavy producer where one suspended megamine, one major expansion ramp, one pressured Zambian asset, and a leveraged balance sheet all interact at once.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: First Quantum adds the concentrated copper and restart-risk layer that the broader diversified miners do not capture cleanly.
- Consumer: there is no direct consumer-angle read, but the packet sits upstream of electrification, wiring, power, grid, and industrial equipment demand, making it a useful physical bottleneck packet rather than a consumer-demand packet.
- Cultural: there is no consumer-culture angle here. The broader social signal is that copper has become strategically important enough that management now frames disruptions, fuel sourcing, and Panama negotiations in explicitly geopolitical and electrification-linked terms.
- Societal: this is one of the clearest packets for the social license and political legitimacy side of mining. Cobre Panama is not a normal mine-ramp problem; it is a live example of how national politics, employment promises, environmental stewardship, and public legitimacy can override normal industrial logic.
- Regulatory: Panama is the central regulatory driver, but Zambia also matters through currency pressure, power-cost sensitivity, and the operating-performance link between mine plans, smelting, and throughput.
- Capital allocation: First Quantum is still a heavy-capex, balance-sheet-managed operator. S3 ramp quality, debt refinancing, recommissioning costs, and whether Taca Taca or La Granja can be funded are all connected.
- Industrial: the packet shows that pure copper systems are not interchangeable. Some depend on giant established assets, some on diversified by-product support, and some on whether a narrower cluster of mines and political approvals can keep the whole platform intact.
- Comparative read: `Freeport-McMoRan` is the giant-asset and operating-rights copper packet, `Southern Copper` is the Peru/Mexico and by-product-supported copper packet, and `First Quantum` is the Zambia-and-Panama restart, leverage, hedge, and project-optionality packet.
