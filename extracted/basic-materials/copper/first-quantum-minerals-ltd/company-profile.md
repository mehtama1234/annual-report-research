## What changed

- First Quantum adds a more concentrated copper system than the giant diversified miners already in the lane. The center of gravity is not portfolio breadth. It is whether Zambia executes, whether Panama restarts on terms that are politically durable, and whether leverage and capex are carried safely while the company waits for full operating normalization.
- The annual base year shows why the packet belongs in the archive. First Quantum describes itself as one of the world's top `10` copper producers with significant interests in nickel, and the annual materials show `2025` copper production of `396 thousand tonnes`, gold production of `152 thousand ounces`, and nickel production of `23.2 thousand tonnes`.
- `2025` operating performance was heavily split by asset. Kansanshi benefited from the S3 expansion and reached its highest annual copper production since `2021`, while Sentinel was pressured by lower grades and maintenance, and Cobre Panama remained a suspended but strategically central asset.
- `Q4 2025` matters because it locked in several structural signals at once: S3 declared commercial production on `2025-12-01`, Q4 copper production was `100,374` tonnes, net debt rose to `$5.192 billion`, and management refinanced the bank facility to push maturities out and preserve flexibility.
- `Q1 2026` is the cleanest read on the cost side of the story. Gross profit was `$278 million` and EBITDA was `$326 million`, but realized hedge losses of `$144 million`, a stronger Zambian kwacha, and fuel pressure tied to Middle East disruption pushed the quarter to a net loss. Management also said there were no derivative contracts outstanding beyond `June 2026`, which matters because it reduces one source of earnings noise going forward.
- `Q2 2026` shows the operational bridge from preservation to partial restart at Cobre Panama. Sales revenue rose to `$1.522 billion`, EBITDA reached `$400 million`, and the company said Cobre Panama produced first concentrate from one milling circuit while Kansanshi's S3 circuit performed above design capacity.
- The latest quarter also shows how binary the Panama asset still is. Cobre Panama produced `3,216` tonnes of copper from stockpiled ore, the stockpile was described as about `38 million` tonnes of ore containing around `70,000` tonnes of recoverable copper, and management said the recommissioning, inventory, and sustaining-capital requirement was about `$250 million`.
- Balance-sheet and operating-risk linkage is central here. `Q2 2026` net debt remained high at `$5.407 billion`, and the quarter still included a negative EBITDA contribution from Cobre Panama plus hedge losses. First Quantum is not a clean spot-copper proxy. It is a leveraged execution case where cost inflation, FX, fuel sourcing, and political permission sets can overwhelm headline copper prices for periods of time.

## Open questions

- Does Cobre Panama become a durable economic restart or remain a recurring political and legal overhang that keeps leverage elevated?
- Once hedging rolls off, how much cleaner does First Quantum's copper exposure become relative to the still-rising cost pressure from fuel and the Zambian kwacha?
