## What changed

- Operating identity: Knife River is an aggregates-based, vertically integrated construction materials and contracting operator rather than a simple quarry or paving name.
- Resource base: the `10-K` says the company had `1.3 billion` tons of aggregate reserves, with about `35%` of `2025` aggregate volume used internally to support downstream ready-mix, asphalt, and contracting work.
- 2025 base year: direct SEC and year-end earnings materials point to revenue of `$3.146B`, net income of `$157.1M`, adjusted EBITDA of `$496.5M`, and backlog of `$1.0B` at year-end.
- Demand quality: backlog was heavily public-funding-linked, with about `89%` of year-end `2025` backlog tied to publicly funded projects.
- Operating model: the company’s `Competitive EDGE` framework explicitly ties pricing, operating efficiency, discipline, growth, and execution together as the main route to margin expansion.

## Open questions

- How durable is Knife River’s current margin profile if public-funding strength remains intact but energy costs and contracting mix stay volatile?
- Does the acquisition pipeline keep improving vertical pull-through and local density, or does it push leverage and integration risk faster than margins improve?
- How much of the current growth runway is tied to structurally durable infrastructure and population-shift demand versus a particularly favorable public-works cycle?
