## What changed

- Business model: Franco-Nevada is the cleanest second royalty-and-streaming comparator in this branch after `Wheaton Precious Metals`, but it is not a duplicate. The company combines precious-metals royalty and stream exposure with a still-material diversified branch that includes oil, gas, NGL, and iron ore revenue.
- Archive fit: this packet gives the gold lane a second capital-light allocator model, allowing a cleaner split between mine operators, streamers, and hybrid diversified royalty systems.
- `2025` base year: official year-end materials show `519K` GEOs sold in `2025`, with `440K` coming from precious metals and `79K` from diversified assets.
- Revenue mix: `85%` of `2025` revenue came from precious metals, which keeps the company gold-led even though it still benefits from non-gold and energy-linked cash flow.
- Profitability structure: the year-end presentation shows annual GEO margin rising to `$3,110` in `2025` from `$2,109` in `2024`, with annual cash cost per GEO at `$325`.
- Balance-sheet posture: the company finished `2025` debt-free and presented `$3.1 billion` of available capital as of `2025-12-31`, which matters because Franco-Nevada's model is built around maintaining optionality for the next royalty or stream acquisition.
- `Q3 2025` pattern: record results were already visible before year-end, with `Q3 2025` revenue of `$487.7 million`, `138,772` GEOs sold, `85%` of revenue from precious metals, and the company back to debt-free status after funding the Arthur Gold acquisition.
- `Q4 2025` / annual pattern: the year-end read is less about one mine or one quarter and more about the shape of the franchise. `2025` actual GEO sales reached `519K`, the company stayed debt-free, and available capital remained large enough to keep layering new deals into the platform.
- `Q1 2026` pattern: revenue rose to `$650.7 million`, GEOs sold rose to `136,353`, operating cash flow reached `$520.4 million`, net income reached `$468.6 million`, and available capital increased to `$3.4 billion` despite multiple new transactions.
- Energy and Cobre Panama matter: unlike a pure gold streamer, Franco-Nevada's `Q1 2026` mix included `$33.5 million` of oil revenue, `$20.6 million` of gas revenue, and `$5.3 million` of NGL revenue, while management also highlighted expected future deliveries from Cobre Panama stockpiled ore.
- Operating structure: this packet is useful because it shows a precious-metals royalty model that remains insulated from direct mining cost inflation while still preserving upside to energy prices and diversified-resource optionality.
