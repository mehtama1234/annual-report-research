## Annual report takeaways

- Newmont is the clearest gold flagship for this lane because the `2025` annual chain shows a mining supermajor that is not just a gold-price proxy. The company is a portfolio-optimization, reserve-life, capital-allocation, and large-project execution platform with gold at the center and copper or silver as meaningful supporting metals.
- AnnualReports confirms the `Basic Materials` / `Gold` taxonomy, but as of `2026-08-10` it still lagged at the `2024 Annual Report and Form 10-K`. The authoritative `2025` annual chain therefore comes from the official IR annual-report PDF and the SEC `10-K`.
- The `2025` annual materials show a business operating at exceptional scale. Newmont reported adjusted EBITDA of `$13.48 billion`, net cash provided by operating activities of `$10.33 billion`, free cash flow of `$7.30 billion`, cash and cash equivalents of `$7.65 billion`, and attributable gold production of `5.889 million` ounces.
- The annual report and the `Q4 2025` release together show a decisive balance-sheet strengthening year. Management said Newmont reduced debt by `$3.4 billion` in `2025`, ended the year in a net cash position of `$2.1 billion`, and had `$7.6 billion` in cash with `$11.6 billion` of total liquidity.
- Full-year `2025` production and cost guidance was achieved with `5.7 million` attributable gold ounces from the core portfolio and `5.9 million` attributable gold ounces in total, plus `28 million` ounces of silver and `135 thousand` tonnes of copper.
- The biggest strategic signal in the annual chain is that Newmont is trying to convert commodity strength into a more repeatable capital-allocation model. Management paired record free cash flow with an enhanced capital allocation framework, continuing share repurchases, a higher dividend, and still-material development spending.
- This packet also matters because it shows how a gold major differs from both a smaller precious-metals company and a hydrocarbon producer. Scale here is tied to reserve depth, project sequencing, processing complexity, jurisdictional diversity, and the ability to keep multiple long-life mines and expansions moving at once.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: `Q2 2026` reported net income was `$2.2 billion`, adjusted net income was `$2.2 billion` or `$2.10` per diluted share, and adjusted EBITDA was `$3.8 billion`.
- Cash flow and capital allocation: cash from operating activities was `$2.9 billion`, free cash flow was a record second-quarter `$2.2 billion`, and shareholder returns since the prior earnings call totaled `$1.9 billion` through dividends and repurchases.
- Operations: Newmont produced approximately `1.3 million` attributable gold ounces, as well as `7 million` ounces of silver and `17 thousand` tonnes of copper, while remaining on track for full-year `2026` guidance of `5.3 million` attributable gold ounces.
- Balance sheet: the company ended the quarter with `$9.0 billion` of cash and `$13.0 billion` of total liquidity, with a net cash position of `$3.4 billion`.
- Management message: management framed `Q2 2026` as another strong quarter where disciplined capital allocation and operating consistency supported both shareholder returns and continued investment in the business.
- Structural themes: `Q2 2026` shows Newmont as a cash-distribution machine when gold prices and operating execution line up. The key point is not just gold leverage, but how scale allows the company to fund repurchases, dividends, and project development simultaneously.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `Q1 2026` reported net income was `$3.3 billion`, adjusted net income was `$3.2 billion` or `$2.90` per diluted share, and adjusted EBITDA was `$5.2 billion`.
- Cash flow and capital allocation: cash from operating activities was `$3.8 billion`, free cash flow reached an all-time quarterly record of `$3.1 billion`, and shareholder returns since the last earnings call totaled `$2.7 billion`.
- Operations: Newmont produced approximately `1.3 million` attributable gold ounces, plus `9 million` ounces of silver and `30 thousand` tonnes of copper.
- Balance sheet and repurchases: the company ended the quarter with `$8.8 billion` of cash and `$12.8 billion` of total liquidity. It had already executed and settled total repurchases of `$6.0 billion` under prior programs and announced an additional `$6.0 billion` share repurchase authorization.
- Management message: management emphasized strong execution, record free cash flow, and confidence that the enhanced capital-allocation framework could support materially larger buybacks without sacrificing guidance or balance-sheet flexibility.
- Structural themes: `Q1 2026` is the clearest expression of Newmont's current model. The company is trying to turn gold-scale cash generation into per-share value creation while still behaving like a long-duration operator with a deep project pipeline.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: full-year `2025` reported net income was `$7.2 billion`, adjusted net income was `$7.6 billion` or `$6.89` per diluted share, and adjusted EBITDA was `$13.5 billion`; fourth-quarter adjusted net income was `$2.8 billion`, or `$2.52` per diluted share.
- Cash flow and capital allocation: full-year cash from operating activities was `$10.3 billion`, free cash flow was an all-time annual record `$7.3 billion`, including a record `$2.8 billion` in the fourth quarter, and the company declared an increased `Q4 2025` dividend of `$0.26` per share.
- Operations and reserves: the company achieved `2025` production and cost guidance, declared total attributable gold reserves of `118.2 million` ounces and resources of `148.7 million` ounces, and retained significant exposure to copper and silver.
- Balance sheet and portfolio actions: Newmont returned `$3.4 billion` to shareholders in `2025`, reduced debt by `$3.4 billion`, and completed its non-core divestiture program with `$4.5 billion` of total after-tax proceeds to date from announced transactions.
- Management message: management described `2025` as a milestone year in which operational execution, portfolio optimization, and debt reduction created a stronger starting point for `2026`.
- Structural themes: `Q4 2025` closes the loop on the annual story. Newmont is not just benefiting from higher gold prices; it is also simplifying the portfolio, strengthening the balance sheet, and using size to make capital returns look institutional rather than opportunistic.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Newmont adds the large-scale gold-major layer that Pan American only partially covers because Pan American is more silver-centered and more explicitly portfolio-transitional.
- Commodity exposure: this is direct gold exposure, but the company’s economics are not one-dimensional. Silver and copper help support by-product economics, while reserve depth and project sequencing determine how much of the gold-price windfall becomes durable value.
- Capital intensity: Newmont is one of the clearest reminders that a miner can throw off massive cash and still remain capital hungry. Ahafo North, Lihir, Cadia, Tanami Expansion 2, and Red Chris all reinforce the need to keep reinvesting in the asset base.
- Industrial: this is a physically heavy system with mine plans, tailings, underground development, processing circuits, and permitting embedded in the model. The business is much closer to infrastructure execution than to a passive commodity ETF.
- Geographic and regulatory risk: the company’s multi-continent portfolio diversifies operating exposure, but it also multiplies the importance of host-government relations, environmental approvals, indigenous consultation, and long-cycle development discipline.
- Shareholder returns: Newmont is a strong cross-company comparison point because it shows a miner trying to hard-code capital returns into the model while commodity conditions are strong, similar in spirit to some upstream oil names but with a different asset-life and reinvestment profile.
- Portfolio optimization: the `2025` filings show a major company actively reshaping itself through divestitures, debt reduction, and repurchases rather than simply adding volume. That makes Newmont a useful template for how scale miners respond when markets reward free cash flow and capital discipline more than empire building.
- Bigger-picture theme: this packet deepens the archive’s extraction layer by showing how a gold major behaves differently from both hydrocarbons and smaller precious-metals names. The core questions become reserve life, margin resilience, jurisdictional spread, and the ability to translate high prices into balance-sheet strength without starving the next wave of projects.
