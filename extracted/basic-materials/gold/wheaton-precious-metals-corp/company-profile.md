## What changed

- Business model: Wheaton is not a conventional miner. The `2025` annual chain shows a precious-metals streaming-and-royalty allocator that monetizes production from partner mines rather than directly operating the underlying assets.
- Archive fit: this is the cleanest gold royalty-and-streaming comparator in the branch. It sits alongside `Newmont`, `Kinross`, and `Lundin Gold` without duplicating any of their mine-operator economics.
- `2025` base year: production reached `689,900` attributable GEOs, revenue reached `$2.3 billion`, net earnings reached `$1.5 billion`, adjusted net earnings reached `$1.4 billion`, and operating cash flow reached `$1.9 billion`.
- Asset quality: at year-end `2025`, Wheaton had streaming and royalty agreements on `23` operating mines and `25` development and other projects, with `85%` of attributable production coming from assets in the lowest half of their cost curves.
- Portfolio evolution: `2025` included the Hemlo and Spring Valley transactions, while the post-year-end BHP Antamina PMPA represented the largest precious-metals streaming transaction in the company’s history.
- Growth framework: `2026` annual guidance was `860,000` to `940,000` GEOs, and long-term production was forecast to rise roughly `50%` to `1.2 million` GEOs by `2030`.
- `Q4 2025` pattern: record annual results and a year-end cash balance of `$1.2 billion` showed the company entering `2026` with major financing capacity before the BHP Antamina step-up.
- `Q1 2026` pattern: revenue rose to `$901 million`, net earnings to `$582 million`, and cash to `$2.2 billion`, while the company closed the `Antamina` transaction and added early new-asset contribution from `Hemlo`, `Fenix`, and `Mineral Park`.
- `Q2 2026` pattern: revenue rose again to `$929 million`, first-half revenue reached `$1.8 billion`, and attributable GEO production reached `202,200` ounces, but the balance sheet shifted sharply after funding the BHP Antamina deal, with cash falling to `$100 million` and debt rising to `$2.0 billion`.
- Operating structure: this packet is useful because it shows a precious-metals exposure model where the key moving parts are contract design, counterparties, balance-sheet flexibility, and portfolio dealmaking rather than direct mine ownership and site-level capex execution.

## Open questions

- How much of Wheaton’s premium multiple should be attributed to the structural advantages of streaming itself versus the unusually large one-time growth step from the BHP Antamina transaction?
- Does the heavier post-Antamina debt load remain a temporary bridge to a larger cash-flow base, or does it introduce more financing sensitivity than investors usually expect from a capital-light streaming model?
