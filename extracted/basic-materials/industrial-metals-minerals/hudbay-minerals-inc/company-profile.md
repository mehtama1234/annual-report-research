## What changed

- Hudbay adds a different kind of copper exposure than the large diversified miners or the more concentrated restart-risk names. This is an Americas-focused copper-and-gold system where gold by-product credits and operational diversity across Peru, Manitoba, and British Columbia materially change what headline copper economics mean.
- The annual materials frame `2025` as a record year: about `$2.2 billion` of revenue, about `$1.1 billion` of annual adjusted EBITDA, a `$600 million` Copper World joint venture transaction with Mitsubishi, an 11th consecutive year of achieving annual consolidated copper production guidance, and a fifth consecutive year of achieving annual consolidated gold guidance.
- The `Q4 2025` year-end read shows why Hudbay belongs in the lane. Revenue reached `$732.9 million`, adjusted EBITDA reached `$385.9 million`, and full-year adjusted EBITDA reached `$1.0609 billion`. Full-year consolidated cash cost was `($0.22)` per pound and full-year sustaining cash cost was `$1.30` per pound, both helped materially by higher gold by-product credits.
- `Q1 2026` is the balance-sheet transformation quarter. Revenue hit a record `$757.3 million`, adjusted EBITDA reached a record `$421.9 million`, cash and cash equivalents exceeded `$1.0 billion`, and net debt fell to only `$5.6 million` after the Mitsubishi cash contribution on the Copper World JV closing.
- `Q2 2026` shows the opposite side of the same system. Revenue fell to `$631.3 million` and adjusted EBITDA to `$321.2 million`, not because the asset base broke, but because Peru faced temporary port closures from ocean swells that delayed about `10,000` dry metric tonnes of copper concentrate shipments into July. That is a useful logistics-throughput signal for the whole branch.
- Cost structure is the other major distinction. Hudbay produced consolidated cash costs of `($1.80)` per pound in `Q1 2026` and `($0.40)` per pound in `Q2 2026`, with gold revenues equal to `39%` of gross revenue in `Q1 2026` and `38%` of total revenue in `Q2 2026`. This is not a simple copper-price packet. By-product credits and metal mix can flip the apparent cost profile entirely.
- Growth optionality is now much more central than it was a year earlier. The company tied its next phase to Copper World, Arizona Sonoran's Cactus project, New Ingerbelle at Copper Mountain, and Snow Lake exploration, which makes Hudbay a cleaner packet for understanding brownfield-plus-greenfield capital allocation inside a mid-tier miner.

## Open questions

- Can Hudbay keep converting its gold by-product support and operating diversity into structurally low copper cash costs if Peru grades stay lower after Pampacancha depletion?
- Does the Copper World and Cactus growth lane deepen Hudbay's edge as an Americas copper consolidator, or does it reintroduce capital-intensity and permitting risk just as the balance sheet has been repaired?
