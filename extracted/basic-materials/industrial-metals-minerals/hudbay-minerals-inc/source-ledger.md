- The user requirement is `2025` annual reports plus the latest three reported quarters as of `Monday, August 10, 2026`.
- For Hudbay, the right mapping is:
  - latest period: `Q2 2026`
  - period minus 1: `Q1 2026`
  - period minus 2: `Q4 2025`
- The annual report itself came from Hudbay's investor materials, while the annual and quarter filing backbone came from the SEC `40-F` and `6-K` exhibits.
