## What changed

- Diversified miner, not a single-lane metals bet: Rio Tinto is the missing global bulk-materials platform in this archive. It combines iron ore scale, integrated aluminium, copper growth, and early lithium buildout inside one capital-allocation system.
- AnnualReports lag is explicit: when collected on `2026-08-10`, AnnualReports still showed the `2024 Annual Report`, so the real `2025` chain had to come from Rio Tinto IR and SEC.
- Annual base year: `2025` consolidated sales revenue was `$57.638 billion`, underlying EBITDA was `$25.363 billion`, underlying earnings were `$10.868 billion`, and profit after tax attributable to owners was `$9.966 billion`.
- Cash flow and payout: `2025` net cash generated from operating activities was `$16.8 billion`, and the ordinary dividend was `$6.5 billion`, showing that Rio still behaves like a large cash-yielding hard-asset platform even while funding growth.
- `H1 2026` acceleration: by `2026-06-30`, sales revenue had risen to `$31.028 billion`, underlying EBITDA to `$14.826 billion`, and operating cash flow to `$9.173 billion`, with Copper, Aluminium and Lithium contributing more than `50%` of underlying EBITDA.
- Operating shape: `Q1 2026` and `Q2 2026` make clear that the group is still anchored by Pilbara iron ore and logistics execution, but the incremental growth narrative is now more heavily tied to Oyu Tolgoi copper, Simandou execution, and lithium ramp.
- System role: Rio is one of the best packets for showing that industrial metals should not be reduced to a single commodity. The real story is transport-heavy iron ore, power-intensive aluminium, capital-heavy copper growth, and long-cycle strategic minerals all competing for attention and capital inside one portfolio.

## Open questions

- How durable is Rio’s shift toward copper, aluminium, and lithium as a share of underlying EBITDA if iron ore remains the main cash engine?
- Does Simandou deepen Rio’s iron ore dominance, or does it mainly fund the next decade of copper and lithium diversification?
