- The saved AnnualReports route did not return usable company-specific Teck content in this pass, so the packet relies on company-hosted annual and quarterly PDFs plus SEC filings for authoritative evidence.
- Quarter-level quantitative and operating detail comes primarily from the company-hosted financial report PDFs, with the SEC `6-K` filings preserved as the formal filing wrappers.
