## Annual report takeaways

- International Paper is the cleanest current extension for the behind-the-shelf consumer infrastructure lane because it widens the paper-and-packaging substrate map beyond `Clearwater Paper`'s SBS and folding-carton exposure into corrugated, containerboard, and shipping-throughput infrastructure.
- AnnualReports still lagged here as of Monday, August 10, 2026: the saved company page shows `Basic Materials / Paper & Paper Products` and still surfaced `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K` as the most recent package.
- The authoritative annual evidence therefore comes from the official investor-relations page and SEC chain. International Paper's IR page exposed a `2025 Annual Report`, and the SEC `10-K` shows a company now framed around `Packaging Solutions North America` and `Packaging Solutions EMEA` after the Global Cellulose Fibers sale.
- Full-year `2025` net sales were `$23.63 billion`, loss from continuing operations was `$2.84 billion`, adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations was `$2.98 billion`, cash provided by operating activities was `$1.70 billion`, and free cash flow was `$(0.16) billion`.
- The weak GAAP headline was dominated by reset charges rather than simple demand collapse. Management disclosed a `$2.47 billion` pre-tax non-cash goodwill impairment in the EMEA business, roughly `$0.96 billion` of accelerated depreciation tied to asset-rationalization decisions, and roughly `$0.63 billion` of restructuring charges.
- The useful read is not "paper" in a narrow commodity sense. International Paper is a corrugated-packaging and box-throughput system tied to box demand, packaging scale, mill reliability, shipping volume, and network performance across North America and EMEA.
- The annual strategic read is also now about separation and simplification. In the `Q4 2025` release management said the company planned to create two independent publicly traded packaging solutions companies in North America and EMEA, which makes the business easier to read as packaging-throughput infrastructure rather than a legacy mixed paper portfolio.

- Segment picture: `Packaging Solutions North America` generated `$3.688 billion` of net sales and `$204 million` of operating profit, while `Packaging Solutions EMEA` generated `$2.287 billion` of net sales and an operating loss of `$(80) million`.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: International Paper broadens `Basic Materials / Paper & Paper Products` beyond SBS paperboard into corrugated, containerboard, and large-scale shipping and box-demand infrastructure.
- Consumer: direct consumer demand appears here indirectly through e-commerce shipments, food and household-product transport, retail replenishment, and the physical box demand that sits behind shelf availability and delivery throughput.
- Cultural: the relevant cultural layer is not brand identity but the normalization of packaging-intensive commerce, home delivery, and high-volume goods movement that depends on corrugated systems consumers almost never see.
- Industrial: the real operating questions are mill reliability, box-volume growth, price-cost balance, maintenance and conversion spending, integration and separation execution, and the tension between North American throughput and softer EMEA demand.
- Sustainability: packaging efficiency, asset rationalization, recycled-fiber cost pressure, and plant closures matter because corrugated scale now sits inside broader supply-chain and waste-efficiency questions rather than only fiber pricing.
- Capital allocation: International Paper is using capital around portfolio simplification, debt reduction, asset-network reshaping, mill conversion, and planned separation rather than only ordinary maintenance capex.

## Why this company matters for the blind-spot lane

- `Graphic Packaging` already anchors consumer-facing packaging conversion.
- `Sonoco` already anchors broader packaging-format and container-control infrastructure.
- `Clearwater Paper` already anchors SBS paperboard substrate and converter-facing packaging materials.
- `International Paper` is the best current extension because it adds broader corrugated and containerboard substrate scale tied to shipping throughput and box demand.
- That makes the useful comparison `conversion` versus `broader packaging control` versus `SBS substrate` versus `corrugated and containerboard throughput infrastructure`, not simply one more paper company.
