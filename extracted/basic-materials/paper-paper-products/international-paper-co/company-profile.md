## What changed

- Annual source chain: International Paper is now a stronger annual-report case than the AnnualReports page suggests because the aggregator still lagged at `2024` while the official IR page already exposed the `2025 Annual Report` PDF.
- Company shape: the business now reads as a packaging solutions platform rather than a broad legacy paper company, especially after the Global Cellulose Fibers sale and the segment reframing around North America and EMEA packaging.
- Behind-the-shelf role: International Paper adds the corrugated and containerboard substrate layer that sits behind box demand, retail replenishment, and shipping throughput, which is materially different from `Clearwater`'s SBS and foodservice substrate position.
- Annual reset: `2025` was a transition year defined by large non-cash impairment and restructuring charges, but the operating read is still useful because it separates headline losses from the underlying packaging-throughput and mill-network story.
- Quarter pattern: `Q4 2025` established the reset and planned separation framing, `Q1 2026` showed debt paydown and commercial progress with weather pressure, and `Q2 2026` showed stronger North American execution with continuing EMEA softness.
- Capital and portfolio shape: the `2025` and `2026` materials repeatedly emphasize portfolio simplification, site actions, mill conversion, acquisition and divestiture work, and planned separation rather than ordinary maintenance-only capital deployment.

## Open questions

- Does International Paper become the archive's strongest proof that the substrate layer should be split between SBS packaging materials and corrugated or containerboard shipping-throughput infrastructure?
- How durable is the North American box-volume and mill-performance recovery if EMEA demand and packaging margins remain soft during the separation process?
