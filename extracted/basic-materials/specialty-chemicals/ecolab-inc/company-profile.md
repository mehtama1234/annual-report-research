## What changed

- Archive quality: Ecolab is a clean taxonomy-blind case because AnnualReports can classify it as `Specialty Chemicals`, but the packet becomes useful only after the annual report and trailing quarters are read as embedded service infrastructure.
- Company shape: the `2025` materials show a four-part operating system in `Global Water`, `Global Institutional & Specialty`, `Global Pest Elimination`, and `Global Life Sciences`, not a narrow chemicals vendor.
- Quarter pattern: `Q4 2025` established the record-year base, `Q1 2026` showed acceleration led by Life Sciences and Global High-Tech, and `Q2 2026` extended that into stronger food, institutional, and water growth with another guidance raise.
- Blind-spot role: Ecolab is the clearest current candidate to widen the behind-the-shelf lane from plant hardware and measurement control into service-heavy hygiene, contamination-control, water, pest, and compliance embedment.
