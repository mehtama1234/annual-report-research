## What changed

- Construction-linked steel: CMC is not just another broad steel producer. The filings position it as a construction-facing system spanning recycling, electric-arc-furnace steelmaking, rebar fabrication, and a newly enlarged precast and construction-solutions platform.
- Annual base year: fiscal `2025` revenue was `$7.798 billion`, net earnings were `$84.7 million`, total assets were `$7.172 billion`, cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were `$1.043 billion`, and total debt plus finance-lease obligations were `$1.354 billion`.
- Portfolio shape: the `2025` annual filing described three reportable segments: `North America Steel Group`, `Emerging Businesses Group`, and `Europe Steel Group`. By `Q1 2026`, management had renamed the emerging-businesses segment to `Construction Solutions Group`.
- Process economics: the `10-K` says CMC manufactures products from approximately `98% recycled material`, which makes it a distinct scrap-based and recycling-linked steel platform rather than a blast-furnace or ore-integrated model.
- Fiscal 2026 inflection: the `Q1` through `Q3 2026` reporting run shows earnings rebuilding sharply as steel product margins improved, downstream backlog held up, and the acquired precast platform began contributing meaningfully.
- Balance-sheet expansion: total assets jumped from `$7.172 billion` at `2025-08-31` to `$9.796 billion` by `2026-05-31` after the precast acquisitions, while total debt plus finance-lease obligations rose from `$1.354 billion` to `$3.400 billion`.
- Liquidity and leverage: quarter-end cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments moved from `$1.043 billion` at fiscal `2025` year-end to `$1.023 billion` in `Q1`, `$495.0 million` in `Q2`, and `$559.8 million` in `Q3`, while management emphasized improving leverage and visibility to below `2x` net leverage ahead of the mid-`2027` goal.
- Demand mix: the `Q3 2026` release tied backlog support to public infrastructure, data centers, semiconductors, and energy buildouts, which makes CMC more exposed to civil and project timing than to auto-sheet or general spot steel narratives.

## Open questions

- How durable is the current margin rebound if scrap and steel spreads normalize after the 2026 construction season?
- Does the precast and construction-solutions buildout become a structurally better business mix, or does it mainly add another capital-intensive project cycle to an already cyclical steel platform?
