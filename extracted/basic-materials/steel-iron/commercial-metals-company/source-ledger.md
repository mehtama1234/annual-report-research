## Reconciliation notes

- The correct trailing-quarter set as of `Monday, August 10, 2026` is `Q3 2026`, `Q2 2026`, and `Q1 2026` because Commercial Metals has an `August` fiscal year-end.
- AnnualReports was useful only for taxonomy and lag confirmation here. It still showed the `2024` annual package rather than a `2025` annual package as of `Monday, August 10, 2026`.
- The official IR annual and SEC-filings pages were saved, but their visible HTML is mostly a B2i shell. They remain useful as confirmation of the official company-hosted routes, while the SEC filings and saved quarter-result pages carry the substantive evidence.
- Balance-sheet liquidity in this packet uses the filed `cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments` line because that is the clean visible balance-sheet value surfaced in the saved SEC HTML. The filings also expose a slightly higher `cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and restricted cash equivalents` total, which was treated as a separate line and not substituted for operating liquidity.
- The `Q1`, `Q2`, and `Q3 2026` `8-K` wrappers were directly verified as results filings under Item `2.02` with attached Exhibit `99.1` press releases.
