## Annual report takeaways

- Nucor gives the Basic Materials archive the clearest domestic steel-system read so far. The annual materials show a business that is much broader than a commodity sheet mill. It spans steel mills, fabricated and downstream steel products, and raw materials, with a strong North American operating base.
- AnnualReports now has a saved local company-page artifact for this branch, and it confirms the `Basic Materials` / `Steel & Iron` taxonomy while still lagging at the `2024 Annual Report and Form 10-K` as of `2026-08-10`. The authoritative `2025` chain remains the official annual report and SEC filing set.
- The company’s operating setup matters. Nucor reports three segments, with steel mills the largest at `62%` of external sales in `2025`. It also recycled about `20 million` gross tons of scrap steel in `2025`, which reinforces the electric-arc-furnace and scrap-based production model at the center of its cost structure.
- The annual evidence suggests that Nucor is trying to become less cyclical at the margin, not by escaping steel, but by moving further into value-added products, fabricated systems, contract business, and end markets with stronger structural demand such as data centers, power, construction, and infrastructure.
- Capital intensity is a central theme. Cash used for capital expenditures increased to `$3.42 billion` in `2025`, and management still expects roughly `$2.50 billion` in `2026` capital expenditures. This is not a harvest story. It is a scale-and-invest story.
- The product and market mix is important. The steel mills segment sold roughly `19.848 million` tons to outside customers in `2025`, and sheet operations were heavily contract-oriented, with around `85%` of sheet steel sales going to contract customers. That matters because it can damp volatility relative to a pure spot-market steel exposure.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: `Q2 2026` net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders were `$1.16 billion`, adjusted net earnings were `$1.11 billion`, net sales were `$10.40 billion`, and EBITDA was `$2.02 billion`.
- Filing status: the quarter is documented through the official results exhibit and wrapper `8-K`, but no separately filed `Q2 2026` `10-Q` was present in the local SEC chain as of `Monday, August 10, 2026`.
- Segment read: earnings before income taxes and noncontrolling interests were `$1.556 billion` in steel mills, `$353 million` in steel products, and `$146 million` in raw materials.
- Cash and capital return: Nucor ended the quarter with `$2.69 billion` of cash and short-term investments, returned about `$479 million` to stockholders in the quarter, and kept its revolver undrawn.
- Management message: management emphasized a second consecutive quarterly record for steel mill shipments, supportive federal trade policy, and broad investment across key U.S. sectors.
- Structural themes: `Q2 2026` is the strongest quarter in the current packet and shows what Nucor looks like when shipments, pricing, and domestic policy support all line up. This is a domestic steel and fabricated-products platform with meaningful operating leverage.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `Q1 2026` net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders were `$743 million`, net sales were `$9.50 billion`, and EBITDA was `$1.51 billion`.
- Segment read: earnings before income taxes and noncontrolling interests were `$1.128 billion` in steel mills, `$285 million` in steel products, and `$45 million` in raw materials.
- Cash and capital return: Nucor ended the quarter with `$2.48 billion` of cash and short-term investments and returned roughly `$250 million` to stockholders year-to-date.
- Management message: management emphasized a strong start to `2026`, a new quarterly shipment record in steel mills, and demand strength across key end markets.
- Structural themes: `Q1 2026` is the transition quarter where the business moved clearly out of the weaker `Q4 2025` setup. The quarter shows the importance of realized selling prices, volumes, and the earnings power of the steel mills segment when conditions improve.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: `Q4 2025` net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders were `$378 million`, adjusted net earnings were `$400 million`, net sales were `$7.69 billion`, and EBITDA was `$918 million`.
- Segment read: earnings before income taxes and noncontrolling interests were `$516 million` in steel mills, `$230 million` in steel products, and `$24 million` in raw materials.
- Cash and capital return: Nucor ended the year with `$2.70 billion` of cash and short-term investments and returned about `$1.2 billion` to stockholders during full-year `2025`.
- Management message: management framed `2025` as a year of safe execution and project delivery, with several major projects coming online and expected to strengthen future earnings power.
- Structural themes: `Q4 2025` is the weaker comparison quarter and shows classic steel-cycle pressure: lower volumes, margin compression in sheet, and weaker raw-material segment earnings. It is useful because it makes the `2026` rebound easier to interpret.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Nucor is strong evidence that Basic Materials includes domestically oriented manufacturing platforms, not just globally priced raw commodities.
- Consumer: direct consumer demand is secondary. The better lens is nonresidential construction, durable goods, capital spending, infrastructure, power, and industrial buildout.
- Cultural: the broader signal is that U.S. industrial policy and physical production still matter. Nucor’s story is about steel, fabrication, recycling, and domestic capacity, not just abstract macro factors.
- Regulatory: trade policy is a first-order variable here. Management repeatedly links results to federal trade policy and reduced unfairly traded imports. Environmental and safety capital requirements also remain material.
- Labor: this is a labor- and execution-heavy industrial system. Mill utilization, fabricated-products throughput, project ramp-up, and safety performance all matter even when labor is not the main investor headline.
- Technology: Nucor is not a software company, but it benefits indirectly from data-center buildout, grid and power investment, and industrial automation. The company is also exposed to technology through process efficiency and value-added manufacturing.
- Capital allocation: Nucor is a clear case where shareholder returns coexist with very large growth and maintenance capex. Capital return is important, but it is not displacing industrial investment.
