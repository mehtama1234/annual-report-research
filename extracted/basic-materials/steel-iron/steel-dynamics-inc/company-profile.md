## What changed

- Domestic metals system: Steel Dynamics is not just another steel name. The filings frame it as a broader industrial-metals platform combining electric-arc-furnace steelmaking, metals recycling, downstream steel fabrication, and a new aluminum flat-rolled buildout.
- Annual base year: fiscal `2025` net sales were `$18.2 billion`, net income was `$1.186 billion`, total assets were `$16.420 billion`, cash plus restricted cash was `$775.3 million`, and total debt was about `$4.211 billion`.
- Segment shape: at `2025-12-31`, segment assets were `$9.234 billion` in steel operations, `$1.463 billion` in metals recycling, `$667.0 million` in steel fabrication, and `$3.906 billion` in aluminum operations. That makes the aluminum buildout too large to treat as a side project.
- Operating model: management repeatedly describes a circular manufacturing model with recycled scrap as the primary input. That places Steel Dynamics closer to a recycling-and-conversion system than to ore-linked integrated steel.
- Fiscal 2026 acceleration: the `Q4 2025` through `Q2 2026` run shows earnings climbing as record shipments and widening metal spreads lifted the steel segment, while recycling and downstream fabrication remained meaningful support layers.
- Balance-sheet and cash-flow read: first-half `2026` operating cash flow reached `$576.3 million`, while total assets rose to `$17.113 billion` by `2026-06-30` and total debt stayed roughly flat at about `$4.182 billion`. This is a capital-heavy platform still funding large growth projects rather than a light-asset steel trader.
- End-market mix: management tied demand to energy, non-residential construction, automotive, industrial, manufacturing, warehouses, healthcare, and especially data centers. That makes Steel Dynamics a good bridge between commodity steel economics and the domestic physical buildout behind power, AI infrastructure, and reindustrialization.

## Open questions

- How durable are current steel spreads if pricing cools after the 2026 surge in domestic demand and trade protection?
- Can the aluminum expansion become a real earnings diversifier, or will it remain a capital-intensive startup story with long payback timing?
