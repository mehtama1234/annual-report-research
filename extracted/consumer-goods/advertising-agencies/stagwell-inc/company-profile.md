## What changed

- Taxonomy and lane fit: Stagwell is a second exact-category `Advertising Agencies` packet, but it is not just another big agency. It is explicitly positioned as a digital-first challenger network built around AI, software, data, and self-service marketer tools.
- Competitive read: the company openly frames its opportunity against larger legacy peers that are distracted by restructuring and mergers. That makes it a useful direct contrast to `Omnicom`, whose current packet is dominated by the IPG integration story.
- Revenue mix: Stagwell repeatedly highlights digital transformation, communications, media and commerce, and The Marketing Cloud rather than only classic creative or media-buying scale.
- Political and advocacy exposure: the quarter chain repeatedly strips out advocacy to show underlying growth, while management still points to communications growth as the political cycle ramps. That makes the packet useful for ad cyclicality and event-driven demand.
- Quarter progression: Q4 `2025` established the annual inflection with improving free cash flow and AI framing. Q1 `2026` added stronger new-business momentum and agentic-application positioning. Q2 `2026` then showed the clearest operating leverage with stronger organic growth, higher adjusted EBITDA, and raised adjusted EPS guidance.
- Technology posture: the annual-report release and quarter materials present AI as workflow infrastructure, not just creative augmentation, with The Machine, Palantir linkage, ID graph tooling, and The Marketing Cloud all positioned as part of a software-plus-services operating layer.

## Open questions

- Can Stagwell keep outgrowing larger agency peers once the easy comparison against merger-disrupted incumbents fades?
- Does the AI and software framing create a real moat, or mainly a faster sales narrative inside a still labor-intensive agency business?
- How durable is the company's ex-advocacy growth if political and communications demand normalize?
- Can a challenger agency sustain margin expansion while still funding acquisitions, AI tooling, and repurchases?
