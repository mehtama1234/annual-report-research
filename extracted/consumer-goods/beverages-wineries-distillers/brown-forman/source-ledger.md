## Most important evidence for the packet thesis

- The `2025` annual report directly ties business pressure to weaker spirits consumption and macro headwinds rather than treating the category as a simple resilient staple lane.
- The Q2 through Q4 FY`2026` chain shows a consumer system that can still protect brand relevance and cash generation while producing much choppier earnings than household staples or routine beverages.
- Brown-Forman broadens the consumer map by showing that some `Consumer Goods` demand is really driven by ritual, identity, premium signaling, and social occasion rather than by simple replenishment.
