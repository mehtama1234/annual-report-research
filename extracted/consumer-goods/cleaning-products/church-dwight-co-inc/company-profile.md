## What changed

- Revenue: FY`2025` net sales were `$6.2032 billion`; then Q1 `2026` and Q2 `2026` delivered reported growth of `0.2%` and `1.6%` with stronger organic growth of `5.0%` and `5.8%`.
- Margin: adjusted gross margin improved to `46.4%` in Q1 `2026` and remained solid at `45.4%` in Q2 `2026`.
- Guidance: the company moved from a January `2026` view of reported sales down about `1.5%` to `0.5%` to a July `2026` outlook of flat to `+1%` reported sales and `4%` to `5%` organic growth.
- Capital allocation: cash from operations was `$1.215 billion` in FY`2025` and `$461.6 million` in the first half of `2026`.
- Portfolio mix: `2025` included exits from VMS, FLAWLESS, SPINBRUSH, and the WATERPIK showerhead business, while THERABREATH, HERO, TOUCHLAND, and MISS MOUTH'S sharpened the higher-growth side of the portfolio.
- Consumer / cultural signals: this is a trusted routine-use household and personal-care platform with a stronger online and acquisition-led innovation engine than several older staples peers.
- Management tone: constructive and increasingly confident, with repeated emphasis on broad-based growth, eCommerce strength, and a balanced value-and-premium mix.
