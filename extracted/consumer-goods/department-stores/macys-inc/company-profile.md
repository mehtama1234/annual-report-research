## What changed

- Macy's adds a direct department-store and portfolio-repair system to `Consumer Goods`, not just another plain retail-format datapoint.
- The annual and quarter materials show the business widening beyond the Macy's core banner through Bloomingdale's, Bluemercury, Macy's Media Network, Macy's Marketplace, and a large credit-card revenue stream.
- Full-year `2025` net sales reached `$21.8B`, comparable sales returned to positive growth at `1.5%`, and other revenue reached `$857M`, with credit card net revenues of `$669M` and Macy's Media Network revenue net of `$188M`.
- The quarter trail shows recent strengthening rather than further deterioration: Q3 `2025` produced the strongest quarter in `13` quarters, Q4 `2025` returned the full year to positive comparable-sales growth, and Q1 `2026` then exceeded guidance and raised the full-year outlook.
- That makes Macy's a useful bridge case between `Kohl's` weak-middle repair, `Ulta` beauty discovery, and broader ecosystem-retail models like `Target`, `Walmart`, and `Amazon`, because the company is trying to build more relationship depth without having the same underlying structural strength.

## Open questions

- How much of the recent improvement is durable Macy's nameplate repair versus a portfolio mix shift toward Bloomingdale's and Bluemercury?
- Can Macy's Marketplace, Macy's Media Network, and credit-card economics keep widening the relationship if the core department-store format weakens again?
- Does the archive need another department-store or mall-sensitive operator after `Kohl's` and Macy's, or is the current pair already enough to define the weak-middle retail lane?
