# Nordstrom Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: Nordstrom
- Sector: Consumer Goods
- Industry: Department Stores
- Ticker: JWN
- Fiscal year-end: January
- Public-reporting status: acquired and taken private on `2025-05-20`
- Last public annual package: fiscal `2024` year-end, filed `2025-03-21`
- Last public quarter-results package: Q4 / fiscal `2024`, released `2025-03-04`

## Why this packet is different

- Nordstrom is not a live `2026` trailing-three-quarter public comp.
- The company was acquired by the Nordstrom family and Liverpool in May `2025`, so the public reporting chain stopped before any normal `Q1 2026`, `Q4 2025`, or `Q3 2025` comparison set could exist.
- That makes Nordstrom useful mainly as a structural contrast case: a department-store operator where the public-company comparison window ended while Macy's remained public and continued reporting.

## Final public annual takeaways

- AnnualReports.com still shows Nordstrom's most recent public annual package as `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K`, which is directionally right because there is no later public annual-report chain after the take-private close.
- The final public annual materials show a department-store and off-price portfolio still trying to stabilize through banner mix, Rack growth, profitability recovery, and transaction-driven end-of-public-market timing rather than through a clean multi-quarter public momentum story.
- The most important archive point is that Nordstrom's path diverged from Macy's and the mall-apparel names. This is not a live public winner-versus-loser case in `2026`; it is a terminal public-company case.

## Final public quarter takeaways

### Final public reported quarter

- Results: Q4 / fiscal `2024` results were released on `2025-03-04`.
- Reported headline: for the fiscal year ended `2025-02-01`, net earnings were `$294M`, EPS was `$1.74`, and EBIT was `$495M`, or `3.4%` of sales.
- Banner mix: fourth-quarter net sales declined at the Nordstrom banner while Nordstrom Rack still grew, which reinforces the idea that off-price was carrying more of the energy than the full-line department-store banner.
- Structural theme: the business was still operationally relevant, but the more important story by spring `2025` was the take-private transaction rather than the next public quarter cadence.

### Reporting stop

- On `2025-05-20`, Nordstrom announced completion of its acquisition by the Nordstrom family and Liverpool.
- Nordstrom common stock ceased trading before the NYSE open on `2025-05-21`.
- After that point, the company stopped being a valid public trailing-quarter collection target for the `2026` archive window.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Department stores: Nordstrom helps show that not every department-store path remains comparable deep into `2026`; ownership structure itself can become the main dividing line.
- Consumer: the company still matters as a higher-income discretionary and fashion retail signal, but the archive should avoid treating it as a live quarter-on-quarter comp once the public chain stops.
- Structural: this is a good reminder that some names drop out of the public evidence set not because the sector stopped mattering, but because the ownership form changed.
- Comparison value: Nordstrom is most useful next to Macy's. Macy's provides the continued public `2026` department-store read; Nordstrom provides the final public endpoint.
