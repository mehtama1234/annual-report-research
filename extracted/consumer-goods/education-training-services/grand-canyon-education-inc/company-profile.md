## What changed

- Exact-category value: Grand Canyon Education gives the frontier a second clean `Education & Training Services` packet rather than leaving the lane dependent on one education-services read.
- Operating model clarity: the company is not just selling tuition exposure. It is monetizing enrollment growth, online program scale, off-campus classroom and laboratory sites, and service revenue tied to partner institutions.
- Workforce signal: ABSN and healthcare-adjacent growth make the packet especially useful for reading labor shortages, clinical training bottlenecks, and professional-upskilling demand.
- Mix pressure: revenue per student remains under pressure from contract modifications and mix shift, even while total enrollments and service revenue continue to rise.
- Capital allocation: the company remains cash generative, but share repurchases and capital expenditures have recently pulled liquidity down from year-end levels.

## Open questions

- How durable is the current enrollment and partner-site growth if healthcare labor demand cools or if university partners tighten spending?
- Does contract renegotiation keep pressuring revenue per student enough to offset some of the scale benefits from partner enrollment growth?
- How much of the recent strength is category-wide workforce demand versus Grand Canyon Education-specific execution in online and off-campus formats?
