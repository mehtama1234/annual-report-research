## Most important evidence for the packet thesis

- The Q4 `2025` reset package shows the central break in the story: management paused separation work and announced `~$600 million` of reinvestment because the business had been underinvested and needed repair.
- Q1 `2026` shows early traction in Taste Elevation and international markets, but still with lower Adjusted EPS and lower Adjusted Operating Income.
- Q2 `2026` shows that investments are beginning to improve share and outlook, but also that the company is still intentionally spending harder to rebuild the U.S. business and defend affordability.
