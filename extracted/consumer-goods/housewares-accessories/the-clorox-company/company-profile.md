## What changed

- Revenue: FY`2025` annual materials described a `$7.1 billion` net-sales company, then the trailing quarter chain moved from Q2 FY`2026` sales down `1%`, to Q3 FY`2026` sales flat, to Q4 FY`2026` sales down `2%` with a much larger organic decline because of the ERP shipment comparison.
- Margin: the annual read showed more than `200` basis points of gross-margin expansion in FY`2025`, but the quarter chain then weakened, with gross margin at `43.2%` in Q2 and Q3 FY`2026` and down to `41.3%` in Q4 FY`2026`.
- Guidance: management reaffirmed the fiscal-year view in February `2026`, updated the outlook in April `2026`, and moved into a more explicit FY`2027` transition-and-recovery framing in August `2026`.
- Cash generation: year-to-date operating cash flow was stable through Q2 FY`2026`, then dropped materially by Q3 because of the Glad joint-venture termination payment.
- Portfolio mix: the annual packet highlighted the VMS divestiture and Glad JV wind-down, while the quarter trail shifted attention to GOJO and the new Clorox Purell platform.
- Consumer / cultural signals: this is a trusted routine-use and sanitation-heavy household platform where value, efficacy, and habit still matter, but category momentum differs materially across cleaning, lifestyle, and international operations.
- Management tone: constructive but not complacent. The tone throughout `2026` is that Clorox is in a transition year, making real progress but still operating in a challenging consumer and cost environment.

## Open questions

- Does GOJO / Purell become a meaningful long-term growth engine, or is it mainly a strategic adjacency that deepens Clorox's hygiene narrative without changing the earnings profile much?
- Once the ERP comparison noise clears, does Clorox return to cleaner organic growth, or does the value-seeking consumer backdrop keep volume and share pressure elevated?
- Is Clorox best understood as a classic staples operator, or as a narrower cleaning-and-health-hygiene platform whose quality depends more heavily on execution and systems modernization?
