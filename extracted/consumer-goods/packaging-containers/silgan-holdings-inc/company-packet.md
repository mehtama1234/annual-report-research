## Annual report takeaways

- Silgan is a clean `AnnualReports` blind-spot extension because the source taxonomy places it in `Consumer Goods / Packaging & Containers`, while the useful system read is narrower and more operational: rigid-container, closure, dispensing, and metal-can control for essential consumer-goods throughput.
- `AnnualReports` taxonomy was verified on `2026-08-10` through the saved `Companies?a=S` listing page, which shows `Silgan Holdings` under `Packaging & Containers / Consumer Goods`. The direct company page was also collectible and exposed the current `2025 Annual Report` and `2025 Form 10-K`.
- The official Silgan IR site exposed a working annual-report PDF path, but the attempted quarter-release PDF links resolved to HTML shell pages. The clean quarter evidence therefore comes from the SEC filing chain, including the saved `Q4 2025` Exhibit `99.1` press release and the `Q1 2026` and `Q2 2026` `10-Q` filings.
- Full-year `2025` net sales were `$6.5 billion`, net income was `$288.4 million`, diluted EPS was `$2.70`, and adjusted net income per diluted share was `$3.72`.
- The company described itself as a leading supplier of sustainable rigid packaging solutions for essential consumer goods products. Management emphasized the strength of a diverse portfolio of consumer-staple products, the completed integration of the `Weener` acquisition, and continued outperformance in high-value dispensing and pet-food products.
- The annual report said Silgan employed over `17,000` people and operated `121` manufacturing plants in North America, Europe, Asia, and South America.
- Segment shape is what makes the packet useful. In `2025`, `Dispensing and Specialty Closures` generated about `$2.7 billion` of sales, `41.8%` of consolidated net sales, and `$321.5 million` of EBIT. `Metal Containers` generated about `$3.1 billion` of sales, `48.4%` of consolidated net sales, and `$243.4 million` of EBIT. `Custom Containers` generated about `$637.6 million` of sales, `9.8%` of consolidated net sales, and `$81.1 million` of EBIT.
- The rigid-packaging role is layered rather than generic. Silgan spans dispensing systems, specialty closures, metal food containers, and custom plastic containers, which makes it a better peer for the packaging-control branch than another broad packaging or paper name.
- Relative to the existing lane, Silgan is most useful as the packaging-side extension after `Sonoco` and `AptarGroup`: `Sonoco` covers wider multi-format packaging control, `AptarGroup` covers dispensing and dosing interface control, and `Silgan` adds rigid-container, metal-can, and closure control concentrated in essential-goods packaging.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: `Q2 2026` net sales were `$1.643 billion`, income before interest and income taxes was `$151.0 million`, net income was `$75.8 million`, and diluted EPS was `$0.72`.
- Segment picture: `Dispensing and Specialty Closures` net sales were `$713.9 million`, `Metal Containers` net sales were `$763.9 million`, and `Custom Containers` net sales were `$165.5 million`.
- Structural themes: by `Q2 2026`, the packaging stack still leaned heavily on consumer-staple throughput rather than discretionary category volatility. The key operating questions were mix inside dispensing, pet-food and food-can demand, custom-container end-market mix, raw-material pass-through, and the balance between closures growth and broader rigid-container stability.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `Q1 2026` net sales were `$1.561 billion`, income before interest and income taxes was `$126.6 million`, net income was `$63.0 million`, and diluted EPS was `$0.60`.
- Segment picture: `Dispensing and Specialty Closures` net sales were `$685.3 million`, `Metal Containers` net sales were `$724.9 million`, and `Custom Containers` net sales were `$151.1 million`.
- Structural themes: Q1 showed the same three-part structure already visible in the annual report. The business did not read like one generic packaging vendor; it read like a company balancing high-value dispensing growth, resilient metal-container demand, and a smaller but meaningful custom-container mix.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: `Q4 2025` net sales were `$1.47 billion`, net income was `$18.2 million`, diluted EPS was `$0.17`, and adjusted net income per diluted share was `$0.67`.
- Full-year bridge: the `Q4 2025` earnings release also confirmed full-year `2025` net sales of `$6.5 billion`, adjusted net income per diluted share of `$3.72`, record full-year `Dispensing and Specialty Closures` and `Custom Containers` adjusted EBIT, and approximately `$160 million` returned to shareholders.
- Segment picture: in `Q4 2025`, `Dispensing and Specialty Closures` net sales were `$643.6 million`, `Metal Containers` net sales were `$675.6 million`, and `Custom Containers` EBIT was `$15.2 million`. The release highlighted `7%` growth in metal containers for pet-food markets and over `17%` sales growth in the dispensing-and-closures segment for the full year.
- Structural themes: Q4 makes the blind-spot value clearer than the sector label does. This is not mainly about branded consumer pull. It is about which company controls the physical container, closure, or dispensing path that lets staple products move reliably through food, beverage, beauty, home-care, and pet-food systems.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: `Packaging & Containers` is directionally right but still too broad. Silgan is most useful as a rigid-packaging and closure-control system rather than as a generic packaging name.
- Consumer: the consumer link is indirect but constant. Consumers may not know Silgan, but staple-food cans, dispensing closures, pet-food containers, fragrance and beauty pumps, and custom rigid containers sit directly in daily-use packaged-product systems.
- Cultural: the relevant cultural layer is not brand symbolism but invisible trust in everyday packaged products. The container has to work, preserve product integrity, dispense correctly, survive distribution, and fit staple-use behavior without friction.
- Societal: Silgan sits close to the resilience side of household consumption because its exposure is tilted toward essential consumer-goods categories rather than highly discretionary, novelty-driven product systems.
- Industrial: the important operating variables are raw-material pass-through, metal and resin cost timing, acquisition integration, volume or mix in pet food and dispensing products, manufacturing efficiency, free-cash-flow discipline, and the concentration of segment economics in closures and cans.
- Business model: Silgan is also useful because it shows that packaging control has internal layers. A company can own the broad format map, the user-interface mechanism, the corrugated throughput layer, or the rigid-container and closure stack. Those are not the same position.

## Why this company matters for the blind-spot lane

- `Graphic Packaging` already anchors carton conversion and packaging middleware.
- `Sonoco` already anchors broader packaging-format and container-control breadth.
- `AptarGroup` already anchors packaging-interface and dispensing-component control.
- `Silgan` is the best next packaging extension because it adds rigid-container, metal-can, and closure-system concentration with heavy essential-goods exposure.
- That makes the useful comparison `carton conversion` versus `broader packaging-format control` versus `dispensing-interface control` versus `rigid-container and closure control`, not just one more packaging packet.
