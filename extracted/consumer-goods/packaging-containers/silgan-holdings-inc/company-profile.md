## What changed

- Archive quality: Silgan is a usable blind-spot packet because the annual report and quarter chain make the packaging branch more specific than the generic `Packaging & Containers` label.
- Company shape: the `2025` materials show a three-part system in `Dispensing and Specialty Closures`, `Metal Containers`, and `Custom Containers`, not one undifferentiated packaging vendor.
- Quarter pattern: `Q4 2025` established the full-year rigid-packaging story, while `Q1 2026` and `Q2 2026` confirmed that the same closure, can, and custom-container stack remained the core operating structure.
- Blind-spot role: Silgan is the cleanest post-`Sonoco` and post-`AptarGroup` extension because it sharpens rigid-container and closure control rather than just adding more broad packaging exposure.
