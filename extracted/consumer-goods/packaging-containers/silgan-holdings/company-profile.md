## What changed

- Silgan exited `2025` as a much broader packaging system than a generic metal-can label suggests. The company now runs three distinct lanes at scale: `Dispensing and Specialty Closures`, `Metal Containers`, and `Custom Containers`.
- The `2025` annual filing showed a business with approximately `$6.5B` of sales, `121` manufacturing plants, and a product mix spanning fragrance and beauty pumps, food and beverage closures, pet and human food cans, and custom plastic containers for consumer health, pharmaceutical, home care, and automotive applications.
- `2025` looked like a growth-and-integration year. Net income rose to `$288.4M` from `$276.4M`, diluted EPS rose to `$2.70` from `$2.58`, adjusted EPS rose to `$3.72` from `$3.62`, and free cash flow increased to `$445.2M` from `$391.3M`.
- In early `2026`, the quarter chain showed a more mixed but still constructive setup: sales kept rising largely because of raw-material pass-through and resilient consumer-staples volumes, but EBIT and EPS were pressured by rationalization charges, tax effects, and tougher comparisons. Even so, management maintained or raised its `2026` earnings and cash expectations through the first half.

## Open questions

- How durable is Silgan's pet-food and dispensing growth if staples consumption normalizes and raw-material pass-through becomes less favorable?
- Can the company keep expanding higher-value dispensing and closures earnings fast enough to offset the more mature, cost-sensitive metal-container base?
- Will tariffs, raw-material inflation, and customer inventory timing keep distorting quarter-to-quarter comparisons inside metal containers and custom plastics?
