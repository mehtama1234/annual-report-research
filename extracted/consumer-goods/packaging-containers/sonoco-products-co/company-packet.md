## Annual report takeaways

- Sonoco is the cleanest current extension for the behind-the-shelf consumer infrastructure lane because it broadens packaging control beyond `Graphic Packaging`'s carton-and-carrier conversion layer and beyond `Clearwater Paper`'s SBS substrate layer.
- AnnualReports still lagged here as of Monday, August 10, 2026: the saved company page shows `Consumer Goods / Packaging & Containers` and still surfaced `2024` and `2023` annual-report links rather than a current `2025` annual package.
- The authoritative annual evidence therefore comes from the SEC chain. Sonoco's `2025` `10-K` shows a global packaging platform with approximately `265` locations in `37` countries serving branded end markets across consumer packaging and industrial paper packaging.
- Full-year `2025` GAAP net income attributable to Sonoco reached `$1.003 billion`, GAAP operating profit reached `$1.018 billion`, and the year included major portfolio actions and acquisition effects, especially the Eviosys acquisition and the ThermoSafe divestiture.
- The useful read is not "paper" in a narrow materials sense. Sonoco combines metal packaging, rigid paper containers, flexible and specialty formats, paper cans, protective and industrial packaging exposure, and broad packaging-control relationships across food, beverage, household, and industrial end markets.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` net sales were `$1.9 billion`, GAAP net income attributable to Sonoco was `$105 million`, diluted EPS was `$1.05`, adjusted net income was `$151 million`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.51`, adjusted operating profit was `$242 million`, and adjusted EBITDA was `$324 million`.
- Guidance: on `July 22, 2026`, Sonoco reaffirmed full-year `2026` guidance for sales, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EPS, and operating cash flow, keeping the framework introduced after Q1.
- Management message: management said `Industrial Paper Packaging` exceeded expectations, highlighted `6%` growth in North America uncoated recycled paperboard trade ton volume and `9%` EMEA/APAC paper-can volume growth, and emphasized record second-quarter operating cash flow.
- Structural themes: Q2 is the clearest proof that Sonoco belongs in the blind-spot lane as a broader packaging-format and container-control platform rather than as another basic paper or carton operator.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` net sales were `$1.7 billion`, GAAP net income attributable to Sonoco was `$67.6 million`, GAAP operating profit was `$127.1 million`, diluted EPS was `$0.68`, adjusted net income was `$119.4 million`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.20`, adjusted operating profit was `$200.8 million`, and adjusted EBITDA was `$276.5 million`.
- Guidance: on `April 21, 2026`, Sonoco kept full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance at `$1.25 billion` to `$1.35 billion`, operating cash-flow guidance at `$700 million` to `$800 million`, and shifted adjusted EPS expectations toward the low end of the prior `$5.80` to `$6.20` range.
- Management message: management said `Consumer Packaging` exceeded expectations while `Industrial Paper Packaging` faced operational and demand pressure, but productivity and favorable price-cost dynamics more than offset weaker volume-mix.
- Structural themes: Q1 shows why Sonoco sharpens the lane: the packaging-control story is not only cartons or substrate pricing, but also multi-format packaging execution, portfolio simplification, and packaging demand spread across multiple end markets.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` net sales were `$1.8 billion`, GAAP net income attributable to Sonoco was `$332.2 million`, GAAP operating profit was `$520.2 million`, diluted EPS was `$3.33`, adjusted net income was `$104.7 million`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.05`, adjusted operating profit was `$187 million`, and adjusted EBITDA was `$272 million`.
- Guidance: on `February 16, 2026`, Sonoco introduced full-year `2026` adjusted diluted EPS guidance of `$5.80` to `$6.20`, adjusted EBITDA guidance of `$1.25 billion` to `$1.35 billion`, and operating cash-flow guidance of `$700 million` to `$800 million`.
- Management message: management emphasized strong fourth-quarter cash generation, continued operating simplification, and a reporting shift toward two segments: `Consumer Packaging` and `Industrial Paper Packaging`.
- Structural themes: Q4 `2025` closes the year with Sonoco looking like a scaled packaging-control and format-diversification system, not simply a paperboard or container commodity producer.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Sonoco broadens `Consumer Goods / Packaging & Containers` beyond carton conversion by adding a more diversified packaging-format and container-control platform.
- Consumer: direct consumer demand is visible only indirectly through the packaged-goods and household brands that rely on Sonoco's packaging formats, substrate conversion, and industrial packaging support.
- Cultural: the relevant cultural layer is convenience packaging, shelf presentation, sustainable-format substitution, and the quiet physical standardization behind everyday branded consumption rather than overt brand identity.
- Industrial: the real operating questions are packaging-volume mix, price-cost balance, mill and paperboard utilization, packaging-format breadth, and acquisition-integration execution.
- Technology: the useful technology layer is packaging engineering, material substitution, protective-format design, and process coordination across a multi-format packaging network rather than consumer software.
- Capital allocation: the company is using capital around packaging-platform reshaping, portfolio simplification, cash generation, and leverage discipline following major acquisition and divestiture activity.

## Why this company matters for the blind-spot lane

- `Graphic Packaging` already anchors carton, tray, carrier, cup, and bowl conversion.
- `Clearwater Paper` already anchors SBS substrate and converter-facing paperboard supply.
- `Sonoco` is the best current extension because it shows that packaging control is wider than either of those layers alone.
- That makes the useful comparison `conversion` versus `substrate` versus `broader packaging-format and container control`, not simply one more packaging company.
