## What changed

- Coverage status: Sonoco is now a real current-year packaging-control case despite AnnualReports lagging at `2024`.
- Company shape: the `2025` annual filing frames Sonoco as a broad packaging platform with about `265` locations in `37` countries, not just a narrow paper or carton operator.
- Packaging-system role: Sonoco sits between `Graphic Packaging` and a wider packaging-interface layer because it spans multiple packaging formats, container types, and end markets.
- Quarter pattern: `Q4 2025` established the simplified two-segment framing, `Q1 2026` exposed uneven demand between segments, and `Q2 2026` showed stronger industrial paper packaging performance and stronger cash generation.
- Capital and portfolio shape: the `2025` and `2026` materials repeatedly emphasize acquisition integration, divestitures, leverage discipline, and operating simplification.
