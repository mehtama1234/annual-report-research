## What changed

- Revenue: fiscal 2025 annual materials show Coty operating through a slower beauty cycle, and the FY2026 quarter set confirms that. FY2026 Q1 net revenues were `$1,577.2 million`, FY2026 Q2 net revenues were `$1,678.6 million`, and FY2026 Q3 net revenues were `$1,281.6 million`.
- Mix: Coty is much more beauty-skewed than the rest of the current consumer-goods sample. Prestige remained about `68%` of sales in FY2026 Q2 and `65%` in FY2026 Q3, with Consumer Beauty under clearer pressure.
- Growth quality: FY2026 Q1 net revenues declined `6%` reported and `8%` LFL; FY2026 Q2 rose `1%` reported but fell `3%` LFL; FY2026 Q3 fell `1%` reported and `7%` LFL. This is a meaningfully weaker demand profile than the essentials-heavy names.
- Margin: pressure is visible even when Coty still protects profitability. Q1 adjusted EBITDA was `$296.1 million`; Q2 adjusted EBITDA was `$330.2 million`; Q3 adjusted EBITDA fell to `$127.0 million`.
- Strategy and capital discipline: management repeatedly references `All In to Win`, then `Coty. Curated.`, plus a continuing strategic review of Consumer Beauty. This is a sharper portfolio and execution-reset story than the other consumer-goods names.
- Category and geography: Prestige fragrance remains structurally stronger, while Consumer Beauty is weaker and the Middle East conflict was a material headwind in FY2026 Q3. China sell-out was a positive talking point in FY2026 Q1 and remained relevant in management framing.
- Consumer / cultural signals: beauty demand is still resilient in fragrance, but this is not the same kind of stable staples demand seen at P&G, Kimberly-Clark, Colgate, or PepsiCo. Consumer behavior here is more trend-, channel-, and execution-sensitive.
- Management tone: more transitional and corrective than the rest of the sector sample. Coty reads as a company with attractive assets but inconsistent operating delivery.

## Open questions

- Is Coty's weakness mostly a company-specific execution problem, or a real warning sign for beauty demand outside fragrance?
- Can Prestige fragrance strength continue to offset weakness in Consumer Beauty if the promotional environment stays heavy?
