## What changed

- Annual setup: the 2025 annual filing frames Estee Lauder as a prestige beauty system rather than a staples platform. The company sells skin care, makeup, fragrance, and hair care products in approximately `150` countries and territories, with a majority of online sales generated in mainland China, the United States, and the United Kingdom.
- Strategy: management now frames the operating reset through `Beauty Reimagined` and the `PRGP`, with the explicit goal of restoring a solid double-digit adjusted operating margin over the next few years.
- Revenue: FY2026 Q1 net sales were `$3.481 billion`, FY2026 Q2 net sales were `$4.229 billion`, and FY2026 Q3 net sales were `$3.712 billion`.
- Organic growth: FY2026 Q1 organic net sales rose `3%`, FY2026 Q2 organic net sales rose `4%`, and FY2026 Q3 organic net sales rose `2%`. That is materially stronger than Coty's recent trajectory and much closer to a recovery profile than a contraction profile.
- Margin: FY2026 Q1 adjusted operating margin was `7.3%`, FY2026 Q2 adjusted operating margin was `14.4%`, and FY2026 Q3 adjusted operating margin was `15.0%`. The pace of recovery is one of the clearest positive operating surprises in the current consumer-goods set.
- Category mix: fragrance is the standout growth engine. Q1 fragrance net sales rose `13%`; Q2 fragrance net sales rose `6%`; and Q3 fragrance net sales rose `10%`, with management also calling out double-digit fragrance growth for the first nine months of FY2026.
- Geography: mainland China moved from a known pressure point to a recovery driver. The company reported mainland-China growth in Q1 and Q2 and said Q3 growth in three of four regions was led by mainland China.
- Risks: this is not a clean defensive-staples read. Tariffs are expected to create roughly `$100 million` of FY2026 profitability headwind, and the company disclosed a Middle East conflict impact plus a securities-class-action settlement accrual in Q3.
- Management tone: much more constructive than Coty and more restructuring-driven than the staples names. Estee Lauder currently reads as a prestige-beauty recovery with improving execution, not as an effortless compounder.

## Open questions

- Is Estee Lauder's recovery mainly a company-specific operational fix, or does it signal a broader prestige-beauty improvement?
- Can fragrance and mainland-China momentum continue to offset softer channels like travel retail and weaker legacy makeup pockets?
