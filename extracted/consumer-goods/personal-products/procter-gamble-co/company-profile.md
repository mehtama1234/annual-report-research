## What changed

- Revenue: fiscal 2025 closed with net sales of `$84.3 billion`, then FY2026 Q2 net sales reached `$22.2 billion`, FY2026 Q3 reached `$21.2 billion`, and FY2026 Q4 reached `$21.2 billion` with much weaker organic momentum by year-end.
- Margin: 2025 core EPS grew `4%`, but FY2026 Q4 core EPS fell `3%` even as full-year FY2026 core EPS still rose `1%` to `$6.89`, showing a more strained second-half margin profile.
- Guidance: P&G maintained fiscal-2026 guidance through FY2026 Q2 and Q3, then in the July 29, 2026 release reset the frame to FY2027 sales growth of `1%` to `3%`, core EPS of `$6.89` to `$7.11`, and adjusted free cash flow productivity of `85%` to `90%`.
- Capital allocation: capital return remains central. P&G returned `$4.8 billion` in FY2026 Q2, `$3.2 billion` in FY2026 Q3, and over `$15 billion` across FY2026, including `$10.2 billion` of dividends and `$5.0 billion` of repurchases.
- Segment mix: quarterly disclosures show the portfolio is broad enough to rotate between categories and regions. FY2026 Q3 had all ten categories growing, while FY2026 Q4 was more mixed with Beauty stronger and Baby, Feminine and Family Care weaker.
- Operational issues: the materials repeatedly point to a difficult consumer, geopolitical volatility, restructuring, and by FY2026 Q4 explicit tariff and reinvestment pressure on margins.
- Consumer / cultural signals: superiority, value, affordability, and daily-use necessity remain the core commercial language. The portfolio is being managed around value delivery rather than premiumization alone.
- Management tone: disciplined and operational. Management consistently emphasizes integrated execution, productivity, and value creation instead of aggressive volume or category-expansion rhetoric.

## Open questions

- How durable is P&G's growth if organic sales remain flat to low-single-digit while reinvestment and tariff pressure keep rising?
- Does the broad portfolio still provide enough insulation if North America and lower-income consumer demand stay soft into fiscal 2027?
