## Reconciliation notes

- AnnualReports still labels the company `Campbell Soup Company`, while the official company and SEC identity in the fiscal `2025` annual materials is `The Campbell's Company`.
- The annual-report artifact is saved from both AnnualReports and the official IR site, which makes Campbell one of the cleaner dual-source annual-report captures in the archive.
- The official IR site provided direct quarter-level release, transcript, and prepared-remarks artifacts for all three in-scope fiscal `2026` quarters.
- The SEC submissions index cleanly reconciles the annual filing, annual report to security holders, and the Q1 through Q3 FY2026 filing chain.

## Most important evidence for the packet thesis

- Fiscal `2025` annual materials show a broader pantry-and-snacks portfolio beyond soup, including the formal company rename and the stronger role of Rao's.
- Q1 FY`2026`, Q2 FY`2026`, and Q3 FY`2026` together show the central split in the business:
  - Meals & Beverages remains supported by at-home cooking and pantry demand.
  - Snacks remains softer and more execution-sensitive.
  - Inflation, tariffs, and other supply-chain costs continue to pressure margins even where consumer demand is still present.
