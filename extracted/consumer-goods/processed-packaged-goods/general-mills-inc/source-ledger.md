## Most important evidence for the packet thesis

- Fiscal `2025` materials show a broad pantry-and-replenishment platform under pressure from value-seeking consumers rather than a company enjoying effortless staples demand.
- Q2 FY`2026`, Q3 FY`2026`, and Q4 FY`2026` show the full reset arc:
  - Q2 shows improving competitiveness and the remarkability-led reinvestment plan.
  - Q3 shows how retailer inventory and weather disruptions can still materially worsen reported results.
  - Q4 shows a better finish, but also how much the current earnings line still benefits from timing, the `53rd` week, and cost productivity.
- Across the chain, General Mills reads as a broad `replenishment interface` company where consumer value, product relevance, and cost-funded reinvestment are now more important than simple shelf presence.
