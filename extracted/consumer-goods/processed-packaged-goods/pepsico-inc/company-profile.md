## What changed

- Revenue: full-year 2025 net revenue reached `$93.9 billion`; quarterly net revenue then moved from a `5.6%` increase in Q4 2025 to `$19.4 billion` in Q1 2026 and `$24.2 billion` in Q2 2026.
- Margin: 2025 reported operating profit fell to `$11.5 billion`, but Q4 2025 showed strong margin recovery; Q1 2026 operating margin reached `16.5%` and Q2 2026 operating margin reached `16.6%`.
- Guidance: management affirmed 2026 guidance in Q4 2025, Q1 2026, and Q2 2026, while leaning harder on affordability initiatives, portfolio restaging, and productivity.
- Capital allocation: the company expects to return about `$8.9 billion` to shareholders in 2026, including roughly `$7.9 billion` of dividends and `$1.0 billion` of share repurchases.
- Segment mix: PepsiCo remains balanced between food and beverage, but international growth is currently stronger than North America and convenient foods remain central to the portfolio.
- Operational issues: North American category demand is softer, consumer budgets are tightening, and management is using affordability actions, mix management, and productivity to protect volume and margins.
- Consumer / cultural signals: zero sugar, hydration, protein, fiber, functional offerings, portion control, and permissible snack positioning are clearly shaping how PepsiCo is trying to stay aligned with current household demand.
- Management tone: management is disciplined rather than exuberant, emphasizing execution, productivity, value architecture, international strength, and selective portfolio evolution over pure pricing.

## Open questions

- How much of PepsiCo’s 2026 resilience comes from real demand strength versus temporary support from affordability actions, acquisitions, and foreign exchange?
- Can North America reaccelerate meaningfully if consumer budgets remain under pressure and pricing becomes less available as a growth lever?
