## What changed

- Revenue and earnings: YETI is visible here as a premium outdoor-utility and gear-attachment platform rather than a generic sporting-goods issuer. Full-year `2025` sales were `$1.8685B`, net income was `$165.4M`, and operating income was `$213.6M`.
- Channel mix: the fiscal `2025` chain showed `DTC` sales of `$1.1278B` and wholesale sales of `$740.7M`, while the `10-K` said the `DTC` channel accounted for `60%` of net sales and wholesale accounted for `40%`.
- Relationship layer: the `10-K` and `10-Q` chain explicitly says YETI sells through its websites, `YETI Authorized` on the Amazon Marketplace, retail stores, and a corporate sales program, which means the company is not just selling gear through stores. It is actively owning and widening the customer relationship.
- Geographic and category mix: full-year `2025` international sales increased `16%` to `$394.4M`, while `Q4 2025` international sales increased `25%` and `Q1 2026` international sales increased `9%`. The core category split also stayed visible, with Drinkware still larger but Coolers & Equipment often growing faster.
- Margin pressure and mitigation: the `Q3 2025` through `Q1 2026` chain repeatedly flagged higher tariff costs, selective price increases, and supply-chain diversification. That makes the packet useful not just for demand quality, but for how a premium consumer brand manages geopolitical cost pressure without surrendering its positioning.
- Blind-spot fit: this is a clean correction to source taxonomy because the company is both taxonomically under-specified and behaviorally richer than the source label suggests. The useful comparison is not only with other sporting-goods companies, but with DTC-control brands, utility-identity products, and outdoor-lifestyle attachment systems.

## Open questions

- How durable is YETI's premium positioning if tariff pressure persists and selective price increases need to do more of the work?
- Can the company keep broadening the relationship through Amazon Marketplace, corporate sales, and retail stores without diluting its premium-control logic?
- Does the current category mix keep drifting toward a broader utility-and-lifestyle platform, or does the business remain structurally anchored by drinkware and cooler-adjacent attachment?
