## Annual report takeaways

- lululemon adds a distinct athletic-apparel, wellness-identity, and community-led consumption lane that the current consumer-goods set did not yet have. It is materially different from pantry staples, beverages, cleaning products, nicotine, or premium spirits because demand here is tied to aspiration, product innovation, brand community, and full-price apparel sell-through.
- The official investor-relations chain exposed the `2025` annual report even though AnnualReports.com still lagged at the `2024` package on 2026-08-10. That makes lululemon another strong example of why the archive has to preserve source taxonomy without relying on the aggregator for current annual collection.
- The `2025` annual results show a business still growing, but with a more uneven mix than the brand halo alone would suggest. Full-year revenue increased `5%` to `$11.1 billion`, diluted EPS was `$13.26`, Americas net revenue decreased `1%`, and international net revenue increased `22%`.
- The core annual story is not just “premium brand strength.” The filings and result pages show a business leaning on international momentum and brand-community relevance while trying to restore healthier full-price trends in North America and protect margin quality.
- lululemon therefore matters to the larger project because it shows a consumer-goods company where the key question is how a culturally resonant performance-and-wellness brand turns community and identity into durable global growth when its home market is softer.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q1 `2026` revenue increased `4%` to `$2.5 billion`, comparable sales increased `1%`, and diluted EPS was `$1.69`.
- Mix signal: Americas net revenue decreased `3%`, while international net revenue increased `22%`; Americas comparable sales decreased `5%`, while international comparable sales increased `13%`.
- Margin signal: gross margin decreased `410` basis points to `54.2%`, and operating margin decreased `730` basis points to `11.2%`.
- Structural read: this quarter is the cleanest proof that lululemon is not just a clean global growth brand at the moment. The business is still growing, but North America softness and lower margin quality mean management is actively repositioning product, pricing, and brand execution.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q4 `2025` revenue increased `1%` to `$3.6 billion`, full-year revenue increased `5%` to `$11.1 billion`, and diluted EPS for the quarter was `$5.01` while full-year diluted EPS was `$13.26`.
- Mix signal: for the quarter, Americas net revenue decreased `4%` while international net revenue increased `17%`; for the full year, Americas net revenue decreased `1%` while international net revenue increased `22%`.
- Margin signal: fourth-quarter gross margin decreased `550` basis points to `54.9%`, and full-year gross margin decreased `260` basis points to `56.6%`.
- Structural read: the year-end quarter shows a brand with strong international demand and real cultural pull, but also a very visible North America repair job. That makes lululemon more analytically useful than a smoother premium-apparel story would have been.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q3 `2025` revenue increased `7%` to `$2.6 billion`, comparable sales increased `1%`, and diluted EPS was `$2.59`.
- Mix signal: Americas net revenue decreased `2%`, while international net revenue increased `33%`; Americas comparable sales decreased `5%`, while international comparable sales increased `18%`.
- Margin signal: gross margin decreased `290` basis points to `55.6%`, and operating margin decreased `350` basis points to `17.0%`.
- Structural read: Q3 is the clearest quarter for showing the underlying split in the model. International momentum is carrying the topline, but the company is already working to stabilize its core U.S. and broader Americas business.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: lululemon adds the athletic-apparel, wellness-identity, and community-driven lifestyle layer to Consumer Goods.
- Consumer: this packet points to a consumer system shaped by aspiration, full-price brand trust, product drops, technical performance claims, and repeat category engagement rather than by simple staple replenishment.
- Cultural: lululemon matters because the brand is bound up with yoga, training, wellness, and a broader lifestyle-performance identity. That makes it one of the cleaner consumer-goods examples where community and self-concept are part of demand formation.
- Geography: the most important near-term operating split is weak Americas trends against much stronger international growth. That is a different global-mix pattern than the one seen in food staples, beverages, or nicotine.
- Channel and execution: the packet is useful because it is not just about brand heat. It also shows how store productivity, digital conversion, full-price selling, and product engine quality still govern whether the identity story turns into attractive earnings.
- Strategic contrast: Nike is the larger wholesale-versus-direct global athletic brand, Bath & Body Works is the weaker specialty-brand repair case, and lululemon is the cleaner premium wellness-and-community comparison with stronger international momentum and a different product-and-culture engine.

## Working interpretation

- lululemon gives the archive a consumer-goods lane built less on replenishment or regulation and more on wellness identity, community affiliation, premium product trust, and technical-apparel relevance.
- It also sharpens the brand-led comparison set. Nike is the larger channel-balance and global-sport brand case, while lululemon is the more focused premium wellness-and-community case with stronger recent international growth and clearer North America softness.
- For the `2025` through `2026` window, lululemon is strong evidence that one important part of Consumer Goods is really about turning cultural affiliation and community-based product trust into global repeat demand without losing full-price discipline.
