## What changed

- Revenue: Crocs moved from `$958M` in `Q4 2025` to `$921M` in `Q1 2026` and then to `$1.179B` in `Q2 2026`.
- Channel mix: the annual filing showed `47.9%` of `2025` revenue came through wholesale, which implies DTC was already the larger revenue channel.
- Brand mix: the quarter chain keeps showing the strength of the core `Crocs Brand` while leaving `HEYDUDE` as the weaker and more repair-dependent brand.
- Consumer and cultural signals: Crocs is a direct read on comfort-first, casual, globally legible consumer identity rather than only on performance footwear or formal fashion.
- Operating pressure: the company still needed active cost savings, debt paydown, and brand repair work, so this is not an effortless-growth story.

## Open questions

- How durable is Crocs-brand growth if casualization stays strong but the novelty layer fades further?
- Can `HEYDUDE` become a second durable demand engine, or does it remain mostly a drag on the cleaner Crocs-brand story?
- Is Crocs best understood as a broad global comfort platform, or as a culturally sticky hero-product system that still depends heavily on one silhouette family?
