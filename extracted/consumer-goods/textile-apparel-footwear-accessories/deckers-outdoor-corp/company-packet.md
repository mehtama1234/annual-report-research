- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q1 2027
  - quarter 2: FY 2026
  - quarter 3: Q3 2026

## Annual report takeaways

- Deckers materially improves the archive because it adds a different kind of discretionary demand engine: performance-running and comfort-lifestyle footwear rather than simple mall apparel or accessible luxury. The core structure is a multi-brand system built around `HOKA`, `UGG`, and `Teva`.
- AnnualReports.com still lagged here. On Monday, August 10, 2026, it showed `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K`, so the official `2025` annual package had to come from Deckers IR and the filing chain.
- The fiscal `2025` annual and year-end materials show FY `2025` revenue increased `16%` to a record `$4.99B`, diluted EPS increased `30%` to a record `$6.33`, `HOKA` revenue increased `23.6%`, and `UGG` revenue increased `13.1%`.
- The strategic read is that Deckers is not just a footwear company. It is a high-margin brand machine that converts two different kinds of consumer desire into growth at once: `HOKA` captures performance, wellness, and running identity, while `UGG` captures comfort, casual fashion, and premium feel.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: `Q1 2027` net sales increased `5.7%` to `$1.020B`; gross margin was `56.4%`; and diluted EPS increased to `$0.94`.
- Operating posture: `HOKA` net sales increased `7.7%` to `$703.5M`, `UGG` increased `4.9%` to `$278.0M`, DTC net sales increased `13.0%`, and international net sales increased `8.4%`.
- Structural themes: this quarter matters because it shows Deckers crossing the `$1B` first-quarter revenue threshold while still leaning on premium full-price demand and deeper consumer connection across channels and geographies.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `FY 2026` revenue increased `10%` to a record `$5.47B`; diluted EPS increased `11%` to a record `$7.02`; and the company said it generated more than `$1B` of free cash flow.
- Operating posture: full-year `HOKA` revenue increased `15.9%` to `$2.587B`, `UGG` increased `8.2%` to `$2.739B`, wholesale grew `12.3%`, DTC grew `6.3%`, and international revenue increased `26.8%`.
- Structural themes: this is the anchor quarter because it makes the whole Deckers model visible at once. `HOKA` is still the faster growth engine, `UGG` remains very large and durable, and the company is pairing premium pricing power with large buybacks and international expansion.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: `Q3 2026` revenue increased `7.1%` to a record `$1.958B`; diluted EPS increased `11%` to a record `$3.33`.
- Operating posture: `HOKA` net sales increased `18.5%` to `$628.9M`, `UGG` increased `4.9%` to `$1.305B`, DTC net sales increased `8.1%`, and international net sales increased `15.0%`.
- Structural themes: this quarter is useful because it shows both growth engines firing at once with strong full-price selling. It also shows that Deckers can balance wholesale and DTC rather than relying on one channel to carry the story.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Deckers strengthens `Consumer Goods` with a premium footwear and lifestyle operator that AnnualReports classifies under `Consumer Goods / Textile - Apparel Footwear & Accessories`.
- Consumer: this is a read on a consumer still willing to spend for performance, comfort, and lifestyle signaling when the brands feel differentiated and premium rather than interchangeable.
- Cultural: Deckers is one of the cleaner culture-and-identity packets in the archive because `HOKA` and `UGG` sit in different but equally charged consumer identities: wellness-performance and comfort-fashion.
- Societal: the quarter chain shows how casualization, health-and-movement culture, and premium comfort have become durable spending lanes rather than temporary trends.
- Operating model: the company combines strong brand portfolios, full-price selling, wholesale reach, owned DTC channels, global expansion, and aggressive capital return.
- Competitive positioning: Deckers is most useful next to `lululemon athletica inc.`, `Tapestry, Inc.`, `Levi Strauss & Co.`, and `Urban Outfitters, Inc.`. It is more function-and-footwear-led than Tapestry, more comfort-and-fashion blended than lululemon, and less mall-sensitive than the traditional apparel packets.

## Working interpretation

- Deckers is not just another apparel or footwear datapoint. It is one of the archive's best proofs that premium consumer demand can be built from two different emotional systems at once: movement-and-performance through `HOKA`, and comfort-and-style through `UGG`.
- The most important evidence is that the annual and quarter chain all point in the same direction: strong full-price demand, international growth, DTC reinforcement, and brand identities that remain culturally resonant rather than purely functional.
- For the `2025` through `2026` window, Deckers is one of the archive's stronger arguments that consumer discretionary resilience is increasingly concentrated in brands that combine product credibility, emotional identity, and channel control.
