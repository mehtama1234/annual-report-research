## What changed

- Revenue and earnings: Deckers moved from FY `2025` revenue of `$4.986B` and diluted EPS of `$6.33` to FY `2026` revenue of `$5.47B` and diluted EPS of `$7.02`, then opened Q1 `2027` above `$1B` in quarterly revenue.
- Brand mix: `HOKA` is the faster-growth performance engine, but `UGG` remains a very large and highly profitable comfort-lifestyle brand.
- Channel mix: the company is proving it can grow through both wholesale and DTC rather than sacrificing one for the other.
- Geography: international growth is consistently stronger than domestic, which matters because it suggests brand expansion rather than only U.S. saturation.
- Consumer and cultural signals: this is a cleaner casualization, comfort, and movement-culture case than classic mall apparel.
- Operating cleanup: the quarter and annual chain also make visible the phase-out of `Koolaburra` standalone operations and the sale of `Sanuk`, which simplifies the portfolio around the stronger brands.
- Capital allocation: Deckers is using its cash generation aggressively through buybacks, including more than `$1B` of repurchases in FY `2026`.

## Open questions

- How long can `HOKA` keep compounding at premium growth rates before the business starts to look more mature?
- Can `UGG` keep broadening beyond its legacy seasonal and boot-centered associations without losing pricing power?
- Does the current mix imply Deckers is really two high-quality brands and one smaller support brand, or is there still room for a wider platform?
