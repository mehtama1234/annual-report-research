## What changed

- Demand profile: PMI behaves less like a classic staple franchise and more like a regulated habitual-consumption platform with strong switching and retention dynamics.
- Revenue mix: smoke-free already reached `41.5%` of full-year `2025` net revenues, `43%` in Q1 `2026`, and approximately `42%` in Q2 `2026`.
- Strategic direction: the company is explicitly trying to shift its long-term center of gravity away from cigarettes and toward heat-not-burn, nicotine pouches, and other smoke-free formats.
- Comparison value: PMI is a stronger cultural and behavioral counterpoint to Coca-Cola, Monster, and Brown-Forman than another generic staples or pantry operator would have been.

## Open questions

- How much of PMI's current strength comes from category migration and how much still comes from the legacy cigarette cash engine?
- Does smoke-free mix continue compounding fast enough to move the company out of the legacy tobacco frame in practice, not just in investor messaging?
- Which future packet would best test this lane: another nicotine operator, a wellness-adjacent behavior system, or another consumer category built around habit under heavier regulation?
