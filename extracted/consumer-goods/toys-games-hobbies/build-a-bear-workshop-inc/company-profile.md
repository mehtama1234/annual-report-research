## What changed in this period

- Build-A-Bear kept growing through `2025`, but the growth engine is no longer just mall-based kid retail.
- The company is increasingly explicit that it is monetizing:
  - multi-generational fandom
  - gifting
  - occasions and commemoration
  - pop-culture licensing
  - asset-light partner-operated expansion
  - commercial and wholesale channels
- `Q1 2026` then exposed the tension in that model:
  - direct-to-consumer traffic and e-commerce weakened
  - but commercial and franchise revenues surged
  - and the tariff refund temporarily lifted reported profitability

## Why this name matters in the archive

- Build-A-Bear adds a ritualized, personalized, and experiential physical-participation layer to the play and fandom frontier.
- It sits between:
  - `Funko`, which monetizes collector-display fandom
  - broader toy companies, which monetize play systems and owned brands
  - lifestyle and recreation names, which monetize occasions and hosted experiences
- Its control point is not simply a toy SKU.
- It is a branded ceremony and memory-making system that converts life moments into customized physical artifacts.
