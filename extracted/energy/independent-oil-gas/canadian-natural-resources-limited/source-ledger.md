## Reconciliation notes

- The authoritative latest-three-quarter window is:
  - `Q2 2026`
  - `Q1 2026`
  - `Q4 2025`
- AnnualReports already displayed the `2025` annual package, so there was no lag conflict to resolve.
- The official CNQ financial-report pages are the primary reporting authority for this packet.
- `Q2 2026` is documented through separate `MD&A`, `Financial Statements`, and supplemental files instead of a single combined interim report PDF.
- CNQ adds a distinct branch packet because it combines:
  - long life low decline reserves
  - oil sands mining and upgrading
  - thermal in situ production
  - large conventional liquids and natural-gas production
  - acquisition-driven reserve and production growth
  - explicit free-cash-flow allocation tied to net-debt thresholds
