## What changed

- Coterra adds a different independent-E&P case than the branch's pure growth, pure gas, or pure international names. The company was a diversified U.S. shale operator built across the `Permian`, `Marcellus`, and `Anadarko`, with commodity optionality, transport sensitivity, and strong capital-discipline framing.
- The `2025` annual materials show a company that had just integrated the Delaware Basin acquisitions and was leaning into scale, efficiency, and shareholder returns rather than simple production growth. The filing emphasizes a top-tier position, commodity mix, and capital-allocation flexibility rather than a single-basin identity.
- The reporting window is the real reason this packet matters. By `May 7, 2026`, Coterra stopped being a standalone company because the Devon merger closed. That means the correct latest-three-quarter window on `August 10, 2026` is not the normal `Q2 2026`, `Q1 2026`, `Q4 2025` pattern. It is:
  - `Q1 2026`
  - `Q4 2025`
  - `Q3 2025`
- That makes Coterra a clean consolidation packet. It captures a strong standalone shale operator just before it disappears into a larger Delaware-led system.
- AnnualReports and the saved company IR route did not yield a separate `2025` shareholder annual-report artifact in this environment, so the annual layer is intentionally anchored in the filed SEC `10-K`.
