## Reconciliation notes

- AnnualReports was useful for taxonomy, but not current enough for the target year. The saved page still showed `2024` as the latest hosted package on `Monday, August 10, 2026`.
- The official Coterra IR route was no longer a normal reporting archive. The saved page was a `404` shell with a banner stating that Coterra completed its merger with Devon on `May 7, 2026`.
- No separate standalone `2025` shareholder annual-report PDF was recovered in the authoritative local chain after the merger disruption.
- The annual anchor for this packet is therefore the filed SEC `10-K`.
- The authoritative standalone quarter chain is intentionally adjusted and complete as of `Monday, August 10, 2026`:
  - `Q1 2026`
  - `Q4 2025`
  - `Q3 2025`
- A standalone `Q2 2026` quarter is intentionally excluded because the merger closed on `2026-05-07`, before a normal standalone `Q2 2026` reporting cadence existed.
- Coterra is a useful packet because it captures both states of the same shale platform:
  - a disciplined, commodity-diversified independent E&P company in `Permian`, `Marcellus`, and `Anadarko`
  - a consolidation endpoint where standalone reporting stops because scale economics and basin concentration become the new strategic logic
