## Reconciliation notes

- AnnualReports was useful for taxonomy and archive confirmation, but not current enough for the `2025` annual-report year because the saved page still showed `2024` as the most recent package on `2026-08-10`.
- The official Devon IR route structure was live-verified, but the local HTML downloads were Cloudflare challenge wrappers. For that reason, the packet is intentionally anchored in:
  - the SEC `ARS` annual-report PDF
  - the SEC `10-K`
  - the SEC `10-Q` and `8-K` chain
  - the SEC Exhibit `99.1` earnings releases
  - the SEC Exhibit `99.2` supplemental financial-information packages
- The quarter window is complete and correctly anchored as of `Monday, August 10, 2026`:
  - `Q2 2026`
  - `Q1 2026`
  - `Q4 2025`
- Devon is a useful lane bridge because the evidence chain spans two states of the business:
  - standalone Devon before the `Coterra` merger closes
  - the enlarged Devon immediately after the `2026-05-07` merger closes
