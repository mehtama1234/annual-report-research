## What changed

- EQT adds the gas-and-Appalachia version of the upstream system, which was underrepresented in the branch's oil-heavy independent-producer set. This is a materially different packet from `Diamondback`, `Devon`, or `Occidental`: basis, compression, curtailments, LNG offtake, power-market linkage, and balance-sheet de-levering all matter more than simple oil realizations.
- The `2025` annual close is useful because it shows EQT as more than a commodity-exposed driller. The annual and year-end materials frame the company as an integrated natural-gas system with upstream, gathering, and transmission operations in Appalachia, with proved reserves up `7%` year over year to roughly `28 Tcfe`, a standardized measure of discounted future net cash flows of about `\$21B`, and PV-10 of about `\$26B` at the SEC price deck.
- The quarter chain then splits into two very different gas environments. `Q1 2026` was a gas-price and free-cash-flow harvest quarter, while `Q2 2026` was a lower-price but still operationally strong quarter where marketing, basis management, compression benefits, and new demand-linked contracts kept the strategic story moving.
- EQT also gives the branch a cleaner domestic gas-demand transition story. By `Q2 2026`, management was explicitly tying future value creation not only to LNG offtake but also to premium power demand through a long-term gas-supply agreement with `CPV Shay Energy Center`. That makes EQT a useful bridge between upstream, pipelines, LNG, and data-center-adjacent power demand.
- AnnualReports was not current enough for the `2025` annual year, and the official IR routes were Cloudflare-blocked locally, so this packet is intentionally anchored in the SEC `ARS`, filed `10-K`, and `Q4 2025` through `Q2 2026` filing and Exhibit `99.1` chain.

## Open questions

- If U.S. gas demand grows through LNG and power, does EQT gain durable pricing and volume optionality, or does Appalachia basis still absorb too much of the upside?
- Can EQT keep its capital-light, de-levering, free-cash-flow story intact while still funding infrastructure-heavy growth projects tied to compression, LNG, and power-supply commitments?
- Does EQT's integrated-gas framing become a stronger competitive advantage over time, or does it simply expose the company more directly to bottlenecks outside the wellhead?
