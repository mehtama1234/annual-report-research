## Reconciliation notes

- AnnualReports was useful for archive confirmation, but not current enough for the `2025` annual-report year because the saved page still showed `2024` as the most recent package on `2026-08-10`.
- AnnualReports taxonomy is also not reliable for analytical classification here because broader AnnualReports listings place EQT under `Oil & Gas Equipment & Services`, while EQT's own filings describe an integrated natural-gas producer and related midstream operator.
- Official EQT IR annual and quarterly routes were live-verified, but the local HTML captures were Cloudflare challenge wrappers. For that reason, the packet is intentionally anchored in:
  - the SEC `ARS` annual-report PDF
  - the SEC `10-K`
  - the SEC `10-Q` and `8-K` chain
  - the SEC Exhibit `99.1` earnings releases
- The quarter window is complete and correctly anchored as of `Monday, August 10, 2026`:
  - `Q2 2026`
  - `Q1 2026`
  - `Q4 2025`
- EQT is a useful lane bridge because the evidence chain spans both:
  - gas-heavy Appalachian upstream economics
  - LNG, basis, compression, and premium-power demand linkages that connect upstream value to infrastructure and electricity markets
