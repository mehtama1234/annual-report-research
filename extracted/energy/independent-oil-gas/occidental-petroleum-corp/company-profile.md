## What changed

- Occidental adds a materially different independent-oil-and-gas packet than `EOG Resources` or `ConocoPhillips`. It still depends on upstream commodity exposure, but it also carries meaningful midstream-and-marketing earnings, Western Midstream equity exposure, balance-sheet repair priorities, enhanced-oil-recovery capabilities, and a more explicit carbon-management and lower-carbon-technology overlay.
- The `2025` annual-report framing is useful for theme work because it combines operating scale with financial repair. The official annual report highlighted record annual production around `1.43 million BOEPD`, about `\$4.3B` of free cash flow before working capital, principal debt reduced to roughly `\$13.8B`, and an expanded resource base. That makes the packet less about pure growth and more about turning scale, cost discipline, and deleveraging into resilience.
- The quarter chain also marks a real portfolio transition. OxyChem was sold on `2026-01-02`, so `Q1 2026` and `Q2 2026` are continuing-operations periods where oil and gas, midstream and marketing, and carbon-management positioning have to stand more clearly on their own.
- AnnualReports is not locally reliable for this name in the current environment. The direct company route saved only a generic landing page, so the packet is intentionally anchored in official Oxy IR and SEC filings, with AnnualReports treated as a live-verified taxonomy reference rather than a usable local source artifact.

## Open questions

- How much of Occidental’s current improvement is durable portfolio quality and cost discipline versus commodity-price assistance and midstream timing effects?

- Can Occidental keep reducing debt toward the `\$10B` milestone while also funding carbon-management ambitions and maintaining competitive oil-and-gas reinvestment?
