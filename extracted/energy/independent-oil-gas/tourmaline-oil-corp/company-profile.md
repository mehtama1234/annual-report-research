## What changed

- Tourmaline adds the gas-heavy Canadian upstream flagship rather than another oil sands heavyweight, pipeline toll-road, or refinery margin story.
- The packet is distinctive because the company is not mainly a crude-growth or service-intensity case. It is a natural-gas-dominant exploration-and-production system built around low-cost Western Canadian gas, condensate, NGLs, storage optionality, and a marketing book that becomes more important when export routes tighten.
- The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain also shows a company actively reshaping volume mix through acquisitions, disposition, and processing choices. Tourmaline bought assets in `2025`, sold Spirit River in early `2026`, and chose to end discretionary deep-cut gas-plant deliveries in the Alberta Deep Basin, accepting lower NGL output to improve economics.
- This makes Tourmaline one of the stronger branch cases for natural-gas leverage, infrastructure dependence, storage timing, export bottlenecks, and the difference between headline production growth and value-maximizing molecule placement.

## Open questions

- Does the market treat Tourmaline primarily as gas beta, or as a more durable free-cash-flow compounder with unusually strong marketing and storage optionality?
- How much long-term value comes from physical market access and storage control rather than from drilling inventory alone?
