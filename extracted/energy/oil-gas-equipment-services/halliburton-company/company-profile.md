## What changed

- Halliburton gives the branch a clean oilfield-services flagship rather than another owner of reserves, pipelines, refineries, or export docks. The packet is fundamentally about what happens between producer intent and delivered well execution.
- The official `2025` annual package showed a strategy built around profitable international growth, maximizing value in North America, capital efficiency, digital and automation deployment, and an energy-transition-adjacent technology posture rather than a simple rig-count story.
- The quarter chain then sharpened the cycle read. `Q4 2025` highlighted strong international positioning and disciplined returns; `Q1 2026` showed a softer cash quarter but a clearer North America recovery signal; `Q2 2026` showed that recovery becoming more visible while international contract awards and digital execution remained central.
- Halliburton is therefore a useful bridge packet between upstream commodity exposure and the operational reality of drilling, completions, intervention, software, automation, and field execution.

## Open questions

- How durable is the North America recovery if oil-price volatility or customer capital discipline tightens again?
- Can international contract depth and digital integration keep margins expanding if Middle East geopolitical disruptions persist?
- Does Halliburton's automation and integrated-well-delivery push become a real structural moat, or mainly a cyclical differentiator during upturns?
