## What changed

- Energy Transfer adds a broader hydrocarbon-transport and export-platform packet than the more toll-road-like midstream cases already in the branch. The system spans natural gas, NGLs, crude, refined products, fractionation, storage, terminals, export docks, and related equity interests in `Sunoco LP` and `USA Compression`.
- The annual and quarterly materials show why transport bottlenecks and export corridors matter as much as pipeline mileage. Energy Transfer repeatedly tied performance to Mont Belvieu, Nederland, Permian takeaway, fractionation, and NGL export growth rather than only to fixed interstate pipe tariffs.
- The `2025` annual-close read is especially useful for theme work. Management explicitly said the partnership generates approximately `40%` of adjusted EBITDA from natural-gas-related assets, that the vast majority of segment margin is fee-based, and that no single segment contributed more than one-third of consolidated adjusted EBITDA. That makes the packet a strong case study in diversified infrastructure dependence rather than single-basin or single-commodity concentration.
- The AnnualReports-versus-authoritative-source split also matters here. AnnualReports still points to the legacy `Energy Transfer Partners, L.P.` taxonomy page and lagged at `2024`, while the official IR site already exposed the `2025 10-K` and the exact required quarter chain. The packet should therefore use AnnualReports for taxonomy confirmation and official IR plus SEC for substance.

## Open questions

- How durable is the current earnings acceleration if export loading, basis opportunities, and Permian-related bottlenecks normalize?
- Does Energy Transfer’s scale create more resilience than execution risk as the capital program shifts deeper into natural-gas pipes, export docks, and fractionation support?
- Over time, does the highest-value part of the system remain fee-based transportation, or does more of the upside migrate toward export optionality, terminal services, and complex system optimization?
