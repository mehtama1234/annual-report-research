## What changed

- MPLX adds a different midstream packet than the branch's crude-corridor names. This is a broader natural-gas, NGL, gathering, processing, fractionation, storage, terminaling, and fuels-logistics system rather than a mainly crude-transport platform.
- The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain makes the strategic posture clear: MPLX is using a large fee-based and cash-return-heavy base to fund another leg of gas and NGL infrastructure expansion across the Permian, Marcellus, Gulf Coast fractionation, and export-linked systems.
- The packet is especially useful because it shows how midstream infrastructure can still carry commodity sensitivity without looking like an upstream producer. The immediate exposure is not reserve depletion but basin activity, producer throughput, utilization, project timing, and whether bottlenecks emerge in gathering, treating, processing, fractionation, or export handling.
- AnnualReports lagged at `2024`, and the official MPLX IR host was partly Cloudflare-blocked locally, so this is another good example of why the archive has to separate taxonomy confirmation from the real evidence chain. The authoritative packet here is the filed SEC set plus the recoverable company-distributed quarter materials.

## Open questions

- How much of MPLX's next growth leg depends on sustained basin throughput growth in the Permian and Marcellus rather than simply capital recycling within an already-strong cash machine?
- Does the heavier tilt toward natural-gas and NGL buildout make the business more durable because it serves multiple value-chain choke points, or more exposed to execution risk because it concentrates capital in long-lived processing, fractionation, and export-connected projects?
- How much longer can MPLX combine large-scale growth capital, `12.5%` annual distribution increases, and leverage discipline before one of those priorities has to give?
