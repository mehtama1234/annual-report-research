## What changed

- ONEOK is a stronger next midstream packet than another pure natural-gas transport name because it adds a broader integrated system built across natural gas, NGLs, refined products, and crude. The company is explicitly framed as a leading midstream operator with gathering, processing, fractionation, transportation, storage, and marine export services.
- The `2025` annual chain shows a company that is materially more integrated than a narrow toll-road packet. Management repeatedly highlighted the approximately `60,000-mile` pipeline network, the combination of legacy ONEOK assets with `Magellan`, and the way acquisitions such as `EnLink` and interests such as `Medallion` are feeding segment growth and optimization opportunities.
- The quarter chain showed a clean progression through the required window. Adjusted EBITDA ran at `$2.145B` in `Q4 2025`, `$1.997B` in `Q1 2026`, and `$2.121B` in `Q2 2026`, while guidance moved from a midpoint of `$8.1B` for `2026` at the annual close to `$8.25B` after `Q1` and `$8.35B` after `Q2`.
- The annual-report archive split is important here. AnnualReports still showed `2024` as the most recent archive package in the saved capture, while ONEOK's official IR annual-reports page already exposed a `2025` annual-report route dated `2026-04-01`. The local Issuu capture was only a stub, so the filed `10-K` is the authoritative annual content source.

## Open questions

- How much of the recent upside is durable volume and integration strength versus unusually favorable optimization and marketing activity tied to `Waha`-to-`Katy` differentials and other short-cycle market dislocations?
- As ONEOK gets larger and more integrated through `Magellan`, `EnLink`, `Medallion`, and associated projects, does the system become more resilient or more operationally complex in ways that make execution risk more important than simple throughput growth?
- Can ONEOK keep translating acquisition-led scale into durable fee-based earnings growth once integration tailwinds fade, or does it still need a strong commodity and basis environment to maximize the value of the network?
