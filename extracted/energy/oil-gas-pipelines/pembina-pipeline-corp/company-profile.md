## What changed

- Pembina adds a distinct Canadian midstream packet built around an integrated chain: gas gathering and processing, liquids transportation, fractionation, storage and logistics, and export-linked infrastructure.
- The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows a company that is not just collecting tolls on one corridor. Its economic logic depends on linking upstream volumes to Peace Pipeline transportation, Redwater fractionation, Heartland extraction, industrial-feedstock customers, and West Coast export optionality.
- That makes the packet useful for the bigger-picture lens around transport bottlenecks, NGL value-chain integration, capital intensity, and why processing and fractionation capacity can matter as much as long-haul crude takeaway.
- AnnualReports lagged at `2024`, but Pembina's official IR and SEC chain was clean enough to support a stronger annual-plus-quarter packet than several other names in the lane.

## Open questions

- How much of Pembina's value creation comes from owning the integrated NGL and facilities stack rather than from being a simple pipeline operator?
- If Western Canadian volumes keep rising, do fractionation and extraction constraints become the real bottleneck rather than pipe takeaway alone?
- Does the combination of industrial-feedstock projects, Cedar LNG adjacency, and West Coast export ambitions make Pembina one of the more strategically important non-U.S. infrastructure packets in this archive?
