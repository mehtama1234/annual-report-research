## What changed

- TC Energy adds a different pipeline packet than both Enbridge and the crude-corridor names. After the `October 1, 2024` spinoff of `South Bow`, the company is now a cleaner natural-gas transmission, storage, Mexico corridor, and power-solutions platform.
- The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows a company that is no longer using liquids-pipeline breadth as part of the main story. Instead, it is leaning into North American gas demand, LNG linkage, in-corridor gas expansions, and power-generation exposure through `Bruce Power` and `Power and Energy Solutions`.
- The packet is especially useful because it gives the branch a major gas-backbone system whose economics are tied to delivery records, LNG facility flows, contracted expansion, and large in-corridor projects rather than to a broader utility-distribution mix like Enbridge.
- AnnualReports did not resolve cleanly for this name, which makes TC Energy another useful example of why the archive has to prioritize official company materials and SEC filings over aggregator completeness.

## Open questions

- Does the cleaner post-`South Bow` portfolio make TC Energy more durable because it is more focused, or does it also remove a layer of diversification that once cushioned the gas and power story?
- How much upside can TC Energy capture from LNG-linked U.S. gas demand and Appalachian and Southeast expansion without taking on a meaningfully different regulatory and project-execution risk profile?
- If North American power demand, data-center load, and gas-fired generation keep rising together, does TC Energy become one of the clearest backbone beneficiaries even without a large local-distribution utility footprint?
