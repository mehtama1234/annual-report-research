## Reconciliation notes

- The authoritative latest-three-quarter window is:
  - `Q2 2026`
  - `Q1 2026`
  - `Q4 2025`
- AnnualReports is used for taxonomy and archive confirmation only.
- The official Cenovus investor page provides a clean `2025` annual report plus the exact target-quarter chain, so it is the primary reporting authority for the packet.
- The SEC annual `40-F`, annual `MD&A`, annual financial statements, and quarter `6-K` wrappers provide the filing backbone and confirm chronology.
- Cenovus adds a distinct branch packet because it combines:
  - oil-sands-heavy upstream production
  - Canadian refining
  - U.S. refining
  - heavy-barrel differential exposure
  - condensate and market-access dependence
  - acquisition-driven scale from `MEG`
  - a reshaped downstream profile after the `WRB` divestiture
