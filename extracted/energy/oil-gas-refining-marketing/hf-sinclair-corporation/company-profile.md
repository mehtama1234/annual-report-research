## What changed

- HF Sinclair adds a much more mixed downstream packet than `Valero`, `Marathon Petroleum`, or `Phillips 66`. It is still a refiner, but the earnings system also depends on renewables, branded fuel marketing, a meaningful midstream network, and a higher-value lubricants and specialty-products platform that exports globally.
- The annual materials make the lane more legible because they show why this is not just another spread-driven refiner. The annual report maps six refining locations, three renewable diesel units, branded-station distribution growth, integrated midstream assets, and a lubricants-and-specialties business that produces across North America and the Netherlands and exports to more than `80` countries.
- The quarter chain is strategically important because `Q2 2026` did not just show stronger refining margins. It also introduced a formal plan to separate `Lubricants & Specialties` through the capital markets and retire the Mississauga base-oil refining assets over `2027`, which effectively reframes HF Sinclair as a company deciding whether its higher-value specialty downstream assets belong outside the main refining complex.
- The direct HF Sinclair IR pages were mostly not locally capturable in this environment because Cloudflare returned challenge wrappers. The packet therefore treats the official IR pages as live-verified and uses the direct company-hosted q4cdn report PDFs plus the SEC filing chain as the authoritative local source set.

## Open questions

- Is HF Sinclair best understood as a refiner with helpful adjacent businesses, or as a diversified downstream system whose higher-value lubricants business is valuable enough to deserve its own market identity?
- How much of the `Q2 2026` surge reflects durable inland and West-coast tightness versus a cyclical high point in crack spreads and inventory-related benefits?
- After a potential `Lubricants & Specialties` separation, does the remaining company become a cleaner cash-return refiner, or a more cyclical and lower-multiple downstream shell with less business-mix cushioning?
