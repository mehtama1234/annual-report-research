## Reconciliation notes

- AnnualReports is useful for confirming the company and its taxonomy, but it still showed `2024` as the most recent annual report as of `Monday, August 10, 2026`.
- Imperial's official IR site was accessible and provided clean direct downloads of the `2025` annual report and the `Q4 2025`, `Q1 2026`, and `Q2 2026` quarter materials.
- The authoritative latest-three-quarter window is:
  - `Q2 2026`
  - `Q1 2026`
  - `Q4 2025`
- The hard filing backbone is the SEC `10-K`, `10-Q`, `8-K`, and submissions chronology.
- Imperial adds a distinct refining-and-marketing packet because it combines:
  - Kearl oil-sands mining exposure
  - Cold Lake in-situ production
  - Syncrude synthetic-crude participation
  - three Canadian refineries
  - nationwide fuel distribution and branded wholesale
  - petrochemicals
  - Strathcona renewable diesel
