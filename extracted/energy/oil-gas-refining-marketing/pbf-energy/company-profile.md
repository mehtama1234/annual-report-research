## What changed

- PBF adds the merchant-refiner version of the downstream lane. It is much less cushioned than `Phillips 66` or `HF Sinclair`, and less scale-stabilized than `Marathon Petroleum` or `Valero`. The key drivers here are crack spreads, refinery uptime, turnaround timing, insurance recoveries, and balance-sheet repair when the cycle turns.
- The `2025` annual materials are useful because they capture the company in a damaged but still strategically positioned state. Management described PBF as one of the largest independent petroleum refiners and suppliers of unbranded transportation fuels and emphasized a highly complex, predominantly coastal refining system just as the Martinez refinery fire was reshaping results and repair priorities.
- The quarter chain matters because it shows a real bridge from disruption to recovery. `Q4 2025` still leaned on insurance support and restart timing, `Q1 2026` was still negative on an adjusted operating basis, and `Q2 2026` finally showed what the system can earn once Martinez is back and refining conditions improve.
- The direct PBF investor-relations pages were not locally usable in this environment because Cloudflare returned challenge wrappers. The packet therefore uses live-verified IR pages for route confirmation, the direct annual-report PDF for the annual layer, and the SEC `10-K`, `10-Q`, `8-K`, and earnings-release exhibits as the authoritative local evidence base.
