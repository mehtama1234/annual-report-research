## What changed

- Suncor adds a more complete upstream-downstream integration case than the branch's pure refiners or upstream names. It combines oil sands mining and in situ production, upgrading, offshore E&P, Canadian and U.S. refining, trading, and the Petro-Canada retail and wholesale network.
- The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows a company that turns oil-sands capital intensity into downstream margin capture and retail-fuel distribution rather than relying on commodity exposure alone.
- That makes the packet valuable for the bigger-picture lens around upstream versus downstream cycles, Canadian energy security, refinery-margin capture, and how integrated systems can cushion some commodity volatility while still depending on operational reliability.
- AnnualReports was not usable for Suncor in this environment, and Suncor's official investor PDFs were Cloudflare-blocked to scripted download. The packet is therefore intentionally anchored in live-verified official URLs plus the local SEC `40-F` and `6-K` chain.

## Open questions

- How durable is Suncor's current operating outperformance if upstream realizations soften while downstream margins normalize?
- Does the integrated model primarily reduce volatility, or does it also create a higher execution bar because oil sands, upgrading, refining, trading, and retail all need to run well at once?
- As Canadian export, refining, and retail systems stay strategically important, does Suncor become one of the clearest energy-security packets in the archive rather than just another large hydrocarbon producer?
