## Reconciliation notes

- AnnualReports did not produce a usable Suncor archive page in this environment, so the packet does not rely on it for company taxonomy or annual-report retrieval.
- Suncor's official financial-reports and events pages were live-verifiable, but direct local downloads from the company host were Cloudflare-blocked in this shell.
- The authoritative latest-three-quarter window is:
  - `Q2 2026`
  - `Q1 2026`
  - `Q4 2025`
- The hard local filing backbone is the SEC `40-F` and `6-K` chain.
- The annual and quarter operating metrics used in the packet come from:
  - the live-verified official Suncor annual and quarter report URLs
  - the official Suncor annual-close and quarter result materials as surfaced through browser-accessible official PDFs
  - the local SEC annual and quarter filing chronology
- Suncor adds a distinct refining-and-marketing packet because it combines:
  - oil sands mining and in situ output
  - upgrading into synthetic crude oil
  - offshore and conventional E&P
  - refining in Canada and the U.S.
  - supply and trading
  - Petro-Canada retail and wholesale distribution
