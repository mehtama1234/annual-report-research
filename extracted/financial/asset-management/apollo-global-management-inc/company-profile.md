## What changed

- Apollo gives the workspace a second alternative-manager model, but it is meaningfully different from Blackstone. The core earnings stack here is fee-related earnings plus spread-related earnings through Athene, supported by origination, retirement services, insurance liabilities, and fee-generating AUM growth.
- AnnualReports already showed Apollo’s `2025 Annual Report and Form 10K` on `2026-08-08`, which makes Apollo one of the cleaner financial names for annual-report collection even though the site still used legacy `LLC` naming.
- Full-year `2025` and `4Q25` materials showed record origination activity of more than `$300B`, inflows of more than `$225B`, quarter fee-related earnings of `$690M`, quarter spread-related earnings of `$865M`, full-year fee-related earnings of `$2.5B`, full-year spread-related earnings of `$3.4B`, full-year adjusted net income of about `$5.2B`, year-end total AUM of about `$938B`, and fee-generating AUM of about `$709B`.
- `1Q26` pushed the model through the `$1T` threshold. Search and filing-linked release evidence show record fee-related earnings of `$728M`, spread-related earnings of `$719M`, adjusted net income of about `$1.21B` or `$1.94` per share, total AUM of about `$1.03T`, fee-generating AUM of about `$836B`, and record inflows of about `$115B`.
- `2Q26` extended the same pattern. Current result summaries show record fee-related earnings of `$785M`, spread-related earnings of `$877M`, adjusted net income of about `$1.31B` or `$2.11` per share, total AUM of about `$1.05T`, fee-generating AUM of about `$858B`, quarterly inflows of about `$60B`, and last-twelve-month inflows of about `$298B`.
- The strategic point is that Apollo is not only a fundraising and realization story. It is also a large-scale credit origination and insurance-spread business. That makes it a different financial-system layer from Blackstone’s more realization- and perpetual-capital-centered private-markets platform.
- As of `2026-08-08`, Apollo’s SEC submissions feed and IR financial-results page showed no `2Q26` Form `10-Q`, so the most recent quarter in this archive relies on the official earnings release, transcript, supplement, and `8-K`.

## Open questions

- How durable is Apollo’s spread-related earnings engine if credit spreads, rates, or retirement-services flows change?
- Does Apollo’s insurance and origination model deserve to be treated as a different financial sub-model from realization-led alternatives managers?
- Can Apollo keep compounding fee-generating AUM and retirement assets without taking on a weaker credit mix or more balance-sheet complexity?
