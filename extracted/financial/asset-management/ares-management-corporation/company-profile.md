## What changed

- Ares gives the archive a fourth alternative-manager evidence set and makes the internal spread among `Asset Management` companies much clearer. This model is more visibly fundraising-led, management-fee-led, and dry-powder-led than Blackstone, Apollo, or KKR.
- AnnualReports was current here. On `2026-08-08`, the Ares company page already showed the `2025` annual report and Form `10-K`, so the aggregator did not lag the issuer on the annual package.
- Full-year `2025` materials showed AUM of about `$622.5B`, FPAUM of about `$384.9B`, available capital of about `$156.0B`, quarterly gross fundraising of about `$35.9B`, annual gross fundraising of about `$113.2B`, annual deployment of about `$145.8B`, fee related earnings of about `$1.78B`, and realized income of about `$1.85B`.
- `1Q26` extended that same pattern with AUM of about `$644.3B`, FPAUM of about `$399.6B`, available capital of about `$158.1B`, gross fundraising of about `$29.5B`, fee related earnings of about `$464.4M`, and realized income of about `$502.7M`.
- `2Q26` pushed the same fee-and-fundraising engine further. The official release and presentation showed AUM of about `$671.3B`, FPAUM of about `$409.9B`, available capital of about `$170.0B`, gross fundraising of about `$36.4B`, fee related earnings of about `$491.1M`, realized income of about `$521.5M`, and after-tax realized income per share of about `$1.29`.
- Compared with Blackstone, Ares reads less realization-timing dependent. Compared with Apollo, it reads less like an insurance-funded spread engine. Compared with KKR, it reads less insurance-economics and monetization heavy. The distinct Ares signature is scale fundraising, fee-paying AUM growth, large dry powder, and broad origination across credit, real assets, infrastructure, secondaries, and private equity.

## Open questions

- How durable is Ares's current fundraising pace if private-markets distributions stay uneven?
- Does the large dry-powder base create a stronger multiyear fee runway than peers, or does it mainly signal later deployment pressure?
- How much of Ares's next earnings step-up is likely to come from management-fee growth versus realizations or performance revenues?
