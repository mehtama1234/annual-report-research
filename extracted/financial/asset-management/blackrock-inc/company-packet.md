## Annual report takeaways

- BlackRock is the most important missing public-markets asset manager in the archive. It turns the asset-management sample from a useful set of sub-models into a more complete platform stack that includes active pressure, hybrid recovery, indexed scale, and dominant global distribution plus technology.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with AUM of `$14.0T`, record net inflows of `$698B`, total revenue of `$24.216B`, revenue used for operating margin measurement of `$21.756B`, adjusted operating margin of `44.1%`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$48.09`.
- The mix matters. Technology services and subscription revenue reached `$1.981B`, which makes BlackRock materially more than a fee-on-AUM story.
- The private-markets and data buildout also matters. HPS added `$165B` of client AUM and `$118B` of fee-paying AUM in `July 2025`, while Preqin added a private-markets data and workflow layer in `March 2025`.
- The annual materials make the current earnings logic unusually clear: BlackRock is monetizing market beta, ETF scale, private markets, outsourcing, technology, and data inside one operating platform.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: Q2 2026

- Results: revenue used for operating margin measurement of `$6.352B`, adjusted operating margin of `45.9%`, adjusted net income attributable to BlackRock of `$2.291B`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$13.91`.
- Flow and AUM signal: AUM reached `$15.3T`, and BlackRock reported record first-half net inflows of `$321B`, including `$192B` in the second quarter.
- Strategic read: this is the cleanest local evidence that the best public asset managers now look like broad financial platforms, not only investment products.

### Quarter minus 1: Q1 2026

- Results: revenue used for operating margin measurement of `$5.993B`, adjusted operating margin of `44.5%`, adjusted net income attributable to BlackRock of `$2.068B`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$12.53`.
- Flow and AUM signal: BlackRock reported `$130B` of quarterly total net inflows and ended the quarter with AUM of `$13.895T`.
- Structural read: the flow engine was broad, with ETFs, private markets, and technology all contributing to a stronger quarter than a plain ETF read would imply.

### Quarter minus 2: Q4 2025

- Results: revenue used for operating margin measurement of `$6.332B`, adjusted operating margin of `45.0%`, adjusted net income attributable to BlackRock of `$2.176B`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$13.16`.
- Flow and AUM signal: BlackRock closed fiscal `2025` with `$14.0T` of AUM, record full-year net inflows of `$698B`, and `$342B` of fourth-quarter net inflows.
- Strategic read: the quarter shows how ETF scale, systematic active, private markets, outsourcing, and acquisition-led capability expansion can all reinforce each other inside one manager.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: BlackRock is the clearest local example of asset management converging with data, technology, model delivery, private markets, and whole-portfolio infrastructure.
- Investor behavior: client migration is not only about cheap beta. The BlackRock evidence shows demand spanning ETFs, systematic active, private credit, outsourcing, and workflow tools.
- Competition: this is the strongest local evidence for a platform-scale winner separating from both narrower active managers and narrower indexed managers.
- Culture and business model: the business reflects a world where clients increasingly buy implementation, analytics, and portfolio plumbing together with asset exposure.
- Watch item: this strength still depends partly on high market levels and successful integration of HPS and Preqin, so not all of the current margin profile should be read as cycle-proof.
