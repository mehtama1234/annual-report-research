## What changed

- BlackRock adds the dominant platform-scale public asset manager to the Financial archive and materially strengthens the asset-management sample.
- Unlike several peers, the AnnualReports page already exposed the `2025` annual report on `2026-08-09`.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with AUM of `$14.0T`, record net inflows of `$698B`, total revenue of `$24.216B`, revenue used for operating margin measurement of `$21.756B`, adjusted operating margin of `44.1%`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$48.09`.
- The annual materials also show the model broadening beyond classic ETF scale: technology services and subscription revenue reached `$1.981B`, HPS added `$165B` of client AUM and `$118B` of fee-paying AUM in `July 2025`, and the Preqin acquisition expanded private-markets data and workflow infrastructure.
- `Q4 2025` showed `$14.0T` of AUM, `$342B` of fourth-quarter net inflows, revenue used for operating margin measurement of `$6.332B`, adjusted operating margin of `45.0%`, adjusted net income attributable to BlackRock of `$2.176B`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$13.16`.
- `Q1 2026` produced `$130B` of quarterly total net inflows, AUM of `$13.895T`, revenue used for operating margin measurement of `$5.993B`, adjusted operating margin of `44.5%`, adjusted net income attributable to BlackRock of `$2.068B`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$12.53`.
- `Q2 2026` produced AUM of `$15.3T`, `$192B` of second-quarter net inflows, revenue used for operating margin measurement of `$6.352B`, adjusted operating margin of `45.9%`, adjusted net income attributable to BlackRock of `$2.291B`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$13.91`.

## Open questions

- How much of BlackRock’s current strength is durable platform expansion versus favorable market levels?
- Do HPS and Preqin deepen the moat enough to keep BlackRock separating from other public asset managers on both flows and margin?
- Does the mix shift toward technology, private markets, and whole-portfolio solutions reduce fee-pressure risk, or mainly offset it while market beta stays supportive?
