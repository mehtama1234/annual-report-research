## What changed

- Brookfield gives CLI 6 a capital-infrastructure model rather than a plain spread, brokerage, or property vehicle. The packet shows value capture coming from fundraising reach, wealth and retirement channels, insurance-linked capital, monetizations, and operating-business cash flow.
- The saved evidence is lighter on local IR artifacts than some other packets, but the controlling filing chain is clear. Brookfield is a foreign issuer here, so the authoritative annual-and-quarter record runs through the 2025 Form 40-F and the in-scope 6-K sequence.
- The quarter chain matters because it shows the same structural logic repeating across late 2025 and early 2026: Brookfield keeps turning institutional demand, insurance growth, and complex transaction capability into a larger capital base.
- Compared with KKR, Brookfield reads as more operating-asset-heavy and more globally diversified. Compared with the insurance brokers, it is more balance-sheet-intensive but also more directly exposed to infrastructure, retirement, and private-capital scale.
