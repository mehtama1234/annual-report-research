## What changed

- Franklin Resources adds a second non-alternative asset-management expression to the Financial archive and shows a more hybrid public-markets plus alternatives model than T. Rowe Price.
- The AnnualReports page still lagged at `2024` on `2026-08-08`, but official Franklin investor-relations pages already exposed the `2025` annual report and full current-quarter reporting set.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with AUM of `$1.661T`, long-term net outflows of `-$97.4B`, operating revenues of `$8.771B`, and an effective investment-management fee rate of `40.5` basis points versus `41.1` basis points in fiscal `2024`.
- Capital return stayed material in the annual materials, with dividends of `$683.7M` and share repurchases of `$240.3M`, even while the business was still absorbing product-mix pressure and Western Asset Management outflows.
- `Q1 2026` showed an important turn in flow quality: operating revenues were `$2.327B`, net income attributable to Franklin Resources was `$255.5M`, diluted EPS was `$0.46`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$0.70`, and total AUM reached `$1.684T` with `+$28.0B` of long-term net inflows.
- `Q2 2026` kept that recovery alive. Total operating revenues were `$2.295B`, net income was `$268.2M`, diluted EPS was `$0.49`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$0.71`, total AUM was `$1.682T`, and long-term net inflows were `+$16.9B`.
- `Q3 2026` made the strongest strategic point so far: total AUM rose to `$1.792T`, average AUM reached `$1.750T`, long-term net inflows were `+$18.4B`, alternatives AUM hit a record `$294.2B`, and Franklin announced a corporate name change to Franklin Templeton, Inc. effective `2026-08-17`.

## Open questions

- Is Franklin now on a durable positive-flow path, or are recent inflows still too dependent on market levels and a handful of stronger channels?
- Can alternatives, private markets, ETFs, SMAs, and Canvas offset continued Western Asset pressure and broader legacy active-fund fee compression?
- How much of the current margin recovery is structural operating improvement versus favorable mix and higher market-driven AUM?
