## What changed

- Invesco adds a third public-markets asset-management pattern to the Financial archive and is the strongest ETF-and-indexing-heavy manager in the current set.
- The AnnualReports page still lagged at `2024` on `2026-08-09`, but official Invesco investor-relations pages already exposed the `2025` annual report and current earnings set.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with AUM of `$2.170T`, average AUM of `$2.000T`, total net flows of `$104.4B`, operating revenues of `$6.377B`, adjusted operating margin of `33.4%`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$2.03`.
- The annual materials also make the business mix very explicit: ETFs and Index represented `$630.2B` of AUM and QQQ alone represented `$407.2B`, while private markets AUM reached `$130.7B` and China JV AUM reached `$132.5B`.
- `Q4 2025` showed ending AUM growth, more than `$19B` of net long-term inflows, operating revenues of `$1.692B`, adjusted operating margin of `36.4%`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$0.62`, but GAAP earnings were distorted by a `$1.8B` non-cash intangible asset impairment.
- `Q1 2026` produced operating revenues of `$1.745B`, adjusted operating margin of `34.5%`, adjusted net income attributable to Invesco of `$260.8M`, adjusted diluted EPS of `$0.57`, and `$21.8B` of net long-term inflows, with ETFs, China JV, fixed income, and multi-asset offset by QQQ and equity outflows.
- `Q2 2026` produced operating revenues of `$1.826B`, adjusted operating margin of `37.5%`, adjusted net income attributable to Invesco of `$322.3M`, adjusted diluted EPS of `$0.71`, and `$45.1B` of record net long-term inflows across ETFs and Index, QQQ, China JV, and private markets.

## Open questions

- How durable is Invesco’s current momentum if ETF and QQQ tailwinds soften?
- Can Invesco keep broadening beyond QQQ concentration into fixed income, private markets, and China JV without giving back fee rate?
- Does the model remain too dependent on market-sensitive indexed exposures, or is the broader capability mix now strong enough to support steadier flows?
