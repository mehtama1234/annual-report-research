## What changed

- KKR gives the archive a third alternative-manager evidence set, but again not the same model as Blackstone or Apollo. The KKR stack is more visibly balanced across fee-related earnings, monetization, insurance economics tied to Global Atlantic, private wealth, asset-based finance, and scaled deployment.
- AnnualReports lagged the issuer here. On `2026-08-08`, the AnnualReports company page still showed `2024` as the most recent annual report, so the `2025` annual package had to come from KKR IR and the SEC.
- Full-year `2025` materials showed record annual figures across fee-related earnings, adjusted net income per share, capital raised, and capital invested. The official `4Q25` release showed quarter FRE of about `$1.0B`, full-year FRE of about `$3.7B`, quarter TOE of about `$1.3B`, full-year TOE of about `$5.0B`, quarter ANI of about `$1.0B`, full-year ANI of about `$4.4B`, year-end AUM of about `$744B`, FPAUM of about `$604B`, capital raised of about `$129B`, capital invested of about `$95B`, and uncalled commitments of about `$118B`.
- `1Q26` extended that same pattern with fee-related earnings above `$1.0B`, TOE of about `$1.3B`, ANI of about `$1.25B`, AUM of about `$758B`, FPAUM of about `$615B`, capital raised of about `$28B`, capital invested of about `$22B`, and uncalled commitments of about `$125B`.
- The `1Q26` call adds the strategic read. Management said total insurance economics over the last twelve months were about `$1.9B`, asset-based finance represented more than `$90B` of AUM, K-Series wealth AUM was above `$38B`, and monetization revenue in the quarter was about `$880M`.
- `2Q26` pushed the fee-and-monetization engine further. The official release showed FRE of about `$1.2B`, TOE of about `$1.5B`, ANI of about `$1.5B`, AUM of about `$796B`, FPAUM of about `$638B`, capital raised of about `$34B` in the quarter and about `$133B` in the last twelve months, capital invested of about `$24B` in the quarter and about `$104B` in the last twelve months, and uncalled commitments of about `$143B`.
- Compared with Blackstone, KKR reads less like a pure realizations-plus-perpetual-capital story. Compared with Apollo, it reads less like a spread-engine story. The distinctive KKR signature is diversified fee growth plus monetization plus insurance-related economics plus continuing deployment into credit, infrastructure, and wealth channels.

## Open questions

- How much of KKR's current earnings power is structurally recurring fee growth versus a favorable monetization window?
- Does Global Atlantic create a durable insurance-economics advantage closer to Apollo, or is it better thought of as one important support layer inside a broader KKR fee-and-deployment model?
- Can KKR keep scaling private wealth, asset-based finance, and insurance-related vehicles without diluting underwriting discipline or fee quality?
