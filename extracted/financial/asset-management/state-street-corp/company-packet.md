# State Street Corp. Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-08

## Snapshot

- Company: State Street Corp.
- Sector: Financial
- Industry: Asset Management
- Ticker: STT
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: 2Q26
  - quarter 2: 1Q26
  - quarter 3: 4Q25

## Annual report takeaways

- State Street broadens the `Asset Management` label inside AnnualReports into a custody-and-asset-servicing model that is operationally different from T. Rowe Price.
- The `2025` annual report shows record total revenue excluding notable items of about `$14.0 billion`, record fee revenue of about `$11.0 billion`, and positive operating leverage of roughly `280` basis points.
- The business model depends on institutional scale: assets under custody and administration, assets under management, asset servicing fees, management fees, FX trading services, software and processing, securities finance, and net interest income.
- The annual read is less about retail fund flows and more about global financial plumbing for institutional clients, especially fund complexes, asset owners, asset managers, and large pools of collateral.
- This is one of the clearest names in the repo for understanding how large financial firms monetize hidden operating infrastructure rather than only explicit consumer or lending demand.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: 2Q26

- Results: EPS of `$3.15`; EPS excluding notable items of `$3.65`; total revenue of roughly `$4.0 billion`.
- Growth signal: fee revenue rose `13%` and total revenue rose `17%`.
- Scale signal: quarter-end AUC/A reached about `$50.7 trillion`; AUM reached about `$5.1 trillion`, both records.
- Interpretation: State Street benefits when markets rise, client assets grow, and institutional servicing activity remains high, but the model is also supported by breadth across servicing, FX, financing, and software-style processing revenue.

### Quarter minus 1: 1Q26

- Results: EPS of `$2.31`; EPS excluding notable items of `$2.66`; total revenue of roughly `$3.8 billion`; fee revenue of roughly `$3.0 billion`.
- Operating message: the quarter still showed strong positive operating leverage and record quarter-end AUC/A and AUM.
- Structural readthrough: the company is not only a passive beneficiary of markets; it is monetizing the operational burden of global asset ownership and fund administration.

### Quarter minus 2: 4Q25

- Results: EPS of `$2.42`; EPS excluding notable items of `$2.97`; total revenue up `7%`; fee revenue up `4%`.
- Year-end message: full-year `2025` closed with record total revenue ex notable items, record fee revenue, and positive operating leverage of `487` basis points in the quarter.
- Capital tone: State Street kept emphasizing share repurchases, dividends, and disciplined expense execution alongside business growth.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: State Street adds custody, administration, and institutional servicing to the financial archive.
- Infrastructure: the company is another clean read on the hidden systems layer of finance, but unlike CME it monetizes asset servicing rather than trading and clearing volume.
- Savings chain: State Street sits beside T. Rowe Price in the investment ecosystem but earns money from a different point in the stack.
- Technology: software and processing fees, servicing automation, and institutional workflow scale matter materially to the model.
- Competition: fee pressure exists here too, but the moat is operational scale, regulation, switching friction, client trust, and embedded servicing relationships.
