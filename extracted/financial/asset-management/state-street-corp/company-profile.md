## What changed

- State Street adds a sixth distinct Financial-sector model to the archive: custody, asset servicing, and asset-management-at-scale rather than deposit-heavy retail banking, card spend, insurance underwriting, active asset management, or exchange infrastructure.
- AnnualReports metadata and official IR materials confirm that State Street sits in `Financial / Asset Management`, but the actual business mix in the filings is much more about custody, fund administration, FX, securities finance, financing, and institutional servicing than a pure T. Rowe Price-style active manager read.
- The `2025` annual report shows record total revenue excluding notable items of approximately `$14.0 billion`, record fee revenue of about `$11.0 billion`, positive operating leverage of roughly `280` basis points, and record quarter-end assets under custody and administration plus assets under management.
- Management frames the model around scale institutional servicing: asset servicing fees, management fees, FX trading services, software and processing, securities finance, net interest income, and a large balance-sheet role in collateral and liquidity intermediation.
- `4Q25` reported EPS of `$2.42` and EPS excluding notable items of `$2.97`, total revenue up `7%`, fee revenue up `4%`, and positive operating leverage of `487` basis points, while the year ended with record fee revenue and strong capital return.
- `1Q26` reported EPS of `$2.31` and EPS excluding notable items of `$2.66`, total revenue of roughly `$3.8 billion`, fee revenue of roughly `$3.0 billion`, and record quarter-end AUC/A and AUM.
- `2Q26` reported EPS of `$3.15` and EPS excluding notable items of `$3.65`, total revenue of roughly `$4.0 billion`, fee revenue up `13%`, total revenue up `17%`, and record quarter-end AUC/A of about `$50.7 trillion` and AUM of about `$5.1 trillion`.
