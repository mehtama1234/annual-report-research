## What changed

- Carlyle gives the archive a fifth alternative-manager evidence set and helps separate a more classic fee-plus-carry-plus-fundraising model from Blackstone, Apollo, KKR, and Ares.
- AnnualReports lagged the issuer here. On `2026-08-08`, the AnnualReports company page still showed `2024` as the most recent annual report, so the `2025` annual package had to come from the SEC and the IR quarterly-results page.
- Full-year `2025` materials showed DE of about `$1.7B`, FRE of about `$1.2B`, realized net performance revenues of about `$357M`, net accrued performance revenues of about `$2.9B`, total AUM of about `$477B`, fee-earning AUM of about `$337B`, perpetual capital fee-earning AUM of about `$111B`, available capital of about `$88B`, inflows of about `$53.7B`, and deployment of about `$54.5B`.
- `1Q26` showed DE of about `$327M`, FRE of about `$300M`, realized net performance revenues of about `$21M`, total AUM of about `$475B`, fee-earning AUM of about `$333B`, available capital of about `$96B`, inflows of about `$13.0B`, and deployment of about `$10.0B`, but GAAP net income attributable to common stockholders swung to a loss of about `$132M`.
- `2Q26` showed DE of about `$472M`, FRE of about `$358M`, realized net performance revenues of about `$115M`, total AUM of about `$485B`, fee-earning AUM of about `$334B`, perpetual capital fee-earning AUM of about `$113B`, available capital of about `$97B`, inflows of about `$16.8B`, and deployment of about `$14.3B`.
- Compared with Blackstone, Carlyle currently reads smaller and less perpetual-capital dominant. Compared with Apollo and KKR, it reads less insurance-linked. Compared with Ares, it reads somewhat more carry- and realization-sensitive. The distinct Carlyle signature is diversified alternatives exposure across Global Private Equity, Global Credit, and AlpInvest with a stronger dependence on fundraising, fee growth, and carry conversion than on insurance or spread economics.

## Open questions

- Is Carlyle's recent step-up in FRE durable enough to hold if realizations slow again?
- Can Global Credit and AlpInvest keep offsetting a more cyclical private-equity carry cadence?
- Does Carlyle deserve to be valued more like a steadier fee platform, or is the business still too performance-revenue sensitive for that?
