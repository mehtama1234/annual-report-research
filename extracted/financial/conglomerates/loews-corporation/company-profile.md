## What changed

- Capital-allocation role: Loews is more exposed to subsidiary-level swings than Berkshire, but it still behaves like a patient-capital system rather than like a single-sector operating company.
- Mix dependence: CNA, Boardwalk, Loews Hotels, and Altium Packaging create a more varied but also more visibly uneven earnings pattern from quarter to quarter.
- Resilience type: the resilience here is not smoothness. It is the ability to absorb volatility across unlike businesses without losing the holding-company logic.
- CLI 6 role: Loews helps widen the conglomerate set beyond Berkshire and shows that smaller-scale diversified allocators still matter in the archive.
- Comparative value: it is a useful midpoint between pure insurers, pure property vehicles, and giant permanent-capital systems.
