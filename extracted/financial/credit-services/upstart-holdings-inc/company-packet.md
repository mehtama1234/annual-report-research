# Upstart Holdings, Inc. Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: Upstart Holdings, Inc.
- Sector: Financial
- Industry: Credit Services
- Ticker: `UPST`
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window as of 2026-08-10:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- Upstart adds a different `Financial / Credit Services` model from `American Express`, `PayPal`, `Synchrony`, `Ally`, and `Affirm`. The recurring engine is not premium-card spending, not a wallet, not merchant-linked private-label financing, not branchless deposit funding, and not merchant-funded installment checkout. It is an AI-led lending marketplace that connects borrower demand, bank and credit-union funding partners, product-specific underwriting, and loan-funding infrastructure through one software surface.
- AnnualReports still showed only the `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K` on `2026-08-10`, even though the official Upstart investor-relations site already exposed the `2025` annual report. This is another direct reminder that the aggregator is useful for taxonomy but not always for current-year coverage.
- The annual scale is now large enough to matter as a real credit and underwriting system. Fiscal `2025` originations reached about `$11.0B` across `1,497,149` loans, total revenue reached about `$1.0B`, revenue from fees reached about `$950M`, net income reached about `$53.6M`, and adjusted EBITDA reached about `$230M`.
- The operating quality improved too. Fiscal `2025` conversion rate reached `19.4%`, up from `15.1%` in `2024`, while management said the company grew originations `86%` and revenue `64%` in `2025` while growing headcount only `18%`.
- The annual materials and full-year release also make the product lane clearer. Upstart says it connects millions of consumers to more than `100` banks and credit unions, more than `90%` of loans are fully automated, and the platform now spans personal loans, automotive loans, home-equity lines of credit, small-dollar relief loans, and the newer revolving-credit `Cash Line`.
- The annual story also shows why the name matters for the broader archive. Management said auto and home originations each grew about `5x` in `2025`, and that `70%` of funding for auto and home loans originated in `Q4 2025` came from `11` different partners with another `13` signed for the coming year. This is not only a consumer-credit story. It is a distribution, funding, automation, and partner-infrastructure story.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent reported quarter: Q2 2026

- Results: originations reached about `$4.2B`, `558,014` loans were originated, total revenue reached about `$365M`, revenue from fees reached about `$348M`, net income reached about `$16.5M`, contribution profit reached about `$193M`, and adjusted EBITDA reached about `$76.9M`.
- Mix read: unsecured revenue from fees reached about `$326M`, up `38%`, while unsecured contribution margin reached `62%`; secured products remained negative on contribution margin but improved to negative `35%` from negative `176%` a year earlier and negative `96%` in `Q1 2026`.
- Strategic read: management said the company re-accelerated core personal-loan growth, moved secured products rapidly toward profitability, funded growth without adding equity capital, and returned to GAAP profitability with an all-time-high contribution profit.
- Structural read: Q2 is the clearest proof that Upstart is not only an AI-personal-loan wrapper. It is becoming a broader credit-origination and funding infrastructure layer with improving economics across unsecured and secured categories.

### Quarter minus 1: Q1 2026

- Results: originations reached about `$3.4B`, `425,356` loans were originated, total revenue reached about `$308M`, revenue from fees reached about `$277M`, net loss was about `($6.6M)`, contribution profit reached about `$137M`, and adjusted EBITDA reached about `$40.5M`.
- Growth read: originations increased `61%` year over year in dollars and `77%` in loan count, showing that demand and marketplace throughput were still rising even before the stronger Q2 profitability step.
- Product and institutional read: management said the company advanced its AI models, applied for a national bank charter, and grew rapidly in home and auto, which matters because it shows Upstart testing whether it can widen from a partner-distributed unsecured model into a more durable multi-product credit infrastructure role.
- Structural read: Q1 shows the tension in the model clearly. Growth remained strong, but margins were still being pulled by product expansion and secured-category buildout. The bigger signal is that management was still willing to push into deeper infrastructure control rather than only harvest short-term personal-loan volume.

### Quarter minus 2: Q4 2025

- Results: `455,788` loans were originated, total originations reached about `$3.2B`, total revenue reached about `$296M`, revenue from fees reached about `$265M`, net income reached about `$18.6M`, contribution profit reached about `$141M`, and adjusted EBITDA reached about `$63.7M`.
- Annual baseline read: full-year `2025` originations reached about `$11.0B`, revenue reached about `$1.0B`, fee revenue reached about `$950M`, net income reached about `$53.6M`, and adjusted EBITDA reached about `$230M`.
- Funnel read: Q4 conversion rate reached `19.4%`, up from `18.0%` in `Q4 2024`, which is important because it suggests the model’s edge is partly showing up in borrower-funnel efficiency, not only raw advertising or rate sensitivity.
- Funding and product read: management said loans on balance sheet fell `20%` quarter over quarter while auto and home funding diversified materially, making year-end `2025` the clearest proof that the company is trying to widen marketplace throughput without becoming trapped as a pure balance-sheet lender.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Upstart adds an AI lending marketplace and bank-partner credit-infrastructure interface to `Financial`, which is different from premium closed-loop cards, open-loop acceptance networks, merchant-linked point-of-sale finance, branchless specialty-credit funding, or embedded installment checkout.
- Consumer and societal behavior: this packet is a direct read on how normalized algorithmic credit discovery and digital-first borrowing have become, especially for households treating approval speed, online experience, and flexible credit access as part of the product rather than as a separate bank relationship.
- Bank and lender behavior: the more interesting signal is not only borrower demand. It is partner demand from banks and credit unions that want AI-led underwriting, automated origination, and lower-cost digital distribution without building the entire front-end and model stack themselves.
- Business model: the key insight is that Upstart monetizes borrower acquisition, fee-based origination, model-driven approval and pricing, platform automation, funding coordination, and product-surface expansion rather than relying primarily on deposit spread economics.
- Technology and AI: Upstart is one of the clearest cases in the archive where AI is the operating product itself. Approval logic, conversion, automation, pricing, and product expansion are all explicitly tied to the same model layer.
- Watch item: the next useful comparison after Upstart is not another generic bank. It is another specialty-finance, fintech-lending, or partner-distributed credit operator that can test whether this AI-marketplace framing is durable across a tougher funding or credit cycle.
