## Annual report takeaways

- Aon deepens the insurance-broker side of CLI 6 by adding a more globally integrated advisory and risk-services franchise beside Marsh and Gallagher.
- The `2025` full-year read centered on `6%` organic revenue growth, double-digit adjusted EPS growth, and continued execution against Aon's `3x3` operating plan rather than on balance-sheet expansion.
- The annual interpretation is that Aon remains one of the lane's cleanest workflow-and-trust businesses because clients repeatedly pay for risk, health, and human-capital advice without requiring Aon to warehouse the heaviest underwriting or funding exposure itself.
- Compared with Marsh, Aon reads as a more explicitly transformation-driven and analytics-heavy version of the same broad thesis: client complexity keeps getting monetized best by the intermediary that owns the advice layer.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: 2Q26

- Results: Aon reported about `$4.2B` of total revenue, `5%` organic revenue growth, adjusted operating margin of about `28.9%`, adjusted EPS of about `$3.81`, and free cash flow of about `$483M`.
- Operating detail: management kept stressing recurring client demand across commercial risk, reinsurance, health, and talent-related advisory work.
- Strategic read: `2Q26` shows that Aon's growth algorithm still works even when the macro backdrop is noisy, because the business is selling expertise and workflow control rather than raw balance-sheet capacity.

### Quarter minus 1: 1Q26

- Results: Aon reported total revenue of about `$5.034B`, `5%` organic revenue growth, diluted EPS of about `$5.63`, adjusted EPS of about `$6.48`, and adjusted operating margin of about `39.1%`.
- Operating detail: cash provided by operations rose sharply year over year, reinforcing that the model converts advisory scale into strong cash generation.
- Structural read: `1Q26` is a good reminder that the lane's highest-quality names can compound through operating discipline and client stickiness rather than through leverage.

### Quarter minus 2: 4Q25

- Results: Aon reported fourth-quarter revenue of about `$4.3B`, `5%` organic revenue growth, and adjusted diluted EPS of about `$4.42`.
- Full-year readthrough: the year-end release framed `2025` around sustained organic growth, margin discipline, and strong free cash flow rather than around one-time market tailwinds.
- Structural read: `4Q25` anchors Aon as one of the purest examples in CLI 6 of monetizing institutional complexity with light capital intensity.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Consumer and societal pattern: Aon monetizes the institutional need to navigate a more volatile, compliance-heavy, and harder-to-self-insure world.
- Industrial pressure: pricing in insurance markets, labor-intensive expertise, and the need to keep analytics and client-service quality high are the central operating burdens.
- Cultural shift: more corporate clients are willing to pay recurring fees for judgment, model-driven guidance, and cross-border coordination rather than trying to keep those functions fully in-house.
- Bigger-picture read: Aon reinforces the CLI 6 signal that the cleanest economics often accrue to the firm controlling advisory workflow rather than to the one carrying the largest asset stack.
