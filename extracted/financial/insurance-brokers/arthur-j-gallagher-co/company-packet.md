## Annual report takeaways

- Gallagher pushes CLI 6 further into the broker lane by showing how an acquisition-heavy insurance intermediary can still preserve attractive economics through scale, distribution, and advisory density.
- The `2025` full-year read highlighted net earnings of about `$2.052B`, steady organic fee growth, and continued acquisition roll-up discipline rather than a balance-sheet-heavy spread story.
- The annual interpretation is that Gallagher monetizes fragmentation in insurance distribution and claims/risk-management services, making consolidation skill itself part of the moat.
- Compared with Aon and Marsh, Gallagher reads as the most acquisition-driven broker in the group, but it still supports the broader fee-heavy trust-franchise thesis.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: 2Q26

- Results: Gallagher reported about `$3.955B` of total company revenue on a reported basis and about `$3.947B` as adjusted, with adjusted diluted EPS of about `$2.84`.
- Operating detail: total brokerage and risk management adjusted earnings remained strong, and the company kept benefiting from scale and acquisition integration across the platform.
- Strategic read: `2Q26` shows that Gallagher can still turn a roll-up model into strong earnings when the acquired distribution engine keeps compounding.

### Quarter minus 1: 1Q26

- Results: Gallagher reported total revenue of about `$4.293B`, net earnings of about `$913M`, and diluted net earnings per share growth versus the prior year period.
- Operating detail: brokerage remained the economic core of the company, while risk management added another durable fee stream with less balance-sheet intensity than a lender or property owner.
- Structural read: `1Q26` is important because it shows the best-case version of the acquisition-and-distribution model: scale improves both relevance and earnings power without needing the funding stack of a bank or REIT.

### Quarter minus 2: 4Q25

- Results: Gallagher reported fourth-quarter net earnings of about `$317M`, with `7%` organic fee growth in brokerage for the quarter and `6%` for full-year `2025`.
- Full-year readthrough: the year-end release emphasized acquisition integration, fee growth, and solid adjusted margin performance.
- Structural read: `4Q25` anchors Gallagher as a broker whose moat is distribution breadth and serial integration skill, not asset sensitivity.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Consumer and societal pattern: Gallagher monetizes the spread of outsourced risk handling, claims support, and employee-benefit complexity across companies of many sizes.
- Industrial pressure: acquisition integration, compensation expense, competition for talent, and insurance-market pricing still matter, but the model remains structurally attractive because clients keep outsourcing complexity.
- Cultural shift: the stronger brokers increasingly function as embedded operating partners, not just policy-placement agents.
- Bigger-picture read: Gallagher strengthens the CLI 6 conclusion that advisory distribution and workflow control are producing cleaner economics than rent, spread, or carry exposure.
