# Marsh Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: Marsh & McLennan Companies, Inc. (`Marsh`)
- Sector: Financial
- Industry: Insurance Brokers
- Ticker: `MMC`
- Fiscal year-end: December 31
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: `2Q26`
  - quarter 2: `1Q26`
  - quarter 3: `4Q25`

## Annual report takeaways

- Marsh gives CLI 6 the fee-heavy insurance-broker and advisory-distribution model, where economics come from workflow ownership, client trust, and repeated placement of risk rather than from carrying large credit or funding exposure.
- In `2025`, Marsh generated about `$27.0B` of revenue, up `10%` on a GAAP basis, with full-year GAAP EPS of about `$8.43` and adjusted EPS of about `$9.75`.
- The annual read is that Marsh is one of the lane's cleanest quality businesses because it monetizes institutional anxiety, regulation, and complexity through advice, market access, and data rather than through asset-risk warehousing.
- Compared with M&T or Annaly, Marsh shows how much better CLI 6 economics can look when the company owns the workflow without needing the heaviest liability stack.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: 2Q26

- Results: Marsh reported `6%` revenue growth, `5%` underlying revenue growth, second-quarter GAAP EPS of about `$2.63`, and adjusted EPS of about `$2.96`.
- Operating detail: the company said GAAP operating income increased `4%` and adjusted operating income increased `5%`, reinforcing steady demand across risk, reinsurance, consulting, and advisory work.
- Strategic read: `2Q26` shows that even after a brand transition, Marsh is still monetizing institutional complexity and recurring advisory need with relatively low balance-sheet burden.

### Quarter minus 1: 1Q26

- Results: first-quarter revenue was about `$7.6B`, up `8%`, while GAAP EPS was about `$2.36` and adjusted EPS was about `$3.29`.
- Operating detail: operating income fell because of a Greensill litigation charge, but adjusted operating income still rose `8%`, and the company continued buying back stock.
- Structural read: `1Q26` is important because it separates one-off legal noise from the underlying quality of the brokerage and consulting engine.

### Quarter minus 2: 4Q25

- Results: fourth-quarter revenue was about `$6.6B`, up `9%`, with GAAP EPS of about `$1.68` and adjusted EPS of about `$2.12`.
- Full-year readthrough: the year-end release framed `2025` around `10%` revenue growth, `4%` underlying revenue growth, `11%` adjusted operating income growth, and continued margin expansion.
- Structural read: `4Q25` confirms Marsh as the batch's strongest example of trust monetization with much lighter capital intensity than banks, mortgage REITs, or conglomerates.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Consumer and societal pattern: Marsh monetizes the institutional need to outsource judgment, navigate insurance complexity, and keep trusted intermediaries close as risk environments get harder.
- Industrial pressure: litigation, compliance, catastrophe pricing, and labor-intensive expertise all matter, but the company still keeps the best economics in the batch because it controls the advisory layer.
- Cultural shift: corporate customers increasingly pay for confidence, data, and expert navigation rather than trying to self-manage every risk-transfer process.
- Bigger-picture read: Marsh is the best evidence in this batch that a lighter-balance-sheet trust franchise can be more resilient than spread-heavy or duration-heavy models.
