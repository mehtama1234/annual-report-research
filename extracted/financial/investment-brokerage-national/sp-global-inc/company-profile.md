## What changed

- S&P Global adds a seventh distinct Financial-sector model to the archive: ratings, benchmarks, pricing, market intelligence, commodity data, and index economics rather than custody, exchange clearing, active management, insurance, card spend, or bank balance-sheet spread.
- AnnualReports metadata and official materials confirm S&P Global fits `Financial / Investment Brokerage - National`, but the actual business mix in the filings is much closer to an index-and-ratings infrastructure platform.
- The `2025` annual filing shows revenue of `$15.336 billion`, operating income of `$6.478 billion`, net income of `$4.471 billion`, and diluted EPS of `$14.66`.
- The annual read is diversified but still highly concentrated in fee-rich information franchises: Ratings generated about `$4.902 billion` of revenue, Market Intelligence about `$2.299 billion`, Commodity Insights about `$1.747 billion`, and Indices about `$1.839 billion`.
- `4Q25` and full-year `2025` results were also shaped by portfolio transition and Mobility-separation planning. The broader strategy still centers on benchmarks, data, analytics, ratings, and workflow software rather than on taking principal risk.
- `1Q26` reported revenue of `$4.171 billion`, operating income of `$2.002 billion`, net income of `$1.395 billion`, and diluted EPS of `$4.69`.
- `2Q26` reported revenue of `$4.146 billion`, operating income of `$1.812 billion`, net income of `$1.217 billion`, and diluted EPS of `$4.12`; the official IR overview also highlighted `11%` revenue growth, `+200 bps` adjusted operating profit margin, and `23%` adjusted diluted EPS growth.
