# Bank of America Corporation Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-08

## Snapshot

- Company: Bank of America Corporation
- Sector: Financial
- Industry: Money Center Banks
- Ticker: BAC
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: 2Q26
  - quarter 2: 1Q26
  - quarter 3: 4Q25

## Annual report takeaways

- 2025 annual report shows revenue of `$113.1 billion`, net income of `$30.5 billion`, and diluted EPS of `$3.81`, reflecting broad-based strength across the company.
- Average deposits increased `3%` year over year to `$1.98 trillion` for 2025, reaching `$2.02 trillion` at December 31, while average loans and leases increased `7%` to `$1.14 trillion`, reaching `$1.19 trillion` at year-end.
- Management frames the company around `Responsible Growth`, a balanced business model, disciplined expense management, and continued investment in people, brand, and technology.
- The annual report is explicit that technology leadership matters strategically, with AI and digital tools positioned as operating leverage and client-service infrastructure.
- BAC also uses the annual materials to argue that it is well-positioned for `2026`, citing strong credit discipline, a constructive U.S. economic outlook, and expectations for more growth-friendly trade policy.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: 2Q26

- Results: revenue of `$31.6 billion`, net income of `$9.1 billion`, diluted EPS of `$1.21`, and net interest income of `$16.0 billion` on an FTE basis.
- Management message: BAC described the quarter as one of its strongest to date, with every business segment posting double-digit net income growth and strong returns.
- Structural themes: broad-based operating leverage, strong markets and investment-banking activity, continued deposit and loan growth, and disciplined expense control.
- Credit tone: provision for credit losses was `$1.4 billion`, net charge-off ratio improved to `0.47%`, and early- and late-stage card delinquencies improved year over year for a fifth consecutive quarter.

### Quarter minus 1: 1Q26

- Results: revenue of `$30.3 billion`, net income of `$8.6 billion`, diluted EPS of `$1.11`, and net interest income of `$15.7 billion` on a GAAP basis / `$15.9 billion` on an FTE basis.
- Management message: BAC opened 2026 with strong momentum, balanced growth across every business segment, and better-than-expected net interest income.
- Structural themes: diversified fee growth in Markets, wealth and investment banking; operating leverage of `290` basis points; and continued investment offset by productivity, digitization, and AI.
- Credit tone: asset quality was stable to modestly improved, with lower year-over-year net charge-offs and improved card delinquency trends.

### Quarter minus 2: 4Q25

- Results: quarterly net income of `$7.6 billion`, diluted EPS of `$0.98`, and full-year `2025` revenue a little over `$113 billion` with EPS of `$3.81`.
- Management message: management emphasized that 2025 closed with solid growth across revenue, earnings, and returns, plus `330` basis points of fourth-quarter operating leverage.
- Structural themes: full-year operating leverage, continued net interest income improvement, capital return, commercial and consumer resilience, and a constructive `2026` outlook.
- Macro tone: management said tariffs had become more understood, deregulation was supportive, and consumer spending in 2025 grew `5%` to roughly `$4.5 trillion` across BAC payment flows.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: the big-bank model still depends on diversified revenue streams and scale.
- Consumer: BAC repeatedly points to resilient consumer spending, stable balances, improving delinquency trends, and steady checking-account growth.
- Regulatory: filings are now on disk for the 2025 annual plus 1Q26 and 2Q26 quarters.
- Capital allocation: annual and quarterly materials show meaningful capital return, including more than `$30 billion` returned in 2025 and `$8 billion` returned in 2Q26.
- Technology: AI, digital usage, and productivity tooling are not side notes; they are core parts of the efficiency and growth story.
