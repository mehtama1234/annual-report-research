## Annual report takeaways

- 2025 annual report shows total net revenue of $182.447 billion and net income of $57.048 billion.
- Year-end 2025 assets reached $4.4249 trillion and deposits reached $2.5593 trillion.
- Management frames 2025 as another year of records across major lines of business.
- The shareholder letter emphasizes resilience, capital strength, geopolitical risk, commodity-price risk, inflation risk, and continued investment in technology and products.
