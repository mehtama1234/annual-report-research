## Annual report takeaways

- The `2025` annual and year-end earnings materials show net income of `$10.31 billion`, core operating income of `$9.95 billion`, core operating EPS of `$24.79`, and consolidated net premiums written of `$54.8 billion`.
- Chubb ended `2025` with a record P&C combined ratio of `85.7%`, which is one of the clearest markers in the workspace that disciplined underwriting still matters more than simple top-line premium growth.
- Management frames the business through three earnings sources: P&C underwriting income, investment income, and life income. That tri-engine framing is central to understanding the company.
- The annual and call materials stress diversification by geography, product, customer segment, and distribution channel rather than dependence on any one market line.
- Life insurance is not peripheral in this file set. Chubb reported record life segment income of `$1.24 billion`, up `13.1%`, while adjusted net investment income reached `$6.95 billion`, up `9.0%`.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: 2Q26

- Results: net income of `$2.85 billion`, core operating income of `$2.84 billion`, EPS of `$7.30`, and core operating EPS of `$7.26`.
- Underwriting tone: P&C underwriting income was `$1.94 billion` with a combined ratio of `83.8%` and a current accident year combined ratio excluding catastrophe losses of `82.2%`.
- Premium and mix: net premiums written were `$14.7 billion`, up `3.6%`; P&C premiums were up `3.0%`, or `6.3%` excluding large-account and E&S property; life premiums were up `7.5%`.
- Investment and balance sheet: adjusted net investment income reached a record `$1.88 billion`, up `11.4%`; book value per share was `$195.45` and tangible book value per share was `$131.93`.
- Management message: some property and financial lines are softening, but diversification and underwriting discipline are still supporting strong returns.

### Quarter minus 1: 1Q26

- Results: total company net premiums grew `10.7%` to more than `$14 billion`, P&C underwriting income was `$1.8 billion`, and the combined ratio was `84.0%`.
- Underwriting and pricing: the current accident year combined ratio excluding catastrophe losses was `82.1%`, showing continued margin discipline despite early signs of market softening.
- Income mix: adjusted net investment income was `$1.84 billion`, and management highlighted that this landed at the high end of prior guidance.
- Capital and book value: tangible book value per share increased `21.5%`.
- Strategy tone: management highlighted small-commercial opportunity, selective AI use, and continued discipline across global lines rather than a chase for volume.

### Quarter minus 2: 4Q25

- Results: quarter net income of `$3.21 billion`, core operating income of `$2.98 billion`, EPS of `$8.10`, and core operating EPS of `$7.52`.
- Underwriting and premium growth: consolidated net premiums written were `$13.1 billion`, up `8.9%`; record quarterly P&C combined ratio was `81.2%`.
- Full-year readthrough: the quarter closed out a record underwriting year and reinforced the role of reserve strength, catastrophe management, and pricing discipline.
- Portfolio message: management explicitly linked performance to a strong fixed-income portfolio yield of `5.1%` and a very large invested-asset base.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Chubb shows that Financial is also an underwriting, catastrophe, reserve, and investment-float story, not only a banking or card-payments story.
- Capital: earnings depend on both operating execution and the quality and yield of a very large invested-asset base.
- Risk culture: discipline matters. Chubb is a useful counterweight to growth-first interpretations because management repeatedly prioritizes underwriting quality over raw volume.
- Macro: catastrophe activity, competitive cycles, and rates all matter directly to reported results.
- Technology: AI appears in the archive as an operating aid and selective commercial tool, but not as the primary narrative driver; underwriting and investment discipline still dominate the story.
