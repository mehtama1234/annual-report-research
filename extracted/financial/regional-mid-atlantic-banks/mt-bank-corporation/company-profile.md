## What changed

- M&T gives the archive a concentrated relationship-bank case rather than a scale-first national-bank case.
- The economics are still classic bank economics: deposits, spread management, credit quality, capital discipline, and fee support.
- The quarter chain shows why regional-bank quality is never as frictionless as a broker or asset-light intermediary. Even a well-run bank carries more quarter-to-quarter earnings noise.
- The operating read is still constructive: stronger loans, steady capital, and regional relationship depth are doing the work instead of acquisition drama or narrative inflation.
- CLI 6 role: M&T is useful because it shows the cleaner version of traditional relationship banking without pretending that the balance-sheet burden disappeared.
