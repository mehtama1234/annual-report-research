# PNC Financial Services Group Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: The PNC Financial Services Group, Inc.
- Sector: Financial
- Industry: Regional / diversified banking comparison inside CLI 6
- Ticker: `PNC`
- Fiscal year-end: December 31
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: `2Q26`
  - quarter 2: `1Q26`
  - quarter 3: `4Q25`

## Annual report takeaways

- PNC gives CLI 6 a balance-sheet-heavy spread and fee-income franchise whose economics depend on deposits, loan growth, margin discipline, credit quality, regulatory capital, and acquisition integration rather than on fee-only financial intermediation.
- Full-year `2025` closed with net income of `$7.0B` and diluted EPS of `$16.59`, while management framed the year around record revenue, positive operating leverage, stronger net interest income, and balance-sheet growth heading into the FirstBank acquisition close.
- The annual-plus-late-`2025` read is that PNC is trying to convert size, branch density, treasury-management relationships, and middle-market reach into better capital-light fee capture while still carrying the normal bank burden of credit, liquidity, and regulatory capital constraints.
- Compared with the rest of this batch, PNC is the cleanest example of a company whose reported resilience still depends materially on funding mix, rate path, reserve discipline, and the ability to defend returns while carrying a heavily supervised balance sheet.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: 2Q26

- Results: PNC reported net income of `$2.1B`, diluted EPS of `$4.81`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$4.85`.
- Operating detail: management highlighted record revenue, record net interest income, record fee income, average deposits of about `$457.0B`, and average loans up about `$12.3B` versus `1Q26` and about `$40.4B` versus `2Q25`.
- Strategic read: this quarter showed why banks still matter in CLI 6 even when they are not glamorous. Scale banking franchises can still produce large earnings when deposit stickiness, loan growth, and integration timing line up.
- What to watch: how much of the quarter's improvement remains durable once acquisition effects normalize and if credit conditions soften.

### Quarter minus 1: 1Q26

- Results: net income was `$1.8B`, diluted EPS was `$4.13`, and adjusted diluted EPS was `$4.32`.
- Operating detail: management said net interest income increased `6%`, net interest margin reached `2.95%`, average loans grew `7%`, and share repurchases were about `$700M`.
- Structural read: `1Q26` marked the first quarter in which the enlarged platform and the FirstBank close visibly mattered, reinforcing that bank earnings can move quickly when a franchise adds funded assets and deposits at scale.

### Quarter minus 2: 4Q25

- Results: fourth-quarter net income was `$2.0B` and full-year `2025` net income was `$7.0B`.
- Operating detail: management emphasized record revenue, `5%` positive operating leverage, and growth in net interest income, margin, noninterest income, loans, and deposits.
- Structural read: the late-`2025` setup matters because it showed PNC entering `2026` with momentum but also with a larger integration and capital-allocation burden than a simpler organic growth story would imply.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Consumer and business behavior: PNC shows that credit demand and cash-management activity have not collapsed, but customers still reward scale, convenience, and relationship breadth rather than novelty.
- Cultural and societal shift: the bank's role remains foundational but relatively invisible; households and businesses treat dependable liquidity, payments, and credit access as utility-like expectations.
- Industrial pressure: spread income, capital rules, acquisition integration, and credit provisioning remain the real operating burdens.
- Bigger-picture read: PNC is a useful CLI 6 anchor because it demonstrates how balance-sheet scale can still work, but only under tighter regulatory and credit discipline than the other financial structures in this batch face.
