## Annual report takeaways

- Regions gives CLI 6 another Southeast regional-bank case, but with a stronger explicit emphasis on treasury management, wealth management, deposit quality, and hedging discipline than a pure spread-income bank read would suggest.
- The `4Q25` and full-year `2025` results chain showed full-year earnings of about `$2.1B`, diluted EPS of about `$2.30`, adjusted EPS of about `$2.33`, and new annual records in wealth-management and treasury-management income.
- The annual interpretation is that Regions is trying to defend bank quality through franchise discipline: low-cost deposits, fee engines, credit control, and efficient expense management.
- Compared with Truist, Regions reads a bit simpler and somewhat cleaner as a classic Southeast relationship bank, with less diversification but also less strategic sprawl.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: `2Q26`

- Results: Regions reported net income available to common shareholders of about `$549M` and diluted EPS of about `$0.64`; on an adjusted basis, earnings were about `$583M` and diluted EPS was about `$0.68`.
- Operating detail: low-cost interest-bearing deposit costs remained about `1.69%`, CET1 was about `10.7%`, and annualized net charge-offs improved to about `42` basis points.
- Strategic read: `2Q26` shows a bank leaning heavily on funding discipline, hedging, and fee resilience rather than on aggressive balance-sheet expansion.

### Quarter minus 1: `1Q26`

- Results: Regions reported net income available to common shareholders of about `$539M` and diluted EPS of about `$0.62`.
- Operating detail: total revenue increased about `5%` year over year; interest-bearing deposit costs were about `1.72%`, CET1 was about `10.6%`, and credit trends improved modestly.
- Structural read: `1Q26` reinforces that the core bull case is not rapid growth. It is stable relationship-banking economics with enough fee support and balance-sheet control to keep returns in the top tier of its peer set.

### Quarter minus 2: `4Q25`

- Results: Regions reported fourth-quarter earnings of about `$514M` and diluted EPS of about `$0.58`; full-year `2025` earnings were about `$2.1B` and diluted EPS was about `$2.30`.
- Balance-sheet and returns detail: `4Q25` interest-bearing deposit costs were about `1.85%`, CET1 was about `10.8%`, and management highlighted about `18.25%` ROATCE for full-year `2025`.
- Structural read: `4Q25` anchors Regions as a high-discipline funding and fee mix story rather than a simple loan-growth story.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Consumer and societal pattern: Regions monetizes household and commercial demand for dependable transaction banking, treasury management, and wealth services, especially across a large Southeast footprint.
- Industrial pressure: deposit pricing, credit quality, hedging execution, and expense discipline remain the decisive pressures.
- Cultural shift: larger relationship banks still matter when they can feel like service utilities instead of just leveraged balance sheets.
- Bigger-picture read: Regions helps test how much of the regional-bank model can be stabilized through fee mix and funding quality before the underlying rate and credit burden takes over again.
