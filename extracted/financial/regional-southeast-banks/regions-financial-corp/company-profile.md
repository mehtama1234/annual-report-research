## What changed

- Regions is one of the cleaner funding-discipline bank cases in the cluster, with more emphasis on treasury management, wealth, and deposit quality than on sheer balance-sheet growth.
- The core thesis is not explosive expansion. It is stable relationship-banking economics supported by cost control, hedging discipline, and fee income.
- That makes it somewhat cleaner than more sprawling regional-bank stories, but it still carries all the usual category burdens around credit, rates, and capital.
- The quarter chain reinforces that the franchise is trying to look utility-like through stability rather than through transformation theater.
- CLI 6 role: Regions helps the archive test how much stability a regional bank can create through funding quality and fee mix before the underlying balance-sheet burden takes over again.
