# Truist Financial Corp. Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

- Company: Truist Financial Corp.
- Sector: Financial
- Industry: Regional - Southeast Banks
- Ticker: `TFC`

- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: `2Q26`
  - quarter 2: `1Q26`
  - quarter 3: `4Q25`

## Annual report takeaways

- Truist gives CLI 6 another large regional-bank case, but with a more Southeast-heavy footprint and a stronger insurance, wealth, and investment-banking mix than simpler spread-driven lenders.
- The `2025` annual filing showed total revenue of about `$20.3B`, net income available to common shareholders of about `$4.974B`, diluted EPS of about `$3.82`, and net interest income of about `$14.423B`.
- The annual interpretation is that Truist is trying to evolve from a merger-cleanup and restructuring story into a more stable regional financial utility with improving returns, better operating leverage, and diversified fee engines.
- Compared with U.S. Bancorp, Truist still reads somewhat heavier and less cleanly diversified, but it gives the lane a useful read on whether scale plus insurance and wealth can improve classic bank economics.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: `2Q26`

- Results: Truist reported net income available to common shareholders of about `$1.519B` and diluted EPS of about `$1.23`.
- Operating detail: total revenue on a taxable-equivalent basis reached about `$5.311B`, net interest income-TE was about `$3.667B`, and the CET1 ratio improved to about `10.9%`.
- Strategic read: `2Q26` showed real operating improvement, with stronger noninterest income, better efficiency, and enough capital strength to support significant repurchases.

### Quarter minus 1: `1Q26`

- Results: Truist reported net income available to common shareholders of about `$1.377B` and diluted EPS of about `$1.09`.
- Operating detail: total revenue on a taxable-equivalent basis was about `$5.197B`, net interest income-TE was about `$3.644B`, and the CET1 ratio was about `10.8%`.
- Structural read: `1Q26` is useful because it shows the model before the second-quarter acceleration, when fee mix and better expense control were already beginning to improve the readthrough.

### Quarter minus 2: `4Q25`

- Results: the year-end quarter anchored full-year net income available to common shareholders at about `$4.974B`, with diluted EPS of about `$3.82`.
- Full-year readthrough: management framed `2025` around improving profitability, a more manageable expense base, and progress toward stronger ROTCE rather than around pure loan growth.
- Structural read: `4Q25` positions Truist as a bank that is trying to earn its way toward utility-like resilience through mix and discipline, even though it still carries the rate, credit, and regulatory burdens of the category.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Consumer and societal pattern: Truist monetizes household, commercial, and institutional demand for integrated banking, payments, insurance, and wealth services.
- Industrial pressure: deposit costs, credit quality, restructuring discipline, and operating leverage remain the crucial operating pressures.
- Cultural shift: larger regional banks still matter when they can combine relationship banking with fee businesses that make the platform feel broader than a simple lender.
- Bigger-picture read: Truist is a good test of how far a regional bank can move toward service-utility quality before the balance-sheet burden reasserts itself.
