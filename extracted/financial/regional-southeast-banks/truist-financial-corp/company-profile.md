## What changed

- Truist is the more mixed and broader Southeast bank in the cluster, with insurance, wealth, and investment-banking exposure layered on top of classic spread banking.
- That makes the earnings mix better than a simple lender, but it also makes the operating story heavier and less clean than a pure fee intermediary.
- The quarter chain points to real operating improvement through fee mix, efficiency, and capital strength, not just loan growth.
- The business is still trying to become a more stable financial utility after a long merger-cleanup phase.
- CLI 6 role: Truist is the test case for how far a large regional bank can move toward utility-like quality before the balance-sheet and restructuring burden reassert themselves.

## Open questions

- Can Truist keep improving returns through mix and discipline, or does the heavier structure keep it from ever looking truly clean relative to brokers or lighter intermediaries?
- How much of the recent improvement is durable operating progress versus a temporarily better rate and expense backdrop?
