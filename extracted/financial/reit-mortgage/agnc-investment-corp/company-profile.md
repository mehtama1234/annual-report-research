## What changed

- Portfolio and funding role: AGNC is a levered Agency MBS and TBA vehicle, not a traditional property owner. That gives the archive a cleaner read on spread-risk intermediation inside real-estate finance.
- Annual scale: the `2025` annual filing said the investment portfolio totaled about `$94.8B` at year-end, up from about `$73.3B` in `2024`.
- Earnings quality: the quarter chain makes clear that reported outcomes can swing sharply with book-value changes and mark-to-market conditions even while spread income remains positive.
- Q1 to Q2 swing: `Q1 2026` had negative economic return and book-value decline, while `Q2 2026` swung back to positive comprehensive income and book-value growth.
- Leverage and hedge discipline: `at risk` leverage ran roughly `7.2x` at year-end `2025`, `7.4x` at `Q1 2026`, and `7.6x` at `Q2 2026`, which reinforces that this business is a funding-and-risk-management system first.
- Management tone: AGNC framed success around tangible book value, economic return, dividend support, hedge ratios, CPR behavior, and spread income rather than around conventional revenue growth or property expansion.
