## What changed

- AGNC gives the archive a pure mortgage-REIT funding-and-spread machine rather than a traditional property owner or bank.
- The `2025` annual filing said the investment portfolio totaled about `$94.8B` at year-end, up from about `$73.3B` in `2024`.
- The quarter chain shows why this business belongs in the capital-structure lane: spread income can remain positive while book value and comprehensive return swing sharply with market conditions.
- `Q1 2026` showed the downside of the model through book-value pressure and negative economic return, while `Q2 2026` showed how quickly outcomes can reverse when spreads and portfolio marks move favorably.
- Evidence quality is now strong enough for proof-standard use because the annual-plus-quarter SEC chain is preserved locally and the official IR routing is documented locally.
