## What changed

- Taxonomy drift became part of the story: AnnualReports still classifies WaFd as `Savings & Loans`, but the current filings and management commentary describe a business that increasingly wants to look like a western commercial and small-business bank.
- Business-model shift became explicit: management said `2025` involved moving from mortgage lending toward providing solutions for businesses, which is unusual because the strategic change is stated so clearly in the annual and quarter chain.
- Deposit mix improved even while the balance sheet stayed disciplined: transaction deposits grew while time deposits declined through `2025`, and borrowings were materially lower at year-end than a year earlier.
- Earnings quality improved through the latest three quarters: EPS moved from about `$0.72` in `Q4 FY2025` to `$0.79`, `$0.82`, and `$0.84` across the next three quarters, while NIM improved from about `2.71%` in `Q4 FY2025` to `2.81%` by `Q2` and held there in `Q3`.
- Credit and growth tradeoffs stayed visible: `Q1 FY2026` showed higher delinquencies and non-performing assets, `Q2 FY2026` showed cleaner credit metrics and active loan growth, and `Q3 FY2026` showed that the company was still balancing commercial growth with reserve building and close loan monitoring.
- Evidence quality: the packet is now `proven` because the AnnualReports taxonomy page, official IR pages, official annual and quarterly PDFs, SEC annual materials, and all three in-scope quarterly SEC filings are preserved locally in this workspace.

## Open questions

- Does WaFd become a genuinely differentiated business-banking franchise, or does it remain a modest-scale regional lender with thrift heritage and only partial repositioning?
- How durable is the improved earnings path if credit stress rises in commercial real estate or small-business lending?
- Is the most useful comparison for WaFd another thrift analog, a smaller regional bank, or a property-finance lender that shows what deposit-funded spread businesses look like without WaFd's branch-trust base?
