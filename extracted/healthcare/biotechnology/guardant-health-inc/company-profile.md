## What changed

- Revenue: Guardant moved from strong oncology growth into a broader two-engine model, with screening becoming large enough to matter rather than remaining a distant option value.
- Mix: the business is increasingly split across oncology testing, biopharma and data, and screening, which reduces reliance on one test franchise while raising the importance of reimbursement and commercial execution in new channels.
- Guidance: the quarter chain supports a view of accelerating adoption, especially in Shield, with management raising `2026` revenue guidance twice as screening volumes and oncology testing both outperformed.
- Strategic read: Guardant is one of the best healthcare-frontier packets for understanding how a diagnostics company can turn data-rich precision medicine into a recurring commercial platform spanning therapy selection, companion diagnostics, recurrence monitoring, and consumer-facing cancer screening.

## Open questions

- How much of Guardant’s growth can persist once the early step-up from Shield launch, guideline support, and coverage wins matures?
- Does screening become a large durable franchise, or does it remain more reimbursement-sensitive and marketing-intensive than the market expects?
- Can Guardant scale screening and oncology simultaneously without letting operating expense growth outrun gross-margin improvement?
