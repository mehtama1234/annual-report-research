## What changed

- Revenue mix: Ionis is no longer mainly a platform-and-partnership story. By late `2025` and the first half of `2026`, the business is being pulled toward a mixed model of independent commercial revenue, legacy royalty streams, and collaboration-funded pipeline economics.
- Commercial proof: `TRYNGOLZA`, `DAWNZERA`, `WAINUA`, and `SPINRAZA` now make the quarter chain feel much more like a specialty-therapeutics operator than a pre-commercial biotech.
- Guidance behavior: `Q4 2025` framed the shift into a fully integrated commercial-stage company, `Q1 2026` raised expectations, and `Q2 2026` showed that guidance can stay intact even when one high-profile cardiovascular readout disappoints.
- Strategic read: Ionis is one of the strongest packets in the current healthcare set for understanding how biotech monetization changes after scientific validation, when the harder question becomes whether a platform can keep converting orphan and specialty launches into a durable franchise stack.

## Open questions

- Can Ionis keep widening commercial revenue fast enough that the company becomes less dependent on collaboration timing and milestone volatility?
- Does `TRYNGOLZA` become a broad metabolic franchise, or does the story remain narrower and more launch-sensitive than current guidance implies?
- How much of the long-term value is still tied to pipeline optionality and external partners rather than fully controlled internal franchise economics?
