## What changed

- Reporting cutoff: Exact Sciences remained a standalone reporter through full-year `2025`, then the Abbott merger closed on `March 23, 2026`, ending the independent quarter chain before `Q1 2026`.
- Revenue mix: the company entered `2026` as a scaled cancer-testing platform with screening revenue as the main economic engine and precision oncology as the second growth pillar.
- Commercial posture: by late `2025`, the business was not only a `Cologuard` story. `Cologuard Plus`, `Oncodetect`, and `Cancerguard` turned the company into a broader portfolio bet on earlier detection, recurrence monitoring, and lower-friction screening adoption.
- Strategic read: Exact Sciences is one of the strongest frontier-healthcare packets for understanding how diagnostics can behave like a consumer-influenced category where convenience, awareness, reimbursement, and channel access matter as much as laboratory science.
