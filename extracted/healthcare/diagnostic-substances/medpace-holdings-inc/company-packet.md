# Medpace Holdings, Inc. Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: Medpace Holdings, Inc.
- Sector: Healthcare
- Industry: Diagnostic Substances
- Ticker: MEDP
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- Medpace expands the healthcare-frontier archive into outsourced clinical-development infrastructure.
- The `2025` annual report shows why the packet matters: the business is not built on owning a molecule, but on selling trial design, execution, regulatory coordination, and therapeutic expertise across the path from preclinical work to Phase IV follow-through.
- This makes Medpace one of the best healthcare names in the archive for understanding how scientific progress becomes an operating business before it becomes a commercial drug franchise.
- The annual evidence also sharpens an important archive pattern: healthcare value often sits in enabling workflows and service layers whose revenue depends on sponsor budgets, trial pipelines, and backlog conversion rather than on end-market prescription demand.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` revenue was `$707.3 million`, up `17.2%`; net new business awards were `$795.7 million`; net book-to-bill was `1.13x`; EBITDA was `$153.4 million` or `21.7%` of revenue.
- Guidance: the quarter supported the view that Medpace was still replenishing its development pipeline while expanding backlog to about `$3.0 billion`.
- Management message: the quarter emphasized continued demand across major therapeutic areas and healthy backlog conversion despite normal award and timing volatility.
- Structural themes: this is the clearest quarter for seeing Medpace as a picks-and-shovels healthcare name tied to research flow, not to one therapy outcome.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` revenue was `$706.6 million`, up `26.5%`; net new business awards were `$618.4 million`; net book-to-bill was `0.88x`; EBITDA was `$149.4 million` or `21.1%` of revenue.
- Guidance: Q1 still showed strong revenue conversion, but the sub-`1.0x` book-to-bill is a useful reminder that quarter-to-quarter awards can move unevenly.
- Management message: the quarter highlighted continued growth in metabolic, oncology, CNS, and AVAI work, along with the scale of the global platform at about `6,300` employees across `46` countries.
- Structural themes: this quarter is the best read on the tension inside CRO economics between visible current execution and noisier future awards.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` revenue was `$708.5 million`, up `32.0%`; net new business awards were `$736.6 million`; net book-to-bill was `1.04x`; EBITDA margin was `22.6%`.
- Guidance: Q4 carried a strong close to `2025` and confirmed that backlog conversion and new awards were still supporting growth at scale.
- Management message: the quarter underscored Medpace’s identity as a scientifically-driven, full-service CRO with particular strength in oncology, cardiology, metabolic disease, endocrinology, CNS, and anti-viral and anti-infective work.
- Structural themes: Q4 `2025` is the quarter where Medpace most clearly looks like an operating system for therapeutic development rather than a simple outsourced staffing vendor.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Medpace is one of the strongest packets in the current healthcare set for understanding trial infrastructure, sponsor dependence, and the commercialization pipeline before product launch.
- Consumer: there is little direct consumer demand signal here; the relevant read is indirect, through which diseases and therapeutic areas attract enough sponsor capital to sustain development intensity.
- Cultural: Medpace reflects a larger healthcare culture shift toward specialization, outsourced expertise, and globally distributed trial execution rather than fully in-house development.
- Societal: the business sits upstream of treatment access, but it is still shaped by the social burden of major disease areas because oncology, metabolic disease, CNS, and AVAI drive sponsor focus.
- Regulatory: trial design, GCP compliance, protocol execution, and regulatory credibility are central to value capture.
- Technology: Medpace’s edge is less about standalone software and more about high-science workflow management, therapeutic expertise, and integrated execution discipline.
- Industrial: backlog conversion, net new business awards, sponsor funding quality, cancellation risk, employee utilization, and geographic operating reach define the business model more than simple volume measures do.
- Capital allocation: the model can look asset-light relative to manufacturing-heavy healthcare names, but it is still highly sensitive to hiring discipline, study mix, and share repurchase choices.

## Thematic interpretation

- Medpace helps explain a larger healthcare pattern: before therapy monetization and before recurring treatment economics, there is a profitable layer in proving whether therapies work at all.
- The company also shows why healthcare infrastructure should not be reduced to labs, devices, or care sites. The archive also needs trial infrastructure because development throughput shapes the future flow of drugs, biologics, and devices.
- A second repeating pattern is that outsourced expertise becomes more valuable as therapeutic science becomes more complex. Medpace sells specialized execution and regulatory know-how rather than consumer familiarity.
- The final cross-company theme is that frontier healthcare runs on connected infrastructure layers: discovery, trials, diagnostics, delivery, monitoring, and recurring administration. Medpace is the trial-and-validation layer in that chain.
