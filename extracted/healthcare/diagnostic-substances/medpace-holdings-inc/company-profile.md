## What changed

- Revenue: Medpace entered `2026` with strong double-digit growth tied to oncology, metabolic, CNS, and AVAI work rather than to any single marketed therapy.
- Margin: profitability stayed strong, but the business remains exposed to study timing, cancellations, and sponsor behavior because backlog is not a guaranteed predictor of future realized revenue.
- Guidance: the quarter chain supports the view that Medpace was still converting backlog and replenishing awards effectively through mid-`2026`, even with periodic book-to-bill volatility.
- Strategic read: Medpace is a strong frontier-healthcare packet because it monetizes the trial-execution layer that sits between scientific ambition and approved commercial products.

## Open questions

- How durable is Medpace's award pipeline if biotech funding conditions weaken or sponsor prioritization shifts?
- Does a focus on full-service execution and therapeutic specialization create a stronger moat than broader scale-based CRO competition?
- How much valuation and earnings quality should investors ascribe to backlog and book-to-bill when contracts remain cancellable without cause?
