## Annual report takeaways

- Johnson & Johnson is the most useful diversified healthcare platform in the archive so far. It adds a business that is not only a drug portfolio or a payer system, but a combined Innovative Medicine plus MedTech operating model.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with reported sales of `$94.2B`, operational sales growth of `5.3%`, adjusted operational sales growth of `4.2%`, diluted EPS of `$11.03`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$10.79`.
- The segment split matters. Innovative Medicine sales reached `$60.401B`, and MedTech sales reached `$33.792B`.
- The annual report also makes the portfolio point more explicit: full-year adjusted net earnings were `$26.2B`, and growth excluding STELARA was `11.5%`, which suggests the underlying business looked better than the headline patent-loss pressure alone.
- This gives healthcare a much better bridge between science-led drug economics, device and procedure demand, pricing and reimbursement pressure, and broad operating execution.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: Q2 2026

- Results: reported sales of `$25.310B`, net earnings of `$5.534B`, diluted EPS of `$2.27`, and adjusted EPS of `$2.90`.
- Segment signal: Innovative Medicine sales were `$16.384B`, and MedTech sales were `$8.926B`.
- Strategic read: this is a broad healthcare platform still finding growth across both core segments while raising guidance in a policy-heavy environment.

### Quarter minus 1: Q1 2026

- Results: reported sales of `$24.062B`, net earnings of `$5.235B`, diluted EPS of `$2.14`, and adjusted EPS of `$2.70`.
- Segment signal: Innovative Medicine sales were `$15.426B`, and MedTech sales were `$8.636B`.
- Structural read: Johnson & Johnson is useful because the same quarter carries pipeline, launch, and reimbursement exposure on one side and procedure-and-device exposure on the other.

### Quarter minus 2: Q4 2025

- Results: reported sales of `$24.6B`, diluted EPS of `$2.10`, and adjusted EPS of `$2.46`.
- Segment signal: Innovative Medicine sales were `$15.763B`, and MedTech sales were `$8.801B`.
- Strategic read: the quarter shows how Johnson & Johnson can absorb product-specific pressure, including STELARA erosion, without collapsing into a one-product or one-subsector earnings story.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Johnson & Johnson broadens healthcare into a more complete operating-platform model that includes drugs, devices, procedures, pipeline execution, and reimbursement complexity.
- Demand: the business shows that persistent healthcare demand still needs portfolio quality and execution to turn into earnings growth.
- Competition: this is the strongest local evidence that diversified healthcare breadth can matter as much as any single blockbuster or reimbursement line item.
- Culture and business model: the company reflects a healthcare system where trust, innovation, approvals, procedure throughput, and access all matter at once.
- Watch item: policy and pricing pressure remain real, especially as product exclusivity and reimbursement scrutiny keep moving.
