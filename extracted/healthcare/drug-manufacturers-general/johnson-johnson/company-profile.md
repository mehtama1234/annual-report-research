- Johnson & Johnson adds a third healthcare model to the archive and broadens the sector beyond payer and pure pharma logic.
- Official Johnson & Johnson sources confirmed the `2025` annual report on `2026-08-09`, and the local archive now includes the annual report PDF, `10-K`, and in-scope quarterly filing chain.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with reported sales of `$94.2B`, operational sales growth of `5.3%`, adjusted operational sales growth of `4.2%`, diluted EPS of `$11.03`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$10.79`.
- The annual materials and `10-K` show a two-engine healthcare platform: Innovative Medicine sales of `$60.401B` and MedTech sales of `$33.792B`, with the annual report also noting full-year adjusted net earnings of `$26.2B` and `11.5%` growth excluding STELARA.
