## What changed

- Revenue: total revenues reached about `$274.9B` in `2025`, then about `$68.5B` in `Q1 2026` and about `$71.7B` in `Q2 2026`, showing continued scale growth across the platform.
- Earnings quality: shareholders' net income was about `$6.0B` in `2025`, while adjusted income from operations was about `$8.0B`; `Q1 2026` and `Q2 2026` both sustained adjusted income from operations a little above `$2.0B`.
- Segment mix: Evernorth remained the larger revenue engine in `2025`, with adjusted revenues of about `$234.953B`, while Cigna Healthcare remained the core benefits and underwriting engine.
- Medical customers and relationships: total medical customers were about `18.118M` at December 31, 2025, while total customer relationships reached about `188.428M`.
- Capital and efficiency: the debt-to-capitalization ratio improved to about `43.0%` at year-end `2025` and about `42.3%` in `Q1 2026`; the company also repurchased about `11.9M` shares for about `$3.6B` in `2025`.
- Management tone: the company kept emphasizing innovation, technology, data, AI, transparency, and easier navigation rather than only cost containment.
