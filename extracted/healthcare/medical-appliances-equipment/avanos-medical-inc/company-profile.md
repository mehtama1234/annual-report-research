## What changed

- Avanos gives CLI 8 a cleaner non-drug medical-products and medical-equipment packet than several alternative targets because the company has a real `2025` annual cycle on AnnualReports and a coherent SEC chain through the latest reported quarter available by Monday, `2026-08-10`.
- The AnnualReports page and the filed annual materials make the route-to-market explicit: Avanos sells directly to hospitals and other healthcare providers and through third-party wholesale distributors.
- The company also sharpens the healthcare middle layer in a different way from AdaptHealth, Baxter, Henry Schein, Intuitive, and Stryker because it sits between procedure rooms, hospital nutrition workflows, and recurring specialty-product replenishment rather than between patients and payors or between providers and broad-line office procurement.

## Working interpretation

- Avanos materially improves CLI 8 because it combines a hospital-facing product channel with direct provider selling and wholesale-distributor routing, which is exactly the sort of hybrid middle-layer structure the frontier needs more of.
- It shows how life-sustaining enteral feeding, neonatal solutions, pain-management consumables, and procedure-adjacent products create recurring institutional dependence even when the company is not a classic drugs wholesaler or broad-line distributor.
