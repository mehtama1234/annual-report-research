## What changed

- Revenue and earnings quality: net operating revenues reached about `$21.310B` in `2025`, then about `$5.368B` in `Q1 2026` and about `$5.628B` in `Q2 2026`. Net income available to common shareholders rose sharply from about `$702M` in `Q1 2026` to about `$826M` in `Q2 2026`.
- Business mix: Tenet is increasingly a blended hospital-plus-ambulatory system rather than a traditional hospital-only operator. At year-end `2025`, the hospital segment operated `50` hospitals and `132` outpatient facilities, while USPI had interests in `533` ambulatory surgery centers and `26` surgical hospitals.
- Operating model: ambulatory migration is not a side business. It is one of Tenet's main margin and growth engines, alongside higher-acuity hospital services.
- Burden profile: provider economics remained highly sensitive to exchange admissions, payer mix, and Medicaid supplemental timing. `Q1 2026` was pressured by the absence of prior-year favorable Medicaid supplemental benefit, while `Q2 2026` benefited from about `$92M` of favorable pre-tax Medicaid supplemental revenue tied to prior years.
