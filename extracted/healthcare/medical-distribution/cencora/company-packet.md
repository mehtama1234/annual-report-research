# Cencora Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: Cencora, Inc.
- Sector: Healthcare
- Industry: Drug Delivery
- Ticker: COR
- Fiscal year-end: September
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q3 FY2026
  - quarter 2: Q2 FY2026
  - quarter 3: Q1 FY2026

## Annual report takeaways

- Cencora is the clearest pharmaceutical-distribution and access-infrastructure addition to the healthcare archive so far.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with revenue of about `$321.3B`, GAAP diluted EPS of `$7.96`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$16.00`.
- The annual filing and AnnualReports description show the real structure of the business: a global pharmaceutical-solutions organization that links manufacturers, providers, pharmacies, physician practices, and patients through distribution, specialty access, logistics, commercialization, consulting, and related workflow services.
- The operating model is organized mainly through `U.S. Healthcare Solutions` and `International Healthcare Solutions`, which matters because this is not a healthcare earnings story driven by drug intellectual property, insurance risk, or hospital occupancy. It is a scale-and-access story inside the physical and administrative drug-delivery layer.
- Fiscal `2025` also showed the tension inside this model: the company posted large revenue growth and higher adjusted earnings, but GAAP optics were noisier because of the `PharmaLex` goodwill impairment and other portfolio-reset effects.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: Q3 FY2026

- Results: revenue of about `$84.8B`, GAAP diluted EPS of `$3.94`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$4.48`.
- Segment signal: revenue growth came from a `4.9%` increase in `U.S. Healthcare Solutions` and a `5.9%` increase in `International Healthcare Solutions`; `U.S. Healthcare Solutions` revenue was about `$74.9B`.
- Strategic read: the latest quarter shows a distribution platform whose earnings are being lifted by specialty depth, `OneOncology`, and operational execution, while still absorbing lower-margin `GLP-1` mix and customer-specific pressure.

### Quarter minus 1: Q2 FY2026

- Results: revenue of about `$78.4B`, GAAP diluted EPS of `$8.40`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$4.75`.
- Segment signal: revenue growth came from a `2.9%` increase in `U.S. Healthcare Solutions` and a `13.0%` increase in `International Healthcare Solutions`; `International Healthcare Solutions` revenue was about `$7.6B`.
- Structural read: the quarter shows why this business should not be read through revenue alone. Gross-profit quality improved with `OneOncology` and a `LIFO` credit, but management also made clear that higher `GLP-1` sales can pressure gross margins even while helping topline growth.

### Quarter minus 2: Q1 FY2026

- Results: revenue of about `$85.9B`, GAAP diluted EPS of `$2.87`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$4.08`.
- Segment signal: management raised adjusted operating income guidance and reaffirmed adjusted diluted EPS guidance while completing the `OneOncology` acquisition.
- Strategic read: the first quarter clarifies the direction of travel. Cencora is using specialty and services expansion to deepen its role in oncology and physician workflows, not just to move more product through a commodity distribution lane.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Cencora adds the drug-distribution, specialty-access, and manufacturer-services layer that sits behind much of the visible healthcare system.
- Demand: this model is resilient because drug flow, specialty access, and provider supply are mission-critical, but earnings quality still depends on margin mix, specialty intensity, product pricing, and operating discipline.
- Competition: scale, network density, customer embedment, and service breadth matter more here than consumer brand or breakthrough-product novelty.
- Culture and business model: this is a hidden-infrastructure healthcare company. It reflects how much of modern care depends on low-visibility intermediaries that connect manufacturers, pharmacies, physicians, health systems, and patients.
- Watch item: `GLP-1` volume, specialty-product mix, acquisition integration, portfolio rationalization, and litigation or impairment noise can all materially change how healthy the earnings picture looks beneath a still-growing topline.
