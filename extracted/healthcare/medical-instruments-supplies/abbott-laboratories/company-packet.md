# Abbott Laboratories Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-09

## Snapshot

- Company: Abbott Laboratories
- Sector: Healthcare
- Industry: Medical Instruments & Supplies
- Ticker: ABT
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- Abbott is the cleanest device-and-diagnostics expansion of the healthcare archive so far.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with sales of `$44.328B` and adjusted diluted EPS of `$4.72`.
- The annual filing shows the portfolio structure clearly: Medical Devices at `$19.290B`, Diagnostics at `$9.888B`, Nutritional at `$8.820B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals at `$6.330B`.
- This matters because the company adds a healthcare model that is not primarily risk-bearing insurance, pure drug portfolio logic, diversified drug-plus-device breadth, or hospital throughput. It is a product-platform model built on diagnostics, devices, nutrition, and global branded generics.
- The annual segment structure also gives the broader healthcare work a better way to separate procedure-linked device growth, testing and diagnostics demand, nutrition resilience, and emerging-market pharmaceutical exposure.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: Q2 2026

- Results: sales of `$12.593B`, adjusted diluted EPS of `$1.31`, and reported sales growth of `13.0%`, including `10.6%` organic growth.
- Segment signal: Diagnostics sales were about `$3.092B`, and management highlighted more than `12%` sales growth in Medical Devices.
- Strategic read: Abbott’s Q2 2026 story is not only about device strength; it is also about diagnostics broadening through the Exact Sciences acquisition and stronger core-lab execution.

### Quarter minus 1: Q1 2026

- Results: sales of `$11.164B` and adjusted diluted EPS of `$1.09`.
- Segment signal: Medical Devices sales were `$5.539B`, Diagnostics sales were `$2.180B`, Nutritional sales were `$1.964B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals sales were `$1.481B`.
- Structural read: the quarter shows how much of Abbott’s healthcare logic sits in product breadth and channel execution rather than simple one-product dependence.

### Quarter minus 2: Q4 2025

- Results: sales of `$11.459B` and adjusted diluted EPS of `$1.34`.
- Segment signal: Medical Devices sales were `$4.874B`, Diagnostics sales were `$2.768B`, Nutritional sales were `$2.186B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals sales were `$1.631B`.
- Strategic read: Abbott closed `2025` with Medical Devices still as the largest engine, but with enough portfolio breadth to avoid a single-subsector healthcare read.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Abbott adds the device-and-diagnostics layer the healthcare sample was missing.
- Demand: demand in this model depends on installed-base diagnostics, device procedures, innovation cadence, and product mix rather than payer spread or hospital occupancy.
- Competition: platform breadth matters directly. Abbott competes through category reach across diagnostics, devices, nutrition, and established pharmaceuticals rather than one narrow franchise.
- Culture and business model: the company reflects a healthcare model where technology, installed instruments, recurring consumables, and procedure-linked devices all matter.
- Watch item: acquisition support, reimbursement complexity, and category-specific testing or procedure cycles can still swing the earnings narrative quickly.
