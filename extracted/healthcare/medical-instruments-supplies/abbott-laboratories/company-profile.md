## What changed

- Abbott gives CLI 8 a diversified diagnostics-and-devices platform whose channel power sits inside provider systems, installed instruments, recurring consumables, and procedure-driven product demand rather than only in direct distribution.
- The `2025` annual report and `10-K` show a four-segment structure: `Medical Devices`, `Diagnostics`, `Nutritional`, and `Established Pharmaceuticals`.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with sales of `$44.328B` and adjusted diluted `EPS` of `$4.72`, while Medical Devices sales were `$19.290B` and Diagnostics sales were `$9.888B`.
- The same annual materials widen the societal and operating read: Abbott is tied to chronic-disease management, diagnostics throughput, cardiovascular and diabetes-device adoption, nutrition resilience, and emerging-market pharmaceutical demand all at once.
- `Q4 2025` produced sales of `$11.459B`, adjusted diluted `EPS` of `$1.34`, Medical Devices sales of `$4.874B`, Diagnostics sales of `$2.768B`, Nutritional sales of `$2.186B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals sales of `$1.631B`.
- `Q1 2026` produced sales of `$11.164B`, adjusted diluted `EPS` of `$1.09`, Medical Devices sales of `$5.539B`, Diagnostics sales of `$2.180B`, Nutritional sales of `$1.964B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals sales of `$1.481B`.
- `Q2 2026` produced sales of `$12.593B`, adjusted diluted `EPS` of `$1.31`, reported sales growth of `13.0%`, organic growth of `10.6%`, Diagnostics sales of about `$3.092B`, and management commentary highlighting more than `12%` sales growth in Medical Devices and more than `14%` sales growth in Core Laboratory.
