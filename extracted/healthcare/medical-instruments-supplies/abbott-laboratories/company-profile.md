## What changed

- Abbott adds the cleaner device and diagnostics layer that the healthcare archive still needed after adding payer, pharma, diversified platform, and provider models.
- The `2025` annual report and `10-K` show a four-segment structure: Established Pharmaceutical Products, Diagnostic Products, Nutritional Products, and Medical Devices.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with full-year sales of `$44.328B`, adjusted diluted EPS of `$4.72`, and ongoing broad-based growth outside the legacy COVID testing unwind.
- The annual materials also show the internal segment mix clearly: Medical Devices sales were `$19.290B`, Diagnostics sales were `$9.888B`, Nutritional sales were `$8.820B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals sales were `$6.330B`.
- `Q4 2025` produced sales of `$11.459B`, adjusted diluted EPS of `$1.34`, Medical Devices sales of `$4.874B`, Diagnostics sales of `$2.768B`, Nutritional sales of `$2.186B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals sales of `$1.631B`.
- `Q1 2026` produced sales of `$11.164B`, adjusted diluted EPS of `$1.09`, Medical Devices sales of `$5.539B`, Diagnostics sales of `$2.180B`, Nutritional sales of `$1.964B`, and Established Pharmaceuticals sales of `$1.481B`.
- `Q2 2026` produced sales of `$12.593B`, adjusted diluted EPS of `$1.31`, and second-quarter reported sales growth of `13.0%`, including `10.6%` organic growth.
- `Q2 2026` also clarifies the diagnostics story: Total Diagnostics sales were about `$3.092B`, the quarter included Exact Sciences after the March 2026 close, and management highlighted more than `14%` sales growth in Core Laboratory and more than `12%` sales growth in Medical Devices.

## Open questions

- How much of Abbott's current diagnostics acceleration is durable underlying demand versus acquisition support from Exact Sciences?
- Does Medical Devices remain the main growth engine, or does diagnostics become a more important earnings driver over the next few quarters?
- How much pricing and reimbursement pressure remains muted by mix, innovation, and geographic breadth rather than actually absent?
