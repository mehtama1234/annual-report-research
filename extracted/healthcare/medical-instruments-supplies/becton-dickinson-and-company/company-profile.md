## What changed

- BD gives CLI 8 a broader institutional healthcare workflow operator than the current office-based distribution, hospital-supply, home-medical, or pure medtech packets.
- The annual report and quarter chain show a company that sits inside medication delivery, specimen management, medication management, patient monitoring, pharmaceutical delivery systems, and interventional procedure workflow rather than only one narrow device category.
- The Waters transaction matters analytically because it turns the `2025` annual report into a before-state and the `2026` quarter chain into an after-state for the current operating structure.

## Working interpretation

- BD materially improves CLI 8 because it widens the healthcare middle layer into hospital, laboratory, and pharmaceutical workflow infrastructure at very large scale.
- It is not a pure wholesaler, but it still belongs in this frontier because it monetizes institutional dependence, embedded workflow, recurring consumables, and service-critical care processes rather than direct consumer pull.
