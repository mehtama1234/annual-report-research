# Boston Scientific Corporation Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-09

## Snapshot

- Company: Boston Scientific Corporation
- Sector: Healthcare
- Industry: Medical Instruments & Supplies
- Ticker: BSX
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- Boston Scientific is the cleanest pure-play medtech addition to the healthcare archive so far.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with net sales of `$20.074B`, GAAP EPS of `$1.94`, and adjusted EPS of `$3.06`.
- The annual and quarter trail make the operating split clear: MedSurg remains a meaningful growth engine, but Cardiovascular is both larger and strategically central.
- This matters because the company adds a healthcare model built much more directly on procedure demand, physician adoption, device innovation, and category leadership than on insurance spread, hospital reimbursement, diversified nutrition-and-diagnostics breadth, or scientific-tools workflow.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: Q2 2026

- Results: net sales of `$5.442B`, GAAP EPS of `$0.61`, and adjusted EPS of `$0.86`.
- Segment signal: MedSurg grew `5.9%` reported and Cardiovascular grew `8.3%` reported; in absolute terms, MedSurg sales were `$1.818B` and Cardiovascular sales were `$3.624B`.
- Strategic read: Boston Scientific is still compounding on category leadership, but the quarter also shows management widening the platform through the `$1.5B` MiRus investment and continued electrophysiology and structural-heart product work.

### Quarter minus 1: Q1 2026

- Results: net sales of `$5.203B`, GAAP EPS of `$0.90`, and adjusted EPS of `$0.80`.
- Segment signal: MedSurg grew `7.8%` reported and Cardiovascular grew `13.5%` reported.
- Structural read: the quarter reinforces that this is a procedure-led and adoption-led medtech model, with `WATCHMAN` and `FARAPULSE` serving as good examples of how clinical evidence and physician uptake feed the earnings story.

### Quarter minus 2: Q4 2025

- Results: net sales of `$5.286B`, GAAP EPS of `$0.45`, and adjusted EPS of `$0.80`.
- Full-year signal: full-year `2025` net sales reached `$20.074B` and full-year adjusted EPS reached `$3.06`.
- Strategic read: Boston Scientific closed `2025` with strong MedSurg growth, even stronger Cardiovascular growth, and an unusually explicit category-leadership framing around procedure innovation and product cadence.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Boston Scientific adds the missing pure-play medtech and cardiovascular-procedure layer to Healthcare.
- Demand: demand in this model depends on procedure volume, clinical data, device innovation, physician adoption, and category share rather than payer pricing or hospital occupancy alone.
- Competition: category leadership matters directly. The company is competing through specific device platforms and clinical adoption curves, not just broad portfolio breadth.
- Culture and business model: Boston Scientific reflects a healthcare model where innovation cycles, procedural embedment, and device-platform leadership are first-order earnings drivers.
- Watch item: this is a stronger growth story than some of the other healthcare packets, but it is also more dependent on sustaining product momentum in specific categories such as electrophysiology, left atrial appendage closure, and structural heart.
