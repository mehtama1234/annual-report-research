## What changed

- Revenue: DexCom entered `2026` with durable double-digit growth, supported by core intensive-diabetes demand and widening use among broader Type 2 and non-insulin populations.
- Margin: gross margin and operating margin expanded materially from late `2025` through the first half of `2026`, which matters because the company is scaling not just units but a recurring sensing platform with better mix and operating leverage.
- Guidance: management introduced constructive `2026` guidance off Q4 `2025`, raised profitability guidance in Q1, and raised the midpoint of revenue guidance plus margin targets again in Q2.
- Strategic read: DexCom is a strong frontier-healthcare packet because it turns glucose monitoring into a recurring wearable, software, reimbursement, and behavior-shaping platform rather than a one-time device sale.

## Open questions

- How much of DexCom's next wave comes from deeper penetration of insulin users versus broader uptake among less acute Type 2 and prediabetes populations?
- Does the company become more consumer-platform-like as Stelo expands, or does reimbursement and clinician workflow remain the real control point?
- How durable are current margins if DexCom keeps pushing into broader-access and more consumer-like channels?
