## What changed

- Revenue: Insulet entered `2026` with strong double-digit growth driven by recurring Omnipod volume, international expansion, and broader automated insulin delivery adoption.
- Margin: the model continued to show operating leverage, but `2026` also surfaced the operational fragility of high-volume device manufacturing through voluntary medical device corrections and configuration-transition costs.
- Guidance: management introduced strong `2026` growth and margin expectations off Q4 `2025`, then raised the outlook again in Q2.
- Strategic read: Insulet is a strong frontier-healthcare packet because it turns insulin delivery into a recurring consumables and platform relationship rather than a simple hardware placement.

## Open questions

- How much of Insulet's next phase comes from deeper Type 1 penetration versus broader Type 2 adoption and new international markets?
- Does the pharmacy-channel and low-upfront-cost model widen the moat, or does it mainly trade pricing power for faster adoption?
- Can Insulet keep scaling consumables and international demand without repeated manufacturing-quality disruptions?
