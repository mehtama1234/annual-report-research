## What changed

- Intuitive adds the robotic-surgery, installed-base, and recurring-instruments-and-services layer that the healthcare archive still lacked after Abbott, Boston Scientific, and Stryker.
- Fiscal `2025` revenue was approximately `$10.12B`, up `21%` from `2024`, and the annual report repeatedly frames the business around procedures, placements, utilization, and platform expansion rather than simple device sales.
- `Q4 2025` revenue was `$2.87B`, with worldwide procedures up approximately `18%`, including da Vinci procedure growth of approximately `17%` and Ion procedure growth of approximately `44%`.
- `Q1 2026` revenue rose to `$2.77B`, with instruments and accessories revenue up `23%`, da Vinci placements rising to `431`, and the installed base continuing to compound.
- `Q2 2026` appears to keep the same pattern intact, with revenue reaching `$2.89B` and the company still compounding through procedure growth, system placements, leasing, service, and installed-base expansion.
- This makes Intuitive different from Boston Scientific and Stryker: it is less about one procedure category or one implant family and more about a robotic-platform model where recurring economics follow a growing installed base and rising utilization.
- The archive quality is materially stronger now that the official `2025` annual report artifact is saved locally instead of only URL-verified.

## Open questions

- How durable is the current growth rate once da Vinci 5 adoption moves past the early upgrade wave?
- Does the robotic-surgery model have more pricing and utilization resilience than other medtech models, or is it simply benefiting from a strong current placement cycle?
- How much of the next phase of growth depends on platform breadth beyond da Vinci, especially Ion and adjacent digital workflow tools?
