# Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-09

## Snapshot

- Company: Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc.
- Sector: Healthcare
- Industry: Medical Instruments & Supplies
- Ticker: TMO
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- Thermo Fisher is the cleanest life-science-tools and serving-science addition to the healthcare archive so far.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with revenue of `$44.56B`, GAAP diluted EPS of `$17.74`, adjusted EPS of `$22.87`, and free cash flow of `$6.34B`.
- The annual report gives the operating structure clearly: Life Sciences Solutions, Analytical Instruments, Specialty Diagnostics, and Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services.
- This matters because Thermo Fisher adds a healthcare-adjacent but still healthcare-classified model built on research tools, diagnostics, lab workflow, biopharma services, and recurring consumables rather than on insurance risk, hospital throughput, or a narrower product franchise.
- The annual narrative also broadens the strategic frame through Solventum filtration and separation, the Sanofi sterile-fill-finish site, the pending Clario expansion, and explicit AI deployment work.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: Q2 2026

- Results: revenue of `$11.99B`, GAAP diluted EPS of `$4.68`, adjusted EPS of `$6.03`, and adjusted operating margin of `22.8%`.
- Growth signal: revenue growth was `10%`, including `5%` organic growth.
- Strategic read: this is a serving-science platform where acquisition support, end-market breadth, and recurring workflow relevance matter as much as any one product cycle.

### Quarter minus 1: Q1 2026

- Results: revenue of `$11.01B`, GAAP diluted EPS of `$4.43`, adjusted EPS of `$5.44`, and adjusted operating margin of `21.8%`.
- Growth signal: revenue growth was `6%`, including `1%` organic growth.
- Structural read: the quarter shows a slower organic pace than Q2, which is useful because it keeps the life-science-tools read from collapsing into a simple one-direction growth story.

### Quarter minus 2: Q4 2025

- Results: revenue of `$12.21B`, GAAP diluted EPS of `$5.21`, adjusted EPS of `$6.57`, and adjusted operating margin of `23.6%`.
- Full-year signal: full-year `2025` revenue reached `$44.56B`, and full-year adjusted EPS reached `$22.87`.
- Strategic read: Thermo Fisher closed `2025` with strong scale, but the more important point is that the earnings model sits across tools, diagnostics, services, and consumables rather than one healthcare sub-vertical.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Thermo Fisher adds the life-science-tools and serving-science layer the healthcare sample was still missing.
- Demand: demand in this model depends on research intensity, pharma and biotech workflow, diagnostics activity, production support, and installed-base consumables rather than payer spread or hospital occupancy.
- Competition: breadth and workflow embedding matter directly. Thermo Fisher competes through category coverage, installed tools, recurring consumables, and services across scientific workflows.
- Culture and business model: the company explicitly frames itself as enabling science and uses PPI and AI as operating-system tools rather than decorative technology language.
- Watch item: acquisition support and mix can materially influence the growth narrative, so the cleanest read will need more than one quarter of evidence.
