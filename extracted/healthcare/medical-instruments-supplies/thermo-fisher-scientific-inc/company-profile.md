## What changed

- Thermo Fisher gives CLI 8 a serving-science and laboratory-workflow packet whose channel power sits inside research tools, diagnostics, biopharma services, installed instruments, and recurring consumables rather than literal medical distribution.
- The `2025` annual report positions the company as the world leader in serving science, with more than `120,000` colleagues and `2025` revenue of `$44.56B`.
- Fiscal `2025` ended with revenue of `$44.56B`, GAAP diluted `EPS` of `$17.74`, adjusted `EPS` of `$22.87`, and free cash flow of `$6.34B`.
- The annual report also shows the operating structure clearly: `Life Sciences Solutions`, `Analytical Instruments`, `Specialty Diagnostics`, and `Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services`.
- The portfolio mix shown in the annual materials is useful for CLI 8 because it reveals a business built on broad scientific workflow. `Analytical Instruments` were about `52%` of revenue, `Life Sciences Solutions` were `22%`, `Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services` were `16%`, and `Specialty Diagnostics` were `10%`.
- The annual narrative also adds strategic context: in `2025`, Thermo Fisher completed the Filtration and Separation acquisition from Solventum, acquired Sanofi's sterile fill-finish site in New Jersey, announced the Clario acquisition, and highlighted a strategic collaboration with OpenAI to expand `AI` use in operations and products.
- `Q4 2025` produced revenue of `$12.21B`, GAAP diluted `EPS` of `$5.21`, adjusted `EPS` of `$6.57`, and adjusted operating margin of `23.6%`.
- `Q1 2026` produced revenue of `$11.01B`, GAAP diluted `EPS` of `$4.43`, adjusted `EPS` of `$5.44`, and adjusted operating margin of `21.8%`.
- `Q2 2026` produced revenue of `$11.99B`, GAAP diluted `EPS` of `$4.68`, adjusted `EPS` of `$6.03`, and adjusted operating margin of `22.8%`.
