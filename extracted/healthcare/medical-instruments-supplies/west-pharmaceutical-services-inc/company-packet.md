## Annual report takeaways

- West is one of the clearest physical-infrastructure packets in the healthcare archive.
- The annual evidence shows why the frontier-healthcare layer is not just drugs and diagnostics. Modern therapies also depend on containment, delivery systems, high-value components, manufacturing transfer, and sterile administration hardware.
- This makes West particularly useful for reading obesity, biologics, and injectable-treatment demand without taking direct molecule risk.
- The `2025` annual read also sharpens a broader pattern: in healthcare, some of the highest-quality recurring demand sits in the physical interfaces that make therapy usable and scalable.
- West therefore improves both the drug-delivery and drug-related-products lanes, even though the source taxonomy places it under `Medical Instruments & Supplies`.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` net sales were `$872.3 million`, up `13.8%`; diluted EPS was `$2.15`; adjusted diluted EPS was `$2.37`.
- Guidance: the quarter supported the view that West is participating in a durable injectable-demand wave rather than a one-quarter anomaly.
- Management message: management highlighted continued high-value product demand and strong execution across the delivery and packaging stack.
- Structural themes: this quarter is a strong read on the physical layer behind biologics and injectable therapy expansion.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` net sales were `$844.9 million`, up `21.0%`; diluted EPS was `$1.92`; adjusted diluted EPS was `$2.13`.
- Guidance: Q1 came with raised full-year revenue and EPS guidance.
- Management message: management framed the quarter as a strong start, with demand extending beyond simple volume and into more complex, higher-value components.
- Structural themes: this is the cleanest quarter for seeing West as a quality mix and qualification business rather than a packaging commodity business.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: full-year `2025` net sales reached about `$3.07 billion`; management described Q4 as strong and introduced `2026` guidance from that base.
- Guidance: the year-end setup already pointed to a constructive 2026 before the company validated it in later quarters.
- Management message: the `Q4 2025` framing emphasized strong HVP Components demand and product mix quality.
- Structural themes: Q4 `2025` is the bridge quarter where West most clearly looks like an enabling infrastructure beneficiary of therapy trends rather than a passive supplier.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: West is one of the best packets in the current set for understanding healthcare’s physical layer.
- Consumer: the relevant end-user effect is patient reliance on injectable and biologic therapies that require safe, convenient, and scalable delivery systems.
- Cultural: the normalization of obesity drugs, biologics, and long-duration injectable treatment expands demand for specialized delivery infrastructure.
- Regulatory: sterile manufacturing standards, validation requirements, customer qualification cycles, and quality reliability are central to value capture.
- Labor: manufacturing quality, process control, and specialized engineering remain crucial because failure at the component layer can disrupt much larger therapy economics.
- Technology: West’s advantage is embedded in containment, packaging, and delivery architecture rather than in consumer-facing or software narratives.
- Capital allocation: the model is attractive because it can ride therapy growth without taking direct molecule-development risk, though it remains exposed to qualification cycles and end-market concentration.
