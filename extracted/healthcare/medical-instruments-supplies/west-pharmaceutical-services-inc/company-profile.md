## What changed

- Revenue: West entered `2026` with strong double-digit growth supported by high-value product demand, especially in HVP Components and other injectable-solution infrastructure.
- Margin: EPS growth outpaced revenue growth, which matters because this is not a commodity packaging business; mix, manufacturing quality, and product complexity can produce real operating leverage.
- Guidance: the company used both Q4 `2025` and Q1 `2026` to guide the market toward stronger 2026 expectations, then validated that view again in Q2.
- Strategic read: West is the most direct packet in the current set for understanding the physical layer behind biologics, obesity therapies, injectables, and device-linked administration.

## Open questions

- How durable is current injectable and HVP demand if obesity and other high-volume therapy booms normalize?
- How much pricing power comes from technical complexity and qualification burden versus cyclical end-market strength?
- Does West remain primarily a components supplier, or is it increasingly a strategic infrastructure partner for high-value therapy delivery?
