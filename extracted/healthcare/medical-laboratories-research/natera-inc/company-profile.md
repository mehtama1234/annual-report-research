## What changed

- Revenue: Natera entered `2026` with sustained high-growth momentum across women’s health, oncology, and organ health, with oncology producing the sharpest incremental acceleration.
- Mix: the company’s revenue quality is being pushed upward by a rising contribution from higher-ASP oncology testing, especially `Signatera`, while the core consumer and provider funnel still depends heavily on prenatal and carrier screening.
- Guidance: management raised full-year `2026` revenue outlook twice, from `$2.62-$2.70 billion` in February to `$2.74-$2.82 billion` in May and then to `$2.85-$2.91 billion` in August.
- Strategic read: Natera is one of the best frontier-healthcare packets for understanding how precision diagnostics become a category-spanning platform that touches consumer screening, oncology recurrence monitoring, transplant surveillance, and payer reimbursement battles at once.

## Open questions

- How durable is current growth if oncology reimbursement or ASP improvement slows?
- Can consumer-facing women’s-health testing keep scaling without greater payer pushback or saturation?
- Does Natera’s clinical-trial and evidence-spending create a durable moat, or mainly defend current reimbursement and adoption?
