## What changed

- Revenue: Quest entered `2026` with accelerating volume-led growth rather than pricing-led growth, supported by health-system collaborations, payer and employer channel breadth, consumer-initiated testing, and advanced-diagnostics uptake.
- Mix: the quarter chain shows a recurring tension between declining revenue per requisition and rising requisition volume, which is a clean read on routine-testing scale economics and payer-sensitive mix.
- Guidance: management raised `2026` outlook after both Q1 and Q2, moving full-year net-revenue guidance from `$11.70-$11.82 billion` in February to `$11.95-$12.05 billion` in July.
- Strategic read: Quest is one of the best frontier-healthcare packets for understanding diagnostics as a recurring information utility tied to provider workflow, employer screening, consumer wellness behavior, and specialty-test innovation.

## Open questions

- How much of Quest's current growth is durable volume share gain versus temporary contribution from acquisitions and collaboration rollouts?
- Can advanced diagnostics and consumer testing offset persistent reimbursement and routine-test mix pressure?
- Does Project Nova create a genuine service and workflow moat, or is it mainly an execution and cost-efficiency program?
