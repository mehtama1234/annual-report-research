## What changed

- Portfolio shape: BrightSpring entered `2026` after recasting around the planned sale of Community Living, leaving a more focused continuing-operations mix built around Pharmacy Solutions and Provider Services.
- Care model: the company is increasingly framed as an integrated home- and community-based platform rather than a simple home-health operator, with pharmacy, infusion, hospice, rehab, supportive care, and home-based primary-care capabilities tied together.
- Financial profile: the latest quarter chain shows rapid revenue and EBITDA growth, stronger profitability, and material deleveraging as the company scales the continuing platform.
- Strategic read: BrightSpring is one of the strongest healthcare-frontier packets for understanding how recurring care infrastructure can become more platform-like when pharmacy and provider workflows are combined around complex patients in lower-cost settings.

## Open questions

- How much of BrightSpring’s edge comes from genuine platform coordination versus simply operating adjacent service lines under one roof?
- Can the company keep translating scale into better value-based positioning without running into reimbursement or integration drag?
- Does the pharmacy-plus-provider model become more defensible over time as patient complexity rises, or does it remain operationally broad enough to dilute focus?
