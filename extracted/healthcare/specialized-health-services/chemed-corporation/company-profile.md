## What changed

- Operating mix: the healthcare relevance of Chemed is increasingly concentrated in VITAS hospice, while the parent still carries the non-healthcare `Roto-Rooter` segment.
- Care model: the quarter chain shows VITAS returning to more normal growth after the `2025` Medicare Cap issue, with stronger admissions, census, and guidance through `2026`.
- Financial profile: the key near-term swing factor is hospice growth and Medicare Cap exposure, not platform breadth or diversified healthcare coordination.
- Strategic read: Chemed is most useful as a hospice and Medicare-cap comparison packet rather than as a full healthcare-infrastructure flagship on the level of `BrightSpring` or `Option Care`.

## Open questions

- How durable is VITAS growth once the recovery from the `2025` Medicare Cap drag fully normalizes?
- Does the mixed parent structure reduce the usefulness of Chemed as a pure healthcare comparison, or does it still provide a clean enough hospice operating benchmark?
- How much of future value creation depends on admission growth and acuity mix versus continued share repurchases and capital allocation at the parent level?
