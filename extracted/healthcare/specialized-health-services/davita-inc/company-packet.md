# DaVita Inc. Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: DaVita Inc.
- Sector: Healthcare
- Industry: Specialized Health Services
- Ticker: DVA
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- DaVita expands the healthcare-frontier archive into unavoidable recurring treatment infrastructure rather than specialty-drug administration or hospital throughput.
- The `2025` annual record matters because it shows a business built on chronic kidney failure treatment density, integrated kidney care, value-based care arrangements, and reimbursement discipline rather than on one product franchise.
- This makes DaVita one of the best healthcare names in the archive for understanding what happens when recurring care is medically non-optional, highly operational, and deeply entangled with government and commercial payment systems.
- The annual evidence also sharpens a key archive pattern: some of healthcare’s most durable cash flows come from repeated treatment needs, but those cash flows are never simple because they remain exposed to reimbursement, labor, and site-of-care execution.
- DaVita is especially useful because it tests whether Option Care’s recurring-care economics are category-wide. The answer is partly yes, but the underlying drivers are different: dialysis depends on treatment frequency and reimbursement per treatment, not on specialty pharmacy and infusion therapy mix.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` revenue was `$3.554 billion`; operating income was `$579 million`; diluted EPS was `$4.02`; operating cash flow was `$490 million`.
- Guidance: the quarter supported the view that DaVita was maintaining strong cash conversion while managing modest treatment growth and stable but pressured per-treatment economics.
- Management message: the quarter highlighted steady treatment growth, rising operating leverage, and continued scale in integrated kidney care, with about `64,900` patients in risk-based arrangements covering about `$5.8 billion` of annualized medical spend.
- Structural themes: this is the clearest quarter for seeing DaVita as recurring chronic-care infrastructure with meaningful value-based-care optionality layered on top of a mature dialysis base.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` revenue was `$3.416 billion`; operating income was `$482 million`; operating cash flow was `$321 million`; U.S. treatments per day averaged about `91,650`.
- Guidance: Q1 showed modest but improving normalized non-acquired treatment growth, which matters because volume stability is a key support for the whole model.
- Management message: the quarter reinforced the importance of value-based kidney care, integrated care arrangements, and home dialysis promotion alongside core in-center treatment.
- Structural themes: Q1 is the best quarter for seeing how much of DaVita’s economics still depend on treatment density and payer mix even while management tries to broaden the model into care coordination and risk-based arrangements.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` revenue was `$3.620 billion`; full-year revenue was `$13.643 billion`; operating income was `$561 million` for the quarter and `$2.044 billion` for the year; operating cash flow was `$541 million`.
- Guidance: the company exited `2025` with a stable `2026` setup tied to treatment volume, productivity, and care-model evolution rather than blockbuster-cycle uncertainty.
- Management message: management framed the business around core dialysis execution, home-dialysis services, integrated kidney care, and value-based care rather than narrow short-term financial engineering.
- Structural themes: Q4 `2025` is the bridge quarter where DaVita most clearly looks like a recurring-treatment utility rather than simply a provider chain.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: DaVita is one of the strongest packets in the current healthcare set for understanding recurring specialized care that is essential, not elective.
- Consumer: there is limited classic consumer demand here, but patient behavior still matters through treatment adherence, modality choice, home-dialysis adoption, and whether earlier intervention delays disease progression.
- Cultural: the company reflects a broader healthcare shift toward chronic-disease management, care coordination, and moving more treatment support toward home-based and value-based models where clinically appropriate.
- Societal: the business sits directly inside the social burden of chronic kidney disease, aging, diabetes, and cardiovascular disease, which makes it a useful packet for seeing healthcare demand that is demographic and disease-driven rather than discretionary.
- Regulatory: government reimbursement, Medicare Advantage, value-based care models, CKCC participation, and rate updates remain central to value capture.
- Technology: the edge here is not software-led in the consumer sense. It is workflow, treatment-network density, home-dialysis support, and data-rich care coordination.
- Industrial: treatments per day, revenue per treatment, patient care cost per treatment, labor utilization, center density, and integrated-care enrollment matter more than simple headline revenue alone.
- Capital allocation: DaVita is a strong example of a mature but still strategic healthcare infrastructure asset, where buybacks, leverage, and cash generation coexist with ongoing operational and policy risk.

## Thematic interpretation

- DaVita helps explain a larger healthcare pattern: some of the most durable healthcare economics come from repeated treatment that patients cannot defer, but the profit pool is still shaped by reimbursement formulas, operating discipline, and site-of-care design.
- The company also shows why recurring care should not be treated as one category. Option Care and DaVita both monetize repeated treatment outside acute hospitals, but Option Care is tied to specialty-therapy routing while DaVita is tied to chronic disease maintenance and treatment frequency.
- A second repeating pattern is that healthcare value is increasingly earned by reducing friction around long-duration disease management. DaVita participates not only in treatment sessions, but in care coordination, home-dialysis support, and integrated kidney care.
- DaVita adds a useful societal and industrial lens to the batch because it ties healthcare economics directly to chronic disease burden, aging, and the difficult operating tradeoffs inside a labor- and reimbursement-heavy service model.
- The final cross-company theme is that modern healthcare systems depend on layered recurring infrastructure: diagnostics, monitoring, administration, specialty routing, and long-term treatment delivery. DaVita is the clearest packet in the current set for the mandatory chronic-treatment layer.
