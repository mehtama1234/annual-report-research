## What changed

- Revenue: DaVita entered `2026` with steadier, lower-volatility recurring demand than Option Care because dialysis is not discretionary or episodic, but it is still shaped by payor mix and reimbursement per treatment.
- Mix: the model depends on treatment density, commercial-versus-government mix, and cost discipline rather than on a specialty-drug routing story. That makes it a clean counterpoint to home-infusion economics.
- Guidance: the quarter chain supports a view of stable recurring cash generation, even as patient-service revenue per treatment stayed under pressure and treatment growth remained modest.
- Strategic read: DaVita is one of the best frontier-healthcare packets for understanding unavoidable chronic-care infrastructure, where value-based kidney care, home dialysis, and care coordination sit beside a large in-center treatment base.

## Open questions

- How much value can DaVita create through integrated kidney care and value-based arrangements versus mature in-center dialysis density?
- Does modest treatment growth plus reimbursement pressure limit operating leverage more than the market expects?
- How durable is home-dialysis and value-based-care expansion if policy incentives or Medicare Advantage economics change?
