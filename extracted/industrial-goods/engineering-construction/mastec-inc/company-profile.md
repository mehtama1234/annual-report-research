## What changed

- Operating identity: MasTec is best understood as a diversified North American infrastructure engineering and construction platform spanning communications, power delivery, pipeline infrastructure, and clean energy and infrastructure rather than as a single-lane contractor.
- Scale and physical reach: the `2025` annual report says MasTec employed more than `35,000` team members and in `2025` built over `15,000` miles of telecommunications networks, laid more than `1,000` miles of oil and gas pipelines, strung or buried more than `14,000` miles of transmission and distribution lines, and installed more than `3.6` gigawatts of renewable energy.
- Fiscal `2025` base year: annual-report highlights show revenue of `$14.3B`, net income of `$422.0M`, adjusted EBITDA of about `$1.2B`, and 18-month backlog of `$19.0B`.
- Demand backdrop: management described demand across energy, communications, power, and infrastructure as exceptional and repeatedly framed backlog growth as the clearest proof point.
- Segment mix: recent quarter commentary shows the growth engine leaning most heavily toward Clean Energy and Infrastructure, Power Delivery, and Pipeline Infrastructure rather than pure telecom alone.
- Competitive direction: the company is increasingly positioning itself as a comprehensive execution platform for large, complex, multi-year infrastructure work, not just a labor supplier on isolated projects.

## Open questions

- How durable is MasTec’s current backlog conversion if permitting, customer capex timing, or policy support for clean energy become more volatile?
- Can the company sustain margin improvement as it absorbs Superior and keeps ramping labor- and equipment-intensive new work?
- How much of the current growth is tied to structurally durable grid, telecom, and data-center demand versus a concentrated burst of project awards that could normalize later?
