## Reconciliation notes

- AnnualReports confirms EMCOR's placement in the contractor lane, but the aggregator lagged the target annual cycle by still listing `2024` as the most recent hosted annual report on `2026-08-09`.
- The official EMCOR IR stack is stronger than many other names in this archive because the company exposes the annual report PDF, quarter release pages, and quarter earnings decks directly.
- The `2025` annual filing is especially useful because it explicitly ties growth in electrical and mechanical services to digital processing, cloud computing, AI, data centers, reshoring, high-tech manufacturing, and energy-transition buildout.
