# Eaton Corporation plc Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-08

## Snapshot

- Company: Eaton Corporation plc
- Sector: Industrial Goods
- Industry: Industrial Electrical Equipment
- Ticker: ETN
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- Eaton gives the industrial set a direct read on electrification infrastructure, power quality, power distribution, data-center buildout, and aerospace demand. It broadens the industrial archive away from heavy machinery and post-spin automation into electrical infrastructure itself.
- The 2025 annual report is explicit that Eaton sees itself on the right side of several structural growth drivers: electrification, digitalization, reindustrialization, and the expansion of data centers and AI.
- Full-year 2025 sales were a record `$27.448 billion`, organic growth was `8%`, segment margins were a record `24.5%`, and adjusted EPS was a record `$12.07`. The company is entering 2026 from a position of real operating strength rather than simple rebound.
- Eaton is especially useful for theme work because it ties AI to grid, transformer, power-distribution, cooling, and backup-power requirements. This is a direct physical-infrastructure expression of the AI buildout.
- The annual materials also show an aggressive capital-deployment posture: about `$13 billion` of announced acquisitions in 2025 and roughly `$1.5 billion` of North American capacity investment to support sustained demand.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 2026 sales were a record `$8.531 billion`, adjusted EPS was a record `$3.15`, organic growth was `14%`, and segment margins were `23.1%`.
- Guidance: on July 31, 2026 Eaton raised full-year 2026 organic-growth guidance to `11-13%` and adjusted EPS guidance to `$13.40-$13.60`.
- Management message: management said data centers remain a key driver, but growth is broader than that, with strength across electrical end markets and aerospace. Sequential margin improvement in Electrical Americas was a major point of emphasis.
- Structural themes: Eaton now reads as a high-conviction power-infrastructure platform rather than just an industrial electrical supplier. The planned Reverse Morris Trust separation of Mobility sharpens that identity further.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 2026 sales were a record `$7.451 billion`, adjusted EPS was `$2.81`, organic growth was `10%`, and segment margins were `22.7%`.
- Guidance: on May 5, 2026 Eaton raised full-year 2026 organic-growth guidance to `9-11%` and adjusted EPS guidance to `$13.05-$13.50`.
- Management message: management highlighted strong backlog and order growth, large capacity-expansion investments, and the closing of major acquisitions including Boyd Thermal and Ultra PCS.
- Structural themes: this quarter shows Eaton using strong demand to justify both organic capacity expansion and inorganic portfolio moves around thermal management and aerospace systems.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 2025 sales were a record `$7.055 billion`, adjusted EPS was a record `$3.33`, full-year 2025 adjusted EPS was a record `$12.07`, and full-year organic growth was `8%`.
- Guidance: on February 26, 2026 Eaton introduced 2026 guidance for `7-9%` organic growth, segment margins of `24.6-25.0%`, and adjusted EPS of `$13.00-$13.50`.
- Management message: management emphasized accelerating orders, continued backlog growth, and confidence that electrification, AI, infrastructure, and aerospace demand would sustain momentum into 2026.
- Structural themes: this quarter closes the year with Eaton already looking less like a traditional electrical-equipment cycle and more like a secular-infrastructure compounder tied to power-system bottlenecks.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Eaton shows that industrial earnings can be led by electrical infrastructure, data-center power demand, and utility-facing capacity rather than by classic machinery volume alone.
- Consumer: consumer demand is not the useful lens here. The relevant demand drivers are hyperscalers, utilities, industrial capex, commercial buildings, and aerospace.
- Cultural: the important cultural layer is the shift in industrial rhetoric around AI. Eaton translates AI into high-voltage infrastructure, thermal management, and resilient power systems rather than into software aspiration alone.
- Regulatory: grid modernization, trade policy, acquisition approval, and industrial-policy incentives all matter indirectly through capacity, infrastructure spending, and end-market timing.
- Labor: labor is secondary to capacity, supply chain, and execution in this evidence set, though factory expansion and buildout obviously require labor availability.
- Technology: this is a first-order technology story inside industrials. Electrical architecture, thermal systems, advanced power conversion, and AI-enabled operations all matter directly.
- Capital allocation: M&A and capacity expansion are central. Eaton is using capital to deepen its position in higher-growth electrical and aerospace niches and to simplify the portfolio through the Mobility separation.
