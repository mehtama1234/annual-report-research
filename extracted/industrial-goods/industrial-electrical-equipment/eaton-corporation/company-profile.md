## What changed

- Revenue: full-year 2025 sales were a record `$27.448 billion`, up `10%`, with `8%` organic growth. Q4 2025 sales were `$7.055 billion`, Q1 2026 sales were `$7.451 billion`, and Q2 2026 sales were `$8.531 billion`, with reported growth accelerating from `13%` to `17%` to `21%`.
- Earnings and margins: full-year 2025 earnings per share were `$10.45` and adjusted EPS was `$12.07`, with record full-year segment margins of `24.5%`. Q4 2025 adjusted EPS was `$3.33`, Q1 2026 adjusted EPS was `$2.81`, and Q2 2026 adjusted EPS was a record `$3.15`. Segment margins were `24.9%` in Q4 2025, `22.7%` in Q1 2026, and `23.1%` in Q2 2026.
- Orders and backlog: Eaton is one of the clearest backlog-led industrial stories in the archive. Q4 2025 highlighted `16%` rolling-order growth in Electrical Americas and `29%` year-over-year backlog growth in the electrical sector. Q1 2026 showed `42%` rolling-order growth in Electrical Americas and total backlog up `48%` in electrical and `28%` in aerospace. Q2 2026 kept that momentum with Electrical Americas rolling orders up `41%`, Electrical Global up `33%`, Aerospace up `17%`, and total backlog at about `$24.1 billion` in the `10-Q`.
- End-market mix: the electrical businesses are the center of gravity. Eaton repeatedly ties growth to data centers, utilities, industrial customers, and broader electrification infrastructure. Aerospace is also strong and provides a second growth leg, while Mobility is being separated and is no longer the strategic center.
- Capital allocation and portfolio: the 2025 annual report says Eaton announced about `$13 billion` of acquisitions in 2025 and roughly `$1.5 billion` of North American capacity investments. Q1 2026 closed about `$11 billion` of acquisitions, including Boyd Thermal and Ultra PCS Limited. Q2 2026 announced the planned separation of the Mobility business through a Reverse Morris Trust transaction expected in the first quarter of 2027.
- Technology and AI linkage: Eaton is one of the cleanest industrial expressions of AI power demand in the repo. The annual report explicitly links growth to digitalization, data centers, and AI. It also highlights partnerships with NVIDIA and Siemens Energy around next-generation power infrastructure and data-center buildout.
- North America and capacity: the 2025 annual report emphasizes North American manufacturing expansion, transformer and voltage-regulator scaling, data-center support in Virginia, and electrical-footprint investment to meet demand.
- Guidance: Eaton entered 2026 guiding to `7-9%` organic growth and `$13.00-$13.50` adjusted EPS. After Q1 2026 it raised organic-growth guidance to `9-11%` and adjusted EPS to `$13.05-$13.50`. After Q2 2026 it raised organic-growth guidance again to `11-13%` and adjusted EPS to `$13.40-$13.60`, while GAAP EPS guidance moved lower due to acquisition and divestiture effects.
- Management tone: confident, investment-heavy, and secular-growth-oriented. Eaton is not framing itself as a normal cyclical electrical-equipment name. It is framing itself as a power-management platform built around electrification, digitalization, data-center power, aerospace growth, and disciplined portfolio shaping.

## Open questions

- How much of Eaton's current electrical surge is tied to a durable multi-year data-center and grid buildout versus a shorter burst of project intensity?
- Does the Mobility separation improve the earnings mix as much as management expects, or does it mainly simplify the story without materially changing growth quality?
