## Annual report takeaways

- Hubbell is a strong next addition because it sharpens the utility-components and grid-hardening layer of the electrical buildout. The company explicitly describes itself as a provider of utility and electrical solutions that help customers operate critical infrastructure reliably and efficiently.
- The annual business framing is especially useful for the archive. Hubbell organizes its portfolio around `In Front of the Meter`, `The Edge`, and `Behind the Meter`, which creates a clean map from utility transmission and distribution to communications and controls to building and industrial power consumption.
- The `10-K` says the Utility Solutions segment provides critical components that allow the grid to transmit and distribute energy and make the grid smarter and more flexible. It specifically calls out T&D components, protection and control devices, smart meters, and communications systems.
- The annual report also makes the strategic demand setup explicit. Management tied `2025` and the outlook into load growth, grid resiliency, transmission and substation demand, and an expanding modern grid, while also showing data-center exposure inside the portfolio map.
- Hubbell is analytically useful because it is not only broad electrical exposure. It sits right at the component and control layer of the grid and the edge of the utility system, which gives the archive a stronger bridge between regulated utilities, industrial electrical equipment, and AI- and load-driven infrastructure demand.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` diluted EPS was `$4.52`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$5.52`, net sales grew `15%`, operating margin was `20.4%`, and adjusted operating margin was `23.9%`.
- Demand and operating indicators: Utility Solutions net sales rose to `$1,026 million`, Electrical Solutions net sales rose to `$686 million`, and management raised adjusted EPS guidance to `$20.25` to `$20.55`.
- Management message: management said megatrends in grid modernization, load growth, and data-center investment drove `10%` organic growth in the quarter with visible demand strength across utility and electrical markets.
- Structural themes: Hubbell is converting the same power and AI-adjacent buildout seen elsewhere into grid components, controls, and behind-the-meter electrical infrastructure demand.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` diluted EPS was `$3.41`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$3.93`, net sales grew `11%`, operating margin was `17.4%`, and adjusted operating margin was `19.8%`.
- Demand and operating indicators: Utility Solutions net sales rose to `$949 million`, Electrical Solutions net sales rose to `$568 million`, and the company raised full-year guidance.
- Management message: management said load growth was driving transmission and substation strength, aging infrastructure resiliency investment was supporting distribution demand, and Electrical Solutions growth was driven by strong data-center and light-industrial markets.
- Structural themes: Q1 is the cleanest early-2026 read on Hubbell as a utility-and-electrical operator benefiting from both sides of the meter, not just from one-off utility projects.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` diluted EPS was `$4.19`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$4.73`, net sales grew `12%`, operating margin was `20.9%`, and adjusted operating margin was `23.4%`.
- Demand and operating indicators: Utility Solutions net sales rose to `$936 million`, Electrical Solutions net sales rose to `$557 million`, and full-year `2025` adjusted diluted EPS reached `$18.21`.
- Management message: management framed `2026` around continued attractive growth in net sales, operating margin, operating profit, and free cash flow.
- Structural themes: Q4 `2025` shows Hubbell entering `2026` as a secular electrification and grid-infrastructure name rather than as a normal short-cycle electrical manufacturer.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Hubbell adds a more utility-facing and grid-component-heavy layer to the industrial electrical set.
- Utilities: this is one of the better bridge names between industrial equipment and regulated utility capex because it sits directly in transmission, substation, distribution, meters, controls, and communications.
- Technology: Hubbell is another first-order physical-technology story because data-center growth and load growth are flowing into power hardware, control devices, and infrastructure on both sides of the meter.
- Real Estate and industrial demand: behind-the-meter exposure also ties Hubbell into commercial, industrial, and owner-operator power needs rather than only into regulated utility systems.
- Capital and execution: acquisitions like DMC Power and continued capacity investment matter because this is a scale-and-positioning story inside a structurally stronger electrical environment.
- Cultural read: Hubbell gives the archive a company whose management language connects electrification, resiliency, load growth, and data centers directly to the physical grid and control layer.
