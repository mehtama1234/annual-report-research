## Annual report takeaways

- Vertiv is the clearest direct industrial bridge from AI demand into critical physical infrastructure. The company repeatedly describes itself as a global leader in critical digital infrastructure and centers the business on power, cooling, and IT infrastructure solutions and services.
- The `10-K` makes the market position explicit. Vertiv serves data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial environments, which means it sits inside the hard infrastructure layer required to support rising compute density.
- Full-year `2025` was already a major step up before the `2026` acceleration. Net sales reached about `$10.23 billion`, operating profit reached about `$1.83 billion`, net income reached about `$1.33 billion`, diluted EPS was `$3.41`, and adjusted diluted EPS was `$6.02`.
- Q4 `2025` also made the forward demand picture unusually clear. Organic orders grew about `252%`, trailing-twelve-month organic orders grew about `81%`, fourth-quarter book-to-bill was about `2.9x`, and backlog rose to `$15.0 billion`.
- Vertiv improves the industrial archive because it ties together themes that were only partially visible elsewhere. `Eaton` shows electrical architecture and power quality, and `Trane` shows cooling and building systems. Vertiv sits at the deployment point where hyperscale and colocation customers need those systems integrated into critical digital infrastructure.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` net sales were `$3,274 million`, adjusted operating margin was `22.6%`, diluted EPS was `$1.27`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.52`, and adjusted free cash flow was `$925 million`.
- Demand and operating indicators: organic sales growth was `18%`, revenue was affected by timing shifts from supply-chain congestion and multi-phased project execution, and the company ended the quarter with `$5.6 billion` of liquidity and a net cash position.
- Management message: management said demand for AI and general compute continues to intensify, deployments are becoming more complex and more infrastructure-intensive, and Vertiv understands how power and thermal infrastructure responds at scale.
- Structural themes: Vertiv is converting AI infrastructure demand into both growth and balance-sheet strength. This is not just an order story anymore; it is flowing into margins, cash generation, and raised guidance.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` net sales were `$2,650 million`, adjusted operating margin was `20.8%`, operating cash flow was `$767 million`, and adjusted free cash flow was `$653 million`.
- Demand and operating indicators: organic sales growth was `23%`, the Americas region grew `44%` organically on strong data-center demand, liquidity reached `$5.0 billion`, and net leverage fell to about `0.2x`.
- Management message: management emphasized deployment speed, reliability, comprehensive services, and rising infrastructure density as customers scaled more ambitious projects.
- Structural themes: this quarter shows Vertiv gaining share and scaling into a stronger capital structure at the same time. The combination of growth, margin expansion, and liquidity improvement is unusually strong for an industrial operator.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` net sales were `$2,880 million`, adjusted operating margin was `23.2%`, operating cash flow was `$1,005 million`, and adjusted free cash flow was `$910 million`.
- Demand and operating indicators: fourth-quarter organic orders grew about `252%`, trailing-twelve-month organic orders grew about `81%`, book-to-bill was about `2.9x`, and backlog increased to `$15.0 billion`, up `109%`.
- Management message: management highlighted deep collaborations with semiconductor industry leaders, strong hyperscale and colocation demand, and clear visibility into another year of major growth.
- Structural themes: Q4 `2025` is the cleanest evidence that the AI buildout was already pushing through power and thermal infrastructure before the strong `2026` revenue conversion showed up.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Vertiv strengthens the industrial case that infrastructure intensity, not generic cyclicality, is the real driver in this part of the market.
- Technology: this is a first-order technology story inside industrials because compute growth is creating direct demand for electrical, thermal, and deployment infrastructure.
- Capital and execution: backlog, capacity expansion, project execution, and working-capital discipline matter as much as product demand.
- Cultural: Vertiv gives the archive a company whose language openly treats AI as a physical systems problem, not only a software or semiconductor opportunity.
- Customer structure: hyperscale and colocation customers matter heavily here, which makes Vertiv a useful bridge between Technology, Real Estate, Utilities, and Industrial Goods.
- Balance-sheet read: by Q2 `2026`, strong earnings conversion had already pushed Vertiv to a net cash position, which materially changes how aggressive the company can be on expansion and strategic optionality.

## Working interpretation

- Vertiv materially improves the archive because it turns the AI-infrastructure thesis into a direct power-and-thermal deployment story rather than an adjacent inference.
- It also helps organize the broader archive correctly: AI demand is not stopping at chips, networking, and cloud. It is pulling in critical physical infrastructure operators with real backlog, margin expansion, and cash generation.
