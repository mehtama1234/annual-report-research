## What changed

- Vertiv materially sharpens the industrial archive's AI-infrastructure read. This is not general electrical exposure or generic HVAC. The company repeatedly describes itself as a global leader in critical digital infrastructure and frames its portfolio around power, cooling, and IT infrastructure solutions and services.
- The annual evidence gives the cleanest direct bridge yet between industrials and data-center buildout. The `10-K` says Vertiv serves data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial environments, which puts it directly inside the physical stack required for compute growth rather than one step removed from it.
- Full-year `2025` results were already strong before the `2026` acceleration. Net sales reached about `$10.23 billion`, operating profit reached about `$1.83 billion`, net income reached about `$1.33 billion`, diluted EPS was `$3.41`, and adjusted diluted EPS was `$6.02`.
- Q4 `2025` made the forward signal unusually obvious. Organic orders grew about `252%`, trailing-twelve-month organic orders grew about `81%`, fourth-quarter book-to-bill was about `2.9x`, and backlog rose to `$15.0 billion`, up `109%` year over year.
- Q1 and Q2 `2026` show the demand is converting into revenue, margins, and cash. Net sales moved from `$2,650 million` in Q1 to `$3,274 million` in Q2. Adjusted operating margin moved from `20.8%` to `22.6%`. Adjusted free cash flow moved from `$653 million` to `$925 million`. By the end of Q2, Vertiv had reached a net cash position.
- The management language is consistent and useful for theme work. Vertiv talks about infrastructure density increasing, deployment timelines compressing, and power and thermal infrastructure becoming more complex at scale. That gives the archive a direct operator saying the AI buildout is making real-world infrastructure harder, denser, and more capital-intensive.
- Vertiv also sits cleanly between other industrial names already in the archive. `Eaton` gives the electrical-architecture and grid-facing layer. `Trane` gives the cooling and building-systems layer. Vertiv connects both around critical digital infrastructure and hyperscale deployment.

## Open questions

- How much of Vertiv's current demand and margin expansion reflects a durable multi-year infrastructure cycle versus a period of unusually intense order conversion and customer urgency?
- As Vertiv keeps expanding capacity and acquisitions, does execution remain this strong once project complexity, supply-chain friction, and deployment scale grow further?
