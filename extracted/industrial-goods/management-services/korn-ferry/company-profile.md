## What changed

- Fiscal framing: this packet uses a `2025` annual-report anchor but the latest three reported quarters are fiscal `2026` periods because Korn Ferry's year ends on `April 30`.
- Business mix: the quarter chain shows institutions buying a broader talent stack than classic executive search alone, spanning consulting, digital, professional search, interim staffing, and RPO.
- Demand signal: remaining fees under existing contracts stayed around `$1.9B` and grew double digits across the latest periods, indicating durable institutional demand despite macro sensitivity.
- Mix and margin tension: Professional Search & Interim is growing faster than some legacy lines, but the company also disclosed that interim-heavy mix can pressure margins relative to permanent placement.
- Acquisition posture: the `2025` annual filing shows the company using acquisitions to deepen interim and specialist talent capabilities, including Trilogy in fiscal `2025` and prior acquisitions such as Salo and ICS.

## Open questions

- How durable is the current top-line growth if enterprise hiring demand softens after the recent run of contracted-fee expansion?
- Does faster Professional Search & Interim growth permanently lower mix-adjusted margins if interim work keeps taking share from permanent placement?
- How much of Korn Ferry's value proposition remains differentiated consulting and leadership infrastructure versus cyclical recruiting volume?
