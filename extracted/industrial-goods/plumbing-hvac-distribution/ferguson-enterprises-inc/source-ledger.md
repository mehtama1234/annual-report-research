## Reconciliation notes

- Ferguson changed its fiscal year-end from July 31 to December 31, creating a five-month transition period from `2025-08-01` through `2025-12-31`.
- For this packet, the annual baseline is intentionally split between the pre-transition `2025-07-31` `10-K` and the `2025-12-31` `10-KT`, with the `2026-02-24` results exhibit used to preserve the full calendar-`2025` management framing.
- The correct trailing-quarter set as of Monday, August 10, 2026 is `Q2 2026`, `Q1 2026`, and the quarter ended `2025-12-31`.
- The latest quarter is source-complete through the `8-K` and Exhibit `99.1` filed on `2026-08-10`; a separate `Q2 2026` `10-Q` had not yet been filed as of the date baseline.
