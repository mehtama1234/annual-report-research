## Annual report takeaways

- Veralto is the cleanest current extension for the behind-the-shelf consumer infrastructure lane because it widens the plant-embedded side from `CECO Environmental`'s heavier emissions and process-control hardware toward quality, water, analytics, monitoring, and recurring measurement workflows.
- AnnualReports still lagged here as of Monday, August 10, 2026: the saved company page classifies Veralto as `Industrial Goods / Pollution & Treatment Controls` but still surfaced `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K` as the most recent hosted annual package.
- Official IR already exposed the `2025 Annual Report`, making this another clean case where the source taxonomy is useful for classification but not current enough for the live annual archive.
- Full-year `2025` sales reached `$5.503 billion`, net earnings reached `$940 million`, adjusted net earnings reached `$977 million`, operating cash flow reached `$1.077 billion`, and non-GAAP free cash flow reached `$1.014 billion`.
- The useful read is not just environmental treatment hardware. Veralto operates through `Water Quality` and `Product Quality & Innovation`, which means water analytics, monitoring, treatment, marking, coding, packaging workflow, and trusted-product-control systems all sit inside one company.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` sales increased `7.6%` year-over-year to `$1.474 billion`, net earnings were `$241 million`, diluted EPS was `$0.98`, adjusted net earnings were `$274 million`, adjusted diluted EPS were `$1.11`, operating cash flow was `$340 million`, and non-GAAP free cash flow was `$328 million`.
- Guidance: on `July 28, 2026`, Veralto raised full-year `2026` core-sales growth guidance to `4.0%` to `4.5%`, raised adjusted diluted EPS guidance to `$4.35` to `$4.43`, and increased free-cash-flow conversion guidance to greater than `100%` of GAAP net earnings.
- Management message: management said `Water Quality` led the quarter with `5.7%` core growth, highlighted demand across industrial water treatment, water reuse, environmental monitoring, and digital workflow solutions, and pointed to bolt-on acquisitions including `In-Situ`, `GlobalVision`, and `Alfaa UV`.
- Structural themes: Q2 is the clearest proof that Veralto belongs in the blind-spot lane as a quality, water, monitoring, and embedded-control platform rather than as another generic pollution-control or industrial-equipment name.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` sales increased `6.7%` year-over-year to `$1.422 billion`, net earnings were `$254 million`, diluted EPS was `$1.02`, adjusted net earnings were `$266 million`, adjusted diluted EPS were `$1.07`, operating cash flow was `$182 million`, and non-GAAP free cash flow was `$170 million`.
- Guidance: on `April 28, 2026`, Veralto raised full-year adjusted diluted EPS guidance to `$4.20` to `$4.28`, up from `$4.10` to `$4.20`, and increased free-cash-flow conversion guidance to approximately `100%` of GAAP net earnings.
- Management message: management emphasized resilient end markets, strategic acquisitions of `In-Situ` and `GlobalVision`, roughly `$300 million` of share repurchases, and a cost-optimization program expected to produce `$65 million` to `$75 million` of annual savings by `2028`.
- Structural themes: Q1 shows why Veralto sharpens the lane: the plant-embedded value is not only emissions control or cleanup intensity, but recurring water-quality workflows, product authentication and coding, digital packaging workflow, and operational monitoring embedded inside customer systems.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` sales increased `3.8%` year-over-year to `$1.396 billion`, net earnings were `$254 million`, diluted EPS were `$1.01`, adjusted net earnings were `$261 million`, adjusted diluted EPS were `$1.04`, operating cash flow was `$311 million`, and non-GAAP free cash flow was `$291 million`.
- Guidance: on `February 3, 2026`, Veralto introduced full-year `2026` adjusted diluted EPS guidance of `$4.10` to `$4.20`, low-to-mid-single-digit core-sales growth guidance, and free-cash-flow conversion guidance of approximately `100%` of GAAP net earnings.
- Management message: management described `2025` as an outstanding year driven by `VES` execution, operating efficiency, mid-single-digit core sales growth, double-digit adjusted EPS growth, and more than `$1 billion` of free cash flow.
- Structural themes: Q4 `2025` closes the year with Veralto looking like a high-trust quality-and-water infrastructure platform tied to clean water, safe food, and trusted essential goods rather than a narrow treatment-controls operator.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Veralto broadens `Industrial Goods / Pollution & Treatment Controls` into a more durable quality-and-water-control layer that sits inside customer operations rather than only at the regulatory edge.
- Consumer: direct consumer demand is indirect, but the company sits behind clean water access, safe food handling, trusted coding and marking, packaging workflow, and essential-goods integrity.
- Cultural: the relevant cultural layer is trust in the physical world: clean water, product authenticity, traceability, quality assurance, and reliable essential-goods handling.
- Societal: Veralto is a strong societal-pattern case because it connects industrial infrastructure to public-health, water-resilience, environmental monitoring, and the quiet control systems that support daily life.
- Industrial: the core industrial logic is recurring workflow embedment, measurement, monitoring, coding, inspection, and water-treatment exposure rather than one-off project hardware alone.
- Technology: the useful technology layer is applied sensing, analytics, digital workflow, and operational monitoring inside physical infrastructure and product-quality systems.
- Capital allocation: Veralto is using capital for bolt-on acquisitions, buybacks, and cost optimization while maintaining a strong free-cash-flow profile.

## Why this company matters for the blind-spot lane

- `CECO Environmental` already anchors heavier plant-embedded emissions, thermal, air, and process-control infrastructure.
- `Veralto` is the best current extension because it shows that plant-embedded control is wider than engineered environmental hardware alone.
- That makes the useful comparison `process-reliability and environmental hardware` versus `quality, water, monitoring, and embedded-control workflows`, not simply one more industrial environmental company.
