## What the business is

ADT is a recurring security-services and smart-home operator serving residential and small-business customers in the U.S. The filings and quarter materials make clear that the business is built around two linked economic engines:

- `Monitoring and related services`
- `Security installation product and other`

That mix matters because the company is not just selling devices. It is selling monitored relationships, installation and service workflows, retention, and a gradually broader smart-home platform. Management repeatedly frames the business around:

- recurring monthly revenue (`RMR`)
- customer attrition and payback discipline
- `ADT+`
- Google Nest and Yale integration
- ambient sensing and presence-detection capabilities
- self-install and broader channel reach through `ADT Blu`
- small-business as well as household security demand

This is exactly why the company belongs in the frontier. It shows a protection-services operator sitting between the end customer and the actual continuity of physical safety, emergency response, and monitoring workflow.

## Fiscal 2025 annual picture

From the full-year `2025` results:

- total revenue increased `5%` to about `$5.1B`
- end-of-period recurring monthly revenue reached about `$359M`
- gross revenue attrition was about `13.1%`
- revenue payback was about `2.3 years`
- GAAP income from continuing operations was about `$601M`, or about `$0.68` per diluted share
- adjusted income from continuing operations was about `$750M`, or about `$0.89` per diluted share
- net cash provided by operating activities was about `$1.9B`
- adjusted free cash flow, including swaps, was about `$863M`, up about `16%`
- the company returned about `$791M` to shareholders through share repurchases and dividends

Management also highlighted:

- continued rollout of the proprietary `ADT+` platform
- integration with Google Nest and Yale devices
- new features such as Trusted Neighbor
- the acquisition of Origin AI and ambient sensing capabilities announced alongside the annual cycle
- lower average debt cost of about `4.3%` and weighted-average maturity extended to about `5 years`

That annual setup matters because it shows ADT as more than a mature alarm company. It is a recurring cash-flow machine trying to deepen customer embedment and widen channel control through smart-home workflow, self-service options, and new sensing capabilities.

## What ADT adds to the cross-company synthesis

This packet adds several high-value signals to the broader map:

- protection services can be a recurring subscription-and-field-service model, not only a route or cash-logistics model
- `RMR`, attrition, and payback act like the operating core of the business, much the way route density or inventory turns do elsewhere in CLI 8
- channel control here means owning the customer relationship, monitoring layer, installation workflow, and upgrade path rather than merely selling equipment
- smart-home and small-business security demand creates a hybrid layer where physical response, digital service, and recurring billing all matter at once
- capital returns and debt optimization are easier to understand when the recurring monitoring base is stable and installation economics remain disciplined

## Bigger-picture interpretation

ADT is one of the clearest examples in the frontier of how service attachment can become the product. The surface label is home security. The deeper operating reality is a recurring protection-services platform built on monitoring contracts, field technicians, installation economics, digital support, and smart-home workflow control.

That makes ADT useful for the archive in two ways:

1. it fills the missing monitoring-heavy protection-services gap between `Brink's` and `Motorola Solutions`
2. it shows how cultural and societal pressure around safety, convenience, remote support, home intelligence, and small-business continuity gets monetized through a middle layer that customers do not want to rebuild themselves

In that sense, ADT is not just an alarm company. It is a recurring operating layer for household and small-business protection, which is exactly the kind of middle-layer frontier the CLI was supposed to keep adding once the base set was in place.
