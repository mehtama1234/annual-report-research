## What changed

- This packet gives CLI 8 a more literal recurring monitoring and household-plus-small-business protection-services operator than the existing `Brink's` and `Motorola Solutions` packets.
- The operating story is not classic guard labor. It is recurring monitored-service revenue, installation economics, subscriber retention, field-service execution, and smart-home platform control.
- The filing chain makes the business split visible between `Monitoring and related services` and `Security installation product and other`, which is exactly the kind of channel-plus-service attach middle layer the frontier was missing.
- The annual and quarter materials also show the strategic shift from a legacy alarm label toward a broader smart-security and home-intelligence platform built around `ADT+`, ambient sensing, Google device integration, self-install expansion, and more digital service support.

## Working interpretation

- `ADT` is one of the cleaner missing-middle-layer additions still available because the archive taxonomy is current, the SEC chain is complete, and the company sits directly between households or small businesses and the actual security response-and-monitoring system.
- It improves CLI 8 by showing a different kind of channel control than cash logistics or public-safety technology: a recurring monitored relationship where customer stickiness, attrition, `RMR`, installation payback, and service workflow all matter at once.
