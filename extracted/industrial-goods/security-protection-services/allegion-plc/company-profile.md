## What changed

- Allegion gives CLI 8 a cleaner physical-access and security-infrastructure packet than the current mix of monitored security, cash logistics, communications systems, and labor-forward secure services.
- The company explicitly spans hardware, software, and electronic solutions across residential and commercial locks, door closers, exit devices, steel doors and frames, access control, and workforce productivity systems.
- Full-year `2025` revenues were `$4,067.3M`, with adjusted `EPS` of `$8.14` and available cash flow of `$685.7M`.
- `Q4 2025` revenues were `$1,033.2M`, up `9.3%` reported and `3.3%` organic, with adjusted operating margin of `22.4%`.
- `Q1 2026` revenues were `$1,033.6M`, up `9.7%` reported and `2.6%` organic, with adjusted operating margin of `21.2%`.
- `Q2 2026` revenues were `$1,151.5M`, up `12.7%` reported and `6.9%` organic, with adjusted operating margin of `24.2%`.

## Open questions

- How durable is Allegion's non-residential Americas strength if European demand remains softer and residential stays weaker?
- How much of the current growth story is structural electronics and access-control attachment versus a favorable near-term mix in non-residential channels?
- Does Allegion belong more with route- and monitoring-based protection services, or as a more software-and-hardware access-control infrastructure operator that widens the lane beyond those models?
