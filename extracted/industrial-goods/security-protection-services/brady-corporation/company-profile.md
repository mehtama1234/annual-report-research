## What changed

- Brady gives CLI 8 a cleaner workplace-control and safety-identification packet than the current mix of monitoring, cash logistics, inspection, communications, labor-forward secure capacity, and access-control names.
- The company explicitly spans identification solutions and specialty products that identify and protect premises, products, and people, which makes it a real proof of middle-layer operating control rather than a loose adjacent industrial name.
- Full-year `2025` sales were `$1.51B`, with record adjusted diluted `EPS` of `$4.60`, and the company returned `$96.4M` to shareholders.
- `Q1 FY2026` sales were `$405.3M`, up `7.5%`, with `2.8%` organic sales growth and adjusted diluted `EPS` of `$1.21`.
- `Q2 FY2026` sales were `$384.1M`, up `7.7%`, with `1.6%` organic sales growth and adjusted diluted `EPS` of `$1.09`.
- `Q3 FY2026` sales were `$435.2M`, up `13.8%`, with `8.2%` organic sales growth and adjusted diluted `EPS` of `$1.50`.

## Open questions

- How durable is Brady's recent acceleration if broader industrial demand weakens again after the Q3 FY2026 improvement?
- How much of the current growth profile comes from structural safety-and-identification workflow demand versus acquisition layering and foreign-exchange help?
- Does Brady belong primarily in protection-services adjacency, or is it better understood as a safety-compliance workflow operator that widens the lane beyond alarms, guarding, and secure logistics?
