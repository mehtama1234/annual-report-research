## Why this company matters

Brink's adds a system that most consumer and industrial maps miss: the physical infrastructure behind still-circulating money. The company sits across cash and valuables management, ATM managed services, digital retail solutions, recurring service contracts, and the transition layer between older cash circulation and more software- and service-mediated retail and banking operations.
