## Why this name matters

- Resideo is one of the strongest next additions to CLI 8 because it captures a combined manufacturer-plus-distributor structure whose `ADI Global Distribution` business is a literal channel-control operator inside low-voltage security, fire, smart-home, and building-products distribution.
- It matters for CLI 8 because the packet shows both sides of the middle layer: branded sensing and controls products on one side and a branch-dense specialty distribution network on the other.
- It is especially valuable because the spin-off of `ADI Global Distribution` was completed on `2026-08-03`, just one week before the research baseline. That makes Resideo one of the clearest ways to explain how the archive can separate product economics from channel-control economics without guessing.

## Annual report takeaways

- AnnualReports still lagged at `2024` on Monday, `2026-08-10`, but the company page still classifies Resideo as `Industrial Goods / Security & Protection Services` and explicitly highlights the `ADI` distribution business, so the taxonomy anchor is still useful even though the latest archive package is missing.
- The combined-company `2025` annual filing shows why Resideo belongs in CLI 8. The company was still operating through `ADI Global Distribution` and `Products & Solutions`, which means the packet directly captures distribution infrastructure, contractor-channel relationships, smart-building security hardware, and building-controls manufacturing inside one annual chain.
- Full-year `2025` net loss was `$527M`, compared with net income of `$116M` in `2024`, but that headline was driven by the accelerated termination of the Honeywell indemnification agreement rather than by a collapse in the operating model.
- The more useful annual operating read is that full-year `2025` adjusted `EBITDA` was a record `$833M`, up `20%` year-over-year, while company-level net revenue reached roughly `$7.5B`.
- The bigger structural point is what the subsequent spin made explicit: `ADI` was not a side business. It was a large distribution network that served more than `100,000` low-voltage contractors through more than `200` physical locations with access to more than `350,000` products, and by mid-`2026` Resideo was actively carving it into a separate public company.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent reported quarter as of 2026-08-10: Q1 2026

- Results: net revenue was `$1,912M`, net income was `$38M`, `GAAP` diluted `EPS` was `$0.17`, and adjusted `EPS` was `$0.65`.
- Growth signal: revenue increased `8%` versus the first quarter of `2025`, and adjusted `EBITDA` rose `28%` to `$215M`.
- Segment read: `ADI Global Distribution` revenue was `$1,206M`, up `8%`, while `Products & Solutions` revenue was `$706M`, up `9%`.
- Strategic read: this is one of the cleanest quarter reads in the frontier because it still shows the combined channel before the spin. The distribution business remained larger than the products business by revenue, but the product side carried much higher margin, which is exactly the kind of split that makes channel control and manufacturer economics analytically separable.

### Quarter minus 1: Q4 2025

- Results: net revenue was `$1,895M`, up `2%`; net income was `$136M`; diluted `EPS` was `$0.73`; and adjusted `EPS` was `$0.50`.
- Margin read: total company gross margin was `29.6%`, up `110` basis points year-over-year, while adjusted `EBITDA` was `$226M`, up `21%`.
- Segment read: `ADI` revenue was `$1,183M`, down `1%`, while `Products & Solutions` revenue was `$712M`, up `6%`.
- Full-year bridge: the company ended `2025` with record adjusted `EBITDA` of `$833M`, even though the headline `GAAP` full-year result was pulled down by the Honeywell indemnification termination cost.

### Quarter minus 2: Q3 2025

- Results: net revenue was `$1,864M`, up `2%`; record net income was `$156M`; fully diluted `EPS` was a record `$0.85`; and adjusted `EPS` was `$0.89`.
- Growth signal: adjusted `EBITDA` was a record `$229M`, up `21%`.
- Segment read: `ADI` revenue was `$1,203M`, up `2%`, with gross margin of `22.6%`, while `Products & Solutions` revenue was `$661M`, up `2%`, with adjusted `EBITDA` of `$92M`.
- Structural read: Q3 `2025` is where the packet becomes especially important for CLI 8. The company had already announced the plan to separate `ADI`, so the quarter gave a live look at the distributor economics before the spin and before the cleaner pure-play framing arrived in `2026`.

## Consumer, cultural, and societal interpretation

- Resideo is partly tied to consumer housing and home-safety demand, but the more important read is societal and institutional: homes, installers, integrators, security dealers, and building operators increasingly rely on connected controls, sensing, fire and safety hardware, and specialized distribution rather than simple one-off device purchases.
- The strongest cultural shift in this packet is that home and small-building automation are no longer only direct-to-consumer categories. They run through contractor channels, specialty distribution, security dealers, and hybrid hardware-plus-software ecosystems.
- The `ADI` side of the packet also captures a broader societal trend toward more instrumented, monitored, and professionally installed environments. That includes security, fire, audio-visual, low-voltage, and adjacent building systems.
- Resideo therefore helps CLI 8 explain how safety, comfort, and control products move through the economy before they ever become visible to an end household or business user.

## Industrial and operating pressures

- This is a middle-layer operator with real exposure to housing cadence, installer demand, foreign exchange, working-capital discipline, and the balance between branch-led and digital distribution.
- Segment economics diverge sharply. `ADI` is the larger revenue engine but with lower margin structure, while `Products & Solutions` has much higher gross and adjusted `EBITDA` margins. That tension is exactly why the eventual separation matters.
- The Honeywell indemnification termination is also not background noise. It reshaped the financial optics of `2025`, reduced future obligations, and made the spin and simplification story easier to underwrite.
- The separation itself is a real operating event rather than a narrative overlay. By `2026-08-10`, the spin had already been completed, `ADI` had begun trading as `ADIG`, and Resideo had repaid `$900M` of debt in connection with the transaction.

## Repeated higher-order patterns

- `Distribution can be hidden inside a manufacturer until the structure breaks apart.` Resideo is one of the best packets in CLI 8 for showing that.
- `Channel ownership and product ownership are separable but complementary.` The annual-plus-quarter chain makes that clear through the contrast between `ADI` and `Products & Solutions`.
- `Low-voltage and building-controls distribution is a real frontier lane.` This is not a generic electronics reseller. It is a specialized contractor and security-products channel with scale, branch infrastructure, and deep embedded relationships.
- `Financial engineering can reveal operating structure.` The spin-off did not create the channel; it exposed it more clearly.
- `Residential demand still runs through B2B intermediaries.` Installers, dealers, and distribution nodes remain crucial even in connected-home and smart-sensing categories.

## Best comparison uses

- Against `WESCO International Inc.`: WESCO shows a broader electrical and communications-infrastructure distributor, while Resideo shows a more security, low-voltage, installer-channel, and building-controls distribution model.
- Against `Allegion plc`: Allegion shows security and access infrastructure through installed products, while Resideo shows how security, sensing, and control products flow through a scaled contractor and distribution channel.
- Against `Brady Corporation`: Brady shows workplace identification and safety-control workflow, while Resideo shows smart-building, security, and low-voltage channel ownership with much larger branch and contractor-network exposure.
- Against `ADT Inc.`: ADT shows recurring monitoring and field installation economics, while Resideo shows the upstream product-and-distribution layer that supplies dealers, contractors, and installers.

## Working interpretation

- Resideo materially strengthens CLI 8 because it gives the frontier a clean case where a large security-and-building-products distributor is first visible inside a combined company and then becomes explicit through a spin-off.
- The bigger pattern is that distribution and channel control do not disappear just because a company also owns products. In many cases the channel is one of the most valuable parts of the enterprise, and Resideo is one of the clearest proofs in the archive.
