## What changed

- Resideo gives CLI 8 one of its clearest electronics-security and low-voltage distribution packets because the `ADI` business is a literal contractor-channel and branch-density intermediary rather than a loose adjacent product line.
- The company explicitly combines `ADI Global Distribution` and `Products & Solutions` inside the `2025` annual and latest reported quarter chain, which makes the channel-versus-product split unusually visible.
- Full-year `2025` adjusted `EBITDA` was a record `$833M`, while headline full-year `GAAP` results were distorted by the Honeywell indemnification termination.
- `Q3 2025` revenue was `$1,864M`, with record net income of `$156M` and record adjusted `EBITDA` of `$229M`.
- `Q4 2025` revenue was `$1,895M`, with net income of `$136M`, adjusted `EBITDA` of `$226M`, `ADI` revenue of `$1,183M`, and `Products & Solutions` revenue of `$712M`.
- `Q1 2026` revenue was `$1,912M`, with net income of `$38M`, adjusted `EPS` of `$0.65`, `ADI` revenue of `$1,206M`, and `Products & Solutions` revenue of `$706M`.
- By `2026-08-10`, the `ADI` spin had already been completed on `2026-08-03`, which sharpened the channel-control interpretation even though `Q2 2026` results were still not yet reported.

## Open questions

- How much of the combined-company margin profile was masking the lower-margin but strategically critical distribution economics inside `ADI`?
- Does the post-spin pure-play Resideo lose any channel leverage that the combined structure previously provided?
- How durable is `ADI`'s contractor-channel growth if low-voltage and security installation demand softens after the separation?
