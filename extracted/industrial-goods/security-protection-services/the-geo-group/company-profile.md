## What changed

- This packet gives CLI 8 the more labor- and institution-heavy outsourced secure-services operator that was still missing after `ADT`, `Brink's`, and `Motorola Solutions`.
- GEO is not a household monitoring company or a cash-logistics network. It is a government-outsourced support-services platform spanning secure facilities, processing centers, reentry centers, transportation, and electronic monitoring.
- The quarter chain also makes visible how immigration enforcement intensity, outsourced detention capacity, community supervision, and secure transportation all create middle-layer demand that sits between public institutions and end populations.
