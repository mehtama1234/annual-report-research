## What changed

- Revenue: full-year `2025` revenue reached `$6.03 billion`, up from `$5.89 billion` in `2024`. Quarterly revenue then moved from `$1.50 billion` in Q4 `2025` to `$1.46 billion` in Q1 `2026` and `$1.74 billion` in Q2 `2026`.
- Earnings and margins: full-year `2025` net income was `$391.0 million` and adjusted EBITDA was `$1.17 billion`. Q2 `2026` adjusted EBITDA was `$409.0 million` with a `23.6%` margin.
- Cash flow: adjusted free cash flow reached `$509.3 million` in `2025`. Management raised the midpoint of `2026` adjusted free-cash-flow guidance from `$520 million` after Q1 to `$550 million` after Q2.
- Business mix: the operating logic runs through Environmental Services plus Safety-Kleen Sustainability Solutions, combining hazardous disposal, response, industrial cleaning, waste-oil collection, and re-refining.
- Hazardous and project intensity: the recent quarter chain repeatedly highlights PFAS-related work, disposal-and-recycling demand, emergency-response projects, industrial cleanup, and manufacturing-linked waste streams.
- Manufacturing and reshoring tie-in: management explicitly tied growth opportunities to reshoring, North American manufacturing expansion, and a new ten-year disposal contract with an estimated value of `$600 million`.
- Acquisition layer: Q2 `2026` added the planned `ES&H` acquisition for `$305 million`, while Q4 `2025` included the announced Depot Connect International environmental-business acquisition for approximately `$130 million`.
