## What changed

- Operating identity: CECO is a pure physical-layer environmental infrastructure operator rather than a software or generic industrial name. Management describes the company as serving industrial air, industrial water, and energy transition markets through Engineered Systems and Industrial Process Solutions.
- Demand mix: the reported growth pattern is increasingly tied to project-heavy power, water, and industrial decarbonization lanes rather than only legacy pollution-control demand.
- Order momentum: the `2025` and `Q1 2026` materials both show the company moving from a mid-cap niche equipment vendor toward a larger backlog-driven platform with more large-project exposure.
- Acquisition step-change: the Thermon acquisition changed the scale and timing complexity of the story. By `2026-08-05`, management explicitly said the second quarter was the first reporting period as a combined company and needed more time to complete financial reporting and review processes; by Monday, `2026-08-10`, the company had published the quarter and filed the related earnings `8-K`.
- Interpretation value: CECO is useful because it lets the archive capture environmental-compliance spend, water and air process infrastructure, and the growing overlap between industrial cleanup, electrification, and AI-adjacent power buildout.
- Latest-quarter reporting nuance: the `Q2 2026` earnings release, presentation, and earnings `8-K` were live later on Monday, `2026-08-10`, but the `10-Q` still had not appeared in the SEC submissions feed at the time of verification.
