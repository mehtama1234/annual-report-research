## What changed

- Demographic operating leverage: Ventas is not simply collecting healthcare rent. The packet shows senior housing behaving like an aging-driven operating system with occupancy, pricing, and investment activity all moving in the same direction.
- Scarcity matters: management repeatedly tied performance to historically low new supply, which means demand conversion depends on existing platform quality and acquisition discipline rather than on speculative development.
- Capital access is part of the moat: the `1Q26` and `2Q26` chain showed heavy investment pacing plus large available liquidity, which makes Ventas more like a demographic allocator than a static yield vehicle.
- Exposure profile: compared with lighter intermediaries elsewhere in CLI 6, Ventas carries more operating and property exposure, but it also sits closer to the direct aging-demand wave than generic real estate names do.
- CLI 6 role: Ventas helps prove that aging-linked property can earn stronger economics when supply is tight and the owner can pair balance-sheet capacity with care-adjacent operating insight.
