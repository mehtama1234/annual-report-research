## What changed

- Host is the missing hospitality-owner contrast the recreation lane needed. Hilton, Marriott, and Hyatt showed branded, fee-heavy, loyalty-driven lodging economics. Host shows what happens when the archive moves one layer down into asset ownership, renovation cycles, property-level exposure, and REIT capital allocation.
- The consumer and demand read is still strong. Full-year `2025` comparable hotel Total RevPAR increased `4.2%`, first-quarter `2026` comparable hotel Total RevPAR increased `4.6%`, and second-quarter `2026` comparable hotel Total RevPAR increased `5.9%`, which says travel demand and out-of-room spend remained constructive into `2026`.
- The business model read is completely different from the asset-light hotel operators already in the lane. Host is a lodging-owner balance-sheet story with portfolio recycling, dividends, debt management, insurance proceeds, and large renovation programs all directly shaping the earnings outcome.
- The cultural and consumer read is also different. Host repeatedly says affluent consumers continue to prioritize travel and experiences, but it monetizes that demand through ownership of luxury and upper-upscale hotel real estate rather than through control of the loyalty program or the operating brand.
- This makes Host analytically useful well beyond hospitality. It is a clean case for separating demand resilience from profit capture and for asking which layer of a consumer system gets the better economics: the brand controller or the capital provider.

## Open questions

- How much of Host's current strength is broad lodging demand versus unusually favorable affluent-travel and special-event mix?
- Over time, does the lodging-owner layer earn enough return to justify the heavier capex, insurance, and balance-sheet burden relative to the asset-light brand operators it depends on?
- Are Host's transformational-capital partnerships with Hyatt and Marriott evidence of durable owner economics, or evidence that the operating brands still hold the stronger strategic position?
