## Annual report takeaways

- Annaly gives CLI 6 the mortgage REIT and housing-finance carry model, where value depends on repo funding, hedging, leverage, asset selection, and dividend defense rather than on a deposit or fee franchise.
- The `2025` annual read was unusually strong for the category: Annaly highlighted about `20.2%` aggregate economic return for the year, including a `5.5%` book-value gain, with conservative leverage and heavy focus on agency liquidity.
- Management's core message was that the company used market calm and investor demand to scale a broader housing-finance platform while still protecting book value and dividend capacity.
- Compared with Marsh and M&T, Annaly is the batch's clearest example that apparently attractive returns can still sit on a much more fragile funding and duration foundation.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter: 2Q26

- Results: Annaly reported `5.5%` economic return for the quarter and book value per common share of about `$20.15`.
- Operating detail: management highlighted a roughly `$109.4B` total portfolio, a highly liquid agency book, economic leverage of about `5.6x`, and a common dividend increase to `$0.75` per share.
- Strategic read: `2Q26` showed the most constructive version of the model, where rate conditions and portfolio construction cooperate enough for the company to defend both book value and payout.

### Quarter minus 1: 1Q26

- Results: first-quarter GAAP net income was about `$0.33` per average common share, earnings available for distribution were about `$0.76` per share, economic return was `1.5%`, and book value per common share was about `$19.82`.
- Operating detail: the first-quarter picture was less powerful than the second quarter, but still extended Annaly's run of positive economic returns and dividend coverage.
- Structural read: `1Q26` is useful because it shows how quickly the category's economics compress when the market backdrop is merely decent instead of especially favorable.

### Quarter minus 2: 4Q25

- Results: management said `2025` delivered about `20%` economic return and roughly `40%` total shareholder return, while the fourth quarter alone generated economic return of about `8.6%`.
- Full-year readthrough: the year-end message stressed portfolio growth, diversified housing-finance capability, and stronger investor demand for mortgage REIT exposure.
- Structural read: `4Q25` anchors Annaly as a carry vehicle that can look excellent when markets cooperate, but whose moat is much less durable than a workflow or deposit franchise.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Consumer and societal pattern: Annaly indirectly monetizes the persistence of U.S. housing finance and agency mortgage liquidity rather than direct household relationships.
- Industrial pressure: repo funding, hedge effectiveness, prepayment behavior, and rate volatility are the real operating pressures.
- Cultural shift: the market still rewards vehicles that can package housing-finance exposure into liquid income, but only while trust in leverage management remains intact.
- Bigger-picture read: Annaly is the batch's best stress test for whether CLI 6 returns are structural or simply favorable carry during the current rate regime.
