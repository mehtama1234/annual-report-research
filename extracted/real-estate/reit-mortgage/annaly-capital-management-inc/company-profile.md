## What changed

- Return profile: the quarter chain moved from a still-positive but more compressed `1Q26` to a much stronger `2Q26`, reinforcing how dependent the model is on rate backdrop, spreads, hedge success, and market cooperation.
- Book value and payout defense: Annaly kept centering book value, economic return, and dividend coverage rather than raw earnings growth, which is the right way to read this category.
- Funding and leverage: repo funding, agency liquidity, and leverage discipline remain the real operating engine. This is not a fee or deposit franchise; it is a packaged carry and housing-finance exposure vehicle.
- Strategic framing: management kept stressing a broader housing-finance platform, but the packet still shows that the moat is thinner and more regime-sensitive than the workflow-heavy or advisory-heavy names elsewhere in CLI 6.
- Cross-lane value: Annaly gives the archive a necessary contrast case for capital-heavy models that can produce attractive returns in good conditions without having the same durability as brokers, asset managers, or aging-linked property owners.
