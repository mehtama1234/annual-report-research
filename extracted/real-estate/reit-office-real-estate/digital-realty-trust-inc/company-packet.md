## What Digital Realty adds to the broader real-estate read

- A second data-center property anchor that tests whether the Equinix pattern is broad or company-specific
- Direct evidence that some real-estate names are being re-rated by AI, connectivity, and power-linked infrastructure demand
- A useful taxonomy dislocation because AnnualReports still labels the company as office real estate even while the operating model is clearly digital infrastructure
- A strong read on private-capital-enabled growth, backlog visibility, and hyperscale plus interconnection demand

## Structural themes

- Taxonomy lag matters: aggregator sector and industry labels can badly understate what the business now is
- AI demand is physical: data-center capacity, powered land, hyperscale leasing, and interconnection are now core real-estate growth drivers
- Private capital is part of the operating model: Digital Realty is not only leasing space, it is using capital structure and joint-venture design as a growth engine
- Backlog has become a key bridge metric: the company’s backlog expansion gives cleaner forward visibility than traditional occupancy-only REIT analysis
