## What changed

- Revenue: AvalonBay’s operating story is steadier than the infrastructure REITs, with same-store residential revenue up about `2.5%` for full-year 2025 and about `1.6%` year over year in Q2 2026.
- Margin: Core FFO per share moved from `$2.85` in Q4 2025 to roughly `$2.83` in Q1 2026 guidance range and then `$2.86` in Q2 2026, while same-store residential NOI growth remained positive but more measured than the digital-infrastructure names.
- Guidance: the company introduced a 2026 outlook in February 2026, raised guidance in April 2026, and then in July 2026 shifted disclosure to emphasize same-store guidance in light of the proposed merger of equals with Equity Residential.
- Capital allocation: AvalonBay remains development- and balance-sheet-driven, with active multifamily pipeline buildout, selective redevelopment, and land held for future residential development rather than private-capital-heavy data-center scaling.
- Segment mix: this is a clean multifamily housing operator centered on apartment rental communities, same-store residential revenue, occupancy, and development deliveries.
- Operational issues: same-store residential revenue and NOI held positive, but the growth rate is narrower than the data-center and logistics names; the main strategic complication in Q2 2026 is the announced merger with Equity Residential.
- Consumer / cultural signals: AvalonBay is the strongest direct housing and urban-renter behavior read in the real-estate tranche because it sits much closer to household formation, affordability, migration, and apartment demand.
- Management tone: management is steady and operating-focused rather than expansive, with attention on same-store residential metrics, development execution, and merger framing.
