## Annual report takeaways

- American Tower is the right next CLI 5 extension because it tests a different physical-infrastructure toll than Corning. Instead of monetizing optics and materials, American Tower monetizes siting, tower tenancy, colocation, and increasingly data-center-adjacent digital-infrastructure rent inside a balance-sheet-heavy REIT structure.
- The `2025` annual anchor is clean at the archive level. On Monday, August 10, 2026, AnnualReports already showed `2025 Annual Report and Form 10K` as the current package, unlike several lagging CLI 5 names.
- The strategic point is broader than towers alone. Management increasingly frames American Tower as a portfolio of communications towers plus highly interconnected data centers, positioned at the intersection of wireless, cloud, and AI.
- That matters for CLI 5 because American Tower shows how physical-network rent can still capture durable economics even when software-control, semicap, optics, and interconnect names are also strengthening. The tradeoff is that the model carries more leverage, capital intensity, regulation exposure, and customer concentration than the lighter control-point names.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` total revenue was `$2.749B`, total property revenue was `$2.688B`, net income was `$888M`, net income attributable to AMT common stockholders was `$868M`, Adjusted EBITDA was `$1.808B`, and AFFO attributable to AMT common stockholders was `$1.264B`.
- Operating read: this quarter is the clearest current proof that American Tower wants to be read as digital infrastructure rather than only as a tower REIT. Management highlighted robust leasing demand across the global tower portfolio, record leasing activity at CoreSite, and strong cloud and AI-driven application scaling.
- Capital and model signal: American Tower remains a landlord-style toll collector with real balance-sheet burden. The company still carried a `4.9x` net leverage ratio and approximately `$9.9B` of liquidity at June 30, 2026, which is very different from the lighter software-control names in CLI 5.
- Structural themes: the quarter shows that AI and network-density economics can accrue not only to chips, optics, and software governance, but also to the rent-bearing physical sites and data-center assets that have to host the resulting traffic and compute flows.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` total revenue was `$2.738B`, total property revenue was `$2.670B`, net income was `$879M`, net income attributable to AMT common stockholders was `$860M`, Adjusted EBITDA was `$1.835B`, and AFFO attributable to AMT common stockholders was `$1.324B`.
- Management message: Q1 is the clearest quarter for the company’s strategic repositioning language. Steve Vondran said American Tower was operating from its strongest strategic position in more than a decade, with a best-in-class portfolio of towers and interconnection-rich data centers.
- Business-model signal: this quarter shows the company trying to improve earnings quality and reduce risk while keeping exposure to secular data, cloud, and AI growth. It is a physical-infrastructure rent model that wants better predictability than a pure buildout story.
- Structural themes: the quarter shows communications-infrastructure rent staying durable when customer demand is driven by rising mobile data usage, cloud adoption, and AI workloads rather than only by traditional carrier expansion.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: American Tower is a specialized REIT and infrastructure landlord sitting inside CLI 5 as a physical-network and digital-infrastructure rent collector.
- Consumer: indirect, through mobile connectivity, wireless coverage, cloud use, and the data-center footprint underlying digital services.
- Cultural: AI adoption and always-on digital life are normalizing the need for more network density, more traffic handling, and more physical sites where equipment and compute can live.
- Regulatory: tower siting, zoning, international operations, REIT structure, interest rates, and foreign-exchange exposure remain central operating realities.
- Labor: the model is less field-service-heavy than telecom operators but much more asset- and financing-heavy than software-control names.
- Technology: multitenant tower real estate, communications sites, colocation-style tenancy, and CoreSite data-center interconnection are the main technical and commercial levers.
- Capital allocation: American Tower is one of the clearest CLI 5 examples of a balance-sheet-heavy toll. It can monetize durable digital dependence, but it must do so while managing leverage, liquidity, capital expenditures, and customer concentration.

## Working interpretation

- American Tower materially improves CLI 5 because it adds the landlord-style infrastructure rent layer that sits between burdened telecom access and the cleaner software-control and physical-chokepoint names. Compared with telecom operators, it avoids direct retail-customer ownership and some operating sprawl. Compared with software-control vendors, it carries much heavier balance-sheet and real-asset burden.
- Compared with Corning and Amphenol, American Tower is not selling enabling components or materials into the buildout. It is monetizing the right to occupy critical physical infrastructure once the buildout arrives. That makes it an important contrast case for where durable AI and connectivity economics settle after the equipment and materials are deployed.
- Compared with Digital Realty and Equinix-style digital-infrastructure landlords, American Tower still has more tower-centered identity, but its CoreSite language makes it clear that the company wants to participate in the same AI and cloud demand stack rather than being read only as a legacy wireless real-estate business.
- The packet also preserves a useful caution. American Tower’s economics are sturdy, but not clean in the same way as the best software-control names. Leverage around `4.9x`, dependence on carrier and large-customer demand, international FX exposure, and REIT payout discipline all keep the model more burdened than the highest-quality control points elsewhere in CLI 5.
- For the `2025` through `2026` window, American Tower is strong evidence that AI and digital-dependence economics can still support landlord-style rent capture, but with a much heavier capital, financing, and policy burden than the lighter software and hidden-technology chokepoint layers.
