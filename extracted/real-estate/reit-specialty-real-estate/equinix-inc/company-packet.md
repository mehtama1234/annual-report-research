## Structural themes

- Real estate is fragmenting: Equinix shows why the property universe cannot be treated as a simple office, retail, or housing cycle when network-dense data-center assets are compounding under AI and cloud demand
- Interconnection matters: this is not just powered-shell capacity; the moat is the ecosystem of customers, clouds, networks, and cross-connect density
- AI demand is showing up physically: Equinix gives the archive a concrete example of AI demand translating into recurring real-estate and infrastructure economics
- Guidance credibility improved through 2026: management raised the outlook twice in two quarters, which supports the broader theme that infrastructure beneficiaries are guiding with more conviction than challenged property categories
