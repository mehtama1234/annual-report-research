## What changed

- Revenue: Equinix moved from `$9.217B` for full-year 2025 to quarterly revenue of `$2.444B` in Q1 2026 and `$2.625B` in Q2 2026, with recurring revenue growth remaining double digit year over year in both 2026 quarters.
- Margin: operating income and adjusted EBITDA expanded sharply, with Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA at `$1.245B` and Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA at `$1.396B`, while operating income rose to `$577M` and then `$665M`.
- Guidance: management set a strong 2026 outlook in February 2026, raised it in April 2026, and raised it again in July 2026.
- Capital allocation: the company continues to fund large-scale digital-infrastructure expansion while using xScale economics, recurring capital discipline, and operating leverage to support AFFO growth.
- Segment mix: Equinix is nominally a REIT, but the economic engine is digital infrastructure, interconnection density, and ecosystem control rather than classic landlord-style rent collection alone.
- Operational issues: bookings, backlog, stabilized-asset revenue growth, and interconnection adds all point to durable demand from AI, cloud, and network workloads; one-time xScale fees also helped Q2 2026 results.
- Consumer / cultural signals: this is a low-direct-consumer company, but it is culturally important because it sits underneath the modern cloud, AI, and network stack that powers enterprise and platform behavior.
- Management tone: management is confident and increasingly explicit that Equinix is an infrastructure beneficiary of AI adoption, not just a passive data-center landlord.

## Open questions

- How much of the near-term acceleration is recurring operating strength versus timing-related xScale fees and transaction effects?
- Can Equinix keep the current level of bookings and interconnection momentum as hyperscaler, AI, and enterprise demand compete for power and capacity?
