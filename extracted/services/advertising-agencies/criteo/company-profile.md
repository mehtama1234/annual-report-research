## What changed in this window

- Criteo moved the ad-tech story closer to transaction surfaces by framing itself as a commerce media platform rather than just a retargeting or open-web ad intermediary.
- Retail media became both the most strategically important growth engine and the clearest concentration risk because changes at two large clients hit reported revenue and contribution ex-TAC across `Q4 2025`, `Q1 2026`, and `Q2 2026`.
- Management leaned hard into `agentic commerce`, recommendation services, audience agents, and AI-driven decisioning, signaling that shopping interfaces and conversational product discovery are becoming premium advertising real estate.
- The latest quarter added a concrete interface-distribution signal: Criteo said it became OpenAI's first advertising technology partner and had more than `2,000` brands advertising on ChatGPT across seven countries.
- Cash generation remained positive over the broader annual period even as the latest quarter showed weaker free cash flow, reinforcing that this is still an ad-infrastructure platform with meaningful operating leverage rather than a low-margin media property.

## Open questions

- How durable is the underlying retail-media growth engine once large-client scope resets normalize?
- Does AI-surface expansion create a durable new demand channel or mainly a short-cycle experimentation budget?
- Can Criteo defend independent open-internet and retailer positioning if closed ecosystems capture more shopping and discovery traffic?
