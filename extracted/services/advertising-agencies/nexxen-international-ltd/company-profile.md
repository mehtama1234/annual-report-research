## What changed in this window

- Nexxen's public-company story shifted further toward a unified programmatic and CTV control layer rather than a generic ad-tech vendor.
- Management repeatedly emphasized exclusive `V` / `VIDAA` ACR data, smart-TV home-screen activation, and enterprise DSP adoption as the company's differentiation points.
- The business mix kept moving away from non-core, non-programmatic activities and toward programmatic revenue, which reached `93%` of full-year `2025` revenue and `94%` in each covered quarter.
- The company leaned harder into AI not as a consumer narrative, but as workflow compression and buyer-efficiency infrastructure through `nexAI`, a redesigned DSP UI, and automation tooling.
- The baseline-date quarter chain matters because `Q2 2026` had not yet been reported by `2026-08-10`; Nexxen's own `2026-07-29` notice scheduled that release for `2026-08-12`.

## Open questions

- Can Nexxen turn its home-screen and ACR-data differentiation into a durable moat against larger platform and DSP incumbents?
- How much of the company's recent improvement reflects true enterprise-share gains versus exposure to a narrower set of strategic partnerships?
- Does AI strengthen Nexxen's positioning through workflow and data advantages, or does it intensify pricing and disintermediation pressure in the broader ad stack?
