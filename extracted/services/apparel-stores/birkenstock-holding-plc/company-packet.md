## Annual report takeaways

- Birkenstock is one of the clearest archive additions for the foot-health, premium-utility, and practical-wellness lane. The annual materials do not read like generic fashion retail. They read like a brand turning comfort, biomechanical function, and identity into a high-margin global system.
- AnnualReports.com still lagged here. On Monday, August 10, 2026, the live page still showed only a `2024 Annual Report`, so the current annual package had to come from Birkenstock's official IR and SEC chain.
- Fiscal `2025` revenue reached about `EUR 2.1B`, up `16%` reported and `18%` in constant currency, supported by `12%` unit growth and `5%` constant-currency average selling price growth.
- The closed-toe story matters. Management said closed-toe shoes outpaced sandals and increased to `38%` of total business, up about `500` basis points, which makes this more than a seasonal sandal brand.
- The strategic read is that Birkenstock sits at the intersection of wellness, comfort, minimalism, durability, and premium casualization. It is one of the archive's clearest proofs that body-awareness and practical utility can become a broad lifestyle purchase rather than a niche orthopedic one.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: `Q2 FY2026` revenue was `EUR 618M`, up `8%` reported and `14%` in constant currency; gross profit margin was `53.9%`; adjusted EBITDA was `EUR 198M`; and diluted EPS was `EUR 0.45`.
- Operating posture: Birkenstock explicitly framed the quarter around resilience despite war, tariffs, and inflation. The Middle East conflict reduced `EMEA` revenue by about `EUR 6M`, while `APAC` grew `22%` reported and `30%` in constant currency.
- Structural themes: this quarter matters because it shows consumers still paying for comfort-and-health-coded premium footwear even when the company is working through external shocks and gross-margin pressure.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `Q1 FY2026` revenue was `EUR 402M`, up `11.1%` reported and `17.8%` in constant currency; gross profit margin was `55.7%`; adjusted EBITDA was `EUR 106M`; and diluted EPS was `EUR 0.27`.
- Operating posture: management highlighted strong holiday demand for clogs, shearling executions, and other closed-toe shoes and boots. `B2B` revenue grew `18%` reported and `24%` in constant currency.
- Structural themes: this is the quarter that makes the wellness-and-utility read harder to dismiss. The growth was not only sandals. It came from colder-weather and more everyday-use silhouettes, which broadens the consumer behavior behind the brand.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: `Q4 FY2025` revenue was `EUR 526M`, up `15%` reported and `20%` in constant currency; gross profit margin was `58.1%`; and adjusted EBITDA margin was `27.8%`.
- Operating posture: `B2B` grew `22%` and `DTC` grew `8%`, while `APAC` revenue increased `33%` reported and `38%` in constant currency.
- Structural themes: the year-end quarter closes the loop on the annual story. The brand was still expanding globally, still pushing price, and still proving that a comfort-led product system can travel across regions and channels.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Birkenstock strengthens `Services` with another retail-like consumer operator that AnnualReports currently places under `Services / Apparel Stores`.
- Consumer: this is a read on a consumer willing to pay for utility, body alignment, comfort, and durability rather than only fashion novelty or visible status.
- Cultural: Birkenstock is a strong signal that health-coded products can become identity products. The brand sits inside a wider shift toward practical wellness, minimalist design, and premium casualization.
- Societal: proper foot health and physical comfort are no longer niche medical-adjacent concerns. They are mainstream branded consumption themes.
- Operating model: Birkenstock combines wholesale scale, direct-to-consumer reach, pricing power, and product extension beyond sandals into clogs, shoes, boots, sleep systems, and natural cosmetics.
- Competitive positioning: Birkenstock is most useful next to `Deckers Outdoor Corp.`, `lululemon athletica inc.`, `Tapestry, Inc.`, `Levi Strauss & Co.`, and `Urban Outfitters, Inc.`. It is less performance-coded than Deckers, less community-and-apparel-coded than lululemon, less visible-status-coded than Tapestry, and more utility-and-body-function-driven than the mall-sensitive apparel packets.

## Working interpretation

- Birkenstock materially improves the archive because it adds the missing foot-health and premium-utility footwear system after Deckers, lululemon, Tapestry, and the other apparel names.
- The most important evidence is that the annual and quarter chain shows several mechanisms working together: unit growth, pricing power, closed-toe expansion, wholesale and direct reach, and broad international demand despite tariffs, war, and inflation.
- For the `2025` through `2026` window, Birkenstock is one of the archive's clearest proofs that comfort, physical wellbeing, and practical utility are not niche consumer edges. They are culturally meaningful and economically strong brand systems.
