## What changed

- Gap adds the missing weaker mall-sensitive and multi-brand apparel repair case to the archive after Levi Strauss.
- The annual and quarter materials show a company whose improvement is real but uneven. Full-year `2025` net sales reached `$15.4B`, comparable sales increased `3%`, and operating margin reached `7.3%`, yet brand-level performance remains split.
- The packet is especially useful because it shows a large apparel portfolio where one brand is clearly reinvigorating, one remains the dependable value engine, one is improving modestly, and one is still structurally weak.
- That makes Gap a better bridge case between Levi, lululemon, Macy's, Kohl's, and Ross than another premium apparel packet would have been.

## Open questions

- Can Gap keep the core Gap brand momentum going long enough for it to matter at the portfolio level rather than only as the current bright spot?
- Is Old Navy durable enough to keep carrying the company if Athleta remains weak and Banana Republic improvement stays partial?
- Does Athleta need a deeper brand reset than the current reinvigoration framing implies?
