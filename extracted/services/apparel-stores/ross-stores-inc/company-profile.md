## What changed

- Ross adds the cleanest value-and-traffic-resilient retail-format case currently in the archive.
- The annual and quarter materials show an operator benefiting from customer traffic, strong merchandising, and off-price value positioning rather than from premium identity or ecosystem lock-in.
- The packet is especially useful because it is a counterweight to Kohl's. Ross is serving a similarly value-sensitive customer base, but its format is working far better: fiscal `2025` sales reached `$22.8B`, comparable store sales grew `5%`, and the business entered fiscal `2026` with significant momentum.
- Store scale also matters. At fiscal `2025` year-end, Ross operated `1,904` Ross Dress for Less locations and `363` dd's DISCOUNTS stores, which gives the archive a strong value-format operator with national density and a second banner serving a more moderate-price customer.

## Open questions

- How much of Ross's recent strength came from structural market-share gains versus a favorable short-term consumer setup such as tax-refund timing?
- Can Ross keep comp growth elevated if tariffs and sourcing pressures intensify again?
- Does the off-price model have more room to take share from weaker department-store and specialty formats than the current results already imply?
