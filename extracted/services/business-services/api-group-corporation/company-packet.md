# APi Group Corporation Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-08

## Snapshot

- Company: APi Group Corporation
- Sector: Services
- Industry: Business Services
- Ticker: APG
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- APi gives the Services sector a fourth company and adds a business-services and infrastructure-maintenance operator rather than another transport or consumer-facing service model.
- The `2025` annual package showed net revenues of `$7.911 billion`, reported net income of `$302 million`, adjusted net income of `$627 million`, adjusted diluted EPS of `$1.48`, adjusted EBITDA margin of `13.2%`, and adjusted free cash flow of `$836 million`.
- The annual filing matters because it makes the service model explicit: APi is built around safety, inspection, monitoring, and specialty infrastructure work, not around shipment volumes or household routine service alone.
- APi also matters for theme work because it adds a service business with a large recurring base, statutorily mandated activity, acquisition-led expansion, and strong backlog visibility.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 2026 net revenues were `$2.3 billion`, diluted EPS was `$0.20`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$0.44`, adjusted EBITDA was `$311 million`, and reported net income was `$99 million`.
- Guidance: on `July 30, 2026`, APi raised full-year `2026` guidance to net revenues of `$8.875 billion` to `$9.025 billion`, adjusted EBITDA of `$1.205 billion` to `$1.245 billion`, and adjusted free-cash-flow conversion of `115%`.
- Management message: management said inspection, service, and monitoring revenues remained strong, project activity stayed robust across both segments, and backlog had exceeded `$5 billion`.
- Structural themes: this quarter is the clearest proof that APi is not just a one-time-project story. The combination of recurring inspection and monitoring work plus record backlog makes it a high-visibility service name.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 2026 net revenues were `$2.0 billion`, diluted EPS was `$0.12`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$0.32`, adjusted EBITDA was `$235 million`, and reported net income was `$57 million`.
- Guidance: on `April 30, 2026`, APi raised full-year `2026` guidance to net revenues of `$8.475 billion` to `$8.675 billion` and adjusted EBITDA of `$1.150 billion` to `$1.210 billion`.
- Management message: management emphasized strength across both Safety Services and Specialty Services while also highlighting the CertaSite acquisition and signed deals for Wtech and Onyx.
- Structural themes: Q1 shows a service operator using recurring revenue, margin leverage, and acquisition deployment at the same time. This is more of a service-platform compounder than a simple cyclical contractor.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 2025 net revenues were `$2.117 billion`, reported net income was `$97 million`, adjusted EBITDA was `$295 million`, and adjusted diluted EPS was `$0.44`. Full-year `2025` net revenues were `$7.911 billion`.
- Guidance: on `February 25, 2026`, APi introduced `2026` guidance for net revenues of `$8.400 billion` to `$8.600 billion`, adjusted EBITDA of `$1.140 billion` to `$1.200 billion`, and adjusted free-cash-flow conversion of approximately `115%`.
- Management message: management described the company as a recurring-revenue, services-focused business that had completed its `13/60/80` targets and moved on to `10/16/60+`.
- Structural themes: this quarter closes `2025` with APi looking like a disciplined recurring-services platform with safety and compliance exposure, not like a one-off construction or capital-goods name.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: APi broadens Services into business services tied to safety, inspection, monitoring, and infrastructure-support activity.
- Consumer: consumer demand matters only indirectly here. The stronger lens is compliance intensity, facility maintenance, data-center and healthcare buildout, and critical-infrastructure spending.
- Cultural: the cultural layer is operating rigor, decentralized leadership, target discipline, and acquisition integration.
- Technology: the technology layer sits in monitoring, electronic security, service workflows, inspection cadence, and lifecycle service relationships rather than in consumer software.
- Labor: labor is central because the business depends on specialized technical field teams, branch execution, and integration of acquired service organizations.
- Capital allocation: APi combines strong cash generation with aggressive M&A deployment, which makes balance-sheet discipline and deal integration core service-model questions.
- Macro sensitivity: APi is a service indicator for compliance, infrastructure modernization, healthcare and data-center activity, and statutorily required safety spend rather than for consumer discretionary behavior.
