## What changed

- Target adds a cleaner middle case between Walmart's bigger ecosystem and Dollar General's more convenience-heavy discount model.
- The `2025` annual package shows a large but uneven mass retail operator: full-year net sales decreased `1.7%` to `$104.8B`, full-year operating income decreased `8.1%` to `$5.1B`, and full-year GAAP EPS was `$8.13`, while management leaned hard into merchandising, guest-experience, technology, and profit recovery for `2026`.
- The quarter trail is what makes the packet important now. `Q3 2025` was weak on the base retail demand line, with net sales down `1.5%` and comparable sales down `2.7%`, but the digital and non-merchandise layers were already moving in a better direction. `Q4 2025` kept core sales soft, yet membership revenue more than doubled from a year ago, Roundel kept growing at a double-digit rate, marketplace growth remained above `30%`, and same-day delivery powered by Target Circle 360 grew more than `30%`.
- `Q1 2026` then made the relationship-system argument much stronger: net sales increased `6.7%`, comparable sales increased `5.6%`, digital comparable sales increased `8.9%`, non-merchandise sales increased `24.6%`, and management called out strong growth in Roundel, Target Circle 360 membership revenue, and the Target+ marketplace.
- That makes Target useful for the archive because it is not only a value or basket retailer. It is trying to widen the same customer relationship through advertising, paid convenience, same-day fulfillment, and marketplace activity without having Amazon's scale or Walmart's global footprint.

## Open questions

- How much of Target's margin recovery depends on the higher-margin non-merchandise layers versus a cleaner core merchandising and inventory cycle?
- Can Target Circle 360 become a durable membership-and-utility interface, or is it mainly an attachment to same-day delivery and convenience?
- Does Target's growth in Roundel and Target+ represent a real platform widening, or only a partial catch-up to Walmart and Amazon?
