## What changed

- Walmart is a strong archive addition because it broadens the retail-media and customer-relationship theme beyond Kroger into mass retail at much larger scale.
- The annual filing states the business is expanding through `membership, advertising, marketplace and fulfillment services, and financial services`, and says these are `mutually reinforcing pieces of our omnichannel model`.
- The fiscal `2025` annual package reinforces the same direction in management language. Walmart said fiscal `2026` delivered revenue growth of `5.1%` in constant currency, adjusted profit growth of `5.4%`, and global eCommerce growth of `24%`.
- The quarter trail then shows the higher-margin layer compounding in real time. In the quarter ended `2025-10-31`, Walmart said it was expanding its ecosystem in higher margin areas such as digital advertising and marketplace.
- By the quarter ended `2026-04-30`, the company was saying growth in global platforms including advertising, membership and marketplace was improving mix and reinforcing long-term strategy.
- The most useful bridge to downstream theme work is that Walmart is not only selling more units through omnichannel retail. It is increasingly monetizing the relationship through advertising, membership, marketplace, and other ecosystem services that sit on top of the core basket.

## Open questions

- How much of Walmart's future margin improvement depends on advertising and marketplace mix versus base retail execution?
- Does Walmart Connect stay structurally advantaged because of first-party commerce data, or does it face the same measurement and privacy pressure as other retail-media systems?
- As the company pushes membership, marketplace, and advertising together, how much of the upside comes from genuine ecosystem strength versus the temporary benefit of a strong consumer-value cycle?
