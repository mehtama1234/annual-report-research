## What changed

- Aggregator gap: Disney is a good example of why the archive cannot stop at AnnualReports.com. As of `2026-08-09`, AnnualReports.com still showed `2024` as the most recent annual report, while Disney's official IR stack already exposed `2025` annual materials and `Q3 2026` quarterly materials.
- Consumer-system breadth: Disney expands the archive beyond retail and pure subscription models into a franchise-driven consumer system that combines streaming, sports, parks, theatrical releases, merchandise demand, and advertising.
- Membership logic: Disney's management commentary is directly relevant to the archive's recurring-relationship theme because it explicitly positions Disney+ as the digital centerpiece of the company and points toward a broader membership ecosystem.
- Cultural signal: Disney is one of the clearest names in the archive for linking culture to economics. The same IP library and fan attachment show up across streaming subscriptions, theatrical momentum, ESPN viewership, park attendance, and consumer products.
- Segment balance: the mix matters. Entertainment, Sports, and Experiences each carry different demand and margin characteristics, which makes Disney more useful as a multi-engine operating model than as a single-line media stock read.
- Physical plus digital: Disney is useful because it monetizes both screen time and physical presence. The archive now has another strong example where digital engagement and in-person demand reinforce one another rather than substituting cleanly.

## Open questions

- How durable is Disney's effort to turn Disney+ from a streaming product into a broader membership layer across media and experiences?
- Does the strongest economic leverage sit in subscription monetization, advertising, franchise content performance, parks pricing power, or cross-segment bundling?
- How much of Disney's current strength should be read as a broad consumer-cultural signal versus a company-specific IP and scale advantage that weaker media operators cannot replicate?
