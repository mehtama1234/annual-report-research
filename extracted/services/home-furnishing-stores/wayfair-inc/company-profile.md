## What changed

- Channel role: Wayfair gives the lane a scaled digital home platform rather than a showroom or curated-brand model.
- Operating system: CastleGate, supplier services, and the Wayfair Delivery Network make this packet as much about fulfillment and physical systems as about consumer taste.
- Demand read: Wayfair is a useful proof case that even a pressured home category can still produce share gains when the operator improves convenience, assortment, and repeat behavior.
- Cross-lane value: Wayfair keeps the lifestyle lane from collapsing into premium aspiration alone by showing the mass-scale logistics and marketplace side of home expression.

## Open questions

- How durable is the current share-gain story if category demand weakens again?
- Is Wayfair's real moat supplier intermediation, delivery density, or consumer habit formation?
- Compared with RH and Williams-Sonoma, does Wayfair earn its advantage more from infrastructure or from merchandising and brand architecture?
