## What changed

- Southwest is analytically useful because it is neither the Delta-style loyalty-currency case nor the United-style premium-network case. It is the clearest airline packet so far where the earnings story runs through a broad commercial reset: bag fees, basic economy, assigned seating, extra legroom seating, Rapid Rewards optimization, online distribution expansion, and free Wi-Fi for loyalty members.
- The `2025` annual and Q4 materials show management describing `2025` as the most ambitious transformation in company history. Full-year operating revenues reached a record `$28.1B`; full-year net income was `$441M`; adjusted net income was `$512M`; adjusted EPS was `$0.93`; and adjusted EBIT was `$574M`.
- The quarter trail matters because it shows the transformation working in sequence. Q1 `2026` produced record first-quarter revenue, sharp year-over-year margin improvement, higher buy-up from base product, and record managed-business performance. Q2 `2026` then added record operating revenue, all-time record managed-business revenue, near-`100 million` Rapid Rewards members, and stronger earnings despite almost `$900M` of higher fuel expense year over year.
- Southwest also adds a useful taxonomy lesson. AnnualReports.com still showed only `2024` as the latest annual package and classified Southwest as `Regional Airlines` on `2026-08-09`, while the live IR stack already exposed the `2025` annual report and the current `Q2 2026` quarter chain.

## Open questions

- How durable is the earnings improvement once the easy year-over-year lift from the commercial reset is absorbed?
- Does Southwest become a stronger long-run relationship business through Rapid Rewards, managed-business revenue, and product segmentation, or does it remain mainly a simpler domestic operator with a temporarily better revenue toolkit?
- How much of the new model depends on preserving the company's historical hospitality and convenience identity while simultaneously charging for elements that used to be embedded in the old product?
