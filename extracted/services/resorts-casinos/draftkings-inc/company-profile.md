## What changed in this period

- DraftKings turned `2025` into a proof year for scale and profitability:
  - revenue passed `$6.0 billion`
  - the company reported positive full-year GAAP net income
  - adjusted EBITDA reached roughly `$620 million`
- At the same time, management expanded the platform beyond Sportsbook and iGaming into:
  - `Jackpocket` lottery courier
  - `DraftKings Predictions`
  - a broader cross-sell and "Super App" framework
- By `Q2 2026`, the company was openly using core-cash-generation language to justify heavier investment into Predictions, even as customer-friendly outcomes and higher promotions hurt near-term revenue conversion.

## Why this name matters in the archive

- DraftKings adds a wagering and real-money participation layer to the recreation lane.
- It does not just monetize viewership or hospitality demand.
- It monetizes:
  - sports attention
  - live-event engagement
  - habit formation
  - cross-sell across multiple real-money products
  - state-by-state legalization and regulatory opening
- That makes it a strong comparison point for:
  - `Caesars` and `MGM` as casino-plus-digital ecosystems
  - `Playtika` as a different kind of repeat-spend real-money-adjacent habit system
  - the broader recreation lane's argument that participation demand is often most profitable when converted into a repeatable, software-mediated relationship
