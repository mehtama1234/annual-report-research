## What changed

- MGM strengthens the archive because it gives the gaming and destination lane a second operator next to Caesars rather than leaving the casino-and-resort argument anchored on one company.
- The annual packet sharpens the operator mix. This is not only a Las Vegas Strip story. The disclosures show four distinct economic surfaces inside one company: Las Vegas Strip Resorts, Regional Operations, MGM China, MGM Digital, plus the unconsolidated BetMGM North America venture.
- The `2025` annual and Q4 materials show a business that produced record topline results with mixed segment quality. Full-year `2025` consolidated net revenues were `$17.5B`, consolidated Adjusted EBITDA was `$2.4B`, Las Vegas Strip revenue was down `4%`, Regional Operations revenue was up `1%`, MGM China revenue was up `11%`, and MGM Digital revenue was up `19%`.
- The quarter trail reinforces the point. Q1 `2026` emphasized record first-quarter consolidated net revenues driven by MGM China, MGM Digital, and growth at the BetMGM North America venture. Q2 `2026` then delivered record consolidated revenue again, a second consecutive quarter of Las Vegas Strip year-over-year revenue growth, and all-time best Regional Operations same-store quarterly revenue.
- MGM is especially useful as a bridge between travel and gaming. It combines resort stays, casino activity, conventions, luxury offerings, regional repeat play, Macau exposure, and digital gaming exposure inside one destination-oriented platform.
- MGM also gives the archive another example of AnnualReports lag. As of `2026-08-09`, AnnualReports still showed only `2024` as the most recent annual package, while the official IR stack was already current at `2025` plus `Q2 2026`.

## Open questions

- How much of MGM's current resilience comes from diversified geography and product mix versus unusually strong Las Vegas and Macau asset quality?
- Does MGM Digital become strategically important mainly through LeoVegas and direct consolidated digital revenue, or mainly through the unconsolidated BetMGM venture keeping the customer relationship active off property?
- Compared with Caesars, is MGM better understood as a destination-portfolio operator with a digital extension, or as a true wallet-and-loyalty system in its own right?
