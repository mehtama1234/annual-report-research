# Chipotle Mexican Grill Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-30

## Snapshot

- Company: Chipotle Mexican Grill, Inc.
- Ticker: CMG
- Sector: Services
- Industry: Restaurants
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Annual report: FY2025
- Quarter window: Q2 2026, Q1 2026, Q4 2025

## Quarter signals

### Q2 2026

Revenue rose 9.3% to $3.35B. Comparable restaurant sales rose 2.2%. Transactions rose 1.0%. Average check rose 1.2%. Digital sales were 38.3% of food and beverage revenue. Restaurant-level margin fell to 25.2% from 27.4%. Chipotle opened 100 company-owned restaurants, including 80 with a Chipotlane.

### Q1 2026

Revenue rose 7.4% to $3.09B. Comparable restaurant sales rose 0.5%. Transactions rose 0.6%. Average check fell 0.1%. Digital sales were 38.6% of food and beverage revenue. Restaurant-level margin fell to 23.7% from 26.2%. Chipotle opened 49 company-owned restaurants, including 42 with a Chipotlane.

### Q4 and FY2025

FY2025 revenue was $11.93B. Comparable restaurant sales fell 1.7%, made up of a 2.9% transaction decline and a 1.2% average-check increase. Operating cash flow was $2.11B. Capital spending was $666.3M. Chipotle had 4,042 restaurants at year-end.

## First read

Chipotle's growth is coming from new stores and a return to positive transactions. The central risk is that the cost of serving each customer is rising faster than the benefit from higher sales. The company-owned model makes this visible in Chipotle's own margins rather than leaving the pressure mainly with franchisees.
