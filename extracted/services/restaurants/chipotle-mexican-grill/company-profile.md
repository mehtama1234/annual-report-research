# Chipotle Mexican Grill Company Profile

Date baseline: 2026-08-30

Chipotle owns and operates almost all of its restaurants. It sells burritos, bowls, tacos, quesadillas, and salads. Its revenue comes mainly from food sold in those restaurants.

At December 31, 2025, Chipotle had 4,042 restaurants. At June 30, 2026, it had 4,186 restaurants, including 4,074 in the United States and 112 international restaurants. It also had 15 international partner-operated restaurants.

The business depends on four things:

- customers coming often enough to fill each restaurant;
- food quality and a simple menu that customers trust;
- stores that serve orders quickly and accurately;
- new restaurants that earn enough to justify their construction cost.

The main comparison with McDonald's is ownership. McDonald's can collect rent and fees from franchisees. Chipotle carries more of the store costs itself, but it also keeps more of the store profit when a restaurant works well.
