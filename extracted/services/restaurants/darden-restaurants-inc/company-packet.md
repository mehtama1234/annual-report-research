# Darden Restaurants, Inc. Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-09-14  
Sector: Consumer services / restaurants  
Industry: Full-service restaurants  
Ticker: DRI  
Fiscal year-end: May

## Why this company matters

Darden closes the recreation cohort’s mature full-service direct-operator gap.
Unlike a restaurant franchisor, Darden owns and operates most of the system.
The company therefore gives a direct test of whether scale, brand portfolio,
menu execution, data, and service can produce common-owner cash after food,
labor, occupancy, maintenance, new restaurants, acquisitions, and corporate
costs.

## Filed and official evidence

- FY2025 sales increased `6.0%` to `$12.1B`; blended same-restaurant sales
  increased `2.0%`; adjusted diluted EPS from continuing operations increased
  `7.5%` to `$9.553`; adjusted EBITDA was `$2.0B`.
- FY2025 capital returned was about `$1.1B`, consisting of `$659M` of
  dividends and `$418M` of repurchases.
- Darden served more than `440M` guests in FY2025.
- The Chuy’s acquisition added `103` restaurants and more than `7,000`
  employees; Darden also added `25` net new restaurants in FY2025.
- In Q1 FY2026, total sales increased `10.4%` to `$3.0B`, driven by blended
  same-restaurant sales growth of `4.7%`, the Chuy’s acquisition, and `22` net
  new restaurants. Reported diluted EPS was `$2.19`; adjusted diluted EPS was
  `$1.97`; Darden repurchased `$183M` of stock.
- Q1 FY2026 same-restaurant sales were `5.9%` at Olive Garden, `5.5%` at
  LongHorn Steakhouse, `(0.2)%` in Fine Dining, and `3.3%` in Other Business.
- The FY2026 Form 10-K states that, at May 31, 2026, Darden owned and operated
  `2,202` restaurants and had `167` restaurants operated by independent third
  parties under development or franchise agreements, plus four contractually
  operated restaurants and one jointly owned restaurant.
- Continuing-operations operating cash flow was `$1,621.7M` in FY2024,
  `$1,707.0M` in FY2025, and `$1,853.1M` in FY2026. Land, buildings, and
  equipment purchases were `$601.2M`, `$644.6M`, and `$734.0M`; Chuy’s
  acquisition cash was `$613.7M` in FY2025.
- The FY2025 annual report explains that Darden considered strategic alternatives
  for Bahama Breeze and closed `15` underperforming locations, leaving `28`
  before the later portfolio actions. It also sold eight Olive Garden Canada
  restaurants and converted the relationship to franchising and area
  development.

## Open evidence questions

- revenue and same-restaurant sales by traffic, price, mix, and digital channel;
- restaurant-level margin after food, labor, occupancy, and marketing;
- maintenance and growth capital per new and existing restaurant;
- Chuy’s integration cost, unit economics, and return on invested capital;
- lease liabilities, debt, interest, and diluted share count;
- franchise royalties versus the economics of company-operated restaurants;
- whether cash conversion keeps pace with adjusted EBITDA and capital returns.
