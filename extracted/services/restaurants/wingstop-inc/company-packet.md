## Annual report takeaways

- The report describes Wingstop as a predominantly franchised, asset-light
  restaurant system: approximately 98% of restaurants were franchisee-owned at
  year-end, with 3,056 system-wide restaurants (pp. 7, 59).
- Fiscal 2025 system-wide sales were $5.343 billion, domestic AUV was about
  $2.0 million, domestic same-store sales declined 3.3%, and total revenue was
  $696.853 million (p. 35).
- Company-owned restaurant labor cost was $29.576 million, or 23.2% of
  company-owned restaurant sales, versus 23.6% in fiscal 2024 (p. 39). This is
  a company-owned scope measure, not a labor measure for the franchise system.
- The report identifies a digital platform, POS/restaurant-management systems,
  and Wingstop Smart Kitchen as operating infrastructure, while also stating
  that digital sales exceeded 60% (pp. 8, 10).
- The report identifies minimum-wage and benefit standards, supplier and
  distributor dependence, commodity prices, and franchisee financing as
  operating risks (pp. 11, 13, 15-16).

## Seven research themes

### Premium-value bifurcation

Reported evidence: the report describes a distinctive flavor proposition,
domestic AUV of approximately $2.0 million, and a 3.3% decline in domestic
same-store sales in fiscal 2025 (pp. 7-8, 35). Interpretation: the packet can
test value, menu, and traffic resilience, but it does not isolate price,
traffic, mix, income cohort, or affordability effects.

### Restaurant margin vise

Reported evidence: company-owned restaurant cost of sales was 75.4% of
company-owned restaurant sales in 2025; labor was 23.2%, food/beverage/
packaging was 36.8%, and other restaurant operating expenses were 17.9%
(p. 39). Interpretation: this is a bounded company-owned margin surface, not
an explanation of system-wide franchisee economics or a causal attribution to
any macro signal.

### Health-conscious consumption

The report describes wings, tenders, sandwiches, fries, and flavor variety,
and discusses food quality, health, sanitation, and nutrition-related
regulation (pp. 7, 11). It does not provide a dated measure of health-oriented
demand, nutrition mix, or a health-conscious consumer shift. The theme remains
contextual and partially absent.

### Labor and automation

Reported evidence: the report says minimum-wage and benefit increases have
raised or may raise labor costs, identifies difficulty hiring qualified
personnel, and describes POS, restaurant-management systems, digital ordering,
and Wingstop Smart Kitchen (pp. 10-11, 13). It does not quantify labor hours,
throughput, service time, or automation attribution.

### Supply-chain and geopolitical volatility

Reported evidence: Wingstop relies on one distributor with 23 geographically
diverse distribution centers, uses preferred suppliers, and identifies
commodity prices, distributor solvency, freight, labor relations, and supply
interruptions as risks (p. 16). The report does not establish a realized
geopolitical shock or its company-specific financial effect.

### Regulatory fragmentation

Reported evidence: the report describes federal, state, and local regulation
covering wages, overtime, workplace safety, sanitation, food safety, zoning,
licensing, nutrition disclosure, and franchise operations (pp. 11, 15).
These passages establish regulatory exposure, not a measured cost or outcome.

### Capital concentration and ownership

Reported evidence: the system is approximately 98% franchised, existing
franchisees opened more than 90% of franchised restaurants in 2024 and 2025,
and franchisee access to financing is a growth dependency (pp. 7-9, 13).
The packet does not establish that ownership concentration caused the sales or
margin outcomes.

## Open evidence boundary

The packet supplies source-owned, dated theme context and several bounded
operating observations. It does not supply like-period traffic decomposition,
franchisee labor hours, service throughput, causal automation evidence,
franchisee-level margin, or a falsifier for the broader signal-to-company
mechanisms. Those remain open in the observatory's LLM-judge handoff.
