## Annual report takeaways

- Etsy is a critical addition because it gives the lane a digital marketplace that still monetizes gifting, taste, ritual, and identity rather than commodity e-commerce.
- The `2025` annual report states that the Etsy marketplace had `86.5M` active buyers and `5.6M` active sellers at year-end, with `74%` of GMS from U.S. buyers and `26%` from buyers outside the U.S.
- The annual report also makes the cultural logic explicit. Etsy says it is a marketplace for unique and creative goods, built around unique items, search and discovery, authentic human connections, and a trusted brand. That is precisely the kind of societal and consumer pattern work the user asked to surface.
- The `10-K` specifically calls out cross-category purchase occasions such as gifting, holidays, and personal milestones including weddings as a key focus area. That makes Etsy a direct consumer-behavior comparator against Signet, RH, and hospitality names even though the model is digital.
- The pressure map is modern and important: cross-border shipping, tariffs, de minimis policy risk, AI-amplified fraud, seller trust, and the ability to keep matching buyers and sellers efficiently.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: the `Q2 2026` shareholder-letter chain remained local-source-complete through the SEC, and the company framed the quarter around growth potential, loyalty, AI-related initiatives, and restructuring-linked execution rather than a simple gross volume update.
- Structural themes: this period matters because Etsy is increasingly trying to improve discovery, trust, and relevance at the same time it modernizes the platform for AI-assisted shopping and seller operations. That keeps the packet aligned with the user's request for bigger-picture patterns rather than just historical numbers.
- Operating read: the current quarter looks less like a static marketplace report and more like a platform-transition period centered on loyalty, AI use cases, and tighter execution.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `Q1 2026` is likewise captured through a shareholder-letter structure, with management emphasizing momentum in customer-centric priorities and the expected future impact of strategic investments.
- Structural themes: this quarter reinforces that Etsy's management is trying to shift the marketplace from stabilization into renewed relevance by improving discovery, trust, and repeat engagement rather than by chasing low-quality volume.
- Operating read: it is a useful bridge between the cleaner numerical `Q4 2025` release and the more forward-looking `Q2 2026` letter.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: `Q4 2025` consolidated GMS was `$3.59B`, consolidated revenue was `$881.6M`, take rate was `24.5%`, non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA was `$222.5M`, and diluted EPS was `$0.92`.
- Marketplace posture: Etsy marketplace GMS was `$3.29B`, active buyers were `86.5M`, active sellers were `5.6M`, app GMS grew `6.6%`, habitual buyers were `5.9M`, and Etsy said Home and Living grew year over year.
- Structural themes: this is the anchor quarter because it shows Etsy turning back toward growth by leaning into gifting, home, search relevance, owned marketing channels, and AI-powered shopping integrations. The release also explicitly describes AI-agent commerce with Google, Microsoft, Stripe, and ChatGPT-linked checkout experiences.

### Quarter minus 3 for continuity

- Results: `Q3 2025` consolidated GMS was `$2.72B`, revenue was `$678.0M`, take rate was `24.9%`, net income was `$75.5M`, and non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA was `$171.9M`.
- Structural themes: management said Etsy and Depop combined returned to year-over-year GMS growth, tying that improvement to customer-centric priorities, AI-powered matching, better retention and reward structures, and stronger discovery.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Consumer trend: Etsy is a clean filed read on consumers still paying for personalized goods, gifts, wedding-adjacent purchases, home-expression categories, and objects that carry social meaning rather than pure utility.
- Cultural and societal shift: the platform sits directly inside the archive's themes of authenticity, creative labor, individuality, and digitally mediated participation. Etsy monetizes the desire to buy from people, not only from brands or warehouses.
- Industrial and operating pressure: marketplace trust, fraud prevention, shipping reliability, tariff exposure, international trade rules, and AI-enabled discovery all show up as real operating constraints.
- Bigger-picture pattern: Etsy demonstrates that consumer demand for differentiated and meaning-rich goods remains durable, but the winning platform has to solve matching, trust, and relevance better every year.
- Technology and interface signal: the AI shopping partnerships and agentic checkout language make Etsy one of the clearer examples of how commerce interfaces may shift from destination sites toward intent-capture inside AI assistants and search layers.

## Working interpretation

- Etsy is one of the best packets in the repo for showing that cultural and consumer-trend analysis is not optional decoration. It is the business model.
- The company is monetizing specialness, personalization, gifting, milestones, and human connection while also adapting to AI-mediated commerce and the logistics realities of cross-border small-seller trade.
- Inside the recreation / lifestyle lane, Etsy functions as the digital marketplace version of ritual and identity spending: less destination travel, more meaning-rich purchase occasions and taste participation.
