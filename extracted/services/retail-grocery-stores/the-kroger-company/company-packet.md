## Annual report takeaways

- Kroger is a high-priority archive addition because it gives the research base a direct grocery operator where the loyalty-data and retail-media story is stated plainly rather than inferred.
- The `2025` annual filing is unusually useful. Kroger says `Kroger Precision Marketing, our retail media business, leverages our rich first-party data and deep customer relationships` to provide targeted, measurable advertising solutions.
- The filing goes beyond generic ad language. Kroger says the platform uses shopping behaviors from millions of loyal households and can reach customers across digital and in-store channels including on-site search, display, social media, connected TV, and in-store placements.
- Kroger also connects the ad layer to the wider operating model. In the `10-K`, management highlights growth of alternative profit businesses and says the company is trying to generate customer loyalty and sustainable growth through Fresh, Our Brands, Personalization, and eCommerce.
- The hard year-end numbers support the structure. Fiscal `2025` delivered more than `$16B` in eCommerce sales and `$1.5B` in operating profit from alternative profit businesses.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q1 `2026` operating profit was `$1.407B`; adjusted FIFO operating profit was `$1.544B`; and adjusted EPS was `$1.58`.
- Operating posture: total company sales were `$46.1B`; adjusted eCommerce sales grew `19%`; and Kroger Precision Marketing profit grew over `20%`.
- Structural themes: this is the key quarter for the page because it directly shows the grocery basket and the advertising / data layer compounding at the same time. The page can now say something precise: Kroger is not only selling groceries, it is monetizing the customer relationship around the basket.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q4 `2025` operating profit was `$1.246B`; adjusted FIFO operating profit was `$1.206B`; and adjusted EPS was `$1.28`.
- Operating posture: fiscal `2025` delivered more than `$16B` in eCommerce sales, `$1.5B` in operating profit from alternative profit businesses, and strong adjusted free cash flow.
- Structural themes: this quarter matters because it closes the year with Kroger showing that the second profit layer is not a rounding error. Alternative profit businesses are large enough to be called out as a fiscal-year highlight.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q3 `2025` total company sales were `$33.9B`; adjusted FIFO operating profit was `$1.089B`; and adjusted EPS was `$1.05`.
- Operating posture: eCommerce sales increased `17%`; excluding fuel and Kroger Specialty Pharmacy, sales increased `2.6%`; and gross margin improved to `22.8%`.
- Structural themes: this quarter is useful because it shows the base grocery engine and the digital layer both moving in the right direction before the stronger full-year and `Q1 2026` retail-media proof arrives.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Kroger gives the archive a direct grocery operator where the customer relationship is monetized through both food retail and an attached media / measurement business.
- Consumer: this is a useful read on how routine household shopping, loyalty enrollment, and personalization can turn an everyday basket into an advertising and data asset.
- Cultural: Kroger matters because habitual grocery shopping is one of the highest-frequency consumer behaviors in the economy. That makes the relationship unusually valuable if the operator can measure it cleanly.
- Loyalty and technology: Kroger is one of the clearest filing-backed examples that first-party retail data can become a sellable ad product without leaving the core commerce loop.
- Business model: the most important business-model signal is that Kroger is using the grocery relationship to build alternative profit businesses that subsidize and strengthen the core retail model.
- Competitive positioning: Kroger is especially useful because the filings show that retail media is not only a speculative future option. It is already a named business line with measurable growth.

## Working interpretation

- Kroger materially improves the archive because it lets the broader strategy work say something more precise than "retailers can monetize data." The company discloses a named retail-media business, explains how it works, and ties it to loyal-household behavior.
- The most important evidence here is not a valuation analogy. It is Kroger's own language that Kroger Precision Marketing uses first-party data and deep customer relationships, plus the company’s year-end disclosure of `$1.5B` in operating profit from alternative profit businesses.
- For the `2025` through `2026` window, Kroger is a strong archive addition for understanding how a grocer can turn first-party data, personalization, and eCommerce into a second profit layer beyond the grocery basket itself.
