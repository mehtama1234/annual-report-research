## What changed

- Kroger is the archive addition that finally makes the grocery-and-retail-media page defensible with direct filings instead of general intuition.
- The `2025` annual filing is explicit. Kroger says `Kroger Precision Marketing, our retail media business, leverages our rich first-party data and deep customer relationships` to provide targeted, measurable advertising solutions.
- The same `10-K` says Kroger Precision Marketing uses the shopping behaviors of millions of loyal households to reach customers across on-site search, display, social media, connected TV, and in-store placements.
- Kroger also folds the idea into the broader business model. The annual filing flags growth in alternative profit businesses and says Kroger is trying to generate loyalty and sustainable growth through Fresh, Our Brands, Personalization, and eCommerce.
- The quarter trail shows the ad layer is not just narrative. In `Q1 2026`, adjusted eCommerce sales grew `19%` and Kroger Precision Marketing profit grew over `20%`.
- The year-close release makes the scale clearer. Fiscal `2025` delivered more than `$16B` in eCommerce sales and `$1.5B` in operating profit from alternative profit businesses.

## Open questions

- How much of Kroger's future margin expansion depends on retail media and other alternative profit businesses versus core grocery execution?
- Does Kroger Precision Marketing stay a genuine second profit layer, or does it become more cyclical if consumer-packaged-goods marketing budgets weaken?
- The filings clearly support the data-and-ad thesis, but how much can Kroger lean into monetization before it risks weakening customer trust or regulatory comfort?
