## What changed

- Ulta adds a much stronger beauty-specialty operator to the archive than the weaker repair cases already present around department stores and specialty brands.
- The annual and quarter materials show a beauty-discovery system rather than just a cosmetics retailer. The company combines stores, salons, app and e-commerce usage, loyalty, marketplace assortment, ad-media capabilities, and category-adjacent wellness expansion.
- The `2025` annual filing makes that widening explicit. UB Marketplace offered more than `200` brands and `5,000` SKUs at fiscal `2025` year-end, while the company also described `UB Media` as a new monetization layer built from loyalty-member data and brand-partner advertising demand.
- International expansion also became more concrete in the window. The `10-K` shows the `2025-07-10` Space NK acquisition, and as of `2026-01-31` the company operated `84` Space NK stores in the U.K. and `2` in Ireland, plus `9` stores in Mexico through a joint venture and two Middle East franchise locations.
- That makes Ulta a useful bridge case between `Bath & Body Works`, `Kohl's` plus Sephora adjacency, and broader wellness or beauty packets elsewhere in the archive. It is more discovery-led, more category-broad, and more platformized than a typical mall specialty chain.

## Open questions

- How durable is Ulta's recent growth once the Space NK benefit normalizes and beauty category growth becomes less supportive?
- Can UB Marketplace and UB Media become material second-layer economics, or do stores, salons, and core product demand still dominate the model?
- Does Ulta belong analytically with specialty retail, wellness, and identity-led consumer systems more than with generic `Services` taxonomy?
