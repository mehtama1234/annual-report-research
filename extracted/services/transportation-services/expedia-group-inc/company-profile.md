## What changed

- Services mix: Expedia gives Services a second travel-intermediary packet beside Booking, but with a different strategic surface centered on a large B2B travel business, an owned advertising network, and the One Key loyalty layer spanning Expedia, Hotels.com, and Vrbo.
- Business model: the value is not in owning travel assets. It sits in trip discovery, booking flow, merchant and agency mix, B2B distribution, advertising inventory, and first-party traveler data.
- Travel-platform read-through: the annual and quarter materials show a business growing through room nights, ADR, B2B expansion, and lodging-led marketplace scale rather than through airline seat ownership or branded-hotel unit growth.
- Interface logic: Expedia is one of the repo's cleaner examples of a travel marketplace trying to turn planning, booking, loyalty, advertising, and partner distribution into one relationship system.
- Taxonomy exception: AnnualReports left sector and industry blank and still lagged at the `2024` annual package on `2026-08-09`, so the archive uses the SEC's `Transportation Services` description for stable placement.

## Open questions

- Is Expedia's strongest long-term edge the consumer-brand and loyalty surface, the B2B distribution layer, or the advertising and data flywheel that sits on top of both?
- How defensible is One Key as a cross-brand loyalty and wallet layer relative to hotel programs, airline miles systems, or Booking's more behavior-led Genius and direct-app stack?
- If travel demand softens, does Expedia hold up better than travel operators because it sits in the marketplace layer, or does pressure simply move into marketing efficiency, B2B mix, and take-rate dynamics?
