## What changed in this period

- AppLovin finished the strategic turn away from direct game ownership after selling its Apps Business to Tripledot on `2025-06-30`.
- The company now presents itself as a single-segment advertising platform centered on `Axon Ads Manager`, `MAX`, `Adjust`, and `Wurl`.
- Management is pushing beyond mobile gaming into web-based e-commerce while arguing that `Axon AI` is the core compounding advantage.
- Reported growth stayed extremely strong through `Q4 2025`, `Q1 2026`, and `Q2 2026`, while cash generation funded large share repurchases.

## Why this name matters in the archive

- AppLovin is not primarily monetizing owned consumer worlds.
- It is monetizing the recommendation, auction, measurement, and distribution layer that sits between advertisers, publishers, and users.
- That makes it a strong comparison point for:
  - Unity's creator and monetization tooling stack
  - Roblox's participation economy
  - game and toy companies that own the worlds but not the ad-market control point
