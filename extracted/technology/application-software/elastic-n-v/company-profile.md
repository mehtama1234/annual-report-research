## What changed

- Elastic gives CLI 5 a lighter software-control layer than NetApp, towers, telecom, or most physical chokepoints. It monetizes the retrieval, observability, and security context that enterprises need before AI-assisted work can be trusted.
- The fiscal `2026` annual filing shows a company with strong gross economics but still transitional GAAP operating leverage. Revenue reached `$1.739B`, gross profit reached `$1.323B`, operating loss was about `-$33.5M`, net income reached `$367.8M`, operating cash flow reached `$326.9M`, cash and cash equivalents ended at `$768.7M`, and remaining performance obligations reached roughly `$1.982B`.
- The reported mix also matters. Subscription revenue dominated the model while professional services remained a smaller support layer. That makes Elastic cleaner than infrastructure-heavy operators, but still dependent on platform adoption, cloud usage, and enterprise standardization rather than pure seat-based governance.
- The quarter chain tightened the same pattern. `Q1 FY2026`, `Q2 FY2026`, and `Q3 FY2026` all showed mid-to-high teens revenue growth, faster subscription growth, rising cloud mix, and steady current RPO growth while management leaned harder into AI, unstructured data, and enterprise context engineering.
- The bigger lane implication is that Elastic sits above governed data storage and below end-user application workflow ownership. It monetizes the enterprise need to make fragmented machine data, logs, documents, and signals searchable, observable, and securable enough for AI-era operations.
