## Annual report takeaways

- PubMatic is one of the strongest remaining attention-and-influence expansions because it explains how the open internet is trying to remain economically relevant after advertising budgets fragment across CTV, mobile app, retail-media adjacency, and AI-mediated workflows.
- The company is not a publisher, broadcaster, or measurement vendor. It sits in the transaction layer between buyers and publishers, which makes it a useful complement to `The New York Times Company`, `Fox Corporation`, `Comscore`, and `Magnite`.
- That positioning matters for the frontier because the archive is not only asking who owns attention. It is also asking who gets paid to organize, route, and optimize attention once audiences are already fragmented.
- PubMatic's `2025` chain is especially valuable because management repeatedly frames the business around independent, AI-powered, open-internet infrastructure. The recurring claim is that value is shifting toward transparent, performant execution across fragmented surfaces rather than toward one dominant legacy demand path.
- The packet materially strengthens the lane because it shows the ad market trying to rebuild scale on top of fragmentation. Even when audiences scatter, buyers still need workflow, curation, measurement-ready inventory, and supply-path efficiency.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` revenue was `$78.6M`, up `11%`; GAAP net loss was `$(1.2)M`; non-GAAP net income was `$5.9M`; adjusted EBITDA was `$19.6M` with a `25%` margin; operating cash flow was `$20.2M`, up `36%`; and free cash flow was `$13.7M`, up `47%`.
- Mix signals: CTV, mobile app, and emerging revenues represented about `60%` of total revenue; CTV represented about `20%`; mobile app was about `25%`; and emerging revenues were about `15%`.
- Adoption signals: since launching in `January 2026`, PubMatic had delivered more than `80` fully autonomous end-to-end campaigns globally and more than `4,000` AI-powered deals to date.
- Market-structure signals: ad spend from mid-market focused DSPs grew over `25%` year over year; Supply Path Optimization remained above `55%` of platform activity; and nearly `92T` impressions were processed in the quarter.
- Interpretation: this is the clearest quarter in the chain for seeing the business after its mix shift. PubMatic is no longer best read as a generic programmatic intermediary. It is being repositioned around AI-enabled execution, diversified demand paths, and a stronger mobile plus CTV revenue base.
- Bigger-picture read: the quarter is frontier-significant because it shows that fragmented media does not only reward content owners. It can also reward the operator that makes fragmented supply more automatable, measurable, and performance-oriented.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` revenue was `$62.6M` versus `$63.8M` a year earlier; GAAP net loss was `$(12.5)M`; non-GAAP net loss was `$(5.4)M`; and management said results exceeded guidance on both revenue and profitability.
- Underlying-growth signal: excluding the legacy DSP referenced in mid-`2025`, the underlying business grew `13%` year over year and still represented `83%` of total revenues.
- Mix and adoption signals: more than `1,000` AI-powered deals had already transacted on AgenticOS; more than `20` AI agents were embedded across the platform; mobile app revenue grew `25%+`; emerging revenues grew `80%+` and represented about `14%` of total revenue.
- Infrastructure signal: Supply Path Optimization represented over `56%` of total activity and nearly `94.2T` impressions were processed, up `26%`.
- Interpretation: Q1 is the bridge quarter where PubMatic argues that reported flatness is masking a deeper remaking of the business. The company is trying to persuade investors that older demand concentration is being replaced by a broader, more AI-shaped open-internet revenue model.
- Bigger-picture read: this quarter is valuable because it separates cyclical and relationship-specific drag from structural change. The key frontier theme is that the open internet can still grow, but only if its infrastructure providers diversify demand and reduce workflow friction fast enough.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: FY `2025` revenue was `$282.9M` compared with `$291.3M` in `2024`; FY gross profit was `$179.8M` with a `64%` margin; GAAP net loss was `$(14.5)M`; non-GAAP net income was `$16.7M`; operating cash flow was `$81.1M`, up `10%`; and free cash flow was `$46.2M`, up `32%`.
- Q4 `2025` results: revenue was `$80.0M`; GAAP net income was `$6.7M`; non-GAAP net income was `$14.4M`; and management said adjusted EBITDA margin reached `35%`.
- Capital-allocation signal: PubMatic repurchased `4.1M` shares in `2025`; through `December 31, 2025` it had repurchased `12.4M` shares for `$181.1M`; and by `June 30, 2026` the cumulative program had reached `15.5M` shares for `$211.4M`.
- Strategic signal: management called Q4 an important structural inflection point and said that excluding political revenues and the legacy DSP referenced in mid-`2025`, the underlying `83%` of the business grew `18%`.
- Mix and platform signals: FY `2025` CTV revenue grew `50%+` excluding political; omnichannel video excluding political grew `17%` and represented `39%` of total revenue; emerging revenues nearly doubled and were about `10%` of total revenue; Supply Path Optimization exceeded `55%` of total activity; almost `336.8T` impressions were processed in `2025`; and over `40%` of new engineering code in the second half of `2025` was written by AI.
- Interpretation: Q4/FY2025 is the key reset quarter. The reported annual revenue decline matters less than the company's claim that its faster-growth surfaces are already large enough to reshape the model.
- Bigger-picture read: this quarter shows a frontier-wide pattern clearly. Attention infrastructure operators increasingly want investors to look through legacy channels and evaluate them on normalized mix, workflow automation, and their ability to capture budget migrating toward streaming, app-based, and AI-assisted formats.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Fragmented attention still recentralizes through infrastructure: PubMatic shows that dispersed inventory can still produce scale economics if the execution layer becomes efficient enough.
- The open internet is defending itself through automation and transparency: the core commercial pitch is not exclusive content or walled-garden identity. It is performance, control, and interoperable access.
- AI is already part of the monetization stack: AgenticOS, AI-powered deals, AI solutions, and AI-assisted engineering all show AI moving from narrative layer into operating layer.
- Media fragmentation is splitting the ad stack into narrower specialists: `Comscore` measures fragmented audiences, `Magnite` optimizes sell-side supply and CTV routing, and `PubMatic` adds a second read on how open-internet monetization depends on supply-path efficiency, demand diversification, and AI-native execution.
- Demand quality matters more than gross volume: management repeatedly distinguishes underlying growth from political spending distortions and from the pressured legacy DSP relationship, which is a useful reminder that not all revenue in the ad stack carries the same strategic quality.
- CTV, mobile app, and emerging revenues are now the real growth engine: by Q2 `2026`, these streams reached about `60%` of revenue, roughly double the level of three years earlier.
- Frontier fit: PubMatic materially improves the archive because it deepens the attention lane without duplicating an existing packet. It is not just another agency or media owner; it is a second strong infrastructure proof point for how fragmented attention gets turned back into cash flow.

## Working interpretation

- PubMatic materially strengthens the frontier because it adds a second strong read on open-internet ad-tech infrastructure without simply repeating Magnite's specific angle.
- The recurring pattern is that fragmented media is becoming more automatable, not less important. As attention disperses, execution infrastructure becomes more valuable if it can simplify buying and preserve transparency.
- The social and industrial pattern is that the advertising market is shifting from broad reach logic toward performance-and-workflow logic. CTV, mobile app, and AI-mediated buying sit at the center of that transition.
- PubMatic also sharpens a useful archive distinction: some media operators monetize trust in content, some monetize trust in measurement, and some monetize trust in execution. The fragmented-attention economy increasingly needs all three.
