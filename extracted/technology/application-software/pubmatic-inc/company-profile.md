## What changed

- Open-internet transaction infrastructure: PubMatic gives the frontier a second strong sell-side ad-tech and fragmented-media monetization packet alongside `Magnite`, but with more direct emphasis on AI-native buying and emerging revenue streams.
- Buy-side diversification: the company repeatedly frames its progress around reducing dependence on legacy DSPs, adding new DSP partners, expanding mid-market demand, and pushing Supply Path Optimization deeper into its activity mix.
- CTV and mobile app mix shift: the quarter chain shows a business being remade around CTV, mobile app, and emerging revenue streams rather than broad undifferentiated digital advertising.
- AI as operating and commercial infrastructure: AgenticOS, AI-powered deals, new AI solutions, and AI-assisted engineering output are all framed as current economic levers rather than distant innovation claims.
- Measurement and transparency logic: PubMatic's emphasis on transparency, control, audience curation, and supply-path logic makes it a strong complement to both `Comscore` and `Magnite` in the fragmented-attention stack.
- Political and buyer-concentration normalization: management repeatedly normalizes results for political revenue and a pressured legacy DSP relationship, which is useful for distinguishing cyclical noise from underlying open-internet demand.

## Open questions

- Can PubMatic keep reducing dependency on legacy DSP concentration fast enough to preserve durable double-digit growth?
- Does agentic advertising become a real scaled economic shift, or does it remain a high-signal but still limited workflow layer?
- How defensible is the company's independent open-internet position if larger buy-side and walled-garden platforms internalize more optimization and data flows?
- Are AI, CTV, mobile app, and emerging revenue streams enough to offset the structural pressure inside older parts of the programmatic ecosystem?
