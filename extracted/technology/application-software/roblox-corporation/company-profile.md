## What changed

- Roblox is a strong archive addition because it turns the play-and-fandom lane from physical-IP comparison only into a physical-plus-digital participation system.
- The filings and shareholder letters make clear that Roblox is not mainly a software-seat or classic app company. Its economics turn on creator supply, virtual-currency bookings, engagement intensity, discovery, safety systems, and expanding monetization layers.
- The useful comparison is not only against other gaming names. It is also against `Hasbro`, `Mattel`, `Spotify`, `Netflix`, and `Airbnb`, because each shows a different kind of recurring participation or interface ownership system.
- The main taxonomy lesson is direct: `Technology / Application Software` is materially weaker than the operating-reality read of creator platform, digital world, and engagement marketplace.

## Open questions

- Can Roblox keep expanding monetization while also increasing age-check friction and safety gating?
- How durable is the shift from a gaming label toward a broader communication, commerce, and advertising platform?
- Will discovery, AI-assisted creation, and creator incentives widen the moat, or mainly increase content supply without improving monetization quality?
