## What changed

- Samsara adds a distinct CLI 5 category: software control over physical operations rather than software control over pure office workflows or pure cyber trust.
- The annual filing repeatedly frames the company as a `Connected Operations Platform` for complex physical operations, not as a generic enterprise SaaS vendor.
- The latest three reported quarters show consistent ARR expansion, improving profitability, and rising free-cash-flow generation while management leans harder into Operational AI and agent-driven automation.
- The packet widens CLI 5 beyond cloud, security, semicap, storage, and network bottlenecks into edge-generated machine data, fleet and site telemetry, and workflow automation inside the physical economy.

## Open questions

- How durable is Samsara’s workflow-control advantage if large industrial, logistics, or telematics incumbents keep adding AI layers on top of existing installed bases?
- Does Samsara belong more with enterprise workflow-control names like ServiceNow, with edge-data creators, or as its own bridge between software governance and physical operations infrastructure?
- How much of the long-term profit pool sits in the device-linked data creation layer versus the higher-level workflow and agent layer that acts on that data?
