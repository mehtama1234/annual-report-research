## What changed

- Unity matters because it pushes the play-and-participation lane away from owned worlds and toward creation infrastructure. Instead of monetizing a single canon, Unity monetizes the tools, monetization rails, and runtime-adjacent systems that other creators use to build and operate worlds.
- The current Unity story is not generic software growth. It is a deliberate portfolio reset toward what the company calls its strategic portfolio: the Unity engine and related Create services, plus monetization products increasingly centered on the Unity Ad Network and Vector.
- The `2025` to `Q2 2026` chain shows a business moving from messy restructuring toward a cleaner platform thesis. `Q4 2025` highlighted strong Vector growth and the fastest Unity 6 adoption the company had seen. `Q1 2026` then absorbed major impairment charges tied to the ironSource sunset and Supersonic exit. `Q2 2026` showed the operating model becoming clearer again, with materially stronger revenue, EBITDA, and cash generation.
- Unity also adds a strong AI-and-infrastructure signal. The company increasingly frames Vector, Unity AI, browser-accessible workflows, and future engine releases as part of one infrastructure agenda rather than side experiments. That makes Unity different from Roblox: Roblox owns a participation economy directly, while Unity sells the creation, deployment, and monetization stack to many studios operating outside a Unity-owned social graph.
- The archive value is that Unity widens the lane from “who owns the world?” to “who supplies the indispensable creation and growth layer underneath many worlds?” That is a more infrastructural, less culturally visible, but still deeply strategic place in the same participation economy.

## Open questions

- Can Unity sustain the recent acceleration once the easy mix benefits from the non-strategic portfolio exit have fully lapped?
- Does Vector become a durable moat in growth and monetization infrastructure, or does it mainly reset Unity back to competitiveness after earlier product missteps?
- Relative to Roblox, is Unity better understood as a toll collector on creator production rather than creator participation, and how much weaker or stronger are those economics over time?
