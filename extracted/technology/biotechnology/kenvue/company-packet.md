## Annual report takeaways

- Kenvue is the right next packet because it adds the consumer-health and everyday-care layer that sits between household staples, personal care, oral care, and wellness-oriented routine use.
- AnnualReports makes the packet analytically useful before the financials even start. As of `2026-08-10`, the site still only exposed a `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K` package and classified Kenvue as `Technology / Biotechnology`, even though the business is plainly a global consumer-health platform.
- The official investor-relations overview gives the scale of the business clearly enough to make the operating reality obvious: `2025` net sales were `$15.1bn` across `3` segments, about `50%` of net sales were outside North America, the company operated in more than `165` countries, and it served about `1.2 billion` people globally.
- The FY`2025` and Q4 `2025` results chain shows a company that ended a weak year on a better quarter. Full-year net sales decreased `2.1%`, organic sales declined `2.2%`, diluted EPS was `$0.76`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.08`, net cash flows from operating activities were `$2.2bn`, and free cash flow was `$1.7bn`. But Q4 itself returned to net sales growth of `3.2%` and organic growth of `1.2%`.
- The management frame at year-end was not simple category momentum. It was execution improvement plus a transaction backdrop. Kenvue said the quarter reflected disciplined execution and easier comparisons while it worked toward completing its value-creating combination with Kimberly-Clark.
- This makes Kenvue valuable to the broader archive because it shows that trusted daily-use health and care categories can still be resilient, but the earnings story is being shaped by seasonality, retailer inventory behavior, category incidence rates, innovation execution, and corporate transformation rather than by effortless staples-like compounding.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` net sales increased `3.0%`, organic sales increased `1.6%`, diluted EPS rose to `$0.24`, and adjusted diluted EPS rose to `$0.31`.
- Cash flow and balance sheet: first-half `2026` net cash flows from operating activities were `$1.2bn`, free cash flow was `$1.0bn`, cash and cash equivalents were `$1.1bn`, and total debt was `$8.5bn`.
- Management message: management said Kenvue delivered a third consecutive quarter of net and organic sales growth, with broad-based gains across every segment and region, while continuing to drive operational efficiencies and strategic investment.
- Guidance: due to the pending Kimberly-Clark transaction, Kenvue again said it would not provide forward-looking guidance.
- Structural read: Q2 is the cleanest proof that Kenvue belongs in the cross-sector consumer-interface work. This is not a software story even if source taxonomy says `Technology`. It is a trust-and-routine-use business where allergy, pain, wound care, oral care, baby care, and skin-health demand still depend on brand strength, retail execution, and eCommerce momentum.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` net sales increased `4.5%`, organic sales increased `0.7%`, diluted EPS increased to `$0.25`, and adjusted diluted EPS increased to `$0.32`.
- Cash flow: first-quarter net cash flows from operating activities were `$0.5bn`, and free cash flow was `$0.4bn`.
- Management message: management described the quarter as an encouraging start driven by sharper execution, improved gross margin, improved operating margin, and EPS expansion, while continuing the business transformation and working toward the Kimberly-Clark combination.
- Segment read: Self Care remained incidence-sensitive, but Skin Health and Beauty grew `8.4%` and Essential Health grew `4.9%`, showing that the portfolio is not moving as one block.
- Structural read: Q1 shows the real mechanism behind the Kenvue packet. The business is exposed to weak cold-and-flu seasons and other category-specific pressure, but it can still grow through innovation, eCommerce acceleration, category leadership, and stronger activations around brands such as Zyrtec, Tylenol, Neutrogena, OGX, Listerine, and Nicorette.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: the Q4 / FY`2025` chain showed Q4 net sales growth of `3.2%`, Q4 organic sales growth of `1.2%`, Q4 diluted EPS of `$0.17`, Q4 adjusted diluted EPS of `$0.27`, FY`2025` net sales down `2.1%`, FY`2025` organic sales down `2.2%`, FY`2025` diluted EPS of `$0.76`, and FY`2025` adjusted diluted EPS of `$1.08`.
- Cash flow and balance sheet: FY`2025` net cash flows from operating activities were `$2.2bn`, free cash flow was `$1.7bn`, and year-end debt was `$8.5bn`.
- Management message: management said it ended `2025` with stronger top- and bottom-line performance in the fourth quarter, while keeping the focus on strategic priorities and the proposed Kimberly-Clark transaction.
- Guidance: Kenvue explicitly said it would not provide forward-looking guidance because of the pending transaction.
- Structural read: Q4 `2025` is the bridge quarter that makes Kenvue useful. The full-year backdrop was weak, but the latest quarter showed improvement. That combination is a better read on consumer-health categories than a simple staples label: the demand is trusted and recurring, but it is also seasonal, promotion-sensitive, and dependent on execution by need state.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Taxonomy mismatch as evidence: Kenvue is one of the repo's best examples of source taxonomy diverging from operating reality. AnnualReports calls it `Technology / Biotechnology`, while the actual business is a global consumer-health and routine-care platform.
- Trusted routine use: this is a daily-life care system built on efficacy, trust, healthcare-professional endorsement, and habit rather than on indulgence or status.
- Seasonal and category sensitivity: Self Care can swing with low seasonal incidence rates, which makes Kenvue a better routine-use comparison for Kimberly-Clark, Colgate, and P&G than another generic packaged-goods name.
- Wellness and eCommerce bridge: strong eCommerce momentum, demand acceleration in skin health and beauty, and repeated brand activations make Kenvue a bridge between staples-like trust and more active consumer-acquisition work.
- Transformation and merger overhang: the `2026` story is partly operational and partly transactional. The pending Kimberly-Clark combination suppresses formal guidance and compresses management's forward framing.
- Comparison value: P&G is broader and more productivity-led, Kimberly-Clark is narrower and more hygiene-led, Colgate is tighter and more efficacy-led, and Kenvue adds a more explicit self-care, wound-care, oral-care, and health-brands routine.

## Working interpretation

- Kenvue materially improves the archive even with incomplete raw-document capture because it closes the missing wellness and consumer-health comparison inside the routine-use and trusted-care lane.
- It also sharpens one of the repo's bigger methodological points: the folder taxonomy should preserve the source label, but the research interpretation has to correct for economic reality. Kenvue is source-classified as `Technology / Biotechnology`, yet it belongs analytically beside consumer staples and care brands.
- For the `2025` through `2026` window, Kenvue is strong evidence that routine care and consumer-health demand remain durable, but the earnings quality depends on seasonal incidence, category mix, brand execution, digital activation, and the ability to keep improving performance while a major transaction is in flight.
