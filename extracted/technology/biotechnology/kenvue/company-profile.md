## What changed

- Revenue: FY`2025` was weak, but Q4 `2025`, Q1 `2026`, and Q2 `2026` each returned to net sales growth.
- Margin: Q1 `2026` margin improvement was strong, while Q2 `2026` still grew but showed some margin giveback from inflation, tariffs, and transactional foreign exchange.
- Guidance: no formal forward-looking guidance was provided in the trailing three-quarter window because of the pending Kimberly-Clark transaction.
- Capital allocation: free cash flow improved from `$1.7bn` in FY`2025` to `$1.0bn` in the first half of `2026`.
- Segment mix: Self Care remains more incidence-sensitive, while Skin Health and Beauty and Essential Health carried more of the recent improvement.
- Operational issues: retailer inventory reductions, low seasonal incidences, tariffs, and transformation work all matter to the recent read.
- Consumer / cultural signals: this is a trust-heavy everyday-care portfolio where efficacy, healthcare endorsement, eCommerce momentum, and brand activation drive repeat behavior.
- Management tone: cautious but more constructive, with repeated emphasis on execution improvement and transformation while the merger process continues.
