## What the company is

Cisco is a networking, security, observability, and enterprise-infrastructure company. In the current archive it is the clearest technology-sector evidence set for the control-layer and connectivity side of enterprise AI buildout rather than the consumer-device layer, pure software layer, or direct accelerated-computing silicon layer.

## Why Cisco matters in this archive

- It adds enterprise networking and security-control exposure to a technology set that previously leaned too heavily toward megacap consumer, cloud, internet-platform, and semiconductor names.
- It helps test whether AI-era spending is broadening from compute and cloud into the network, observability, and secure-infrastructure layers that make enterprise and hyperscale deployments usable.
- It introduces a recurring-revenue and installed-base infrastructure model that is economically different from Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, and NVIDIA.

## 2025 annual highlights

- Fiscal 2025 revenue: `$56.7B`
- The 2025 annual report says recurring revenue was more than half of total revenue.
- Cisco highlighted more than `$2B` of AI infrastructure orders in fiscal 2025.
- The annual materials emphasized Splunk integration, Cisco Silicon One, AI-native security including Hypershield, and AI-ready campus and datacenter refresh demand.

## Early read

- Cisco looks like a second-order beneficiary of AI capex rather than the first compute beneficiary.
- The evidence set suggests the AI spend wave is broadening into networking, security, and observability instead of remaining isolated in chips and cloud capacity.
- Cisco is also a useful counterweight because its revenue base is much more mature and recurring than NVIDIA's, which helps distinguish hype-driven acceleration from installed-base monetization and platform refresh demand.
