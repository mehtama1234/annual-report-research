## What changed

- Corning adds an important physical-layer proof point to CLI 5. The `2025` annual anchor and Q4 `2025` through Q2 `2026` chain show a company turning optical-density demand, advanced materials, manufacturing scale, and customer-backed infrastructure expansion into stronger sales, margins, and cash flow.
- The annual archive lag matters. AnnualReports still showed `2024` as the latest package on `2026-08-10`, which makes Corning another example where taxonomy and archive confirmation are useful but not authoritative for the current annual window.
- The growth mix became more explicitly AI-linked. Management repeatedly highlighted hyperscaler agreements, AI data-center optical-connectivity demand, domestic manufacturing expansion, and the Photonics Market-Access Platform.
- The quarter chain showed sustained scale and improving quality. Core sales moved from `$4.41B` in Q4 `2025` to `$4.35B` in Q1 `2026` to `$4.74B` in Q2 `2026`, while core EPS moved from `$0.72` to `$0.70` to `$0.78`.
- The underlying business read is broader than one product line. Optical Communications is the clearest AI beneficiary, but Corning also presented display, specialty materials, solar, automotive, and broader manufacturing capability as part of one materials-and-platform system.
- Corning's annual filing also sharpened the operating logic: by year-end `2025`, management said it had already met the Springboard growth and core-operating-margin targets a year ahead of plan and expected to keep increasing capacity and technology capability while sharing risk appropriately with customers.
