## What changed

- Canon gives the frontier a much larger and more diversified imaging flagship than `GoPro`.
- The packet links consumer cameras to network cameras, creator products, office hardware, medical imaging, and semiconductor equipment rather than isolating imaging as a narrow hobbyist endpoint.
- Canon also gives the archive a better read on how a mature Japanese industrial platform manages tariffs, memory costs, FX, and mix while still using cameras and network cameras as growth engines.

## Open questions

- How much of Canon's imaging strength is durable creator and prosumer demand versus a temporary cycle in mirrorless and compact cameras?
- Can imaging remain a growth engine if memory and component costs stay elevated?
- Does Canon's size make it a better read on imaging-system durability, or does diversification dilute the lane's direct consumer signal relative to `GoPro`?
