# Apple Inc. Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-08

## Snapshot

- Company: Apple Inc.
- Sector: Technology
- Industry: Consumer Services
- Ticker: AAPL
- Fiscal year-end: September
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: FY2026 Q3
  - quarter 2: FY2026 Q2
  - quarter 3: FY2026 Q1

## Annual report takeaways

- 2025 net sales were `$416.2 billion`, up `6%` year over year, with Services and iPhone as key growth contributors.
- 2025 total gross margin was `$195.2 billion`, and total gross margin percentage improved to `46.9%`; Services gross margin reached `75.4%`.
- 2025 R&D expense rose to `$34.6 billion`, reinforcing that product, silicon, software, and platform investment remain central to Apple’s model.
- 2025 segment data shows Europe, Japan, and Rest of Asia Pacific growing strongly, while Greater China declined `4%` in the annual period.
- Apple’s 2025 risk discussion is explicit that tariffs and other trade measures can materially affect supply chain, component availability, pricing, and gross margin.
- Capital return remained aggressive in 2025: `$89.3 billion` of repurchases and `$15.4 billion` of dividends.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: FY2026 Q3 revenue was `$109.4 billion`, net income was `$29.8 billion`, and diluted EPS was `$2.02`.
- Guidance: no detailed long-range guidance extracted from the saved materials, but management emphasized installed-base strength and June-quarter records.
- Management message: Apple called this its strongest June quarter ever, with double-digit growth across iPhone, Mac, and Services and in every geographic segment.
- Structural themes: iPhone and Services remain the core growth engines, Mac also contributed strongly, and tariff refunds provided a meaningful temporary margin tailwind.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: FY2026 Q2 revenue was `$111.2 billion`, net income was `$29.6 billion`, and diluted EPS was `$2.01`.
- Guidance: quarter included a new `$100 billion` share-repurchase authorization and a `4%` dividend increase to `$0.27`.
- Management message: Apple described FY2026 Q2 as its best March quarter ever, with all geographic segments up double digits and Services reaching a new all-time high.
- Structural themes: iPhone cycle strength was the main revenue driver, Services kept compounding, and capital return remained a first-order part of the story.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: FY2026 Q1 revenue was `$143.8 billion`, net income was `$42.1 billion`, and diluted EPS was `$2.84`.
- Guidance: no new buyback authorization in the saved Q1 materials, but Apple highlighted nearly `$54 billion` of operating cash flow and almost `$32 billion` returned to shareholders.
- Management message: Apple called FY2026 Q1 a record-breaking holiday quarter, with all-time highs for total company revenue, EPS, iPhone revenue, and Services revenue.
- Structural themes: installed-base expansion, ecosystem monetization, holiday-season iPhone demand, and very high cash generation remain central.

Note: for many companies this third slot will be a late-2025 quarter needed to complete the trailing three-quarter set as of 2026-08-08.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Apple reinforces that consumer technology is still bifurcating between hardware cycle winners with ecosystem lock-in and weaker, less differentiated device exposure elsewhere.
- Consumer: Apple’s strongest signals remain premium-device demand, ecosystem retention, and service monetization rather than pure unit volume alone.
- Cultural: management still frames Apple products as category-defining consumer objects, and the installed base above `2.5 billion` active devices is a major cultural and commercial moat.
- Regulatory: the 2025 10-K highlights continuing DMA and DOJ antitrust pressure, alongside broader trade and tariff risk.
- Labor: no major labor-distress signal appears in the current evidence set; the operating model continues to absorb rising R&D and SG&A while keeping margins strong.
- Technology: AI and software innovation are rising in prominence, with FY2026 Q3 explicitly highlighting the all-new Siri AI and broader software updates from WWDC26.
- Capital allocation: Apple continues to pair very large buybacks with steady dividend growth and strong operating cash flow conversion.
