## What changed

- Revenue: 2025 net sales were `$416.2 billion`, up `6%` year over year; FY2026 Q1/Q2/Q3 each posted double-digit year-over-year revenue growth.
- Margin: 2025 total gross margin percentage was `46.9%`, with Services gross margin at `75.4%`; FY2026 Q3 gross margin reached `50.1%`, helped by tariff refunds.
- Guidance: Apple’s quarterly materials continue to emphasize records and installed-base expansion rather than detailed long-range public guidance in the saved evidence set.
- Capital allocation: 2025 included `$89.3 billion` of share repurchases and `$15.4 billion` of dividends; FY2026 Q2 added a new `$100 billion` repurchase authorization and a dividend increase to `$0.27`.
- Segment mix: Services remains a major profit-supporting engine, while iPhone remains the main revenue driver; Greater China was weak in 2025 annual results but materially stronger through FY2026 Q1-Q3.
- Operational issues: tariffs and other trade measures are an explicit 2025 risk factor; FY2026 Q3 reported a favorable impact from tariff refunds.
- Consumer / cultural signals: Apple’s installed base surpassed `2.5 billion` active devices in FY2026 Q1, reinforcing ecosystem lock-in and recurring-services potential.
- Management tone: management remains assertively optimistic, emphasizing records, device-base growth, AI and software innovation, and geographic breadth.

## Open questions

- Is FY2026 growth being driven primarily by iPhone cycle strength, or is Services and regional recovery now doing more of the incremental work?
- How durable is the FY2026 tariff benefit versus the ongoing trade and supply-chain risk flagged in the 2025 10-K?
