## What changed

- Pure Storage materially deepens the CLI 5 storage-persistence layer because it sits closer than Seagate or Western Digital to the control plane that governs how enterprise data is stored, moved, updated, and monetized as a service.
- The annual and quarter chain no longer reads like a simple hardware array vendor. It reads like a company trying to turn the storage substrate into a broader operating layer for enterprise data, with recurring subscription revenue, ARR, RPO, and control-plane software sitting on top of physical flash infrastructure.
- That matters because it changes where the hidden AI-era toll may sit. Instead of monetizing only raw capacity or media density, Pure / Everpure tries to monetize unified control, continuous modernization, Evergreen-style subscriptions, hybrid-cloud portability, cyber resilience, and AI-ready data management.
