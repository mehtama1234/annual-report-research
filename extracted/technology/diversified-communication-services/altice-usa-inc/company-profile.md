## What changed

- Reporting identity: by the 2025 annual and 2026 quarterly source window, the issuer was presenting itself publicly as `Optimum Communications, Inc.` even though the archive lane and historical investor framing still connect it to `Altice USA, Inc.`.
- Revenue and operating pressure: full-year `2025` revenue fell to `$8.6B`, and quarterly revenue then fell again to `$2.07B` in Q1 2026 and `$2.02B` in Q2 2026. The business is still very large, but the fixed-line base remains under real pressure.
- Customer mix shift: broadband losses remained significant at `-62k` in Q4 2025, `-64k` in Q1 2026, and `-40k` in Q2 2026, but mobile lines expanded from `623k` at FY2025 to `674k` in Q1 2026 and `724k` in Q2 2026 as the company leaned harder into convergence.
- Fiber and network posture: the company ended FY2025 with `3.1M` fiber passings and `716k` fiber customers, then continued adding passings in 2026 while pushing higher-speed adoption and using Lightpath as an AI-grade enterprise and hyperscaler connectivity wedge.
- Balance-sheet deterioration: FY2025 liabilities were `$33.0B` against stockholders' deficiency of `$(2.314)B`. By Q2 2026, liabilities had risen to `$33.083B` and stockholders' deficiency had deepened to `$(5.549)B`, while current debt surged to roughly `$4.148B`.
- Financing stress: the Q1 2026 filing disclosed substantial doubt about the company's ability to continue as a going concern because of near-term maturities, and the Q2 2026 period added preferred-unit financing, a private exchange, and a tender offer as part of the capital-structure response.

## Open questions

- Can convergence, mobile growth, fiber upgrades, and Lightpath enterprise demand offset enough fixed-line decline to stabilize the core cable franchise before leverage and refinancing pressure force harsher strategic outcomes?
- Does Optimum's simplification narrative represent a durable reset in customer economics, or is it mostly a defensive response while the balance sheet stays too tight to fully absorb subscriber pressure?
