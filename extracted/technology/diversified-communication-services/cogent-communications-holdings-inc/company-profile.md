## What changed

- Revenue base: the `2025` annual filing showed full-year service revenue of `$975.8M`, down from `$1.036B` in `2024`, with a net loss of `$(157.3)M`. Cogent is still a shrinking top-line story overall, but the mix inside that revenue base is moving.
- Network shape: Cogent describes itself as a facilities-based provider of low-cost, high-speed Internet access, private network services, optical wavelength and optical transport services, and colocation. The company operates two networks, an IP Network and an Optical Wave Network, and served customers in `57` countries in the `2025` filing.
- Speed and footprint: the `2026` quarter filings say on-net service speeds range from `100` Mbps to `400` Gbps and customer categories include corporate, net-centric, and enterprise accounts. That matters because the traffic mix is increasingly machine-heavy and wholesale-like, not just office connectivity.
- Mix shift: Q4 2025, Q1 2026, and Q2 2026 all show the same pattern of declining off-net and non-core revenue while wavelength and on-net revenue rise. In Q1 2026, on-net revenue increased to `$135.6M` while off-net fell to `$89.0M`; by Q2 2026, on-net revenue including wavelengths reached `$150.2M`.
- Sprint asset integration: the Sprint wireline acquisition created the Optical Wave Network and the Cogent Fiber business. Management continues to describe Cogent as being in a unique position to monetize the acquired fiber assets and harvest cost and revenue synergies, which makes asset monetization a core part of the equity story rather than a side note.
- Balance-sheet pressure and relief: in Q2 2026 Cogent sold ten owned data centers for net proceeds of `$224.2M`, booking a gain of `$130.7M`, and ended the quarter with total cash and restricted cash of `$369.7M`. That sale improved liquidity, but it also underscores how much of the story is still about funding, leverage, and refinancing rather than pure demand growth.

## Open questions

- Can Cogent turn rising wavelength and on-net mix into sustained service-revenue growth, or will off-net runoff and non-core attrition keep masking the quality improvement?
- How much long-term value comes from monetizing Sprint-acquired physical assets versus operating them inside a differentiated optical and IP platform?
