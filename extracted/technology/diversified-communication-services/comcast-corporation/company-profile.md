## What changed

- Connectivity mix: Comcast is no longer just defending a cable bundle. The quarter chain shows a broader convergence model where broadband pressure is partially offset by wireless line growth, Business Services expansion, and a larger effort to deepen the total customer relationship.
- Wallet-control framing: management increasingly talks about customer relationships, go-to-market simplification, and wireless penetration inside its footprint rather than treating broadband as a stand-alone utility SKU. That is a material change in how Comcast is trying to defend the household account.
- Revenue quality: full-year `2025` revenue was `$123.7B`, net income attributable to Comcast was `$20.0B`, adjusted net income was `$16.0B`, and adjusted EBITDA was `$37.4B`, but those headline numbers are shaped by Hulu-sale noise, the Versant separation, and increasingly different earnings profiles across connectivity, media, studios, and parks.
- Event and franchise monetization: Comcast's media and entertainment stack is showing that a connectivity incumbent can monetize sports rights, IP, and live-event concentration as a customer-acquisition and engagement engine. Olympics, the Super Bowl, the NBA, FIFA, Peacock, and Universal franchises are not side stories; they are part of the same household-attention and subscription wallet fight.
- Capital intensity remains real: Q1 `2026` capex was `$2.4B` and Q2 `2026` capex was `$2.9B`, with Connectivity & Platforms capex rising faster than the content side. This remains a physical-network and customer-equipment business even when the surface narrative shifts toward streaming and franchise monetization.

## Open questions

- Can Comcast's broadband reset and wireless attachment strategy meaningfully stabilize the residential relationship, or does the company simply offset slower broadband economics with more complicated bundle and content monetization?
- Does Peacock's progress and broader content-event leverage create durable ecosystem power, or does it mainly raise the cost and complexity of defending the same household wallet?
