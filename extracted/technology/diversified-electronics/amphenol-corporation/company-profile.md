## What changed

- Amphenol expands CLI 5 into a part of the hidden physical stack that the lane still needed: connectors, interconnect systems, antennas, sensors, fiber, high-speed cable, and signal-integrity tooling that sit between semiconductor output and functioning AI, cloud, and communications systems.
- The annual package already showed scale. Fiscal `2025` net sales reached `$23.1B`, up `52%` from `2024`, and the company entered `2026` with major acquisition momentum, including the January `2026` close of CommScope's CCS business.
- The strategic clue is that Amphenol is not a generic low-value component supplier. Management repeatedly tied the latest quarter chain to exceptional IT datacom growth, book-to-bill above `1.2x`, fiber-optic interconnect, high-speed digital and RF signal integrity, and the accretive expansion of the CommScope platform.
- The latest quarter chain also showed that the model is not only volume-driven. Q2 `2026` GAAP and adjusted operating margin reached `29.5%` and `29.8%`, and the company still returned `$515M` to shareholders while continuing acquisition-led expansion.
- The packet also preserves a useful operating caution: some of the most important current upside is tied to acquisition integration, datacom strength, and tariff recoveries, so the physical-interconnect story is strong but not entirely frictionless.
