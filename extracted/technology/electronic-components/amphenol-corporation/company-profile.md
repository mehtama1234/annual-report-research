## What changed

- Revenue scale: full-year `2025` sales reached `$23.1B`; quarterly sales then moved from `$6.4B` in Q4 `2025` to `$7.620B` in Q1 `2026` and `$8.758B` in Q2 `2026`.
- Profitability: adjusted operating margin was `26.2%` for full-year `2025`, `27.5%` in Q4 `2025`, `27.3%` in Q1 `2026`, and `29.8%` in Q2 `2026`, with the latest quarter helped by an `$80M` tariff-recovery benefit.
- Cash generation: operating cash flow was `$5.4B` and free cash flow was `$4.4B` in full-year `2025`; quarterly operating and free cash flow were `$1.7B` and `$1.5B` in Q4 `2025`, `$1.1B` and `$831M` in Q1 `2026`, and `$1.6B` and `$1.2B` in Q2 `2026`.
- Segment mix: Communications Solutions has become the clearest AI-and-bandwidth exposure point, with segment sales rising to `$4.535B` in Q1 `2026` and operating margin reaching `30.6%`, then operating margin reaching `33.6%` in Q2 `2026`.
- Acquisition leverage: the CommScope CCS acquisition closed in January `2026` and contributed about `$871.5M` of net sales through March 31, `2026`; by Q2, management had raised its full-year `2026` CommScope sales expectation to `$4.6B` from `$4.1B`.
- Demand signaling: orders were `$9.4B` in Q1 `2026` and `$10.7B` in Q2 `2026`, with book-to-bill above `1.2x` in both quarters, indicating demand was not just acquisition optics.
- Capital allocation: Amphenol continued its usual mix of acquisitions, dividends, and buybacks, returning nearly `$1.5B` to shareholders in `2025`, then repurchasing shares and paying dividends again in both reported `2026` quarters.

## Working interpretation

- Amphenol is one of the clearest ways to capture the physical content layer behind AI, cloud, mobile, defense, and electrification demand without defaulting back to semiconductors or application software.
- The bigger lesson is that bandwidth demand does not only monetize through carriers, datacenters, and software platforms. It also monetizes through connector density, fiber content, power management, antennas, sensors, and signal-integrity hardware embedded in every network and compute buildout.

## Open questions

- How much of the current earnings acceleration is a durable content-per-system shift tied to AI and datacom architecture, versus a nearer-cycle spike amplified by CommScope and tariff timing?
- Can Amphenol preserve its decentralized acquisition model and margin quality as larger datacom and power-platform opportunities become more strategically central to the company?
