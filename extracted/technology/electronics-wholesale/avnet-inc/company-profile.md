## What changed

- Avnet materially expands the CLI 8 technology-distribution lane by adding a second flagship electronics wholesaler beside `Arrow Electronics, Inc.` with a more explicit emphasis on inventory days, return on working capital, and the `Electronic Components` plus `Farnell` split.
- The `2025` annual chain shows Avnet is not just moving boxes. The company combines semiconductors, interconnect, passive and electromechanical products, embedded components, engineering support, programming, testing, labeling, supply-chain management, and materials-management services.
- Full-year `2025` sales were `$22.2008B`, with `Electronic Components` at `$20.7550B` and `Farnell` at `$1.4458B`.
- Fiscal `2025` was a down year, with total sales down `6.6%`, gross margin at `10.7%`, operating income margin at `2.3%`, adjusted operating income margin at `2.8%`, and diluted `EPS` at `$2.75`, which makes the later `2026` recovery more analytically useful.
- `Q2 FY2026` sales were `$6.3190B`, `Q3 FY2026` sales were `$7.1198B`, and `Q4 FY2026` sales reached a record `$8.2954B`, showing strong volume recovery and operating leverage inside the channel.
- The quarter chain makes the economics unusually visible: inventory days improved from `77` in `Q3 FY2026` to `71` in `Q4 FY2026`, while full-year fiscal `2026` inventory days improved by `18` days year over year to `81`.

## Open questions

- How much of Avnet's current acceleration is cyclical semiconductor normalization versus structurally stronger supplier and customer positioning?
- Can `Farnell` sustain its sharper margin recovery and act as a more service-attached complement to the higher-volume `Electronic Components` engine?
- If downstream industrial, automotive, or cloud-infrastructure demand softens, how much operating leverage reverses through the same inventory and working-capital system?
- Does the stronger `2026` rebound further entrench Avnet as a preferred intermediary, or does it mainly reflect temporary channel restocking after a weaker `2025`?
