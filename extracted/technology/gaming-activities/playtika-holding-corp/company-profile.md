## What changed in this period

- Playtika kept shifting revenue mix toward direct-to-consumer web payments, which lowered platform-fee drag and became one of the clearest recurring themes across `Q4 2025`, `Q1 2026`, and `Q2 2026`.
- The company also became more dependent on SuperPlay as a growth engine, especially through `Disney Solitaire` and other acquired titles.
- That growth came with real pressure:
  - contingent-consideration volatility tied to the SuperPlay earnout
  - elevated media-buy investment
  - a large debt stack
  - more cautious consumer-spending commentary by `Q2 2026`

## Why this name matters in the archive

- Playtika is a useful bridge between traditional game publishers and the broader participation-economy names already covered in the repo.
- It monetizes habit, repeat engagement, and live-ops optimization more than tentpole launch spectacle.
