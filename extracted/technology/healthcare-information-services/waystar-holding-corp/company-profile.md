## What changed

- Revenue and margins: Waystar moved from about `$1.099B` of fiscal `2025` revenue to about `$313.9M` in `Q1 2026` and about `$319.7M` in `Q2 2026`, while keeping adjusted EBITDA margins around `43%`.
- Business mix: the business is a software toll on healthcare payment and revenue-cycle complexity, with subscription revenue growing faster than volume-based revenue in both `Q1` and `Q2 2026`.
- Workflow role: the annual report makes clear that the company sits across pre-service, mid-cycle, and post-service workflows, including prior authorization, coding and documentation accuracy, claims management, denial prevention, and patient payments.
- Scale and embedding: the `2025` annual filing says the company served over `30,000` clients representing more than `1M` distinct providers, with over `500` channel-partner integrations including PM and EHR systems.
- AI and data signal: the company said approximately `50%` of its solutions leverage AI and that its platform processed over `7.5B` healthcare-payment transactions in `2025`, representing over `$2.4T` in gross claims volume.
- Management tone: Waystar sounds less like a basic software vendor and more like a mission-critical provider operating system for reimbursement, payment routing, and administrative simplification.
