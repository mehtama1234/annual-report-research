## What changed

- Exact-category ad-infrastructure depth: this packet adds a clean `Information Technology Services` attention-infrastructure operator that sits between advertisers and publishers without owning the content franchise itself.
- Identity continuity: the archive now explicitly captures the shift from `Outbrain Inc.` / `OB` to `Teads Holding Co.` / `TEAD`, which matters because AnnualReports and current filings use different public identities for the same issuer.
- Open-internet monetization read: the packet ties together publisher yield, advertiser outcomes, omnichannel CTV growth, and direct-response weakness in one chain.
- Brand versus performance convergence: management repeatedly frames Teads as a full-funnel system trying to bridge branding and conversion across `CTV` and the open internet.
- Publisher operating-system angle: `Q2 2026` adds a stronger publisher-side infrastructure read through `Teads EngageOS`, which management positions as AI-driven yield optimization across the reader session.

## Open questions

- Can Teads stabilize its Direct Response and SME exposure while keeping its higher-margin enterprise and `CTV` businesses growing fast enough to carry the platform?
- How durable is the company's omnichannel positioning if the open-web performance market remains soft while larger walled gardens keep their measurement and demand advantages?
- Does the Teads combination create a lasting full-funnel platform, or mainly a larger but still cyclical advertising middleware business?
- Can publisher-side tools such as `EngageOS` deepen switching costs enough to make the company more than a campaign-execution layer?
