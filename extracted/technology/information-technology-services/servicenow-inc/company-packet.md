## Annual report takeaways

- ServiceNow is the right next CLI 5 extension because it adds the workflow-orchestration and AI-governance layer that sits above infrastructure ownership and turns enterprise complexity into recurring software revenue.
- The annual materials show a business that is much less about moving traffic or selling devices and much more about governing how large organizations route work, approvals, incidents, data, AI agents, and internal operating complexity.
- Full-year FY2025 subscription revenue reached `$12.883B`, total revenue reached `$13.278B`, operating cash flow reached `$5.444B`, free cash flow reached `$4.636B`, GAAP net income reached `$1.748B`, and non-GAAP diluted EPS reached `$3.51`.
- The margin profile is the main structural clue. FY2025 GAAP operating margin was `13.5%` and non-GAAP operating margin was `31%`, which shows a software-quality business monetizing workflow depth without the capex burden that shapes access operators and much of the physical-infrastructure layer.
- ServiceNow also makes CLI 5 broader and more defensible. It shows that one of the strongest infrastructure profit pools may sit in the software layer that governs enterprise work and AI behavior rather than in the transport, device, or network layer beneath it.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` revenue reached `$3.987B`, subscription revenue reached `$3.877B`, cRPO reached `$13.20B`, and total RPO reached `$29.0B`.
- Operating read: Q2 is the strongest quarter for seeing the control-tower thesis. ServiceNow AI crossed `$1B` of annual contract value, which suggests customers are not just experimenting with AI. They are buying workflow-governed AI deployment at scale.
- Capital and model signal: this remains a software-like infrastructure model with strong cash conversion, high recurring revenue, and no telecom-style network burden.
- Structural themes: Q2 reinforces the idea that enterprise buyers increasingly need an orchestration layer for AI, security, identity, and work routing. ServiceNow is trying to become that layer.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` revenue reached `$3.770B`, subscription revenue reached `$3.671B`, cRPO reached `$12.64B`, and total RPO reached `$27.7B`.
- Operating read: customers spending more than `$1M` in Now Assist ACV grew more than `130%` year over year, which is one of the clearest signals in the lane that AI deployment is moving from pilots to governed enterprise programs.
- Structural themes: Q1 shows why ServiceNow matters in CLI 5. Enterprises do not only need compute and security. They also need a way to route, govern, approve, and operationalize AI in day-to-day work.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` revenue reached `$3.568B`, subscription revenue reached `$3.466B`, cRPO reached `$12.85B`, and total RPO reached `$28.2B`.
- Full-year frame: full-year FY2025 subscription revenue reached `$12.883B`, total revenue reached `$13.278B`, operating cash flow reached `$5.444B`, and free cash flow reached `$4.636B`.
- Capital-allocation signal: ServiceNow repurchased about `3.6M` shares for `$597M`, authorized an additional `$5B` share repurchase program, and announced an imminent `$2B` accelerated share repurchase.
- Structural themes: the year-end release is the strongest single document for seeing the AI-governance story clearly. Now Assist net new ACV more than doubled year over year, and management kept framing ServiceNow as the operational layer for enterprise AI reinvention.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- CLI 5 role: ServiceNow adds the workflow-orchestration and AI-governance layer to the lane.
- Enterprise and institutional read: large organizations increasingly need a control system for how work moves across departments, approvals, incidents, and now AI agents.
- Cultural and societal read: AI adoption inside enterprises is normalizing the idea that automation must be governed, observable, and embedded into existing work systems rather than simply bolted on at the model layer.
- Industrial read: subscription expansion, cRPO and RPO depth, workflow breadth, AI agent rollout, and selective acquisition layering are the main operating levers.
- Capital-allocation read: the business is much lighter than telecom or physical infrastructure. The main execution burden is platform breadth and product integration, not debt-heavy asset ownership.
- Monetization-system read: ServiceNow monetizes enterprise fear of workflow fragmentation, governance failure, approval bottlenecks, and unmanaged AI sprawl.

## Working interpretation

- ServiceNow sharpens CLI 5 because it shows that one of the strongest infrastructure profit pools may sit in workflow-governed enterprise software rather than in the physical systems underneath it.
- Compared with Palo Alto, ServiceNow is a different kind of software control point. Palo Alto monetizes trust, threat posture, and cyber standardization; ServiceNow monetizes workflow routing, approvals, enterprise process governance, and AI operationalization.
- Compared with telecom and cable operators, ServiceNow is again close to the opposite model: very light capital burden, very high recurring mix, and demand tied to organizational complexity rather than to household access volume.
- For the `2025` through `2026` window, ServiceNow is strong evidence that AI is not only a compute or software-assistant story. It is also a workflow-governance story. Enterprises are paying for the system that tells the organization how to use AI safely and productively.
