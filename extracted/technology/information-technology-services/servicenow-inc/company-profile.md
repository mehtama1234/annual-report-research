## What changed

- Revenue: full-year FY2025 subscription revenue reached `$12.883B` and total revenue reached `$13.278B`; the trailing three reported quarters rose from `$3.568B` in Q4 2025 to `$3.770B` in Q1 2026 and `$3.987B` in Q2 2026.
- Margin: full-year FY2025 GAAP operating margin was `13.5%`, non-GAAP operating margin was `31%`, GAAP net income was `$1.748B`, and non-GAAP diluted EPS was `$3.51`; the trailing three quarters showed GAAP operating margins of `12.5%`, `11%`, and `15%` with non-GAAP operating margins of `32%`, `31.5%`, and `32%`.
- Cash flow: full-year FY2025 operating cash flow reached `$5.444B` and free cash flow reached `$4.636B`, equal to a `35%` non-GAAP free-cash-flow margin.
- Backlog and commitments: cRPO moved from `$12.85B` in Q4 2025 to `$12.64B` in Q1 2026 and `$13.20B` in Q2 2026, while total RPO remained very large at `$28.2B`, `$27.7B`, and `$29.0B`.
- Capital allocation: ServiceNow repurchased about `3.6M` shares for `$597M` in Q4 2025, authorized an additional `$5B` share repurchase program, and announced an imminent `$2B` accelerated share repurchase.
- Segment mix: the economic center is subscription software layered across enterprise workflow automation, IT service management, AI governance, data fabric, security, and increasingly broader employee and customer workflows.
- Operational issues: management is using acquisitions and product breadth to push ServiceNow from workflow software toward a wider AI control-tower and enterprise-orchestration role, with Armis, Veza, and Moveworks all framed as strategic extensions.
- Consumer / cultural signals: ServiceNow is less a direct consumer signal than an enterprise operating-system signal; it shows how large organizations are trying to govern, route, and operationalize AI inside day-to-day work rather than only buying raw model capacity.
- Management tone: management is assertive and increasingly expansive, repeatedly describing ServiceNow as the AI control tower for business reinvention and linking workflow orchestration to security, governance, and autonomous agents.

## Open questions

- Can ServiceNow keep AI growth above the rest of the business without diluting its workflow-software margin profile?
- Do the security and identity acquisitions create durable platform breadth, or do they add integration risk before the market proves the full control-tower thesis?
