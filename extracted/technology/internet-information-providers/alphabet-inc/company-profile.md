## What changed

- Revenue: 2025 full-year revenue reached `$402.8 billion`; quarterly revenue rose from `$113.8 billion` in Q4 2025 to `$109.9 billion` in Q1 2026 and `$119.8 billion` in Q2 2026.
- Margin: Q4 2025 operating margin was `31.6%`, Q1 2026 operating margin expanded to `36.1%`, and Q2 2026 operating margin was `34.0%`.
- Guidance: the core guidance story is rising AI capex. Q4 2025 pointed to `2026` capex of `$175-$185 billion`; Q2 2026 raised that to `$195-$205 billion`.
- Capital allocation: Alphabet continued its dividend program, raising the quarterly dividend by `5%` to `$0.22` in Q1 2026.
- Segment mix: Search, YouTube, subscriptions, and Cloud all remained growth drivers, but Google Cloud and enterprise AI demand are accelerating faster than the rest of the mix.
- Operational issues: reported net income is materially affected by unrealized investment gains, especially in Q1 and Q2 2026, so GAAP earnings outpaced underlying operating growth.
- Consumer / cultural signals: Gemini app, AI Overviews, YouTube, and paid subscriptions keep Alphabet deeply embedded in daily consumer behavior while also pulling users into paid AI plans.
- Management tone: management is highly aggressive on AI opportunity, technical infrastructure, and long-duration investment, with little sign of retrenchment.

## Open questions

- How durable is current Search growth once AI product usage shifts more activity away from traditional monetization surfaces?
- Can Cloud and enterprise AI growth continue at this pace without margin pressure from the rising capex and third-party capacity bridge?
