## What changed

- Revenue: full-year `2025` revenue reached `$2.1679 billion`; quarterly revenue then rose from `$639.8 million` in Q1 `2026` to `$696.1 million` in Q2 `2026`.
- Gross margin: full-year `2025` GAAP gross profit reached `$1.6154 billion`, or about `74.5%` of revenue; Q1 `2026` GAAP gross margin was about `71.2%`, and Q2 `2026` GAAP gross margin was about `71.8%`.
- Profitability: Cloudflare remained GAAP-loss-making, with a full-year `2025` operating loss of `$207.2 million`; Q2 `2026` GAAP operating loss widened to `$205.7 million` because the restructuring charges flagged in Q1 ran through the model.
- Cash generation: full-year `2025` operating cash flow reached `$603.1 million`; capex reached `$315.6 million`, implying free cash flow of about `$287.5 million`. Q1 `2026` free cash flow reached `$84.1 million`, and Q2 `2026` free cash flow reached `$56.4 million`.
- Forward demand: remaining performance obligations rose from about `$2.4958 billion` at `2025-12-31` to about `$2.5435 billion` at `2026-03-31` and `$2.7320 billion` at `2026-06-30`.
- Deferred revenue: current contract liabilities rose from about `$684.2 million` at year-end `2025` to about `$755.1 million` in Q1 `2026` and `$812.2 million` in Q2 `2026`.
- Customer scale: the live investor overview on `2026-08-10` said Cloudflare had `45%+` of the Fortune 500 as paying customers and `4,600+` large customers as of Q2 `2026`.
- Operating model: Q1 `2026` introduced the internal `agentic AI-first` restructuring, making the company both a seller of AI-era network infrastructure and a live case of AI pressure on knowledge-work organization.

## Working interpretation

- Cloudflare is one of the best names in the archive for showing that the internet stack is being rebuilt around performance, security, identity, developer tooling, and machine-to-machine traffic at the same time.
- The company sits between domain ownership and application experience. It is not just a CDN and not just a cybersecurity vendor. It is a traffic-control, application-delivery, and trust layer that monetizes the need to secure and accelerate a more automated internet.

## Open questions

- How durable is Cloudflare's pricing and platform leverage if hyperscalers, incumbent security vendors, and adjacent edge platforms keep converging on the same enterprise budget pools?
- Can Cloudflare convert strong RPO growth and AI-era narrative strength into cleaner GAAP earnings over time, or will platform expansion and stock-based compensation keep deferring that crossover?
