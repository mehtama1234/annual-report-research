## What changed

- Mix shift: FY2025 revenue rose to `$624.0M`, up `15%`, but the important change was mix, not just scale. Network services remained the base at `$477.8M`, yet security grew faster to `$125.1M` and other revenue, including compute and observability, grew to `$21.1M`.
- Margin direction: FY2025 GAAP gross margin improved to `57.1%` from `54.4%`, and Q4, Q1, and Q2 each set new gross-margin records. That suggests the business is becoming less like a raw traffic utility and more like a policy, protection, and control layer.
- Retention recovery: LTM NRR rose from `110%` in Q4 2025 to `113%` in Q1 2026 and `117%` in Q2 2026. The improvement matters because it indicates expansion inside the installed base after a long period when usage volatility and customer digestion weighed on the story.
- Demand concentration: Fastly still is not a fully diffuse platform. The 2025 `10-K` said a single customer exceeded `10%` of revenue in 2025 and that affiliated business units of one company also represented `10%` of revenue. The Q2 2026 supplement also showed the top ten customers at `37%` of revenue.
- Revenue quality: Enterprise and large-customer concentration remains high. Q4 2025 enterprise customer count was `628`, Q1 2026 was `634`, and Q2 2026 eased to `624`, while large-customer revenue stayed at `94%` of total revenue across the recent quarter set.
- Traffic thesis: The company is increasingly describing the internet not as human page-view traffic alone, but as a machine-governance problem involving AI crawlers, agent traffic, API discovery, bot management, anti-piracy, and application security.

## Open questions

- Can Fastly keep migrating from a narrower CDN identity into a broader edge-security-and-control platform without letting customer concentration and usage volatility dominate the economics again?
- Does AI become a durable demand tailwind for Fastly because more machine traffic requires policy, authentication, monetization, and anti-abuse controls, or does it mainly increase network load without proportional value capture?
