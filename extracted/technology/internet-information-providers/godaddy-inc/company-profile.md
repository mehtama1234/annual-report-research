## What changed

- Revenue: full-year `2025` revenue reached `$4.9511 billion`; revenue then rose to `$1.2669 billion` in Q1 `2026` and `$1.2980 billion` in Q2 `2026`.
- Profitability: full-year `2025` operating income reached `$1.1273 billion`, or about `22.8%` of revenue, and Q1 plus Q2 `2026` both expanded operating margins versus the prior year.
- Cash generation: full-year `2025` operating cash flow reached `$1.5994 billion`, with only `$23.9 million` of capex, supporting free cash flow of roughly `$1.5755 billion`, which management consistently rounded to about `$1.6 billion`.
- Recurring layer: ARR reached `$4.3362 billion` in Q4 `2025`, `$4.2884 billion` in Q1 `2026`, and `$4.4214 billion` in Q2 `2026`.
- Customer monetization: ARPU rose from `$242` in Q4 `2025` to `$246` in Q1 `2026` and `$250` in Q2 `2026`, showing the mix shift toward higher-value software, commerce, and attached services.
- Capital allocation: the company kept using strong cash generation for buybacks, with `2025` repurchases of `10.2 million` shares for about `$1.6 billion`, then `9.8 million` shares repurchased year to date through `2026-07-29` for `$851.8 million`.
- Platform strategy: the quarter chain increasingly framed GoDaddy around Airo, AI-native experiences, Agent Name Service, Agentic Resource Discovery, and the developer platform rather than around basic domain registration alone.

## Working interpretation

- GoDaddy is the best immediate comparison to VeriSign because it monetizes the customer-facing edge of internet identity rather than the registry-control center.
- The company sits where domains, websites, hosting, commerce, marketing, and small-business operations converge, which makes it a recurring software-attached infrastructure platform for everyday entrepreneurs rather than a pure application or pure hosting name.

## Open questions

- How durable is GoDaddy’s customer growth and pricing power if AI lowers website-creation friction but also shifts discovery and commerce toward fewer intermediary platforms?
- Can the company turn AI-led product attachment into a structurally better business mix without over-relying on buybacks and without weakening the lower-growth Core domain base that still funds the rest of the model?
