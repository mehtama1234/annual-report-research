# Meta Platforms, Inc. Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: Meta Platforms, Inc.
- Sector: Technology
- Industry: Internet Information Providers
- Ticker: META
- Fiscal year-end: December
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026
  - quarter 3: Q4 2025

## Annual report takeaways

- Meta is a high-value archive addition because it gives the repo a dominant consumer attention and advertising platform with a daily-use behavioral loop, not just a subscription or enterprise-software model.
- The AnnualReports lag is itself important. As of `2026-08-09`, AnnualReports.com still showed Meta's `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K`, while Meta's own annual-report artifact and SEC filing chain already supported the full `2025` year-end package.
- The `2025` annual package shows scale plus reinvestment pressure at the same time. Full-year revenue was `$200.966B`, income from operations was `$83.276B`, net income was `$60.458B`, diluted EPS was `$23.49`, capital expenditures were `$72.22B`, and DAP averaged `3.58B` in December `2025`.
- The operating logic is not just "sell ads to a large audience." The annual filing and quarter trail show a platform where recommendation quality, AI-assisted monetization, messaging, creator surfaces, and future hardware ambitions all sit on top of a huge installed behavior base.
- That makes Meta especially useful to the archive because it is both cultural and infrastructural. It captures everyday attention, business demand, and AI investment concentration inside one operator.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` revenue was `$60.801B`, net income was `$15.848B`, and diluted EPS was `$6.18`.
- Operating detail: DAP averaged `3.60B`, ad impressions increased `14%`, average price per ad increased `12%`, and capital expenditures were `$31.08B`.
- Management message: management framed the quarter around AI already improving the core ad business while also opening new product and enterprise opportunities.
- Structural themes: this quarter is useful because it shows the two-sided Meta story clearly. The consumer and ad machine is still compounding, but the cost of AI infrastructure, legal issues, and restructuring can materially affect margin even with strong revenue growth.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` revenue was `$56.311B`, net income was `$26.773B`, and diluted EPS was `$10.44`.
- Operating detail: DAP averaged `3.56B`, ad impressions increased `19%`, average price per ad increased `12%`, and capital expenditures were `$19.84B`.
- Management message: the strategic language centered on strong app momentum and the release of the first model from Meta Superintelligence Labs.
- Structural themes: Q1 is the cleaner growth quarter. It shows AI being used not as abstract future positioning, but as a live driver of ad performance, recommendation quality, and broader platform relevance.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` revenue was `$59.893B`, Q4 net income was `$22.768B`, and diluted EPS was `$8.88`. Full-year `2025` revenue was `$200.966B` and full-year net income was `$60.458B`.
- Operating detail: DAP averaged `3.58B` in December `2025`, fourth-quarter ad impressions increased `18%`, fourth-quarter average price per ad increased `6%`, and fourth-quarter capital expenditures were `$22.14B`.
- Management message: management emphasized strong `2025` business performance, growing AI acceleration, and a larger long-term compute buildout.
- Structural themes: this is the key quarter for the packet because it closes the annual-report year with the clearest combination of daily consumer habit, ad monetization strength, and an explicit willingness to spend heavily on AI infrastructure and talent.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: Meta broadens Technology beyond enterprise software, cloud, chips, and devices into a dominant consumer platform where monetization depends on attention, relevance, and advertising performance.
- Consumer: Meta is one of the clearest archive cases where billions of daily behaviors become a direct commercial asset.
- Cultural: the cultural signal is strong because Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, and Threads shape social communication, creator distribution, and ambient media consumption.
- AI and platform control: Meta is directly relevant to the AI platform split because it shows a large consumer-attention operator using AI to reinforce discovery, ad targeting, business messaging, and future interfaces.
- Investment concentration: Meta is also a strong capex case. The company is visibly shifting more cash flow into data-center, compute, and talent concentration to protect and extend platform power.
- Regulation and trust: this is one of the archive's clearest technology names for seeing regulation, legal proceedings, privacy pressure, and access restrictions as live earnings variables rather than background disclosures.

## Working interpretation

- Meta materially improves the archive because it gives the research base a dominant daily-use consumer platform where attention, identity, advertising, messaging, and AI relevance all reinforce one another.
- The clearest lesson is not merely that Meta has large revenue. It is that the same relationship layer can monetize through ad performance today while also justifying very large forward investment in infrastructure, models, and interface control.
- For the `2025` through `2026` collection window, Meta is one of the strongest names for connecting consumer behavior, cultural reach, AI-led monetization, and capital-intensity concentration in a single packet.
