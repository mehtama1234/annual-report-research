## What changed

- Revenue: full-year `2025` revenue reached `$11.556 billion`; quarterly revenue then moved from `$3.170 billion` in Q1 `2026` to `$3.583 billion` in Q2 `2026`.
- Mix: merchant solutions remained the core engine, at `$8.804 billion` in `2025`, then `$2.420 billion` in Q1 `2026` and `$2.781 billion` in Q2 `2026`, while subscription solutions rose from `$2.752 billion` in `2025` to `$750 million` and `$802 million` in the next two quarters.
- Gross profit: full-year `2025` gross profit reached `$5.555 billion`; Q1 `2026` gross profit reached `$1.546 billion`; Q2 `2026` gross profit reached `$1.708 billion`.
- Operating income: full-year `2025` income from operations reached `$1.468 billion`; Q1 `2026` income from operations was `$382 million`; Q2 `2026` income from operations was `$488 million`.
- Merchant throughput: full-year `2025` GMV reached `$378.4 billion`; Q1 `2026` GMV reached `$100.7 billion`; Q2 `2026` GMV reached `$115.6 billion`.
- Recurring merchant base signal: MRR reached `$205 million` at `2025-12-31`, `$212 million` in Q1 `2026`, and `$221 million` in Q2 `2026`.
- Payments attachment: Shopify Payments penetration reached `65.6%` in `2025`, then `67%` in Q1 `2026` and `68%` in Q2 `2026` on a quarterly GMV basis.
- Capital allocation: the board authorized a `$2 billion` buyback program with Q4 `2025` results; Shopify then repurchased `$514 million` of shares in Q1 `2026` and `$1.4 billion` in Q2 `2026`.

## Working interpretation

- Shopify is best understood as merchant operating infrastructure rather than as a simple storefront-software vendor.
- The company monetizes the commerce stack across subscriptions, payments, lending, advertising, channels, and merchant tooling, which makes it one of the clearest platform-dependence and recurring-service names in the technology archive.

## Open questions

- Can Shopify keep turning AI from a feature narrative into higher attachment, higher merchant retention, and stronger take-rate capture without triggering more channel dependence on large external discovery platforms?
- How durable is the current margin and cash profile if payments mix, lending exposure, and buyer-acquisition services keep growing faster than the higher-margin subscription layer?
