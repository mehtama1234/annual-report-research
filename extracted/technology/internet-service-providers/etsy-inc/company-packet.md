- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: Q2 2026
  - quarter 2: Q1 2026

## Annual report takeaways

- Etsy is a strong blind-spot case because the source problem is not absence but misframing. As of `2026-08-10`, `AnnualReports.com` still placed the company under `Services / Specialty Retail, Other` and still surfaced the `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K`, even though Etsy official IR already exposed the `2025` integrated annual report and the `2025` Form `10-K`.
- The `2025` annual evidence chain shows a company that is more useful than a generic specialty-retail label. Etsy is a creator-merchant trust marketplace, a discovery-and-self-expression commerce system, and a seller-tools plus ads and payments monetization layer.
- The full-year `2025` annual stack closed at real scale: consolidated GMS reached `$11.917B`, revenue reached `$2.884B`, revenue take rate reached `24.2%`, adjusted EBITDA reached `$734.5M`, and free cash flow reached `$638.8M`.
- The same annual materials also showed the internal composition of the system. `2025` GMS included Etsy marketplace GMS of `$10.461B`, Depop GMS of `$1.075B`, and Reverb GMS of `$381.3M`, while year-end Etsy marketplace active buyers reached `86.5M` and active sellers reached `5.6M`.
- The annual and year-end release language makes the business-model logic explicit. Management repeatedly framed Etsy as the destination for unique and creative goods, emphasized human connection as a core differentiator, and highlighted a capital-light model where services revenue and ads can widen monetization beyond the core marketplace transaction.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: `Q2 2026` continuing-operations GMS was `$2.583B`, revenue was `$668.3M`, marketplace revenue was `$456.1M`, services revenue was `$212.2M`, revenue take rate was `25.9%`, and adjusted EBITDA was `$195.4M`.
- Demand and growth: Etsy marketplace GMS grew `7.5%` year over year, while continuing-operations GMS grew `1.0%`. Revenue grew `6.2%` on a continuing-operations basis and `9.3%` for the Etsy marketplace alone.
- Buyer and relationship signals: the Q2 shareholder letter said active buyers improved by about `350K` sequentially to about `87M` on a trailing-twelve-month basis, returning to roughly stable year-over-year levels. Habitual and repeat buyer cohorts each showed slight sequential gains for the first time since `2023`.
- Behavioral and monetization signals: management said average order value remained the largest contributor to GMS growth, trailing-twelve-month GMS per active buyer increased to `$124`, and app-led behavior kept strengthening. Take-rate expansion was led primarily by Etsy Ads, with Offsite Ads also helping.
- Strategic posture: Q2 also carried the completed `Depop` sale, a restructuring plan affecting about `220` employees or roughly `12%` of the workforce, and an updated full-year outlook calling for mid-single-digit Etsy marketplace GMS growth.
- Structural themes: Q2 is strong evidence that Etsy belongs in the creator-merchant marketplace blind spot. The important question is not whether it is a retailer or an internet company. The more useful question is how trust, uniqueness, repeat-buyer deepening, and ad-supported seller monetization interact inside the platform.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `Q1 2026` continuing-operations GMS was `$2.460B`, revenue was `$631.3M`, marketplace revenue was `$432.8M`, services revenue was `$198.5M`, revenue take rate was `25.7%`, and adjusted EBITDA was `$184.7M`.
- Demand and growth: Etsy marketplace GMS was about `$2.5B`, up `5.5%` year over year, with currency-neutral growth of `3.6%`.
- Buyer and app signals: management said active buyers grew sequentially for the first time in two years, active sellers and GMS per buyer also improved, and mobile-app GMS continued to outpace non-app GMS while reaching about `47%` of total GMS.
- Cash and capital signals: net cash provided by operating activities of continuing operations was `$102.5M`, and management said it converted about `50%` of adjusted EBITDA to free cash flow during the quarter.
- Guidance signal: after the stronger first quarter, management said it expected Etsy marketplace GMS to grow in the low-single-digit range for the full year `2026`, with year-over-year Etsy GMS growth expected in each quarter of the year.
- Structural themes: Q1 shows that Etsy is thickening the relationship system rather than just waiting for a macro bounce. The company is trying to make app usage, repeat behavior, and discovery quality more important than one-time transaction traffic alone.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: the FY `2025` / `Q4 2025` release said consolidated Q4 GMS was `$3.593B`, revenue was `$881.6M`, and take rate was `24.5%`, while FY `2025` GMS reached `$11.917B`, revenue reached `$2.884B`, and adjusted EBITDA reached `$734.5M`.
- Etsy marketplace-specific read: Etsy marketplace Q4 GMS was `$3.293B`, up `0.1%` year over year. Etsy marketplace app GMS grew `6.6%` and represented about `46%` of GMS.
- Buyer and seller signals: year-end Etsy marketplace active sellers totaled `5.6M`, active buyers totaled `86.5M`, and trailing-twelve-month habitual buyers totaled `5.9M`.
- Strategic transition: the FY `2025` release also announced the agreement to sell `Depop` to `eBay` for `$1.2B` in cash, sharpening the company toward a cleaner core Etsy marketplace story. `Reverb` had already been sold earlier in `2025`.
- Operating and platform signals: management said growth in revenue was driven primarily by continued strong consolidated on-site ads performance and emphasized AI-shopping partnerships with `OpenAI`, `Google`, `Microsoft`, and `Stripe`.
- Structural themes: Q4 `2025` is the bridge quarter that makes Etsy analytically useful. It shows a marketplace where the quality of discovery, buyer-seller trust, app engagement, and ad monetization matter more than any old retail bucket on the source taxonomy page.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- AnnualReports mismatch as signal: Etsy materially strengthens the blind-spot lane because the source taxonomy gives it a technically valid page but a weak frame. `Services / Specialty Retail, Other` misses the real comparison set.
- Creator-merchant trust marketplace: Etsy is the archive’s cleaner case for a marketplace built around uniqueness, craft, taste, and seller identity rather than broad recommerce, commodity assortment, or fulfillment scale.
- Relationship ownership thickening: the `2026` quarter letters repeatedly emphasize active-buyer stabilization, repeat behavior, app penetration, and future loyalty work. That is relationship-system deepening, not just volume recovery.
- Second monetization layer: the annual and quarter materials make it clear that services revenue, Etsy Ads, Offsite Ads, and payments economics are material to the model. This is a real platform-style monetization stack under a consumer-facing marketplace.
- Portfolio simplification and focus: `Reverb` was sold in `2025` and `Depop` was sold on `2026-07-30`, which makes Etsy more useful as a focused core marketplace case rather than a looser portfolio of adjacent platforms.
- Comparative positioning: Etsy is most useful next to `eBay`, `Amazon`, `Airbnb`, and `Instacart` in the blind-spot work, not next to ordinary specialty retail or generic software names.

## Working interpretation

- Etsy materially strengthens the blind-spot lane because it is the archive’s better lighter-weight marketplace case for creator-led, trust-heavy, identity-adjacent commerce.
- `Amazon` shows scale marketplace plus logistics and ads. `eBay` shows recommerce and enthusiast discovery. `Instacart` shows grocery-demand aggregation and retailer enablement. Etsy adds the handmade, creative-merchant, and human-connection version of the same broader marketplace blind spot.
- Together these names show why the `AnnualReports` tree is too blunt for the real research objective. The important comparison set is not old retail or internet-service labels. It is:
  - scale marketplace and infrastructure through `Amazon`
  - recommerce and enthusiast discovery through `eBay`
  - grocery-demand aggregation and retailer enablement through `Instacart`
  - creator-merchant trust and identity commerce through `Etsy`
