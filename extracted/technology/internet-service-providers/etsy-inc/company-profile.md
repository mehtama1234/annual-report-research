## What changed

- AnnualReports blind spot: Etsy is covered by `AnnualReports.com`, but the `Services / Specialty Retail, Other` label and lagging `2024` annual package make it a materially weaker source frame than Etsy's actual operating model.
- Marketplace trust layer: Etsy is more usefully understood as a creator-merchant marketplace built around trust, discovery, uniqueness, and seller identity rather than as a plain retailer.
- Relationship thickening: the `2026` quarter letters show the company explicitly leaning into direct relationships, app-led shopping, loyalty work, and repeat-buyer behavior rather than just gross merchandise volume.
- Second monetization layer: the `2025` annual and `2026` quarter materials show meaningful services revenue, Etsy Ads strength, and payments-related monetization expansion, which is exactly the sort of second-layer economics the source taxonomy does not show.
- Portfolio simplification: the period also captures strategic cleanup, with `Reverb` already sold in `2025` and `Depop` sold to `eBay` on `2026-07-30`, which sharpens Etsy back toward a more focused core marketplace story.

## Open questions

- Is Etsy’s long-term moat primarily trust and differentiated inventory, or the increasingly owned relationship through app behavior, loyalty work, and repeat-buyer deepening?
- How durable is recent Etsy marketplace GMS improvement if the macro backdrop weakens or if the high-AOV mix benefit fades?
- Does the stronger blind-spot framing come more from creator-commerce identity and merchant trust, or from the seller-tools and ad-monetization layer sitting behind the marketplace?
