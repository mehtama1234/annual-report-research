## Reconciliation notes

- `AnnualReports.com` does cover `Etsy`, but it does so in a way that weakens the real comparison set. The page is classified as `Services / Specialty Retail, Other`, which is materially less useful than the company’s operating reality.
- The annual-package lag also matters. As of `2026-08-10`, `AnnualReports.com` still surfaced the `2024` annual package while official IR already exposed the `2025` integrated annual report and the `2025` Form `10-K`.
- The most useful annual interpretation comes from combining the `2025` integrated annual report PDF, the `2025` Form `10-K`, and the FY `2025` results release.
- The quarter letters matter because they make Etsy’s model legible as:
  - a creator-merchant trust marketplace
  - a discovery and self-expression commerce system
  - a seller-tools, ads, and payments monetization layer
