## Annual report takeaways

- Taboola is a strong media-fragmentation packet for this frontier because it sits in the demand-and-distribution layer of the open web. It is not a publisher, not a carrier, and not a pure ad-verification firm. It monetizes the space between advertiser intent and publisher or OEM distribution, especially outside the dominant search and social platforms.
- The official investor framing is unusually direct. Taboola says it empowers businesses to grow through performance advertising technology that goes beyond search and social and delivers measurable outcomes at scale. That matters because it makes the packet a direct read on what the open internet thinks its next economic story has to be.
- The annual chain also gives the frontier a broader cultural and industrial signal. Taboola says it reaches over `600M` daily active users across publishers and OEMs, and it specifically highlights relationships such as `Yahoo`, `NBC News`, `Samsung`, and `Xiaomi`. That shows how attention is being aggregated through distributed surfaces rather than one owned destination.
- The `2025` annual financial shape is strong enough to matter. Full-year `2025` revenue reached about `$1.912B`; ex-TAC Gross Profit reached about `$713.5M`; adjusted EBITDA reached about `$215.5M`; free cash flow reached about `$163.4M`; and full-year net income improved to about `$42.3M` from a loss in `2024`.
- The most important annual interpretation is strategic, not just numeric. `2025` is framed by management as a turning point validating `Realize`, Taboola’s larger performance-advertising platform. That makes the packet useful for understanding how open-web advertising tries to evolve from content recommendation and native placements into a broader alternative customer-acquisition system.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: Q2 `2026` revenue was about `$476.8M`, up `2.4%`; gross profit was about `$139.5M`, up `2.9%`; ex-TAC Gross Profit was about `$192.4M`, up `11.8%`; net income was about `$4.3M`, improved from a net loss of about `$(4.3)M`; adjusted EBITDA was about `$55.5M`, up `22.8%`; free cash flow was about `$17.3M`.
- Margin and guidance signals: adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to about `28.8%` from `26.2%`. Management said the company again beat ex-TAC Gross Profit and adjusted EBITDA guidance and raised full-year guidance for both metrics.
- Platform signal: the quarter supports the idea that `Realize` is becoming the company’s main growth story rather than a side product. The economic mix is improving even with modest top-line growth.
- Interpretation: Q2 is a strong frontier quarter because it shows a company with moderate revenue growth but stronger profit conversion, which suggests the open-web ad stack is trying to win on efficiency and measurable outcomes rather than on raw traffic growth alone.
- Bigger-picture read: this quarter makes Taboola useful as a frontier packet because it shows how performance demand can be rerouted into publisher and OEM surfaces outside the biggest walled gardens, while still being framed in the language of AI, automation, and measurable outcomes.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: Q1 `2026` revenue was about `$466.4M`, up `9.1%`; ex-TAC Gross Profit was about `$168.1M`, up `10.8%`; net income was about `$59.1M`, improving from a net loss of about `$(8.8)M`; adjusted EBITDA was about `$26.7M`; adjusted EBITDA margin was about `15.9%`; free cash flow was about `$90.3M`, compared to about `$36.1M` a year earlier.
- Operating signals: management described the quarter as exceeding the high end of guidance, which matters because it shows Realize-led growth arriving with stronger-than-expected cash generation.
- Strategic signal: the quarter came with explicit messaging around distribution breadth and product expansion beyond search and social, reinforcing the company’s effort to claim a durable category instead of a niche ad format.
- Interpretation: Q1 is the clearest operating quarter in the chain because it shows open-web performance demand still growing at a healthy rate while profit and cash flow improved much faster.
- Bigger-picture read: the quarter suggests that fragmentation does not necessarily mean economic weakness. It can also mean more auction surfaces and more monetizable interfaces for a company that can aggregate intent and distribution well enough.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: Q4 `2025` revenue was about `$522.3M`; gross profit was about `$175.6M`; ex-TAC Gross Profit was about `$212.8M`; net income was about `$50.1M`; adjusted EBITDA was about `$86.1M`; free cash flow was about `$46.9M`.
- Full-year `2025` results: revenue reached about `$1.912B`; gross profit reached about `$569.5M`; ex-TAC Gross Profit reached about `$713.5M`; net income reached about `$42.3M`; adjusted EBITDA reached about `$215.5M`; free cash flow reached about `$163.4M`.
- Capital and balance-sheet signals: year-end cash, cash equivalents, short-term deposits, and investments were about `$120.9M`; long-term loan and revolving credit facility balance was about `$102.3M`; management emphasized share-count reduction and ongoing investment in AI-driven innovation.
- Interpretation: Q4/FY2025 is the quarter that makes Taboola a frontier-quality packet rather than just another ad-tech name. Management explicitly calls `2025` a turning point validating `Realize`, accelerating growth, improving advertiser outcomes, and differentiating through proprietary intent data plus deep open-web distribution.
- Bigger-picture read: this quarter shows the open web trying to defend its relevance by becoming more performance-driven, more AI-mediated, and more economically accountable. Taboola’s pitch is that search and social are not the only scaled acquisition channels that can deliver outcomes.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- The open web is trying to build its own performance layer: Taboola is a strong packet for showing how publishers, OEMs, and recommendation surfaces are being reorganized into an alternative customer-acquisition network.
- Distribution itself is a strategic asset: the company’s scale comes from sitting across many publisher and device endpoints rather than from owning one dominant consumer app.
- AI is becoming an interface and monetization bridge: summer `2026` company messaging around AI answer engines, conversational AI, `DeeperDive`, and `Realize+` suggests the business is trying to stay relevant as traffic shifts beyond classic article-page clickthroughs.
- Yahoo and large-partner exposure still matter: the packet remains useful because it combines frontier growth language with the older operating truth that a few large relationships can still shape the economics.
- Fragmented attention is being recentralized through workflow, not only content ownership: Taboola does not need to own the content brand if it can own enough of the advertiser intent, recommendation logic, and distribution rails.
- Performance demand can stabilize the lane differently from pure brand advertising: compared with publishers or broadcasters that depend more on cyclical brand budgets, Taboola is much more exposed to measurable outcomes and direct-response logic.

## Working interpretation

- Taboola materially deepens the first sub-lane because it gives the frontier another strong media-fragmentation anchor, but from the advertiser-demand and open-web distribution side rather than from measurement or verification.
- The key repeated pattern here is that the non-walled-garden internet is trying to survive by becoming more performance-native. Taboola’s language about going beyond search and social is not just branding. It is a direct statement about how customer acquisition is being reorganized.
- The societal and cultural signal underneath the numbers is that attention is moving through more surfaces than ever: publisher pages, OEM feeds, recommendation units, chat and answer interfaces, and AI-mediated discovery layers. Taboola is trying to sit inside that sprawl as the transaction and routing layer.
- For this frontier, Taboola also helps tie together `NYT`, `Fox`, `Magnite`, `PubMatic`, and `DoubleVerify`. Those packets show trusted content, scaled video attention, sell-side routing, open-internet automation, and independent proof. Taboola adds the demand-routing and recommendation-distribution side of the same fragmented system.
