## What changed

- Akamai gives CLI 5 a cleaner edge-distribution and security-control bridge than a pure CDN legacy case. It is no longer mainly an internet-delivery utility. It is becoming a mix of delivery infrastructure, application security, zero-trust security, and distributed cloud infrastructure.
- The `2025` annual package makes the transition explicit. Management says security solutions now generate the largest portion of revenue, while delivery still faces DIY substitution and pricing pressure.
- The latest three reported quarters show the same pattern intensifying: security and Cloud Infrastructure Services continue to grow, while delivery and other cloud applications continue to decline.
- The company is also useful because it sits between classic internet plumbing and newer AI-economy infrastructure. Management is tying CIS demand to frontier-model and robotics workloads while still monetizing the older internet-delivery and application-protection layers underneath business traffic.

## Open questions

- How durable is Akamai's security-and-cloud transition if delivery remains structurally pressured and if CIS wins require heavier capital or lower margin than the legacy software-security mix?
- Does Akamai belong closer to the security-control layer, to the hidden edge-and-delivery infrastructure layer, or as its own hybrid bridge between them?
- If AI workloads increasingly need distributed cloud and edge proximity, can Akamai become a more important infrastructure-control layer than investors still assume?
