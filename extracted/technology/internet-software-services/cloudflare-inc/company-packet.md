# Cloudflare, Inc. Company Packet

## Why this company belongs in CLI 5

Cloudflare fills a missing control layer inside CLI 5. The company is classified under `Internet Software & Services`, but its economic role is broader than a point product or a simple content-delivery network. It monetizes a unified edge-native stack spanning connectivity, Zero Trust, application protection, developer tooling, Workers runtime, AI Gateway, and increasingly the machine-to-machine control rails that management describes as the `Agentic Internet`. That makes Cloudflare one of the best direct contrasts to Akamai's transition story and one of the strongest bridges between trust software, runtime infrastructure, and internet-edge control.

## Annual anchor: year ended `2025-12-31`

The annual filing and annual-report package show Cloudflare becoming a stronger proof that one of the AI-era tolls sits at the protected edge and developer-runtime layer, not only in raw compute, cybersecurity policy, or physical networking hardware. Fiscal `2025` revenue reached roughly `$2.168B`. Remaining performance obligations reached about `$2.496B`. Gross profit remained high at roughly `$1.615B`, or about `75%` gross margin. Net cash provided by operating activities reached about `$603.1M`, and free cash flow reached about `$260.6M`.

The strategy language matters as much as the metrics. The annual materials repeatedly emphasize `Cloudflare One`, `SASE`, developer-based solutions including `Cloudflare Workers`, `Workers AI`, and `AI Gateway`. The practical implication is that Cloudflare is not merely trying to optimize web traffic. It is trying to own the layer where enterprises secure traffic, place applications, run code close to users and data, and increasingly govern AI-driven interactions at the internet edge.

## What this company helps prove

- Sector role: Cloudflare is a connectivity-cloud and protected-runtime control layer rather than a plain delivery network or a pure cybersecurity point tool.
- Consumer signal: consumers and workers increasingly assume that applications, commerce, media, and AI-assisted services should be fast, reliable, and secure everywhere, which raises the value of the invisible edge stack underneath them.
- Cultural and societal signal: the broader internet is normalizing machine-assisted interaction, low tolerance for latency, and low tolerance for security failure at the same time.
- Institutional signal: enterprises are increasingly willing to consolidate around platforms that combine network services, security controls, developer tooling, and workload placement rather than buying those layers separately.
- Technology signal: Cloudflare shows that AI monetization is not only about chips, models, or governed data. There is also a meaningful toll at the layer that secures, routes, executes, and increasingly mediates agent traffic.
- Industrial signal: the company's recurring economics come from platform attachment, developer adoption, and traffic or security dependence, while still carrying more infrastructure reality than the cleanest pure software-governance names.
- Capital-allocation signal: Cloudflare is lighter than telecom and data-center landlords, but heavier than the narrowest trust software vendors because the network footprint and runtime layer themselves matter.

## Working interpretation

Cloudflare strengthens CLI 5 because it shows that a major AI-era profit pool may sit in the hidden layer between software governance and physical network ownership. Akamai already proved that protected edge execution and internet-facing application security can become a recurring toll even for a company with legacy delivery baggage. Cloudflare goes further by starting from a more integrated platform identity: network services, Zero Trust, developer runtime, Workers, AI Gateway, and now `Agentic Internet` control.

Compared with Akamai, Cloudflare looks more natively aligned with the newer platform direction. Akamai is still managing the tension between declining delivery economics and expanding security plus cloud relevance. Cloudflare still has margin tension and infrastructure burden, but its management language, product mix, and growth story are already oriented around the next stack. Compared with CrowdStrike or Zscaler, Cloudflare is less purely a trust-policy layer and more of a runtime plus traffic plus developer-control layer. Compared with Snowflake, it governs safe execution and internet-facing control rather than governed enterprise data context.

The caution is that Cloudflare still has to prove how cleanly this category scales. Gross margins have come down from older levels, free-cash-flow conversion is not as pristine as the lightest software-control names, and management is already restructuring around the AI opportunity. Even so, Cloudflare adds one of the most important missing categories to CLI 5: the edge-native platform that may mediate more of the internet as AI agents, machine-to-machine traffic, and protected distributed execution become normal.
