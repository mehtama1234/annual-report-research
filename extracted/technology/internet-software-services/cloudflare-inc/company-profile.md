## What changed

- Cloudflare gives CLI 5 a much cleaner edge-native counterpart to Akamai. Instead of a legacy delivery network trying to climb toward security and runtime control, Cloudflare already presents itself as a unified connectivity cloud spanning network services, Zero Trust, developer tooling, Workers, AI Gateway, and increasingly AI-agent infrastructure.
- The `2025` annual package shows the scale of that model. Fiscal `2025` revenue reached roughly `$2.168B`, remaining performance obligations reached `$2.496B`, gross margin stayed high, and free cash flow reached about `$260.6M`.
- The quarter chain then makes the strategic tilt explicit. Q1 `2026` called AI the biggest tailwind in Cloudflare's history while also pairing that opportunity with a restructuring toward an `agentic AI-first operating model`. Q2 `2026` pushed the framing further by describing Cloudflare as sitting at the center of `AI answer engines`, `agent-driven commerce`, and the `Agentic Internet`.
- This makes Cloudflare one of the clearest CLI 5 bridges between security control, protected runtime placement, developer platform monetization, and the newer machine-to-machine internet layer that AI agents may require.

## Open questions

- Can Cloudflare keep the edge-native platform narrative cleaner than Akamai while still absorbing the infrastructure and go-to-market burden that comes with running a broad connectivity cloud?
- Does Cloudflare belong closer to trust software, to developer platform infrastructure, or as its own internet-edge control category that sits between those stacks?
- How durable is the `Agentic Internet` framing if AI-traffic monetization grows faster than traditional web-delivery or enterprise security budgets?
