## Reconciliation notes

- Cloudflare now has a usable annual-plus-quarter evidence chain for the `2025` annual window and latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10`:
  - AnnualReports archive confirmation
  - official SEC `10-K` and `ARS` annual package
  - SEC `8-K`, earnings exhibit, and `10-Q` chain for `Q1 2026`
  - SEC `8-K`, earnings exhibit, and `10-Q` chain for `Q2 2026`
  - official Cloudflare results routing plus the year-end `10-K` for `Q4 2025`
- Important correction:
  - `2025-q4-8k.html` on disk is not the year-end earnings wrapper; it is the `2026-02-10` filing with primary document `cloud-20260206.htm`
  - `2025-q4-ex99-1.html` on disk is only a `NoSuchKey` error page from a guessed SEC path
  - those two files should not be used as substantive quarter evidence
- The quarter-level `Q4 2025` metrics used in the packet come from the official issuer results route and are reconciled back to the `2025` annual filing.
- The Cloudflare IR HTML pages and the `Q4 2025` investor presentation PDF were shell-collected only as routing artifacts because direct substantive page fetches were challenge-blocked by Cloudflare in this environment.
