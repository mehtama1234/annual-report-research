## What changed

- DigitalOcean gives CLI 5 a lightweight developer-and-builder infrastructure layer rather than an enterprise-heavy control plane or a physical chokepoint. It monetizes simpler cloud infrastructure for smaller teams, startups, digital businesses, and increasingly AI-native builders.
- The `2025` annual filing shows a company with improving scale and cash generation before the more aggressive AI acceleration that appears in `2026`. Revenue reached `$901.4M`, gross profit reached `$539.6M`, operating income reached `$157.0M`, net income reached `$259.3M`, operating cash flow reached `$309.6M`, and cash and cash equivalents ended the year at `$254.5M`.
- The quarter chain shows the model shifting faster toward larger and more AI-intensive customers. `Q4 2025` revenue reached `$242.4M`, `Q1 2026` revenue reached `$257.9M`, and `Q2 2026` revenue reached `$281.2M`, while AI customer ARR and million-plus-dollar customer ARR rose sharply.
- The strongest interpretive change is that DigitalOcean is no longer just a simplified cloud host for small developers. The filings increasingly position it as an `AI-Native Cloud` or `agentic inference cloud`, trying to capture AI growth without becoming a full hyperscaler.
- That shift matters for CLI 5 because it adds a distinct lane test: whether a lighter builder-first cloud platform can capture a real infrastructure toll by simplifying adoption for startups, SMBs, digital businesses, and AI-native customers that do not want the full complexity of the largest cloud platforms.
