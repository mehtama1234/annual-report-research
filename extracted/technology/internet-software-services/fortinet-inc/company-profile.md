## What changed

- Product cycle strength returned: FY2025 product revenue grew `16%`, then quarterly product revenue accelerated from `$691M` in Q4 2025 to `$645M` in Q1 2026 and `$773M` in Q2 2026, showing that Fortinet’s hardware and appliance layer still matters when upgrade cycles and AI-era infrastructure demand reaccelerate.
- Service layer remained the anchor: FY2025 service revenue reached `$4.58B`, or about two-thirds of total revenue. Even with stronger product growth, the company still reads as a recurring service-and-subscription franchise wrapped around a large installed hardware base.
- Billings and deferred revenue remained strong: FY2025 billings were `$7.55B`, Q1 2026 billings were `$2.09B`, and Q2 2026 billings were `$2.37B`. Deferred revenue rose to `$7.12B` at year-end 2025 and current deferred revenue rose from `$3.64B` at year-end to `$3.84B` by June 30, 2026.
- Cash generation stayed unusually high: FY2025 operating cash flow reached `$2.59B` and free cash flow reached `$2.21B`. Q1 2026 free cash flow was about `$1.01B` and Q2 2026 free cash flow was `$966M`, which is an important contrast with many infrastructure-software names that still have much lighter real cash conversion.
- Platform perimeter widened: the live IR site and quarter chain show the company leaning harder into `Unified SASE`, `Security Operations`, `OT Security`, `AI Security`, and `FortiGuard AI Services` rather than remaining only a firewall vendor.
- Industrial footprint matters more than pure software identity: Fortinet serves enterprises, service providers, government, industrial-edge, and mobile-network environments. That makes it a better infrastructure and physical-network-adjacent name than a simple cybersecurity-software label would suggest.

## Open questions

- Can Fortinet keep extending from firewall dominance into higher-growth SASE, SecOps, OT, and AI-security lanes without letting investors reclassify it as a lower-multiple appliance vendor whenever product cycles normalize?
- Does the product rebound reflect durable platform pull-through, or is part of the 2026 acceleration a catch-up cycle that will be harder to sustain once the refresh wave passes?
