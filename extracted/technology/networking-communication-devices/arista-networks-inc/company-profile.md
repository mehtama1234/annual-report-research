## What the company is

Arista is a client-to-cloud networking company focused on AI fabrics, datacenter switching and routing, campus networking, observability, and network operations software. In the current archive it is the cleanest incremental technology comparison for AI-networking and control-plane demand between the hyperscale cloud layer and the broader enterprise infrastructure layer.

## Why Arista matters in this archive

- It strengthens the networking and control-plane side of the technology framework with a more direct AI-fabric winner than `Cisco`.
- It helps show that the AI buildout is not only compute and cloud spend. Ethernet fabrics, observability, campus, routing, and operational control are becoming monetizable bottlenecks too.
- It adds a high-margin, software-augmented networking operator that is structurally stronger than the weaker hardware and turnaround names already in the set.

## 2025 annual highlights

- Full-year 2025 revenue: `$9.006B`
- Full-year 2025 GAAP net income / diluted EPS: `$3.511B` / `$2.75`
- Management said 2025 was a validation year for `Arista 2.0`, with cumulative shipments reaching `150M` ports.
- The annual materials and year-end release emphasized AI networking, campus expansion, CloudVision, network observability, and a `client-to-cloud` platform spanning AI centers, data centers, campuses, and WAN environments.

## Early read

- Arista is one of the clearest proof points that AI infrastructure economics extend into networking fabrics and control planes, not just GPUs and hyperscale cloud.
- It materially improves the archive because it sharpens the distinction between mature installed-base networking exposure and a faster AI-networking winner with stronger direct growth.
- Arista is also a useful bridge between `NVIDIA` and `Cisco`: it is closer than Cisco to the AI-fabric growth engine, but still monetizes a networking-and-operations layer rather than the underlying compute silicon.
