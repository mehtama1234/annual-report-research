## What Vistance adds to the broader technology read

- A stressed communications-infrastructure and carrier-budget case rather than another clean software or hyperscale beneficiary
- Direct evidence that bandwidth demand does not guarantee durable value capture for every network vendor; asset quality, portfolio position, and capital structure matter as much as category exposure
- A useful comparison against stronger physical-layer winners such as Ciena, Corning, Amphenol, and Cloudflare because Vistance shows what remains after advantaged assets are sold and the residual platform has to stand on narrower economics
- A clean archive example of how debt, restructuring, component exposure, channel dependence, and operator capex timing can dominate the story even inside a technology lane tied to network growth
