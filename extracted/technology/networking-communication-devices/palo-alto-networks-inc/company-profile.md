## What changed

- Revenue: full-year FY2025 revenue reached `$9.2215B`; the latest three reported quarters then moved from `$2.5B` in FY2026 Q1 to `$2.6B` in FY2026 Q2 and `$3.0B` in FY2026 Q3.
- Margin: full-year FY2025 operating income reached `$1.2429B`, equal to a `13.5%` operating margin, and net income reached `$1.1339B`; the quarter set then showed GAAP net income of `$334M` and `$432M` in FY2026 Q1 and Q2 before a GAAP net loss of `$177M` in FY2026 Q3 as acquisition-related effects became visible, while non-GAAP EPS stayed at `$0.93`, `$1.03`, and `$0.85`.
- Cash flow: full-year FY2025 cash flow from operations reached `$3.716B` and non-GAAP free cash flow reached `$3.4698B`; FY2026 Q3 alone delivered `$871M` of operating cash flow and `$910M` of adjusted free cash flow, with trailing-12-month adjusted free-cash-flow margin at `38.5%`.
- Backlog and commitments: NGS ARR advanced from `$5.9B` in FY2026 Q1 to `$6.3B` in FY2026 Q2 and `$8.1B` in FY2026 Q3, while RPO expanded from `$15.5B` to `$16.0B` to `$18.4B`.
- Capital allocation: Palo Alto remains a cash-generative software-and-platform operator with high deferred-demand visibility rather than a capex-heavy infrastructure owner; the emphasis is on margin discipline, acquisition integration, and platform-scale sales execution.
- Segment mix: subscription and support revenue made up `80.5%` of total FY2025 revenue, showing how much of the economic center has shifted from hardware into recurring software, cloud, and support layers.
- Operational issues: management is using platformization and AI-security positioning to consolidate customer spend across network, cloud, security-operations, identity, and observability layers, but the model now carries heavier integration and accounting noise from CyberArk and Chronosphere.
- Consumer / cultural signals: Palo Alto is not a consumer-demand read; it is a trust, threat, and enterprise-risk read showing that AI adoption expands cybersecurity budgets, governance needs, and willingness to standardize on fewer vendors.
- Management tone: management is direct and aggressive, framing the company as the data-and-security partner of choice in the AI era and repeatedly arguing that fragmented defenses are failing against modern threats.
- CLI 5 role: Palo Alto is the security-control and trust-infrastructure layer that tests whether the strongest infrastructure economics increasingly sit in software-governed assurance rather than only in traffic carriage or networking hardware.

## Open questions

- Does Palo Alto’s platformization story create durable control-layer pricing power, or does it increasingly depend on acquisition breadth and cross-sell complexity?
- How much of FY2026 Q3 acceleration is durable organic demand versus the near-term reporting effect of CyberArk and Chronosphere?
