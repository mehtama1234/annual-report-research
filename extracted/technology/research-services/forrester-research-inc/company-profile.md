## What changed

- Exact category closure: this is the cleanest exact-category `Research Services` addition available in the current frontier and materially improves the archive beyond relying on adjacent research or advisory proxies.
- Business model clarity: Forrester is selling interpreted judgment, workflow relevance, and client retention infrastructure rather than only selling data. The materials repeatedly frame the model around research, consulting, events, continuous guidance, and customer-obsession execution.
- AI repositioning: the quarter chain repeatedly moves AI from theme language into product strategy. Q4 `2025` highlights the launch of `Forrester AI Access`; Q1 and Q2 `2026` highlight AI-enabled workflow integration and accelerated Forrester AI adoption.
- Operating pressure: despite the AI push, the annual and quarter chain show declining contract value, declining revenue, restructuring, and large goodwill impairment charges tied to weak bookings, macro pressure, and stock-price deterioration.
- Portfolio narrowing: the `2025` annual materials explicitly say strategy consulting will be discontinued for new selling and the remaining consulting business will center on content marketing consulting and advisory.
- Decision-market read: this is not just a technology-research company. It is a direct lens into how institutions buy outside judgment when technology change, customer-experience pressure, and AI adoption overwhelm in-house capacity.

## Open questions

- Can Forrester stabilize or grow contract value once the restructuring and AI product push are fully in market?
- Is AI adoption a true pricing and retention lever, or mainly a defensive response to erosion in legacy research subscriptions?
- How much of future margin improvement depends on cost takeout and portfolio pruning versus real demand recovery in core research memberships?
