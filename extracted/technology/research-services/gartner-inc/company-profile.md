## What changed

- Lane fit: Gartner is one of the strongest institutional-interface anchors available for this frontier because it monetizes research, executive decision support, conferences, and consulting tied directly to enterprise uncertainty and organizational adaptation.
- Business framing: the `2025` annual report says Gartner delivers actionable, objective business and technology insights on mission-critical priorities and serves more than `13,000` enterprises across about `90` countries and territories.
- Segment structure: the company now frames its core segments as `Business and Technology Insights`, `Conferences`, and `Consulting`, with the former `Research` segment renamed in `2025` to better describe the value provided to clients.
- Annual scale: the `2025` filing says total revenue reached about `$6.5B`, Insights revenue reached about `$5.1B`, Conferences revenue reached about `$644.7M`, Consulting revenue reached about `$552.5M`, and year-end contract value reached about `$5.2B`.
- Operating indicators: management explicitly tracks contract value, consulting backlog growth, client retention, wallet retention, conference attendance and exhibitors, and consulting utilization. The annual filing says Insights client retention was `85%` in `2025`, up from `84%` in `2024`.
- Competitive pressure: the company says it expects more competition from increased adoption of AI services, which makes Gartner useful for reading how institutional knowledge intermediaries defend value when more information becomes cheap or machine-generated.

## Open questions

- Can Gartner keep pricing power and retention high as AI tools compress the perceived scarcity of generic information?
- How much of current demand is driven by durable mission-critical advisory needs versus temporary uncertainty around technology, macro conditions, and organizational change?
- Does the shift toward broader business-and-technology insights make Gartner more resilient, or does it expose the company to a wider set of competitors across software, consulting, and data products?
