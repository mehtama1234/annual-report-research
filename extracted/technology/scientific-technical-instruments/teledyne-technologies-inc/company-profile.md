## What changed

- Revenue scale: fiscal `2025` net sales reached `$6,115.4M`; quarterly sales then moved from `$1,612.3M` in Q4 `2025` to `$1,560.1M` in Q1 `2026` and `$1,662.5M` in Q2 `2026`.
- Earnings quality: full-year `2025` diluted EPS was `$18.88`; quarterly non-GAAP EPS was `$6.30` in Q4 `2025`, `$5.80` in Q1 `2026`, and `$6.28` in Q2 `2026`.
- Cash generation: Teledyne emphasized more than `$1.0B` of free cash flow for the second consecutive year at the end of `2025`; quarterly cash from operations and free cash flow were `$379.0M` and `$339.2M` in Q4 `2025`, `$234.0M` and `$204.3M` in Q1 `2026`, and `$315.2M` and `$284.7M` in Q2 `2026`.
- Segment mix: Teledyne’s structure is broader than a pure test-and-measurement company, with `Digital Imaging`, `Instrumentation`, `Aerospace and Defense Electronics`, and `Engineered Systems`.
- Demand tone: the quarter materials showed particularly strong momentum in infrared detectors, defense surveillance systems, unmanned and counter-unmanned applications, marine instrumentation, environmental sensing, and selected test-and-measurement lines.
- Balance-sheet use: management paired strong cash generation with debt reduction and acquisition activity, including a `$450M` debt maturity payment during Q2 `2026` and continued review of additional acquisitions.
- Taxonomy mismatch: AnnualReports places Teledyne in `Industrial Goods` and `Aerospace/Defense Products & Services`, but the operating evidence shows the company also belongs in a broader instrumentation-and-sensing infrastructure map.

## Working interpretation

- Teledyne is one of the clearest ways to capture the broader sensing-and-instrumentation layer behind defense modernization, marine and environmental monitoring, industrial imaging, and selected electronic test markets.
- The bigger lesson is that the physical layer behind AI, autonomy, defense, energy, and industrial automation is not just about moving data. It is also about collecting, imaging, detecting, and interpreting it in hard environments.

## Open questions

- How durable is the current defense, space, and unmanned-systems acceleration relative to the more cyclical commercial short-cycle instrumentation businesses?
- Can Teledyne keep its acquisition-driven sensing-and-mission-systems platform coherent without slipping into a conglomerate discount as the portfolio broadens further?
