## What changed

- Zscaler is one of the cleanest trust-governance additions to CLI 5. The fiscal `2025` annual anchor and the FY2026 `Q1` through `Q3` chain show a company turning Zero Trust access control, AI security, and data-governance burden into high-quality recurring revenue, strong RPO growth, and durable cash generation.
- The official annual source chain is lagged outside the SEC. On Monday, August 10, 2026, both AnnualReports and Zscaler's own annual-reports page still pointed to `2024`, so the fiscal `2025` SEC `10-K` is the controlling annual authority for the packet.
- The quarter chain showed sustained scale and quality. Revenue moved from `$788.1M` to `$815.8M` to `$850.5M`; ARR moved from `$3.204B` to `$3.359B` to `$3.525B`; deferred revenue moved from `$2.351B` to `$2.355B` to `$2.477B`; and non-GAAP operating margin moved from `22%` to `22%` to `23%`.
- The strategic clue is broader than secure web gateways or network access. Management repeatedly framed Zscaler as a cybersecurity platform for the AI era, with demand centered on AI Security, Zero Trust Everywhere, Data Security, AI-agent identity, sovereignty, and policy control across distributed enterprise workflows.
- The acquisition pattern matters. Red Canary, SPLX, SquareX, and the intended Symmetry Systems acquisition all extend Zscaler from core Zero Trust access control into MDR, AI red teaming, browser security, and AI-agent communication governance.
- The IR home page reinforces the latest operating picture. On `2026-08-10`, the live site highlighted Q3'26 revenue of roughly `$850M`, ARR of `$3.525B`, RPO of `$6.459B`, `748` customers with `$1M+` ARR, `4,003` customers with `$100K+` ARR, approximately `81%` non-GAAP gross margin, `23%` non-GAAP operating margin, `16%` free-cash-flow margin, and about `$3.5B` of cash and short-term investments.

## Open questions

- Does Zscaler now make the trust-governance layer of CLI 5 cleaner than the broader Palo Alto and CrowdStrike reads, or does it simply isolate the access-and-policy portion of the same profit pool?
- How durable is the current AI-security and AI-agent-governance narrative if enterprise AI budgets consolidate into a narrower set of bundled platforms?
- Can Zscaler keep expanding into MDR, browser isolation, and AI-agent governance without diluting the simplicity and margin quality that currently make it a clean control-point read?
