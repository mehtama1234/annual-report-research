## What Intel adds to the broader technology read

- A weaker-moat and more stressed semiconductor incumbent
- Direct exposure to process-node execution and U.S.-based leading-edge manufacturing ambitions
- A capital-heavy foundry and packaging story rather than a cleaner fabless AI supplier story
- A test of whether AI demand can support a turnaround instead of only rewarding already-dominant platforms
