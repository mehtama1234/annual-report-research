## What the company is

Intel is the most useful current technology-sector evidence set for a capital-heavy semiconductor incumbent trying to rebuild execution, process leadership, and foundry relevance while still carrying large legacy scale across CPUs, datacenter compute, and enterprise infrastructure.

## Why Intel matters in this archive

- It adds a weaker-moat and more operationally stressed technology case to a sector set that otherwise leaned heavily toward current winners.
- It tests whether AI demand can support an incumbent turnaround instead of only reinforcing the strongest platforms and suppliers.
- It adds direct exposure to manufacturing intensity, U.S. foundry ambitions, process-node execution, and the tension between strategic relevance and financial fragility.

## 2025 annual highlights

- Full-year revenue: `$52.9B`
- Full-year GAAP EPS attributable to Intel: `$(0.06)`
- Full-year non-GAAP EPS attributable to Intel: `$0.42`
- Q4 2025 / full-year release said revenue was flat year over year for the full year and noted the deconsolidation of Altera in the third quarter of 2025.
- Intel highlighted the introduction of its first products on Intel `18A`, described as its most advanced process technology developed and manufactured in the United States.
- The 2025 release and annual materials framed Intel around CPUs in the AI era, stronger customer demand, and rebuilding the company around execution and manufacturing depth.

## Trailing reported quarter highlights

### Q2 2026

- Revenue: `$16.1B`
- GAAP EPS attributable to Intel: `$(2.16)`
- Non-GAAP EPS attributable to Intel: `$0.42`
- Management called this Intel's strongest revenue growth in more than `15` years.
- The Q2 2026 release emphasized AI-driven demand across CPUs, ASICs, advanced packaging, and Intel's wafer foundry network.

### Q1 2026

- Revenue: `$13.6B`
- GAAP EPS attributable to Intel: `$(0.73)`

- Management tied the quarter to the growing role of CPUs and advanced packaging in AI inference and agentic workloads.
- Intel highlighted Google, NVIDIA DGX Rubin host-CPU selection, and packaging-capacity expansion as evidence that it still sits inside major AI infrastructure deployments.

## Early read

- Intel is not currently the cleanest AI winner; it is the cleanest stressed-incumbent and execution-turnaround technology case in the archive.
- The evidence suggests AI demand can help Intel, but the company still has to prove that demand converts into durable segment strength, process execution, and economically sound foundry scaling.
- Intel is the best contrast so far for testing whether strategic importance and manufacturing ambition can coexist with weaker margins, restructuring pressure, and a much less secure moat than NVIDIA, Microsoft, Alphabet, Apple, or even Cisco.
