# KLA Corporation Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Snapshot

- Company: KLA Corporation
- Sector: Technology
- Industry: Semiconductor Equipment & Materials
- Ticker: `KLAC`
- Fiscal year-end: June
- Target annual report year: `2025`
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: `Q4 FY26`
  - quarter 2: `Q3 FY26`
  - quarter 3: `Q2 FY26`

## Annual report takeaways

- KLA is the clearest manufacturing bottleneck owner in this first `CLI 5` comparison set.
- The annual and quarter chain show a business monetizing semiconductor process-control necessity rather than end-user traffic or access subscriptions.
- That is what makes KLA valuable for the lane: it proves that some of the best infrastructure economics sit upstream at narrow technical choke points where customers cannot afford failure.
- The current reporting sequence also reinforces that process sensitivity, yield protection, and advanced-node or packaging precision remain hard budgets to cut even inside a cyclical capex market.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: `Q4 FY26` revenue was `$3.66B`, and the same release said fiscal `2026` total revenue reached `$13.58B`.
- Operating posture: the year-end print reinforces management's view that process-control capability remains strategically necessary.
- Structural themes: this is the strongest evidence in the batch that infrastructure complexity can create supplier economics materially better than the economics of the underlying network or asset owners.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: `Q3 FY26` revenue was `$3.415B`, GAAP diluted EPS was `$9.12`, and non-GAAP diluted EPS was `$9.40`.
- Operating posture: revenue outperformed the midpoint of guidance.
- Structural themes: outperformance matters because it suggests KLA is not only participating in a cycle. It is sitting at a point where customers still have limited flexibility to defer spend.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: `Q2 FY26` revenue was `$3.30B`, GAAP diluted EPS was `$8.68`, and non-GAAP diluted EPS was `$8.85`.
- Operating posture: performance was above the midpoint of guidance.
- Structural themes: this quarter shows why semicap comparisons matter in the lane. Some infrastructure winners are paid not for carrying traffic, but for enabling precision before any traffic exists.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: KLA is the batch's semiconductor process-control bottleneck owner.
- Consumer: the demand is indirect, but it sits underneath all compute, cloud, and device expansion.
- Cultural: more AI and compute intensity raise the need for precision in the physical stack.
- Regulatory: KLA faces less direct consumer-style scrutiny than telecom or platforms, but still depends on technology leadership and customer capex health.
- Labor: specialized technical expertise and manufacturing discipline matter materially.
- Technology: inspection, process control, yield, packaging, and advanced-node complexity are the core story.
- Capital allocation: KLA converts technical indispensability into strong cash generation without owning the full end-to-end compute footprint.

## Lane role and thematic interpretation

- Lane role: semiconductor process-control bottleneck owner.
- What makes it distinctive in this batch: KLA is the most concentrated beneficiary of manufacturing precision, yield management, and advanced-node complexity.
- Main moat: indispensable placement at inspection and process-control choke points.
- Main fragility: customer capex cycles still matter, even if KLA's tools are harder to cut than less essential equipment.
- Bigger-picture read: KLA is the batch's strongest evidence that the best economics in infrastructure can sit at narrow but unavoidable technical bottlenecks.
