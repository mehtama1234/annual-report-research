## What changed

- KLA is one of the clearest proof names for the idea that infrastructure profit pools can sit far upstream of carriers, clouds, or enterprise software. The company monetizes process-control necessity, yield protection, and manufacturing precision.
- The saved source set is clean on annual and quarter coverage. AnnualReports is useful for taxonomy and continuity, but it lagged at 2024 on 2026-08-10, so the controlling annual and quarter chain comes from KLA IR and the SEC.
- The quarter sequence keeps pointing to the same structural conclusion: customers can trim many parts of a capex stack, but they cannot easily underfund the tools that protect yield and process accuracy at advanced nodes and packaging layers.
- KLA therefore matters to CLI 5 not just as another semicap name, but as a narrow toll point where technical failure is expensive and substitution is difficult.

## Open questions

- Does KLA deserve to be treated as the cleanest semicap bottleneck in the lane, or do Lam and Applied ultimately capture a broader share of AI-era manufacturing complexity?
- How much of the current strength is structural process-intensity growth versus a favorable capex window that could cool?
- Will advanced packaging and heterogeneous integration widen KLA's moat further, or mainly preserve its current role?
