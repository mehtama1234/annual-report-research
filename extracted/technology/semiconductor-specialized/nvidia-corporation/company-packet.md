# NVIDIA Corporation Company Packet

Date baseline: 2026-08-08

## Snapshot

- Company: NVIDIA Corporation
- Sector: Technology
- Industry: Semiconductor - Specialized
- Ticker: NVDA
- Fiscal year-end: January
- Target annual report year: 2025
- Target quarter window:
  - quarter 1: FY2027 Q1
  - quarter 2: FY2026 Q4
  - quarter 3: FY2026 Q3

Note: NVIDIA is an off-calendar reporter. The relevant annual package for the `2025 annual report` collection rule is the fiscal `2026` annual report because it covers the business through `2026-01-25` and captures most of calendar `2025`.

## Annual report takeaways

- The annual report is explicit that NVIDIA sees AI as infrastructure, not just product demand. It describes data centers as AI factories and argues that every company and country will build AI infrastructure.
- FY2026 revenue reached `$215.9 billion`, up `65%`, with Data Center revenue up `68%` year over year to `$193.7 billion`; Gaming still grew `41%`, but it is now secondary to the AI infrastructure engine.
- The annual package frames the current cycle as a full-stack buildout across chips, systems, networking, facilities, power, cooling, software, and reference designs, which is a stronger industrial framing than the software-centric materials from Microsoft or Alphabet.
- The business mix shows how concentrated the economic engine has become: Data Center produced roughly `90%` of FY2026 revenue, while Professional Visualization and Automotive still grew quickly from much smaller bases.
- The report also makes clear that this is not a clean low-capex software story. NVIDIA is exposed to real manufacturing, supply, energy, networking, export, and deployment constraints even while margins remain exceptional.

## Quarter-by-quarter takeaways

### Most recent quarter

- Results: FY2027 Q1 revenue was `$81.6 billion`, operating income was `$53.5 billion`, net income was `$58.3 billion`, and diluted EPS was `$2.39`.
- Guidance: management leaned on architecture demand, AI-factory economics, and customer diversification rather than a narrow unit forecast; China Data Center revenue was not included in outlook.
- Management message: management said it capitalized on an inflection in inference demand, ramped Blackwell systems across hyperscalers, model builders, AI clouds, and sovereign customers, and described datacenters as revenue-generating AI factories.
- Structural themes: Data Center revenue hit `$75.2 billion`, networking reached `$14.8 billion`, Q1 included record buybacks of `$20.2 billion`, and an additional `$80 billion` repurchase authorization was approved.

### Quarter minus 1

- Results: FY2026 Q4 revenue was `$68.1 billion`, operating income was `$44.3 billion`, net income was `$43.0 billion`, and diluted EPS was `$1.76`.
- Guidance: management said Gaming would face supply constraints in FY2027 Q1 and beyond, while AI and accelerated-computing demand remained the central growth driver.
- Management message: fiscal-year revenue reached `$215.9 billion`, Data Center reached `$62.3 billion` in the quarter, and networking reached `$11.0 billion` as NVLink fabric and Ethernet platforms ramped.
- Structural themes: this is the cleanest bridge quarter showing NVIDIA as both chip supplier and AI datacenter systems provider.

### Quarter minus 2

- Results: FY2026 Q3 revenue was `$57.0 billion`, operating income was `$36.0 billion`, net income was `$31.9 billion`, and diluted EPS was `$1.30`.
- Guidance: management argued that demand for AI infrastructure still exceeded expectations and said the company had visibility to roughly `$0.5 trillion` of Blackwell and Rubin revenue from the start of `2025` through the end of calendar `2026`.
- Management message: management said customers were leaning into three platform shifts: accelerated computing, powerful AI models, and agentic applications.
- Structural themes: Data Center revenue reached `$51.2 billion`, Blackwell Ultra became the leading architecture across customer categories, and sovereign plus hyperscale AI-factory buildouts became a central part of the story.

Note: for many companies this third slot will be a late-2025 quarter needed to complete the trailing three-quarter set as of 2026-08-08.

## Signals to feed into higher-level analysis

- Sector: NVIDIA is the clearest proof that the technology AI buildout is not just a software narrative; there is a parallel picks-and-shovels infrastructure profit pool with extraordinary scale.
- Consumer: gaming still matters as a brand and ecosystem anchor, but it no longer explains the core economics of the company.
- Cultural: management’s language implies AI is moving from software feature set to industrial capacity race, which changes how to read the entire technology sector.
- Regulatory: export controls, China policy, energy access, and sovereignty requirements are direct operating variables, not side constraints.
- Labor: the business still depends on specialized engineering and ecosystem talent, but the more immediate constraints read as supply-chain, datacenter, and infrastructure capacity rather than headcount alone.
- Technology: Blackwell, Rubin, NVLink, Spectrum-X, InfiniBand, CUDA, and factory-scale reference designs position NVIDIA as a full-stack accelerated-computing platform rather than a standalone chip vendor.
- Capital allocation: large buybacks and the dividend increase show confidence, but the more important allocation signal is continued ecosystem and supply-chain investment to support multi-layer infrastructure demand.
