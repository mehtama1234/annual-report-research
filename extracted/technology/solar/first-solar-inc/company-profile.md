## What changed

- Taxonomy and lane fit: First Solar is the clearest business-substance solar flagship currently available for this frontier even though AnnualReports classifies it as `Semiconductor - Specialized` because the company sells thin-film solar modules built on a semiconductor process.
- Industrial positioning: the `2025` annual report reframes the business around electrification, utility-scale power demand, and domestic manufacturing rather than around a generic renewables narrative. Management explicitly links demand to AI, data centers, advanced manufacturing, and broader grid load growth.
- Capacity and backlog logic: the annual package points to approximately `13 GW` of U.S. annual nameplate capacity in `2025`, more than `17 GW` projected by `2027`, and about `50.1 GW` of contracted backlog valued at roughly `$15.0B` exiting `2025`.
- Operating model: `2025` results show a step-change year with about `$5.2B` of net sales, `$14.21` of diluted EPS, and about `$2.4B` of net cash, driven by record module volume, Section 45X monetization, and new-factory ramp activity.
- Quarter progression: Q1 `2026` showed record first-quarter revenue and strong margin expansion; Q2 `2026` showed higher EBITDA and sales volume but also exposed contract-termination risk and continued cash draw from working capital and South Carolina expansion.

## Open questions

- How durable is utility-scale solar demand if policy support, trade regimes, or permitting timelines move against current assumptions?
- Can First Solar preserve contract discipline and pricing certainty while competitors and customers keep testing the limits of backlog flexibility?
- How much of today's profitability is structural technology advantage versus temporary support from domestic industrial policy and Section 45X credit economics?
