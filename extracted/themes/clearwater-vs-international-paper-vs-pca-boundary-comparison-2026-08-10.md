## Executive read

All three companies monetize hidden fiber and packaging-material infrastructure, but they do not control the same layer.

- `Clearwater Paper` is the SBS paperboard substrate and mill-execution case
- `International Paper` is the broader corrugated and containerboard throughput scale case
- `Packaging Corporation of America` is the tighter North American corrugated and containerboard peer-density case

That means the best current ordering is:

1. `Clearwater Paper`: paperboard substrate and packaging-materials infrastructure
2. `International Paper`: corrugated and containerboard throughput infrastructure
3. `Packaging Corporation of America`: North America corrugated and containerboard throughput peer infrastructure

This is not a quality ranking.

It is an ordering from the more focused converter-facing SBS substrate layer into broader corrugated scale and then into the cleaner North American corrugated peer-density read.

## The core comparison

| Company | What visible demand or activity it quietly supports | What the company mainly controls | What widens the model beyond the base layer | Best current read |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| `International Paper` | box demand, shipping throughput, retail replenishment, and corrugated packaging flow across large geographies | corrugated packaging, containerboard mill networks, packaging-throughput infrastructure, and broader goods-movement exposure across North America and EMEA | separation execution, mill-reliability improvement, commercial actions, and restructuring-heavy network simplification | corrugated and containerboard throughput infrastructure |
| `Packaging Corporation of America` | North American box demand, shipment density, and corrugated throughput with tighter mill-and-plant visibility | containerboard mills, corrugated products plants, sheet feeders, and North American corrugated-throughput capacity with direct exposure to shipment growth, price realization, and mill utilization | Greif integration, export reduction, inventory discipline, freight and recycled-fiber cost pressure, and more concentrated operating control | North America corrugated and containerboard throughput peer infrastructure |

## What the filings now show

### 1. `Clearwater Paper` is the clearest SBS substrate case

Clearwater is the cleanest proof that the packaging stack starts before the converter and carton layer.

The FY `2025` through Q2 `2026` chain shows the operating shape clearly:

- FY `2025` net sales from continuing operations of `$1.6B`
- FY `2025` adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations of `$107M`
- FY `2025` volumes of `1,236,114` tons with average net selling price of `$1,167` per ton
- Q1 `2026` net sales of `$360M`
- Q2 `2026` net sales of `$375M`

The useful read is not "paper" in a generic commodity sense.

The useful read is converter-facing SBS substrate exposure shaped by:

- mill execution
- planned outages
- operating-rate recovery
- oversupplied industry conditions
- foodservice and folding-carton demand

That is why `Clearwater` is first in this comparison.

### 2. `International Paper` is the clearest broad corrugated-throughput scale case

International Paper matters because it shows that the same broader materials branch widens beyond SBS and converter supply into large-scale corrugated and containerboard throughput tied to shipping volume and packaging network performance.

The FY `2025` through Q2 `2026` chain shows that clearly:

- FY `2025` net sales of `$23.63B`
- FY `2025` adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations of `$2.98B`
- Q1 `2026` net sales of `$5.97B`
- Q2 `2026` net sales of `$6.00B`

The management framing is also structurally different from Clearwater.

International Paper is not mainly about one focused SBS substrate market. It is increasingly about:

- corrugated scale
- containerboard mill networks
- box-demand infrastructure
- North America versus EMEA packaging performance
- portfolio simplification and planned separation into two packaging-solutions companies

That makes International Paper second because it is the broader and noisier scale expression of the corrugated-throughput branch.

### 3. `Packaging Corporation of America` is the clearest tighter corrugated-density peer case

PCA matters because it keeps the corrugated-throughput branch from collapsing into only International Paper's broader restructuring-heavy read.

The FY `2025` through Q2 `2026` chain shows the operating shape clearly:

- FY `2025` net sales of `$9.0B`
- FY `2025` reported net income of `$774M`
- FY `2025` diluted EPS excluding special items of `$9.84`
- Q1 `2026` net sales of `$2.4B`
- Q2 `2026` net sales of `$2.5B`

The useful read is not simply "another containerboard company."

The useful read is a tighter North American corrugated-throughput system built on:

- containerboard mills and corrugated plants
- shipment growth and per-day shipment records
- mill utilization and inventory control
- price realization
- Greif integration outperformance

That makes PCA third because this ordering moves from focused SBS substrate into broader corrugated scale and then into the cleaner North American corrugated peer-density case.

## The most useful boundary split

The cleanest way to separate the three is:

### `Clearwater Paper`: SBS substrate first

Clearwater matters most when the question is who supplies the packaging material before conversion in folding cartons and foodservice formats.

The distinguishing asset is:

- SBS paperboard production
- converter-facing supply
- outage and operating-rate discipline
- pricing exposure in a focused substrate market

### `International Paper`: corrugated scale first

International Paper matters most when the question is who owns a broad corrugated and containerboard system tied to shipping throughput, box demand, and large mill-network performance.

The distinguishing asset is:

- corrugated packaging scale
- containerboard mill networks
- North America and EMEA packaging exposure
- restructuring and separation-driven simplification

### `Packaging Corporation of America`: North American corrugated peer density first

PCA matters most when the question is who offers the cleaner North American corrugated-throughput peer read through tighter shipment, mill, inventory, and price-realization visibility.

The distinguishing asset is:

- North American corrugated density
- mill and sheet-feeder throughput
- shipment records and price realization
- Greif integration and concentrated operating control

## What each company proves

### `Clearwater Paper`

`Clearwater Paper` proves that the hidden packaging stack starts at the focused substrate layer, where mill execution, outages, and converter relationships matter before branded packaging ever appears.

### `International Paper`

`International Paper` proves that the same hidden branch can widen into corrugated-throughput infrastructure where box demand, mill reliability, and shipping-volume-linked network scale matter more than focused SBS economics.

### `Packaging Corporation of America`

`Packaging Corporation of America` proves that the corrugated-throughput branch itself has an internal peer split between broader restructuring-heavy scale and tighter North American density and operating control.

## Why the taxonomy fails here

`AnnualReports` can place these companies into:

- `Paper & Paper Products`
- `Packaging & Containers`

That is enough for source collection.

It is weak for analysis.

The stronger distinction is:

- focused SBS substrate supply versus
- broader corrugated and containerboard throughput scale versus
- tighter North American corrugated peer density

Those are different monetization choke points inside the same hidden packaging-material and throughput layer.

## Highest-confidence conclusions

- `Clearwater` is the clearest SBS substrate and mill-execution anchor in this branch
- `International Paper` is the clearest broader corrugated and containerboard throughput scale counterpoint
- `Packaging Corporation of America` is the clearest tighter North American corrugated peer-density counterpoint to International Paper
- the useful comparison is not one paper or packaging company versus another, but focused substrate supply versus broader corrugated scale versus concentrated corrugated-throughput density
- this three-way split is the cleanest current proof that the substrate and corrugated branch of the behind-the-shelf lane can be broken into reusable subtypes instead of left as one generic paper-or-packaging bucket
