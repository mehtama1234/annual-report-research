# Etsy Versus eBay Versus Airbnb Boundary Comparison

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Scope

This memo is the direct comparison built from the trust-marketplace blind-spot lane.

It focuses on the cleanest three-way split currently available in the archive:

- `Etsy`
- `eBay`
- `Airbnb`

The useful question is not whether all three are marketplaces.

The useful question is what kind of trust system each marketplace actually organizes:

- creator-merchant and identity-commerce trust
- enthusiast, recommerce, and category-liquidity trust
- host-guest and trip-flexibility trust

## Executive read

The three companies all monetize decentralized supply, but they do not solve the same trust problem.

- `Etsy` is the creator-merchant and identity-commerce trust case
- `eBay` is the enthusiast and recommerce trust case
- `Airbnb` is the host-guest and trip-flexibility trust case

That means the best current ordering is:

1. `Etsy`: creator-merchant and identity-commerce trust marketplace
2. `eBay`: enthusiast and recommerce trust marketplace
3. `Airbnb`: host-guest and trip-flexibility trust marketplace

This is not a quality ranking.

It is an ordering by how trust is created, moving from human-scaled uniqueness trust to category-liquidity trust to higher-stakes booking-confidence trust.

## The core comparison

| Company | What the participant mainly returns for | What the company mainly controls | What widens the model beyond the base transaction | Best current read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Etsy` | unique goods, gifting, self-expression shopping, repeat buyer discovery, and human-scaled commerce | seller identity, creative supply, discovery quality, app behavior, and buyer-seller trust around uniqueness | Etsy Ads, Offsite Ads, services revenue, payments, and take-rate expansion | creator-merchant and identity-commerce trust marketplace |
| `eBay` | category discovery, collecting, resale, enthusiast browsing, and C2C liquidity | seller supply, category depth, trust tools, discovery mechanics, and resale confidence | advertising, promoted listings, seller tools, focus-category depth, and recommerce economics | enthusiast and recommerce trust marketplace |
| `Airbnb` | trip planning, repeat app booking, flexible travel choice, and trusted stays or local trip add-ons | host supply, guest identity, booking confidence, app behavior, and flexible-payments conversion | services, experiences, hotels, and flexible-payments-led trip-surface widening | host-guest and trip-flexibility trust marketplace |

## What the filings now show

### 1. `Etsy` is the clearest creator-merchant and identity-commerce trust case

Etsy is not most useful as a generic specialty-retail or internet-service name.

The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows a marketplace where trust is built around uniqueness, seller identity, and creative supply:

- full-year `2025` consolidated GMS of `$11.917B`
- full-year `2025` revenue of `$2.884B`
- full-year `2025` revenue take rate of `24.2%`
- year-end `2025` Etsy marketplace active buyers of `86.5M`
- year-end `2025` Etsy marketplace active sellers of `5.6M`
- `Q2 2026` revenue take rate of `25.9%`

That is why Etsy is first in this comparison.

The filings show that the trust system is emotional and identity-linked. Buyers return because they expect distinctive supply and creative human connection, while sellers trust that discovery tools and ad products can surface the right demand.

### 2. `eBay` is the clearest enthusiast and recommerce trust case

eBay differs because the repeat behavior is less about personal creative identity and more about liquidity, category depth, and enthusiast confidence.

The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows that structure clearly:

- full-year `2025` GMV of `$79.6B`
- full-year `2025` revenue of `$11.1B`
- `Q1 2026` advertising revenue of `$581M`
- `Q2 2026` advertising revenue of `$596M`
- `Q2 2026` revenue of `$3.1B`, up `15%`
- management leaning into `Focus Categories`, `C2C`, `recommerce`, collectibles, and the `Depop` acquisition

eBay is second because it is the clearest proof that trust can also be category-led and liquidity-led. The key return loop is not only discovery of something unique, but confidence that enthusiast supply and resale depth will keep the marketplace useful.

### 3. `Airbnb` is the clearest host-guest and trip-flexibility trust case

Airbnb differs from both retail-like marketplaces because the decision is more episodic, more expensive, and more trust-sensitive.

The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows that clearly:

- full-year `2025` gross booking value of `$91.3B`
- full-year `2025` revenue of about `$12.2B`
- over `5M` hosts by year-end `2025`
- over `2.5B` guest arrivals by year-end `2025`
- `Q1 2026` app-booked nights at `63%` of total and about `20%` of global GBV from `Reserve Now, Pay Later`
- `Q2 2026` revenue of `$3.6B` and gross booking value of `$27.2B`

Airbnb is third because this ordering moves from identity-led goods trust and enthusiast-liquidity trust into a higher-stakes booking-confidence system where trust and flexibility are the product surface.

It remains a strong blind-spot case because `Application Software` does not explain a host-guest trust economy at all well.

## The most useful boundary split

The cleanest way to separate the three is:

### `Etsy`: uniqueness trust first

Etsy matters most when the question is who owns creative-supply trust and discovery around self-expression, gifting, and human-scaled commerce.

The distinguishing asset is:

- seller identity
- uniqueness
- emotional discovery
- ad-and-services monetization around creative supply

### `eBay`: liquidity trust first

eBay matters most when the question is who owns category confidence, enthusiast supply, and resale liquidity.

The distinguishing asset is:

- category depth
- resale confidence
- enthusiast behavior
- advertising and seller-tool monetization around richer supply

### `Airbnb`: stay trust first

Airbnb matters most when the question is who owns booking confidence, host supply, and travel flexibility without owning the underlying assets.

The distinguishing asset is:

- host credibility
- booking flexibility
- app-booking behavior
- travel-surface widening through services and flexible payments

## What each company proves

### `Etsy`

`Etsy` proves that a large public marketplace can still be built around creative identity, uniqueness, and human connection rather than broad commodity assortment or logistics density.

### `eBay`

`eBay` proves that trust can be monetized through recommerce, enthusiast participation, seller density, and category discovery even without a thick membership bundle.

### `Airbnb`

`Airbnb` proves that travel demand can be captured through host trust, flexible payments, app behavior, and trip confidence rather than through hotel ownership or classic airline-or-hotel loyalty currencies.

## Highest-confidence conclusions

- `Etsy` is the clearest creator-merchant trust case, not a plain specialty-retail or internet-service packet
- `eBay` is the clearest enthusiast and recommerce trust case, not just a mature online marketplace
- `Airbnb` is the clearest host-guest and trip-flexibility trust case, not a useful fit for a shallow software label
- the practical comparison is not marketplace versus marketplace versus marketplace, but identity-commerce trust versus recommerce-liquidity trust versus booking-confidence trust
- the right current order is `Etsy` first on uniqueness and seller-identity trust, `eBay` second on category-liquidity and enthusiast trust, and `Airbnb` third on higher-stakes stay trust and flexibility
