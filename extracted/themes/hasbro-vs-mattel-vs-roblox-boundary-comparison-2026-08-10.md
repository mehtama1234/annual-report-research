# Hasbro Versus Mattel Versus Roblox Boundary Comparison

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Scope

This memo is the direct comparison built from the fandom, play, and participation blind-spot lane.

It focuses on the cleanest three-way split currently available in the archive:

- `Hasbro`
- `Mattel`
- `Roblox`

The useful question is not whether all three touch play or entertainment.

The useful question is what kind of participation system each company actually organizes:

- collectible ruleset and organized-play fandom
- brand-universe and entertainment-extension play
- creator-built digital-world participation

## Executive read

The three companies belong in the same broader participation family, but they do not monetize the same kind of fan relationship.

- `Hasbro` is the collectible-ruleset and organized-play fandom case
- `Mattel` is the brand-universe and entertainment-extension case
- `Roblox` is the creator-world and digital participation case

That means the best current ordering is:

1. `Hasbro`: collectible ruleset and organized-play fandom system
2. `Mattel`: brand-universe and entertainment-extension play system
3. `Roblox`: creator-built digital-world participation system

This is not a quality ranking.

It is an ordering by how the participation loop is created, moving from collectible and ruleset depth to brand-world extension to open creator-world engagement.

## What the filings now show

### 1. `Hasbro` is the clearest collectible-fandom and organized-play case

Hasbro is not most useful as a generic toy seller.

The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows a fan system led by rulesets, collectibles, crossover content, and digital extension:

- full-year `2025` revenue up `14%`
- `Wizards of the Coast and Digital Gaming` revenue up `45%` for full-year `2025`
- `MAGIC: THE GATHERING` up `59%` in `2025`
- `Monopoly Go!` contributing about `$168M` in full-year `2025`
- `Q2 2026` revenue of `$1,139.6M`, up `16%`
- `Magic: The Gathering` passing `$500M` of quarterly revenue for the first time in `Q2 2026`

That is why Hasbro is first in this comparison.

It is the clearest proof that repeat players, organized communities, collectible behavior, crossover sets, and digital or licensed extension can form one durable fandom flywheel.

### 2. `Mattel` is the clearest brand-universe and entertainment-extension case

Mattel differs because the recurring demand is less about ruleset depth and more about widening iconic brands across formats.

The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows that structure clearly:

- full-year `2025` net sales of `$5,348M`
- fourth-quarter `2025` net sales up `7%` to `$1,766M`
- `Q1 2026` net sales of `$862M`, up `4%`
- `Q2 2026` net sales of `$1,125M`, up `10%`
- `Hot Wheels` continuing to drive vehicle growth
- games, theatrical-release-linked action figures, and `Mattel163` digital expansion widening the model

Mattel is second because it is the clearest proof that play can be monetized through brand universes, movies, licensing, mobile games, and adult-fan extension rather than mainly through organized-play communities.

### 3. `Roblox` is the clearest creator-world participation case

Roblox differs from both physical-IP cases because the core engine is not branded product ownership first.

It is recurring world participation.

The `2025` through `Q2 2026` chain shows that clearly:

- full-year `2025` revenue of `$4.891B`, up `36%`
- full-year `2025` bookings of `$6.788B`, up `55%`
- `Q4 2025` bookings of `$2.222B`, up `63%`
- `Q1 2026` revenue of `$1.442B`, up `39%`, with `132 million` average daily active users
- `Q2 2026` revenue of `$1.469B`, up `36%`, with over `120 million` DAUs and over `29 billion` hours engaged

Roblox is third because this ordering moves from franchise-led physical-plus-digital fandom systems into a creator-built participation platform where discovery, communication, safety, monetization per hour, and virtual currency matter as much as headline growth.

It remains a strong blind-spot case because `Application Software` does not explain a digital-world participation economy at all well.

## The most useful boundary split

The cleanest way to separate the three is:

### `Hasbro`: ruleset fandom first

Hasbro matters most when the question is who owns repeat fan participation through game systems, collectible logic, and organized communities.

The distinguishing asset is:

- ruleset depth
- collectible cadence
- crossover-release intensity
- digital and licensed extension around a strong play core

### `Mattel`: brand world first

Mattel matters most when the question is who owns family-franchise and toy-brand attachment across shelves, screens, games, and licensing.

The distinguishing asset is:

- iconic brand universes
- theatrical and media extension
- licensing breadth
- adult-fan and collector widening

### `Roblox`: participation surface first

Roblox matters most when the question is who owns the environment where users and creators keep returning, spending, building, and interacting.

The distinguishing asset is:

- creator supply
- discovery and engagement loops
- virtual currency
- communication, safety, and monetization infrastructure

## What each company proves

### `Hasbro`

`Hasbro` proves that organized play, collectible identity, and crossover content can support a large recurring fandom system rather than a seasonal toy business.

### `Mattel`

`Mattel` proves that iconic toy brands can widen into broader franchise worlds where entertainment, digital games, and collector extension matter as much as shelf velocity.

### `Roblox`

`Roblox` proves that the same broader participation lane also includes creator-built digital worlds where recurring value comes from engagement depth, social interaction, creator labor, and multi-layer monetization.

## Highest-confidence conclusions

- `Hasbro` is the clearest collectible-ruleset fandom case, not a plain `Toys & Games` packet
- `Mattel` is the clearest brand-universe and entertainment-extension case, not just another toy portfolio
- `Roblox` is the clearest creator-world participation case, not a useful fit for a shallow `Application Software` label
- the practical comparison is not toy company versus toy company versus software company, but organized-play fandom versus franchise-brand extension versus creator-world participation
- the right current order is `Hasbro` first on collectible and ruleset depth, `Mattel` second on brand-world extension, and `Roblox` third on open participation-surface logic
