## Executive read

The shared system is not sector. It is repeat-relationship monetization.

The archive now supports several distinct forms:

- stored-value routine
- digital loyalty at scale
- paid utility membership
- convenience-plus-membership retail
- status currency and co-brand economics
- loyalty-funded distribution
- wallet continuity across physical and digital surfaces
- premium card membership
- low-friction recurring access membership

## Working comparison frame

| Relationship type | Core companies | Main repeated behavior | Main widening layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stored-value routine | `Starbucks` | daily beverage habit | preload, rewards, deferred revenue |
| Digital loyalty at scale | `McDonald's` | everyday food occasions | loyalty-member sales, app usage, digital offers |
| Paid utility membership | `Costco` | stock-up trips and value trust | membership fees |
| Convenience-plus-membership retail | `Target` | retail basket and same-day utility | membership, retail media, marketplace, fulfillment |
| Status currency and co-brand economics | `Delta` | repeat travel and premium trip behavior | miles, American Express remuneration, partner economics |
| Loyalty-funded distribution | `Marriott` | repeat branded stays | loyalty concentration, co-brand economics, fee network |
| Wallet-linked entertainment identity | `Caesars` | resort visits and wagering | rewards identity, universal wallet, cross-surface play |
| Premium card membership | `American Express` | card spend and premium consumer behavior | annual fees, merchant relationships, closed-loop data |
| Low-friction recurring access membership | `Planet Fitness` | affordable wellness attendance | dues, annual fees, retention-sensitive membership base |

## What matters

### 1. Loyalty is not one model

The archive should distinguish whether the relationship is prepaid, fee-funded, partner-funded, status-led, convenience-led, or wallet-linked.

### 2. The best systems widen the economics beyond the base purchase

These companies monetize more than the initial trip, spend event, or visit.

### 3. The relationship layer can be cultural as well as financial

`Costco`, `Starbucks`, `Delta`, `Caesars`, and `Planet Fitness` all show different ways repeated behavior becomes identity, ritual, belonging, or habit.

## Best current use

Use this memo when the better comparison is relationship structure, not formal sector.
