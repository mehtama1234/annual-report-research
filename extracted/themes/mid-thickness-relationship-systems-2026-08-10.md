# Mid-Thickness Relationship Systems

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Scope

This memo defines the middle band between the thickest wallet and membership systems and the thinner models that still rely mainly on traffic, price, product, or format execution.

## Executive read

Mid-thickness systems have meaningful owned relationships, but the monetization is more indirect than in the thickest systems.

These companies usually show:

- direct behavior
- app usage
- loyalty participation
- workflow ownership
- service trust
- first-party demand capture

But they usually do not show one dominant disclosed fee, wallet, or partner-remuneration engine.

## What matters

### 1. Mid-thickness systems often own workflow rather than wallet

The relationship is real, but it is usually widened through service, workflow, app behavior, direct mix, or channel architecture rather than through a clearly financialized member engine.

### 2. Mid-thickness is a real category, not a vague in-between bucket

The archive should keep it as a distinct band because many important companies sit here.

### 3. These are often the key transition cases

They show where categories may be thickening into stronger customer-ownership systems over time.

## Best current use

Use this memo when the relationship is meaningful and strategically visible, but not yet thick enough to qualify as a full wallet, membership, or status engine.
