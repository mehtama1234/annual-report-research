## Executive read

The archive now supports several distinct ecosystem types rather than one generic platform category:

- commerce and merchant flywheels
- trip and marketplace networks
- local-commerce and fulfillment networks
- device and operating-system ecosystems
- participation and creator-economy platforms
- recurring attention and cultural-distribution platforms

The key analytical question is not whether a company is digital. It is whether one repeated interface is being widened into multiple monetization layers.

## Working platform map

| Ecosystem type | Core companies | Main owned interface | Main expansion layers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commerce and merchant flywheel | `Amazon`, `eBay`, `Walmart`, `Target`, `Ulta` | shopping, search, account, seller access, loyalty or membership | ads, marketplace or seller services, subscriptions, services, fulfillment, assortment expansion |
| Trip and marketplace network | `Airbnb`, `Booking`, `Expedia` | booking flow, trust, discovery, app behavior | payments, ads, B2B distribution, experiences, hotels, ancillary trip surfaces |
| Local-commerce and fulfillment network | `DoorDash` | recurring local ordering, merchant discovery, memberships, delivery coordination | ads, memberships, merchant tooling, grocery and retail widening, logistics density |
| Device and operating-system ecosystem | `Apple` | device base, identity, software, app and services attachment | services monetization, subscriptions, payments, ecosystem lock-in, developer distribution |
| Participation and creator-economy platform | `Roblox` | persistent digital-world engagement, creators, discovery, identity, communication | virtual currency, creator economics, ads, commerce, AI-assisted creation |
| Attention, media, and cultural-distribution platform | `Netflix`, `Spotify`, `Alphabet`, `Meta` | recurring attention, discovery, entertainment, search, recommendation | ads, subscriptions, creator or partner distribution, AI assistance, adjacent consumer extensions |

## What matters

### 1. The repeated interface is the real asset

The strongest ecosystem businesses own a surface that consumers or merchants return to repeatedly.

### 2. The best cases monetize the same relationship more than once

Examples already visible in the archive:

- `Amazon`: shopping, Prime, seller services, advertising, fulfillment
- `Apple`: device installed base, services, subscriptions, payments, lock-in
- `DoorDash`: convenience habit, memberships, merchant tools, advertising, logistics
- `Roblox`: engagement, creators, virtual currency, ads, commerce

### 3. Not all ecosystem thickening is the same

The archive should keep separating:

- interface control
- merchant or seller dependence
- creator participation
- recurring paid attention
- trip orchestration
- local fulfillment coordination

## Best current use

Use this memo when the source taxonomy hides the fact that a company is really an interface-expansion system rather than a plain retailer, software vendor, or media operator.
