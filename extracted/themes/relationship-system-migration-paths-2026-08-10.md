## Executive read

The most useful next question is no longer only which systems are thick, mid, or thin.

It is which mid-thickness systems are actively thickening.

That matters because the archive is trying to understand not only current company structure, but where the consumer and platform economy is moving.

The clearest thickening signals in the current repo are:

- more direct consumer behavior
- higher app or direct-mix usage
- more explicit loyalty or member participation
- more payments or checkout control
- stronger media or advertising monetization
- more workflow ownership across adjacent services
- more first-party data capture

Those signals do not guarantee that a company becomes a full Delta, Costco, Marriott, or Caesars style thick system.

But they do show which categories are moving in that direction.

## What thickening looks like

### 1. A system thickens when the relationship becomes more measurable

The first sign is usually not a new label.

It is better disclosure:

- loyalty revenue
- direct mix
- app mix
- member participation
- marketplace or media layer usage
- service attachment
- partner-funded economics
- first-party data capture

The company starts making the relationship legible as an operating asset, not only as a marketing outcome.

### 2. A system thickens when adjacent workflow gets pulled under the same interface

This is the second sign:

- one booking becomes a broader trip flow
- one retail trip becomes media, marketplace, and service activity
- one project visit becomes Pro workflow and service attachment
- one beverage purchase becomes data capture and repeat activation

That is often how a mid-thickness system becomes strategically stronger without immediately becoming a classic paid-membership business.

## Current likely migration paths

### 1. Highest-likelihood thickening: Booking

`Booking` is the cleanest current candidate to thicken further.

Why:

- merchant gross bookings continue rising as a mix of total bookings
- direct behavior remains high
- mobile app mix is already in the high `50%` range
- Genius participation is high
- Connected Trip behavior is becoming more explicit
- payments and fintech capabilities are widening

Why this matters:

- the company is moving beyond demand aggregation toward transaction orchestration
- more of the traveler workflow is being captured inside one surface
- the relationship is becoming easier to measure through direct mix, merchant share, app behavior, and trip attachment

Most likely thickening route:

- deeper payments control
- stronger direct traveler identity
- more cross-vertical trip attachment
- more explicit monetization of repeat travel behavior

### 2. Highest-likelihood thickening: Airbnb

`Airbnb` is the other very strong thickening candidate.

Why:

- app-booked nights are already a majority of total nights booked
- `Reserve Now, Pay Later` and `Pay Less Upfront` make checkout flexibility part of the core interface
- hotels, services, and experiences are widening the trip surface
- cross-use between hotels and homes is already disclosed
- AI support resolution is improving operating leverage inside the same interface

Why this matters:

- Airbnb is thickening through trust, identity, and app behavior instead of points
- the relationship can become stronger even without a classical loyalty wallet
- the platform is moving toward broader trip ownership

Most likely thickening route:

- more repeat app dependence
- broader travel and local-services surface
- more payments-enabled conversion and identity continuity
- more measurable traveler lifecycle economics

### 3. Strong thickening candidate: Ulta

`Ulta` is one of the clearest non-travel mid-band names that may thicken meaningfully.

Why:

- loyalty-member data already sits at the center of the retail model
- UB Marketplace widens assortment beyond store shelves
- UB Media creates an explicit second monetization layer
- salons make the relationship more recurring and service-linked
- app, e-commerce, and physical discovery all reinforce one another

Why this matters:

- beauty is one of the most plausible categories to become more interface-like
- Ulta already has several thickening ingredients inside one system
- the company is no longer just a specialty retailer with nice comps

Most likely thickening route:

- more explicit monetization of loyalty-member behavior
- stronger media economics
- deeper service-plus-product loop
- more marketplace-assisted discovery and repeat behavior

### 4. Moderate thickening candidate: Lowe's

`Lowe's` is thickening more slowly, but the direction is still visible.

Why:

- management explicitly frames the model around Pro, online, home services, loyalty, and distribution
- the operating surface is broadening from store visits into project workflow
- Pro and service relationships are usually stickier than DIY transaction traffic

Why this matters:

- home improvement can become more relationship-owned without becoming a consumer wallet business
- the thickening here is likely to be workflow and service led rather than points or preload led

Most likely thickening route:

- stronger Pro-account dependence
- deeper service attachment
- more delivery and distribution integration
- more measurable loyalty and project behavior

### 5. Moderate thickening candidate: United

`United` is already mid-thickness and could thicken further, but the route is narrower than for Booking or Airbnb.

Why:

- loyalty revenue is already growing
- app utility, digital self-service, and connectivity are rising
- the airline keeps talking about brand-loyal customers
- premium, business, and loyalty revenue all grew in the recent quarter chain

Why this matters:

- the relationship is becoming more visible operationally
- the airline is building a stronger owned travel surface through product and digital tools

Constraint:

- the company still discloses the economics less explicitly than Delta
- a lot of the story still lives in network quality and unit revenue rather than in a fully visible partner-funded loyalty engine

Most likely thickening route:

- more explicit loyalty monetization
- more measurable app-led engagement
- deeper premium and connectivity attachment

### 6. Lower-to-moderate thickening candidate: Hilton

`Hilton` is a strong business, but it may stay mid-thickness longer.

Why:

- the company clearly benefits from repeat stay behavior and loyalty-linked distribution
- the core operating disclosure still centers on RevPAR, pipeline, fee growth, and owner demand
- the consumer relationship is important, but the filing surface remains more lodging-network than wallet-network

Why this matters:

- Hilton may continue compounding without becoming much more visibly financialized at the customer-relationship layer

Most likely thickening route:

- more explicit loyalty concentration disclosure
- more visible co-brand or distribution economics
- stronger digital booking and app-relationship metrics

### 7. Lower-likelihood thickening, but still strategically important: Coca-Cola

`Coca-Cola` is the clearest example where thickening may happen without ever looking like a classic loyalty business.

Why:

- connected packaging is capturing first-party data
- global cultural campaigns recruit and reactivate consumers at scale
- pack-price-channel architecture keeps functioning like interface design

Constraint:

- most of the relationship is still mediated through retail channels, bottlers, and route-to-market architecture
- the company may become more data aware without becoming a thick direct-customer system

Why this still matters:

- it shows that legacy consumer-goods categories can move toward thicker activation without owning a classic app wallet

Most likely thickening route:

- more connected-packaging and first-party data systems
- more direct activation layered onto retail and bottler channels
- stronger measurable recruitment and repeat behavior around major brand campaigns

## What this means for the broader research project

- The archive can now use mid-thickness names as forward-looking indicators, not just as in-between buckets.
- The best future blind-spot additions are the ones that clarify whether a category is thickening into direct relationship economics.
- This is especially useful in travel marketplaces, beauty, home improvement, and routine consumer systems where the surface is changing before the full economics are separately disclosed.

## Highest-confidence conclusions

- `Booking`, `Airbnb`, and `Ulta` are the strongest current thickening candidates in the repo.
- `Lowe's` and `United` are meaningful but slower thickening cases, where workflow and service quality still matter more than one dominant wallet mechanic.
- `Hilton` may continue to compound mainly through network and fee strength even if the customer-relationship economics remain less explicitly disclosed.
- `Coca-Cola` is a special case: the relationship may thicken through data and activation rather than through direct membership or wallet monetization.
