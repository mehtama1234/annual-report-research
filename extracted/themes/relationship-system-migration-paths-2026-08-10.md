## Executive read

The useful next question is not only which systems are thick, mid, or thin.

It is which mid-thickness systems are actively thickening.

The clearest thickening signals in the archive are:

- more direct behavior
- higher app or direct-mix usage
- more explicit member participation
- stronger media or advertising layers
- more payments or checkout control
- broader workflow ownership
- more first-party data capture

## What matters

### 1. Better disclosure is usually the first sign

The company starts making the relationship legible as an operating asset rather than only as a marketing outcome.

### 2. Adjacent workflow gets pulled under the same interface

One trip, order, project, or routine becomes a broader owned surface.

### 3. Not every strong system should become a classic loyalty wallet

Some categories will thicken through workflow, service, or platform control rather than through prepaid balances or fee-funded membership.

## Best current use

Use this memo to screen whether a category is moving toward stronger customer-ownership economics or remaining mainly product-, traffic-, or workflow-led.
