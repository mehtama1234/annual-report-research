## Executive read

Retail is not one thing.

The archive already supports several distinct retail systems:

- utility-and-membership retail
- replenishment retail
- bargain-discovery retail
- brand-led relationship retail
- beauty-and-discovery retail
- retail-service hybrids
- marketplace and recommerce retail
- pro-and-built-environment retail

## Working retail-system map

| Retail system | Core companies | What repeats | What makes the model stronger than a plain sale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Utility-and-membership retail | `Amazon`, `Costco`, `Walmart`, `Target` | repeat household spending plus convenience and account-level frequency | subscriptions or membership, ads, marketplace or seller services, fulfillment density, digital convenience |
| Replenishment retail | `Kroger`, `Dollar General`, `Dollar Tree` | recurring essentials trips and fill-in behavior | loyalty data, retail media, basket repetition, price architecture, convenience density |
| Bargain-discovery retail | `Ross`, `Burlington`, `TJX`, `Ollie's` | repeat trip behavior tied to branded value and discovery | traffic-led share capture, fast turns, branded-value signaling, closeout flexibility |
| Brand-led relationship retail | `Nike`, `lululemon`, `Levi Strauss`, `Abercrombie`, `Gap`, `American Eagle`, `Urban Outfitters`, `Victoria's Secret`, `Bath & Body Works`, `Tapestry`, `Deckers`, `Crocs`, `Birkenstock`, `YETI` | repeat identity-linked shopping and product-cycle participation | DTC control, pricing discipline, customer-data ownership, category authority, emotional or cultural relevance |
| Beauty-and-discovery retail | `Ulta` | repeat replenishment plus experimentation and services attachment | salon ecosystem, marketplace assortment, media monetization, app-and-loyalty reinforcement |
| Retail-service hybrid | `Best Buy` | repeat electronics and services interaction | services attachment, ads, marketplace expansion, omnichannel support |
| Marketplace and recommerce retail | `eBay` | repeat search, listing, enthusiast participation, and resale activity | ads, seller supply, trust tools, collectibles culture, lower-inventory marketplace economics |
| Pro-and-built-environment retail | `Home Depot`, `Lowe's` | maintenance, repair, remodel, and professional workflow demand | pro ecosystem capture, delivery density, services, trade relationships, project-linked repeat demand |

## What matters

### 1. The biggest split is relationship type, not channel format

The strongest retail divide is not physical versus digital.

It is utility, replenishment, discovery, identity, service, marketplace, or project workflow.

### 2. The second monetization layer is the best quality marker

Membership fees, ads, marketplace services, delivery convenience, loyalty data, and category extension are what separate stronger systems from plain merchandise margin.

### 3. Retail is one of the clearest consumer-behavior windows in the archive

Different retail systems sit on different consumer realities:

- convenience and utility
- affordability pressure
- value-hunt behavior
- identity and self-presentation
- maintenance and project workflow

## Best current use

Use this memo when the source taxonomy hides the fact that several companies are competing inside adjacent retail systems even if they are filed under different sectors.
