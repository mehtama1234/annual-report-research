# Thick vs. Thin Relationship Systems

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Scope

This memo separates businesses with a thick disclosed relationship layer from businesses where repeat demand is real but the relationship remains thinner and more dependent on price, product, traffic, or format.

## Executive read

The archive does not support one generic loyalty story.

Some systems are thick:

- the relationship is identified directly
- the filings disclose the relationship economics
- a second monetization layer is explicit

Other systems are thinner:

- repeat behavior is real
- stickiness often comes from price, value, convenience, discovery, or category culture
- the filings do not show the same formalized member or wallet layer

## What matters

### 1. Thick systems disclose the relationship as part of the model

The relationship is measurable and strategically central.

### 2. Thin systems can still be strong

Thin does not mean weak. It means the economics live closer to traffic, product, assortment, convenience, or operational design.

### 3. Cultural stickiness is not the same as relationship thickness

Some companies are behaviorally sticky without having a thick wallet, membership, or status engine.

## Best current use

Use this memo to separate customer-ownership economics from price-, traffic-, or discovery-led repeat demand.
