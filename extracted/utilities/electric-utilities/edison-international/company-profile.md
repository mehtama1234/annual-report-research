## What changed

- Utility type: Edison adds the clearest wildfire-stressed California electric utility model in the current archive. This is not just a growth utility or a merchant counterexample. It is a utility where climate adaptation, liability architecture, catastrophe funding, and regulatory credibility are central to the investment case.
- Capital plan: the `2025` annual report and Q1 `2026` materials frame a very large grid-hardening and resilience buildout, with `2025` capital expenditures of `$6.729B`, `2025` rate base of `$48.206B`, and a `2026`-`2030` capex plan of roughly `$38B` to `$41B`.
- Growth algorithm: management continues to frame the business around `5%` to `7%` core EPS CAGR from `2025` to `2030`, with `2026` core EPS guidance of `$5.90` to `$6.20`.
- Stress angle: unlike the cleaner regulated wires stories, Edison’s archive now has direct exposure to wildfire-fund mechanics, potential ignition association, recovery funding structures, self-insurance layers, securitization support, and the politics of who absorbs catastrophe costs.
- Demand tone: Edison is still a clean-energy and electrification utility, but the tone is more about resiliency, affordability, and system stability than about hyperscaler load conversion.
- Quarter progression: the trailing-quarter set moved from Q4 `2025` core EPS of `$1.86` to Q1 `2026` core EPS of `$1.42` and Q2 `2026` core EPS of `$1.54`, while guidance stayed intact and the wildfire-mitigation and regulatory story remained central.

## Open questions

- How durable is California’s current wildfire-liability and recovery architecture if another large fire event occurs before the state further rebalances catastrophe-cost sharing?
- How much of Edison’s valuation and funding resilience now depends on continued legislative and regulatory willingness to support recovery tools such as securitization and the Wildfire Fund?
- Does Edison’s climate-adaptation and wildfire-hardening model become a template for other stressed utilities, or is California too structurally specific?
