## What changed

- Earnings: NextEra moved from full-year `2025` adjusted EPS of `$3.71` to Q1 `2026` adjusted EPS of `$1.09` and Q2 `2026` adjusted EPS of `$1.15`, while continuing to target the high end of the `2026` adjusted EPS range of `$3.92` to `$4.02`.
- Utility growth: Florida Power & Light remains a major rate-base machine. FPL regulatory capital employed moved from `$68.0B` in `2024` to `$73.5B` in `2025`, then from `$71.4B` in Q1 `2025` to `$77.7B` in Q1 `2026`, and from `$72.9B` in Q2 `2025` to `$79.7B` in Q2 `2026`.
- Development backlog: NextEra Energy Resources closed `2025` with backlog near `29.8 GW`, added `4 GW` in Q1 `2026`, and added another `3.6 GW` in Q2 `2026`, with battery storage and large-load opportunities remaining central.
- Business mix: unlike AEP, NextEra is not just a regulated utility. It combines a large Florida regulated platform with an energy-infrastructure development engine spanning renewables, storage, natural gas, transmission, and large-load hub strategy.
- Large-load angle: the company is increasingly pitching itself as a provider of full-stack solutions for hyperscalers and other large-load customers, including data-center hubs, gas generation, renewables, storage, and transmission.
- Strategic shift: by Q2 `2026`, the proposed Dominion combination became part of the story, reinforcing that scale, regulated capital opportunity, and load-growth positioning are now strategic priorities.
- Management tone: management is more overtly growth-oriented than AEP. The tone emphasizes backlog conversion, hub pipeline buildout, long-duration EPS compounding, and system-scale development capability.

## Open questions

- How much of the large-load and hub pipeline converts into contracted earnings versus staying conceptual pipeline?
- Does the proposed Dominion combination deepen the utility moat or add execution and regulatory complexity that slows conversion?
- How durable is the Energy Resources backlog advantage if supply-chain, permitting, or hyperscaler demand timing shifts?
