## What changed in this window

- Essential remained a water-utility company by taxonomy, but operationally it is a blended regulated-network platform across water, wastewater, and natural gas.
- The capital story intensified: the company invested more than `$1.4B` in `2025`, then reiterated a `$1.7B` infrastructure plan for `2026`.
- Acquisition-led municipal-system consolidation remained a core growth mechanism, with signed transactions spanning multiple states and DELCORA still part of the longer-term pipeline discussion.
- PFAS compliance, pipe replacement, and reliability spending kept reinforcing that utility resilience is not a passive yield story but a large execution and regulatory-capital story.
- The pending American Water merger became a major interpretive lens because it tied utility-scale growth to regulatory approval sequencing, cost discipline, and the creation of a larger multi-state water platform.
