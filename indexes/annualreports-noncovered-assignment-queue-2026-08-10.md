## First routing step

Before using the buckets below:

- screen the name with the short ownership rule
- route consumer and cultural candidates through the consumer-behavior core
- route hidden-layer and infrastructure candidates through the infrastructure core

Then use the queue to decide whether the next move is `Take now`, `Take only if needed`, or `Leave to other threads`.

If the work lands inside one of the mature infrastructure lanes, use the matching extension shortlist before taking another company so the next move is reuse-first and subtype-opening rather than generic packet accumulation.

## Bucket meanings

- `Take now`: highest-fit work for this thread because the analytical gain comes mainly from a taxonomy blind spot
- `Take only if needed`: valid blind-spot work, but lower urgency or better handled as extension after stronger lanes are advanced
- `Leave to other threads`: ordinary sector work unless a company clearly sharpens a blind-spot system

## Leave to other threads

Unless a target clearly improves one of the blind-spot systems, leave these to the ordinary sector threads:

- standard upstream materials and commodity names
- ordinary machinery, transportation, and industrial backlog companies
- conventional utilities
- generic banks and insurers without stronger wallet, membership, or relationship-system importance
- straightforward pharma, medtech, and hospital packetization
- ordinary software or media issuers with no marketplace, ecosystem, participation, attention-system, or interface-control angle
- ordinary consumer brands whose only value is more category coverage

## Fast collision test

Keep a company in this thread only if at least one of the following is true:

1. the company materially strengthens a cross-sector system memo
2. the company is mainly useful because of relationship ownership, coordination, loyalty, recurring attention, recurring behavior, or hidden infrastructure logic
3. the formal `AnnualReports` label is materially weaker than the operating reality

If none of those are true, leave the company to the normal sector thread.

## Default assignment rule

If there is a collision between this thread and a normal sector thread:

- this thread keeps the name when the best explanation is system-level
- the normal sector thread keeps the name when the best explanation is ordinary industry coverage
