## Highest-confidence uses

- separate beauty discovery interfaces from brand-owner beauty systems
- separate accessible growth beauty from prestige repair
- keep weaker or more uneven portfolio beauty distinct from stronger recovery cases
- preserve the beauty lane as a behavior and relationship system rather than a plain `Personal Products` bucket

## Current best next moves

1. Extend the lane only if another name materially sharpens the discovery-versus-brand or prestige-versus-access split.
2. Compare this index against owned-demand and retail-system indexes when the goal is testing where beauty behaves like retail, media, service, or pure brand control.
3. Use the related memo when the goal is explaining why beauty is one of the stronger cultural blind spots in the `AnnualReports` taxonomy.
