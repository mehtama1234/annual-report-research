## Why this ordering

- `Ecolab` has already established the service-led hygiene-and-compliance branch beside `Veralto`
- `Silgan` has already established the rigid-container and closure-control branch beside `Sonoco` and `AptarGroup`
- `Casella Waste Systems` has now established the regional-density waste branch beside `Waste Management`, `Republic Services`, and `Clean Harbors`
- `International Paper` and `Packaging Corporation of America` have now established that the substrate branch also splits into broader corrugated-throughput scale versus tighter North American corrugated peer density
- the next move inside this lane should only happen if a later target adds a distinctly different physical-layer subtype rather than another operator in an already-covered branch

## Strict take rule

Take one of these names for the blind-spot thread only if the expected result is one of these:

- a sharper packaging-conversion versus packaging-substrate split
- a sharper packaging-format versus packaging-interface split
- a sharper general-waste versus specialized-environmental-service split
- a sharper engineered-environmental-control versus embedded-quality-or-water-control split

If the result is only another materials, paper, packaging, or environmental-services packet, leave the name to the normal sector thread.

## Bottom line

The next high-payoff extension inside this lane is not another random industrial name.

It is the next company that most clearly sharpens one of two open boundaries:

- whether another packaging-control or substrate name opens a genuinely different physical layer beyond carton conversion, broader format breadth, dispensing interface, rigid-container concentration, SBS substrate, corrugated-throughput scale, and North American corrugated peer density
- whether another hidden-infrastructure name opens a genuinely different post-use, plant-embedded, or compliance-service subtype beyond broad route density, scale-peer route density, hazardous specialization, regional-density concentration, heavier plant hardware, embedded quality-and-water control, and service-led hygiene embedment
