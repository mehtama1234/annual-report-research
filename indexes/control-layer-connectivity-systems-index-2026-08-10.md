## Highest-confidence uses

- separate edge control, installed-base enterprise network control, architecture-specialist AI networking, cyber-governance, active transport, and upstream optical-content supply on one page
- separate newer edge-native control from incumbent delivery-to-security transition inside the same nominal internet bucket
- separate upstream optical input from stressed downstream connectivity equipment and active transport on one page
- sort the lane by the real network layer controlled rather than by formal source taxonomy
- keep cloud, AI, security, transport, and optical-input demand in one reusable blind-spot screen
- explain why these names belong in one modern infrastructure lane even when the site splits them across older technology categories
