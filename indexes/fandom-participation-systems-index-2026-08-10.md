## Highest-confidence uses

- separate physical-plus-digital fandom systems from digital-native participation systems
- separate collectible-ruleset fandom, brand-universe extension, and creator-world participation on one page
- separate creator-world participation from publisher-managed franchise monetization
- preserve play and fandom as a recurring cultural system rather than a generic toy or software bucket
- keep sports live-services participation distinct from broader franchise or creator-economy models
