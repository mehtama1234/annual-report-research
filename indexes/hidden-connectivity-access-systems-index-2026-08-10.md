## Highest-confidence uses

- separate global tower ownership, domestic tower simplification, pure-play tower rent, route-density terrestrial ownership, broader fiber-backbone ownership, orbital resilient-coverage infrastructure, and heavier-bandwidth sovereign or mobility satellite systems on one page
- sort the lane by the real access-layer structure controlled rather than by formal REIT taxonomy
- preserve the distinction between connectivity ownership and the higher-layer control, transport, and optical systems built on top of it
- explain why these names belong in a hidden-connectivity lane even when the site classifies them as ordinary real estate
