## Highest-confidence uses

- separate true owned-demand systems from broader traffic and interface-control systems
- sort the lane by channel posture instead of by legacy product category
- separate service-linked owned demand, hybrid brand control, and category-interface control on one page
- compare where service layers deepen the relationship beyond the first sale
- keep track of where identity, fit, comfort, status, utility, or beauty drive the owned-demand system
