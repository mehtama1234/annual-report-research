## Highest-confidence uses

- separate commerce flywheels, trip interfaces, local-demand coordination, device ecosystems, creator-world participation, and recurring attention platforms on one page
- sort the lane by main owned interface rather than by formal source taxonomy
- keep installed-base lock-in, paid-attention habit, and creator-participation systems in one searchable screen
- preserve the broader platform boundary while letting the fuller `Netflix`, `Spotify`, `Alphabet`, `Meta`, `Disney`, and `Snap` attention roster live in [Recurring Attention Systems Index](/indexes/recurring-attention-systems-index-2026-08-10.md)
- explain why `Consumer Services`, `Application Software`, `Music & Video Stores`, `Transportation Services`, and similar legacy labels are too blunt for modern ecosystem businesses
- identify where the main relationship asset is utility, trust, orchestration, participation, or attention
