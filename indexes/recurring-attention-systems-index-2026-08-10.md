## Highest-confidence uses

- separate paid entertainment access, mixed discovery monetization, dominant ad-funded social attention, attention-plus-infrastructure control, franchise-linked membership ecosystems, and lower-moat ad dependence on one page
- keep recurring attention as a first-class blind-spot system rather than letting it dissolve into generic internet, media, or software labels
- compare where the economic center is subscription, mixed monetization, advertising, infrastructure leverage, or physical-plus-digital fan monetization
- explain why `Music & Video Stores`, `Internet Information Providers`, and `Entertainment - Diversified` are too blunt for modern attention businesses
- identify where attention is mainly cultural access, ambient communication, discovery, or multi-format fan attachment
