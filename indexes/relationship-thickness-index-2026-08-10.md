## Highest-confidence uses

- compare thick, mid, and thin systems without flattening all repeat behavior into one loyalty story
- separate explicit membership monetization, interface-led thickening, and thinner traffic-led repetition on one page
- separate workflow ownership from fee-funded or wallet-funded relationship ownership
- identify where customer economics are explicit versus where they remain more indirect
- screen new blind-spot additions by asking where the relationship layer actually sits

## Current best next moves

1. Reuse the direct `Costco` versus `Ulta` versus `Ross` comparison first when the question is whether a retail-facing model is thick, mid-thickness, or thinner in relationship design.
2. Extend the index only if another company materially sharpens the thick-versus-mid or thin-to-mid boundary.
3. Use this file with [Loyalty Wallet Systems Index](/indexes/loyalty-wallet-systems-index-2026-08-10.md) when the goal is separating formal economic relationship systems from broader workflow or cultural stickiness.
4. Reuse this map when evaluating whether a category is thickening over time or remaining mainly price, traffic, assortment, or operational-throughput driven.
