## Highest-confidence uses

- separate broad staples trust from narrower efficacy-led and wellness-adjacent care systems
- separate shelf-based repeat demand from digital-native replenishment and care management
- preserve routine use as a behavior system rather than a flat staples bucket
- keep low-consideration repeat trust distinct from discovery, ritual, and discretionary identity systems
