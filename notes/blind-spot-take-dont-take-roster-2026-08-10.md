## Purpose

This note is the shortest practical answer to:

"If the other threads are covering the normal `AnnualReports` industry tree, which names should this blind-spot thread take, and which names should it leave alone?"

It is not a claim that these companies have no `AnnualReports` industry page.

It is a claim that the browse-tree label is too weak to explain the real system the company belongs to.

Do not use thin, stale, awkward, sparse, or incomplete browse-tree page quality as a reason to claim a company here.

## Don't take

Usually leave these to the normal sector threads:

- ordinary commodity, mining, chemical, and upstream materials names
- plain industrial backlog and machinery names
- generic utilities
- ordinary regional banks and plain insurers
- straightforward pharma, medtech, and hospital names
- generic software or hardware issuers with no stronger ecosystem, attention, infrastructure, or workflow-control logic
- generic telecom names with no stronger access-layer, route-density, or control-layer angle
- ordinary consumer brands whose only contribution is more category coverage
- ordinary retailers where the industry label already explains the business well enough

## Fast rule

Take the company here only if the better question is:

"what system does this company actually control?"

Leave it to the normal thread if the better question is:

"what `AnnualReports` industry page does this belong to?"

## Bottom line

If the other threads are covering the browse tree, this thread should mostly own:

- behind-the-shelf infrastructure
- control-layer infrastructure
- hidden connectivity and access ownership
- physical execution and embedded workflow systems

Everything else should stay conditional and system-led rather than becoming generic uncovered-sector cleanup.
