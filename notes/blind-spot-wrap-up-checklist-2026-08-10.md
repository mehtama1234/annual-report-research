## Track 2: Research Wrap-Up

This track is complete only when the blind-spot lane has reached a credible stopping point as a research program rather than only as a governance system.

### A. Core infrastructure lanes

Each of the four default first-claim lanes should be at a stable reuse-first state:

- behind-the-shelf consumer infrastructure
- control-layer and connectivity infrastructure
- hidden connectivity and access-layer ownership
- physical execution and embedded workflow systems

For each lane, completion means:

- the lane memo is live
- the lane index is live
- direct comparison structure is live
- extension shortlist logic is live
- the next move is selective subtype sharpening rather than obvious missing-anchor repair

### B. Conditional outer lanes

These do not need exhaustive packet count.

They need enough boundary quality that another worker would not confuse them with ordinary sector coverage:

- marketplace and coordination
- recurring attention
- owned-demand and channel control
- loyalty, wallet, and membership
- consumer behavior and cultural-demand systems
- modern built-environment systems

For each lane, completion means:

- the core boundary is already legible
- the next useful move would be a true subtype improvement rather than a basic missing comparison
- ordinary sector threads could cover adjacent names without weakening the blind-spot archive

### C. Packet and memo discipline

- new names are added only when they materially sharpen a live blind-spot system
- ordinary uncovered-sector cleanup is no longer leaking into this lane
- direct comparison memos are used to widen systems before new packet sprawl begins

### D. Program stop test

Mark research wrap-up complete only when all of these are true:

- the four default infrastructure lanes no longer have an obvious missing anchor or missing boundary that weakens the archive
- the conditional outer lanes all have usable system boundaries and no high-payoff missing comparison is still obvious
- the next best additions would be marginal rather than structurally important
- another worker could reasonably stop without feeling the blind-spot archive is missing a major system the `AnnualReports` browse tree fails to express
