# CLI 4 Kickoff Brief

Date baseline: 2026-08-10
Repo: `annual-report-research-new-lanes`

## Lane

`CLI 4: Healthcare Frontier`

## Mission

Open a healthcare lane that compares frontier or high-attention healthcare narratives against scaled recurring-care systems.
Do not treat healthcare as one undifferentiated bucket.
Build a lane that shows where reimbursement, procedure volume, care delivery, installed base, and therapeutic innovation actually sit.

## Industries to cover

- Health Care Plans
- Hospitals
- Long-Term Care Facilities
- Medical Appliances & Equipment
- Medical Instruments & Supplies
- Drug Manufacturers - Major

## Batch target

Complete `3` to `4` flagship companies in one coherent comparison set.
Use `2025` annual reports plus the latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10`.

## Strong first-batch shapes

- health plan plus hospital plus medtech equipment plus major drug maker
- recurring-care operator plus installed-base device company plus therapeutic platform plus care-delivery system
- reimbursement-heavy operator plus procedure-volume operator plus tools-and-equipment supplier plus pharmaceutical scale player

## What the lane should clarify

- where recurring care sits versus frontier healthcare narratives
- how reimbursement and utilization shape the real economics
- whether the moat comes from network scale, distribution, installed base, procedure workflow, or intellectual property
- how aging, chronic disease burden, staffing stress, and institutional complexity recur across different healthcare models

## Cross-company themes to extract

- reimbursement pressure versus pricing power
- installed base and procedure pull-through
- utilization, occupancy, admissions, or case-mix sensitivity
- policy dependence and regulatory complexity
- recurring care demand versus one-time innovation excitement
- capital intensity and labor intensity inside care delivery
