## Lane

`CLI 5: Connectivity, Telecom, and Technical Infrastructure`

## Mission

Build the missing connectivity and physical-infrastructure layer behind software, cloud, AI, and communications.
Do not let the lane collapse into only software platforms or only chip-equipment names.
Make the stack visible from network ownership and traffic gateways into storage, measurement, systems control, and fab-enablement layers.

## Batch target

Complete `3` to `4` flagship companies in one coherent comparison set.
Use `2025` annual reports plus the latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10`.

If the lane keeps opening cleanly, extend beyond the first `3` to `4` toward a broader `4` to `8` flagship-company map rather than stopping at a narrow starter quartet.

## What the lane should clarify

- where connectivity economics sit versus application-layer software economics
- whether traffic growth is monetized by networks, platforms, measurement vendors, security layers, or equipment suppliers
- how AI and cloud demand propagate into fiber, wireless, storage, systems control, testing, and semiconductor-capex chains
- which businesses look utility-like and recurring versus cyclical and buildout-dependent
- where regulation, spectrum, capex intensity, and technical complexity create durable moats

## Cross-company themes to extract

- capex burden versus pricing power
- control-point software versus asset-heavy infrastructure
- traffic growth without proportional profit capture
- bottleneck ownership in testing, inspection, process control, and observability
- security and systems-management layers as trust infrastructure
- recurring network-like revenue versus cyclical build-cycle revenue
- persistent storage, data-retention, and archive-density demand as a physical layer beneath transport and compute
- consumer, cultural, and institutional normalization of always-on digital life, AI-assisted workflow, and retained data trails
- which companies carry the physical burden stack versus which companies capture the cleaner monetization stack
