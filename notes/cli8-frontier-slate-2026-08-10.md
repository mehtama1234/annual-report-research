## Selected core slate

### 1. Wholesale and distribution infrastructure

- Ferguson plc
  - why: building products, plumbing, HVAC, waterworks, fire and fabrication distribution with dense branch and DC infrastructure
  - likely archive role: the built-environment channel-control case
- WESCO International Inc.
  - why: electrical, industrial, communications, utility, and supply-chain services distribution
  - likely archive role: the multi-category industrial and network-infrastructure distribution case
- W.W. Grainger, Inc.
  - why: industrial MRO, safety, inventory availability, and embedded procurement workflows
  - likely archive role: the procurement power and service-level discipline case

### 2. Institutional supply and replenishment

- McKesson Corporation
  - why: pharmaceutical distribution, oncology, specialty, and care-delivery infrastructure
  - likely archive role: the healthcare-distribution backbone case
- Cencora, Inc.
  - why: drug distribution, specialty logistics, manufacturer services, and provider dependence
  - likely archive role: a second healthcare-supply intermediary with a different service mix
- Sysco Corporation
  - why: foodservice distribution, route density, restaurant/institutional replenishment, and local service economics
  - likely archive role: the food and hospitality replenishment case

### 3. Asset access and operating support

- United Rentals, Inc.
  - why: rental versus ownership economics, fleet utilization, service attachment, and uptime dependence
  - likely archive role: the clearest large-scale rental-access infrastructure case

### 4. Service-layer physical interface

- Hyatt Hotels Corporation
  - why: hospitality as the physical service interface where procurement, labor, occupancy, owner relationships, and loyalty systems meet
  - likely archive role: the institutional and travel-facing physical interface case

## Why these eight

- They cover all four required sub-lanes with public-company scale.
- They keep the frontier centered on the middle layer rather than drifting into pure manufacturing or pure consumer demand.
- They create strong contrasts across goods distribution, healthcare replenishment, route and branch density, rental access, and hospitality operations.
- They avoid over-relying on companies already heavily used in the earlier frontier bundles.

## Early evidence observations

- AnnualReports.com currently lags at `2024` for at least Ferguson, WESCO, and McKesson as of `2026-08-10`.
- That lag is useful evidence for this frontier because the assignment explicitly requires AnnualReports taxonomy and archive confirmation, but not blind dependence on the aggregator for the current annual chain.
- The authoritative annual and quarter chain will need to come primarily from official investor relations pages and SEC filings for most of this slate.
