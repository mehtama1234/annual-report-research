# Collection Window

Date baseline: 2026-08-08

This project is not trying to collect every filing ever published.

Current target window:

## Annual materials

- `2025` annual report
- `2025` annual filing such as `10-K`, `20-F`, or equivalent

## Quarterly materials

Collect `2026` quarterly materials first, and use late `2025` only when needed to complete the latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-08`.

For many calendar-year reporters that means:

1. `2026 Q2`
2. `2026 Q1`
3. `2025 Q4`

For off-calendar fiscal reporters, use the latest three reported fiscal quarters available by `2026-08-08` and preserve the company's actual fiscal labels.

Examples:

- Apple may require fiscal-quarter labels that do not match calendar-quarter names.
- Microsoft may require fiscal-year quarter labels such as `FY2026 Q4`.
- Retailers with January or February year-ends may shift the exact quarter mapping.

## Why this matters

This keeps the research centered on `2025` annual disclosures and the most current operating read-through from `2026`, while still respecting fiscal-calendar differences.
