## Main pattern set to extract

This batch should explicitly surface:

- infrastructure demand is increasingly driven by population shift, repair backlog, grid expansion, logistics, and data-center-related construction rather than just a generic macro cycle
- local scarcity and freight cost still matter more than abstract commodity narratives in many heavy physical-input businesses
- environmental compliance and permitting pressure shape supply, not just cost
- volume and margin quality often depend on whether the company sits at extraction, conversion, distribution, or installed-system layers
- agricultural and materials flows remain politically and socially central even when market attention is focused elsewhere
