# End-To-End Insight Master Instruction

Date: 2026-08-11
Repo: `annual-report-research`

## Purpose

Use this as the operator-ready instruction block for any thread working inside this repo.

This page is meant to be the shortest complete version of the standard:

- what the work is
- what the work is not
- what sources count
- what a finished company looks like
- what a finished lane looks like
- what insight extraction actually means
- what must be handed off before stopping

## Plain-English Goal

Your job is to turn company filings into evidence-backed explanations of how the economy is changing.

You are not just collecting annual reports.

You are using annual reports, annual filings, and recent quarter chains to extract:

- consumer behavior shifts
- cultural and societal normalization patterns
- industrial and operating pressures
- technical and workflow dependencies
- trust, compliance, and verification demand
- who owns the relationship
- who carries the burden stack
- what recurring economics repeat across companies
- what would prove or break the thesis next

If the work stops at document collection or company summary, it is not finished.

## The Required Evidence Chain

Every strong output should complete this chain:

`source document -> exact fact -> company packet -> company strategy read -> lane economics -> cross-company comparison -> plain-English insight -> watch metric -> thesis breaker`

If the chain breaks in the middle, the work is still intermediate.

## Master Instruction Block


## What A Finished Insight Should Sound Like

The archive should aim for this sentence shape:

`Because {exact fact} at {company} and {exact fact} at {company}, this suggests {plain-English conclusion}. It matters because {economic meaning}. It weakens if {specific metric or event}.`

That forces the work to stay concrete.

## What To Avoid

Avoid:

- filing inventories with no interpretation
- lane summaries made of company lists
- management language copied without translation
- trend claims with no reported facts
- one-company anecdotes presented as system-wide truth
- vague words like `tailwind`, `platform`, `consumer strength`, or `AI benefit` without mechanism

## Closeout Rule

If another thread cannot continue the work from your files and commit history without reopening the whole lane from scratch, the handoff is not done.
