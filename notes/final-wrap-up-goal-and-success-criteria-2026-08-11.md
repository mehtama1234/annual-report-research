# Final Wrap-Up Goal And Success Criteria

Date baseline: `2026-08-11`
Repo: `annual-report-research`

## Purpose

This note defines the final end-to-end goal for wrapping up the current archive push.

It is not a blank-slate build brief.

It assumes the archive already has:

- real company packets
- lane summaries
- proof pages
- cross-company themes
- handoff notes
- repeated-signal analysis

The remaining job is to close the archive honestly rather than to keep expanding it loosely.

The standard is not:

- "collect more filings"
- "start more folders"
- "sound insightful in general terms"

The standard is:

- turn the existing research base into a usable archive that connects company facts to sector structure, operating pressure, consumer behavior, cultural shifts, burden transfer, and exact next watchpoints
- make those conclusions easy for a skeptical reader to verify
- leave the repo in a state where another worker can continue without reconstructing the logic from scratch

## The Meaty End-To-End Goal

Take the current archive from:

- strong but uneven

to:

- interpretation-rich
- evidence-grounded
- continuation-ready
- honest about what is fully proven versus only directionally strong

In one sentence:

Finish `annual-report-research` as a plain-English, evidence-backed archive that lets a skeptical reader move from company filings to lane economics, cross-company patterns, consumer and societal shifts, operating pressure, and exact next watchpoints without depending on vague claims, missing flagship roles, or misleading evidence references.

In operating terms, the final job is to finish an archive that can answer all of the following across the active lanes:

1. `What changed in the real economy and why?`
2. `Which exact companies prove that change?`
3. `What hard facts in the filings support the conclusion?`
4. `What repeated patterns show up across multiple companies, not just one?`
5. `Where do consumer, cultural, demographic, or industrial shifts show up in actual operations?`
6. `Who captures the upside and who carries the cost, labor burden, capital burden, inventory burden, or regulatory burden?`
7. `What remains unresolved, thin, qualified, or directionally strong but not fully proven?`
8. `What exact names or packets should be pursued next?`

That means the wrap-up goal is not just packet completion. It is:

- packet completion
- packet honesty
- lane synthesis
- cross-lane synthesis
- evidence portability
- handoff clarity

## Required Outputs Of The Wrap-Up

Before this archive can be called wrapped up, it should produce all of the following in a coherent form:

### 1. Company-level outputs

For each flagship company that matters to the final lane set:

- a source-complete or honestly qualified packet
- a thematic interpretation that explains what the company reveals about the lane
- explicit use of `2025` annual materials plus the latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10`
- clear source-authority ordering where AnnualReports taxonomy lagged company IR or SEC filings

### 2. Lane-level outputs

For each major active lane:

- a readable lane summary
- a set of flagship companies that carry the lane
- repeated signals that recur across those companies
- burden splits showing who benefits and who absorbs pressure
- exact next names where coverage is still thin

### 3. Cross-lane outputs

The archive should also leave behind:

- cross-company themes
- cross-lane themes
- concrete proof pages that connect sectors rather than leaving each packet isolated
- simple-language explanations of the bigger pattern, not just company-by-company facts

### 4. End-state outputs

The final checkpoint should include:

- a wrap-up handoff note
- completed companies
- qualified companies
- partial companies
- the strongest repeated signals
- the exact remaining frontier names
- the evidence-reference model
- the commit hash that defines the checkpoint

## What “Wrap This Up” Actually Means

Wrapping this up does not mean:

- downloading every possible filing
- starting unlimited new packets
- chasing every uncovered company
- making the repo look cosmetically complete

Wrapping this up means finishing the highest-value remaining closure work across five layers at once:

1. `lane closure`
2. `proof quality`
3. `writeup quality`
4. `reference quality`
5. `final handoff quality`

The final state should let a reader answer:

1. `What changed in this part of the economy?`
2. `Which companies prove it?`
3. `Who benefits and who carries the burden?`
4. `What patterns repeat across lanes?`
5. `What remains uncertain?`
6. `What exactly should the next filing reader watch?`

## The Five Final Workstreams

### 1. Lane Closure

Close the thinnest requested sub-lanes so the archive no longer feels like a few strong islands surrounded by thin edges.

The main unresolved lane-role gaps are:

- recreation:
  - owner-versus-brand comparison depth
  - fuller integration of the already-existing second food-wholesale anchor into the main recreation proof layer
- CLI 4:
  - second payer
  - second hospital
  - stronger long-term-care or scaled-care contrast if needed
- CLI 5:
  - pure-play healthcare-IT or hospital-workflow comparator if the goal is full explicit closure beyond the now-completed bridge
- CLI 6:
  - second life-insurance anchor
  - mortgage REIT anchor
  - cleaner savings-and-loans or thrift analog if feasible

This does not require dozens of new names.

It requires enough exact additions to remove the most obvious missing-role holes.

### 2. Proof Quality

Upgrade the weakest evidence chains so the archive can distinguish:

- `proven`
- `qualified but usable`
- `not yet canonically repaired`

This means:

- closing the highest-priority quarter-window ambiguities
- tightening source-authority language
- repairing misleading packet or ledger claims
- upgrading the best qualified cases where possible

The key standard is:

- do not claim stronger evidence than the current repo actually proves

### 3. Writeup Quality

Raise the remaining weaker writeups toward the newer proof-page standard.

The main requirement is:

- every important claim should feel concrete, causal, and backed by named examples

That means the writeups should not stop at:

- "aging creates demand"
- "AI increases infrastructure needs"
- "loyalty matters"
- "trust is valuable"

They should show:

- which exact company facts led to that conclusion
- why that pattern repeats across multiple companies
- where the burden sits
- what would weaken or break the conclusion

The required writing standard is:

- simple language
- concrete facts
- visible chain of reasoning
- no cliche theme language standing in for proof
- no unsupported "macro insight" that cannot be traced back to packets

### 4. Reference Quality

Normalize the evidence-reference model so the repo points to evidence in an intentional, comprehensible way.

Because raw offload to Google Drive is intentional, the real remaining reference problem is not "raw is outside Git."

It is:

- many packets still point directly to sibling worktrees instead of:
  - the current repo
  - or the documented offload / manifest / resolver model

The final repo should make it obvious whether a source is:

- local in the current repo
- intentionally offloaded and recoverable through the manifest layer
- inherited and not yet canonically normalized

### 5. Final Handoff Quality

The last step is not just to stop editing.

The last step is to leave a clean continuation surface.

That means the final handoff should state:

- which lanes are effectively closed
- which lanes are coherent but still thin at the edge
- which packets are proven
- which packets are qualified
- which packets should not be used as proof-standard yet
- which exact names should be pursued next
- which commit is the wrap-up checkpoint

## The Success Criteria

The wrap-up is successful only if all of the following are true.

### A. Lane Success

- each major active lane has a coherent explanation, not just scattered packets
- the thinnest high-priority role gaps have been closed or explicitly deferred with exact next targets
- the archive no longer depends on one single flagship name to carry an entire requested sub-lane
- each lane shows more than filing coverage; it shows operating structure, demand drivers, pressure points, and repeated patterns

### B. Interpretation Success

- the main lane writeups and proof pages explain:
  - what changed
  - why it changed
  - who benefits
  - who carries the burden
  - what repeats across companies
- major claims are supported by concrete company examples rather than only theme language
- older weakly evidenced notes have either been upgraded or clearly superseded by stronger proof pages
- major social, consumer, demographic, and industrial conclusions are explicitly tied back to company facts rather than asserted as free-floating macro commentary

### C. Evidence Success

- the highest-priority thin-lane packets have explicit `2025` annual plus latest-three-quarters logic
- source-authority ordering is clear where AnnualReports lagged
- misleading packet or ledger claims have been removed
- the repo clearly distinguishes `proven` from `qualified`
- the most important claims can be walked back to named filings, quarters, and packet examples without guesswork

### D. Reference Success

- the repo no longer casually relies on arbitrary sibling-worktree paths without explanation
- the evidence path for important packets is either:
  - local and inspectable
  - intentionally offloaded and documented
  - or explicitly marked as inherited and not yet canonically normalized
- a future reader can tell how to recover the underlying evidence without reverse-engineering past threads

### E. Handoff Success

- the final continuation layer tells another worker:
  - what is complete
  - what is qualified
  - what is still partial
  - what the next exact targets are
  - what commit defines the wrap-up checkpoint
- the archive can be resumed by a new worker with minimal oral history

## Non-Negotiable Questions The Final Archive Must Answer

If the wrap-up is real, the archive should answer questions like these clearly and concretely:

- how aging shows up not as a slogan but as specific demand in distribution, diagnostics, hospitals, devices, home care, insurance, and long-term support
- how participation, leisure, gifting, travel, hosting, and occasion spending split between brands, owners, wholesalers, operators, and physical asset holders
- how enterprise and telecom infrastructure economics split between visible software winners and hidden physical-control-layer toll collectors
- how healthcare economics split between innovation winners and scaled recurring-care burden carriers
- how capital-heavy sectors turn balance-sheet strength, float, spread, underwriting, and property structure into strategic advantage or vulnerability

If a major lane cannot answer those kinds of questions with named examples, it is not wrapped up yet.

## The Ending Criteria

Stop only when the archive can honestly pass this ending test:

### 1. Lane-ending test

A skeptical reader can open each major lane and see:

- flagship companies
- lane summary
- repeated signals
- burden split
- next questions

without feeling that a key requested bucket is still obviously missing.

### 2. Proof-ending test

A skeptical reader can tell, for the most important packets:

- which cases are fully proven
- which are qualified
- why they are qualified
- what exact evidence is still missing if they are not fully proven

### 3. Writeup-ending test

A skeptical reader can read the main proof pages and say:

- "I see the exact companies"
- "I see the facts"
- "I see the causal chain"
- "I see why this is a bigger pattern"
- "I see what would break the claim"

### 4. Reference-ending test

A skeptical reader is not forced to guess whether a broken-looking local path is:

- intentional offload
- stale sibling-worktree dependence
- or a missing file

The repo should tell them.

### 5. Handoff-ending test

Another worker can continue from the repo without reconstructing:

- which lanes are actually done
- which packets are only qualified
- which gaps still matter most
- how the evidence-storage model works

### 6. Skeptical-reader ending test

A skeptical outsider should be able to inspect the archive and conclude:

- this is not just a download archive
- this is not just a set of shallow company notes
- this actually explains the structure of the lanes
- the conclusions are backed by named facts
- the uncertain parts are admitted rather than hidden

## What Does Not Need To Be True Before Stopping

Do not wait for impossible perfection.

The wrap-up does not require:

- every packet in the archive to be fully proven
- every historical note to be rewritten
- every external dependency to be physically moved into Git
- every possible sub-lane to have maximum depth

The right standard is:

- the major insights are real
- the major lanes are coherent
- the major evidence claims are honest
- the remaining uncertainty is explicit

## What Should Still Count As Failure

Do not call the project wrapped up if any of these remain true:

- the archive still depends on vague cross-sector claims with no concrete examples
- a major lane still has only isolated packets and no coherent proof page or summary logic
- a packet still overclaims source completeness that the repo cannot actually support
- the reader still cannot tell whether an evidence path is local, offloaded, inherited, or stale
- the next worker would still need private thread history to understand what is complete

## The Concrete Final Sequence

The highest-yield order for finishing is:

1. close the biggest remaining lane-role gaps with a small number of high-value names
2. upgrade or clarify the highest-priority qualified packets
3. raise the weakest still-important writeups to the proof-page standard
4. normalize the most important non-canonical evidence references
5. leave one final wrap-up handoff that states:
   - completed
   - qualified
   - partial
   - next exact names
   - reference model
   - commit hash

## Bottom-Line Goal Statement

The end-to-end goal is to finish `annual-report-research` as a serious research archive that:

- opens the requested sectors end to end rather than sampling them
- ties company filings to real-world patterns rather than stopping at document collection
- explains consumer, cultural, demographic, industrial, and capital-allocation shifts in plain language
- proves those explanations with concrete company evidence
- distinguishes strong proof from qualified inference
- and leaves behind a clean checkpoint another worker can extend without confusion

That is the wrap-up target for `annual-report-research` as of `2026-08-11`.
