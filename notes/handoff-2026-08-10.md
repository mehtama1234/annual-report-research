## Theme model being built

This work is not just storing reports. Each company is being mapped into larger recurring themes across 2025 annual reports and late-2025 / 2026 quarterly reporting.

Current recurring lanes include:

- comfort and casualization
- premiumization vs value pressure
- direct-to-consumer relationship control
- fandom, play, and collectible identity
- health, trust, and routine spending
- supply chain, tariffs, and manufacturing concentration
- AI and digital engagement where management makes it operationally real

The next thread should keep treating each company as both:

1. a document collection unit
2. a thematic signal feeding cross-company synthesis
