# Insight Extraction Hub

Date: 2026-08-11
Repo: `annual-report-research`

Use this page as the starting point for insight-producing work in this repo.

## What This System Is For

The repo should not stop at source collection. It should convert filings into a structured view of:

- company strategy
- industry economics
- cross-company themes
- concrete evidence
- societal, cultural, consumer, industrial, and technical shifts
- aha moments
- watchlist metrics
- thesis breakers
- next research targets

The operating rule is:

No broad claim without exact filing evidence. No company name as evidence. No sector label as explanation.

## Workflow For A New Company

1. Confirm AnnualReports taxonomy and archive status.
2. Collect or verify the 2025 annual report.
3. Collect or verify the 2025 annual filing.
4. Collect or verify the latest three reported quarters as of the run date.
5. Create or update the source ledger.
6. Create the company packet using the packet template.
7. Add a company-level strategy read.
8. Add exact annual and quarter evidence.
9. Identify the real business, growth engine, economic lever, constraint, and thesis breaker.
10. Identify the customer, cultural, societal, industrial, technical, or capital-structure signal.
11. Name peer comparisons.
12. Add next filing watchlist.

The company is not complete until the packet explains what the company is doing and what bigger signal it helps prove.

## Workflow For A New Lane

1. Choose 4-8 flagship companies when enough clean candidates exist.
2. Make sure each company has a distinct role in the lane.
3. Complete source ledgers and packets for the flagship companies.
4. Write an industry lane summary.
5. Build a company comparison table.
6. Extract cross-company themes.
7. Write exact evidence tables.
8. Identify aha moments.
9. Add thesis breakers.
10. Add watchlist metrics.
11. Recommend next names.
12. Record commit hash and raw blob status.

The lane is not complete until it explains how the lane works and what repeated patterns the flagship companies prove together.

## Workflow For A New Theme

1. State the plain claim.
2. Gather exact facts from at least three companies.
3. Explain what each fact proves.
4. Build the causal chain.
5. Write the stronger conclusion.
6. State what would weaken or disprove the claim.
7. Add next filing metrics.
8. Add next companies to test.

The theme is not complete if it only names companies.

## Evidence Quality Ladder

Use the strongest available evidence.

1. Exact reported number from annual filing, quarterly filing, release, or packet.
2. Exact management action or repeated management message.
3. Exact operating metric.
4. Exact segment or business-mix disclosure.
5. Exact source status or reporting cadence note.
6. Company name only.
7. Sector label only.

Items 6 and 7 are not evidence. They are pointers to where evidence might be found.

## Minimum Insight Shape

Every serious insight should be writable as:

```text
Because {company exact fact} and {company exact fact}, this suggests {plain conclusion}. The conclusion is supported by {metric/result/management action}, not by the company name. It would weaken if {specific metric or event}.
```

If it cannot be written that way, the insight is not ready.

## Closeout Standard

- source gaps

- company-level insights
- cross-company themes
- aha moments
- watchlist metrics
- thesis breakers
- recommended next names
