# Insight Extraction Templates

Date: 2026-08-11
Repo: `annual-report-research`

Use these templates when creating new company packets, lane summaries, theme memos, and run closeouts.

The point is to make every run produce evidence-backed insight, not just source collection.

Use the templates as one connected evidence chain:

`source coverage -> annual and quarter facts -> company strategy read -> lane economics -> repeated pattern -> plain-English insight -> watch metric -> thesis breaker`

If a later section cannot be traced back to earlier packet evidence, the artifact is still intermediate.

## Quality Gate Checklist

Before calling a company packet complete:

- AnnualReports taxonomy is checked.
- AnnualReports archive status is stated.
- Company IR and SEC were used when AnnualReports lagged.
- Annual report evidence is exact.
- Three-quarter evidence is exact or cadence adaptation is explained.
- Packet has company-level strategy read.
- Packet has operating model in plain English.
- Packet identifies real business, growth engine, economic lever, constraint, and thesis breaker.
- Packet names the customer, cultural, societal, industrial, technical, or capital-structure signal.
- Packet states burden versus cleaner economics.
- Packet has next filing watchlist.
- Source pointers are present.

Before calling a lane batch complete:

- 4-8 flagship companies are completed when the lane supports it.
- Each company adds a distinct role.
- Lane summary explains industry economics.
- Lane summary states which packet inputs the comparison relies on.
- Company comparison table exists.
- Cross-company themes use exact evidence.
- Aha moments are stated.
- Thesis breakers are stated.
- Next recommended names are stated.
- Commit hash is recorded.
- Raw blob status is recorded.

## Minimal Acceptable Insight

The minimum acceptable insight has this shape:

```text
Because {company exact fact} and {company exact fact}, this suggests {plain conclusion}. The conclusion is not proven by the company name; it is supported by {metric/result/management action}. It would weaken if {specific metric or event}.
```

If an insight cannot be written in that form, it is not ready.

## Skeptical Reader Test

- Do the templates force a writer to name which exact packet inputs support a company, lane, or theme conclusion?
- Can another thread pick up one of these templates and continue the evidence chain without guessing what sections are missing?
- Do the templates require who benefits, who carries burden, and what would weaken the claim?
- What kinds of weak writeup would still slip through if the templates were followed carelessly?
