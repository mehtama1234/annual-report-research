# Lane completion audit — 2026-09-14

This audit checks whether the principal integrated lanes contain the evidence
needed for an investment conclusion. A lane is not marked complete merely
because it has a comparison table or a passing structural verifier. “Proven”
means the current archive contains exact filed facts, a causal operating
interpretation, an explicit burden split, a denominator-specific cash test, a
watchlist, a falsifier, and a traceable source route. “Qualified” means the
mechanism is well supported but one or more company-level or current-period
tests remain open. “Partial” means the lane has useful evidence but does not yet
support a portable conclusion across its priority companies.

## 1. Healthcare and aging

### Evidence that is currently established

The synthesis separates six economic layers rather than treating healthcare as
one industry:

- staffed treatment and hospital access;
- caregiver hours and equipment-based aging support;
- diagnostics, devices, instruments, and consumables;
- medicine and practice distribution;
- payer, claims, pharmacy, and care coordination; and
- healthcare property.

The current evidence table includes DaVita, Option Care, Addus, AdaptHealth,
Brookdale, Welltower, Quest, Cencora, HCA, Abbott, Intuitive Surgical, Stryker,
UnitedHealth, and Ventas. It records filed revenue, operating income or
adjusted measures, cash, patient, resident, or equipment scale, medical-cost
payable movement, acquisitions, and property-level cash growth where available.
The causal analysis states which party supplies clinicians, caregivers,
facilities, inventory, reimbursement or resident funding, or required capital.

UnitedHealth and Cigna now supply a paired payer case. UnitedHealth's
FY2023–FY2025 cash bridge separates medical-cost payable timing from
repeatable earnings, while its Q2 2026 checkpoint separates repricing,
medical-cost improvement, Optum margin, cyber remediation, subsidiary capital,
and proposed repurchases. Cigna's FY2025–Q2 2026 chain adds the pharmacy-
services and benefits-administration view, with adjusted income, SG&A,
medical-cost, reserve, capital, debt, and per-share cash tests. Both dossiers
keep the conclusion qualified until later filings show claims paid, reserve
development, retained pharmacy economics, and common cash after required
insurance capital.

### Why the conclusion remains qualified

Repeated medical need does not establish durable owner cash. The open questions
are company-specific:

- whether labor cost and reimbursement preserve the margin after direct care;
- whether adjusted EBITDA converts after equipment, service, receivables, and
  denials;
- whether procedure adoption produces utilization and consumable cash rather
  than only installed systems;
- whether distribution growth remains worth its thin spread, working capital,
  debt, and litigation burden;
- whether payer cash includes timing from medical payables and reserve changes;
  and
- whether property improvement is retained after operator support, renewal
  capital, debt, and resident affordability.

The authoritative synthesis is [Healthcare, aging, and owner cash](../analysis/cross-sector/healthcare-aging-to-owner-cash-forensic-synthesis-2026-09-14.md).
Company-specific evidence routes are linked from its table and from the
[company/cohort map](../analysis/cross-sector/company-and-cohort-writing-map-2026-09-14.md).

## 4. Financial intermediation and property

### Evidence that is currently established

The synthesis keeps deposits, client assets, AUM, collateral, insurance float,
policyholder liabilities, property cash flow, and common-owner cash separate.
It distinguishes banks, closed-loop payments, exchanges and clearing,
custody, public-market managers, alternative-capital platforms, insurance
brokers, insurance carriers, hotel brands, property owners, and levered
mortgage vehicles.

The current evidence table includes JPMorgan, American Express, CME, State
Street, T. Rowe Price, BlackRock, Apollo, Aon, Gallagher, Chubb, Marriott,
Host Hotels, and Annaly. It records deposits, credit costs, rewards liability,
restricted collateral, AUC/A, AUM, fee rates, outflows, FEA/ANI/FRE-type
measures, acquisition cash, RevPAR, repo, leverage, book value, and dividends
where relevant.

### Why the conclusion remains qualified

The models cannot share one denominator. A bank requires credit, liquidity,
technology, conduct, and regulatory-capital tests. An asset manager requires
fee-paying capital, flows, fee rates, compensation, and distribution tests. A
broker requires organic fee growth plus explicit acquisition returns. An
insurer requires claims and required surplus. A property owner requires
normalized NOI or FFO after renewal capital and debt. A mortgage vehicle
requires repo, hedges, spread, prepayments, book value, and dilution.

The current Blackstone and Gallagher upgrades show why headline fee growth is
not enough: partner claims, realization or acquisition timing, earnouts, debt,
integration, amortization, and dilution must be charged against common-owner
returns. The authoritative synthesis is [Financial intermediation to
common-owner cash](../analysis/cross-sector/financial-intermediation-to-owner-cash-forensic-synthesis-2026-09-14.md).

## 5. Energy and infrastructure

### Evidence that is currently established

The power-demand synthesis separates a regulated utility's recovery mechanism
from competitive generation, midstream logistics, equipment and control points,
powered real estate, and field execution. It makes the burden transfer visible:
construction and financing begin before every customer dollar is collected, and
the party closest to labor or physical delivery is not necessarily the party
with the cleanest economics.

The regulated-wires comparison adds a direct Exelon/AEP test. Exelon presents
more established recovery visibility; AEP presents more large-load upside but
requires proof that signed demand becomes credit-supported, energized, paid
load while generation, transmission, debt, and dilution are funded. The work
also keeps NextEra's rate-base and capital plan, ONEOK's integrated logistics,
equipment makers, powered real estate, and contractors in separate economic
categories.

### Why the conclusion remains qualified

Electricity demand is durable, but a demand forecast, signed load, rate base,
backlog, tax-credit proceeds, or adjusted EBITDA is not common cash. The open
tests are whether projects enter service on schedule, receive recovery or
customer funding, earn an acceptable return after financing, and avoid shifting
costs to existing customers or shareholders. The contractor cases also require
direct conversion from backlog and contract assets to billed and collected cash.

The authoritative synthesis is [Power demand to owner cash](../analysis/cross-sector/power-demand-to-owner-cash-forensic-synthesis-2026-09-13.md).
The focused comparison is [Regulated wires versus large-load growth](../analysis/cross-sector/regulated-wires-versus-large-load-forensic-comparison-2026-09-14.md).

### Completion test

The lane has the required economic map, burden split, filing facts, an initial
eight-company filing bridge, valuation questions, watchlist, falsifiers, and
source routes for its current flagship cases. It remains qualified because the
bridge uses different periods and residual definitions, and current
within-model valuation work is not yet uniform across utilities, midstream,
equipment, property, and contractors.

## Cross-lane conclusion

The work now supports a qualified cross-sector finding: the cleanest economics
usually sit with the party controlling a repeated relationship, qualified
workflow, scarce access point, or trusted interface, but the conclusion fails
when the burden carrier’s costs return to the platform through support,
guarantees, partner failure, regulation, or lower retention. Demand measures
become investment evidence only after they are converted into collected cash,
less operating labor, working capital, maintenance or replacement capital,
liabilities, acquisitions, financing costs, and diluted ownership.

This audit is a control document, not a claim that the research system is
finished. It identifies exactly what remains before a final closeout can be
defended.
