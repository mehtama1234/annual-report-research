## Strongest themes already visible

### 1. Interface ownership matters more than legacy sector labels

The strongest current anchors already show that the right organizing question is often:

- who owns the recurring relationship
- who controls the installed base
- who gets paid to reduce complexity
- who remains essential because the physical system cannot be bypassed

`Charter Communications` and `AT&T` now make this theme much stronger. Charter reframes household account ownership around total customer relationships and total connectivity customers; AT&T reframes the telecom side around convergence rates, fiber expansion, and the split between `Advanced Connectivity` and `Legacy`.

`Comcast Corporation` now sharpens the same theme from the cable-and-platform side because it shows a larger hybrid version of the same fight: broadband, wireless, Peacock, premium content, and account ownership have to work together after the old cable bundle stops being the organizing center.
