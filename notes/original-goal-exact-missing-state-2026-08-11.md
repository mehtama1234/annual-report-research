## What is missing in the synthesis, not just in the folders

The remaining editorial gap is not "more themes" in the abstract.

The missing standard is stronger claim-to-evidence wiring.

That means:

- when the archive says aging creates concrete business needs, it should point straight to the exact packets showing occupancy, staffing, device demand, diagnostics volume, reimbursement complexity, and family-care spillover
- when the archive says AI and cloud spending do not reward every layer equally, it should point straight to the exact packets showing carrier burden, interconnect tolls, software control, testing complexity, and storage-orchestration monetization
- when the archive says trust and complexity outsourcing create cleaner economics, it should point straight to the exact packets showing broker workflow economics, fee-like intermediation, spread-risk exposure, occupancy dependence, and liability-channel control
- when the archive says consumers still spend around ritual, identity, leisure, gifting, and hosting, it should point straight to the exact packets showing loyalty behavior, room-rate logic, gaming participation, gifting durability, and food-service demand
