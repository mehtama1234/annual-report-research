# Original Goal Gap Audit

Date baseline: `2026-08-11`

## Purpose

This note checks the current `annual-report-research` state against the actual frontier goal that drove the recent push:

- open genuinely new annual-report-research frontiers rather than sampling one company
- cover whole lanes, not isolated names
- use `2025` annual reports plus the latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10`
- produce source-complete company packets plus thematic interpretation
- leave lane summaries, cross-company themes, strongest repeated signals, exact next names, and commit-hash handoffs
- treat consumer, cultural, societal, industrial, and operating pattern-finding as a primary output rather than as optional commentary

The repo now contains a large amount of material. The gap is not that nothing was done. The gap is that several lanes are still uneven, thin in key sub-lanes, or not closed in the exact disciplined shape the goal required.

## Overall verdict

- `done`: there is enough packet and synthesis work on disk to prove that the archive moved materially into new healthcare, connectivity, capital-structure, and recreation territory
- `thin`: several requested sub-lanes still have only one or two flagship packets and therefore are not really closed
- `missing`: some lane-level closure work is still absent, especially the final "why this conclusion is true" synthesis that ties broad claims back to exact company evidence
- `process failure`: too much recent time went into repo-maintenance, audit, and handoff mechanics instead of finishing frontier interpretation and lane closure
