# Recreation / Participation Kickoff Brief

Date baseline: 2026-08-10
Repo: `annual-report-research-new-lanes`

## Lane

`Recreation, Lifestyle, and Participation Demand`

## Mission

Open a lane that captures lifestyle, gifting, occasion, experiential, and identity-linked spending.
Do not treat this as generic consumer discretionary coverage.
Build a comparison set that shows the difference between routine demand and higher-emotion, higher-participation, higher-occasion demand systems.

## Industries to cover

- Home Furnishing Stores
- Home Furnishings & Fixtures
- Jewelry Stores
- Specialty Retail, Other
- Food Wholesale
- Hospitality

## Batch target

Complete `3` to `4` flagship companies in one coherent comparison set.
Use `2025` annual reports plus the latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10`.

## Strong first-batch shapes

- hospitality plus specialty retail plus home-furnishing exposure plus gifting or occasion-linked retailer
- travel or lodging platform plus furnishing or design-led operator plus jewelry or event-linked demand name plus food-service distribution support layer
- participation-heavy or identity-heavy consumer name plus experiential operator plus home or lifestyle operator plus occasion-spend contrast case

## What the lane should clarify

- where occasion-driven demand differs from routine-use demand
- how identity, gifting, celebration, and participation show up in consumer behavior
- which businesses rely on leisure, event, travel, or milestone spending rather than everyday repetition
- how premiumization, trade-down, and value architecture behave in higher-emotion categories

## Cross-company themes to extract

- gifting versus self-purchase behavior
- occasion-led demand versus everyday demand
- participation, identity, and taste signaling
- hospitality or travel exposure as demand amplifier
- premiumization versus affordability pressure
- inventory, merchandising, and event-cycle sensitivity
