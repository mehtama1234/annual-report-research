# Remaining Insight Execution Board

Date: 2026-08-11
Repo: `annual-report-research`

## Purpose

Use this board to turn the remaining end-to-end goal into the next concrete research batches.

This is not the broad statement of ambition.

This is the execution surface for what should be built next.

## Packet Inputs Used

- the remaining end-to-end insight goal that defines what substantive work is still left
- the insight-driven next-lane queue that names the highest-value open comparison sets
- the active lane board and lane runbook that define how a finished batch should be structured
- the current proof pages and company packets that reveal which roles are still missing and which themes need sharper support

## How To Use This Board

For the next real research run:

1. Pick one batch below.
2. Complete the named flagship packets.
3. Produce the required lane comparison output.
4. Strengthen the linked proof pages with the new packet evidence.
5. Leave the listed watch metrics, contradictions, next names, and handoff fields.

Do not treat a batch as done when the new packets exist.

Treat it as done only when the lane comparison and proof-page layer are stronger because of those packets.

## Batch 1: Healthcare Outside The Hospital And Aging Systems

### Companies

- `UnitedHealth Group`
- `HCA Healthcare`
- `Thermo Fisher`
- `Abbott`
- `Johnson & Johnson`

### Core Question

Where do recurring-care economics become clean, and where do labor, reimbursement, and throughput burden remain heavy?

### What This Batch Must Clarify

- payer versus provider versus tools versus devices
- who owns coordination
- who absorbs labor and reimbursement strain
- whether recurring-care economics are cleaner in workflow, diagnostics, devices, or insurance layers

### Required Outputs

- `4-5` source-complete company packets
- one healthcare burden-carrier comparison
- one payer-versus-provider-versus-tools lane summary
- one updated watchlist for reimbursement, labor, and utilization
- one proof-page strengthening pass for the healthcare comparison pages already in the repo

### Exact Proof Pages To Strengthen

- `analysis/cross-sector/healthcare-outside-hospital-proof-2026-08-10.md`
- `analysis/cross-sector/healthcare-burden-vs-workflow-comparison-2026-08-11.md`
- `analysis/cross-sector/healthcare-testing-distribution-delivery-comparison-2026-08-11.md`
- `analysis/cross-sector/aging-as-operating-reality-comparison-2026-08-11.md`

### Key Metrics To Extract

- medical care ratio
- Optum revenue and cash flow
- admissions and same-facility revenue
- labor cost and payer mix
- instruments, consumables, and order flow
- diagnostics, devices, diabetes care, and gross margin
- pharma/device segment growth and free cash flow

### Done Gate

This batch is done only if the archive can show, with exact evidence, whether the cleanest recurring-care economics sit in care delivery, payment coordination, diagnostics workflow, or installed devices.
