Verified acquisition stop-point:

- Nordstrom announced completion of its acquisition by the Nordstrom family and Liverpool on `2025-05-20`.
- Nordstrom common stock ceased trading before the opening of the NYSE on `2025-05-21`.
- Because of that, there is no normal public-company `2026` latest-three-quarter window to collect.

Scope implication:

- Nordstrom is not part of the live `2025 annual + late-2025 / 2026 trailing-three-quarter` public-comparable set.
- It remains useful as a terminal public-company department-store case and as a structural contrast against Macy's, which stayed public and continued reporting into `2026`.
