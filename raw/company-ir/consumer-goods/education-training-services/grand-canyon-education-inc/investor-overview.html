Corporate Profile

With 30 years of proven expertise, Grand Canyon Education, Inc. (GCE) is uniquely positioned in the education services industry. GCE has developed significant technological solutions, infrastructure and operational processes to provide superior services that support the students, faculty and staff of its partner institutions. GCE’s services include: marketing, strategic enrollment management, counseling services, financial services, technology, technical support, compliance, human resources, classroom operations, curriculum development, faculty recruitment and training, among others.

For more information about GCE, visit gce.com.
