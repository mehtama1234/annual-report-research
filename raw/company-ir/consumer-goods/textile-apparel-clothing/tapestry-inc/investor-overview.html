About us

Our global house of iconic accessories and lifestyle brands unite the magic of Coach and Kate Spade New York. We build emotional connections with consumers, bringing creativity, quality, and value to consumers around the world. With disciplined execution and the consumer at the heart of everything we do, we are translating insights into action at scale, driving durable growth and shareholder value.

Tapestry, Inc. Investor Day

Key insights, strategic updates, and future outlook in one exclusive event.

DISCOVER WHAT DIFFERENTIATES TAPESTRY

Amplify Growth Strategy

At Tapestry's September 2025 Investor Day, the Company's senior leadership team presented its Amplify growth strategies, focused on four key pillars:

01

Build Emotional Connections with Consumers

Driving new customer acquisition, with a focus on Gen Z consumers entering the market to build brand love and lifetime value

02

Fuel Fashion Innovation & Product Excellence

Leading with handbags and leathergoods with targeted lifestyle expansion in footwear

03

Deliver Compelling Experiences to Drive Global Growth

Sustaining growth in North America and accelerating momentum in international markets, prioritizing Greater China and Europe

04

Ignite the Power of Our People

Future-proofing growth by continuing to develop a consumer-obsessed culture that is agile and always looking forward
