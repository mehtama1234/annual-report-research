## Verified facts from the official IR chain

- The reports-and-filings page exposed `PMI's 2025 Annual Report` on 2026-08-10 and described it as the source for the company's `2025` business performance and progress toward a smoke-free future.
- The annual-report PDF link exposed on the page is `https://www.pmi.com/content/dam/pmicom/global/docs/investor_relation/pmi-2025-annual-report.pdf`.
- The Q4 / FY2025 results page dated `2026-02-06` states that full-year `2025` reported diluted EPS was `$7.26` versus `$4.52` in `2024`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$7.54`, net revenues surpassed `$40 billion`, and close to `$17 billion` of revenue came from the smoke-free business.
- The same Q4 / FY2025 page states that the smoke-free business accounted for `41.5%` of total net revenues in full-year `2025`, smoke-free products were available in `106` markets, and estimated adult consumer usage exceeded `43 million`.
- The Q1 2026 results page dated `2026-04-22` states that reported diluted EPS declined `9.3%` to `$1.56`, adjusted diluted EPS grew `16.0%` to `$1.96`, net revenues increased `9.1%` to `$10.1 billion`, and smoke-free products were available in `108` markets.
- The Q2 2026 results page dated `2026-07-22` states that reported diluted EPS declined `7.7%` to `$1.80`, adjusted diluted EPS grew `15.2%` to `$2.20`, net revenues increased `10.4%` to `$11.2 billion`, and smoke-free products were available in `109` markets.
