ESG Overview

Connecting Care Through a Diverse Network

The Company is proud to partner with an extensive and highly diversified network of referral sources, including acute care hospitals, sleep labs, pulmonologists, skilled nursing facilities, and clinics.
AdaptHealth services beneficiaries of Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial insurance payors, reaching approximately 4.8 million patients annually in all 50 states through its network of approximately 668 locations in 48 states.
