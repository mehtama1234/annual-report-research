Financial Highlights

  • Revenue of $1,151.5 million, increased +12.7%, and +6.9% on an organic basis¹
  • Allegion Americas organic revenue¹ increase of +8.9%
  • Allegion International organic revenue¹ decrease of -1.2%
  • Adjusted² operating income margin of 24.2%, an increase of +50 basis points
  • Adjusted² EPS of $2.40, an increase of +17.6%
  • Outlook for 2026 full-year reported revenue growth of +7.5% to +8.5%, compared to FY-2025
  • Outlook for 2026 full-year organic revenue¹ growth of +3.5% to +4.5%, compared to FY-2025
  • Outlook for 2026 full-year reported EPS of $7.95 to $8.10
  • Outlook for 2026 full-year adjusted² EPS of $8.85 to $9.00

1 Organic revenue excludes acquisition/divestitures and currency impacts
2 See press release for non-GAAP reconciliations
