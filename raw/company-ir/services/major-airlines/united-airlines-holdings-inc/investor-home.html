Company Profile

United Airlines (NASDAQ: UAL) is the largest airline in the world, with more than 100,000 employees helping 175 million customers reach over 370 destinations across six continents each year. As the leading carrier across the Atlantic and Pacific, United connects travelers to international cities throughout Asia, Australia, Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, and Africa, serving as the flag carrier of the United States. Through its United Next growth plan, the airline hired thousands of new employees and plans to add hundreds of new aircraft to its mainline fleet of over 1,000 by 2033 - the most among U.S. carriers. United’s industry-leading MileagePlus® loyalty program has over 130 million members who enjoy miles that never expire and no blackout dates for award seats. Customers benefit from an elevated travel experience with award-winning digital tools, Starlink Wi-Fi, seatback entertainment, larger bins, and friendly service. United’s seven domestic hub cities include Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN), Houston (IAH), Los Angeles (LAX), Newark/New York (EWR), San Francisco (SFO), and Washington D.C. (IAD), operating more than 5,000 daily departures.
