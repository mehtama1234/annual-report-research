# Epson 2025 Integrated Report Browser Extract

Date captured: 2026-08-10
Source URL: https://corporate.epson/en/investors/publications/pdf/integrated-report/epson_ir2025_all_e.pdf

Key observations captured from the official PDF through browser access:

- Epson frames customer environmental impact mitigation as part of its growth strategy.
- The report says Epson inkjet printers use `Heat-Free Technology`, which lowers power consumption because ink is ejected mechanically rather than by heating.
- The report argues that replacing laser printers with Epson inkjet printers reduces the environmental impact of printing and can lower broader social emissions.
- Epson says it aims to become `underground resource free` by `2050` by reducing new underground resource consumption, increasing recycled materials, and expanding circular resource use.
- The report says emissions avoided by replacing laser printers with Epson A3 color inkjet printers amounted to `9,800 t-CO2` in FY `2024`.

Useful thematic observations:

- Epson is selling office and print hardware not only on device performance but on energy, consumables, and lifecycle efficiency.
- The environmental claim is economically relevant because it ties hardware replacement cycles to utility cost, regulatory pressure, and procurement preference rather than only to consumer gadget novelty.
