# Epson Q3 2025 Results Browser Extract

Date captured: 2026-08-10
Source URL: https://corporate.epson/en/investors/publications/financial-reports/2025/pdf/results_2025_3q_e.pdf

Key reported metrics:

- Period: `Nine Months ended December 31, 2025`
- Revenue: `¥1,043,825 million`, up `2.0%`
- Business profit: `¥63,797 million`, down `13.7%`
- Profit from operating activities: `¥58,385 million`, down `7.1%`
- Profit before tax: `¥58,740 million`, down `12.6%`
- Profit attributable to owners of the parent company: `¥35,445 million`, down `25.2%`

Segment observations:

- Printing solutions revenue rose `3.2%` to `¥755.3 billion`; segment profit fell `8.0%` to `¥89.0 billion`.
- Visual communications revenue fell `13.6%` to `¥137.4 billion`; segment profit fell `52.7%` to `¥11.4 billion`.
- Manufacturing-related and wearables revenue rose `14.7%` to `¥153.8 billion`; segment profit improved to `¥8.2 billion` from a prior-year loss.

Management and operating observations:

- Epson said office and home inkjet printer hardware revenue was level, with unit sales of ink cartridge printers down and unit sales of high-capacity ink tank printers up in Western Europe and emerging markets.
- Revenue from office shared inkjet printers expanded in Japan, North America, and emerging markets.
- Ink revenue shifted: ink bottles and office shared inkjet ink grew, but cartridge ink declined as the installed base migrated toward high-capacity systems.
- Visual communications was pressured by weaker education demand in Europe and the U.S., weaker business projector demand in China, and softer home-projector demand in China and North America.
- Epson said `Fiery`, acquired in `December 2024`, contributed positively to revenue and segment profit during the period.
