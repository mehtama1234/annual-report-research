# Epson Q1 2026 Results Browser Extract

Date captured: 2026-08-10
Source URL: https://corporate.epson/en/investors/publications/financial-reports/2026/pdf/results_2026_1q_e.pdf

Key reported metrics:

- Period: `Three Months ended June 30, 2026`
- Revenue: `¥367,241 million`, up `14.4%`
- Business profit: `¥22,122 million`, up `12.0%`
- Profit from operating activities: `¥20,601 million`, up `45.7%`
- Profit before tax: `¥20,726 million`, up `60.4%`
- Profit attributable to owners of the parent company: `¥12,984 million`, up `96.4%`

Segment observations:

- Epson reorganized reporting into `Precision Innovation`, `Industrial & Robotics`, `Office & Home Printing`, and `Visual & Lifestyle`.
- `Precision Innovation` revenue rose `23.5%` to `¥49.8 billion`; segment profit rose `62.1%` to `¥15.5 billion`.
- `Industrial & Robotics` revenue rose `17.9%` to `¥82.0 billion`; segment profit rose `44.1%` to `¥5.5 billion`.
- `Office & Home Printing` revenue rose `12.3%` to `¥167.0 billion`; segment profit fell `21.7%` to `¥11.4 billion`.
- `Visual & Lifestyle` revenue rose `7.2%` to `¥67.2 billion`; segment profit fell `30.1%` to `¥4.2 billion`.

Management and operating observations:

- Epson cited AI-related demand and solid U.S. investment support in the macro backdrop.
- High-capacity ink tank printers and office shared inkjet printers supported Office & Home Printing revenue.
- Office & Home Printing profitability was pressured by higher crude-oil-related costs despite revenue growth.
- Visual products benefited from demand related to the `FIFA World Cup`.
- Epson raised its FY `2027` outlook to `¥1,510.0 billion` revenue and `¥105.0 billion` business profit.
