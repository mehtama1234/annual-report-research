## Collection notes

- CACI uses a June fiscal year-end, so the latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10` are fiscal `Q4 2026`, fiscal `Q3 2026`, and fiscal `Q2 2026`, not calendar `Q2/Q1/Q4`.
- The company-hosted annual-reports and quarterly-results pages expose the annual report PDF, quarter release pages, quarter presentations, and company-linked filing PDFs.
- The SEC submissions JSON is the authoritative filing chain for accession numbers and dates. Company IR and SEC HTML were both retained locally because the latest quarter set crosses from a `10-Q` cadence into a new `10-K`.
- No official transcript links were surfaced for the in-scope `FY26` quarter set on the pages collected in this pass.
