Why Invest in Leidos

Leidos, a Fortune 500® digital and mission innovator, delivers solutions at the intersection of national security, health, and critical infrastructure. With a global workforce of approximately 50,000, we are carrying out our NorthStar 2030 strategy focusing on five growth engines where demand is expanding and our expertise sets us apart: defense tech, managed health, digital infrastructure and cyber, energy resilience, and mission software.

Leidos’ NorthStar 2030 Strategy

  • Defense Tech: Enabling faster awareness and response in contested domains
  • Managed Health: Delivering trusted health services for mission readiness
  • Digital Infrastructure & Cyber: Designing secure systems that reduce risk and improve results
  • Energy Resilience: Engineering reliable, safe power for critical infrastructure
  • Mission Software: Turning mission data into clear insight with adaptable software
