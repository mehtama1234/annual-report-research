Powering Homes and Businesses Across America

We operate and maintain the nation’s largest electric transmission system, along with an extensive network of distribution lines to deliver energy to millions of customers across multiple states. Our company is also among the country’s leading electricity producers, with a substantial portfolio of diverse owned and contracted generating capacity.
