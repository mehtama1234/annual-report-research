A Powerful Investment

Our financial strength is driven by consistent growth, strategic innovation and a steadfast commitment to delivering reliable service. With a customer-first mission, we deliver safe, reliable, affordable energy and are committed to meeting future demand. Our five-year, $78 billion capital plan and 69 GW of new load by 2030, backed by signed agreements, positions us for success as we evolve to meet the needs of a changing energy landscape.

Investment Highlights

We are positioned for long-term, reliable growth.

7%-9% Long Term Growth Rate

Pure-play electric utility positioned in high-growth regions of the country

$78 Billion Capital Plan

Transmission, distribution and generation investments

Largest Transmission Network in the U.S.

Provides ~55% of operating earnings from predictable revenues and includes 90% of the nation’s 765kV lines

Best-in-Class Load Growth

An additional 69 GW by 2030, backed by signed customer financial commitments
