Exelon was named to this index for 19​ consecutive years, in recognition of the company's corporate sustainability performance. Inclusion is based upon an in-depth analysis of economic, environmental and social criteria, such as climate change strategies, energy consumption, human resources development, knowledge management, stakeholder relations and corporate governance.
