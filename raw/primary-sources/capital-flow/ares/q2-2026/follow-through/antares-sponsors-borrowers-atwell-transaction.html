How We Partner

Built for Sponsors.
Designed to Support Borrowers.

Antares maintains trusted relationships with private equity sponsors and borrowers across multiple phases of growth, providing senior secured financing backed by disciplined underwriting, direct engagement, and consistent execution across market cycles.

  • Lead Lender, Core FocusLead Lender, Core FocusFocused on senior secured, primarily first-lien financing, with a consistent role as lead lender in sponsor-backed transactions.
  • Built on Relationships Built on Relationships Over 30 years of sponsor partnerships drive consistent deal flow, repeat engagement, and trusted execution.
  • Incumbency Advantage Incumbency Advantage High levels of repeat borrower activity reflect our position within sponsor portfolios and our ability to support companies over time.
  • Scale That DeliversScale That DeliversAbility to commit and hold $1B+, supported by capital markets expertise and flexible structuring.

Execution You Can Rely On

Sponsors and borrowers engage directly with underwriting and portfolio teams from the outset. This enables faster decisions and greater certainty of execution.

We commit meaningful capital and structure flexible solutions across new platform investments, add-ons, refinancings, and evolving capital needs. Our focus is reliability, from initial underwriting through the life of an investment.

“We partner with private equity sponsors with a shared focus on execution and long-term outcomes. Our goal is to deliver resilient financing solutions designed to hold up across cycles and support growth over time.”

Capabilities

Solutions Across the Growth Cycle

Antares provides flexible financing solutions across a range of structures to support sponsors and borrowers. These solutions are designed with a focus on execution, structural integrity, and long-term alignment.

  • Senior Secured Financing SolutionsSenior Secured Financing SolutionsSenior secured, primarily first-lien financing for new platform investments, add-ons, refinancings, and unitranche structures. Antares supports clients through multiple phases of growth with speed, discipline, and consistency.
  • Structuring, Execution, and Ongoing SupportStructuring, Execution, and Ongoing SupportLead arranger and bookrunner capabilities supported by deep capital markets expertise with continuity from underwriting through portfolio management.
  • Selective Co-Investment CapabilitiesSelective Co-Investment CapabilitiesAntares makes selective equity co-investments alongside sponsors where appropriate, supporting execution and long-term alignment.

“For borrowers, reliability matters. We aim to be a consistent financing partner—bringing thoughtful underwriting, direct engagement, and support through multiple phases of growth, across multiple transactions, and often through changes in sponsor ownership.”
