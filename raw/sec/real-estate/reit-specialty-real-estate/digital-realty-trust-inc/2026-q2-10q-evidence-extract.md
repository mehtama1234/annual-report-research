# Digital Realty Q2 2026 evidence extract

Primary filing: [Digital Realty Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1297996/000110465926089296/dlr-20260630x10q.htm)

Primary results release: [Digital Realty Q2 2026 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1297996/000110465926086270/dlr-20260723xex99d1.htm)

Period: six months ended June 30, 2026; dollars in millions unless noted.

## Cash and capital deployment

- Net cash provided by operating activities: `$1,595.227M`, versus `$1,040.322M` in the prior-year period.
- Cash-basis development projects: `$1,477.072M`.
- Enhancement and improvements: `$13.673M`.
- Recurring capital expenditures: `$136.339M`.
- Total cash-basis capital expenditures excluding indirect costs: `$1,627.084M`.
- Indirect costs capitalized: `$157.1M`, including `$72.7M` of capitalized interest.
- The filing describes approximately `$2.170B` of 2026 acquisitions/asset-acquisition spend versus `$218M` in 2025 in the investing-activity comparison.
- The Astra Enterprise Park land acquisition included `$377.6M` of cash and `$104.0M` of operating-partnership units.
- Common distributions were approximately `$877.035M` at the operating-partnership level; preferred distributions were approximately `$20.362M`.

## Funding and dilution

- Year-to-date ATM issuance: approximately `$2.5B` net proceeds from approximately 13.5M common shares at an average `$184.94` per share.
- Q2 ATM issuance: approximately `$1.2B` net proceeds from 6.2M shares at a weighted-average `$191.63` per share.
- Consolidated debt: `$18.768B`; 93.6% fixed or swapped and 6.4% unhedged variable-rate debt.
- Pro-rata secured debt of unconsolidated entities: approximately `$2.0B`.
- Net debt to adjusted EBITDA: `4.7x`; debt plus preferred to enterprise value: `22.3%`; fixed-charge coverage: `5.2x`.

## Operating and non-GAAP surface

- Six-month diluted AFFO per share: `$4.39`, versus `$3.45` in the prior-year period.
- Six-month FFO available to common stockholders and unitholders: `$1,682.303M`; diluted FFO per share and unit: `$4.73`.
- Q2 bookings were `$307M` of annualized GAAP base rent at 100% share and `$208M` at Digital Realty’s share.
- At the end of Q2, backlog was approximately `$1.9B` at 100% share and `$1.4B` at Digital Realty’s share.
- July hyperscale leases added `$410M` of annualized GAAP base rent at 100% share, or `$205M` at Digital Realty’s share; these are signed-rent indicators, not current cash.

## JV and private-capital perimeter

- In May, two development projects were contributed to a fund for gross proceeds of approximately `$447M` against aggregate carrying value of approximately `$439M`.
- The company uses unconsolidated entities and private-capital vehicles; the parent-level bridge therefore requires partner contributions, ownership, project debt, guarantees, distributions, promote economics, and remaining capital obligations.

## Filing interpretation

The current period shows genuine operating improvement, but it also shows a capital-intensive growth machine. OCF exceeded recurring capex by roughly `$1.459B` while development capex consumed roughly `$1.477B` before indirect costs, acquisitions, and other investment activity. The next promotion gate is a project-level stabilized-return bridge after equity issuance, debt, preferred claims, leasing costs, partner economics, and commencement timing.
