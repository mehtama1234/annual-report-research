# Equinix Q2 2026 evidence extract

Primary filing: [Equinix Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1101239/000110123926000147/eqix-20260630.htm)

Period: six months ended June 30, 2026; dollars in millions unless noted.

## Cash flow and commitments

- Net cash provided by operating activities: `$1,784M` versus `$1,753M` in the prior-year period.
- Purchases of other PP&E: `$2,834M` versus `$1,739M`.
- Real-estate acquisitions: `$224M` versus `$99M`.
- Business acquisitions: `$0M` versus `$182M`.
- Dividends paid: `$1,029M`.
- Senior-note proceeds: `$2,419M`; senior-note repayment: `$700M`; other debt repayment: `$682M`.
- Cash and cash equivalents at June 30: `$979M`; short-term investments: `$1,245M`.
- Senior-note principal: approximately `$19,873M`; estimated interest over instrument lives and facility fees: approximately `$4,500M`.
- Total lease payments in material commitments: approximately `$5,000M`.
- Unaccrued capital-expenditure commitments: approximately `$6,100M`, primarily IBX equipment and construction/opening work, with the majority payable within 12 months.
- Other non-capital purchase commitments: approximately `$2,100M`, including power purchases and open orders.

## Working-capital and earnings perimeter

- Accounts receivable increased from `$1,001M` at January 1 to `$1,256M` at June 30; the cash-flow statement shows a `$258M` operating cash use from receivables.
- Deferred revenue increased from `$303M` combined current/non-current to `$374M`; remaining performance obligations were approximately `$15.0B`.
- Stock-based compensation was `$273M` in the first half, versus `$240M` in the prior-year period.
- Six-month AFFO attributable to common stockholders was `$2,233M` versus `$1,919M`; diluted AFFO per share was `$22.57` versus `$19.59`.
- Equinix defines AFFO to include recurring capital expenditures, which it describes as expenditures that extend useful lives or support current revenues. The definition also adds back SBC, non-real-estate depreciation, amortization, impairment, transaction costs, straight-line items, and other adjustments.

## JV/VIE perimeter

- Equity-method investments were `$751M`, including `$735M` in xScale joint ventures.
- Most listed xScale ventures have 20% Equinix ownership; several are identified as VIEs.
- VIE-related commitments included a `$392M` loan commitment, `$118M` of future equity contribution commitments, and `$48M` of maximum future payments under debt guarantees in the disclosed table.

## Filing interpretation

The filing supports a current-period cash-perimeter test, not a promotion. OCF improved only `$31M` year over year while PP&E purchases increased by `$1.095B`. The company is funding a growth build that can be economically attractive, but the common-owner bridge must separately model recurring capex, expansion capex, real-estate acquisitions, debt, leases, power commitments, JV/VIE claims, lease-up, and dilution.
