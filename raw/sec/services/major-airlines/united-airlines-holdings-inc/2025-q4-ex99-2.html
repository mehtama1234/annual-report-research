Profit Sharing: Based on profit sharing plans in current labor agreements, the Company expects to accrue between $45 million and $75 million in the first quarter of 2026. The accrual is based on the sum of the methodology below.

•Approximately 3.8% of total adjusted pre-tax earnings up to a 6.9% adjusted pre-tax margin4, plus approximately 3.6% of total adjusted pre-tax earnings above a 6.9% adjusted pre-tax margin4.
•Approximately 5.9% of total adjusted pre-tax earnings up to $2.5 billion adjusted pre-tax earnings, plus approximately 11.7% of total adjusted pre-tax earnings above $2.5 billion adjusted pre-tax earnings.
•Approximately 3.3% for all adjusted pre-tax earnings above the prior year’s full-year adjusted pre-tax earnings.

Profit sharing adjusted pre-tax earnings is a non-GAAP financial measure that is calculated as GAAP pre-tax income (loss), excluding operating and non-operating special charges and unrealized (gains) losses on investments, net, profit sharing expense and share-based compensation program expense. The Company estimates that share-based compensation expense for the purposes of the profit sharing adjusted pre-tax earnings calculation will be approximately $33 million for the first quarter of 2026.
