# AEP Q2 2026 evidence extract

Primary filing: [AEP Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4904/000000490426000059/aep-20260630.htm)

Primary results release: [AEP Q2 2026 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4904/000000490426000055/a2q20268kpressreleaseex991.htm)

Period: six months ended June 30, 2026; dollars in millions unless noted.

## Operating cash and capital

- Net cash from operating activities: `$3,421M`, versus `$2,671M` in H1 2025.
- Construction expenditures: `$5,606M`, versus `$4,020M`.
- Acquisitions of generation facilities: `$1,315M`.
- Acquisitions of nuclear fuel: `$51M`.
- Contribution-in-aid-of-construction advances: `$425M`.
- Net cash used in investing activities: `$6,602M`.
- Mechanical OCF less construction expenditures: approximately `$(2,185M)`.
- Mechanical OCF less construction, generation-facility acquisitions, and nuclear fuel: approximately `$(3,551M)` before construction advances and other investing items.

## Funding and balance sheet

- Total debt at June 30: `$52,836M`; debt-to-total-capital: `61.4%`.
- Net available liquidity: approximately `$7.25B`.
- AEP reported `$273M` of increased common-stock issuance in financing cash flow, and the filing describes a `$3.5B` ATM capacity plus forward equity agreements.
- 2026 budgeted capital expenditures: approximately `$12.8B`; 2026–2030 plan: `$77.937B`.

## Load and customer claims

- AEP added approximately 6 GW of signed load agreements in Q2, bringing contracted load growth through 2030 to approximately 69 GW.
- The signed-load set includes hyperscalers, data centers, and industrial customers.
- AEP identified up to `$16B` of expected cost offsets for residential customers supported by fully executed take-or-pay electric-service agreements.
- Five states had approved large-load tariffs, with three additional state filings pending.
- DOE loans and grants were described as providing approximately `$1.4B` of estimated customer benefits over their lives; these benefits are not the same as AEP common-owner cash.

## QoE and financial-shenanigans boundary

The filing states that operating earnings are non-GAAP and may exclude items not considered indicative of ongoing performance. The current research must keep operating EPS, signed load, tariff approval, take-or-pay agreements, construction work in progress, regulatory assets/liabilities, customer deposits, billed revenue, collected cash, debt, and equity issuance separate.

The current period improves the load-growth and funding map but does not prove that 69 GW becomes billed and collected customer revenue, that `$78B` of planned capital earns an allowed return, or that affordability offsets accrue to common owners after debt, taxes, regulatory lag, and dilution.
