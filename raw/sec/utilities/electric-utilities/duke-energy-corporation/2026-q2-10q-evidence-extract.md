# Duke Energy Q2 2026 evidence extract

Primary filing: [Duke Energy Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20290/000132616026000040/duk-20260630.htm)

Period: six months ended June 30, 2026; dollars in millions unless noted.

## Cash and capital

- Net cash provided by operating activities: `$4,272M`, versus `$5,040M` in H1 2025.
- Capital expenditures: `$8,240M`, versus `$6,428M`.
- Mechanical OCF less capex: approximately `$(3,968M)`.
- Proceeds from sale of Piedmont's Tennessee business: `$2,501M`.
- Long-term debt issued: `$6,464M`; long-term debt redeemed: `$4,831M`.
- Contributions from noncontrolling interests: `$2,827M`.
- Dividends paid: `$1,693M`.
- Accrued capital expenditures: `$2,574M` at the consolidated level.

## Financing and project claims

- Duke’s Q2 debt activity included `$4.675B` of new issuances across Duke and subsidiaries; the filing separately identifies a `$1.5B` parent issuance and subsidiary first-mortgage-bond issuances.
- The filing identifies current maturities, convertible-note terms, VIE-related debt, preferred dividends, NCI, and regulatory assets across the legal-entity perimeter.
- Capital expenditures were concentrated in Electric Utilities and Infrastructure; the segment table reports `$8.024B` for EU&I and `$216M` for other segments.

## Anderson County project boundary

- The planned new combined-cycle project is co-owned with Central Electric Power Cooperative and North Carolina Electric Membership Corporation.
- Duke Energy Carolinas is expected to own approximately 1,170 MW, NCEMC 100 MW, and CEPC 95 MW, subject to final contractual terms.
- Approval, construction, ownership, customer service, rate-base treatment, and customer recovery remain separate proof objects.

## Filing interpretation

Duke’s current cash surface is materially investment-heavy: OCF was below capex before financing, asset-sale proceeds, NCI contributions, and debt issuance. The Piedmont sale and NCI funding improve liquidity but are not recurring utility operating cash. The Anderson project remains an approval/ownership/recovery case, not yet a paid-capex and collected-customer-cash proof.
