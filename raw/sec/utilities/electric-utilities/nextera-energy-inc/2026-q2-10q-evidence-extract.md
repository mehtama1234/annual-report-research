## Regulatory and collection boundary

Clause recovery, storm-related costs, franchise fees, AFUDC, regulatory assets/liabilities, customer bills, deposits, and true-ups are separate proof objects. Aggregate FPL operating cash does not prove that a named category such as Distribution Inspection generated the claimed billed and collected dollars.

## Filing interpretation

FPL shows a regulated cash-conversion surface stronger than AEP or Duke on this period’s mechanical bridge, but it still spends heavily to serve load and relies on debt, regulatory recovery, and parent-company funding relationships. The next upgrade requires a named category or project’s billing determinant, receipts, cost allocation, and earned return.
