Annual Report Takeaways

  • Alcoa adds the aluminum layer that the current Basic Materials starter set was still missing. This matters because aluminum sits between mining and fabrication: it links bauxite and alumina to smelting, casting, power, transport, packaging, construction, and industrial applications.
  • AnnualReports now has a saved local company-page artifact for this branch, and unlike several other names in the batch it already showed Alcoa's `2025 Annual Report` and `2025 Form 10-K` as of `Monday, August 10, 2026`. The authoritative annual chain still remains the official annual report PDF and SEC filing set.
  • The `2025` annual filing shows a vertically integrated upstream metals platform with direct and indirect ownership of `25` operating locations across `8` countries on `5` continents and approximately `14,900` employees in `16` countries as of year-end.
  • Alcoa is simpler than several other sector names. It reports through two businesses, `Alumina` and `Aluminum`, which makes it a clean operating read on price, volume, energy, and restart execution rather than a highly diversified materials story.
  • The annual base year was strong. Full-year `2025` revenue was `$12.8 billion`, net income attributable to Alcoa was `$1.2 billion`, adjusted EBITDA excluding special items was `$2.0 billion`, and cash from operations was `$1.2 billion`.
  • The strategic read is not only “aluminum prices were good.” Alcoa also used `2025` to reshape the portfolio and balance sheet by selling its `25.1%` Ma'aden stake, reducing debt to `$2.4 billion`, announcing the permanent closure of the Kwinana refinery, and pushing the San Ciprian restart through a joint-venture structure.

Latest Quarter Chain

  • Results: `Q2 2026` revenue reached a quarterly record of `$4.0 billion`, net income attributable to Alcoa was `$407 million`, adjusted net income attributable to Alcoa was `$562 million`, adjusted EBITDA excluding special items was `$901 million`, and free cash flow was `$422 million`.
  • Operating read: alumina production declined `6%` sequentially to `2.2 million` metric tons because of Pinjarra instability and gas-supply disruption associated with Cyclone Narelle, while aluminum production rose `5%` to `636,000` metric tons due to the completed San Ciprian restart and other capacity restarts.
  • Pricing and shipments: alumina third-party shipments were flat sequentially at `1.6 million` metric tons, while aluminum shipments increased `18%` sequentially. The quarter also benefited from higher average aluminum prices and stronger value-add sales.
  • Strategic moves: management highlighted the AliGroup transaction, final investment approval for a gallium production plant in Australia, and a `$65 million` investment at the Mosjoen smelter in Norway.
  • Structural themes: `Q2 2026` shows Alcoa at the intersection of favorable aluminum pricing, restart execution, tariff pass-through, and geopolitical energy costs. It is one of the cleaner examples in the archive of how upstream metals earnings can surge even while operating conditions remain fragile.
  • Results: `Q1 2026` sales were `$3.193 billion`, net income attributable to Alcoa was `$425 million`, diluted EPS was `$1.60`, and total segment adjusted EBITDA was `$654 million`.

Signal Map

  • Sector: Alcoa is strong evidence that Basic Materials should be read as a chain of physical conversion systems, not only extraction businesses. Aluminum sits between mining and manufacturing and gives the sector a clearer metals-processing layer than copper or steel alone.
  • Consumer: the business is upstream, but the demand map matters. Packaging, transportation, construction, wire, and industrial applications sit downstream from the quarterlies, making Alcoa a useful bridge between industrial demand and end-use consumption.
  • Cultural: this is another reminder that the physical economy still depends on difficult, power-intensive, unionized, capital-heavy industrial systems. Aluminum is not a side story to the modern economy.
  • Regulatory: tariffs, energy policy, environmental remediation, mine and refinery approvals, and geopolitical supply disruption are all direct earnings variables here.
  • Labor: collective bargaining and plant-level labor agreements remain material. `Q2 2026` specifically highlighted completed new collective bargaining agreements in Australia, the U.S., and Canada.
  • Technology: Alcoa is not a software beneficiary, but it matters to electrification, transport, industrial products, packaging, and potentially strategic materials such as gallium. The digital economy still rides on mined and processed inputs.
