  • The `2025` annual report frames American Water as the largest regulated water and wastewater utility in the United States, serving approximately `14 million` people.
  • The annual materials make the growth model explicit: the company invested `$3.2B` in capital in `2025`, added nearly `40,000` customer connections through acquisitions and organic growth, and plans to invest between `$46B` and `$48B` over the next decade in infrastructure renewal, water quality, resiliency, technology, and acquisitions.

Instead of arguing that hyperscaler load offsets household bills, it emphasizes average residential water bills staying well under `1%` of median household income while still pursuing regulated capital recovery and acquisition-led system expansion.
