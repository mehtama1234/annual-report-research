Annual Report Takeaways

  • Apollo is the clearest counterpoint to Blackstone in the current alternative-manager set. The model is not just management fees plus realizations; it is management fees plus spread earnings through Athene, backed by origination, retirement services, and insurance-scale balance-sheet sourcing.
  • Full-year `2025` closed with record origination activity of more than `$300B`, inflows of more than `$225B`, fee-related earnings of about `$2.5B`, spread-related earnings of about `$3.4B`, adjusted net income of about `$5.2B`, total AUM of about `$938B`, and fee-generating AUM of about `$709B`.
  • The key structural variable here is not only private-markets fundraising. It is Apollo’s ability to manufacture and distribute investment-grade and private credit at scale into insurance and retirement channels.
  • This makes Apollo a bridge between alternative asset management and balance-sheet-intensive financial intermediation. The business reads partly like an asset manager and partly like a spread-and-origination platform.
