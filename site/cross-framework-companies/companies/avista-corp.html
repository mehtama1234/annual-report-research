  • Avista also broadens the utility set geographically and structurally. It serves roughly `429,000` electric customers and `386,000` natural-gas customers across Washington, Idaho, and Oregon, while AEL&P adds a small Alaska electric business in Juneau.
  • The year-end release makes the strategic constraint clear: Avista is still a growth utility, but part of the `2026` story is offset by a large industrial customer choosing to return to market procurement sooner than expected, reducing `2026` utility guidance by about `$0.12` per diluted share.
