Annual Report Takeaways

  • Birkenstock is one of the clearest archive additions for the foot-health, premium-utility, and practical-wellness lane. The annual materials do not read like generic fashion retail. They read like a brand turning comfort, biomechanical function, and identity into a high-margin global system.
  • AnnualReports.com still lagged here. On Monday, August 10, 2026, the live page still showed only a `2024 Annual Report`, so the current annual package had to come from Birkenstock's official IR and SEC chain.
  • Fiscal `2025` revenue reached about `EUR 2.1B`, up `16%` reported and `18%` in constant currency, supported by `12%` unit growth and `5%` constant-currency average selling price growth.
  • The closed-toe story matters. Management said closed-toe shoes outpaced sandals and increased to `38%` of total business, up about `500` basis points, which makes this more than a seasonal sandal brand.
  • The strategic read is that Birkenstock sits at the intersection of wellness, comfort, minimalism, durability, and premium casualization. It is one of the archive's clearest proofs that body-awareness and practical utility can become a broad lifestyle purchase rather than a niche orthopedic one.

Latest Quarter Chain

  • Results: `Q2 FY2026` revenue was `EUR 618M`, up `8%` reported and `14%` in constant currency; gross profit margin was `53.9%`; adjusted EBITDA was `EUR 198M`; and diluted EPS was `EUR 0.45`.
  • Operating posture: Birkenstock explicitly framed the quarter around resilience despite war, tariffs, and inflation. The Middle East conflict reduced `EMEA` revenue by about `EUR 6M`, while `APAC` grew `22%` reported and `30%` in constant currency.
  • Structural themes: this quarter matters because it shows consumers still paying for comfort-and-health-coded premium footwear even when the company is working through external shocks and gross-margin pressure.
  • Results: `Q1 FY2026` revenue was `EUR 402M`, up `11.1%` reported and `17.8%` in constant currency; gross profit margin was `55.7%`; adjusted EBITDA was `EUR 106M`; and diluted EPS was `EUR 0.27`.
  • Operating posture: management highlighted strong holiday demand for clogs, shearling executions, and other closed-toe shoes and boots. `B2B` revenue grew `18%` reported and `24%` in constant currency.
  • Structural themes: this is the quarter that makes the wellness-and-utility read harder to dismiss. The growth was not only sandals. It came from colder-weather and more everyday-use silhouettes, which broadens the consumer behavior behind the brand.
