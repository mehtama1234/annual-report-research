Annual Report Takeaways

  • Chewy is a strong Services addition because it gives the archive a digital-first pet-care and replenishment relationship rather than another store-led value format, restaurant routine, or travel loyalty system.
  • AnnualReports.com was only partially useful here. On Monday, August 10, 2026, it exposed the current `2025 Annual Report and Form 10K`, but it left sector and industry blank, so the archive had to fall back to SEC SIC `Retail-Catalog & Mail-Order Houses` inside `Trade & Services`.
  • The `2025` annual and year-end materials show that the business is larger and more profitable than a simple e-commerce label implies. Full-year net sales reached `$12.60B`, adjusted EBITDA reached `$719.2M`, and management exited the year with record free cash flow of `$562M` and `21.3M` active customers.
  • The strategically important point is that Chewy is widening beyond one-time online pet-product purchases. The company describes websites and mobile applications that let pet parents manage merchandise, health, wellness, and prescription needs, while the operating model leans heavily on repeat behavior, Autoship, pharmacy, and service attachment.
  • The filings and releases make that relationship layer explicit. In the current window, Chewy repeatedly frames itself as a trusted destination for pet parents, says it works with about `4,000` brands, and says its digital surfaces offer about `190,000` products and service offerings.

Latest Quarter Chain

  • Results: Q1 `2026` net sales increased `7.7%` to `$3.36B`, gross margin increased `50` basis points to `30.1%`, net income was `$94.8M`, diluted EPS was `$0.23`, adjusted EBITDA was `$253.1M`, and adjusted EBITDA margin reached `7.5%`.
  • Operating posture: management said the quarter included nearly `200,000` net customer additions and record quarterly profitability despite a more dynamic consumer backdrop.
  • Structural themes: this quarter is important because it shows Chewy behaving like a recurring pet-care interface. Growth and margin expansion are being supported by customer retention, replenishment behavior, and service depth rather than by a one-time promotional retail burst.
  • Results: fiscal `2025` net sales reached `$12.60B`, up `6.2%` year over year or `8.3%` on a normalized basis; adjusted EBITDA was `$719.2M`; adjusted EBITDA margin reached `5.7%`; and diluted EPS for the year was `$0.52`. Q4 `2025` net sales were `$3.26B`, net income was `$39.2M`, diluted EPS was `$0.09`, and adjusted EBITDA margin reached `5.0%`.
  • Operating posture: management highlighted record free cash flow of `$562M` and `21.3M` active customers at year-end, which makes the year-end packet the clearest proof that Chewy's customer base is large, sticky, and increasingly profitable.
  • Structural themes: this is the anchor quarter for the packet because it closes the year with the strongest evidence that Chewy is building a repeat pet-parent operating surface around Autoship, pharmacy, prescriptions, and digital account management rather than only moving pet food online.

Signal Map

  • Sector: Chewy strengthens `Services` with a digital pet-care relationship system that sits between retail, healthcare adjacency, and routine household replenishment.
  • Consumer: this is one of the archive's better direct reads on recurring spending tied to pets, wellness, convenience, and emotionally durable household priorities rather than broad discretionary trade-up.
  • Cultural: pet spending here looks less like occasional indulgence and more like a managed family routine. The language around pet parents, prescriptions, care, and convenience makes the relationship unusually sticky.
  • Technology and interface: Chewy is a useful interface case because websites, mobile apps, account management, prescriptions, and Autoship all matter directly to the economics. Digital is not just checkout.
  • Health and service adjacency: the packet is also useful because the company repeatedly ties the relationship to pet health, wellness, and prescriptions, which makes it a bridge case between classic retail and consumer-health behavior.
  • Competitive positioning: Chewy is especially useful next to `Ulta`, `Kroger`, `Walmart`, and `Dollar General`. Those names each show routine behavior, but Chewy is the cleaner digital-first case where replenishment, care management, and service layers are native to the platform.
