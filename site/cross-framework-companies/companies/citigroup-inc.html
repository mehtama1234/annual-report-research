Annual Report Takeaways

  • The `2025` annual report shows revenue of `$85.2 billion`, net income of `$14.3 billion`, EPS of `$6.99`, RoTCE of `7.7%`, and CET1 of `13.2%`, with adjusted revenue up `7%` and adjusted RoTCE up to `8.8%` excluding notable items.
  • Citi argues that `2025` was a turning point: record revenue in each of the five businesses, positive operating leverage for a second straight year, and a shift from being defined by remediation to being defined by what the bank is building.
  • The center of gravity is the global institutional network. Services revenue grew `8%` in `2025`, Markets revenue grew `11%`, Banking revenue grew `32%`, Wealth revenue grew `14%`, and U.S. Personal Banking revenue grew `5%`.
  • Management repeatedly ties the thesis to shifting trade corridors, geopolitically shaped capital flows, digitized finance, and the value of being able to move liquidity, collateral, and payments across borders.
  • Citi is unusually direct about AI and digital infrastructure as core operating tools: AI-assisted coding, service agents, onboarding, underwriting, Wealth workflows, and tokenized / always-on payments infrastructure all appear in the annual narrative as live execution levers rather than abstract future bets.

Latest Quarter Chain

  • Results: revenue of `$24.8 billion`, net income of `$5.8 billion`, EPS of `$3.15`, and RoTCE of `13.0%`.
  • Management message: Citi called it the best quarterly revenue in a decade, with double-digit revenue growth for the firm and four of five businesses.
  • Structural themes: Services posted its highest-ever quarterly revenue; Markets topped `$7 billion` again; Banking revenue increased `34%`; and Wealth extended its growth streak while drawing more assets from existing clients.
  • Capital and operating tone: Citi bought back `$4.0 billion` under its new repurchase plan, plans to raise the dividend `12%`, and said transformation work passing internal-audit validation is starting to translate into lower expense pressure and broader AI deployment.
  • Results: revenue of `$24.6 billion`, net income of `$5.8 billion`, EPS of `$3.06`, and RoTCE of `13.1%`.
  • Management message: the year opened with broad-based strength, especially in Services, Markets, Banking, and Wealth, with the diversified model framed as a source of trust during uncertainty.

Signal Map

  • Sector: Citi strengthens the case that scale-bank resilience is increasingly about network breadth, capital-markets connectivity, and the ability to support cross-border operating flows rather than only domestic spread income.
  • Consumer: Citi’s consumer commentary is narrower than BAC’s but still constructive, with prime-heavy cards, resilient spend, and better-than-expected credit performance.
  • Regulatory: unlike BAC, Citi keeps remediation, capital-rule advocacy, and internal-control modernization in the foreground of the operating story.
  • Capital allocation: Citi pairs heavy capital return with explicit claims that simplification and lower stress losses are improving flexibility.
  • Technology: digital payments, tokenized cash rails, AI deployment, and workflow automation are central to the investment case and to management’s explanation of future operating leverage.
