Annual Report Takeaways

  • Invesco is the most useful ETF-and-indexing-heavy public asset manager in the archive so far. It gives the financial set a large-scale read on how indexed products, QQQ concentration, China JV exposure, fixed income, and private markets combine inside one platform.
  • Fiscal `2025` ended with AUM of `$2.170T`, average AUM of `$2.000T`, and total net flows of `$104.4B`, which is a materially stronger flow picture than the current T. Rowe or Franklin annual baselines.
  • Operating revenues reached `$6.377B`, adjusted operating margin reached `33.4%`, and adjusted diluted EPS reached `$2.03`.
  • The mix matters. ETFs and Index accounted for `$630.2B` of AUM, QQQ accounted for `$407.2B`, China JV accounted for `$132.5B`, and private markets accounted for `$130.7B`.
  • The annual materials also show a business adapting structurally: QQQ converted from a unit investment trust to an open-end ETF on `December 20, 2025`, and management kept leaning into private markets, China, indexed products, and wealth-distribution channels.

Latest Quarter Chain

  • Results: operating revenues of `$1.826B`, adjusted operating margin of `37.5%`, adjusted net income attributable to Invesco of `$322.3M`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$0.71`.
  • Flow and AUM signal: `$45.1B` of record net long-term inflows, led by ETFs and Index, QQQ, China JV, and private markets.
  • Strategic read: this is the strongest quarter so far for showing how Invesco can monetize both classic indexed exposures and a broader multi-channel product mix.
  • Results: operating revenues of `$1.745B`, adjusted operating margin of `34.5%`, adjusted net income attributable to Invesco of `$260.8M`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$0.57`.
  • Flow and AUM signal: `$21.8B` of net long-term inflows, with ETFs and Index, China JV, fundamental fixed income, and multi-asset overcoming outflows from QQQ and fundamental equities.
  • Structural read: Invesco’s flow engine is broader than a single product, but the QQQ concentration still matters enough to swing the quarter-level narrative.

Signal Map

  • Sector: Invesco adds the clearest read yet on ETF-and-index franchise economics inside the broader asset-management bucket.
  • Investor behavior: the platform shows that asset flows are not moving in one direction. Indexed scale, China exposure, fixed income, and private markets can all win simultaneously while active-equity books still leak.
  • Competition: this is the strongest local evidence set for the idea that asset managers with major ETF and indexed franchises can defend or improve economics even when other parts of active management stay under pressure.
  • Culture and business model: investor demand is becoming more wrapper-aware, benchmark-aware, and channel-aware. Invesco is a direct expression of that shift.
  • Watch item: QQQ concentration is both a strength and a dependency. It helps scale and distribution economics, but it also concentrates flow and revenue sensitivity.
