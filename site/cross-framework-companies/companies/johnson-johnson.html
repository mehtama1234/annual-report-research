Annual Report Takeaways

  • Johnson & Johnson is the most useful diversified healthcare platform in the archive so far. It adds a business that is not only a drug portfolio or a payer system, but a combined Innovative Medicine plus MedTech operating model.
  • Fiscal `2025` ended with reported sales of `$94.2B`, operational sales growth of `5.3%`, adjusted operational sales growth of `4.2%`, diluted EPS of `$11.03`, and adjusted diluted EPS of `$10.79`.
  • The segment split matters. Innovative Medicine sales reached `$60.401B`, and MedTech sales reached `$33.792B`.
  • The annual report also makes the portfolio point more explicit: full-year adjusted net earnings were `$26.2B`, and growth excluding STELARA was `11.5%`, which suggests the underlying business looked better than the headline patent-loss pressure alone.
  • This gives healthcare a much better bridge between science-led drug economics, device and procedure demand, pricing and reimbursement pressure, and broad operating execution.

  • Strategic read: this is a broad healthcare platform still finding growth across both core segments while raising guidance in a policy-heavy environment.
