Annual Report Takeaways

  • Kenvue is the right next packet because it adds the consumer-health and everyday-care layer that sits between household staples, personal care, oral care, and wellness-oriented routine use.
  • AnnualReports makes the packet analytically useful before the financials even start. As of `2026-08-10`, the site still only exposed a `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K` package and classified Kenvue as `Technology / Biotechnology`, even though the business is plainly a global consumer-health platform.
  • The official investor-relations overview gives the scale of the business clearly enough to make the operating reality obvious: `2025` net sales were `$15.1bn` across `3` segments, about `50%` of net sales were outside North America, the company operated in more than `165` countries, and it served about `1.2 billion` people globally.
  • The FY`2025` and Q4 `2025` results chain shows a company that ended a weak year on a better quarter. Full-year net sales decreased `2.1%`, organic sales declined `2.2%`, diluted EPS was `$0.76`, adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.08`, net cash flows from operating activities were `$2.2bn`, and free cash flow was `$1.7bn`. But Q4 itself returned to net sales growth of `3.2%` and organic growth of `1.2%`.
  • The management frame at year-end was not simple category momentum. It was execution improvement plus a transaction backdrop. Kenvue said the quarter reflected disciplined execution and easier comparisons while it worked toward completing its value-creating combination with Kimberly-Clark.
  • This makes Kenvue valuable to the broader archive because it shows that trusted daily-use health and care categories can still be resilient, but the earnings story is being shaped by seasonality, retailer inventory behavior, category incidence rates, innovation execution, and corporate transformation rather than by effortless staples-like compounding.
