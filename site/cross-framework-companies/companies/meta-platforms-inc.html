Annual Report Takeaways

  • Meta is a high-value archive addition because it gives the repo a dominant consumer attention and advertising platform with a daily-use behavioral loop, not just a subscription or enterprise-software model.
  • The AnnualReports lag is itself important. As of `2026-08-09`, AnnualReports.com still showed Meta's `2024 Annual Report and Form 10K`, while Meta's own annual-report artifact and SEC filing chain already supported the full `2025` year-end package.
  • The `2025` annual package shows scale plus reinvestment pressure at the same time. Full-year revenue was `$200.966B`, income from operations was `$83.276B`, net income was `$60.458B`, diluted EPS was `$23.49`, capital expenditures were `$72.22B`, and DAP averaged `3.58B` in December `2025`.
  • The operating logic is not just "sell ads to a large audience." The annual filing and quarter trail show a platform where recommendation quality, AI-assisted monetization, messaging, creator surfaces, and future hardware ambitions all sit on top of a huge installed behavior base.
  • That makes Meta especially useful to the archive because it is both cultural and infrastructural. It captures everyday attention, business demand, and AI investment concentration inside one operator.
