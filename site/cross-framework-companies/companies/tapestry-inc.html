  • Operating posture: Q2 set record quarterly revenue, operating profit, and EPS, with gross margin at `75.5%`, inventory at `$896M` versus `$937M` a year earlier, and management raising the full-year outlook again.
  • Structural themes: this is the cleanest holiday-quarter proof that the model is working as an affordable-luxury relationship engine rather than only as a wholesale accessories company. The combination of margin expansion, inventory discipline, and continued guidance raises points to brand desire plus operating control.
