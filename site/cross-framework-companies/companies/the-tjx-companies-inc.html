Annual-Report Starting Point

Annual Report Takeaways

  • TJX is a strong blind-spot addition because `Services / Apparel Stores` is too shallow a label for what the company actually represents in the archive. The more useful read is a scaled off-price bargain-discovery and treasure-hunt retail system built on value perception, flexible buying, dense store footprints, and repeat in-store traffic.
  • AnnualReports was not current enough to anchor the annual package. As of Monday, August 10, 2026, the live AnnualReports page still showed `MOST RECENT 2024 Annual Report and Form 10K`, while the official and SEC chain already supported the fiscal `2025` annual package for the year ended `2026-01-31`.
  • The annual package was strong. Fiscal `2026` net sales were `$60.4B`, up `7%`; consolidated comparable sales increased `5%`; net income was `$5.5B`; and diluted earnings per share were `$4.87`, up `14%` versus fiscal `2025`.
  • The strategic language matters as much as the numbers. TJX describes itself as the leading off-price retailer of apparel and home fashions in the U.S. and worldwide and says it sells quality, fashionable, brand name, and designer merchandise at prices generally `20%` to `60%` below full-price retailers' regular prices on comparable merchandise.
  • The scale is also a major signal. The annual filing said TJX operated over `5,200` stores across its four segments, while the official overview page said the company operated in ten countries across three continents as of fiscal year-end.
  • That combination makes TJX especially useful for the blind-spot lane. It is evidence that bargain discovery is not just a stressed-consumer residual behavior. At scale, it is a durable retail system with its own operating model, cultural logic, and demand rhythm.

Latest Quarter Chain

  • Results: `Q1 FY27` net sales were `$14.3B`, up `9%`; consolidated comparable sales increased `6%`; net income was `$1.3B`; and diluted earnings per share were `$1.19`, up `29%` versus the prior year.
  • Operating posture: pretax profit margin reached `12.0%`, up `1.7` percentage points versus last year and well above plan. Management raised full-year FY27 comp-sales, pretax-profit-margin, EPS, and buyback guidance after the quarter.
  • Structural themes: this quarter matters because it shows the value-hunt system still accelerating even after a strong annual close. Management said all divisions delivered strong comparable sales growth and increases in customer transactions, while branded merchandise availability remained outstanding.
  • Results: `Q4 FY26` net sales were `$17.7B`, up `9%`; comparable sales increased `5%`; net income was `$1.8B`; and diluted earnings per share were `$1.58`, up `28%` versus the prior year.
  • Full-year anchor: the same release closed fiscal `2026` with net sales of `$60.4B`, comparable sales up `5%`, net income of `$5.5B`, and diluted earnings per share of `$4.87`.
  • Operating posture: Q4 pretax profit margin reached `13.5%`, while adjusted pretax profit margin reached `12.2%`, both above plan. Management also said annual sales surpassed `$60B`, calling it a major milestone for the company.

Signal Map

  • Sector: TJX gives the archive a major off-price retail-format case from inside `Services / Apparel Stores`, reinforcing that source taxonomy does not cleanly map modern retail systems.
  • Consumer: this is one of the archive's clearest reads on value-hunt, bargain-discovery, and transaction-led retail demand. Consumers keep showing up when the mix of recognizable brands, pricing gap, and constantly changing assortment is attractive enough.
  • Cultural: TJX matters because it shows that treasure-hunt shopping is not only functional. It is also experiential. The consumer draw is part value and part repeated discovery.
  • Societal: the company is filing-backed evidence that mainstream consumers remain highly responsive to bargain-led formats even when many other discretionary retail channels are uneven, over-positioned, or struggling to hold attention.
  • Business model: the moat is not built first on memberships or ads. It is built on global sourcing flexibility, opportunistic buying, dense physical distribution, multi-banner scale, inventory turn, and consumer willingness to revisit stores for new finds.
  • Competitive positioning: TJX is most useful next to `Ross`, `Burlington`, `Walmart`, and weaker middle-market department-store or apparel names. It shows the stronger end of the value-hunt system at much larger scale.
