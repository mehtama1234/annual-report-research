Business Model Read

Sector: UPS pushes the Services sector toward transport, delivery, and logistics infrastructure rather than toward only brokered freight or consumer-facing services.

Annual Report Takeaways

  • UPS gives the Services sector a second company in the same industry as CHRW, but with a very different operating model: a large physical parcel-and-logistics network instead of an asset-light freight broker.
  • The `2025` annual package shows a company deliberately sacrificing lower-quality volume, especially from Amazon, in order to improve revenue quality and reshape the network for better long-term margins.
  • Full-year `2025` revenue was `$88.7 billion`, operating profit was `$7.9 billion`, non-GAAP adjusted operating profit was `$8.7 billion`, diluted EPS was `$6.56`, and non-GAAP adjusted diluted EPS was `$7.16`.
  • The annual filing matters because it ties the service story to real package density, customer concentration, automation, and network economics. UPS delivered `5.2 billion` packages in `2025` and served `1.6 million` shipping customers.
  • UPS also matters for theme work because it frames services not as pure recurring revenue, but as a capital-intensive network whose economics improve or weaken with mix, route density, labor, and automation.

Latest Quarter Chain

  • Results: Q2 2026 revenue was `$22.8 billion`, diluted EPS was `$0.71`, non-GAAP adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.76`, operating profit was `$930 million`, and non-GAAP adjusted operating profit was `$2.1 billion`.
  • Guidance: on `July 28, 2026`, UPS raised full-year `2026` consolidated revenue guidance to about `$91.2 billion`, non-GAAP adjusted operating profit guidance to about `$8.65 billion`, and non-GAAP adjusted diluted EPS guidance to about `$7.22`.
  • Management message: management said the Amazon glide-down and related network reconfiguration were successfully completed as designed and that UPS entered the second half of `2026` with strong momentum.
  • Structural themes: this quarter is the clearest example of the tension inside UPS right now. GAAP profits were heavily distorted by transformation charges, but the adjusted margin story and guidance raise say management believes the network reset is working.
  • Results: Q1 2026 revenue was `$21.2 billion`, diluted EPS was `$1.02`, non-GAAP adjusted diluted EPS was `$1.07`, operating profit was `$1.27 billion`, and non-GAAP adjusted operating profit was `$1.32 billion`.
  • Guidance: on `April 28, 2026`, management reaffirmed full-year `2026` targets of about `$89.7 billion` of revenue and a non-GAAP adjusted operating margin of about `9.6%`.

Signal Map

  • Sector: UPS pushes the Services sector toward transport, delivery, and logistics infrastructure rather than toward only brokered freight or consumer-facing services.
  • Consumer: consumer demand matters through residential delivery, e-commerce flow, and returns volume, but the stronger analytical lens is package density, mix, and route economics.
  • Cultural: the cultural layer is operational rigor, labor management, and transformation execution rather than a product narrative.
  • Technology: the technology layer includes automation, network optimization, RFID-enabled tracking, digital shipping tools, and process redesign.
  • Labor: labor is central here. Workforce reductions, collective bargaining exposure, and network productivity are direct earnings variables.
  • Capital allocation: UPS remains highly cash-generative but also meaningfully capital-intensive, with `2026` capital expenditure guidance of about `$3.0 billion`.
