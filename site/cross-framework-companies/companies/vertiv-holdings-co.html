Annual Report Takeaways

  • Vertiv is the clearest direct industrial bridge from AI demand into critical physical infrastructure. The company repeatedly describes itself as a global leader in critical digital infrastructure and centers the business on power, cooling, and IT infrastructure solutions and services.
  • The `10-K` makes the market position explicit. Vertiv serves data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial environments, which means it sits inside the hard infrastructure layer required to support rising compute density.
  • Full-year `2025` was already a major step up before the `2026` acceleration. Net sales reached about `$10.23 billion`, operating profit reached about `$1.83 billion`, net income reached about `$1.33 billion`, diluted EPS was `$3.41`, and adjusted diluted EPS was `$6.02`.
  • Q4 `2025` also made the forward demand picture unusually clear. Organic orders grew about `252%`, trailing-twelve-month organic orders grew about `81%`, fourth-quarter book-to-bill was about `2.9x`, and backlog rose to `$15.0 billion`.
  • Vertiv improves the industrial archive because it ties together themes that were only partially visible elsewhere. `Eaton` shows electrical architecture and power quality, and `Trane` shows cooling and building systems. Vertiv sits at the deployment point where hyperscale and colocation customers need those systems integrated into critical digital infrastructure.
