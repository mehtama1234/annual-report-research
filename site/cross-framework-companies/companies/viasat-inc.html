  • Institutional and cultural shift: resilience has become a purchasing category. Governments, airlines, maritime operators, and regulated or safety-sensitive customers increasingly value redundancy, sovereign control, assured coverage, and mission continuity, not just raw throughput or low headline pricing.
  • Industrial pressure: Viasat shows how AI-era and cloud-era narratives still depend on older physical bottlenecks. More data movement and more distributed computing increase the value of backhaul, satellite augmentation, tactical networking, secure links, and remote telemetry, but they also magnify the penalty for launch delays, leverage, and execution misses.
