Latest Quarter Chain

  • Results: `Q1 2026` sales were `$380.4M`, net income was `$9.9M`, and adjusted net income was `$19.8M`.
  • Channel and category posture: wholesale channel sales increased `19%` to `$183.6M`, `DTC` channel sales were flat at `$196.8M`, Coolers & Equipment sales increased `11%` to `$156.1M`, and Drinkware sales increased `5%` to `$216.9M`.

  • Structural themes: this quarter matters because it shows a real hybrid control model. Consumer demand across websites, Amazon Marketplace, and YETI retail remained strong, but the mix still shifted visibly between `DTC`, wholesale, and corporate sales.
  • Margin nuance: profitability was pressured by incremental tariffs even as the topline stayed strong, which is exactly the kind of operating tension a premium physical-goods brand has to manage.
  • Results: `Q4 2025` sales were `$583.7M`, net income was `$58.2M`, and adjusted net income was `$71.8M`.
