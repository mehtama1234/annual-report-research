Abercrombie & Fitch Co.

13 consecutive quarters of growth with Hollister delivering record results shows that youth apparel can still compound when cultural relevance and omnichannel execution stay sharp

Standout figure
Hollister full-year 2025 revenue grew 15% and achieved record results while Abercrombie brand returned to Q4 growth after weaker full-year, with consolidated operating margin at 13.3%
Watch next
Whether Abercrombie brand momentum can sustain Q1 2026 leadership or whether Hollister reverts to carrying the growth story, and whether EMEA's recent softness signals broader international demand weakness or merely geopolitical headwinds.

Why It Is Distinctive

Abercrombie proves that mall apparel is not uniformly broken if brands stay culturally legible and execution stays disciplined. The company is neither a pure heritage power like lululemon nor a struggling format like department stores. It is a real two-brand global omnichannel winner where internal mix rotation and geographic balance matter as much as absolute growth rate.
