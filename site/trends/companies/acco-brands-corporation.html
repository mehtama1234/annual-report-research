ACCO Brands Corporation

Q2 2026 sales $415.1M (+5.1%) with EPOS acquisition adding growth; full-year 2025 net sales declined 8.5%—ACCO is a shrinking office-edge business pivoting toward gaming and peripherals.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 adjusted EPS $0.84 (down from $1.02); multi-year cost-out program delivered $60M+ cumulative savings with $100M target by end of 2026; EPOS deal (closed Jan 30, 2026) adds tech-peripheral exposure.
Watch next
EPOS integration and whether tech peripherals can truly offset office-product decline. Retailer concentration (top 5 customers = $485M) and Amazon dependence mean channel power shifts could quickly pressure margins. FX and tariff volatility remain headwinds.

Why It Is Distinctive

ACCO is not riding office recovery. Core office-product demand is secular decline (lower paper intensity, hybrid work normalization). The strategy is cost discipline, acquisition (EPOS) to reach peripherals and gaming, and reliance on back-to-school seasonality and retailer timing (especially Amazon at 10% of sales). Management execution matters more than category growth.
