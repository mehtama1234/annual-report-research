Watch next
Organic growth sustainability and reimbursement stability. Goodwill pressure and working-capital intensity mean reported earnings can lag actual recurring demand. Any deterioration in equipment-mix or diabetes-supply reorder rates would signal softening underlying demand.

Why It Is Distinctive

AHCO is not a clinical or diagnostic operator. It is a logistics, documentation, reimbursement, and resupply business whose value depends on patient retention, delivery reliability, and payor approval cycles. Home medical equipment demand is sticky but sensitive to reimbursement changes. Working capital and reorder dynamics can obscure underlying recurring demand quality.
