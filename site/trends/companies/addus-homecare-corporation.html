Healthcare · Home Health Care (Specialized Health Services)

Addus HomeCare Corporation

Aging-in-place labor-intensive recurring care platform consolidating regional fragmentation into scaled local density

Standout figure
Q2 2026 revenue $377.4M; personal care 78.4% of revenue, hospice 17.0%, home health 4.6%; adjusted EBITDA $49.2M; record annual revenue of $1.42B in FY2025 with 5th consecutive year of double-digit growth
Watch next
State rate increases (Texas and Illinois mentioned) and reimbursement environment; caregiver wage pressure and retention; acquisition integration success; whether profitability can grow alongside scale or whether labor inflation caps margins.

Why It Is Distinctive

Addus does not sell clinical acuity or innovation. It sells lower-cost care continuity, caregiver density, state rate support, and acquisition-built local scale. The company monetizes the economic truth that keeping people at home is cheaper than institutional care and that fragmented markets consolidate toward scaled operators with compliance systems.
