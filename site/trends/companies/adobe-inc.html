Adobe Inc.

Subscription-and-ARR software platform monetizing creative, document, and customer-experience workflow through recurring fees, upsells, and emerging AI-first capabilities.

FY2025 ARR of $25.20B; FY2025 revenue of $23.77B; Q2 FY2026 ARR of $27.10B; AI-first ARR more than tripled YoY and exceeded $500M in Q2 FY2026.
Watch next
Ability to sustain non-GAAP margin profile while investing in AI capabilities, growth rate of AI-first ARR and migration of customers to AI-enhanced products, and competitive positioning against both legacy vendors and emerging AI-native tools.

Why It Is Distinctive

Adobe is not a generic software or subscription story. The company monetizes creative, document, and customer-experience workflow through direct relationship with content creators, knowledge workers, and marketing teams. The business shows ARR progression and RPO stability (~$22B across the trailing set) characteristic of mature platform software. AI-first revenue is accelerating, showing the company's ability to layer premium capabilities on top of the core workflow install base without requiring fundamental customer re-adoption.
