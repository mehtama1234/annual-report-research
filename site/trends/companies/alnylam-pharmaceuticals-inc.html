Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

Platform concentration risk crystallized: Q2 2026 TTR revenue of $1.03B (+153% YoY) now drives enterprise value, but management lowered ATTR-CM guidance, proving launch durability remains unproven

Standout figure
Q2 2026 TTR revenues of $1.03B (AMVUTTRA and ONPATTRO combined), crossing $1B quarterly product revenue threshold for first time, but company lowered TTR guidance post-Q1 learnings; Rare franchise (GIVLAARI, OXLUMO) at $142M total
Watch next
Whether AMVUTTRA ATTR-CM guidance reset reflects demand-side reality (market smaller than expected, adoption curve less steep) or supply-side friction (payer access, patient identification, geographic rollout), and whether pipeline programs can move fast enough to reduce franchise dependency before patient saturation pressures pricing.

Why It Is Distinctive

Alnylam was a platform biotech; it is becoming a concentrated franchise company. The RNAi thesis still holds, but operational reality shifted: one approved indication (ATTR-CM) now dominates earnings. That concentration creates both extraordinary upside and vulnerability. Unlike traditional biotech pre-approval, this company must now prove launch can sustain and that pipeline replenishment is real. The guidance reset mid-2026 shows the franchise is harder to model than management implied entering the year.
