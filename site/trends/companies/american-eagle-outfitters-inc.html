American Eagle Outfitters, Inc.

Multi-brand portfolio increasingly dependent on Aerie cultural relevance while core American Eagle banner remains flat or weak.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 net revenue $5.5B up 3%; Aerie comparable sales up 9% while American Eagle flat; Q1 2026 Aerie comparable sales up 25% versus American Eagle down 2%.
Watch next
Whether American Eagle stabilizes or continues declining as an owned brand, and whether Aerie's 25% growth can offset core-brand weakness at the portfolio level.

Why It Is Distinctive

American Eagle is a study in internal portfolio imbalance. Aerie (intimate, comfort, lifestyle) is the cultural and growth engine; American Eagle (core youth denim/apparel) is flat or weak. Unlike Gap (which has similar splits), the AEO split is more binary and extreme. Unlike lululemon, AEO lacks premium positioning. The company is increasingly an Aerie-led portfolio masked by holding-company consolidation.
