American Express Company

Premium-card ecosystem with rich first-party data and merchant network is being positioned as infrastructure for AI-driven agentic commerce rather than traditional payments.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 revenue $72.2B with net income $10.8B; billed business $1.67T; 65% of new accounts on fee-paying products; Millennial and Gen Z consumers now largest share of U.S. consumer spending.
Watch next
Whether AI-commerce integration can create meaningful new revenue streams and whether premium card demand sustains if economic conditions soften for Millennial and Gen Z cohorts.

Why It Is Distinctive

AmEx is not a commodity payments processor. It is monetizing premium customer identity, high-value merchant relationships, closed-loop data, and now explicitly AI-agent integration. The annual narrative directly discusses AI-powered agents discovering products, making decisions, and completing payments, with AmEx embedded in that flow. Card-member fee revenue posted its 30th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth, showing the premium category remains durable.
