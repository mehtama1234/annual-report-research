Industrial Goods · Industrial Equipment Wholesale

Applied Industrial Technologies, Inc.

Fiscal Q3 2026 organic sales +6.0% (vs. flat FY2025) with Engineered Solutions +9.3%—AIT is pivoting from bearing distribution toward technical MRO, automation, and datacenter supply systems.

Standout figure
Fiscal 2025 record sales $4.6B (organic -2.3%); Engineered Solutions now ~40% of sales (vs. 18% ten years ago); fiscal Q3 guidance raised to EPS $10.64–$10.75 from prior $10.10–$10.85.
Watch next
Mix durability as capital-spending recovery broadens beyond MRO. LIFO expense and tariff uncertainty remain margin headwinds. Service Center can benefit from break-fix urgency, but Engineered Solutions is more project-timing dependent.

Why It Is Distinctive

AIT is a technical-distribution platform where margin expansion and growth depend on engineering support, service depth, automation capability, and cross-sell rather than stocked replacement parts alone. Datacenter, semiconductor, robotics, and process infrastructure demand are shifting the earnings mix. Hydradyne and Thompson Industrial Supply acquisitions deepen fluid-power and local service density.
