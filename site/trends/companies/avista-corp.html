Why It Is Distinctive

Avista is not a pure growth utility; it is a smaller, hydro-sensitive, mixed electric-and-gas system where weather variability, water availability, and specific customer concentration still drive material earnings variance. Unlike larger utilities talking grid buildout and electrification tailwinds, Avista is defending against a large industrial customer defection. This keeps Avista grounded in the fundamentals: rate-case execution, operating discipline, and manageable capital deployment rather than narratives of boundless load growth.
