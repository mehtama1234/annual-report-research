Bath & Body Works, Inc.

Specialty retailer in brand reinvention mode through Consumer First Formula; Q1 2026 down 3% but foundation built around product, brand, marketplace expansion.

Standout figure
Q1 2026 net sales $1.378B (down 3%); FY2025 net sales down 0.2% despite holiday seasonality; 40M active loyalty members; 1.927M+ U.S./Canadian stores.
Watch next
Consumer First Formula execution and product-led momentum; direct and international growth acceleration; tariff and freight cost trends; holiday season performance.

Why It Is Distinctive

Bath & Body Works is the weaker specialty-brand case showing that scale and loyalty membership alone do not guarantee growth without product innovation and brand cultural relevance. The transformation is explicit and necessary rather than optional.
