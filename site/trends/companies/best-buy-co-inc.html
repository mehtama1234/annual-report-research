Services · Electronics Stores

Best Buy Co., Inc.

Electronics retailer trying to deepen customer relationship through Marketplace, Best Buy Ads, services, and omnichannel execution rather than relying only on merchandise sales.

Standout figure
FY26 enterprise comparable sales increased 0.5%; Q1 FY27 enterprise comparable sales increased 2.0%; Best Buy Ads and Marketplace initiatives drove margin expansion alongside modest merchandise growth.
Watch next
Marketplace and Best Buy Ads revenue growth and margin contribution, ability to convert service trust and upgrade cycles into durable profit streams, and category demand normalization after recent computing and mobile softness.

Why It Is Distinctive

Best Buy is not a pure retailer defending a low-growth core. It is trying to own service, fulfillment, traffic, and seller/advertiser monetization around the same customer base. The company repeatedly pairs comparable-sales commentary with Marketplace, Best Buy Ads, services, and omnichannel execution, suggesting management sees future economics as a layered customer-relationship problem. The business carries meaningful category volatility (gaming, computing, mobile phones strong; home theater, appliances weak), but management is leaning into incremental profit streams rather than only merchandising.
