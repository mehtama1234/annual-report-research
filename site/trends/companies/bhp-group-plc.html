BHP Group Plc

Copper now drives 51% of underlying EBITDA; BHP is repositioning into a future-facing materials allocator, not just a bulk-ore processor.

Standout figure
Record 2 Mt copper production in FY2025, up from ~1.8 Mt two years prior. Copper now contributes more to underlying earnings than iron ore's historical dominance.
Watch next
Copper growth project progress (Cerro Colorado, Copper South Australia, Escondida, Resolution, Faraday) and whether cost control holds amid diesel inflation. Any softness in capital discipline or project delays would test the copper-centrality thesis.

Why It Is Distinctive

BHP is being rethought as a copper-and-potash platform rather than a plain iron-ore major. The half-year results show copper explicit as central; FY2026 guidance raised to 1.9–2.0 Mt. Meanwhile, potash sits as a long-duration agricultural-input and resource-security option. This is strategic rebalancing, not reactive commodity chasing.
