Booking Holdings Inc.

Global travel-booking platform with $26.9B FY2025 revenue, merchant mix rising to 73% of bookings, and $1.9B ARR reach showing shift toward direct consumer and cross-vertical transactions.

Standout figure
FY2025 revenue of $26.9B with merchant gross bookings growing to 73% of total mix; flight gross bookings increased 29%; Q2 2026 merchant mix rose to 73% from 69% year-over-year.
Watch next
Middle East geopolitical impact on international travel demand and fuel costs; merchant mix stabilization at higher levels and whether agency conversions continue; Connected Trip monetization and whether ancillary attach improves margins; international exposure to FX and travel policy shifts.

Why It Is Distinctive

Booking is not a travel company; it is a distribution and transaction orchestration platform. The economics depend on merchant mix, direct consumer behavior, mobile-app usage, loyalty participation, payment attachment, and cross-vertical transaction volume rather than on supply inventory alone.
