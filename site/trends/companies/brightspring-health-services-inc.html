BrightSpring Health Services, Inc.

Home-and-community healthcare platform integrating pharmacy, infusion, and provider services around complex patients is scaling faster than legacy single-service operators.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 revenue $3.873B with home-health average daily census up 54% YoY to 46,448; revenue per script $314.20; full-year 2026 guidance increased to $15.1-15.4B revenue and $820-845M adjusted EBITDA.
Watch next
Whether home-health census growth can persist without deteriorating quality or payor acceptance and whether specialty-pharmacy margin compression from GLP-1 and other high-volume drugs pressures overall economics.

Why It Is Distinctive

BrightSpring is not just a home-health or hospice operator. It ties pharmacy and provider workflows together around the same complex populations, creating scale and coordination advantages over single-service competitors. The Community Living divestiture sharpened focus toward higher-margin provider and pharmacy infrastructure. Script economics, home-health census, and density all compound together rather than in isolation.
