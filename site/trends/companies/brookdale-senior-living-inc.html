Standout figure
Q1 2026 weighted average occupancy 80.0% and RevPAR up 5.9%; full-year 2025 adjusted EBITDA up 19.1% to $450.3M despite net loss from continuing operations of $263.5M.
Watch next
Whether occupancy can sustain above 80% and whether operating leverage can eventually overcome net loss profile as occupancy compounds.

Why It Is Distinctive

Brookdale is the archive's direct graying-market operator. Unlike abstract demographic thesis, occupancy (80%), RevPAR, resident mix, community staffing, and acuity all determine real earnings. Unlike hospital chains, leverage and care-delivery complexity still constrain value. Operating improvement is real, but net loss shows balance-sheet stress remains.
