Watch next

Why It Is Distinctive

Brookfield is not a simple asset manager or operator. It's an integrated capital-allocation machine combining fee-bearing AUM, insurance-linked capital and float, direct operating businesses, and long-duration principal investing. Growth compounds through capital formation and asset monetization rather than pure management-fee margin.
