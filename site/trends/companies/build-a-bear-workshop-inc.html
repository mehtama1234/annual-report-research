Build-A-Bear Workshop, Inc.

Direct-to-consumer traffic weakness partially offset by strong commercial and franchise growth shows ceremony-driven retail can monetize multiple channels even when mall traffic softens

Standout figure
Q1 2026 commercial and international franchise revenues grew 34.1% to $11.8M while direct-to-consumer store net retail sales fell 5.1%, with consolidated e-commerce demand down 26.1%
Watch next
Whether partner-operated and wholesale channel growth can sustain momentum through a difficult direct consumer backdrop, and whether tariff refunds in Q1 2026 mask underlying store demand pressure or represent a genuine stabilization of traffic.

Why It Is Distinctive

Build-A-Bear is one of the clearest examples of a physical retail concept turning into a participation and ritual system rather than just a merchandise business. The Heart Ceremony, customization, and emotional investment create customer attachment that transcends traditional toy retail. The business model has widened beyond mall stores into partner-operated, wholesale, and franchise channels, making the direct-to-consumer core less dominant even as it weakens.
