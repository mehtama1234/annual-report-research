Builders FirstSource, Inc.

Professional-builder supply platform defending differentiation through prefabrication, value-added services, and digital tools in a weak housing-affordability environment.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 net sales down 8.8% to $3.9B, adjusted EBITDA margin declined to 8.5%; FY 2025 net sales of $15.8B and adjusted EBITDA margin of 10.1% despite soft housing backdrop.
Watch next
Housing-affordability pressures and repair-and-remodel demand offsetting new-construction weakness, execution of prefabrication and digital capabilities as competitive moat, and whether management can sustain margin discipline without near-term housing rebound.

Why It Is Distinctive

Builders FirstSource is not a plain lumber-and-commodities distributor. It is a national professional-builder platform with 585 locations serving new residential construction and repair-and-remodel. The company emphasizes prefabricated components, turnkey services, and digital solutions as structural advantages. Management is defending a differentiated platform in a weak starts environment by investing in technology, value-added solutions, and positioning as a full-service builder partner, not by chasing volume.
