Why It Is Distinctive

Bunge is the agricultural throughput operator sitting between farmers and global food-fuel-feed demand systems. Unlike pure fertilizer names, it controls origination, storage, processing, and distribution across multiple crops and geographies, turning geopolitical and weather volatility into an advantage for a company with enough network density to redirect flows rather than just absorb disruption.
