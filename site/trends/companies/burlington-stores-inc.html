Burlington Stores, Inc.

Off-price acceleration driven by branded bargain discovery and trade-down behavior, with Q1 2026 total sales up 14% and comp stores up 6%

Standout figure
Q1 2026 comp store sales rose 6%, and FY25 net income was $610M on 2% comp growth, showing momentum building throughout the year
Watch next
Watch whether off-price comp growth can sustain as a standalone trend, or whether it represents temporary trade-down that will normalize when consumer confidence stabilizes

Why It Is Distinctive

Burlington is a pure value-and-bargain play without loyalty membership or ad-platform widening. Success depends entirely on store traffic, merchandise turn, and off-price shopping behavior. The model is overwhelmingly physical-store driven (99%+ of sales from stores).
