Cameco Corporation

Nuclear fuel cycle control system capturing contract-backed long-term uranium demand and Westinghouse equity upside

Standout figure
Long-term commitments to deliver ~230M pounds of uranium at ~28M pounds annually over next 5 years; Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA $391M down from Q2 2025 $352M due to lower equity earnings from Westinghouse and delivery timing, not uranium weakness
Watch next
Westinghouse profitability and global reactor construction pipeline (Dukovany mentioned); Q1 2026 showed uranium segment driving improvement through higher realized prices; whether contracted uranium base continues to rise as energy security justifies more reactor capex.

Why It Is Distinctive

Cameco is not a pure uranium-price play. It monetizes the entire fuel cycle through mining, procurement, inventory management, conversion, long-term contracting, and a strategic stake in Westinghouse. The company's margins depend on contract visibility and reactor deployment as much as spot uranium prices.
