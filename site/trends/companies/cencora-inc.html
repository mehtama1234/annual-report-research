Why It Is Distinctive

Cencora is not a pharmaceutical-manufacturer story; it is a distribution and access-infrastructure layer connecting manufacturers, providers, pharmacies, and patients. GLP-1 volume helps topline but pressures margins, while specialty depth (OneOncology acquisition) and services attachment aim to improve mix. The business remains resilient because drug flow and provider access are mission-critical.
