Why It Is Distinctive

Chewy is a digital-native pet-care relationship system built on Autoship, prescriptions, wellness services, and account management rather than a transactional e-commerce model. The company monetizes routine behavior and emotional attachment to pets, creating stickier relationships than generic retail while adding healthcare-adjacent service layers.
