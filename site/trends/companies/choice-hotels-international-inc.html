Choice Hotels International, Inc.

Q2 2026 conversion pipeline expansion and U.S. RevPAR growth of 1.3% show lower-price-point lodging can trade up mix through franchise economics and owner alignment rather than pure demand recovery

Standout figure
Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $175M with franchise fees up 6% to $188M, U.S. conversion agreements expanding with 9,400 new rooms awarded, and global pipeline above 77,800 rooms with 97% concentrated in extended stay, midscale, and upscale brands
Watch next
Whether U.S. conversion pipeline can sustain current pace without requiring price concessions that compress franchisee economics, and whether international development, especially in Europe, can replicate North American success.

Why It Is Distinctive

Choice is distinct within lodging because it operates as an asset-light franchisor focused on selective segment mix upgrading rather than raw system breadth. The company monetizes owner demand for conversions and better economics through extended-stay depth and midscale-to-upscale positioning. This makes Choice less dependent on cyclical RevPAR strength and more dependent on franchisee unit economics and capital discipline.
