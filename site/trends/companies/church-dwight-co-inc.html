Church & Dwight Co. Inc.

Narrower household-and-personal-care portfolio refreshed through portfolio pruning and tuck-in acquisitions, increasingly reliant on eCommerce scale.

Standout figure
eCommerce reached 25.5% of total consumer sales in Q2 2026 (22.7% growth), significantly above traditional retail maturity curves for staples brands.
Watch next
Whether organic growth rate can accelerate beyond the 4-5% guided range, mix of inherited mature brands versus faster-growing acquired brands, and Amazon-dependent eCommerce channel health.

Why It Is Distinctive

Church & Dwight reads as more acquisition-active and eCommerce-forward than Kimberly-Clark or Colgate, but narrower than Procter & Gamble. The company is not defending a static legacy portfolio; it is actively pruning weaker assets like SPINBRUSH and WATERPIK while acquiring THERABREATH, HERO, and MISS MOUTH'S to refresh growth. Newer brands carry stronger digital momentum.
