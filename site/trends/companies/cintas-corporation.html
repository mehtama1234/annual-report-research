Why It Is Distinctive

Cintas is not apparel retail. It is outsourced workplace services (uniforms, facility services, first aid, safety, fire protection) monetized through route density and customer location density. The model produces pricing power, retention, cross-sell, and strong free cash flow because switching costs are high once installed.
