Basic Materials · Paper & Paper Products

Clearwater Paper Corp.

Focused paperboard operator moving from diversified paper conglomerate to cost-disciplined SBS supplier inside persistently oversupplied industry.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 SG&A fell from 8.4% to 6.5% of sales; fixed-cost reductions exceeded $50M while maintaining ~$50M per outage execution discipline.
Watch next
Whether SBS industry capacity rationalization or demand recovery drives the next leg—2026 guidance sits at $1.4B-$1.5B revenue but margin recovery depends on pricing, not just volume or cost.

Why It Is Distinctive

Not a simple commodity play. Clearwater is showing that operational excellence and product focus (closed-toe shoe expansion, sustainable positioning) matter alongside pricing. Still, the underlying SBS market at 83% utilization and declining average selling prices ($1,167 per ton in 2025) remains the limiting factor.
