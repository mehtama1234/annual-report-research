Watch next
Whether new products (U.S. Treasury clearing, 24/7 crypto, prediction markets) expand TAM enough to justify valuations, and whether CME's market-data pricing power persists if rival exchanges or decentralized systems gain traction.

Why It Is Distinctive

CME is a market-infrastructure monopoly, not a financial services company competing on relationships or returns. When clients need to hedge across rates, equity indexes, FX, energy, agriculture, and metals, CME captures the activity through clearing, contract innovation, and benchmark pricing power. Data is increasingly valuable (clients pay $238M quarterly for data alone), and resilience in volatile markets creates pricing power. This is as close to regulated infrastructure toll as financial markets allow.
