Consumer Goods · Personal Products

Colgate-Palmolive Co.

Science-backed daily-use category leadership in oral care and pet nutrition creates resilient defensive demand that repels private-label competition through brand strength and innovation.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 net sales $20.382B with organic growth of 1.4%; Hill's Pet Nutrition remains major growth and margin asset; net cash from operations set record at $4.198B.
Watch next
Whether Hill's Pet Nutrition can sustain growth momentum and whether private-label pressure in oral care remains contained by brand loyalty and efficacy perception.

Why It Is Distinctive

Colgate is not competing on price or broad portfolio diversification like P&G. It is competing on category leadership, science-based innovation, and advertising support in habitual daily-use categories. Gross margins remain strong at 61%+ because the category franchises have sustainable pricing power relative to private label even in cost-of-living pressure environments.

What the Pet Economy Reveals About Everything Else
