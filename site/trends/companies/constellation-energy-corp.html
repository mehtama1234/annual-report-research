Constellation Energy Corp

Nuclear and clean-generation platform monetizes scarce reliable baseload through long-dated PPAs with data centers and tech buyers: not transmission/distribution regulated utility, but commercial generation strategist.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 adjusted operating earnings guidance raised to $11.50-12.50 per share (full-year 2026); 920 MW of new long-term PPAs with investment-grade customers; Calpine acquisition expands platform by 55 GW fleet.
Watch next
Whether data-center power-purchase agreement demand can sustain beyond initial wave, and whether regulatory approvals for nuclear license extensions and new PPAs keep pace with commercial pipeline growth.

Why It Is Distinctive

Constellation is not a traditional regulated utility. It owns and contracts scarce clean and reliable generation to hyperscalers and large industrial users. The value is from commercial contracting, life extensions, and plant restarts, not from rate regulation or dividend stability alone.
