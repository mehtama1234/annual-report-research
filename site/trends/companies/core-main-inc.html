Core & Main, Inc.

Water-and-fire-protection distributor monetizing replacement and modernization demand rather than only new construction, with double-digit category growth in smart solutions.

Standout figure
FY 2025 net sales of $7.647B; fusible HDPE, treatment-plant solutions, and geosynthetics each grew at double-digit rates, showing shift toward complex-project and infrastructure-modernization demand.
Watch next
Whether municipal infrastructure spending and federal appropriations continue to support replacement cycles, ability to expand smart-metering and treatment-plant solutions revenue, and execution of acquisitions like Canada Waterworks integration.

Why It Is Distinctive

Core & Main is not a simple cyclical building-products wholesaler. It is a specialty distributor focused on water, wastewater, storm drainage, and fire-protection products sold to municipalities, private water companies, and professional contractors. The company operates in large and highly fragmented markets with an estimated addressable market of ~$50B and estimates itself as one of only two national distributors at this scale. The business is replacement-and-upgrade infrastructure driven, not new-construction dependent. Private-label expansion, sourcing optimization, and value-added services are structured margin-expansion levers.
