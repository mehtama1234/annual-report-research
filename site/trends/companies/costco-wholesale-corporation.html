Costco Wholesale Corporation

Paid-membership retail model compounds through value perception, frequency, and digital integration rather than promotional gimmick.

Standout figure
Fiscal 2025 net sales $269.9B up 8.1%; membership fees $5.323B; Q3 2026 total company comparable sales 9.8% or 6.6% adjusted; digitally-enabled sales up 21.5%.
Watch next
Whether digital growth (20%+ comps) can continue offsetting warehouse traffic normalization, and if membership price increases face consumer resistance.

Why It Is Distinctive

Costco's loyalty is embedded in the membership fee, not app-only rewards or airline-style points. Value trust, warehouse volume, membership fee growth, and digital enablement reinforce each other. Unlike traditional retailers, Costco monetizes membership and private-label economics on top of merchandise margins. Unlike software subscriptions, Costco is capital-intensive and volume-dependent.
