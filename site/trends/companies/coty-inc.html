Coty Inc.

Prestige fragrance resilience masks mass beauty weakness, creating a portfolio where strategy review risk sits alongside genuine category strength

Standout figure
FY2026 Q3 net revenues down 7% LFL but adjusted EBITDA of $127M shows fragrance and Prestige categories offsetting Consumer Beauty pressure
Watch next
Whether strategic review of Consumer Beauty produces a restructuring or divestiture that cleanly separates higher-margin Prestige franchises from lower-growth mass categories, and whether fragrance and skincare innovation can sustain current momentum without major marketing-efficiency challenges.

Why It Is Distinctive

Coty gives the consumer-goods set evidence that beauty is not a unified category. Prestige fragrance can still have attractive subcategories and price realization even when mass beauty and broader Consumer Beauty are weaker and more promotional. The company's exposure to geopolitics, category softness, and portfolio risk makes it materially less stable than staples-heavy names.
