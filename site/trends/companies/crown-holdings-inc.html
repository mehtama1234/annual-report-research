Crown Holdings, Inc.

Record adjusted EBITDA of $2.092B in 2025 with 5% global beverage can volume growth and 2.5x leverage shows Crown is a scaled converter, not a commodity box player.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 adjusted EPS raised to $8.30–$8.50 FY guidance (up from $7.90–$8.30); global beverage volumes up 5% in both Q1 and Q2 2026 despite Middle East conflict disruptions.
Watch next
Capacity utilization and mix leverage as can demand continues; any retreat in aluminum pricing or beverage throughput would test the leverage and shareholder-return model. Brazil, Greece, Spain, and India expansion execution matters for 2026–2027 growth.

Why It Is Distinctive

Crown is the rigid-packaging infrastructure backbone for beverage, food, aerosol, and industrial distribution. Beverage cans still lead, but the business diversifies across closures, equipment, transit packaging, and plant placement. Aluminum pass-through and capital deployment (both capex for growth and buybacks) work together; this is not harvesting a stagnant box business.
