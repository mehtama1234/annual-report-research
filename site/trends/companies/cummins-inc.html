Why It Is Distinctive

Cummins is not primarily a truck-engine maker anymore. It's a distributed-power and industrial-uptime platform where Engine weakness is offset by Power Systems and Distribution serving grid-constrained data centers, microgrids, and backup-power demands. The engine franchise provides cash; the power-systems pivot drives margin expansion.
