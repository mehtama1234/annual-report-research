Delta Air Lines, Inc.

SkyMiles loyalty ecosystem monetized $8.2B American Express remuneration in 2025 (growing to $10B projected), representing 13% of total revenue and proving miles-as-currency business rivals the airline operation itself

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 American Express remuneration of $8.2B (11% YoY growth), Q2 2026 AE remuneration of $2.4B (up 16%), loyalty and related revenue growing 19%, 12% of award miles flown on Delta in 2025 from award travel, SkyMiles deferred revenue growing as miles presold to co-brand partners
Watch next
Whether SkyMiles can sustain growth if American Express co-brand economics compress, whether third-party mile-selling saturation limits growth, and whether the airline's operational performance (fuel costs, labor, disruption) impacts miles' perceived value and redemption behavior.

Why It Is Distinctive

Delta is not only an airline; it is a payments and partner-ecosystem company that happens to operate flights. The SkyMiles business (selling miles to American Express, other co-brand partners, and third parties) is a recurring, high-margin revenue stream worth $8.2B annually. This is a loyalty currency operating inside a travel network, not a points overlay on tickets. Management explicitly frames SkyMiles as designed to create high-margin, resilient cash flows and deepen customer engagement beyond air travel.
