Dollar General Corporation

Consumables-heavy neighborhood discount chain proving operational recovery through traffic gains, margin discipline, and large-scale remodel execution.

Standout figure
FY2025 comp sales +3.0%, EPS +34.1% to $6.85, Q1 2026 traffic +1.4%, gross margin improved 65bps; Project Renovate and Project Elevate remodeled 4,254 stores in 2025.
Watch next
2H 2026 traffic elasticity and whether margin improvement from shrink and mix can sustain 2-3% comps as base compares to increasingly favorable prior-year comparisons.

Why It Is Distinctive

Dollar General is traffic-led value retail with 81% consumables mix—different from Dollar Tree's multi-price or Ross's off-price discovery. The retail model depends on shrink reduction, inventory control, and mature-store refresh rather than merchandise innovation.
