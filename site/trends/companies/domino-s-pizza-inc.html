Domino's Pizza, Inc.

Delivery-and-carryout franchise system where 85%+ of U.S. sales come from digital channels: not a menu innovation story, but a transaction frequency and royalty model.

Standout figure
FY2025 total revenues $4.94B; digital penetration 85%+ of U.S. retail sales; $0.375 per-digital-transaction technology fee; Q2 2026 same-store sales +0.1% U.S. but net store growth +209 globally.
Watch next
Whether digital-transaction fees can expand without eroding franchisee unit economics, and whether same-store sales inflection can come from app engagement and personalization without relying on aggregator channel expansion.

Why It Is Distinctive

Domino's is not competing on pizza quality or menu breadth. It's optimizing transaction counts, ordering friction reduction, delivery speed, and technology fees. The franchise model and digital attachment let the company grow unit economics even when same-store sales soften.
