Why It Is Distinctive

DoorDash is misclassified as internet access. It is a local-commerce coordination platform that monetizes recurring convenience behavior, merchant aggregation, membership, and logistics density. The company's real asset is the coordination layer between consumers, merchants, and couriers, not direct retail or delivery alone.
