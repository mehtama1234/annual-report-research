Duke Energy Corporation

Regulated utility becoming a data-center electricity supplier and grid-infrastructure builder with 7.6 GW of secured data-center ESAs and $103B capital plan through 2030

Standout figure
Q1 2026 secured data-center ESAs of 7.6 GW (up from 4.5 GW), with roughly 5 GW under construction and 15.4 GW in late-stage pipeline, plus 20 gas turbines on order through GE Vernova partnership
Watch next
Watch whether data-center load materialization matches ESA pipeline (particularly the 15.4 GW late-stage portion), whether generation build costs hold to assumptions, and whether regulatory approval for rate recovery remains constructive as capital intensity rises sharply

Why It Is Distinctive

Duke is not just a utility serving existing demand. It is actively marketing large-load capacity to data centers and advanced manufacturing, positioning itself as infrastructure enabler rather than passive rate-recovery operator.
