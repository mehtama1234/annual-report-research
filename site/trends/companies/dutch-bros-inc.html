Why It Is Distinctive

Dutch Bros is not a restaurant in the traditional sense; it is a ritual-frequency and stimulation-led beverage interface built on drive-thru convenience, speed, repeat reward, and social energy. The model depends on transaction growth (not just unit count), which signals genuine behavioral stickiness rather than promotional traffic.
