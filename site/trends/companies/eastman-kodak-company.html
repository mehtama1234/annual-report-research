Eastman Kodak Company

Legacy imaging company survives through print systems, film, and industrial materials rather than through camera nostalgia or consumer devices.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 revenue up 18% to $311M with gross margin expanding to 26% from 19%; Advanced Materials & Chemicals revenue up 40% to $105M; operational EBITDA up from $9M to $36M shows underlying improvement.
Watch next
Whether commodity input inflation returns or whether manufacturing efficiency improvements can continue to outpace material cost pressure.

Why It Is Distinctive

Kodak is not chasing consumer-photo recovery. It is executing as a lean industrial print-and-materials platform. Growth is coming from pricing, manufacturing efficiency, operational discipline, and mix rather than from broad demand acceleration. Silver and aluminum commodity costs remain a constraint, but the company is outrunning them through execution and cost work.
