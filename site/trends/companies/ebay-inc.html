Why It Is Distinctive

eBay is not a generic marketplace. It is a category-specialist commerce network where enthusiast demand, supply specialization (collectibles, pre-loved fashion), and ad-enabled discovery compound together. Unlike Amazon (inventory-centric) or retail (brand-centric), eBay monetizes the trust layer and discovery experience. Recommerce, collectibles, and first-party advertising are now growth drivers rivaling the core listing business, making eBay a media+marketplace hybrid.
