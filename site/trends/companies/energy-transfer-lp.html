Energy Transfer LP

Midstream is really an export-loading, fractionation, and storage infrastructure system where value comes from bottleneck relief and long-term commercial contracts, not just tariff tolls.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA raised to $18.8B-$19.1B guidance; NGL transportation volumes up 13%; NGL exports up 25%; fully subscribed Nederland expansion adds 240,000 bpd ethane capacity and 55,000 bpd LPG; signed long-term agreements for ~300,000 bpd on y-grade assets.
Watch next
Whether ethane export capacity expansion can sustain returns as shale growth moderates and whether international energy demand justifies long-term fractionation and export capacity investments.

Why It Is Distinctive

Energy Transfer is not passive pipe ownership. The company is actively building connected corridor infrastructure from basin gathering through fractionation and export docks. Lake Charles LNG suspension shows capital discipline: management chose to redirect capital toward natural-gas infrastructure projects with better risk-return profiles rather than pursuing a headline LNG export facility.
