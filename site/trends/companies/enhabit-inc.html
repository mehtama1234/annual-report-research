Enhabit, Inc.

Clinical home-health and hospice operator caught between patient preference for home care and reimbursement pressure that penalizes volume growth.

Standout figure
Q1 2026 net service revenue $264.8M; home-health revenue up 0.6% despite 9.5% admission growth—reimbursement decline and payor-mix shift offset volume gains.
Watch next
Kinderhook take-private (closed May 2026) signals public markets undervalued reimbursement-sensitive home-based care—watch for post-deal strategic reset and whether private ownership accelerates branch pruning and margin capture.

Why It Is Distinctive

Enhabit sits in the skilled nursing and episodic-payment middle layer, not personal care or acute therapy. This makes it more Medicare-dependent and more exposed to reimbursement reset risk than alternative-site operators, but also more integrated with clinical workflows.
