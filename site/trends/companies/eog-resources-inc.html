EOG Resources, Inc.

Non-integrated producer with 70% cash-return discipline and proved reserves held 99% in US turns commodity volatility into shareholder payback.

Standout figure
$10.04B operating cash flow on $4.98B net income in 2025; Q2 2026 crude realizations jumped to $98.15/barrel from $72.47/barrel in Q1, proving operating leverage.
Watch next
Whether the 70% return framework is maintained if oil prices soften below $75/barrel, and whether NGL pricing premium to Mont Belvieu persists as production volumes climb.

Why It Is Distinctive

EOG embeds capital discipline through a formal 70% cash-return commitment and holds nearly all reserves domestically, avoiding the global supermajor complexity. Earnings can swing sharply with oil price, but the framework channels cash back to shareholders predictably rather than subsidizing growth projects.
