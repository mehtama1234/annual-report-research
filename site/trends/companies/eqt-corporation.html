EQT Corporation

Appalachian gas abundance becomes monetized infrastructure rather than spot-price commodity when linked to LNG and premium power demand through long-term contracts.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 free cash flow of $330M on $1.014B adjusted operating cash flow; 5-year LNG offtake SPA for 0.5 MTPA starting 2028; 10-year power supply agreement for 325,000 Dth/d shows gas translated into contracted revenue streams.
Watch next
Whether LNG export tariffs and domestic power demand growth can sustain the premium pricing for Appalachian gas or whether structural oversupply returns if these markets don't materialize.

Why It Is Distinctive

EQT is not a simple shale producer betting on Henry Hub prices. The company is converting Appalachian reserve depth and dry-gas advantage into infrastructure linkages to LNG and power demand. The strategic inflection is that gas is being valued not just as a commodity but as a critical input for energy transition infrastructure. Long-term contracting removes some price volatility while capturing premium value.
