Why It Is Distinctive

Equinix is not a traditional property REIT. It monetizes network density, interconnection ecosystem depth, and digital-infrastructure scarcity through high-recurring gross margin and compounding EBITDA leverage. The moat is the density and lock-in of clouds, networks, and customers, not property appreciation.
