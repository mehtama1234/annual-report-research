Exact Sciences Corporation

Cologuard Plus and Cancerguard portfolio expansion created a screening platform for earlier, more frequent testing rather than a single-product franchise; Abbott merger (closed March 2026) transforms company from independent diagnostics player into part of larger ecosystem

Standout figure
Q4 2025 screening revenue $695M (79% of $878M total); full-year 2025 revenue $3.25B; Abbott acquisition closed March 23, 2026 at $2.4B enterprise value
Watch next
Abbott integration strategy and maintained momentum post-close, blood-based CRC screening progress (Freenome license), reimbursement expansion and guideline inclusion, competitive response from Quest and Labcorp platforms

Why It Is Distinctive

Exact monetizes screening participation and friction reduction more than specialist decision support. The moat sits in demand creation, channel access, payer normalization, and product-stack expansion, not just in assay science. The company deliberately built a portfolio (Cologuard, Cologuard Plus, Oncodetect, Cancerguard) to deepen participation rather than create a single mega-test.
