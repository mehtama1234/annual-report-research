Whether B2B growth can sustain >20% CAGR and whether Expedia can maintain advertising pricing power as travel intent becomes more commoditized across booking platforms.

Why It Is Distinctive

Expedia is a three-layer platform: consumer brands (Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo), B2B distribution infrastructure (agency and affiliate gross bookings of $35.7B in 2025), and advertising network ($758M in 2025). One Key loyalty unifies cross-brand earn and redeem. Unlike Booking (more merchant-focused), Expedia layers B2B distribution and advertising explicitly atop consumer brands, creating wider intermediation. This is the clearest proof that travel-marketplace value sits in workflow ownership across multiple stakeholder types.
