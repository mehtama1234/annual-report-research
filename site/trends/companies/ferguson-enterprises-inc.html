Ferguson Enterprises Inc.

Contractor-facing distribution platform is taking share despite residential weakness through strength in non-residential, waterworks, and large capital projects using bolt-on M&A and branch density.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 sales $8.751B with operating margin improving to 10.2%; non-residential revenue up 8%; price inflation in low single digits; management raised 2026 guidance to mid-single-digit growth; completed five acquisitions in quarter.
Watch next
Whether residential demand recovery is material enough to improve total company growth if non-residential and waterworks strength moderate and whether acquisition pace strains integration capabilities.

Why It Is Distinctive

Ferguson is not purely cyclical on residential construction. It makes money through repair, maintenance, improvement, waterworks, and large capital projects—categories with different demand drivers. The company takes share through availability, local density, technical support, and simplified job procurement rather than through product innovation. Acquisitions (FloWorks in flow control) show the company moving toward higher-complexity channels.
