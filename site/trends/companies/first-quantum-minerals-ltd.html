Why It Is Distinctive

First Quantum is not a diversified miner or a pure copper platform. It is a leverage, restart, and political-approval packet where one suspended megamine, one major expansion ramp, and one pressured Zambian asset all interact on a heavily indebted balance sheet. Stockpile processing and recommissioning costs matter as much as grade or commodity price.
