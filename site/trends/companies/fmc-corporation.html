FMC Corporation

Patent cliff and post-Rynaxypyr erosion force manufacturing footprint reset amid 18% revenue decline

Standout figure
New active ingredients (fluindapyr, Isoflex, Dodhylex) generated $200M of 2025 sales, up 54%, as legacy chemistry sales fell 18% YoY
Watch next
Whether new active ingredient ramp (currently $200M of $3.5B revenue) can sustain mid-double-digit growth fast enough to offset legacy portfolio decline and justify the debt load ($4.5B outstanding) without forcing additional asset sales.

Why It Is Distinctive

FMC sits between commodity fertilizers and patent-dependent biotech. Unlike nutrients, crop-protection chemistry erodes faster when patents expire. The company is not waiting for demand recovery; it is actively divesting India operations, consolidating manufacturing footprint via Project Foundation, and reshaping its route to market toward direct large-grower relationships in Brazil. This is a restructuring play, not a cyclical trough.
