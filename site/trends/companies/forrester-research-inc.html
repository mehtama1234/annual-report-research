Forrester Research Inc.

Contract value down 6% in 2025 with $110.7M goodwill impairment but Forrester AI Access launch shows knowledge intermediaries must become AI workflow infrastructure to remain relevant

Standout figure
FY2025 total revenue of $396.9M down from $432.5M with contract value down 6% to $292.4M, but Q2 2026 revenue of $100.2M and GAAP net income of $15.3M show stabilization trajectory
Watch next
Whether Forrester AI Access and workflow integration can stabilize contract value and drive subscription expansion, and whether goodwill impairment has bottomed or whether further adjustments signal deeper structural pressure on the research-services model.

Why It Is Distinctive

Forrester is the knowledge intermediary selling curated judgment and interpreted industry change to enterprises facing technology and business transformation. The company monetizes trusted interpretation rather than broad distribution, but faces pressure to embed research into client workflows and prove operational value rather than only provide informational content.
