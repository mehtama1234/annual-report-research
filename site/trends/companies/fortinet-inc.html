Why It Is Distinctive

Fortinet is not a cloud-only zero-trust story like Zscaler. It sells hardware, edge enforcement, branch gear, and OT security alongside software. The company monetizes convergence: buyers want fewer boxes and fewer vendor relationships. Service revenue ($1.27B Q2) outpaces product revenue, but product cycles can reignite when refresh, AI-adjacent, or OT deployments accelerate.
