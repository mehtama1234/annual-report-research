Fortis Inc.

Cross-border regulated utility holding company monetizing long-duration capital deployment across multiple jurisdictions rather than relying on single-market growth narratives.

Standout figure
2025 revenue $12B, total assets $75B; $28.8B five-year capital plan expected to grow rate base from $42.4B (2025) to $57.9B (2030), a 7% CAGR; Q2 2026 capital expenditures $2.7B on track.
Watch next
Continued capital-plan execution across multiple jurisdictions and approval processes, ability to realize approved projects on schedule without cost overruns, and FX headwinds and hurricane/storm exposure from Caribbean and U.S. portfolio.

Why It Is Distinctive

Fortis is not a single-market utility or a merchant generation case. It is a regulated holding-company platform spread across five Canadian provinces, ten U.S. states, and the Caribbean. The investment case is less about one headline hyperscaler pipeline and more about making a multi-jurisdiction regulated platform compound through repeated approvals, transmission opportunities, and steady dividend growth support. Regulatory diversification is a business model: the company is effectively a portfolio of regulated franchises rather than one monolithic utility.
