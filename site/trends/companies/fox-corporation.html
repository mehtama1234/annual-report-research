Why It Is Distinctive

Fox monetizes scarcity. Live sports, live news, and national events still command time-sensitive viewing and premium advertising. The company uses affiliate fees, retransmission, political cycles, and sports rights to create hybrid economics rather than relying on pure ad sales. Tubi bridges into AVOD without abandoning ad-supported heritage.
