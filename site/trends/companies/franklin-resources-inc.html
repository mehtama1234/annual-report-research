Franklin Resources, Inc.

Hybrid asset manager transitioning from fee-compression pressure through product-mix shift toward alternatives, SMAs, ETFs, and private-markets capabilities.

Standout figure
Q3 2026 alternatives AUM reached record $294.2B; long-term net inflows were positive $18.4B; won-but-unfunded pipeline reached $28.6B, showing strategic traction.
Watch next
Whether Western Asset Management (still a reported drag) can stabilize or separate, success of alternatives and private-markets fundraising, and ability to maintain or improve fee rates through richer product mix.

Why It Is Distinctive

Franklin is not a pure alternatives manager or a narrower active-public-markets house. The business spans mutual funds, institutional accounts, SMAs, ETFs, alternatives, multi-asset, and cash management. The effective investment-management fee rate fell from 41.1 bps (2024) to 40.5 bps (2025), but the company is defending through higher-utility products rather than relying only on legacy active funds. The strategic answer is visible in product mix: emphasis on non-U.S. equity, multi-asset, alternatives, ETFs, SMAs, cash-management scale, and private-markets capabilities.
