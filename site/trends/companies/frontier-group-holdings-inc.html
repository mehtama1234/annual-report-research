Frontier Group Holdings, Inc.

Ultra-low-cost carrier optimized for ancillary extraction and fleet density: not a premium airline, not a network carrier, but aggressive RASM and revenue-per-passenger monetization.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 RASM up 28% to 11.52 cents on 8% higher capacity; ancillary revenue per passenger ~$67.57; Q2 adjusted EBITDA turned positive at ~$3M from negative territory year-ago despite early lease returns.
Watch next
Whether RASM gains can hold as capacity discipline and aircraft returns stabilize supply-demand in low-fare market, and whether the leap from fleet loss to modest profitability can be sustained through 2027 without demand destruction.

Why It Is Distinctive

Frontier is not trying to become a premium airline. It's relentlessly optimizing for ultra-low-cost-carrier economics: high seat density, stage-length management, aggressive ancillary monetization, and fleet productivity. First-class seating and Wi-Fi are margin play, not model reset.
