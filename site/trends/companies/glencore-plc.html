Why It Is Distinctive

Glencore combines conventional mining with a powerful marketing engine that can swing earnings when freight, energy, and commodity markets are dislocated. Unlike Rio Tinto, Vale, or BHP (which focus mainly on assets and logistics), Glencore's trading capability lets it monetize volatility. The H1 2026 results show marketing benefiting from 'materially disrupted energy, freight, and other markets.'
