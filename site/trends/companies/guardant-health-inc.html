Guardant Health Inc.

Precision-oncology information infrastructure: Shield screening platform making cancer blood testing more behavioral, commercially scalable, and accessible than traditional diagnostic pathways.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 Shield tests 66,000 up from Q1 44,000 and Q4 38,000; oncology revenue $219.1M of $335M total; management raised FY2026 revenue guidance to $1.34-$1.36B implying 36-38% growth.
Watch next
Whether Shield can sustain 50%+ quarterly growth after UnitedHealth coverage expansion, and whether oncology revenue per test holds as molecular scope broadens and FDA companion-diagnostic wins intensify competitive pressure.

Why It Is Distinctive

Guardant is not just a liquid-biopsy innovator. It's a hybrid information and screening platform where oncology serves specialists while Shield tries to normalize cancer screening for broader populations through Quest partnerships and UnitedHealth coverage, turning precision medicine into an access and commerce problem.
