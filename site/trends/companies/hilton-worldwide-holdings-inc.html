Hilton Worldwide Holdings Inc.

Asset-light hospitality compounder monetizes franchise fees and pipeline growth without owning most properties.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 9,158 properties and 1,351,351 rooms; Q2 2026 system-wide comparable RevPAR up 3.9% on currency-neutral basis; development pipeline at 541,300 rooms.
Watch next
Whether Select by Hilton and broader brand launches can sustain 6%+ net unit growth, and if premium RevPAR growth holds as market matures.

Why It Is Distinctive

Hilton is an asset-light brand and franchise platform, not a property owner. Value comes from brand portfolio, franchise fees, pipeline depth, and owner demand for Hilton brands. Unlike hotel REITs, Hilton has much lighter balance-sheet burden. Unlike pure lodging operators, Hilton's moat is franchise system and brand equity, not property ownership.
