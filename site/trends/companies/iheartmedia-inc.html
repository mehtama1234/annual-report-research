Services · Broadcasting - Radio

iHeartMedia, Inc.

Legacy radio operator trying to rebuild around digital audio, podcasts, and measurable cross-platform advertising rather than relying only on broadcast scale.

Standout figure
Q1 2026 Digital Audio Group revenue ~$327M, up 18%; podcast revenue ~$147M, up 27%; Multiplatform Group revenue ~$493M, up 4%; Digital Audio Group adjusted EBITDA flat at ~$86.8M.
Watch next
Sustainability of podcast and digital audio growth rate as market matures, management's ability to keep cost discipline while defending leverage ratios, and whether programmatic revenue can reach and sustain $200M run rate.

Why It Is Distinctive

iHeartMedia is not just a radio company defending stale broadcast economics. The business still carries 860+ broadcast stations in ~160 markets, but the cleaner growth story sits in digital audio and podcasts. Management explicitly frames audio as a 'companionship business' where listeners use radio, podcasts, streaming, personalities, social, and events as everyday presence. The company monetizes through one-of-a-kind cross-platform audio advertising with data targeting and attribution measurement. Leverage and liquidity remain pressures, but underlying digital audio momentum shows the business trying to rebuild around measurable formats.
