Imperial Oil Limited

Canada's integrated refiner monetizing bitumen feedstock into domestic fuels and chemical products, with renewable diesel start-up adding lower-emissions optionality

Standout figure
Q2 2026 net income of C$2.190B and refinery throughput of 331,000 bpd despite maintenance turnarounds, with Strathcona renewable diesel facility start-up in 2025
Watch next
Watch whether renewable diesel economics and capacity utilization can improve, and whether downstream refinery leverage holds as heavy-crude differentials tighten post-TMX expansion

Why It Is Distinctive

Imperial is tightly integrated around Kearl, Cold Lake, Syncrude, three Canadian refineries, and Esso/Mobil wholesale distribution. The model absorbs bitumen and synthetic-crude variability through domestic refining rather than monetizing as pure commodity.
