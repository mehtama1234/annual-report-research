Intuitive Surgical, Inc.

Installed base of 11,100+ da Vinci systems and 995 Ion systems growing procedure volumes 16-17% while instruments-and-accessories revenue grows 23%, proving platform stickiness.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 revenue of $2.89B; worldwide procedures grew approximately 17% including da Vinci procedures up 16% and Ion procedures up 39%; instruments and accessories revenue rose 23%, showing strong platform leverage.
Watch next
Ion procedure adoption trajectory and whether it can accelerate meaningful contribution; international expansion opportunity and market penetration in existing geographies; da Vinci 5 adoption and whether new platform features justify customer trade-up spending.

Why It Is Distinctive

Intuitive monetizes not single device sales but installed-base economics: system placements drive recurring procedure growth, which drives instruments-and-accessories demand, which drives service revenue and utilization deepening. The moat is workflow embedment, not product novelty.
