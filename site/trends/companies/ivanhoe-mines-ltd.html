Ivanhoe Mines Ltd.

JV-structured African copper platform where smelter integration and corridor logistics create economics as complex as the ore grade itself

Why It Is Distinctive

Unlike pure copper producers, Ivanhoe depends on financing architecture, smelter ramp-up (currently 60% capacity), sulphuric acid by-product credits, and the Lobito Railway Corridor reliability to translate ore into realized value. The company is a platform where capital-intensive development (Platreef spent $252M in 2025), tax settlement risk, and downstream integration matter as much as copper prices.
