KBR, Inc.

Federal mission contractor with backlog of $16.8B planning 2027 spin-off of Mission Technology Solutions, creating pure-play federal-tech and commercial-infrastructure companies.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 revenue of $7.786B with 64% from federal defense/intel and federal civilian clients; Mission Technology Solutions planned spin targeting January 2027; backlog grew to $16.8B with options reaching $6.3B.
Watch next
MTS spin-off execution timing and stranded-cost exposure; federal budget environment for defense and civilian agencies; commercial and industrial capex trends affecting STS backlog; contract mix and whether fixed-price work becomes more common or remains manageable.

Why It Is Distinctive

KBR is not a generic construction name. It monetizes program complexity, federal mission criticality, defense readiness, and long-cycle client dependence through cost-reimbursable and time-and-materials contracts. The planned MTS spin-off reflects belief that federal and commercial economics deserve separate platforms.
