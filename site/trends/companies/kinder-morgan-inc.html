Why It Is Distinctive

Unlike other upstream or downstream names, Kinder Morgan monetizes the physical bottleneck itself through fee-based contracts with creditworthy customers. The business reads less like commodity exposure and more like infrastructure toll where LNG linkage, storage optimization, and project backlog conversion drive value independent of whether crude or gas prices are rising or falling.
