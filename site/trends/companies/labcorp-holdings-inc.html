Why It Is Distinctive

Labcorp operates as infrastructure, not episodic care. The company monetizes recurring sample volume, central-lab capacity, trial support, and provider relationships. It participates in both day-to-day testing and outsourced drug-development workflow, making it essential to both modern healthcare delivery and drug creation.
