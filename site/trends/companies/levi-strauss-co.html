Why It Is Distinctive

Levi is using a durable cultural brand to own more of the consumer relationship through direct stores, eCommerce, and category expansion (Beyond Yoga acquisition). Unlike wholesale-dependent apparel names, four straight quarters of high-single-digit organic growth. Unlike pure premium, Levi maintains mass accessibility while operating like a branded relationship platform.
