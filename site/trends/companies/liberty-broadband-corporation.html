Liberty Broadband Corporation

Charter voting stake at 25.01% with ongoing debt refinancing and merger-agreement restrictions shows connectivity value capture sits above operating company through ownership, leverage, and capital-structure engineering

Standout figure
Q2 2026 total stockholders' equity of $3.748B with total debt of $1.223B after paying down debentures and using Charter term loan support, Charter voting-power control maintained at 25.01%
Watch next
Whether the post-GCI divestiture charter makes Liberty Broadband a cleaner pure Charter story, and whether margin-loan refinancing and merger timelines can be navigated without forcing asset sales or unwinding the strategic Charter relationship.

Why It Is Distinctive

Liberty Broadband is analytically useful precisely because it is a levered wrapper around Charter's recurring cable economics operated through exchangeable debt, margin financing, voting caps, and transaction timing rather than through direct service delivery. This exposes how much value capture can sit in financial and control structures above the operating company.
