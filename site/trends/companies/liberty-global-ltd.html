Liberty Global Ltd.

International fixed-mobile convergence holding company monetizes connectivity through operating assets, JVs, and portfolio optimization rather than through simple subscriber growth.

Standout figure
Fiscal 2025 revenue $4.8785B with operating cash flow $1.2111B; Q1-Q2 2026 realized $773.8M in capital-recycling proceeds (EdgeConneX, UPC Slovakia sales) pushing cash to $2.4186B.
Watch next
How much value gets realized through infrastructure monetization versus traditional operating growth, and whether synergies from portfolio actions materialize.

Why It Is Distinctive

Liberty Global is a connectivity holding company as much as an operating company. Value comes through Virgin Media O2 (JV), VodafoneZiggo (JV), stake sales (EdgeConneX, ITV), and portfolio simplification. Unlike pure operators, Liberty depends on JV governance and asset-sale timing. Fixed-mobile convergence is central, but competitive and regulatory pressure continue eroding ARPU and subscriber counts.
