Macy's Inc.

Macy's Media Network and Marketplace are lifting attached-revenue layers while Bloomingdale's and Bluemercury show luxury and beauty outpacing the core nameplate; this is portfolio repair masquerading as single-banner health

Standout figure
Q1 2026 Bloomingdale's comparable sales +10.2% and Bluemercury +6.4% vs. Macy's nameplate +1.6%; Media Network revenue $38M in Q1 2026, credit card revenue $172M
Watch next
Macy's nameplate comps trend vs. go-forward basis growth, Media Network revenue acceleration, credit-card economics sustainability, whether luxury and beauty momentum can offset the legacy core

Why It Is Distinctive

Macy's is using portfolio widening, media monetization, marketplace expansion, and financial-adjacent revenue to repair what is a structurally pressured middle-format retailer. The company is explicit that stronger spend shows up disproportionately in luxury, beauty, and new interface layers rather than in core Macy's merchandise traffic.

The Department Store Was Always a Bet on the Middle Class—and It Stopped Working

  • Q1 2026 comparable sales: core Macy's nameplate grew only 1.6%, while Bloomingdale's grew 10.2% and Bluemercury grew 6.4%, showing the recovery is concentrated in luxury-positioned banners serving higher-income customers
  • Full-year 2025 net sales reached $21.8B with only 1.5% comparable sales growth; media revenue ($188M) and credit-card revenue ($669M) now carry higher-margin responsibility than core merchandise, showing business model widening away from traditional retail
  • Macy's Media Network revenue reached $188M in full-year 2025, growing from $38M in Q1 2026, revealing a shift where customer data monetization increasingly supplements merchandise margin as core business

Macy's Autopsy: What a Department Store Does When the Middle Disappears

  • Macy's nameplate comparable sales increased 1.6% in Q1 2026, while Bloomingdale's increased 10.2% and Bluemercury increased 6.4%
  • Full-year 2025 net sales reached $21.8B; credit card revenues net reached $669M; Macy's Media Network revenue net reached $188M
  • The company ended 2025 with $1.2B of cash and cash equivalents and returned $448M to shareholders
  • Q3 2025 described as strongest quarter in 13 quarters, with Macy's Reimagine 125 locations showing 2.3% growth on an owned basis and 3.2% on owned-plus-licensed-plus-marketplace basis
  • Full-year 2025 comparable sales increased 1.5% overall, with go-forward business growth cited as key metric
