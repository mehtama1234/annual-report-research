Maplebear Inc. dba Instacart

Grocery-demand aggregation and retailer-enablement platform is scaling retail-media and data monetization faster than order-delivery revenue, transforming economics from commission-based to subscription-plus-advertising.

Standout figure
FY2025 GTV $37.224B; total revenue $3.742B with advertising and other revenue $1.065B (28% of total); orders 338.8M; Q1 2026 first quarter to surpass $10B GTV and $1B total revenue; adjusted EBITDA $1.087B.
Watch next
Whether first-party data monetization can continue growing faster than order delivery and whether retailer embeddedness creates defensibility or commoditizes the service role.

Why It Is Distinctive

Instacart is not just an on-demand grocery app. It is embedded inside retailer ecommerce through Storefront, in-store through Caper and FoodStorm, and mediated through pricing parity and loyalty integration. The platform simultaneously aggregates customer demand, powers retailer-owned channels, and monetizes brands through Carrot Ads (310+ partners, 9,000+ active brands). This middle-layer control creates a different economics profile than pure marketplace.
