Mattel, Inc.

Franchise-and-brand platform monetizing toys, entertainment, licensing, and digital games as reinforcing flywheel rather than as isolated product lines.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 net sales up 10% to $1,125M; vehicles (Hot Wheels) grew 14% to $463M; action figures, games, and other grew 35% to $358M, driven by theatrical-release-linked products and games.
Watch next
Whether self-published mobile games can scale to meaningful contribution levels, integration of Mattel163 and quality of digital titles, and whether theatrical-release strategy remains sustainable when films underperform.

Why It Is Distinctive

Mattel is not strongest when read as generic toy demand. It is stronger when read as IP-portfolio and entertainment-extension operator where toys, theatrical releases, digital games, licensing, adult-fan collections, and creator platforms reinforce one another. The full Mattel163 acquisition and $150M strategic investment show the company pivoting from product-focused to brand-ecosystem strategy. Barbie, Hot Wheels, and American Girl operate across toys, media, and digital.
