McDonald's Corporation

Franchise-and-loyalty platform monetizing everyday food occasions through value positioning, digital ordering, and scaled membership engagement rather than relying only on unit economics.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 global comparable sales increased 1.3%; trailing-twelve-month systemwide sales to loyalty members increased 20% to $40B; 220M 90-day active loyalty users as of quarter-end.
Watch next
Loyalty penetration in non-70-market geographies and ability to accelerate membership in mature markets, sustainability of value-pricing strategy amid input-cost inflation, and franchise-unit health as affordability pressure persists globally.

Why It Is Distinctive

McDonald's is not strongest when read as a QSR operator with passive demand. The company explicitly discloses loyalty metrics as core performance indicators, showing that digital customer relationships and membership engagement now shape the operating narrative. Global comparable sales increased 3.1% in 2025; systemwide sales to loyalty members reached $40B and 220M active users. The franchise model lets corporate benefit from rent, royalties, and systemwide sales growth while digital engagement increases traffic and mix. Value positioning and loyalty integration matter more than premium product innovation.
