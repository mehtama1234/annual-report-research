Q1 FY2027 adjusted EPS of $9.93 up 20% shows healthcare distribution is becoming higher-value services platform around specialty, oncology, and biopharma rather than bulk prescription commodity

Standout figure

Watch next
Whether specialty, oncology, and multispecialty can maintain double-digit growth as primary care and bulk generic distribution mature, and whether the Apollo transaction and Medical-Surgical Solutions refinement produce cleaner, higher-margin business segments.

Why It Is Distinctive

McKesson is the healthcare middle layer that connects manufacturers, pharmacies, providers, and health systems through drug distribution, technology, and service workflows. The company matters because it shows how healthcare demand is mediated through logistics, reimbursement-adjacent infrastructure, specialty handling, and provider-facing operational support rather than only through direct provider-patient relationships.
