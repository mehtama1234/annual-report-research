Meta Platforms, Inc.

Dominant consumer-attention and advertising platform investing heavily in AI infrastructure and capability to defend and extend behavioral-loop monetization.

Standout figure
FY2025 revenue $200.966B, net income $60.458B, diluted EPS $23.49; capital expenditures $72.22B; Q2 2026 capital expenditures $31.08B, showing sustained multi-year AI infrastructure commitment.
Watch next
Sustainability of capex intensity as AI infrastructure spending moderates or stabilizes, competitive threats to attention dominance from emerging platforms, and regulatory outcomes affecting data use and algorithm control.

Why It Is Distinctive

Meta is not only selling ads to a large audience. The company monetizes everyday attention, business demand, and AI investment concentration. Recommendation quality, AI-assisted monetization, messaging, creator surfaces, and future hardware ambitions all sit on top of a huge installed behavioral base (3.58B DAP in December 2025). The business captures daily behaviors as direct commercial asset and is explicitly shifting more cash flow into datacenters, compute, and talent concentration. Regulation, legal proceedings, privacy pressure, and access restrictions are live earnings variables.
