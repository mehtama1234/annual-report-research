MetLife, Inc.

Global liability-and-asset management system monetizing longevity risk transfer, pension de-risking, and disciplined capital return at scale.

Standout figure
2025 adjusted EPS of $8.89, adjusted ROE of 16.0%, and nearly $4.4B returned to shareholders; 2Q26 adjusted EPS of $2.43, showing earnings consistency.
Watch next
Investment portfolio performance and duration management in rising-rate environments, pension risk transfer volume growth, and sustainability of capital-return guidance amid regulatory and economic pressures.

Why It Is Distinctive

MetLife is less a simple insurer than a global liability-and-asset management system whose strength depends on balance-sheet flexibility, investment income, pension risk transfer, and disciplined capital release. The company monetizes the need for retirement security, protection, and pension de-risking through underwriting, spread income, investment results, and longevity/morbidity management. Compared with regional banks, it carries less direct credit-cycle visibility and more sensitivity to investment income, reserving, product mix, and shareholder-return capacity.
