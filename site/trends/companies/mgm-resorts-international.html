Watch next

Why It Is Distinctive

MGM is not a pure Las Vegas or gaming company; it is a diversified destination and gaming portfolio where geographic and product mix offset one another. When Las Vegas is soft, regional gaming and Macau provide cushion. Digital wagering and BetMGM grow independently. This portfolio approach allows management to defend returns and distributions even when one segment weakens. The company is also capital-intensive (MGM Osaka under development for 2030), which limits financial flexibility but creates long-term growth optionality.
