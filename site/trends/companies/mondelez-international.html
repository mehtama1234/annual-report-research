Consumer Goods · Confectioners

Mondelez International

Q2 2026 showed volume recovery and 4% dividend raise, but unprecedented cocoa cost headwinds in 2025 proved that snacking indulgence demand is resilient—profitability depends on pricing power.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 organic revenue grew 4.3% but volume/mix fell -3.7%; Q2 2026 volume/mix turned positive at +0.7%, signaling cost absorption easing and consumer tolerance stabilizing.
Watch next
Cocoa price stabilization and whether margin recovery outpaces cost savings in H2 2026. Consumer demand in developed markets (Europe in particular) remains soft; pricing power cannot offset volume loss indefinitely.

Why It Is Distinctive

MDLZ is not Coca-Cola (routine beverage) or PepsiCo (broad convenience). It is a branded indulgence-and-affordable-treat platform (Oreo, Cadbury, Ritz, LU) where cocoa and other raw-material costs are not background noise—they directly compress profitability. Emerging Markets remain a growth buffer for developed-market softness.
