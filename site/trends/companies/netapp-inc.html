NetApp, Inc.

Hybrid-cloud and data-platform company monetizing control, portability, and resilience across storage hardware, software, and cloud services rather than only storage capacity.

Standout figure
FY2026 revenue $6.925B, services revenue $3.731B (exceeding product revenue of $3.194B); gross margin 70.1%; operating cash flow $2.067B; free cash flow $1.869B.
Watch next
Public Cloud revenue growth rate and ability to convert cloud-services demand into durable margin, channel-dependence management as 76% of FY2026 revenue came through indirect pathways, and competitive positioning against both traditional infrastructure vendors and hyperscalers bundling more capabilities.

Why It Is Distinctive

NetApp is not a pure hardware vendor or pure software company. It monetizes the control layer that makes data portable, governable, and sticky across on-premises and hyperscale environments. Services revenue exceeding product revenue ($3.731B vs $3.194B) shows the business is not only monetizing hardware refresh cycles; it is monetizing installed-base dependence and ongoing data complexity. The hybrid-cloud and public-cloud segments show where value increasingly sits: making infrastructure interoperable rather than forcing customers to choose.
