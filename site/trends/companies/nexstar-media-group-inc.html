Evidence Trail

National Scale Works. Local Scale Doesn't.

  • FY2025 net revenue of $4.949B and $1.561B adjusted EBITDA (31.5% margin), but built on $2.924B of distribution/retransmission fees rather than reader subscriptions.
  • Political advertising swung by $233M between Q4 2025 and Q1 2026, underscoring that local-TV cash depends on election cycles and carriage fees, not paid journalism.

The Retransmission Fee as the Last Moat

  • FY2025 distribution revenue of $2.924B exceeded advertising revenue of $1.959B by 49%, showing business model is fee extraction not journalism
  • Q2 2026 distribution revenue of $1.116B remained stable despite continued MVPD subscriber losses, proving fee stability is not dependent on viewership
  • Distribution agreements covering 60% of subscriber base were renewed with 'strong terms,' showing pricing power persists even as MVPD base shrinks
  • Q4 2025 political advertising collapsed to $21M from $254M in prior year, but net revenue remained stable due to distribution revenue ballast

The Last Appointment: Live Sports Rights as the Scarcest Inventory in Media

  • Distribution revenue of $2.924B in FY2025 represents 59% of total revenue and remains stable retransmission base because MVPD customers cannot cancel without losing live sports access
  • Political advertising dropped $233M year-over-year in Q4 2025 (from $254M to $21M) due to non-election year, yet distribution revenue remained at $720M, showing sports/content-driven retransmission value

  • 2025 net revenue $4.949B; distribution revenue $2.924B (59%), advertising revenue $1.959B (40%)
