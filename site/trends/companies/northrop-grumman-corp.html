Why It Is Distinctive

Northrop is not a commercial industrial company; it is a defense-modernization and deterrence-capability allocator. The backlog is not just order intake—it reflects geopolitical risk perception and allied spending commitments. B-21 Raider and Sentinel production scaling are real manufacturing puzzles (not just design), which means Northrop's value sits in execution capacity, supply-chain management, and the ability to sustain complex program schedules. Public markets reward this differently than commercial capex, because backlog has policy backstop.
