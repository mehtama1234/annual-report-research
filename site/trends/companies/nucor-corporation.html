Nucor Corporation

Domestic steel operator pivots from commodity exposure into value-added, contract-heavy, infrastructure-linked margins.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 record steel mill shipments; Q2 2026 adjusted net earnings $1.11B on record domestic capacity utilization; 85% of sheet sales are contract-backed.
Watch next
Federal trade policy stability and whether infrastructure capex wave holds; recycled-scrap availability and energy cost trends.

Why It Is Distinctive

Nucor is not a simple commodity mill. It recycles scrap, favors contract customers over spot markets, and is investing heavily ($2.5-3.4B capex annually) into data centers, grid modernization, and downstream fabrication. Margin quality is structurally better than pure commodity exposure.
