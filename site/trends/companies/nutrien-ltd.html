Nutrien Ltd.

Potash automation and retail scale create a durable agricultural platform more resilient than pure commodity exposure.

Standout figure
49% of potash ore tonnes mined using automation in 2025, paired with 1,800+ retail locations serving 600,000+ customer accounts.

Why It Is Distinctive

Nutrien combines upstream mining automation with a massive retail agronomy network that acts as a buffer to commodity price swings. The company is not just selling fertilizer; it is selling integrated solutions, crop consulting, and financing through proprietary products and relationships that make the retail business higher-quality than pure volume trading.
