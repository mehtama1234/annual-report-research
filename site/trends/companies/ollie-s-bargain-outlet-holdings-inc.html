Consumer Goods · Discount, Variety Stores

Ollie's Bargain Outlet Holdings, Inc.

Closeout-driven treasure-hunt retail is compounding through store growth (record 86 stores opened in FY2025) and loyalty expansion while the broader consumer weakens—bargain discovery beats trade-down necessity.

Standout figure
Fiscal 2025 net sales $2.649B with 3.7% comp growth; Ollie's Army loyalty grew 12.1% to 17.0M members; Q1 FY2026 gross margin expanded 80 basis points to 41.9%
Watch next
Whether margin sustainability holds as tariff refunds normalize and whether the comparable-store growth rate can stay above 3% through 2026.

Why It Is Distinctive

Ollie's monetizes opportunistic closeout and excess-inventory sourcing rather than branded off-price appeal. The treasure-hunt model and 'good stuff cheap' positioning sit between TJX/Ross (apparel-heavy off-price) and Dollar chains (low-ticket convenience). Tariffs create tailwind through excess-inventory cycles rather than only headwind.
