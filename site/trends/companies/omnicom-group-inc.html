Why It Is Distinctive

Omnicom is not creative-house or media-buying simplicity. It is a control layer that helps clients navigate fragmented media, identity, targeting, and measurement at scale. The merger with IPG creates platform scale and data density that smaller or narrower agencies cannot match.
