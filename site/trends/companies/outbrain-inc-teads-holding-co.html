Watch next
Whether CTV Ensemble and EngageOS can turn publisher relationships into higher-margin recurring revenue, and whether the company's gross-profit growth trajectory can sustain if brand-advertising budgets soften faster than expected.

Why It Is Distinctive

Teads/Outbrain is middleware between publishers, advertisers, and streaming surfaces where value accrues by coordinating inventory, targeting, and measurement even without owning content franchises or consumer billing. The company benefits from media fragmentation and publisher need for yield optimization, but suffers when weaker SME and direct-response demand exposes cyclicality in fragmented markets.
