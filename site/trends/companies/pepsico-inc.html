PepsiCo Inc.

Q2 2026 showed momentum in international (7% organic growth) and convenient foods volume, but U.S. affordability pressure persists—56% food, 42% beverage, 44% international gives resilience but not auto-growth.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 free cash flow $8.2B with $7.6B dividends paid; net revenue $93.9B; Walmart and affiliates represent 14% of revenue, showing retailer power concentration.
Watch next
North America affordability and whether brand restaging (Alani Nu, poppi) can regain share. Retailer consolidation and private-label pressure remain headwinds to margin expansion.

Why It Is Distinctive

PEP is a staples compounder but not a growth machine. Productivity savings now fund affordability moves and reinvestment; North America is the stress point where value and convenience still matter but household budgets are tightening. International breadth and the balanced food/beverage mix provide stability, not momentum.

  • Management message across 2025-2026 quarters repeatedly states that productivity improvements help 'protect earnings quality' and fund 'reinvestment' even as consumer budgets tighten; automation substituting for labor in manufacturing and logistics
