Delaware Basin low-cost manufacturing model delivered $1.6B adjusted free cash flow in 2025 while consolidating 30k net acres via 700 transactions, replacing 100% of developed inventory

Watch next
Whether Waha gas dislocation (trading as low as -$9.52/Mcf in 2026 Q2) will force sustained high-GOR curtailment that limits oil production growth, and whether the company's transport optionality (700+ MMcf/d firm capacity planned by 2027) will materialize and sustain realized gas premiums.

Why It Is Distinctive

Permian Resources is not just another upstream driller; it is a Delaware Basin manufacturing and land-consolidation machine. The company treats shale as a scalable production system where bolt-on M&A (not mega-deals), D&C efficiency, working-interest improvement, and Waha gas management are core value drivers. Unlike diversified independents, Permian is hyper-focused on one basin, which creates execution leverage and allows aggressive capital-return discipline (low leverage, dividend raises) even under commodity pressure.
