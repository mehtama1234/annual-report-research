Philip Morris International

Smoke-free category migration is working operationally: 42% of revenue now from IQOS, ZYN, and related products, and nearly 43M adult consumers using them globally.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 smoke-free net revenues grew 11.7%; smoke-free accounted for ~42% of total net revenues; smoke-free products available in 109 markets with over 43M legal-age consumers using them as of year-end 2025.
Watch next
Whether smoke-free volume and margin can fully offset combustibles decline and whether marketing claims around reduced-harm products survive regulatory and litigation pressure.

Why It Is Distinctive

PMI is not a declining tobacco relic defending cigarettes. It is a habit-system company actively migrating user consumption toward higher-margin smoke-free products without losing scale or pricing power. The cash engine stays intact while the revenue base shifts toward lower-harm, higher-growth categories. This is a regulated transition story, not pure market contraction.
