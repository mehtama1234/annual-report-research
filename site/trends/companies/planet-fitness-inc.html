Q2 2026 total revenue of $365.2M with 23 new clubs opened in quarter bringing total to 2,930, with Q2 franchise-heavy expansion, recurring dues, and equipment placement sustaining adjusted EBITDA of $152.8M
Watch next

Why It Is Distinctive

Planet Fitness is a blind-spot fit because taxonomy misses that this is a recurring-fee, membership-conditioned, low-friction wellness access system rather than just recreation. The franchise-heavy expansion model, $15 entry price point, and non-intimidating positioning create mass-market wellness participation through affordability and accessibility rather than prestige or elite positioning.
