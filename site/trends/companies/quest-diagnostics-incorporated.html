Why It Is Distinctive

Quest sits at intersection of medical necessity and consumerized health behavior. Testing is moving upstream (earlier detection), broader in menu (advanced assays), and more consumer-directed (questhealth.com). The company controls diagnostic routing and testing-volume layer across hospitals, physicians, health plans, employers, and consumers.
