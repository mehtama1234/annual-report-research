Why It Is Distinctive

Realty Income is the less glamorous but structurally steadier retail-property case compared with destination malls like Simon. The company monetizes long-duration contractual cash flow from individual tenant relationships rather than merchandising or experiential traffic. It is one of the lane's most utility-like vehicles where economics depend on occupancy, lease duration, tenant quality, and funding access rather than on participation in discretionary consumer activity.
