Regeneron Pharmaceuticals Inc.

Q2 2026 revenue up 17% to $4.3B with Dupixent dominating ($6.0B annualized pace)—Regeneron is a concentration play, not a diversified pipeline.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 revenue $14.3B; Dupixent and EYLEA franchises drive the lion's share; Q2 2026 GAAP EPS $12.23 boosted by acquired IPR&D intensity and dividend/buyback initiation in 2025.
Watch next
Dupixent label expansion velocity and competitive positioning (especially in atopic disease markets). Any EYLEA slowdown or Dupixent peak-sales signal would directly test the concentration thesis. Pipeline conversion (Regeneron's own development programs) is the long-term risk.

Why It Is Distinctive

REGN is extremely profitable but extremely concentrated. Dupixent expansion (atopic dermatitis, asthma, food allergies) and EYLEA HD (retinal disease) are the core earnings drivers. Pipeline risk is not abstract; acquired IPR&D spending and the need to refresh blockbuster economics remain live P&L variables. This is a specialist franchise company, not a diversified pharma platform.
