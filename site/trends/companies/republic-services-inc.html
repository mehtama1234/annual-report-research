Republic Services, Inc.

Route-density environmental-services network with pricing power is monetizing both recurring collection and emerging sustainability projects without relying on volume growth.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 revenue $4.430B with adjusted EBITDA $1.423B at 32.1% margin; pricing in excess of cost inflation; full-year 2026 guidance raised to $17.2-17.3B revenue and $5.525-5.55B adjusted EBITDA.
Watch next
Whether pricing discipline can hold as waste volumes soften and whether sustainability-project returns justify the capex being deployed.

Why It Is Distinctive

Republic is not a defensive trash-collection story. It is a route-density pricing and sustainability-asset platform. The company is monetizing landfill, recycling, hazardous-waste, field services, and renewable-natural-gas projects as parts of one integrated network. Acquisitions and density matter more than volume. The business model depends on local permit position and scale rather than on consumer discretion.
