Services · Apparel Stores

Ross Stores, Inc.

Off-price value retailer posting FY2025 sales of $22.8B (up 8%) with 5% comp growth driven by customer traffic, compelling bargains, and disciplined inventory management.

Standout figure
FY2025 sales of $22.8B up 8% with net income of $2.1B and EPS of $6.61; Q1 2026 sales increased 21% with comp growth of 17% and operating margin of 13.4% due to strong traffic and merchandise leverage.
Watch next
Tariff impact on sourcing costs and whether the off-price model can absorb tariffs better than traditional retail; brand-name supplier availability and closeout inventory as other retailers reduce excess; customer traffic sustainability in more stable consumer environment.

Why It Is Distinctive

Ross succeeds through off-price value positioning and branded-bargain sourcing rather than brand ownership or ecosystem scale. The moat is execution: merchandise assortment quality, customer engagement through marketing, and inventory discipline in capturing closeout availability.
