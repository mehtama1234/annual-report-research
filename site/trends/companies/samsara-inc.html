Samsara Inc.

Connected operations platform reaching $1.991B ARR in Q1 FY2027 with $432M net new ARR in FY2026, powered by labor-capacity constraints and operational AI demand in physical industries.

Standout figure
Q1 FY2027 ARR of $1.991B; full-year FY2026 ended Jan 31 with revenue of $1.619B and non-GAAP operating margin of 17%; company frames next growth layer around Operational AI agents automating work and unlocking worker capacity.
Watch next
Operational AI and agent adoption pace and whether customers can prove measurable productivity gains; customer concentration and whether vertical expansion reduces dependence on large transportation/logistics base; competitive intensity from enterprise-software incumbents and point-solution vendors.

Why It Is Distinctive

Samsara is not digital-office software. It is a control layer for physical operations—fleets, equipment, sites, frontline workers—monetizing data collection, workflow visibility, and now AI-driven automation. The moat depends on customer operational ROI and labor-productivity proof rather than feature adoption.
