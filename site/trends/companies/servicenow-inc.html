ServiceNow, Inc.

Workflow-orchestration and AI-governance platform converts enterprise process complexity into recurring software margin without physical infrastructure burden.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 revenue $3.987B with cRPO $13.20B; Now Assist ACV crossed $1B with customers spending $1M+ growing 130% year-over-year; subscription margin ~31% non-GAAP.
Watch next
Whether Now Assist ACV growth of 130% can sustain as enterprise AI pilots become standard operations, and if broad platform expansion dilutes focus or strengthens moat.

Why It Is Distinctive

ServiceNow monetizes enterprise workflow routing, approvals, incidents, and now AI operationalization—not devices, transport, or compute. It is a control layer for how organizations route work, not a platform for delivering work. Unlike Oracle, ServiceNow takes almost no physical capex burden. Unlike Palo Alto, ServiceNow is wider in use case but less specific in security focus.
