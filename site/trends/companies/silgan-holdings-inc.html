Why It Is Distinctive

Silgan is rigid packaging and closure control, not broad packaging. The business spans high-value dispensing systems, specialty closures, metal food containers, and custom plastic—each with different margin and volume profiles. Pet-food and food-can demand anchor the economics; discretionary categories (cosmetics closures) are upside.
