Simon Property Group Inc.

Premium retail REIT compounds through destination quality and occupancy resilience, not on retail transformation narrative.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 domestic occupancy 96.5%; Q1 2026 Real Estate FFO $3.45 per diluted share with 95.8% domestic occupancy and guidance raised.
Watch next
Whether premium-destination thesis can sustain if consumer participation softens, and if luxury-only focus risks missing broader retail disruption.

Why It Is Distinctive

Simon monetizes physical participation in premium retail destinations (top tier malls and outlets), not generic retail space. Occupancy above 95% shows sustained tenant demand and customer traffic. Unlike struggling mall REITs, Simon's asset quality and tenant mix defend rent. Unlike brokers, Simon is highly capital intensive and exposed to tenant health cycles.
