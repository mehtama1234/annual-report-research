Sirius XM Holdings Inc.

Q2 2026 free cash flow $593M (+48% YoY) with record low 1.4% churn; revenue $2.16B (+1%)—Sirius XM is a hybrid audio platform where subscriber relationship stability matters more than audience growth.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 revenue $8.558B (SiriusXM segment $6.417B, Pandora/Off-Platform $2.141B); adjusted EBITDA $2.665B; Q2 2026 guidance raised to $8.525B revenue, $2.625B adjusted EBITDA, $1.375B free cash flow.
Watch next
Churn trajectory and trial-to-paid conversion; if subscriber retention deteriorates, the whole model weakens despite Pandora ad strength. Market sentiment on audio consumption habits and macro ad spending will shape Pandora performance even as SiriusXM base holds.

Why It Is Distinctive

SIRI is not pure ad-supported radio facing cyclical pressure. It operates ~93% subscription/equipment in SiriusXM (captured customer billing) and ~75% advertising in Pandora. The hybrid model means direct relationships + ad monetization coexist. Low churn (1.4%), large trial funnel (~7.5M), and 255M combined monthly audience provide scale without requiring explosive growth. Podcast upside (41% growth in FY2025) is incremental.
