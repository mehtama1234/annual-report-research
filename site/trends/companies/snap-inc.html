Why It Is Distinctive

Snap owns massive user attention but monetizes through advertising and emerging direct revenue layers (subscriptions reached 24M users in 2025). Unlike search or infrastructure platforms, Snap's moat remains contested and execution-dependent. User scale does not guarantee pricing power or defensible cash flows without adjacent monetization.
