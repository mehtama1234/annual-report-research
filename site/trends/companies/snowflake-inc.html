Why It Is Distinctive

Snowflake is not an analytics or data-warehouse tool; it is enterprise data control and AI-workflow infrastructure. The company monetizes the governed layer where enterprises consolidate data, manage access and interoperability, run applications, and attach AI agents and model workflows to trusted context. Unlike Oracle (database-legacy burden) or ServiceNow (workflow-governance focus), Snowflake sits at the data-plane toll. Management's explicit 'agentic enterprise' framing and Cortex Agents positioning prove this is strategic positioning toward AI systems operating on trusted enterprise data.
