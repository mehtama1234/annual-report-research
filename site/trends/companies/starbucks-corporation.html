Why It Is Distinctive

Starbucks operates a stored-value and prepaid-rewards system where unredeemed card balances and Stars sit in deferred revenue, creating a working-capital moat. The turnaround is not only about traffic; it is about rebuilding service capacity and customer habit while managing labor cost and inflation.
