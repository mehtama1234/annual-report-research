Why It Is Distinctive

Stryker is a hospital capital-equipment and procedure-workflow company, distinct from distributed provider networks, drug makers, or payers. The company embeds robotics (Mako), enabling technologies, and instruments into surgeon and hospital systems, creating recurring installed-base value. The Q1 cyber incident and Q2 recovery prove that even strong operational franchises face disruption risk, but also that procedure demand is resilient when capital systems prove themselves in workflows.
