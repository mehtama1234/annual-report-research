Sun Country Airlines Holdings, Inc.

Hybrid airline-and-cargo operator dynamically reallocates aircraft and crew across scheduled service, charter, and outsourced Amazon flying rather than competing on loyalty or premium status.

Standout figure
Q1 2026 cargo revenue up 64% YoY to $46.1M; passenger revenue flat at $285.3M; company flew 20 cargo aircraft for Amazon by 2025-12-31 and intentionally reduced scheduled-service departures by 14%; 13 consecutive profitable quarters.
Watch next
Whether the Allegiant merger closes and how integration affects the hybrid model; also whether cargo flying can sustain if Amazon reroutes logistics or demand softens.

Why It Is Distinctive

Sun Country is important because it tests how far an airline can go without thick loyalty and premium economics. The moat is not customer love or status accumulation but the ability to redeploy aircraft and crew across multiple revenue streams. Amazon cargo concentration shows the model depends on embedded logistics relationships more than diverse travel demand.
