Why It Is Distinctive

Sunoco is distinct from other refining-and-marketing names because it is not primarily about crack spreads and product margins. The business is about wholesale fuel distribution, dealer networks, pipeline systems, terminal throughput, and storage—a complete movement-and-delivery stack that monetizes gallon volume and supply-chain control rather than just refining economics. Even refinery downtime can be absorbed by the distribution and logistics network.
