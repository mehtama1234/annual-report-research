Sunrun Inc.

Residential solar-and-storage platform monetizes household energy ownership through subscriptions, batteries, and distributed grid-edge assets

Standout figure
Q2 2026 record storage attachment rate 74%; contracted net earning assets $3.677B; Aggregate Subscriber Value $1.175B with 1.206M customers
Watch next
Whether storage pricing and grid-services participation can expand faster than customer-acquisition costs rise; whether affiliate-channel pause and direct-sales ramp stabilize customer economics

Why It Is Distinctive

Sunrun is not a rooftop contractor. It owns an installed base of household energy systems, increasingly battery-backed, and monetizes recurring customer relationships plus grid-services participation. Storage attachment rates climbing from 71% to 74% show batteries are becoming central, not optional. Financing, safe-harbor timing, and tax-equity execution still constrain cash generation.
