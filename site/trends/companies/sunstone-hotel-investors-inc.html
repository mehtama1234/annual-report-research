Sunstone Hotel Investors, Inc.

Concentrated luxury and upper-upscale hotel owner monetizing renovation-driven value creation and asset recycling, not pure demand appreciation

Standout figure
Q2 2026 RevPAR up 9.3% to $263.61 and Andaz Miami Beach contributed materially to quarterly growth, but Sunstone also sold Hilton New Orleans and is managing disposition timing
Watch next
Watch whether renovated properties (Andaz Miami Beach, Wailea) can sustain strong RevPAR growth after initial ramp, and whether Sunstone's capital recycling and buyback strategy can overcome concentration risk in a weaker travel environment

Why It Is Distinctive

Sunstone is neither a scale-heavy lodging REIT like Host nor a fee-based operator. It is a small high-end portfolio owner dependent on renovation execution, destination and occasion-driven demand, and discounted-valuation share repurchases.
