Synchrony Financial

Merchant-linked point-of-sale financing model now moves average active accounts back to growth after Q2 inflection, showing consumer demand for embedded credit remains durable

Standout figure
Q2 2026 $49.8B purchase volume up 8% with average active accounts of 68.3M slightly positive and net interest margin of 15.08%, up 30 basis points, while credit quality maintained at 5.5%-6.0% net charge-off range
Watch next
Whether average active account growth can sustain upward momentum and new account additions stay healthy, and whether higher consumer delinquency in stressed segments forces net charge-off rates above the stated 5.5%-6.0% long-term target.

Why It Is Distinctive

Synchrony monetizes recurring consumer credit through partner relationships and point-of-sale integration rather than through network tolls or premium card status. The company helps merchants and providers close transactions in retail, home, health, auto, and local-services settings where financing availability itself changes demand. This is different from both generic bank lending and pure card networks.
