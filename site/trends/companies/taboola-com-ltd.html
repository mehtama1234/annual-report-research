Why It Is Distinctive

Taboola monetizes open-web fragmentation by aggregating advertiser intent and publisher distribution across 600M+ daily active users without owning the underlying content. The company positions itself as a performance-advertising alternative to search and social by offering measurable customer acquisition and outcome-driven budgeting through Realize platform.

The Replacement That Has Not Arrived

  • Taboola reached $1.912B in full-year 2025 revenue with $713.5M in ex-TAC Gross Profit by sitting between 10,000+ publishers and advertisers, taking a middle cut without creating new reporting
  • Company reaches 600M+ daily active users across publishers and OEMs specifically by redirecting advertising demand away from owned media toward distributed surfaces, fragmenting the publisher base

The Open-Internet Middleware Scramble

  • Full-year 2025 revenue reached $1.912 billion; ex-TAC Gross Profit reached $713.5 million; but Taboola kept $215.5 million in adjusted EBITDA, meaning the company extracted significant spread from publisher traffic without funding publisher operations
  • Q2 2026 ex-TAC Gross Profit grew 11.8% while reported revenue grew only 2.4%, showing the company is extracting greater value from publisher inventory even as publisher monetization opportunities narrow
  • Company reaches over 600M daily active users across publishers and OEMs; this represents the audience scale that could fund journalism but does not because Taboola monetizes distribution without funding content

The Race Against Decay: Print to Digital

  • Q2 2026 revenue was $476.8M (up 2.4%); ex-TAC Gross Profit was $192.4M (up 11.8%); adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 28.8% from 26.2%; net income improved from prior-year loss
