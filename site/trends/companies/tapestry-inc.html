Why It Is Distinctive

Tapestry is not mall-apparel or performance-wellness. It is emotional-connection and brand-heat concentration in handbags/leathergoods through Coach and Kate Spade. The company explicitly recruits Gen Z, controls distribution through DTC channels, and uses data/analytics for demand. Tariff exposure is real but the brand moat appears strong enough to offset.
