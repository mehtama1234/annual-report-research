TC Energy Corporation

Post-liquids-spinoff gas-backbone platform still in expansion mode, benefiting from LNG demand and in-corridor growth projects.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 U.S. Natural Gas Pipelines average flows rose to 27.0 Bcf/d, LNG facility deliveries averaged 3.9 Bcf/d; company sanctioned ~$3B of new growth projects in first half of 2026.
Watch next
Sanctioning and execution of major projects like the US$1.5B Appalachia Supply Project, LNG export demand cyclicality, and U.S. policy toward gas-fired generation and pipeline expansion.

Why It Is Distinctive

TC Energy is not a crude-toll-road company and not a distribution utility. After spinoff of South Bow (liquids pipelines), it is a focused gas-transmission and contracted-power platform whose fortunes depend on LNG demand, gas-fired generation, cross-border flows, in-corridor expansions, and asset reliability. The company is presenting itself as a disciplined gas-and-power infrastructure allocator with attractive repeatable expansion opportunities, not as a broad integrated energy-transport portfolio.
