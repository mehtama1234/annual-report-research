Tenet Healthcare Corporation

Blended acute-care and outpatient-surgery platform with operating margin expansion driven by higher acuity and ambulatory mix shifting despite payer headwinds.

Standout figure
2Q26 adjusted EBITDA raised full-year 2026 outlook to $4.83B-$5.03B from prior guidance, signaling provider earnings can still expand when operating leverage outpaces payer pressure.
Watch next
Sustainability of Medicaid supplemental revenue benefits that do not repeat, payer-mix pressure in exchange enrollment, and continued execution in transitioning volume to higher-margin outpatient settings.

Why It Is Distinctive

Tenet is not only a hospital chain. It is a blended acute-care, outpatient, and ambulatory-surgery platform with 50 hospitals and 132 outpatient facilities, plus USPI's interest in 533 ambulatory surgery centers. The second pure-hospital comparison (after HCA) shows how providers shift care toward surgery centers and outpatient settings to protect margins. Quality depends on payer mix, acuity, Medicaid supplemental programs, ambulatory expansion, and capital discipline rather than on passive admission volumes.
