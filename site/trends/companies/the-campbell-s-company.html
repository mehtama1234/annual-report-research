Why It Is Distinctive

Campbell's is not broadly resilient like Coca-Cola. It's segmented: Meals & Beverages (pantry, soups, sauces) show at-home cooking demand, but Snacks (Goldfish, Pepperidge Farm) face category softness. The tariff impact and inflation hit gross margin harder than simple staple-demand stability suggests.
