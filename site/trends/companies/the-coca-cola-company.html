Why It Is Distinctive

Coca-Cola is not simply defending soda volume; it is re-architecting the beverage relationship through zero-sugar recruitment, pack pricing (single-serve vs multi-packs), geographic affordability options, and cultural activation (FIFA 2026 campaign with connected packaging). The company proved in 2025 that flat unit volume + 5% organic growth is possible through revenue-growth management and data-driven consumer targeting. This is a brand-and-mix story, not a volume story.
