Sulfur affordability and Brazil inventory volatility trump nominal crop-demand strength

Why It Is Distinctive

Unlike CF Industries' cleaner nitrogen-and-gas exposure or Nutrien's retail network model, Mosaic monetizes phosphate-potash conversion and Brazil distribution, creating a business where working capital, regional asset quality, and input-cost shocks can overwhelm headline demand signals. Potash resilience is real, but sulfur bottlenecks and Brazil restructuring keep reported profitability messy even when underlying crop-system demand remains intact.
