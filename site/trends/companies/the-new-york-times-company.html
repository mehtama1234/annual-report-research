The New York Times Company

Trust and habit are becoming monetizable scarcities in a fragmented information environment; direct subscriber ownership and bundle economics replace mass-reach advertising dependency.

Standout figure
Full-year 2025 subscription revenue $1.951B of $2.825B total; 12.78M total subscribers with 12.21M digital-only; digital-only ARPU $9.72; operating cash flow $584.5M; free cash flow $550.5M.

Why It Is Distinctive

NYT is not a legacy newspaper defending shrinking circulation. It owns daily habits and direct billing relationships across News, The Athletic, Audio, Cooking, Games, Wirecutter, and Podcasts. The company is investing more in journalism while expanding profit because the audience relationship is direct, sticky, and diversified. Digital advertising remains cyclical but premium inventory and stronger marketer demand coexist with subscription strength.
