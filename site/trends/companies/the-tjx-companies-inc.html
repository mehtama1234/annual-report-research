The TJX Companies, Inc.

Off-price value-hunt system generated Q1 FY27 sales of $14.3B (up 9%) and comparable sales growth of 6%, proving bargain discovery and treasure-hunt retail remain durable primary demand, not trade-down recession behavior

Standout figure
Q1 FY27 net sales of $14.3B (up 9%), comparable sales up 6%, net income of $1.3B, diluted EPS of $1.19 (up 29%), pretax profit margin of 12.0% (up 1.7 pts), full-year FY26 sales of $60.4B, comparable sales up 5%, net income of $5.5B
Watch next
Whether TJX can maintain comparable sales growth and margin expansion as Amazon penetration and apparel e-commerce accelerate, and whether dense store economics hold as rent and labor costs rise.

Why It Is Distinctive

TJX is not a residual discount retailer; it is a scaled treasure-hunt system where bargain discovery, brand-mix sophistication, global sourcing flexibility, and dense store networks create a durable consumer behavior pattern. Five thousand+ stores globally, multiple banners, and constant assortment churn drive repeat traffic independent of macro stress. This is primary demand for value and novelty, not secondary trade-down.
