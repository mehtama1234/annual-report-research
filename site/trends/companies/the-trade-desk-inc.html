The Trade Desk, Inc.

Buy-side advertising-decisioning infrastructure platform defending open-internet positioning through measurable outcomes, identity, and CTV expansion amid complex media fragmentation.

Standout figure
FY2025 revenue $2.896B, adjusted EBITDA $1.196B; gross spend reached $13.4B; customer retention remained >95% for 12th consecutive year; Q2 2026 revenue $715M with strong underlying platform positioning.
Watch next
Q2 2026 slowdown sustainability and path back to double-digit organic revenue growth, execution of Databricks CustomerLake and vendor partnerships to maintain open-internet competitiveness, and profitability and margin trends as integration and technology investment accelerate.

Why It Is Distinctive

The Trade Desk is not an ad-tech vendor or a media outlet. It is buy-side decisioning infrastructure that helps advertisers navigate fragmented media without forcing them entirely into one walled garden. The company monetizes complexity: as media fragmentation increases and consumer measurement becomes harder, the value of the allocation layer grows. Management frames the business around CTV, retail data, identity, and AI decisioning rather than only mass reach. Customer retention >95% and open-internet positioning show embedded workflow value.
