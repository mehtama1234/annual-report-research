Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc.

Serving-science and laboratory-workflow platform: Analytical Instruments (52% revenue) monetizes research tools, diagnostics, and biopharma production support, not end-demand branding.

Standout figure
FY2025 revenue $44.56B; Q2 2026 revenue $11.99B +10% (5% organic); Pharma segment growth led by GLP-1 therapies, biologics, and systemic nasal drug delivery integration with consumables and services.
Watch next
Whether organic growth can accelerate beyond current 1-5% pace as research spending normalizes and GLP-1 component demand proves durable, and whether acquisition integration continues supporting margin expansion amid rising AI infrastructure costs.

Why It Is Distinctive

Thermo Fisher is not a direct medical distributor or hospital device vendor. It owns the scientific workflow layer: instruments, lab systems, diagnostics, recurring consumables, and services that research institutions, biopharma, and testing labs depend on daily. The moat comes from installed-base stickiness and workflow indispensability, not distribution reach.
