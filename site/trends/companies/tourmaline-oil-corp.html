Tourmaline Oil Corp.

Canada's largest natural-gas producer optimizing storage, processing, and portfolio mix to maximize realized value beyond raw production volumes.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 natural gas as 81% of sold production; storage capacity scheduled to expand from 4.0 Bcf to 14.0 Bcf by April 2027, enabling seasonal optionality in pricing.
Watch next
Ability to navigate pipeline export restrictions (Q2 2026 required third-party gas purchases to meet contracts), success of storage optimization strategy as Dimsdale agreements mature, and durability of natural-gas prices in light of alternative energy policy pressures.

Why It Is Distinctive

Tourmaline is not a generic gas producer or a volume-growth story. It is a continental natural-gas and condensate optimization system where storage, processing choices, export-pipeline constraints, gas marketing, and NGL mix all materially shape realized value. The company explicitly uses storage to inject in lower-price periods and withdraw in higher-price periods, turning infrastructure control into financial advantage. Portfolio engineering through acquisitions shows management-quality focus beyond raw drilling cadence.
