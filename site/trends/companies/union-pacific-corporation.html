Union Pacific Corporation

Pricing discipline and operating-ratio improvement drive rail logistics value more than pure carload growth in a freight intensity view.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 record operating revenue $6.9B with net income $2.0B; full-year guidance improved to high-single-digit EPS growth with pricing above inflation and operating-ratio improvement.
Watch next
Whether improved 2026 guidance proves pricing discipline is sticking or reflects temporary commodity/freight tailwinds, and if the Norfolk Southern merger path affects capital or strategic direction.

Why It Is Distinctive

Union Pacific monetizes pricing power, fuel-surcharge pass-through, and operational efficiency (operating ratio improving) rather than carload volume. Domestic intermodal, grain, chemicals, and automotive diversity show economic breadth. Unlike trucking, rail has fixed-cost scale; unlike logistics software, Union Pacific owns and operates the network.
