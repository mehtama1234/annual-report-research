United Airlines Holdings, Inc.

Premium-network airline earns loyalty through operational quality, product improvements, and digital utility rather than through partner-remuneration loyalty currency.

Standout figure
FY2025 revenue $59.1B; Q2 2026 premium revenue +16% and loyalty revenue +11%; TRASM +12.1%; Starlink installed on 450 aircraft with customer satisfaction >2x higher than traditional Wi-Fi.
Watch next
Whether Starlink Wi-Fi deployment and app integration can sustain premium revenue momentum, and whether capacity discipline in response to fuel volatility can hold without triggering revenue headwinds.

Why It Is Distinctive

United is not Delta. It frames customer relationships around brand-loyal preference built through reliability, premium segmentation, international breadth, app utility, and digital self-service rather than around a financial loyalty currency. The model still uses MileagePlus, but the emphasis is operational excellence first.
