Why It Is Distinctive

UPS is distinct from asset-light freight brokers because it operates a capital-intensive physical parcel-and-logistics network. The strategic choice to sacrifice lower-quality Amazon volume for higher-margin package mix shows the company believes network economics and route density matter more than absolute volume. This makes UPS a test of whether operational rigor can sustain earnings quality through e-commerce cycle moderation.
