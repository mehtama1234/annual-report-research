Upstart Holdings, Inc.

AI lending marketplace scales personal-loan originations and rapidly adds secured products, proving automated credit can work across categories.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 originations $4.2B with conversion rate 19.4%, adjusted EBITDA $76.9M; secured products improved from negative 96% to negative 35% contribution margin year-over-year.
Watch next
Whether national bank charter application materializes and whether secured-product path to profitability can stick as rate environment tightens and partner funding appetite evolves.

Why It Is Distinctive

Upstart is not a card network, not a merchant-linked fintech, not a branchless specialty lender. It is an AI-driven lending marketplace connecting borrowers, bank/CU partners, and products through automated underwriting. Unlike fintech balance-sheet lenders, Upstart charges fees and passes funding risk to partners. Unlike partnerships, Upstart owns the underwriting model and customer funnel.

Evidence Trail

When the Algorithm Is Wrong and Nobody Is Responsible

  • Fiscal 2025 originated $11.0B across 1,497,149 loans with over 90% fully automated; no human loan officer reviewed these applications
  • Q4 2025 conversion rate reached 19.4%, up from 15.1% in 2024, demonstrating algorithmic approval confidence at scale
  • Q2 2026 originations reached $4.2B, demonstrating the model's scale and the absence of human review across millions of lending decisions

Who Owns the Algorithm Owns the Relationship: Where the Value Goes

  • Connects consumers to more than 100 banks and credit unions; more than 90% of loans are fully automated, with approval, pricing, conversion, and fraud all tied to one model layer.
  • In 2025 originations grew 86% and revenue 64% while headcount grew only 18%; Q2 2026 contribution profit hit an all-time-high $193M at a 62% unsecured contribution margin, and conversion rose from 15.1% to 19.4%.
