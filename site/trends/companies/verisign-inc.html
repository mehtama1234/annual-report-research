Why It Is Distinctive

VeriSign owns a toll bridge on the open internet: the registry, resolution authority, and root-server operation for .com and .net. Revenue is recurring. Capex is minimal. Margins are high. The moat is contractual (ICANN agreement through 2030) and infrastructural (critical for DNS resolution). AI and app concentration pose the largest medium-term risks.
