Viasat, Inc.

Satellite-broadband and defense-connectivity provider sits at physical internet layer, turning orbital assets and spectrum into mission-critical recurring revenue.

Standout figure
FY2026 full-year record revenue $4.6B and record adjusted EBITDA $1.6B with free cash flow $177M; backlog up 15%; 23 in-service/operational satellites spanning Ka, L, S bands.
Watch next
Whether ViaSat-3 constellation expansion and satellite launches on schedule, and if government and military funding continues supporting satcom momentum against cyber and geopolitical risk.

Why It Is Distinctive

Viasat is not a software or tower company. It owns orbital spectrum, satellite assets, ground infrastructure, and bandwidth capacity. Unlike terrestrial connectivity, Viasat depends on launch execution, satellite longevity, and technical milestones. Customer dependence (airlines, governments, ships, military) creates recurring revenue but also concentrated risk. The capital intensity and technical risk are much higher than software names.
