Why It Is Distinctive

Vulcan is one of the cleanest physical-infrastructure moats in the archive because local scarcity, freight burden, and permitted reserves create durable competitive position independent of broader industrial cycles. The company demonstrates that unit economics and pricing discipline matter far more than chasing volume in materials where transport cost can overwhelm product value.
