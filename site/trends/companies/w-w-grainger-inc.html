W.W. Grainger, Inc.

Channel-control MRO distributor monetizing procurement workflow embeddedness through scale, assortment, and operational reliability rather than through brand ownership.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 High-Touch Solutions N.A. sales increased 11.9%, Endless Assortment sales increased 13.5% reported and 20.6% on organic basis, demonstrating dual-channel momentum.
Watch next
Ability to grow Endless Assortment channel while maintaining High-Touch Solutions margin profile, execution of digital purchasing tools and data integration, and sustainability of procurement-software-like stickiness in competitive environment.

Why It Is Distinctive

Grainger is not selling products faster; it is making itself operationally easier to buy from than anyone else. The company owns the procurement relationship, assortment logic, and fulfillment reliability that let customer sites keep running. Active customers exceeded 4.6 million; products offered globally exceeded 35 million. The dual model (High-Touch Solutions for larger customers, Endless Assortment for smaller digital buyers) shows channel embeddedness working across different buying behaviors. Distributor strength does not depend on owning the underlying products.
