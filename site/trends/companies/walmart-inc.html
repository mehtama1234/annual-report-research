Why It Is Distinctive

Walmart explicitly frames these services as ecosystem pieces rather than silos. The quarterly evidence (advertising +37-53%, membership +15-17%, eCommerce +24-28%) proves the ecosystem monetization thesis is real and accelerating. This turns routine household traffic into a platform economics model.
