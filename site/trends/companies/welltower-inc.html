Welltower Inc.

Q2 2026 seniors housing operating same-store NOI +20.5% with occupancy gains—Welltower is a property-and-care-delivery hybrid where operator quality and demographic demand drive earnings more than passive rent collection.

Standout figure
Q2 2026 net income $0.61/share, normalized FFO $1.60/share; total portfolio same-store NOI +15.5%; seniors housing operating same-store revenue +9.2%; full-year 2025 FFO growth ~22%.
Watch next
Occupancy sustainability and operator labor pressure; if wage inflation outpaces pricing power in seniors housing, NOI margin recovery could stall. Net debt to EBITDA (~3.0x) is healthy, but capex intensity remains high. Acquisition discipline matters more than property counts.

Why It Is Distinctive

WELL is not a pure real-estate play. It is a seniors housing operating platform where earnings increasingly depend on occupancy, operator execution, resident affordability, and labor dynamics. The REIT wrapper provides financing and capital access, but the economic engine is care delivery and facility utilization. Demographic tailwind (aging) is real but operationally mediated by labor costs and occupancy rates.
