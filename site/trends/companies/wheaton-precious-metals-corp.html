Wheaton Precious Metals Corp.

Capital-light streaming model redefined the gold exposure through $4.3B Antamina deal, moving from $689k oz production to $1.2M oz guidance by 2030

Standout figure
Q2 2026 record revenue of $929M, net earnings of $543M, operating cash flow of $650M, with BHP Antamina deal adding $4.3B of upfront capital deployed and net debt rising to $1.9B
Watch next
Whether 2030 production guidance of 1.2M GEOs proves achievable and whether the temporary leverage (3.3x net debt) normalizes cleanly as Antamina and other projects ramp, given that integration execution remains a counterparty risk, not direct Wheaton control.

Why It Is Distinctive

Wheaton is not a mine operator; it is a streaming and royalty capital allocator layered above partner mines. The Antamina transaction (largest streaming deal ever) shows the model at work: instead of building mines, Wheaton paid $4.3B for production rights, immediately generated record cash flow, and extended projected production 50% by 2030. This proves capital markets prefer pure-play streaming economics over integrated mining.
