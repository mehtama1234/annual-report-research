Xerox Holdings Corp

Mature office-equipment platform defending print decline by shifting 79% of revenue to post-sale services, IT attachment, and workflow software

Standout figure
Q2 2026 revenue of $1.922B with post-sale revenue at $1.346B (70% of total), adjusted operating margin of 10.6%, and pro forma revenue down 3.7%
Watch next
Watch whether Lexmark and ITsavvy integrations can deepen enough to offset continued legacy-print erosion, and whether the company can demonstrate that post-sale revenue can grow despite device-installation pressure

Why It Is Distinctive

Xerox is not a copier seller anymore; it is trying to become an installed-base services and IT-attachment platform. The company acquired ITsavvy and Lexmark to widen beyond print, using integration and cost control to mask underlying device-demand decline.
