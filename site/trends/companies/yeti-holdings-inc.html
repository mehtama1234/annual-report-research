Why It Is Distinctive

YETI is not a traditional sporting-goods retailer. It is a premium-utility brand monetizing outdoor identity, gifting, and direct customer control. The real strength sits in DTC, Amazon Marketplace presence, and corporate-sales layering rather than wholesale dependency.
