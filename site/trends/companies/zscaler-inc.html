Zscaler, Inc.

Cybersecurity-and-policy platform monetizing enterprise control layer as complexity increases around remote work, cloud migration, unmanaged devices, and AI agents.

Standout figure
FY2026 Q3 ARR $3.525B, RPO $6.459B; 748 customers with $1M+ ARR and 4,003 customers with $100K+ ARR; operating cash flow $198M, free cash flow $136M in Q3 FY2026.
Watch next
Ability to convert premium growth into durable GAAP profitability beyond non-GAAP framing and cash generation, execution of acquisitions as AI-security stack broadens, and competitive positioning against platform vendors and hyperscalers bundling more access control.

Why It Is Distinctive

Zscaler is not selling security software to consumers. It monetizes the enterprise control layer that decides who, what, and which machine gets to talk to which application or dataset under what policy. The business behaves more like a policy utility attached to cloud and network movement than like traditional endpoint security. Zero Trust, AI Security, and data governance are turning into recurring traffic-and-governance utilities. Acquisitions (SPLX, SquareX, planned Symmetry Systems) show the company assembling an AI-security stack faster than inventing every module internally.
