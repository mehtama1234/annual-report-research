# Frontier Lane Master Instruction

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

## Core mandate

Your job is not to sample one company.
Your job is to make annual-report-research materially more explanatory than it was before the run, whether that means opening a genuinely new lane or materially strengthening one that already exists.

Cover the assigned `AnnualReports.com` industries end to end for a sustained lane run.
Aim to complete `4` to `8` flagship company packets when the lane has enough breadth and source depth.
If the lane is source-constrained, prefer `3` to `4` fully developed flagship packets over a larger number of shallow starts.

Use `2025` annual reports plus the latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10`.
Treat `AnnualReports.com` as taxonomy and archive confirmation, but use company IR and SEC as authoritative when `AnnualReports.com` lags.

For every completed company, produce both:

- a source-complete packet
- a thematic interpretation

Do not treat thematic work as extra.
The societal, cultural, consumer, industrial, technical, and bigger-picture pattern work is one of the main outputs.
For operating purposes, a packet is not truly source-complete unless it can explain what broader repeated reality the filing chain helps prove.

## What the work includes

This work is not just:

- downloading `2025` annual reports
- collecting the latest three reported quarters around late `2025` and `2026`

It is also:

- identifying consumer trends
- identifying cultural and societal shifts
- identifying industrial and operating pressures
- identifying technical, regulatory, and capital-allocation constraints
- identifying workflow, platform, franchise, and IP monetization systems
- identifying participation, loyalty, occasion, and identity-linked demand systems where relevant
- finding the bigger-picture patterns that repeat across companies
- deciding which signals already look structural and which still look provisional
- deciding who carries the burden stack and who captures the cleaner surplus stack

## End-to-end operating standard

Every lane run should try to leave behind:

- `4` to `8` flagship company packets when realistic
- a lane summary
- cross-company themes
- strongest repeated signals
- recommended next names inside that lane
- a usable handoff with exact continuation logic

Every completed company should make the lane smarter, not just longer.
A packet is not done if it only has document collection.
A packet is done when filing coverage and thematic interpretation are both usable in cross-company comparison.

The packet fields should also do explicit analytical work:

- annual takeaways + latest three-quarter chain
  - prove: what changed and whether the direction is strengthening, weakening, or persisting
- plain-English operating model
  - prove: what job the company really performs in the system
- strategy read
  - prove: how management is responding to the pressure or opportunity
- growth engine + economic lever
  - prove: what is really carrying the story and what actually moves the economics
- operating constraint
  - prove: where the system is strained
- exact supporting facts
  - prove: the claim directly rather than by implication
- burden-versus-beneficiary interpretation
  - prove: who gets cleaner economics and who absorbs the messy work
- thesis breaker + watchlist
  - prove: the work is falsifiable and ready for continuation

Different claim types also need different proof burdens:

- consumer claim
  - show: what behavior changed and which facts prove it
- cultural or societal claim
  - show: which real-life pressure is creating demand and why it is broader than one company
- industrial or operating claim
  - show: where the strain sits and what happens economically when that pressure changes
- technical or infrastructure claim
  - show: where software is the control layer and where physical bottlenecks still decide outcomes
- capital or balance-sheet claim
  - show: who must carry property, inventory, debt, or financing burden to keep the system working
- cross-company pattern claim
  - show: exact support from at least three companies and what evidence would weaken the broader pattern

Do not update shared repo-wide indexes continuously during exploration.
Do index and synthesis updates at the end of a coherent batch, or leave the batch ready for integration by another thread.

Commit coherent checkpoints as you go.
Do not wait until the whole frontier is finished before creating usable commits.

If you run out of obvious thread prompts, pick the next best adjacent flagship company on your own and continue.
Do not stop just because one company, document chain, or sub-industry is awkward.

## Priority expansion map

### CLI 4: Healthcare Frontier

Mission:
Compare frontier healthcare with scaled recurring-care systems, care operators, devices, plans, and major therapeutics.

Core and expanded areas:

- health care plans
- hospitals
- long-term care facilities
- medical appliances and equipment
- medical instruments and supplies
- drug manufacturers - major

What to prove:

- whether the cleanest economics sit with payer interfaces, care operators, installed-base device workflows, instruments and supplies, or major therapy portfolios
- how aging, reimbursement pressure, utilization, labor intensity, and installed-base workflow control interact
- where recurring care demand is translated into the strongest surplus capture

- company profiles

- commit hash

## Interpretation standard

At the company level, explain:

- what changed in the annual
- what changed across the latest three reported quarters
- what management is emphasizing
- what demand system the company serves
- what monetization system the company uses
- which customer, cultural, societal, industrial, technical, or capital-allocation pressures matter most
- whether the company sits in the burden stack or the cleaner surplus stack
- what repeated adaptation pattern the company reveals beyond its own sector language
- what behavior, routine, institution, or operating dependency is becoming more normalized because of those same pressures
- which packet fields are actually doing the analytical work

At the lane level, explain:

- what several management teams are reacting to at once
- what behavior is normalizing across customers, institutions, or society
- which repeated signals seem structural
- which repeated signals still seem provisional
- where the cleanest economics in the lane actually sit
- which adjacent names are the best next tests of the pattern
- what larger lived-system shift multiple management teams are describing in different vocabularies
- which pressures are spreading socially, culturally, operationally, technically, or institutionally rather than just cyclically
