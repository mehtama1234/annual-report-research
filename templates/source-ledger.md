# Source Ledger Template

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

Use evidence tags:

- `[Disclosed]` company filing, press release, or official investor-relations material
- `[Filed]` SEC filing or exhibit
- `[Reported]` credible press or transcript provider
- `[Estimated]` derived or analyst estimate
- `[Speculative]` weak or unverified
- `[verify]` found but not yet confirmed directly

The ledger should make it easy to answer:

- which exact source supports which claim
- which period that source belongs to
- how strong the source is
- what still needs verification before the claim is safe to reuse
