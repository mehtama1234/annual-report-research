# Status Rubric

Date baseline: 2026-08-10

Use these labels consistently across lane runs, handoffs, and integration notes.

## Evidence status

- `saved-locally`
  - The source artifact is on disk in the repo.
- `verified-not-saved`
  - The source was authoritatively confirmed through company IR, SEC, or another accepted source, but the file is not saved locally.
- `missing`
  - The artifact has not been confirmed or collected yet.

## Packet status

- `source-complete`
  - The annual, annual filing, and latest three reported quarters as of `2026-08-10` are present or explicitly verified, and the packet includes thematic interpretation.
- `partial-usable`
  - Enough evidence exists to support a real packet and cross-company comparison, but one or more required artifacts are still missing or only partially verified.
- `collection-started`
  - Raw evidence gathering has begun, but the packet is not yet decision-grade.
- `not-started`
  - No meaningful packet work has begun.

## Handoff company status

- `completed`
  - The company is ready to count as part of the finished flagship batch.
- `partial`
  - The company matters to the lane and has meaningful work done, but it should not yet count as fully completed.
- `deferred`
  - The company was considered, but it is intentionally not part of the active batch.

## Integration status

- `integrated`
  - Relevant indexes and synthesis layers were updated after the batch.
- `ready-for-integration`
  - The packet and handoff are complete enough that another thread can integrate them without redoing research.
- `not-ready-for-integration`
  - Evidence, packetization, or thematic interpretation is still too incomplete for clean integration.

## Thematic quality status

- `theme-complete`
  - The company or lane clearly surfaces consumer, cultural, societal, industrial, operating, technical, or capital-structure repeated-pattern signals, plus the relevant monetization system.
- `theme-thin`
  - Basic interpretation exists, but the bigger-picture consumer, social, industrial, or monetization pattern work is still weak.
- `document-only`
  - Filing collection exists, but meaningful thematic interpretation does not.
